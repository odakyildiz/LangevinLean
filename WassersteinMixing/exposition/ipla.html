Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Proof: the particle lift and the sharp bias

The zero-dimensional case has zero parameter error, as noted above. For the following argument suppose DN>0D_N>0DN​>0, so L≥m>0L\ge m>0L≥m>0. The scaled IPLA recursion is the Gaussian ULA recursion for VNV_NVN​ with step h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N. Since ∑i∥(δθ,N δzi)∥2=N∥δz∥2\sum_i\|(\delta\theta,\sqrt N\,\delta z_i)\|^2=N\|\delta z\|^2∑i​∥(δθ,N​δzi​)∥2=N∥δz∥2, the potential VNV_NVN​ is C2C^2C2, NmNmNm-strongly convex and has an NLNLNL-Lipschitz gradient.
