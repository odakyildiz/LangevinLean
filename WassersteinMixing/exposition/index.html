01Deriving the potential contract from the source Hessian bounds §

The source assumes precisely V∈C2V\in C^2V∈C2 and αId≼∇2V≼βId\alpha I_d\preccurlyeq\nabla^2V\preccurlyeq\beta I_dαId​≼∇2V≼βId​. Symmetry and the quadratic-form bounds give ∥∇2V(x)∥≤β\|\nabla^2V(x)\|\le\beta∥∇2V(x)∥≤β. The mean-value theorem makes ∇V\nabla V∇V globally β\betaβ-Lipschitz. Integrating the lower Hessian inequality along line segments gives the strong supporting-plane inequality, hence α\alphaα-strong convexity.

Thus the first-order contract used by the existing Langevin library is a consequence of the paper's original assumptions. The final proof works directly under C2C^2C2; it does not require the source proof's temporary smoothness and polynomial-growth assumption or a smoothing limit.

03Proving the actual reversible Markov semigroup §

Evaluating an observable at XtX_tXt​ is an isometry from L2(π)L^2(\pi)L2(π) into the common probability space. Compress evaluation at ttt against evaluation at zero to define PtP_tPt​. Future Brownian increments are independent of the complete initial/Brownian past. Finite shifted Euler maps and their genuine L2L^2L2 limits prove

E[f(Xa+t)∣Fa]=Ptf(Xa),Ps+t=PsPt.\mathbb E[f(X_{a+t})\mid\mathcal F_a]=P_tf(X_a), \qquad P_{s+t}=P_sP_t.E[f(Xa+t​)∣Fa​]=Pt​f(Xa​),Ps+t​=Ps​Pt​.

For reversibility, realize MALA with the same Brownian Gaussian proposals and independent uniform accept/reject variables. Its anchored finite-step laws are exactly reversible. The proved rejection-distance estimate and Euler convergence give convergence in probability to the actual diffusion; stationarity and density upgrade every observable to strong L2L^2L2 convergence. Self-adjointness passes to PtP_tPt​. Its contraction and strong continuity follow from actual evaluation isometries and state continuity.

09Passing the actual stationary comparison to transport §

The synchronous trajectories give actual couplings of π\piπ and each stationary-start Gaussian ULA law. Their uniform L2L^2L2 bound therefore bounds the actual quadratic transport infimum. To prove its triangle inequality, disintegrate two couplings with the same middle marginal and construct a genuine triple law. Minkowski bounds its endpoint cost; taking both coupling infima gives

W2(μ,ξ)≤W2(μ,ν)+W2(ν,ξ).W_2(\mu,\xi)\le W_2(\mu,\nu)+W_2(\nu,\xi).W2​(μ,ξ)≤W2​(μ,ν)+W2​(ν,ξ).

Apply this with the stationary-start ULA law between π\piπ and π^\widehat\piπ, then use the proved attraction to the constructed invariant law. Theorem 1 follows.
