The density model built from the joint potentials

This section constructs the tilted latent model from the joint potentials U1,…,UMU_1,\dots,U_MU1​,…,UM​ of assumption A3 and identifies its optimizer. For a parameter a∈Rdθa\in\mathbb R^{d_\theta}a∈Rdθ​ and a datum mmm, the unnormalized posterior density on Rdz\mathbb R^{d_z}Rdz​ is ρam(z)=e−Um(a,z)\rho_a^m(z)=e^{-U_m(a,z)}ρam​(z)=e−Um​(a,z), its normalizer is Zm(a)=∫ρam dzZ_m(a)=\int\rho_a^m\,dzZm​(a)=∫ρam​dz, and the posterior is the probability measure

πam(dz)=ρam(z)Zm(a) dz.\pi_a^m(dz)=\frac{\rho_a^m(z)}{Z_m(a)}\,dz.πam​(dz)=Zm​(a)ρam​(z)​dz.

The log evidence is ℓ(a)=M−1∑mlog⁡Zm(a)=−V(a)\ell(a)=M^{-1}\sum_m\log Z_m(a)=-V(a)ℓ(a)=M−1∑m​logZm​(a)=−V(a), with VVV the average marginal potential of the previous section. We show that measurability, positivity, integrability and positivity of the normalizers follow from A3, so the construction requires no further hypothesis; that the free energy F(a,q)=−ℓ(a)+M−1∑mKL(qm∥πam)F(a,q)=-\ell(a)+M^{-1}\sum_m\mathrm{KL}(q^m\|\pi_a^m)F(a,q)=−ℓ(a)+M−1∑m​KL(qm∥πam​) of this model has exactly one global minimizer (a⋆,πa⋆)(a_\star,\pi_{a_\star})(a⋆​,πa⋆​​); and that every posterior has a finite second moment. The last step transfers uniqueness to the paper's literal density integral

F~(a,q)=1M∑m=1M∫Rdzq~m(z)log⁡q~m(z)ρam(z) dz\widetilde F(a,q)=\frac1M\sum_{m=1}^M\int_{\mathbb R^{d_z}}\tilde q^m(z)\log\frac{\tilde q^m(z)}{\rho_a^m(z)}\,dzF(a,q)=M1​m=1∑M​∫Rdz​​q~​m(z)logρam​(z)q~​m(z)​dz

on the domain of collections qqq with finite relative entropies and finite second moments; here q~m\tilde q^mq~​m is the Lebesgue density of qmq^mqm.

This domain is the corrected form of the paper's P2\mathcal P_2P2​ domain (extra assumption Extra A1).

The optimizer a⋆a_\stara⋆​ is the target of both main convergence theorems.

02The density model and its evidence §

Mathematical statement and proof

Density models. Let ν\nuν be a measure on a measurable space ZZZ. A density model with MMM data consists of functions ρam:Z→R\rho_a^m:Z\to\mathbb Rρam​:Z→R, indexed by parameters aaa and data mmm, such that each ρam\rho_a^mρam​ is measurable, strictly positive and ν\nuν-integrable with Zm(a)=∫ρam dν>0Z_m(a)=\int\rho_a^m\,d\nu>0Zm​(a)=∫ρam​dν>0. Its posteriors are πam(dz)=Zm(a)−1ρam(z) ν(dz)\pi_a^m(dz)=Z_m(a)^{-1}\rho_a^m(z)\,\nu(dz)πam​(dz)=Zm​(a)−1ρam​(z)ν(dz); these are probability measures, because the density ρam/Zm(a)\rho_a^m/Z_m(a)ρam​/Zm​(a) is nonnegative and has integral Zm(a)/Zm(a)=1Z_m(a)/Z_m(a)=1Zm​(a)/Zm​(a)=1. Its posterior family has log evidence ℓ(a)=M−1∑mlog⁡Zm(a)\ell(a)=M^{-1}\sum_m\log Z_m(a)ℓ(a)=M−1∑m​logZm​(a) and posteriors πam\pi_a^mπam​.

Claim. Let λ>0\lambda>0λ>0 and let every UmU_mUm​ be differentiable and λ\lambdaλ-strongly convex. Then ρam(z)=e−Um(a,z)\rho_a^m(z)=e^{-U_m(a,z)}ρam​(z)=e−Um​(a,z), with ν\nuν Lebesgue measure on Rdz\mathbb R^{d_z}Rdz​, is a density model, and its log evidence satisfies

ℓ(a)=1M∑m=1Mlog⁡Zm(a)=−V(a)for every a.\ell(a)=\frac1M\sum_{m=1}^M\log Z_m(a)=-V(a)\qquad\text{for every }a.ℓ(a)=M1​m=1∑M​logZm​(a)=−V(a)for every a.

Proof. Measurability. By the previous step the slice z↦Um(a,z)z\mapsto U_m(a,z)z↦Um​(a,z) is differentiable, hence continuous; composing with the continuous functions t↦−tt\mapsto-tt↦−t and exp⁡\expexp gives a continuous, hence Borel measurable, function. Positivity. The exponential is positive. Integrability and positive normalizer. These are the facts recalled in the marginal section: strong convexity gives the Gaussian bound e−Um(a,z)≤Ce−λ∥z∥2/4e^{-U_m(a,z)}\le Ce^{-\lambda\|z\|^2/4}e−Um​(a,z)≤Ce−λ∥z∥2/4, and the integral of a positive continuous integrable function is positive.

Thus normalizability of the posteriors is a consequence of A3, not an additional assumption.

05The literal density integral on the finite-second-moment domain §

Mathematical statement and proof

The paper writes the free energy as the literal density integral F~\widetilde FF of the summary. The variational section on regular density models proves the following facts for every density model with M≥1M\ge1M≥1 data.

  • If qqq is admissible at aaa, then each qmq^mqm has a Lebesgue density q~m\tilde q^mq~​m, constructed from the Radon–Nikodym derivative r=dqm/dπamr=dq^m/d\pi_a^mr=dqm/dπam​ as q~m=r ρam/Zm(a)\tilde q^m=r\,\rho_a^m/Z_m(a)q~​m=rρam​/Zm​(a); the function q~mlog⁡(q~m/ρam)\tilde q^m\log(\tilde q^m/\rho_a^m)q~​mlog(q~​m/ρam​) is integrable; and ∫q~mlog⁡(q~m/ρam) dz=KL(qm∥πam)−log⁡Zm(a)\int\tilde q^m\log(\tilde q^m/\rho_a^m)\,dz=\mathrm{KL}(q^m\|\pi_a^m)-\log Z_m(a)∫q~​mlog(q~​m/ρam​)dz=KL(qm∥πam​)−logZm​(a). Consequently F~(a,q)=F(a,q)\widetilde F(a,q)=F(a,q)F(a,q)=F(a,q) on admissible pairs.
  • Let D\mathcal DD be any set of collections that contains every posterior collection πb\pi_bπb​. Then qqq is admissible at aaa, q∈Dq\in\mathcal Dq∈D, and F~(a,q)≤F~(b,r)\widetilde F(a,q)\le\widetilde F(b,r)F(a,q)≤F(b,r) for every admissible (b,r)(b,r)(b,r) with r∈Dr\in\mathcal Dr∈D, if and only if aaa maximizes ℓ\ellℓ and q=πaq=\pi_aq=πa​.

Claim. Let M≥1M\ge1M≥1, λ>0\lambda>0λ>0, and let every UmU_mUm​ be differentiable and λ\lambdaλ-strongly convex. Then there is exactly one pair (a,q)(a,q)(a,q) such that

  1. qqq is admissible at aaa and every qmq^mqm has a finite second moment, and
  2. F~(a,q)≤F~(b,r)\widetilde F(a,q)\le\widetilde F(b,r)F(a,q)≤F(b,r) for every bbb and every rrr admissible at bbb whose measures have finite second moments.

Proof. Let D\mathcal DD be the set of collections whose measures all have finite second moments. By the previous step every posterior collection lies in D\mathcal DD. By the second cited fact, a pair satisfies (1) and (2) if and only if aaa maximizes ℓ\ellℓ and q=πaq=\pi_aq=πa​. By the variational characterization this holds if and only if (a,q)(a,q)(a,q) is a global minimizer of FFF. By the third step there is exactly one such pair. □\square□

In particular the paper's Proposition 1 holds on its finite-second-moment domain with the literal integral: the unique optimizer is (a⋆,πa⋆)(a_\star,\pi_{a_\star})(a⋆​,πa⋆​​).

How the Lean proof follows this argument

In existsUnique_p2_literalOptimizer, D.literalEnergy is F~\widetilde FF; the trial density used inside it is the Radon–Nikodym construction of the first cited fact (the definition trialDensity of RegularVariational.DensityModel), so no density of the trial law is assumed.

  1. domain is the predicate “every measure has finite second moment”, written with MemLp (fun z ↦ z) 2, and hpost proves that posteriors satisfy it by posterior_memLp.
  2. heq p chains two equivalences: D.literal_minimizers_on_iff hM domain hpost (the second cited fact) and the reversed D.family.global_minimizers_iff hM (the variational characterization).
  3. obtain ⟨p, hp, hu⟩ takes the unique global minimizer from existsUnique_variationalOptimizer, and the final term transports existence and uniqueness through heq.
