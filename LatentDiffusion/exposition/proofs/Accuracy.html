How the Lean proof follows this argument

budget_le_epsilon names the five bounds hb, hp, hd, hw and ho. burnin proves the logarithmic estimate. scaled_sqrt_le handles the particle and Brownian terms by comparing squares; Real.sqrt_le_left handles the oracle term. Positive denominators are discharged by positivity and field_simp. The last nlinarith adds the five allocations to hbudget.
