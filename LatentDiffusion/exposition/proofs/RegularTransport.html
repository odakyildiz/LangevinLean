Displacement convexity of the free energy

This section proves the strong geodesic convexity of the tilted free energy along displacement interpolations, the paper's Lemma on geodesic convexity,.

The extra transport assumptions are R4.

Let ZZZ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space of dimension ddd, let μ\muμ be a probability measure on ZZZ (the initial law) and let T:Z→ZT:Z\to ZT:Z→Z be a transport map.
