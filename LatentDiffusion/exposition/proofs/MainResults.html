particle_oracle_parameter_bound

**New A.** Every iteration obeys one explicit parameter RMS bound, with separate initialization, finite-particle, time-discretization and centered stochastic-oracle errors. Here K=M*N. The independent-copy residual assumption uses zero L² cross moments and a per-copy second moment V; its 1/sqrt(K) rate is derived. `σ` bounds only the centered oracle innovations.
