01The derivative of a normalizer is its negative weighted score §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Under the stated local domination assumptions, DZi(a)[v]=−∫e−Ui(a,z)⟨∇aUi(a,z),v⟩ dν(z).DZ_i(a)[v]=-\int e^{-U_i(a,z)}\langle\nabla_aU_i(a,z),v\rangle\,d\nu(z).DZi​(a)[v]=−∫e−Ui​(a,z)⟨∇a​Ui​(a,z),v⟩dν(z)..

Proof. For almost every zzz, the chain rule differentiates a↦e−Ui(a,z)a\mapsto e^{-U_i(a,z)}a↦e−Ui​(a,z) to the displayed integrand. The operator norm of that derivative is bounded by the common integrable dominating function throughout the neighbourhood. The dominated differentiation theorem therefore exchanges differentiation and integration. The weighted gradient is Bochner integrable by the same bound. Continuity of the inner-product functional permits it to be moved outside the Bochner integral, giving the claimed derivative for every direction vvv. □\square□

How the Lean proof follows this argument

normalizer_hasFDerivAt defines the derivative-valued integrand D, checks its measurable and dominated hypotheses, and applies hasFDerivAt_integral_of_dominated_of_fderiv_le. The final heq moves the continuous linear inner-product map through the integral. HasFDerivAt expresses the full Fréchet derivative, not merely directional differentiability.

02A local evidence maximizer has zero total posterior score §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If aaa is a local maximizer of ℓ\ellℓ, then ∑i∫∇aUi(a,z) dπi,a(z)=0\sum_i\int\nabla_aU_i(a,z)\,d\pi_{i,a}(z)=0∑i​∫∇a​Ui​(a,z)dπi,a​(z)=0.

Proof. Positivity of Zi(a)Z_i(a)Zi​(a) permits the logarithmic chain rule. Dividing its derivative by Zi(a)Z_i(a)Zi​(a) converts the weighted score integral into a posterior expectation, so ∇ℓ(a)=−M−1∑i∫∇aUi(a,z) dπi,a(z)\nabla\ell(a)=-M^{-1}\sum_i\int\nabla_aU_i(a,z)\,d\pi_{i,a}(z)∇ℓ(a)=−M−1∑i​∫∇a​Ui​(a,z)dπi,a​(z). A differentiable real function on the whole parameter space has zero derivative at a local maximum: for each direction its restriction to a real line has a local maximum at zero, whose derivative is zero. Thus the averaged score vanishes. Since M>0M>0M>0, multiplication by MMM yields the unaveraged identity. Posterior integrability is furnished by the normalizer domination assumptions, rather than inferred from formal cancellation. □\square□
