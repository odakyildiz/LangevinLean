Actual law distances along characteristic curves

Optimal anchors and square-integrable characteristic curves prove upper-right distance slopes and integrated metric-speed bounds.

OptimalAnchor

A regular optimal-transport realization to a fixed target. Minimality is over genuine measure couplings, not a distance or convergence bound. In a characteristic-flow application the anchor is Tt(Yt)T_t(Y_t)Tt​(Yt​), where TtT_tTt​ is a regular optimal transport map from the current law to the fixed target.

distance_right_slope

Corrected source metric-speed lemma, in the stronger upper-Dini form. The ordinary distance derivative need not exist at a crossing time.
