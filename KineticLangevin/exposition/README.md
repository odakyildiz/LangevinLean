# Kinetic Langevin source correspondence

This development concerns the user-supplied `papers/kinetic_Langevin/mainDR_dec_18.tex`, by Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand. The published paper is [On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions](https://doi.org/10.3150/19-BEJ1178), *Bernoulli* 26(3), 1956–1988 (2020). The audit uses the supplied TeX, whose exact public version is not encoded in its filename. It does not assume that every statement in that draft is identical to the published edition.

`reference_audit.json` records the SHA-256 of the supplied source, all 3 theorem environments, 4 lemmas, 4 propositions and 1 remark, and all 48 source labels. Every statement is preserved verbatim with its source file and line range. The source labels `th:3` and `th:4` correspond to printed Theorems 2 and 3 because the theorem counter is consecutive. Equations defining both algorithms and their noise covariances are included in the label ledger.

The authored mathematics in `docs/kinetic_content.py` explains the continuous dynamics, synchronous coupling, transformed energy, friction choices, frozen-force exponential integrator, correlated noise, local defect, geometric recurrence, Hessian method and Gaussian tail argument. `module_notes.json` pairs the exact declarations with detailed mathematical proofs corresponding to the checked arguments. The site generator displays the source next to these notes. The material describes the paper's argument; only the explicitly checked Lean declarations constitute formal coverage. A recurrence theorem with a local-error hypothesis does not by itself prove the Gaussian-chain convergence theorem.

The audit records the supplied source's corrections: the inconsistent `exp1` prefactor; the unstated stationary reference behind the second-order local-error estimate; omitted stochastic Hessian-difference terms in Proposition 3's subtraction; the reversed comparison of $\psi_2$ and $\varphi_2$; and the dimension restriction used in the final simplification of the fourth Gaussian moment. The corrected probability-law results and the accuracy guarantee retaining every tail term are proved.

The informal refined condition-number bound at source lines 702–708 has a separate correction. Set $\kappa=M/m$, $\eta=M_2/m^{3/2}$, and $\rho=\kappa_2=\eta^{2/3}+\kappa p^{-1/3}$. When neglecting the exponential tail, the corrected bound includes the factor $\sqrt{\kappa/\rho}$ multiplying $\sqrt p\,\rho^2+\sqrt{p/\varepsilon}\,\rho^{5/4}$. The printed expression is justified if $\rho\ge\kappa$, or after replacing $\rho$ by $\max(\kappa,\rho)$. For natural $t\ge2$, set $m=1$, $M=t^2$, $M_2=1$, $p=t^6$, and $\varepsilon=1$. Then $\rho=2$, the exact normalized count coefficient is at least $20t^4$, and the printed expression is at most $8t^3$. No uniform multiplicative constant repairs that gap. This counterexample concerns the proposed sufficient step-size bound; no algorithmic lower bound is asserted. The audit records the Lean verification status of this additional step-and-count calculation separately from the complete all-tail sampler guarantee.

The continuous probability model uses the constructed `IsPreBrownianReal` coordinate laws and a selected solution continuous in $L^2$ on each finite horizon. Its kinetic integral equation, stationary marginals, source independence and fixed-time probability laws are checked directly. A separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is not asserted; this distinction does not add a hypothesis to the proved law or numerical convergence bounds.

The original `papers/` tree is a private reference input for `main` and must be excluded from every `gh-pages` publication. The public explanatory pages must use bibliographic URLs and the audit's source identifiers rather than copying the TeX source tree into the publication artifact.
