Langevin algorithms in Lean

Formalization of Langevin-based stochastic algorithms and their convergence bounds for sampling and particle methods, with mathematical proofs alongside Lean. These pages are prepared by Omer Deniz Akyildiz with AI assistance as part of an ongoing effort to formalize Langevin algorithms comprehensively. This is draft work, and the pages may contain mistakes.

01
Wasserstein distance

Unadjusted Langevin under Strong Convexity

Wasserstein error bounds for Gaussian ULA in both stable step-size regimes.

Source: Dalalyan and Karagulyan (2019).

Published bounds; Gibbs stationarity proved.

Full proof (PDF)

02
Relative entropy

Unadjusted Langevin under LSI

Relative-entropy convergence of Gaussian ULA from a log-Sobolev inequality and finite initial entropy.

Source: Vempala and Wibisono (2019).

Includes finite relative entropy at every iterate.

Full proof (PDF)

03
Interacting particles

Interacting Particle Langevin under Strong Convexity

Finite-time parameter error from lifted ULA convergence and marginal concentration.

Source: Glyn-Davies et al. (2025).

The extension to unequal parameter and latent dimensions and the entropy analogue are unpublished.

Extra assumptions: Entropy extension: lifted-target LSI and finite initial joint entropy; the specialization assumes linear scaling of the LSI constant.

Full proof (PDF)

04
Nonsmooth models

Proximal particle Langevin

Corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA and MYPGD bounds, with proximal-map calculus and mean-field limits.

Source: Cordero Encinar et al. (2025).

Partial paper coverage; corrected bounds and proofs are unpublished.

Full proof (PDF)

05
Kinetic sampling

Kinetic Langevin Monte Carlo

Wasserstein convergence for the kinetic diffusion and KLMC/KLMC2 samplers, with Gibbs stationarity and accuracy guarantees.

Source: Dalalyan and Riou-Durand (2020).

Includes unpublished corrections to the source proofs.

Extra assumptions: A dimension restriction for the simplified KLMC2 bound, and a curvature condition for the printed tail-free iteration bound.

Full proof (PDF)

06
Kinetic interacting particles

Kinetic Interacting Particle Langevin Monte Carlo

Parameter error for the kinetic particle diffusion, KIPLMC1 and KIPLMC2, with sharp marginal concentration.

Source: Valsecchi Oliva and Akyildiz (2026).

Includes Gaussian sampler bounds and explicit accuracy guarantees.

Full proof (PDF)

07
General stochastic optimisation

Particle-based Generalised Stochastic Optimisation

General optimiser and sampler models, contraction and particle bounds, and time-uniform discretisation bounds for two new methods.

Source: Zhang and Akyildiz (2026).

Four main theorems under explicit assumptions; the two discretisation bounds are new and unpublished.

Extra assumptions: Extra A1–Extra A3 for the stated theorem branches; Extra A4–Extra A5 for the additional numerical bounds.

Full proof (PDF)

08
Latent diffusion and inference

Training Latent Diffusion Models with Interacting Particle Algorithms

Variational identities, marginal optimizers, interacting-particle error bounds and explicit accuracy choices.

Source: Wang, Kuntz and Akyildiz (2026).

19 corrected statements proved under extra assumptions; the literal printed statements are not certified as stated.

Extra assumptions: Extra A1–Extra A7, as specified for each corrected paper result.

Full proof (PDF)

09
Average-smoothness sampling

Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness

LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian LMC and control-variate SGLD bounds with exact smoothness constants.

Source: Dalalyan and Karagulyan (2026).

Main sampling theorems with the source hypotheses and constants.

Full proof (PDF)
