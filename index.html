Wasserstein distance

Unadjusted Langevin under Strong Convexity

Geometric contraction and discretization bias for Gaussian ULA, with the stationary reference constructed from the potential assumptions.

Paper references

Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv

The displayed coefficient 1.65 and both step-size regimes follow Theorem 1 of Dalalyan and Karagulyan (2019).

Interacting particles

Interacting Particle Langevin under Strong Convexity

A finite-time parameter error bound, using the lifted ULA system and concentration of the parameter marginal.

Published source and unpublished extension

Alex Glyn-Davies, Connor Duffin, Ieva Kazlauskaite, Mark Girolami, and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Statistical Finite Elements via Interacting Particle Langevin Dynamics. SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification 13(3), 1200–1227. Audited version: arXiv:2409.07101v2

Source: Theorem 1, equation (34), and Appendix B of arXiv:2409.07101v2. The independent-dimension extension and its proof presented here are unpublished.

Kinetic sampling

Kinetic Langevin Monte Carlo

Exponential integrators, transformed-energy contraction, Gaussian innovations, and a source-by-source coverage audit.

Published source; corrected checked proofs

Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

Actual kinetic mixing, Gibbs stationarity, KLMC and KLMC2 sampling bounds, and accuracy guarantees are checked, with the supplied source corrections stated explicitly.

Kinetic interacting particles

Kinetic Interacting Particle Langevin Monte Carlo

Shared exponential integration for KIPLMC1, OBABO for KIPLMC2, scaled geometry, chain laws, and parameter concentration.

Published source; checked diffusion and sampler proofs

Paul Valsecchi Oliva and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2026). Kinetic interacting particle Langevin Monte Carlo. Statistics and Computing 36, article 221. arXiv

Actual KIPLD mixing, both Gaussian particle sampler RMS bounds, sharp MMLE concentration, exact integrator reuse, and explicit accuracy guarantees are checked.
