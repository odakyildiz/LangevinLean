Sampling algorithms · Lean + Mathlib

LangevinLean

An early research project for formalizing sampling algorithms, starting with strongly convex ULA and IPLA.

ULA supplies reusable analysis, Brownian and Gibbs theory, and sampling estimates. IPLA adds the particle construction and parameter concentration, with independent parameter and latent dimensions. Further methods can build on the shared library.
