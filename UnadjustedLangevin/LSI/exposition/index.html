02The functional-inequality consequences used by the proof §

Three consequences of LSI and smoothness control the moments. Their constants matter, because they determine the allowed step size.

Consequently ν\nuν has a finite exponential square moment for a sufficiently small positive coefficient. The entropy variational inequality

The equilibrium score moment. For a smooth potential, integrate div⁡(e−U∇U)\operatorname{div}(e^{-U}\nabla U)div(e−U∇U) with compact cutoffs and let their radius tend to infinity. The tail estimate and linear growth of ∇U\nabla U∇U justify the limit. Thus

03From LSI to transport, then to the current gradient moment §
