\section{The ULA update}
For $E=\mathbb R^d$, define
\[
 T_h(x,z)=x-h\nabla U(x)+\sqrt{2h}\,z.
\]
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:ula}
Let $U:E\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable and $m$-strongly convex on
$E$, with $L$-Lipschitz gradient, where $L\ge0$. For $h\ge0$ and
any $x,y,z\in E$,
\[
 \|T_h(x,z)-T_h(y,z)\|
 \le\sqrt{1-2hm+h^2L^2}\,\|x-y\|.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The shared noise cancels:
\[
 T_h(x,z)-T_h(y,z)
 =(x-h\nabla U(x))-(y-h\nabla U(y)).
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:monotone} supplies the inner-product hypothesis of
Lemma~\ref{lem:step}; Lipschitz continuity supplies its norm
hypothesis. Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:step}.
\end{proof}
\textit{Lean theorem:} \texttt{ula\_shared\_noise\_contract}
in \texttt{ULAupdate.lean}.

The theorem keeps $m\in\mathbb R$ and does not assume a step-size
restriction. The coefficient is strictly below one whenever
$m>0$, $h>0$, and $hL^2<2m$. The proof also covers $d=0$; in that
case every pair of points is equal, even if the radicand is negative.
This is the deterministic estimate used in a synchronous coupling;
no probabilistic or Wasserstein statement is assumed or proved here.
