Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness

The average-smoothness convergence argument and its supporting probability estimates.

Supplied manuscript; checked sampling proofs and separate source mistakes

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. Verified counterexamples to the false Gaussian-design proposition, its empirical fourth-moment event and an intermediate SGLD transient comparison have separate pages linked from the top of the overview. The complete source inventory distinguishes this main-theorem review scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (undated manuscript). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. Supplied Arxiv_version_May_26.tex; publication status not established.

Theorem 1: constant-step convergence

Mathematical statement

W22(νk,π)≤e−2mkhW22(ν0,π)+(Mav+m)hp2m.W_2^2(\nu_k,\pi)\le e^{-2mkh}W_2^2(\nu_0,\pi) +\frac{(M_{\rm av}+m)hp}{2m}.W22​(νk​,π)≤e−2mkhW22​(ν0​,π)+2m(Mav​+m)hp​.

Theorem 2 (th:3): LMC with the exact Laplacian condition

Mathematical statement

Let f∈C3(Rp)f\in C^3(\mathbb R^p)f∈C3(Rp) be mmm-strongly convex with M∞M_\inftyM∞​-Lipschitz gradient, m>0m>0m>0, and assume exactly

∥∇Δf(x)∥2≤MΔ2p(x∈Rp),MΔ≥0.\|\nabla\Delta f(x)\|^2\le M_\Delta^2p\qquad(x\in\mathbb R^p),\quad M_\Delta\ge0.∥∇Δf(x)∥2≤MΔ2​p(x∈Rp),MΔ​≥0.

For the actual Gaussian LMC chain and 0<h<1/M∞0<h<1/M_\infty0<h<1/M∞​,

W2(νk,π)≤(1−mh)kW2(ν0,π)+(2M∞Mav+MΔ)hpm.W_2(\nu_k,\pi)\le(1-mh)^kW_2(\nu_0,\pi) +\frac{(2M_\infty\sqrt{M_{\rm av}}+M_\Delta)h\sqrt p}{m}.W2​(νk​,π)≤(1−mh)kW2​(ν0​,π)+m(2M∞​Mav​​+MΔ​)hp​​.

The proof derives a weighted Dynkin identity for the constructed stationary Brownian diffusion from fresh Gaussian Euler steps. Compact C² approximations of its score preserve precisely the source C³ assumption; the Gibbs score moment and gradient-generator bound retain the exact average smoothness. Clipping and the transport infimum give the displayed constant without an optimal-coupling premise. An extended version covers arbitrary probability initializations. No Hessian Lipschitz condition or fourth derivative is used.

The SGLD main theorem with exact component RMS smoothness

Mathematical statement

Let f=∑i=1nfif=\sum_{i=1}^n f_if=∑i=1n​fi​, each component have a Lipschitz gradient, and the full potential be nmˉn\bar mnmˉ-strongly convex. With its unique minimizer θ∗\theta_*θ∗​, the actual update is

X+=X−hn(∇fI(X)−∇fI(θ∗))+2h Z,X^+=X-hn(\nabla f_I(X)-\nabla f_I(\theta_*))+\sqrt{2h}\,Z,X+=X−hn(∇fI​(X)−∇fI​(θ∗​))+2h​Z,

where the uniform index and standard Gaussian are independent of each other and the initialization. The component seminorms define exactly Mrms2=n−1∑i∥∇fi∥Lip2M_{\rm rms}^2=n^{-1}\sum_i\|\nabla f_i\|_{\rm Lip}^2Mrms2​=n−1∑i​∥∇fi​∥Lip2​, and Mˉav=Mav(f)/n\bar M_{\rm av}=M_{\rm av}(f)/nMˉav​=Mav​(f)/n, as in the source. For 0≤h≤mˉ/(3nMrms2)0\le h\le\bar m/(3nM_{\rm rms}^2)0≤h≤mˉ/(3nMrms2​),

W22(νk,π)≤(1+nmˉh)−kW22(ν0,π)+(Mˉav+2mˉ+2Mrms2mˉ)hpmˉ.W_2^2(\nu_k,\pi)\le(1+n\bar m h)^{-k}W_2^2(\nu_0,\pi) +\left(\bar M_{\rm av}+2\bar m+ \frac{2M_{\rm rms}^2}{\bar m}\right)\frac{hp}{\bar m}.W22​(νk​,π)≤(1+nmˉh)−kW22​(ν0​,π)+(Mˉav​+2mˉ+mˉ2Mrms2​​)mˉhp​.

The estimator is proved unbiased, its variance is computed exactly, and disintegration constructs the required coupling. No convexity assumption on the individual components or additional derivative order is used. The extended version covers every probability initialization.
