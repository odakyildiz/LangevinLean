ANKI DECK VERIFICATION

163 notes, 163 forward review cards, ten numbered study subdecks.
37 beginner Lean cards and 10 proof-roadmap cards precede the detailed proof.

The APKG was exported using the installed Anki application's native backend,
then imported into an isolated temporary Anki collection. All 163 cards were
present, new and unsuspended. Reimporting the same package added no duplicate
cards. Every imported card rendered a populated question and answer template.
No learning progress or deck presets are included.

Mathematical and source review checked the authored cards against the current
Lean files and the matching explanatory PDF. Exact snippets are extracted
from the referenced source ranges and include original line numbers.
Review corrections covered inequality signs, differentiability assumptions,
the auxiliary-versus-actual step distinction, and precise tactic behavior.

All 326 card faces were rendered with Anki's bundled MathJax at desktop and
mobile widths (652 layout checks). No MathJax errors, unresolved template
fields, or remaining horizontal clipping/overflow were found after repair.
Representative cards from every proof stage, beginner fronts/backs, and
night-mode cards were visually inspected for legibility and layout.

The manifest records the final package hash, counts and source-file hashes.
The Lean proof files and explanatory PDF were not modified by this task.
