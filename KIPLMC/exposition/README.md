# KIPLMC source correspondence

This development concerns the user-supplied `papers/KIPLMC/main.tex` and its six section files, by Paul Felix Valsecchi Oliva and O. Deniz Akyildiz. The published paper is [Kinetic interacting particle Langevin Monte Carlo](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11222-026-10980-z), *Statistics and Computing* 36, article 221 (2026). The related arXiv record is [2407.05790](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05790). The exact public-version identity of the supplied multifile TeX is not encoded, so the supplied source itself is the correspondence authority.

`reference_audit.json` records source hashes, all 2 algorithms, 2 assumptions, 6 propositions, 2 theorems, 4 remarks and 3 lemmas, and all 74 source labels. Statement text is preserved verbatim with source file and line range. The audit includes the auxiliary geometry and algorithm-equivalence results, the explicit constants in the OBABO argument, complexity comparisons, and the distinct status of numerical experiments.

The long mathematical explanation in `docs/kinetic_content.py` derives the particle rescaling, lifted curvature and smoothness, powered likelihood marginal, sharp concentration radius, algorithm recursions, and the three-way error decomposition. KIPLMC1 must reuse the Dalalyan–Riou-Durand frozen-force exponential integrator, with its exact correlated Gaussian noise. KIPLMC2 composes O(η/2), B(η/2), A(η), B(η/2), O(η/2). It is different from the Hessian-corrected algorithm called KLMC2 in the first paper.

`module_notes.json` gives mathematical statements and detailed proofs corresponding to the Lean arguments. Formal coverage is limited to the checked declarations. The development proves Brownian KIPLD convergence and kinetic phase Gibbs stationarity, as well as both Gaussian-chain parameter RMS theorems. It proves the OBABO contraction internally and compares the numerical step with an exact Gibbs-preserving O–Hamiltonian–O step. Accumulating the local error gives a stronger direct sampling estimate, which implies the supplied particle-dependent coarse rate with an explicit constant. No external contraction, bias or discrete invariant-law hypothesis remains in the numerical theorem.

The supplied version of Theorem 2 includes both a transient ηN√(dθ+Ndx) term and a persistent η√(N(dθ+Ndx)) term, and gives small-error step count Õ(dθ√dx/ε³). It must not be replaced silently with an older source's rate. Independent Gaussian velocity initialization is required for reuse of the kinetic theorem, even though the supplied Theorem 1 lists only the marginal Gaussian laws. The logistic example explicitly does not satisfy global joint strong convexity without further regularization.

The continuous probability model uses the constructed `IsPreBrownianReal` coordinate laws and a selected solution continuous in L² on each finite horizon. Its kinetic integral equation, stationary marginals, source independence and fixed-time probability laws are checked directly. A separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is not asserted; this distinction does not add a hypothesis to the proved law or numerical convergence bounds.

The original `papers/` tree remains a reference input on `main`. Exclude it from every `gh-pages` publication; the public pages can cite the article without publishing the supplied source files.
