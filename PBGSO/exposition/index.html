Theorem 2 — Contraction under joint twisted monotonicity

Paper assumptions. A1 and A3, deterministic initial optimiser states, and initial sampler laws in P2\mathcal P_2P2​. For the stationary-target and uniqueness conclusions, also A2.

Added condition — constant diffusion branch. For every optimiser pair and every joint probability law with finite quadratic moment, the signed sampler work ⟨Δz,DzΔb⟩\langle\Delta z,D_z\Delta b\rangle⟨Δz,Dz​Δb⟩ is absolutely integrable (FiniteCouplingWork). This is the ordinary finite signed-integral reading of universal A3, imposed separately from the conditional inequality. It excludes some superlinear dissipative models admitted by an extended-negative-work reading.
Explicit measurability — constant diffusion branch. The projected observation and drift are jointly Borel, the optimiser response is jointly Borel in time, state and gradient, and each frozen drift is Borel.
Explicit regularity — linear-growth branch. Each frozen drift and diffusion column is Borel. Along the canonical strong solutions, drift and diffusion are jointly strongly measurable, and the drift is progressively strongly measurable. The parameter projection is continuous, as Euclidean coordinate projections are. Moments, stochastic-integral matching and the energy inequality are proved from these primitives and A1(ii).
Solution and target interpretation. Solutions satisfy their original local SDE and optimiser integral equation. Observation integrals are defined for the comparison laws. A2 has the weak-SDE meaning in Theorem 1.
