Exact minimum eigenvalue and positive definiteness of the source matrix

The explicit smaller-root rate is identified with the actual minimum of the two eigenvalues of the symmetric source matrix using its trace and determinant. The three strict source conditions then prove matrix positive definiteness, in addition to the actual law-level monotonicity certificate.

Assumptions and formal scope. The matrix is [[a,-c/2],[-c/2,b]]. Its eigenvalue identity holds for every real a,b,c, including repeated eigenvalues. Positive definiteness assumes a>0, b>0 and c²<4ab; those quantities are instantiated by the source metric and primitive separated constants in SeparatedSchur.
