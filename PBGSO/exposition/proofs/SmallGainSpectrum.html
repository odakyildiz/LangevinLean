The exact two-dimensional small-gain rate

Separate optimiser and sampler dissipation produces a two-dimensional quadratic form in the square roots of the optimiser and sampler coupling energies. Its least eigenvalue is the contraction rate. The result identifies that eigenvalue exactly, including the cross-dependence of both components and the diffusion correction.

Assumptions and formal scope. The matrix is (a−c/2−c/2b)\begin{pmatrix}a&-c/2\\-c/2&b\end{pmatrix}(a−c/2​−c/2b​). Its eigenvalue identity holds for every real a,b,ca,b,ca,b,c, including repeated eigenvalues. Positive definiteness assumes a>0a>0a>0, b>0b>0b>0 and c2<4abc^2<4abc2<4ab; those quantities are instantiated by the source metric and primitive separated constants in SeparatedSchur.

Proof. A real symmetric matrix has two real eigenvalues. Its trace and determinant give their sum a+ba+ba+b and product ab−c2/4ab-c^2/4ab−c2/4; the quadratic formula therefore gives the stated smaller eigenvalue. The assumptions say a,b>0a,b>0a,b>0 and c2<4abc^2<4abc2<4ab. Thus (a−b)2+c2<(a+b)2(a-b)^2+c^2<(a+b)^2(a−b)2+c2<(a+b)2, and the positivity of a+ba+ba+b shows ρ>0\rho>0ρ>0. Both eigenvalues are at least ρ\rhoρ, proving positive definiteness.

For an admissible coupling write u=∣s−s′∣ou=|s-s'|_ou=∣s−s′∣o​ and v=(∫∣z−z′∣s2dΛ)1/2v=(\int|z-z'|_s^2d\Lambda)^{1/2}v=(∫∣z−z′∣s2​dΛ)1/2. The separate coefficient bounds, the coupling marginal identities and Cauchy–Schwarz give the total work bound −au2−bv2+cuv-au^2-bv^2+cuv−au2−bv2+cuv. The matrix spectral bound gives au2+bv2−cuv≥ρ(u2+v2)au^2+bv^2-cuv\ge\rho(u^2+v^2)au2+bv2−cuv≥ρ(u2+v2). Negating proves exactly the joint inequality. Observation integrability follows from its Lipschitz bound and finite second moment; sampler signed-work integrability remains among the admissible coupling premises. □\square□

How the Lean proof follows this argument

smallGainMatrix_min_eigenvalue uses the spectral theorem trace and determinant identities, then splits on the order of the two indexed eigenvalues. It identifies the nonnegative square root with their nonnegative difference, so the arbitrary eigenvalue indexing disappears. separated_source_criterion first rewrites the smallest eigenvalue using separatedMinimumEigenvalue_eq_rate, obtains positivity from separatedSpectralRate_pos, and uses posDef_iff_eigenvalues_pos for positive definiteness. Its last component is separated_joint_monotonicity_spectral, the established coupling-work estimate from the primitive SeparatedAssumptions.
