HOL Gaussian rates on actual random-initial particle/noise spaces

A measure-preserving pullback transports the entire four-kernel innovation array and coupled Brownian increments. Isometric L2L^2L2 pullback preserves the proved noise budget and finite-horizon HOL bound, with the actual raw-particle force lifts on the same space.

Assumptions and formal scope. The generic theorem assumes a measure-preserving noise projection; ParticleNoiseSpace constructs it on actual independent initial/noise particle seeds. Positive step at most one, α>0\alpha>0α>0, γ≥0\gamma\ge0γ≥0, primitive forces, compatible original integral segments and finite-horizon moments/modulus remain explicit. No Gaussian noise-error premise is supplied, and the full temporal/spatial joint law is derived.
