Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Derived HOL Gaussian/Brownian coupling error

The actual canonical Gaussian responses are compared with their coupled Brownian Euler increment. Kernel coefficient bounds and the Brownian isometry derive an h sqrt(h) local noise discrepancy with an explicit constant and exact sqrt(d) factor.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume alpha>0, gamma≥0 and a nonnegative segment width at most one. These results concern the constructed kernel Gaussian law, not an assumed unknown innovation error. The expanded printed covariance is identified separately in HOLCovariance; the full nonlinear reference SDE interpretation remains separate.
