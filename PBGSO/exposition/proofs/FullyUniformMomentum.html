Theorem New A: constructed time-uniform M-MFPO parameter bound

Source A3 constructs the contracting raw particle metric. The actual global particle process, Gaussian coupling, uniform moments and affine local errors give a positive fixed step threshold and all-iteration parameter bound.

Assumptions and formal scope. Finite-dimensional physical coordinates, independent globally jointly Lipschitz forces, nonnegative friction, positive N,ρN,\rhoN,ρ, P2\mathcal P_2P2​ initial/target laws, an actual frozen invariant trajectory, zero target mean observation and A5 are explicit. No metric, process, reference, moment, local defect or final recurrence is supplied. Constants may depend on fixed NNN.
