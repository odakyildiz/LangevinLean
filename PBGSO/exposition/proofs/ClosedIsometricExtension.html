Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Computed isometric extension to a literal closed range

A map on actual finite represented inputs is well defined because equal inputs have zero output distance. Its actual uniformly continuous extension to the literal closure is constructed and proved isometric, preserving represented values and approximation limits.

Assumptions and formal scope. The primitive finite-data distance identity and completeness of the output are explicit. No norm, continuity or isometry property is imposed on the completed map as an extra conclusion premise.
