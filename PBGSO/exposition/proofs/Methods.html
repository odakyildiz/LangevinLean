Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

M-MFPO's concrete splitting map and finite-step robustness

Force kicks, position drifts and thermostats are bounded separately, then composed for the actual OBABO map. The coupled force may be nonconservative, and the optimiser receives no thermal noise. A proved recurrence yields a finite-step perturbation bound.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a global Lipschitz bound for the full coupled force, a nonnegative Lipschitz constant, thermostat factor of absolute value at most one, and the displayed innovation perturbation bounds. These are pathwise robustness bounds. Their amplification factor can exceed one; they are not a Gibbs mixing or uniform-in-time convergence theorem.
