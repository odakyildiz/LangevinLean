Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Original predictable model equations commute with common-source transport

Actual merely time-L2L^2L2 adapted model coefficients and their computed Brownian integral pull back under genuine measure-preserving maps and enlarge to valid common Driver filtrations. Every original model equation, adaptation property and actual marginal output law is preserved.

Assumptions and formal scope. No coefficient time continuity, stochastic moment identity, final integral identity or output-law compatibility is a premise. The true Driver and original time-integrable model data remain explicit.
