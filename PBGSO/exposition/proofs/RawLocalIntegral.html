PBGSO overview / PBGSO/RawLocalIntegral.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed local Brownian integrals from raw local energy

First-hit restricted original columns give actual computed continuous martingales. Their stopped consistency is proved; horizon exhaustion glues a local noise process with simultaneous stopped identities.

Assumptions and formal scope. Raw columns are jointly strongly measurable and AE adapted. A nonnegative progressive pathwise-L1L^1L1 scalar controls their square sum; no global expected coefficient moment, local noise, stopped equation or energy conclusion is supplied.
