Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual integral energy family with weak original drift

The actual original drift work plus diffusion energy is integrable in time, and its primitive equals the expected quadratic energy difference at every subhorizon.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only original raw computed equations, continuous paths, pathwise L1 drift with random L² variation budget and adapted product-square diffusion enter. No time-L1(L2) drift, supplied generator/energy family or stochastic isometry is assumed.
