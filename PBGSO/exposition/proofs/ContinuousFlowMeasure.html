Lebesgue preservation by the continuous frictionless flow

Determinant one gives a measure-preservation statement once each time slice is globally invertible. Autonomous ODE uniqueness provides the inverse Φ−t\Phi_{-t}Φ−t​. The finite-dimensional change-of-variables theorem then proves preservation of Lebesgue measure on the complete phase space.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only the primitive C1C^1C1 arbitrary force, actual all-real ODE and identity initial map are assumed for the phase theorem. No bijectivity, inverse, flow regularity, global original-force Lipschitz bound or measure/Jacobian-preservation conclusion is supplied. The generic divergence-free theorem has explicit zero trace.

01Uniqueness, inverse time and change of variables §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For a C1C^1C1 force and actual global frictionless phase trajectories as in the preceding result, every map x↦Φ(t,x)x\mapsto\Phi(t,x)x↦Φ(t,x) preserves Lebesgue measure, for each real ttt.

Proof. For fixed s,xs,xs,x, the two curves u↦Φ(s+u,x)u\mapsto\Phi(s+u,x)u↦Φ(s+u,x) and u↦Φ(u,Φ(s,x))u\mapsto\Phi(u,\Phi(s,x))u↦Φ(u,Φ(s,x)) solve the same autonomous ODE and agree at zero. Uniqueness on a compact interval containing zero and the desired time gives Φ(s+t,x)=Φ(t,Φ(s,x))\Phi(s+t,x)=\Phi(t,\Phi(s,x))Φ(s+t,x)=Φ(t,Φ(s,x)). Taking s=−ts=-ts=−t shows that Φ(−t,⋅)\Phi(-t,\cdot)Φ(−t,⋅) is a two-sided inverse. Therefore each time slice is bijective. It is C1C^1C1 by the preceding smooth-dependence result, and its determinant equals one everywhere.

The change-of-variables identity for an injective differentiable map says that the pushforward of the measure ∣det⁡DΦt(x)∣dx|\det D\Phi_t(x)|dx∣detDΦt​(x)∣dx is Lebesgue measure restricted to its image. Here the density is identically one and the image is the whole space by surjectivity. Thus (Φt)#dx=dx(\Phi_t)_\#dx=dx(Φt​)#​dx=dx. Measurability follows from continuity, completing the measure-preservation assertion. No common potential for the force is used. □\square□

How the Lean proof follows this argument

flow_add applies trajectories_unique_Icc to the shifted and restarted curves. flow_bijective obtains both injectivity and surjectivity from negative-time composition. phase_flow_measurePreserving_source reduces to divergenceFree_flow_measurePreserving, which derives joint regularity, bijectivity and determinant one before invoking map_withDensity_abs_det_fderiv_eq_addHaar. Its simplification uses determinant one and surjectivity to turn the weighted image measure into unrestricted volume.

divergenceFree_flow_measurePreserving

Mathematical role and assumptions

A globally defined divergence-free C1C^{1}C1 autonomous ODE preserves Lebesgue measure at every real time. Joint C1C^{1}C1 regularity, bijectivity, and determinant one are all derived from the primitive field and ODE.

Read this statement with the module assumption note above. The complete proof appears alongside it; the full source preserves every shared variable and typeclass.
