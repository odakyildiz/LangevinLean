PBGSO overview / PBGSO/ContinuousFlowMeasure.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Literal continuous source-flow Lebesgue preservation

The source C1 field and actual global trajectories imply the flow group identity and negative-time inverse by ODE uniqueness. Derived differentiability and determinant one then give actual MeasurePreserving via Jacobian change of variables.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only the primitive C1 arbitrary force, actual all-real ODE and identity initial map are assumed for the phase theorem. No bijectivity, inverse, flow regularity, global original-force Lipschitz bound or measure/Jacobian-preservation conclusion is supplied. The generic divergence-free theorem has explicit zero trace.
