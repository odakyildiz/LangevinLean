Natural path filtrations fit the proved null-augmented past

An ambient-measurable version of an AE past-measurable variable is actually measurable in the same past augmented by ambient null sets. Entire natural path filtrations therefore pull into that valid augmented Brownian filtration.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual ambient measurability and AE past adaptation are explicit. No complete filtration, causal equality on exceptional paths or future-independent null augmentation is assumed; the latter was proved separately.
