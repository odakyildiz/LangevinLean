Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Finite signed work derives the positive-work Fubini condition

Universal finite signed coupling work gives compact-uniform quadratic positive sampler work. Actual product-L2L^2L2 states and the original optimizer integral equations yield integrable positive joint work.

Assumptions and formal scope. FiniteCouplingWork is explicit and distinct from conditional A3. Product-L2L^2L2 state moments are inputs to this intermediate lemma and derived independently by coercive stopping in the natural pipeline. No response Lipschitz, drift moment, energy identity or final contraction is assumed.

Mathematical role and assumptions

Product-L2L^{2}L2 state moments, obtained independently by stopped coercivity, justify actual positive-work Fubini under the explicit finite-integral A3 convention. The sampler drift itself may have superlinear norm.
