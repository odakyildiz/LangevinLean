Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

A finite parameter bound from the original optimizer equation

The optimizer integral equation with merely L1L^1L1 drift yields actual path continuity. Compactness gives a nonnegative finite bound for every continuous parameter projection along the original trajectory.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only the original finite-horizon optimizer integral equation, its genuine integrable drift and continuity of the parameter projection are supplied. No optimizer pointwise derivative, trajectory-continuity or parameter-bound conclusion is assumed.
