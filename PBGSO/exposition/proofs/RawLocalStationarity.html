Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Source stationarity for the actual local strong equation

Frozen sampler invariance, zero mean gradient and the optimizer fixed point construct the complete constant-optimizer original local equation. Same-driver source uniqueness proves the literal stationary Dirac optimizer tensor sampler law.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original sampler has continuous adapted paths, pathwise Bochner-L1L^1L1 drift and an actual locally constructed diffusion integral; no time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2) drift or growth is required. Source same-driver uniqueness, actual frozen invariant trajectory, observation domain and matching initial variables remain explicit.
