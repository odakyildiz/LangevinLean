Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

AE past-adaptation of actual stopped states

Finite dyadic stopping selections and a genuine measurable limit prove AE current-past measurability of the original state evaluated at a bounded stopping time.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original state is AE adapted with almost-sure continuous paths, and genuine stopping events are explicit. No complete filtration or all-path continuity is assumed.

Mathematical role and assumptions

Adapted original states with almost-sure continuous paths remain AE-adapted after genuine bounded stopping, proved by actual finite random selections in the current past algebra.
