Bounded linear maps of original local Brownian noise

The actual mapped diffusion and scaled common local gauge yield stopped-noise identities. Genuine localization and gauge invariance prove one probability-one event of original local-noise commutation.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only the original local diffusion data and the given bounded linear map are required. No global expected coefficient moment, final noise matching or energy identity is supplied.
