Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

A checked distinction between finite and extended-negative signed work

An actual probability law has finite quadratic moment but nonintegrable fourth power. The scalar drift −x−x3-x-x^3−x−x3 is pointwise strongly dissipative, yet its signed coupling work against zero is not integrable for that P2\mathcal P_2P2​ law.

Assumptions and formal scope. No stochastic contraction counterexample is claimed. The result demonstrates that universal finite signed work is substantive; an extended-negative-work interpretation includes more superlinear systems.
