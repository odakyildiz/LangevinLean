Derived local error of the printed Gaussian update

The exact restarted process and numerical step use the same Brownian path. The numerical three-component Gaussian innovation is built from the prescribed kernels; its fourth component supplies the exact endpoint Brownian increment. The error norm here is the sum of the four coordinate norms, which bounds the ordinary product norm.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only primitive force regularity, α>0\alpha>0α>0, γ≥0\gamma\ge0γ≥0 and the actual Brownian source are supplied. No reference moment, drift modulus, local-error or numerical stability conclusion is a premise.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The three definitions show the exact local coefficients; holLocalGrowthConstant and holScalarNoiseConstant are the already derived printed-method constants used in them. higherOrderRestart_local_error constructs r, B, P and M. UniformIntegral.local_regularity proves the radius and increments, then hmod multiplies the increment estimate by the drift Lipschitz constant. hend replaces the endpoint input by the fourth actual innovation using higherOrderRestartInput_endpoint. It applies higherOrder_pullback_integral_local_error, unfolds the two constants, and closes by exact algebra. Thus no local-error or reference-modulus premise is supplied.
