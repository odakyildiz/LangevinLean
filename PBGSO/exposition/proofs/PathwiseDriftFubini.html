Fubini for the actual localized drift work

Raw joint measurability, pathwise integrability and a genuine square-integrable localization radius derive product work integrability and its actual expectation/time interchange.

Assumptions and formal scope. The raw paths and radius domination are explicit. No time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2) drift, product work-integrability or Fubini conclusion is supplied.
