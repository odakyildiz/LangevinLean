Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Source particle error from the actually computed continuous-column integral

Actual raw particle/copy model drift and diffusion identities are driven by the completed finite-column Brownian integral. Its martingale, weighted stochastic isometry, original energy balance, reference-only V/NV/NV/N fluctuation and source A3 yield the exact empirical RMS interaction floor. The actual parameter-to-target triangle is also proved.

Assumptions and formal scope. Data requires Bochner L1L^1L1-time L2L^2L2 drift and continuous adapted L2L^2L2 diffusion in this module. Only the reference cloud has the iid law, with uniform observation variance and deterministic reference optimiser explicit. No martingale, stochastic-isometry, weak-test-domain or final error premise remains; source minimal A1/localization is separate.
