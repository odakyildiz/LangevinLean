Primitive linear growth constructs the original model realization

Natural original raw strong equations and primitive joint linear growth derive the parameter radius, drift gauge, global coefficient moments, local-to-global noise identity and actual predictable model data.

Assumptions and formal scope. Joint raw coefficient measurability, progressivity, Borel coefficient maps and the original continuous adapted local equation are explicit. No global moment, drift-control gauge, matching noise or stronger process package is supplied.
