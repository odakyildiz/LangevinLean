Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed joint representatives of arbitrary L1L^1L1-time L2L^2L2 variables

Actual finite-valued time families have literal strongly measurable joint representatives. A proved L2L^2L2-to-L1L^1L1 bound makes their product-L1L^1L1 lifts Cauchy. Completion, product-AE subsequences and uniqueness in measure construct a genuine jointly strongly measurable product-integrable representative with exact AE-time L2L^2L2 sections.

Assumptions and formal scope. The time measure is finite, the spatial measure a probability, and the output a complete real Hilbert space. No measurable global L2L^2L2 evaluation map or joint representative is assumed. Every actual time-set Bochner integral has its proved section identity.
