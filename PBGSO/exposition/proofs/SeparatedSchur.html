Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Full determinant small-gain criterion at the explicit spectral formula

The smaller quadratic root gives the exact two-by-two quadratic-form lower bound, including degenerate and zero off-diagonal cases. The three source strict determinant conditions prove positive rate, and primitive separated conditions imply actual full law-level joint monotonicity at that rate.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume SeparatedAssumptions and the displayed two positive diagonal conditions and determinant inequality for positivity. The rate is the explicit formula (a+b−(a−b)2+c2)/2(a+b-\sqrt{(a-b)^2+c^2})/2(a+b−(a−b)2+c2​)/2, where the off-diagonal entries are −c/2-c/2−c/2. The algebraic lower bound and positive rate are proved; SmallGainSpectrum additionally identifies this formula with the actual matrix minimum eigenvalue.
