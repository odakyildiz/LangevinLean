L2L^2L2 drift primitives from bounded path variation

Joint raw measurability and a literal pathwise total-variation cap construct actual spatial L2L^2L2 drift primitives and exact raw realizations.

Assumptions and formal scope. The drift is only pathwise Bochner L1L^1L1 with bounded variation budget. No spatial L2L^2L2 drift value or time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2) drift is assumed.
