Closed arithmetic expressions for every new local-error coefficient

Direct algebra certifies the complete M-MFPO and HOL local constants, including all nested coefficient, Gaussian and drift-Lipschitz terms. The identities connect the human-readable formulas to the existing local-error estimates.

Assumptions and formal scope. The HOL simplifications require positive α\alphaα and the displayed nonnegative friction/Lipschitz constants; MMM arithmetic uses its nonnegative primitive constants. These are proved equalities of definitions, without a supplied estimate or new analytic assumption.
