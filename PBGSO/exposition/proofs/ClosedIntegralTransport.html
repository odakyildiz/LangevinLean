Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Computed closed-domain integrals commute with source transport

Actual finite adapted coefficients pull back under measure-preserving maps and enlarge to valid larger Driver filtrations, preserving both literal time-L2L^2L2 inputs and Brownian sums. Closure and actual norm-limit uniqueness prove transport of the computed integrals and all observation-time cuts.

Assumptions and formal scope. The source map must be genuinely measure preserving and the enlarged filtration must itself form a valid Driver. No final integral identity, output-law equality or extra approximation premise is supplied.
