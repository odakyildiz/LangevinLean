Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual past-dependent Gaussian sums are martingales and isometries

On a genuine iid Gaussian path source, arbitrary measurable coefficients depend on the complete strict past. Fresh-past independence, conditional centering and Gaussian second moments derive the actual predictable-sum martingale, weighted isometry and L2L^2L2 distance identity.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume finite noise coordinates, square-integrable coefficient columns and their measurable finite-past dependence. The symmetric continuous metric weight is arbitrary. This is a finite unit-grid construction; continuous stochastic integration is not claimed here.
