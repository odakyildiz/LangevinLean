Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual local Brownian noise is independent of energy gauge

Double-stopped computed integrals agree for original raw diffusion fields equal on one all-time full-probability event. Actual gauge exhaustion derives simultaneous horizon equality of their local noise.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only actual locally square-integrable raw columns with one-event horizon equality are used; gauges may differ. No global expected diffusion square, drift growth or gauge/noise identity is supplied.
