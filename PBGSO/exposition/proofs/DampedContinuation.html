Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual exponential continuation after stopping

The literal stopped original state is continued by deterministic rate-rho exponential damping. Its exact raw drift, primitive and dissipation identities are proved.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original pathwise equation and genuine bounded stop are explicit. No stochastic product rule or after-stop energy inequality is supplied.
