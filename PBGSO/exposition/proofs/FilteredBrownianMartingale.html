Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual stopped predictable Brownian sums are continuous-time martingales

A single stopped Brownian increment multiplied by a genuine left-endpoint adapted random column has the exact conditional centering identities before and after that endpoint. Finite stopped sums therefore form actual continuous-time martingales; the limit wrapper uses actual L² convergence.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume primitive actual Driver laws/filtration/independence, adapted square-integrable coefficients and the displayed deterministic grid. Neither noise martingale nor limiting martingale identities are supplied. FilteredIntegralMartingale derives the needed convergence for actual completed continuous integrands.
