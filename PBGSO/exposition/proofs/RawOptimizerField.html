Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Primitive Borel maps derive the original population field measurability

A jointly continuous sampler representative and actual population observation integrals construct a measurable horizon extension of the optimizer field, preserving its exact original values and integrability.

Assumptions and formal scope. Original raw model data and primitive Borel observation/response composites are explicit. No coefficient growth, diffusion constancy, global drift moment or supplied population-field measurability is assumed.

Mathematical role and assumptions

The actual original optimizer field has a globally strongly measurable extension retaining every horizon value and its original ordinary integral.
