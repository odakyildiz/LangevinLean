Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Source joint law monotonicity implies constructed additive contraction

The actual common-space L² sampler pair gives a genuine joint pushforward law. Its marginals, second moments, Bochner observation and weighted work identities derive lifted drift dissipation from source A3. Picard then constructs both integral solutions with the exact Euclidean L² metric rate.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume primitive globally Lipschitz independent observation, response and sampler maps compatible with Model, autonomous optimiser response, constant diffusion columns, a common continuous L² sampler input starting at zero, and source twisted joint monotonicity. No lifted drift or energy inequality is assumed. The common input is not automatically identified with Model's diffusion: a samplerBrownianInput K implementation must separately match the columns sigma_i=sqrt(2) K e_i; AdditiveDiffusion constructs a normalized K that proves this identity for any finite constant column family. Strong-SDE adaptation and realization-independent output laws remain separate.
