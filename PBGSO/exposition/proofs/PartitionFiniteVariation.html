Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Pathwise drift-work and quadratic-variation limits

Original Bochner-L1L^1L1 drift primitives and continuous state/noise paths imply exact drift-work quadrature convergence and vanishing finite-variation quadratic and cross terms.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original raw path continuity and pathwise L1L^1L1 drift are explicit. No time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2) drift or variation-limit conclusion is assumed. Probabilistic expectation/localization limits are separate.
