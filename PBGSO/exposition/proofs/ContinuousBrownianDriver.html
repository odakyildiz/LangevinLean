Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Continuous modifications preserving the full Brownian Driver

Coordinate past-series modifications retain the exact original filtration. Fixed-time identities derive all spatial increment independence and full future-past independence, yielding a valid continuous Driver.

Assumptions and formal scope. The input is an actual finite-coordinate Driver. No path regularity, independence or output-integral identity for the modified process is assumed.
