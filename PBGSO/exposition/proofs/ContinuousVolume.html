Continuous Jacobian preservation from an actual C1C^1C1 joint flow

The determinant derivative is derived from its multilinear form. Differentiating the actual integral ODE under a compact-set domination bound derives its spatial variational equation. The frictionless phase field has zero trace, so the fixed-time derivative has determinant exactly one.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume finite Euclidean coordinates, a C1C^1C1 position force, an actual global joint C1C^1C1 flow Φ\PhiΦ, its ODE at every time/state and Φ(0,x)=x\Phi(0,x)=xΦ(0,x)=x. No variational or determinant equation is assumed. FlowSourceRegularity now derives joint C1C^1C1 dependence from primitive C1C^1C1 field regularity and globally existing individual trajectories. Older C2C^2C2-flow lemmas remain supporting results.
