Exact Wasserstein distance of deterministic-optimiser product laws

The actual law is identified with the literal Dirac optimiser tensor sampler measure in the Euclidean WithLp 2 product. Lifted and projected genuine couplings and their infima prove its quadratic transport distance equals ∥ϑ−ϑ~∥2+W22(ν,ν~)\sqrt{\|\vartheta-\widetilde\vartheta\|^2+W_2^2(\nu,\widetilde\nu)}∥ϑ−ϑ∥2+W22​(ν,ν)​. Full weak product-law contraction follows from the derived generator law theorem.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume complete real Hilbert coordinates with Borel and second-countable structure and probability sampler laws with finite second moments. The dynamic theorem retains the actual coupled weak generator, quadratic test domain and same-marginal synchronous realization for every initial coupling. No product-distance identity or transport conclusion is assumed.
