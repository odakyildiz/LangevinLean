Original constant-noise contraction from one-sided work

Actual common constant diffusion cancels in the original local equations. Pathwise L1L^1L1 drift and positive-work Fubini then let literal source A3 derive expected quadratic and RMS decay.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original raw strong equations and explicit parameter-cross positive-work integrability are required. No drift time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2), L2L^2L2 total-variation budget, noise cancellation or energy conclusion is assumed. Minimal A1(i) alone is not asserted to imply the extra work condition.
