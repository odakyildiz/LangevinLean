The original optimizer equation has literal zero Brownian columns

An actual deterministic optimizer integral equation is encoded as local raw dynamics. Bounded-linear-map naturality proves its constructed Brownian noise vanishes.

Assumptions and formal scope. Ordinary pathwise/integral ODE data and the actual Driver are explicit. No zero-noise output identity or stronger stochastic-process package is supplied.
