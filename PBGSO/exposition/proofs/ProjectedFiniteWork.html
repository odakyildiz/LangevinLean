The projected criterion exposes the universal finite U5 domain

Constant diffusion cancels the difference columns, so the named ordinary finite signed-integral domain of U5 gives FiniteCouplingWork. The full canonical spectral criterion then returns positive rate, actual joint monotonicity and that domain together.

Assumptions and formal scope. The universal finite sampler-drift domain is explicit and independent of the conditional U4/U5 inequalities. Primitive matrices, coordinate projections and all original criterion hypotheses remain; no stochastic energy or law-contraction conclusion is assumed.
