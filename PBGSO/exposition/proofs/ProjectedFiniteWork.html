The finite signed-work domain of the projected criterion

A dissipativity inequality stated only when its integral exists does not establish integrability for every finite-second-moment coupling. The projected criterion therefore records its finite signed drift-work domain separately. For constant diffusion, that domain is exactly the sampler coupling-work domain.

Assumptions and formal scope. The universal finite sampler-drift domain is explicit and independent of the conditional U4/U5 inequalities. Primitive matrices, coordinate projections and all original criterion hypotheses remain; no stochastic energy or law-contraction conclusion is assumed.

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Assume all hypotheses of the canonical projected criterion: literal block projections, positive definite optimiser and sampler metric and auxiliary matrices, β≥0\beta\ge0β≥0, βr−Ao−As>0\beta r-A_o-A_s>0βr−Ao​−As​>0, projected strong monotonicity, auxiliary dissipation, constant diffusion and integrability of its projected expressions. Assume additionally that for every pair of optimiser states and every probability coupling Λ\LambdaΛ with ∫(1+∥z∥2+∥z′∥2)dΛ<∞\int(1+\|z\|^2+\|z'\|^2)d\Lambda<\infty∫(1+∥z∥2+∥z′∥2)dΛ<∞, the signed sampler drift work is integrable. Then the same positive generalized-eigenvalue rate gives joint dissipativity, and the complete sampler coupling work is integrable for all these couplings.

Proof. The preceding projected criterion supplies the positive rate and its joint inequality. In the sampler coupling work, every column difference is σi−σi=0\sigma_i-\sigma_i=0σi​−σi​=0. Hence the finite sum of their quadratic squares is zero, and the sampler work equals the signed drift work pointwise. The added domain hypothesis makes precisely that remaining expression integrable. Combine this integrability assertion with the two conclusions of the projected criterion. No inequality about a totalised integral is used to infer finiteness. □\square□

How the Lean proof follows this argument

FiniteSamplerDriftDomain quantifies over all probability couplings with the displayed quadratic weight integrable. finiteCouplingWork_of_U5_domain rewrites the work by samplerCouplingWork_eq_drift_of_constant and applies hfinite. canonical_projected_source_criterion_finite obtains the rate and monotonicity from canonical_projected_source_criterion, then packages this separate finite-work result as its third component.
