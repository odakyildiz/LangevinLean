Joint C1C^1C1 flow dependence derived from primitive globally Lipschitz fields

The actual path-space Picard map is C1C^1C1, and its contraction gives the invertibility needed by the Banach implicit function theorem. Uniqueness derives the semigroup identity; scaled time and iteration extend short-time dependence to all times. The phase-flow determinant is then one without supplying any regularity of the flow.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a C1C^1C1 globally Lipschitz primitive field, a complete proper real normed state space, actual all-time ODE trajectories and the identity initial map. Continuity in initial data, a variational equation and joint flow differentiability are conclusions. Removing global Lipschitz regularity is discharged for finite-dimensional source fields by FlowSourceRegularity through actual compact-orbit localization.
