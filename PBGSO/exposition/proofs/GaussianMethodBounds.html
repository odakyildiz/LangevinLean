Finite-horizon bounds with actual Gaussian-coordinate innovations

The final wrappers instantiate the two finite-horizon square-root-step estimates with actual Gaussian/Brownian innovations. M-MFPO has zero optimiser innovations and d\sqrt dd​ noise radius; HOL uses the constructed correlated response triple and its proved explicit coupling constant. Reference premises are restricted to k<nk<nk<n.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the displayed separate Lipschitz maps on the normed/L2L^2L2 coordinates, actual compatible integral-reference segments and finite-horizon force/auxiliary and modulus bounds. To identify these statements with the original raw nonlinear particle implementations, their force maps must be proved equal to the pointwise L2L^2L2 lifts of the raw empirical and sampler forces. ParticleMethodLp proves that identification for the actual encoded finite-particle implementations and supplies their specialized final Gaussian bounds.
