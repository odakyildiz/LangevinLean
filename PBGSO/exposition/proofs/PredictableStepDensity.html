Density derived for arbitrary time-L2L^2L2 adapted columns

Actual past averages converge in time-L2L^2L2 and have continuous adapted pointwise representatives. Their deterministic Brownian-step approximations and the Fubini identification place every finite-horizon time cut of the original columns in the literal closed predictable-step domain.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume only actual time-L2L^2L2 columns and AE-time past adaptation. No time continuity, supplied approximation, integral isometry or martingale identity is a premise. This is integrand density, not SDE well-posedness.

Mathematical role and assumptions

Every time-L2L^{2}L2 column family adapted to the actual past belongs, after horizon restriction, to the literal closure of finite predictable Brownian steps. Past averaging, continuous adapted approximation and Fubini prove membership.
