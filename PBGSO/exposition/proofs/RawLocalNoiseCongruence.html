Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual common-diffusion local noise agrees simultaneously

Original globally square-integrable raw diffusion columns with product-AE equality give identical local noise at every deterministic time. Actual continuous paths and a dense sequence upgrade this to one event on the whole horizon.

Assumptions and formal scope. Both original raw diffusion product-square moments and their product-AE identity are explicit; no drift growth or state moment is imposed. No noise cancellation, gauge independence or simultaneous output identity is supplied.
