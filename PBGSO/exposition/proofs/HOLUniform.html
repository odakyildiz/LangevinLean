Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Time-uniform actual printed HOL strong discretisation error

Constructed Picard restarts contract, their Gaussian local defects have explicit affine growth, and the original integral equation yields uniform reference moments. Small-step absorption gives the all-iteration h\sqrt hh​ bound.

Assumptions and formal scope. The generic theorem displays primitive weighted drift dissipativity and metric coercivity, subsequently derived from source A3. Numerical contraction, local errors, uniform moments and error recurrences are not assumptions.
