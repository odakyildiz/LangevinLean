Natural drift-energy gauges for the original local equation

A common measurable null-set normalization adds the actual pathwise drift norm to the diffusion gauge and preserves the original local Brownian noise on one full-measure event.

Assumptions and formal scope. Original raw drift joint measurability, past progressivity and almost-sure pathwise L1L^1L1 are explicit. Drift domination, the new gauge, coefficient identities and all-time local-noise equality are derived; no global drift moment is required.
