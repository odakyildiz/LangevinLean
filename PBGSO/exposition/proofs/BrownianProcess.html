Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual Brownian-coordinate inputs for general mean-field dynamics

Given real pre-Brownian coordinate processes, a fixed continuous linear map produces an L2L^2L2-continuous sampler input. Gaussian increment moments yield its square-root norm bound, and Picard constructs the actual general integral solution with zero optimiser input.

Assumptions and formal scope. Coordinate pre-Brownian properties are hypotheses. A probability measure, complete state spaces, primitive LipschitzDynamics and a fixed diffusion map are required for existence. The code does not infer joint coordinate independence from marginal Brownian laws, prove adapted sample-path strong SDE existence, or cover variable diffusion.
