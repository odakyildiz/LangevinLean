Exact numerical norms of the standard Euclidean cloud encoding

The reindexing map has norm one and inverse norm N\sqrt NN​ in positive dimension, with explicit zero-dimensional cases. These identities give exact certified force constants 2ℓF2\ell_F2ℓF​ and 2 N\sqrt NN​ ell_x.

Assumptions and formal scope. Positive particle count and the standard coordinate encoding are explicit. Exact nonzero norms use Nonempty spatial index; the separate empty-index statements cover dimension zero. No norm comparison is assumed.
