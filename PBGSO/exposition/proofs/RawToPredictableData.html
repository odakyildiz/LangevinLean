Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Raw original equations supply literal predictable Model data

Actual spatial sections and product Fubini construct every L² state/coefficient curve and the original sampler integral equation, preserving the raw sampler law.

Assumptions and formal scope. This intermediate factory explicitly requires raw fixed-time/product moments, raw adaptedness, actual original computed equation, optimizer equation and observation integrability. RawLinearGrowthModel derives its moment and global-noise hypotheses from the original local linear-growth equation.
