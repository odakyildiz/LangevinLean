Primitive separate conditions imply actual joint law monotonicity

Actual coupling marginal identities and the Lipschitz observation bound control the Bochner mean gradient. The primitive same-law optimiser, response, frozen-sampler, parameter-drift and diffusion-column estimates then prove full twisted joint monotonicity with the explicit source diagonal rate.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume SeparatedAssumptions: optimiser and frozen-sampler metric dissipation, gradient-response and observation Lipschitz bounds, parameter drift bound and the full squared diffusion-column bound. The derived rate is min⁡(rO,rS)−κLσ2/2−κ(LΨLF+Lb)/2\min(r_O,r_S)-\kappa L_\sigma^2/2-\sqrt\kappa(L_\Psi L_F+L_b)/2min(rO​,rS​)−κLσ2​/2−κ​(LΨ​LF​+Lb​)/2; positivity is needed for contraction. This proves the source simpler diagonal branch, not its exact two-by-two minimum-eigenvalue identification.
