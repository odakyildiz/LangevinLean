Physical Hilbert state and diffusion for original independent copies

The flattened Hilbert reference cloud has actual physical diffusion columns, a local energy formed from the genuine coordinate gauges, its complete P2\mathcal P_2P2​ law, continuous paths and joint measurable representative.

Assumptions and formal scope. The one-copy original raw solution and finite independent complete seeds are explicit. Physical coefficient identities and iid laws are derived. This module does not infer a cloud equation from matching coordinate laws; RawReferenceCloudEquation separately proves the complete physical equation using actual local-noise transport. alone.
