Actual Gaussian OBABO local error with square-root temporal regularity

The literal thermostat, force kick and drift coefficients give local consistency and explicit affine initial-radius coefficients.

Assumptions and formal scope. Primitive force Lipschitz regularity, actual component integral equations, the displayed reference increments and physical half-noise identity are explicit. Later modules derive these radii, increments and noise identities.
