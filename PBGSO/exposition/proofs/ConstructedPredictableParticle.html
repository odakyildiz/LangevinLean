Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Source particle interaction bound from computed time-L2L^2L2 Itô noise

Original raw full particle/copy drift and diffusion are driven by the actually computed predictable Brownian integral. Its martingale/isometry, actual joint-law energy, reference-only iid V/NV/NV/N forcing and source A3 derive the exact source empirical RMS interaction floor. An actual-law parameter triangle is proved internally.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only Bochner L1L^1L1-time L2L^2L2 drift and adapted time-L2L^2L2 packed diffusion are required; no coefficient continuity, residual martingale, stochastic isometry, energy bound or weak-test-domain premise is supplied. The reference iid marginal, deterministic reference optimiser, synchronous initial condition and uniform observation variance are explicit. Minimal source A1 realization/localization remains separate.
