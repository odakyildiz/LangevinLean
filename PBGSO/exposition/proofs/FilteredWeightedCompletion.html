Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Full-vector completed integrals have derived weighted increment isometry

A genuine bounded symmetric weight acts columnwise on the finite time-L2L^2L2 Hilbert space. Finite moment formulas pass to completed integrals; actual domain subtraction and literal interval cuts derive stochastic integral subtraction and weighted increment moments.

Assumptions and formal scope. The generic theorem displays represented-domain membership and exact deterministic interval integrand identities. No stochastic moment/isometry conclusion is assumed. FilteredContinuousIntegrands instantiates both membership and those identities for actual continuous adapted L2L^2L2 diffusion columns.
