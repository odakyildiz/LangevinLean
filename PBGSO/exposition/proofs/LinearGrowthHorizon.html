Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

One moment constant for every time in the original horizon

The computed endpoint moment theorem applies at every subhorizon with the same original integral horizon and a single localization-independent constant.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual original computed integral equations and primitive quadratic coefficient growth are explicit. No change-of-noise-horizon identity or uniform moment conclusion is supplied.
