Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed stochastic integrals commute with actual source pullback

Actual coefficient composition under a measure-preserving map transports the adapted domain, finite Brownian sums, time-L2L^2L2 column embedding and L2L^2L2 limits. The actual constructed integral is proved equal to the pulled-back original integral under the fully transported Driver and filtration.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a genuine measure-preserving source map and actual Driver. No final integral identity, limiting stochastic isometry or output-law equality is supplied. Together with the constructed canonical/particle Drivers, this supports law coherence across actual initial-coupling probability spaces; it does not itself construct a general SDE solution.
