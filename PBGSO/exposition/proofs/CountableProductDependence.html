Countable spatial dependence inside arbitrary sample spaces

Every product-measurable field valued in a countably generated state space remains measurable over an actual countably generated spatial subalgebra of its original sample algebra.

Assumptions and formal scope. This is exact measurable dependence, not an assumed ambient measure separability or an almost-everywhere factorization. ProductLpSections adds a genuine finite-measure cover before constructing its spatial L2 curves.
