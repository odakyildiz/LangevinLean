Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Natural original sampler equations yield controlled raw dynamics

A joint continuous representative, proved null augmentation and primitive Borel drift derive progressivity, a genuine variation gauge and an actual raw realization with all original state/coefficient/noise identities on one event.

Assumptions and formal scope. Natural original local sampler equations and primitive coefficient measurability are explicit. No growth, global drift L2L^2L2, expected variation, stronger raw Data or energy conclusion is supplied.
