PBGSO overview / PBGSO/StoppingLpLimit.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Stopped coefficient domains and actual stochastic limits

Nested dominated convergence identifies literal original columns times the bounded-stop indicator as a predictable completed-domain member, and proves L2 convergence of the actual dyadic stopped integrals.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual closed integrands, a bounded stopping time and genuine stopping measurability are explicit. This module does not alone identify the continuous original noise at every random time.
