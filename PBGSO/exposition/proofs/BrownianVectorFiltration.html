Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Joint future-past independence with an independent initial state

Independent coordinate pairs are rearranged to prove full vector future-past independence on the actual canonical Brownian source. Pairing an independent random initial state gives the actual natural initial/noise filtration and increment independence from its complete past.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume finite spatial coordinates and the displayed probability/measurability structure. The canonical product construction supplies independence; no final vector independence identity is a premise. These are filtration foundations, not a solution existence or general Itô theorem.
