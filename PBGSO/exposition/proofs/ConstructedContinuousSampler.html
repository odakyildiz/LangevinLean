PBGSO overview / PBGSO/ConstructedContinuousSampler.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Continuous original sampler paths with Brownian modification derived

The continuous Driver is constructed internally and its actual integral invariance proves one jointly measurable continuous sampler/noise version satisfying the original drift equation on a single horizon event.

Assumptions and formal scope. The input is actual original computed-integral Data with time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2) drift and adapted time-L2L^2L2 diffusion, plus strong measurability of the original joint drift on the horizon. Brownian continuity, noise matching, pathwise drift integrability and per-time AE adaptation are derived. This does not identify the whole minimal source A1 class.
