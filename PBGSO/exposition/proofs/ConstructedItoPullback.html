PBGSO overview / PBGSO/ConstructedItoPullback.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual model equations survive source pullback and valid enlargement

The computed Brownian integral commutes with a genuine measure-preserving source map and a valid larger full Driver filtration. Original model equations, coefficient realizations, adaptation and actual output laws transfer together, producing genuine coupled model trajectories.

Assumptions and formal scope. The raw coefficient/equation data remain the primitive inputs. No integral identity, output-law compatibility or final coupling-energy premise is supplied. The continuous-column module is an explicit realization subclass.
