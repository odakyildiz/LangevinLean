Canonical initial/Wiener-path Driver with full joint past

The proved initial/path probability map pushes an original canonical Brownian source to literal independent initial state and Wiener paths. Its evaluation process and full joint initial/path natural filtration form an actual Driver.

Assumptions and formal scope. The initial law is a probability on the displayed Borel second-countable state space. Brownian laws, spatial and whole-path independence, initial independence and the input product law are derived; no unrelated Gaussian-seed inverse is required.
