Volume preservation of the frictionless kick and drift maps

The deterministic splitting consists of momentum kicks and position drifts, each a shear. Their composition preserves product Haar measure for an arbitrary measurable coupled force, without requiring it to be conservative or differentiable.

Assumptions and formal scope. The measure is SFinite and translation invariant, the force measurable, and the state space has the displayed complete Hilbert/Borel/second-countability structure. Friction and random thermostat stages are excluded. ContinuousVolume separately derives the continuous Jacobian assertion from the source C1C^1C1 force and globally defined original trajectories. FlowSourceRegularity derives joint C1C^1C1 dependence.
