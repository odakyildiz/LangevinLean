Measure preservation of the frictionless splitting

Use velocity-first coordinates (p,q)(p,q)(p,q). A force kick Kh(p,q)=(p−hF(q),q)K_h(p,q)=(p-hF(q),q)Kh​(p,q)=(p−hF(q),q) and position drift Ah(p,q)=(p,q+hp)A_h(p,q)=(p,q+hp)Ah​(p,q)=(p,q+hp) are translations of one coordinate while the other is fixed. Their composition Kh/2∘Ah∘Kh/2K_{h/2}\circ A_h\circ K_{h/2}Kh/2​∘Ah​∘Kh/2​ is the frictionless B–A–B part of the algorithm.

Assumptions and formal scope. The measure is SFinite and translation invariant, the force measurable, and the state space has the displayed complete Hilbert/Borel/second-countability structure. Friction and random thermostat stages are excluded. ContinuousVolume separately derives the continuous Jacobian assertion from the source C1C^1C1 force and globally defined original trajectories. FlowSourceRegularity derives joint C1C^1C1 dependence.

Statement and proof

01Translation invariance of each shear §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let QQQ be a complete real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure and a second-countable topology. Let μ\muμ be an S-finite translation-invariant measure on QQQ. For any measurable F:Q→QF:Q\to QF:Q→Q and any real step hhh, both shears and their B–A–B composition preserve μ⊗μ\mu\otimes\muμ⊗μ.

Proof. For a nonnegative measurable test ggg, Tonelli gives

∫g(p−hF(q),q) dμ(p)dμ(q)=∫[∫g(p,q) dμ(p)]dμ(q),\int g(p-hF(q),q)\,d\mu(p)d\mu(q)=\int\left[\int g(p,q)\,d\mu(p)\right]d\mu(q),∫g(p−hF(q),q)dμ(p)dμ(q)=∫[∫g(p,q)dμ(p)]dμ(q),

because for each fixed qqq the substitution p↦p−hF(q)p\mapsto p-hF(q)p↦p−hF(q) is a translation. This proves the kick assertion. Reverse the integration order and translate q↦q+hpq\mapsto q+hpq↦q+hp to prove the drift assertion. The assumptions on FFF ensure joint measurability of the kick, while the drift is continuous. A composition of measure-preserving maps is measure preserving, so the three-stage map preserves product measure. S-finiteness is the measure-theoretic hypothesis used for product integration. Neither differentiability nor a gradient representation of FFF is needed. Thermostats and friction are not among these three maps. □\square□

How the Lean proof follows this argument

forceKick, positionDrift and interwovenBAB are the exact kinetic kick, drift and Verlet maps. The three preservation theorems apply the previously proved KineticLangevin shear and composition results. Measurable F is the only force regularity premise in this measure-theoretic argument; Measure.IsAddLeftInvariant supplies translation invariance, and SFinite is retained explicitly.
