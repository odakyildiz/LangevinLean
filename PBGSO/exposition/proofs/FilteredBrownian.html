Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Primitive filtered vector Brownian drivers and actual predictable sums

An arbitrary finite coordinate Brownian driver retains its actual filtration, coordinate adaptation, spatial increment independence and complete future-vector independence from the full past. These primitive properties derive finite predictable-vector martingales and weighted isometry with pathwise deterministic refinement.

Assumptions and formal scope. Driver contains only Brownian laws, filtration, adaptation and independence. Square-integrable random coefficient columns are measurable at the actual left endpoint. No stochastic-integral, final martingale or isometry conclusion is a Driver field. CanonicalBrownianDriver constructs these properties for the actual canonical initial/noise source.
