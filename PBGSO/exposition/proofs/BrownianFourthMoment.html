Actual Gaussian fourth moments and increment tails

The actual fourth moment of the standard Gaussian supplies a finite positive-or-zero constant. Scaling derives the exact centered variance-v fourth moment and actual pre-Brownian increment tail bounds.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a probability source and actual scalar pre-Brownian laws. The constant is its actual integral, not an assumed moment bound; its exact numerical value is unnecessary.
