PBGSO overview / PBGSO/RawDiffusionTransport.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Original raw integrals under coupling and Driver changes

Product-AE coefficient agreement gives equality of actual computed raw integrals. Measure-preserving pullback, valid filtration enlargement and Brownian modification preserve those integrals.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual raw coefficient measurability, product square integrability, adaptation and the proved measure-preserving or valid Driver identities are explicit. No output-law or matching L2L^2L2 coefficient identity is assumed.
