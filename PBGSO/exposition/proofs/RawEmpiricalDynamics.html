The native finite-average empirical stochastic equation

The time-dependent model encodes the literal finite-average response, a shared random optimizer and physical sampler noise with zero optimizer diffusion. Natural original solutions yield controlled raw data.

Assumptions and formal scope. An actual original interacting solution and initial L2L^2L2, together with primitive Borel observation/drift/response maps, are explicit. Arbitrary finite-particle existence is not inferred from mean-field A1, and no final pair law or error is supplied.
