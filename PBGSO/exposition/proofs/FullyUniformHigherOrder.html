Explicit higher-order parameter error for all iterations

The primitive particle force assumptions construct both the exact interacting trajectory and the actual printed Gaussian scheme. The metric is derived from joint source dissipativity. Its coercivity, local error constants and Brownian radius yield a positive, data-defined step interval and a time-uniform numerical error term.

Assumptions and formal scope. Finite-dimensional coordinates, separate globally jointly Lipschitz forces, α>0,  γ≥0\alpha>0,\;\gamma\ge0α>0,γ≥0, positive N,ρN,\rhoN,ρ, P2\mathcal P_2P2​ initial/target laws, actual frozen invariant trajectory, zero target mean observation and A5 are explicit. No numerical contraction, reference moment, drift modulus, error recurrence or matching-law conclusion is assumed. Constants may depend on fixed NNN.
