Theorem New B: constructed time-uniform printed HOL parameter bound

The literal printed Gaussian implementation is compared with the actual constructed particles in the A3 metric. Uniform moments and derived local errors yield an all-iteration parameter bound below a positive fixed step threshold.

Assumptions and formal scope. Finite-dimensional coordinates, separate globally jointly Lipschitz forces, alpha>0/gamma>=0, positive N/rho, P2 initial/target laws, actual frozen invariant trajectory, zero target mean observation and A5 are explicit. No numerical contraction, reference moment, drift modulus, error recurrence or matching-law conclusion is assumed. Constants may depend on fixed N.
