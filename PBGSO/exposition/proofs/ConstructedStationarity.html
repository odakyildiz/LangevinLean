Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Stationary laws for the actual computed predictable integral

An actual frozen invariant sampler constructs the constant-optimizer model Data and, under same-initial uniqueness, its literal Dirac optimizer tensor sampler stationary law.

Assumptions and formal scope. The frozen process obeys the computed original integral equation in the time-L1(L2) drift and adapted time-L2 diffusion class. Actual invariant law, observation integrability, zero mean gradient and optimizer fixed point are explicit. Minimal source A1 realization remains separate.

DataUniqueness

Mathematical role and assumptions

Same-driver uniqueness restricted to the explicit actual-data class. The inputs are original integral equations, not a stationarity or energy conclusion. This is the exact well-posedness part needed below.
