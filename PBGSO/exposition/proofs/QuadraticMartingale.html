Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Expected quadratic derivative from actual martingale structure

The residual is the actual L2L^2L2 process minus its initial value and Bochner drift integral. A standard martingale representative yields weighted orthogonality through conditional expectation. Exact forward and backward quadratic expansions and the increment isometry derive the expected energy derivative without a forward-equation premise.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume norm-continuous L2L^2L2 state, drift and finite diffusion columns, an adapted state, a standard martingale representative of the actual residual, symmetric continuous metric weight and its explicit weighted increment isometry. Constructing a multiplicative stochastic integral and proving that isometry for it remain analytic obligations.
