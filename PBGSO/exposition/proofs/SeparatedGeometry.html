Weighted metric and probability quadratic-energy estimates

Weighted Cauchy–Schwarz and Euclidean comparisons follow from actual symmetric positive quadratic weights. Probability-space L2L^2L2 estimates derive the bound on the expected square root of a nonnegative quadratic coupling energy.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the displayed positive lower and finite upper metric comparisons, probability normalization and actual integrability/measurability of the energy. Neither an operator-norm comparison nor a Jensen/Cauchy–Schwarz conclusion is silently supplied as a premise.
