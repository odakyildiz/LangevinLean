Continuous martingale versions from actual maximal bounds

A terminal-L2L^2L2 Cauchy family of actual continuous-path martingales yields an internally selected fast subsequence with almost-sure uniform continuous limit, through the proved maximal inequality and Borel-Cantelli.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual approximating martingales, terminal L2L^2L2 convergence and their continuous paths are explicit. Uniform convergence and continuity of the limit are conclusions.
