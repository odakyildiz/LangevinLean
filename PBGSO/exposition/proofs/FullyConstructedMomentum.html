Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

M-MFPO target bound with its process and reference constructed

Primitive independent forces construct the actual heavy-ball/underdamped model and interacting particle dynamics. Original same-sample L2L^2L2 coordinates, physical Brownian increments, Gaussian innovations and mesh-uniform moments/moduli derive the complete reference and zero initial discretization error.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the displayed separate global joint Lipschitz bounds for FFF and GGG, canonical Euclidean cloud encoding, nonnegative friction, positive source A3 rate, P2\mathcal P_2P2​ initial/target laws, actual frozen invariant sampler, zero target mean observation and uniform variance. The target optimiser momentum is zero and its fixed point is derived. No supplied reference, moment/modulus, final law equality or weak-process package remains. The bound concerns fixed finite horizons and constructed additive integral dynamics.
