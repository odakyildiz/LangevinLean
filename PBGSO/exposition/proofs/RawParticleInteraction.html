Exact original particle interaction under local Brownian equations

Damped original localizations derive sharp forced energy before Fatou removes the threshold. The actual original empirical Wasserstein RMS has exactly the source interaction floor.

Assumptions and formal scope. Original raw physical drift/diffusion identities, continuous adapted state, initial L2L^2L2 and simultaneous local-integral equation are explicit. Positive N,ρN,\rhoN,ρ, source A3, response Lipschitz, original reference iid marginal/deterministic optimizer, uniform observation variance and synchronous initialization remain visible. No linear growth, global drift moment, weak test, work estimate, stochastic isometry or energy conclusion is supplied.
