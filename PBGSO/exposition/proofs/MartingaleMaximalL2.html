Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Derived weak-L2L^2L2 martingale maximal inequality

Conditional Jensen proves that the actual squared norm of a square-integrable martingale is a submartingale. The finite-grid Doob maximal inequality then bounds every monotone observation grid by its actual terminal second moment.

Assumptions and formal scope. The martingale, filtration and ordered finite observation grid are explicit. No maximal inequality is assumed. This is a finite-grid foundation; a simultaneous continuous modification is not asserted.
