Original raw equations derive their actual stopped equations

The literal state at min⁡{time,stop}\min\{\mathrm{time},\mathrm{stop}\}min{time,stop} and masked raw drift satisfy exactly the stopped original integral equation. Genuine eventual horizon exhaustion gives pointwise original-state convergence.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only the original simultaneous path equation and bounded nonnegative stop are used. Joint measurability assumptions appear where needed; no stopped SDE, stopped state moment or L2L^2L2 coordinate identity is supplied.
