Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed Brownian drivers on actual independent particle seeds

A measure-preserving source map pulls back the full filtration and derives every primitive vector Brownian Driver property. The proved joint initial-cloud/whole-noise product map then constructs the actual flattened particle Driver, whose coordinate process is exactly the original per-particle Brownian coordinate.

Assumptions and formal scope. The generic pullback uses a genuine measure-preserving map and an actual Driver; no output-law or adaptedness conclusion is assumed. The particle specialization derives that map from ParticleNoiseSpace and requires only the stated probability initial law and finite coordinates. Its filtration includes the entire initial cloud and all joint noise pasts.
