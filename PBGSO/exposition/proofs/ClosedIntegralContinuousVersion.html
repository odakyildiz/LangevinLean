Continuous versions of actual completed stochastic integrals

Actual stopped-grid martingales and the maximal completion theorem construct continuous all-time and jointly measurable finite-window versions of the computed integral.

Assumptions and formal scope. This intermediate theorem assumes coordinate Brownian path continuity. ContinuousBrownianDriver now derives a valid modification with that property; integral modification invariance preserves the original computed noise.
