Full particle interaction bound with time-integrable coefficients

The actual particle/copy drift and diffusion, joint-law pushforwards and source A3 derive expected energy balance and the exact source empirical RMS interaction bound. The reference-cloud iid marginal supplies V/NV/NV/N forcing; empirical-statistic measurability is proved.

Assumptions and formal scope. The actual drift is Bochner L1L^1L1-time L2L^2L2 and diffusion time-L2L^2L2. At this general interface the residual standard martingale and normalized stochastic increment isometry are explicit. No test-domain, final energy, residual-measurability or continuous-coefficient premise is required. A constructed-integral wrapper discharges the stochastic interface separately.
