Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual Brownian coordinate inclusion and zero columns

Injecting finite Brownian coordinates into a larger valid Driver and padding the original diffusion by zero preserves the actual finite sums, completed domains and computed raw integral.

Assumptions and formal scope. The index injection and actual coordinate identities are explicit. Coefficient embeddings, zero-column sums, approximation and integral equality are derived; no stochastic-output equality is supplied.
