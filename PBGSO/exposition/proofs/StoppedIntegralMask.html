Exact completed finite-grid stopped integral and energy

The original column coefficients multiplied by actual stopping indicators lie in the completed domain, and their computed integral equals the original noise at the finite-grid stopping time, with exact weighted energy.

Assumptions and formal scope. An actual finite-grid stopping index and valid Driver are explicit. Domain membership, coefficient identification and computed-noise equality are derived.
