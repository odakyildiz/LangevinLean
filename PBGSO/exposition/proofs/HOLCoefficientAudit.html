Checked counterexample to exact linear-flow identification

At α=γ=1\alpha=\gamma=1α=γ=1 the derivative of the printed μ22\mu_{22}μ22​ coefficient is computed exactly. At h=1h=1h=1 its difference from μ32\mu_{32}μ32​ equals 1−3e−11-3e^{-1}1−3e−1, which is proved nonzero.

Assumptions and formal scope. This is a concrete analytic counterexample to a necessary fundamental-matrix identity. It does not invalidate the printed numerical map or substitute a corrected covariance law; the implementation error budgets retain the actual coefficients.
