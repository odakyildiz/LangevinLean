A checked obstruction to the exact linear-flow interpretation

The printed coefficients define the numerical method, but an exact linear flow must also satisfy the differential equations of its fundamental matrix. At unit parameters, one necessary coefficient identity fails by a nonzero exact quantity.

Assumptions and formal scope. This is a concrete analytic counterexample to a necessary fundamental-matrix identity. It does not invalidate the printed numerical map or substitute a corrected covariance law; the implementation error budgets retain the actual coefficients.

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. At α=γ=1\alpha=\gamma=1α=γ=1, one has μ22′(t)=−1+e−t\mu_{22}'(t)=-1+e^{-t}μ22′​(t)=−1+e−t, and μ22′(1)−μ32(1)=1−3e−1≠0\mu_{22}'(1)-\mu_{32}(1)=1-3e^{-1}\ne0μ22′​(1)−μ32​(1)=1−3e−1=0. Consequently the printed coefficients cannot coincide at all positive times with the exact fundamental matrix of u′=v, v′=−u−vu'=v,\ v'=-u-vu′=v, v′=−u−v.

Proof. Substituting unit parameters into the printed formula gives μ22(t)=2−t−e−t\mu_{22}(t)=2-t-e^{-t}μ22​(t)=2−t−e−t. Differentiating yields −1+e−t-1+e^{-t}−1+e−t. Substituting the unit parameters and t=1t=1t=1 in the other printed coefficient and subtracting gives exactly 1−3e−11-3e^{-1}1−3e−1. Since e<3e<3e<3 and e−1>0e^{-1}>0e−1>0, multiplication gives 1<3e−11<3e^{-1}1<3e−1, so the defect is negative and in particular nonzero. In an exact fundamental matrix, the first equation u′=vu'=vu′=v forces the derivative of the response of uuu to its initial uuu to equal the response of vvv to that same initial datum, namely μ22′=μ32\mu_{22}'=\mu_{32}μ22′​=μ32​. Their mismatch contradicts that necessary identity. It does not alter the defined numerical map, and the local-error analysis above uses the actual printed coefficients instead. □\square□

How the Lean proof follows this argument

hol_mu22_unit_hasDeriv differentiates the exponential with the chain rule and differentiates the two affine terms. hol_linear_coefficient_defect rewrites by this derivative and simplifies the two printed coefficient expressions. hol_linear_coefficient_not_exact combines the exact defect with Real.exp_one_lt_three, positivity of the inverse exponential, and Real.exp_add. The contradiction is exact real arithmetic, not floating-point evaluation.
