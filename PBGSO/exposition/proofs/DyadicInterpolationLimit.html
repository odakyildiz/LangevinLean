Uniform continuous limits of actual polygons

Completeness of continuous maps turns summable eventual adjacent-increment bounds into actual uniform convergence of the dyadic polygons.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the displayed summable deterministic increment budget; the Brownian probability module derives it almost surely from genuine Gaussian laws.
