Continuous martingale versions with Brownian regularity derived

The explicit continuous Driver modification and actual completed-integral invariance construct a continuous martingale version of every actual closed integral in the original filtration.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only the primitive actual Driver and literal closed integrand are supplied. No Brownian path continuity, filtration completion, stochastic-output identity or uniform convergence premise remains.

Mathematical role and assumptions

Every actual completed integral admits an actual continuous martingale modification in its original filtration. No Brownian path-continuity, computed-output-matching or filtration-completion hypothesis is supplied.
