Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Original integral equations give exact numerical reference segments

Subtracting two actual global integral equations and shifting the Bochner integral gives the exact step equation. The physical Brownian input increment is identified with the implementation reference innovation for both methods.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the actual global integral equation and continuous drift. The exact segment equation and Gaussian-input matching are conclusions. Moment and modulus estimates are derived separately from the actual source process by the method reference constructors.
