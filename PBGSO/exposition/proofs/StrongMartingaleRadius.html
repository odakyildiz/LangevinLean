Constructed square-integrable continuous martingale radii

Finite strong Doob, countable monotone maxima and dense-time continuous paths construct an actual path radius with second moment at most four times terminal energy.

Assumptions and formal scope. An actual L² martingale and AE continuous paths on the finite horizon are explicit. No path-radius variable or maximal second moment is supplied.
