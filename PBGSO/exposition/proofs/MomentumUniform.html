Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Time-uniform actual OBABO strong discretisation error

Picard restarts and primitive drift dissipativity give exact contraction; affine local errors and derived global reference moments are absorbed into a uniform h\sqrt hh​ error.

Assumptions and formal scope. The generic theorem displays metric coercivity and original drift monotonicity; final particle clients derive them from source A3. No numerical contractivity, local defect, moments or final recurrence are assumed.
