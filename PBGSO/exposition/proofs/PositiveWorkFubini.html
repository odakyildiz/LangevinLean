Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

One-sided work yields exact raw expected energy

Pathwise Bochner-L1L^1L1 dynamics imply the literal quadratic fundamental theorem. Integrability of only positive work yields full product absolute-work integrability, expected balance and decay, without a total-variation L2L^2L2 budget.

Assumptions and formal scope. Nonnegative quadratic energy, initial-energy integrability, actual path equation and positive-work integrability are explicit. The last condition is an additional analytic criterion. NaturalFiniteWorkContraction derives it from the explicit finite signed-work convention; A4/A5 derive it for stationary-target comparisons.
