05From the phase error to a linear metric §

Claim. Let EEE and HHH be real normed spaces and C:E×E→HC:E\times E\to HC:E×E→H a bounded linear map, where E×EE\times EE×E carries the norm max⁡{∥z1∥,∥z2∥}\max\{\|z_1\|,\|z_2\|\}max{∥z1​∥,∥z2​∥}. Then for all z,w∈E×Ez,w\in E\times Ez,w∈E×E

∥C(z−w)∥≤∥C∥ (∥z1−w1∥+∥z2−w2∥).\|C(z-w)\|\le\|C\|\,\bigl(\|z_1-w_1\|+\|z_2-w_2\|\bigr).∥C(z−w)∥≤∥C∥(∥z1​−w1​∥+∥z2​−w2​∥).

Proof.
