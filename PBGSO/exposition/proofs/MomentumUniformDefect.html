Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

OBABO affine local error from primitive integral data

One-step state and drift radii and increment moduli are derived from the actual kinetic integral equation. The Gaussian-coupled local defect is explicitly affine in the random initial norm.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only global force Lipschitz regularity, nonnegative friction, the original continuous integral equation and Brownian increment/half-noise primitives remain; no local-error or moment conclusion is assumed.
