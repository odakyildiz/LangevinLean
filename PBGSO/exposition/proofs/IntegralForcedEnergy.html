Forced-energy comparison from actual integral balances

An absolutely continuous Grönwall argument and a regularized square-root chain rule derive the exact forced bound E(T)≤(c/ρ)(1−e−ρT)\sqrt{E(T)}\le(c/\rho)(1-e^{-\rho T})E(T)​≤(c/ρ)(1−e−ρT) from an actual integral energy balance and AE-time dissipation. Compact-range Lipschitz regularity justifies the square-root limit.

Assumptions and formal scope. Positive ρ\rhoρ, nonnegative forcing, the explicit integrability and integral-equation premises are required. No pointwise energy derivative or pre-existing final energy estimate is assumed.
