Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Natural constant-noise target relaxation with the true law distance

Source response Lipschitz and reference variance derive positive work for the natural original reference/target equations. Actual coupling transports and the genuine infimum give the initial optimizer/Wasserstein factor.

Assumptions and formal scope. Canonical original raw reference and stationary-target equations, primitive Borel fields, source A3/A4/A5 and target observation L1L^1L1 are explicit. No extra growth/work/moment, old-process package or output-noise identity is supplied.
