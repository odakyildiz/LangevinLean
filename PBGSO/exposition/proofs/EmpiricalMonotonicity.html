Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual empirical couplings and finite-cloud joint monotonicity

The finite empirical pair measure has the intended empirical marginals. Integrating joint monotonicity gives the exact finite averaged work bound, including all synchronous diffusion columns. The population-force discrepancy is isolated as its actual empirical observation fluctuation.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume positive particle count, the general model, the positive twisted metric and its law-level joint monotonicity. Finite Dirac integrability is proved without particle independence. Dynamic independent-copy product laws and the stochastic interacting/copy generator equation are separate inputs.
