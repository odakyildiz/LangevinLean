Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual uniformly convergent martingale series foundations

A Borel–Cantelli argument constructs a literal telescoping-series limit uniformly in time from summable dense-observation increment tail bounds and actual continuous approximant paths. Actual finite stopped predictable Brownian sums have continuous paths when the coordinate Driver paths do.

Assumptions and formal scope. Dense observation set, actual continuity of approximants and the displayed summable tail budget are explicit. No uniform convergence or limiting path continuity is assumed. Coordinate pre-Brownian laws alone do not supply continuous coordinate paths.
