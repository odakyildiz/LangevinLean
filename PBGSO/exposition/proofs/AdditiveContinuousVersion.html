Continuous original mean-field sampler paths from actual integral solutions

The primitive global sampler bounds give a genuine jointly Lipschitz sampler field. The actual complete mean-field integral equation projects to its sampler equation and derives all L1L^1L1-time drift and nonlinear realization conditions. A constructed joint representative then gives one jointly strongly measurable version with actual initial value, continuous paths and the literal original sampler equation for every horizon time on one full-measure event.

Assumptions and formal scope. The actual raw additive input must be jointly strongly measurable, have the stated per-time L2L^2L2 identity and almost-sure continuous paths. No joint drift or final path equation is supplied. Combining the exact per-time version identity with proved L2L^2L2 adaptation gives AE current-past measurability; strict measurability in an uncompleted natural filtration and a Brownian continuous modification are not asserted here.
