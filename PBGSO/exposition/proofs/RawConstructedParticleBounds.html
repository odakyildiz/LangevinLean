Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Sharp source interaction bound for the original native particle system

The natural paired construction supplies all physical coefficients, common noise and original iid reference law internally; localized forcing and Fatou yield the exact empirical Wasserstein RMS floor on the original native solution.

Assumptions and formal scope. The actual interacting native solution lives on the constructed independent whole-seed product, with synchronous original initial coordinates. Positive N,ρN,\rhoN,ρ, A3/A4/A5, actual original mean-field solution and primitive Borel fields are visible. No paired process, cloud law, noise matching, energy, global drift moment or final bound is assumed.
