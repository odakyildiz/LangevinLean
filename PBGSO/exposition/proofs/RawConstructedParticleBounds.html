Independent reference particles and the interaction error

Take N>0N>0N>0 independent copies Zˉti\bar Z^i_tZˉti​ of one actual mean-field sampler trajectory, with its deterministic complete optimiser sts_tst​. The interacting empirical solution uses the corresponding Brownian coordinate in each particle equation. Its complete optimiser StNS^N_tStN​ is shared by the particles. Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Suppose actual mean-field and empirical strong solutions exist on [0,T][0,T][0,T]. Assume Borel observation, drift and optimiser maps, joint dissipativity with ρ>0\rho>0ρ>0, a quadratic metric of condition number κ\kappaκ, and optimiser response Lipschitz constant LΨ≥0L_\Psi\ge0LΨ​≥0 in its mean-observation argument. The observation along every mean-field law is square integrable and has centred second moment at most V≥0V\ge0V≥0. Initialise the empirical solution almost surely with the same optimiser and reference cloud. Then

(E[∥STN−sT∥2+W2(ν^TN,νˉ^TN)2])1/2≤LΨρκVN(1−e−ρT).\left(\mathbb E\big[\|S^N_T-s_T\|^2+W_2(\widehat\nu^N_T,\widehat{\bar\nu}^N_T)^2\big]\right)^{1/2}\le\frac{L_\Psi}{\rho}\sqrt{\frac{\kappa V}{N}}(1-e^{-\rho T}).(E[∥STN​−sT​∥2+W2​(νTN​,νˉTN​)2])1/2≤ρLΨ​​NκV​​(1−e−ρT).

Proof. Construct the reference cloud on the NNN-fold product of the original probability space. By the product pushforward identity its coordinates are independent and each has the original mean-field law. Its empirical observation fluctuation is a sum of independent centred vectors. The mixed inner products have expectation zero; expanding the squared norm gives centred variance at most V/NV/NV/N.

Pair each empirical particle with its own reference particle. Localisation of the actual coupled Brownian equations justifies the weighted energy derivative. Joint dissipativity bounds the matching drift part by −2ρE-2\rho E−2ρE. The remaining optimiser response is at most LΨL_\PsiLΨ​ times the empirical observation fluctuation; Cauchy–Schwarz bounds its energy contribution by 2cE2c\sqrt E2cE​, with the metric factors giving the final constant LΨκV/NL_\Psi\sqrt{\kappa V/N}LΨ​κV/N​ after conversion to Euclidean distance. The initial paired error is zero. The forced square-root comparison yields (c/ρ)(1−e−ρT)(c/\rho)(1-e^{-\rho T})(c/ρ)(1−e−ρT). The labelled particle pairs give a coupling of their empirical measures, so the empirical Wasserstein cost is no larger than their mean squared labelled displacement. Thus the same bound controls the stated distance. □\square□

How the Lean proof follows this argument

originalPair is the original interacting trajectory paired with the constructed independent copies. original_finite_particle_interaction_bound builds RawConstructedParticleCopy.build from the three Borel hypotheses and calls finite_particle_interaction_bound. That proof establishes the actual reference cloud product law with reference_cloud_law, optimiser identity with reference_optimizer, and zero initial discrepancy with initial_coupling. It applies the proved paired bound, then uses pair_state and integral_congr_ae to replace the constructed pair by the original native state. These identities are conclusions of the construction.
