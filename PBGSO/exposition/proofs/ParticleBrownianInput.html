Actual copied Brownian inputs equal flattened block diffusion

Independent complete initial/noise seeds produce the actual copied sampler inputs. Their full L2L^2L2 equality with an explicit flattened particle/spatial block-column Brownian input is proved, with exactly zero optimiser diffusion.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the finite constant sampler diffusion map and probability initial sampler law. The common complete Brownian source, flattening and full input identity are constructed; no final covariance, noise-law or matching equality is a premise. This is an additive input construction.
