Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Physical diffusion equals the original method input in L2L^2L2 coordinates

The actual source model block diffusion is transformed through the proved implementation coordinate maps. This yields exactly momentum-only kinetic forcing and final-auxiliary-only HOL forcing on the same random initial/noise space.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the literal flattened Euclidean particle encoding, complete real Hilbert optimiser coordinate and actual coordinate pre-Brownian inputs. The encoding condition is needed for independent isotropic original particle noise. No final diffusion or input matching identity is supplied.
