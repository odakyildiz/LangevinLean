Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Finite-signed-work source contraction across arbitrary original sources

Actual Wiener-path law uniqueness identifies two original raw solutions with their canonical counterparts. The proved finite-work comparison yields the exact optimal initial Wasserstein factor.

Assumptions and formal scope. Original raw equations, direct A1 canonical/path strong-existence instances and same-driver uniqueness, primitive Borel fields, positive A3 and independent FiniteCouplingWork are explicit. No ambient separability, A4, response continuity, drift-norm growth, work or energy premise is imposed. Extended-negative work is outside this convention.
