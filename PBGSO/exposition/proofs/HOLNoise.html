Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed jointly Gaussian HOL innovations and Brownian coupling

The printed mu13, mu23 and mu33 response kernels are integrated on a canonical Brownian segment, together with a constant fourth kernel for its Brownian increment. The joint Gaussian law, integral Gram covariance, spatial dimension factor and independent blocks across times and spatial coordinates are proved.

Assumptions and formal scope. The construction uses the canonical Brownian source and a finite coordinate index. The fourth response is proved equal to the scaled Brownian increment. HOLCovariance separately identifies the manuscript's expanded covariance formulas. Identification of an end-to-end nonlinear SDE law remains separate.
