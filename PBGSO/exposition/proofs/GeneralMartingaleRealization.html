Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual full state-dependent diffusion quadratic weak realization

Actual L2L^2L2 drift and diffusion columns realize the general model, and the drift residual is a standard martingale. Adaptation and the synchronous weighted increment isometry derive the actual quadratic pair forward equation. The source joint criterion then proves energy decay without a supplied energy or weak forward equation.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume continuous L2L^2L2 state, drift and diffusion, their pointwise model identities, actual optimiser ODE, observation integrability, adapted state, martingale residual and SynchronousIsometry. Diffusion may depend on parameter and state. Marginal tests are empty and the pair domain the quadratic singleton. Constructing the intended stochastic integral/isometry and richer marginal Itô domains remains separate.
