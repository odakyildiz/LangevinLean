Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual continuous Brownian paths have universal Wiener law

A single measurable null cover constructs actual continuous scalar/vector path variables. Their Borel algebra is generated by raw evaluations; projective finite-dimensional uniqueness proves the Wiener law and independent product law.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only original Brownian laws, actual evaluation measurability and path continuity enter the generic scalar/vector statements. The generic vector theorem exposes coordinate independence; BrownianDriverPathLaw derives it from every primitive Driver. No inverse onto unused Gaussian seed directions or final product-law match is assumed.
