Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Bounded stopping of actual continuous Brownian integrals

Continuous computed versions and dyadic stopped limits construct actual continuous martingales whose paths satisfy the stopped identity simultaneously at every observation time; exact original-column weighted stopped energy follows.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual closed integrands and genuine bounded stopping events are explicit. This intermediate API asks for Brownian coordinate continuous paths, supplied by the proved valid continuous Driver modification. Minimal-A1 localization and its coefficient hypotheses remain distinct.
