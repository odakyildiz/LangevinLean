PBGSO overview / PBGSO/ConstructedAdaptation.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual selected mean-field and particle processes are adapted

The original canonical initial variable and physical Brownian input are identified with the actual canonical initial/noise Driver. The same argument identifies complete particle initial clouds and packed Brownian input. Actual integral equations and primitive Lipschitz maps then derive current-past measurability of the selected canonical mean-field and interacting particle states.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the displayed primitive globally Lipschitz additive model and P₂ initial law. No initial/input measurability, solution-adaptedness or final law identity is a premise. The particle theorem applies to the actual constructed realization structure via its original equation and initial identity. Strongly adapted representatives have per-time a.e. equality; simultaneous sample-path continuity requires a further version argument.
