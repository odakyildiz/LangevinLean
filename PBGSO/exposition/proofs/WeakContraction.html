Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Full multiplicative-noise energy from an actual coupled generator

The synchronous pair generator is computed on the weighted quadratic test with all state- and parameter-dependent diffusion columns retained. The weak forward equation and optimiser ODE derive the energy derivative, Grönwall decay and ordinary quadratic coupling-cost estimate.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume actual marginal weak solutions and a synchronous pair forward equation, the displayed observation/test/generator integrability, quadratic separation in the pair test domain, and twisted joint monotonicity. No scalar energy inequality is assumed. Strong-SDE existence, quadratic-domain justification and equivalence with this coupled weak solution notion remain implementation obligations.
