Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Linear-growth law contraction across original probability spaces

Actual cross-source law uniqueness transfers the canonical optimal-coupling contraction to the original raw solutions, retaining the exact initial-law Wasserstein factor.

Assumptions and formal scope. The canonical solutions satisfy primitive original raw growth and regularity, with source same-driver uniqueness on the proved valid drivers. No original output matching, noise identity, moment bound or coupling inequality is supplied. The bare constant-diffusion alternative remains separate.
