The exact source parameter prefactor from natural target equations

Natural original constant-noise reference/target solutions have the genuine optimal-law relaxation factor. Combining this with the original raw particle floor gives the exact source4 parameter expression.

Assumptions and formal scope. The actual physical particle/copy raw Data remain explicit until the final natural pairing factory. Source A3/A4/A5, primitive Borel maps, actual target stationarity and observation L1L^1L1 are visible. No final work, moment, weak realizer, coupling output or parameter triangle is assumed.

Mathematical role and assumptions

Source4's exact exponential-plus-particle parameter estimate in the natural constant-diffusion realization class. The initial relaxation cost is the genuine optimizer/Wasserstein distance, obtained by infimizing over all actual initial coupling realizations.
