HOL-MYPGD consistency against its actual integral equations

The exact appendix coefficients and parameter-first update are compared with the higher-order Euler map. A derived explicit budget retains coefficient, splitting and innovation errors; the reference Euler defect follows from its integral equation.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the displayed parameter Lipschitz force bound, force and velocity bounds, an integrable reference drift and a square-root drift modulus. Innovation mismatch with the reference final-coordinate input stays explicit. No Gaussian covariance coupling, proper prior, smoothed target or stationary law is inferred.
