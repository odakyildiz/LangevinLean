Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Computed original work is product integrable with random variation

Actual computed noise, original raw equations and a random square-integrable total-variation budget derive product integrability of the literal weighted drift work.

Assumptions and formal scope. Raw joint measurability, continuous original paths, actual coefficient square integrability and the random L2 variation budget are explicit. No original drift L2 curve, supplied martingale, maximal radius or work-integrability premise enters.
