Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

The physical independent reference cloud solves its actual SDE

Bounded linear state-map and noise-index-injection identities derive the coordinate noises and the complete original Hilbert-cloud stochastic equation from the physical diffusion columns. Its reference marginal law is also derived.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original one-copy raw solution and finite independent complete seeds are explicit. Neither ambient countable generation/separability nor coefficient/noise/law/equation matching is supplied. Packing this cloud with a separate interacting original solution is the next assembly layer.
