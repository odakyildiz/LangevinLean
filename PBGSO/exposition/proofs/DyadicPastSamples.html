Strict past dyadic times and polygon comparison

Floored dyadic times stay in the past, approach the current time and differ from actual polygon values by the adjacent-grid increment budget, including the right endpoint.

Assumptions and formal scope. Nonnegative times and positive grid size are explicit. No adaptation or continuity premise enters these deterministic estimates.
