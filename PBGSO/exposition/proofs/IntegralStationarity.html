Stationarity from actual sampler integral equations and uniqueness

The complete parameter- and state-dependent diffusion remains in an actual pathwise sampler integral equation. The frozen invariant sampler and optimiser fixed point form a genuine constant-optimiser integral solution. Same-driver pathwise uniqueness proves its full output law is literally the deterministic optimiser tensor frozen sampler law.

Assumptions and formal scope. The stochastic integral operator JJJ and admissible process class CCC are supplied explicitly. No Itô implementation is constructed here. Assume actual frozen invariant trajectory and law, finite second moment, observation integrability, zero mean gradient, optimiser fixed point and same-driver pathwise uniqueness of the displayed integral equations. Intended Itô/adapted process-class instantiation remains separate.

Mathematical role and assumptions
