Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Full finite-particle empirical interaction rate from actual weak laws

The exact reference-cloud product marginal gives its Var/N fluctuation. Weighted Cauchy–Schwarz and primitive optimiser response Lipschitz continuity derive the forcing term in the actual full multiplicative generator equation. The resulting regularized energy comparison gives the source empirical RMS rate.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the actual particle/copy weak forward equation with its quadratic test domain, positive N and rate, full joint monotonicity, zero initial energy, an almost-surely deterministic reference optimiser, the actual iid cloud marginal, L² observation and uniform variance, and the displayed residual/empirical-metric AE measurability. The interacting variables need not be independent. Strong-SDE-to-weak-equation and evolved-copy-law identification remain explicit.
