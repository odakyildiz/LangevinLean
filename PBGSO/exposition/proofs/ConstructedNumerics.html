Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Numerical target estimates for constructed additive particle processes

Actual frozen invariance and primitive maps construct the continuous target, every target coupling and interacting/copy process. Gaussian numerical error bounds combine with its exact source parameter estimate on the actual independent initial/noise particle space.

Assumptions and formal scope. These intermediate wrappers retain an explicit L2L^2L2 identity between the supplied integral reference and actual interacting parameter coordinate. FullyConstructedMomentum and FullyConstructedHigherOrder subsequently derive the reference, all mesh-uniform regularity and that coordinate identity internally; the general multiplicative strong-SDE bridge remains open.
