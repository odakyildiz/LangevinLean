Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Exact physical Brownian and implementation innovation identities

The kinetic two-half Gaussian innovation sum equals the physical full-step Brownian input. The fourth HOL response kernel equals the physical final-auxiliary Brownian input after actual whole-source pullback.

Assumptions and formal scope. The identities use actual Brownian coordinates and physical coefficient signs. HOL uses an actual measure-preserving canonical-noise projection, constructed by ParticleNoiseSpace for independent initial/noise seeds. No covariance or innovation-matching premise substitutes for the proved L² equalities.
