Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed global additive interacting/copy particle processes

Actual iid seed pullbacks of the one-copy integral process build the reference cloud. Picard constructs the shared-optimiser interacting process with the true empirical force and common input. Its actual quadratic weak generator, iid reference marginal, deterministic reference optimiser and zero initial energy are all derived.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume positive NNN, primitive globally Lipschitz autonomous additive maps compatible with the model, a global one-copy integral reference, continuous sampler input from zero and the uniform observation variance. The final ParticleParameterApproximation is constructed, not supplied. Its test domain is the particle quadratic singleton; richer Itô equations and general multiplicative input remain separate.
