Actual progressive windows in real time

Real-time restrictions and bounded windows of genuinely progressive processes retain the actual past-product measurability used by local coefficient energies.

Assumptions and formal scope. The process progressive measurability and displayed filtration/time restrictions are explicit. No path-energy adaptation conclusion is supplied.

realExtension

Mathematical role and assumptions

Literal extension of a nonnegative-time process to real time. Negative times are evaluated at zero and subsequently removed by the raw time mask.
