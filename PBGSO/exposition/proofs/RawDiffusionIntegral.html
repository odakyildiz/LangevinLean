Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Computed Brownian integration of original raw fields

Actual joint raw diffusion columns supply their own spatial L² sections and predictable completion. Martingale representatives, continuous paths, zero input and exact original-column weighted increment energy are derived.

Assumptions and formal scope. Raw joint strong measurability, expected integrated square norm, actual AE past-adaptation are explicit. Spatial sections now require no ambient measure separability. No L² curve, approximation, stochastic isometry or continuous noise version is supplied.
