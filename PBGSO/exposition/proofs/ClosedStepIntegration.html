Actual Itô integral on the closed predictable-step domain

The domain is literally the closure of actual finite adapted Brownian-step coefficient embeddings in time-L2L^2L2. The computed isometric extension gives its actual stochastic integral, derived finite approximating sequences and weighted moments. It agrees with the earlier refining-grid construction on that domain.

Assumptions and formal scope. A domain member is a genuine closure member, not a field containing a final error bound or stochastic isometry. PredictableStepDensity proves membership from raw time-L2L^2L2 adapted columns; full source SDE existence remains separate.
