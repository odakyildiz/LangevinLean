Expected quadratic energy with pathwise L1L^1L1 drift

Actual martingale orthogonality and derived pathwise partition limits prove the expected quadratic balance with the original drift primitive.

Assumptions and formal scope. Original continuous state/noise paths, pathwise Bochner-L1L^1L1 drift, point-time L2L^2L2 and a square-integrable path radius are explicit. No time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2) drift or energy balance is assumed.
