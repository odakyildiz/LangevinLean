Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Source target comparison from natural original constant-noise solutions

Natural original raw mean-field solutions and a stationary target derive the positive-work criterion through response Lipschitz and reference variance. The actual law and parameter relaxation bounds follow.

Assumptions and formal scope. Primitive Borel maps, source A3/A4/A5, actual stationary target and its observation L1L^1L1 are explicit. This version uses the genuine realized initial coupling cost; an optimal-law infimum needs its separate all-couplings construction. No stronger process, energy, cross-drift growth or final work premise is supplied.
