Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual stopped state moments from original equations

Initial L2L^2L2, actual noise L2L^2L2 and the original raw integral equation derive the state L2L^2L2 value and its explicit three-term second-moment bound.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original raw drift has a genuine pathwise variation cap and joint measurability. No stopped state moment, stronger coefficient curve or time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2) drift is supplied.
