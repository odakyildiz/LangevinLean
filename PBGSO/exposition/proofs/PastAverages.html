Actual time-L2L^2L2 translation averages

The actual measure-preserving translation action is Bochner averaged in the genuine time-L2L^2L2 space. FTC proves convergence to every original integrand, while norm contraction and Lipschitz continuity are derived. Literal pointwise moving means of locally integrable curves are continuous.

Assumptions and formal scope. No original time continuity is assumed. Linking the time-L2L^2L2 average to its pointwise moving mean is a separate Fubini identity supplied by LpIntegralSections; adaptedness is supplied by AdaptedPastAverages.
