Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Native interacting and constructed reference solutions form the physical pair

An original native empirical solution and natural one-copy equation construct the genuine independent reference cloud and their actual full paired local SDE, with all physical drift, diffusion, coordinate and simultaneous noise identities derived.

Assumptions and formal scope. Primitive Borel fields, original initial L2, the original native interacting solution and natural mean-field solution are explicit. A1 alone is not asserted to construct an arbitrary interacting system. No final coefficient, law, noise or raw paired-process compatibility is supplied.
