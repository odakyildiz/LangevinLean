Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Derived weak-L2L^2L2 bounds for countably many observation times

Any finite set of earlier observations is internally ordered and the terminal observation appended. The finite-grid Doob bound passes through countable unions, giving the same actual terminal-second-moment bound for every countable family of earlier observation times.

Assumptions and formal scope. No observation ordering, process time continuity or maximal inequality is assumed. This is a countable-observation estimate; extending a martingale to simultaneous continuous sample paths requires a separate construction.

Mathematical role and assumptions
