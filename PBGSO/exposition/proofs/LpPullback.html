Actual mean-field dynamics and laws under probability-space pullback

A measure-preserving map pulls back L2L^2L2 variables by a genuine linear isometry. Primitive lifted maps, Bochner expectations, the complete field and actual integral equations commute with that pullback. The output sampler law is proved unchanged by the actual change of variables.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the displayed measure-preserving map, probability/finite measures where required, complete real Banach coordinates and primitive Lipschitz maps. The optimiser coordinate is unchanged and the common input is actually pulled back. No equality of forces or output laws is supplied as a hypothesis; stochastic adaptation remains separate.
