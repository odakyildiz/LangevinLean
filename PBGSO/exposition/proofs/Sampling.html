Independent-copy empirical gradient variance

The general observation map's actual finite empirical average is integrated on the finite product sampler law. The variance identity and its root mean square version give exactly the one-copy variance divided by NNN, and its square root divided by N\sqrt NN​.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a probability sampler law, positive particle count, and an L2L^2L2 observation into a complete real Hilbert space with compatible Borel structure. Independence is supplied by the product law. Identifying dynamically evolved independent copies with that law and connecting the result to the interacting coupling are separate obligations.

gradient_sampling_variance_of_cloud_law

Mathematical role and assumptions

The same variance identity on any actual probability space carrying a reference cloud with the iid product marginal. This transfers the sampling bound to an interacting/reference joint law without asserting independence of that cloud from the interacting optimiser or particles.
