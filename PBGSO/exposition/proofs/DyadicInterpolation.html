Literal dyadic polygon interpolation

Finite continuous hat sums reproduce endpoint interpolation and bound consecutive polygon levels by actual fine-grid adjacent increments.

Assumptions and formal scope. These are deterministic finite identities and norm bounds on the unit window. The original sampled curve need not be continuous.
