PBGSO overview / PBGSO/ProductLpSections.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed spatial L2L^2L2 sections of raw product fields

Literal raw spatial sections form actual Bochner time-L2L^2L2 curves; their norm, product energy and pointwise realization are proved by an isometric currying construction.

Assumptions and formal scope. Raw joint strong measurability and product square integrability are explicit. The spatial measure is sigma finite and the Hilbert target separable. The field itself determines a countably generated spatial subalgebra, enlarged by a finite-measure cover; actual pullback derives the original section curve. No ambient measure separability, coefficient curve or curve measurability is supplied.
