Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Raw local strong equations derive global moments

Genuine first-hit stops, capped drift variation and actual computed noise give localized spatial moments. The original linear growth gives a localization-independent Volterra bound; Fatou derives original state moments and global coefficient square integrability. The local noise is proved equal to the original global computed integral.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original simultaneous raw equation is driven by the constructed local integral, initial state is L², raw drift is jointly measurable and controlled by the local path energy, and the primitive linear-growth bound has nonnegative constants. No original global moment, stopped equation, coefficient curve, stochastic energy or local-to-global noise identity is supplied.
