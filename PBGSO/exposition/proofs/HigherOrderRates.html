Explicit HOL coefficient and small-step bounds

Elementary exponential remainder estimates bound all seven printed coefficient remainders by explicit multiples of h². They give a quadratic deterministic local budget and an explicit 1+Kh upper bound for the actual map amplification.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume alpha is positive, gamma nonnegative, and 0<h≤1; force Lipschitz constants and the required force and auxiliary bounds are nonnegative where stated. These estimates quantify coefficient cancellation. Stochastic innovation-coupling and reference modulus estimates remain necessary for an overall convergence rate.
