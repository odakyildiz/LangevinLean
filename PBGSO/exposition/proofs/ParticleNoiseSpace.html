Actual independent particle seeds preserve flattened canonical noise

Each particle seed pairs its initial sampler state with an independent canonical Brownian source. Projecting initial states away and flattening particle/spatial noise indices preserves the entire canonical source measure. The actual initial cloud has the iid product marginal and is independent of the entire flattened Brownian source; their full joint product law is derived.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a probability initial sampler law and the displayed finite particle index. No final noise-law identity is supplied. This is a measure-preserving full-source construction; numerical reference equations and their moments/moduli remain separate.
