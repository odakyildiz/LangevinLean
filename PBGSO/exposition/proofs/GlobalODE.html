PBGSO overview / PBGSO/GlobalODE.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed actual ODE trajectories for every real time

Forward and reverse global Picard integral solutions are glued at zero. The fundamental theorem of calculus gives their actual ODE derivatives, including the matched two-sided derivative at zero. Thus every initial state has a genuinely all-real-time trajectory.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a complete real Banach state space and a globally Lipschitz primitive field. Neither trajectory existence nor regularity of the selector in the initial state is a premise. Joint C¹ dependence is subsequently derived by FlowDifferentiability for C¹ fields.
