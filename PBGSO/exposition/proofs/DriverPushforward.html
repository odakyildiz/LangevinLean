Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Valid Brownian Drivers descend through probability maps

Actual finite-dimensional laws, spatial increment independence and future-past independence transport along a measure-preserving map, constructing all target Driver fields.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual probability preservation, target evaluation adaptation, fixed-time AE source/target process identities and target-past comap inclusion are explicit. These are structural input-map conditions, not a supplied target Brownian law or independence claim.
