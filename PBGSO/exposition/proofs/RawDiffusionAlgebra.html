Subtraction and synchronous energy of raw diffusion fields

Spatial sections commute with subtraction outside the actual time null set. The computed raw integrals subtract exactly and have the original synchronous difference weighted isometry.

Assumptions and formal scope. Both raw fields are jointly strongly measurable, adapted and expected time-square integrable. The metric weight is selfadjoint where required; no linearity or stochastic energy conclusion is supplied.
