Raw expected energy with random variation budgets

The original raw equation and actual martingale construct the L2L^2L2 primitive and maximal radius from a random square-integrable total-variation budget, yielding the quadratic balance.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original paths, actual martingale and random L2L^2L2 variation budget are explicit. No time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2) drift, maximal radius or energy conclusion is supplied.

Mathematical role and assumptions

The original raw integral equation derives its exact expected quadratic energy. The finite path variation cap is the one provided by actual stopping; the path radius and primitive L2L^{2}L2 variables are constructed within the proof.
