Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual independent-Gaussian M-MFPO all-iteration bound

Actual Brownian coordinate half-steps construct the Gaussian implementation innovations, their norms and exact physical-input identity. The full-state error is uniform over every iteration.

Assumptions and formal scope. Primitive ParticleForceData, nonnegative friction, the original global integral equation and metric drift dissipativity/coercivity are explicit here. FullyUniformMomentum constructs the process and metric internally.
