Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Continuous adapted diffusion columns belong to the actual integral domain

Compactness and proved dyadic time-L2L^2L2 convergence construct the actual domain element for continuous adapted finite L2L^2L2 columns, including all deterministic interval cuts. Its value is the literal time-cut column function; the completed weighted increment moment follows from the finite Brownian construction.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a primitive actual Driver, norm-continuous finite L2L^2L2 column paths and their current-past AE measurability. Domain membership, grid convergence, a horizon radius and stochastic increment isometry are derived rather than supplied. The minimal source A1 coefficient regularity and actual solution identification remain separate.
