Expectation limits of actual partition pairings

Literal weighted pairings of actual L2L^2L2 states and primitives have the required measurability, integrability and expectation limits.

Assumptions and formal scope. The displayed raw path convergence and genuine L2L^2L2 domination are explicit intermediate hypotheses. Later raw finite-variation factories construct their radii internally.
