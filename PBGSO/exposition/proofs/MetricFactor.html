Constructed positive finite-dimensional metric factors

A positive symmetric metric operator is factored into a Euclidean linear map, with inner-product and norm-comparison identities proved.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the original finite-dimensional real Hilbert space and symmetric positive metric; no factor, square-root operator or coercivity conclusion is supplied.

exists_factor

Mathematical role and assumptions

Every finite-dimensional symmetric nonnegative weight admits an actual finite Euclidean factor. The factor and its squared-norm identity are proved from a positive rank-one decomposition, rather than assumed.

Read this statement with the module assumption note above. The complete proof appears alongside it; the full source preserves every shared variable and typeclass.
