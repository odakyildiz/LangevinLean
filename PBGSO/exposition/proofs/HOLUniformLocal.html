Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Printed HOL coefficients give absolute affine local remainders

The actual parameter-first endpoint-force scheme has an absolute local remainder growing linearly in the input norm. Primitive force bounds also give a global full-drift Lipschitz constant.

Assumptions and formal scope. Separate global joint Lipschitz force conditions and physical coefficient signs suffice. No Lipschitz remainder estimate or numerical contractivity is asserted.
