Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

The full canonical source projected-field spectral criterion

Literal parameter/auxiliary and position/auxiliary Euclidean coordinates construct the actual matrix metric. SPD source D and auxiliary matrices and positive residual c construct both block K matrices, their positivity and all coordinate identities. The exact generalized spectral source rate then proves full joint law monotonicity.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the four source matrices are SPD, literal source coordinate projections, U4 score monotonicity, the two separate U5 inequalities, nonnegative beta, c=beta r-AOpt-ASam>0, constant diffusion and the explicit finite coupling integrability contract. Parameter and position dimensions are nonempty; auxiliary dimensions may be zero. No metric coercivity, coordinate-form identity, K positivity or final spectral comparison is supplied as a premise.
