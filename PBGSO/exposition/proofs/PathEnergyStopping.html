Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Genuine first-hit stopping times from path energy

Continuous monotone adapted scalar path energies yield actual bounded first-hit stops, their energy caps, threshold monotonicity and horizon exhaustion.

Assumptions and formal scope. Continuity, monotonicity and current-past measurability of the actual path energy are explicit. PathwiseLocalEnergy derives them for the literal local coefficient integral. No stopping-time or cap conclusion is assumed.
