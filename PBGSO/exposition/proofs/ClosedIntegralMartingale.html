PBGSO overview / PBGSO/ClosedIntegralMartingale.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Computed closed-domain integrals form actual martingales

Actual finite stopped predictable sums converge on compatible refinements to the computed integral of every literal closed-domain member. Conditional L² projection gives an adapted square-integrable standard martingale representative, with exact per-time equality to the computed L² values.

Assumptions and formal scope. No limiting martingale, convergence or stochastic-isometry premise is supplied. This covers general closed predictable-step integrands; sample-path continuity is not inferred solely from L² convergence.
