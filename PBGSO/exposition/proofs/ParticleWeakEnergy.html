Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Interacting–copy generator on actual finite-cloud quadratic energy

A genuine quadratic form on the paired optimiser/cloud state yields its first and second Fréchet derivatives. The full synchronous generator and actual weak forward equation derive the expected energy derivative, retaining precisely the population-gradient empirical fluctuation.

Assumptions and formal scope. The displayed weak forward equation, probability and moment contracts must be checked for a chosen interacting/copy stochastic implementation. The source strong-SDE-to-generator and independent-copy law identifications are not inferred merely from this generator computation.
