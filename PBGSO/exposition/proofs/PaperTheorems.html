Quadratic coupling geometry and scalar comparison

Let Do,DsD_o,D_sDo​,Ds​ be symmetric bounded quadratic weights. Assume 0<ℓ≤u0<\ell\le u0<ℓ≤u and ℓ∥v∥2≤⟨v,Dv⟩≤u∥v∥2\ell\|v\|^2\le\langle v,Dv\rangle\le u\|v\|^2ℓ∥v∥2≤⟨v,Dv⟩≤u∥v∥2 for each of the two state spaces. The joint dissipativity assumption includes both drift work and the diffusion correction arising from a synchronous stochastic coupling. The scalar comparison results apply once a continuous energy and its right derivative have been justified.

Assumptions and formal scope. The comparison theorems take continuity, right derivative and dissipation inequalities for a supplied scalar energy. WeakContraction separately derives that energy inequality from the actual synchronous weak generator, and LawContraction passes it to Wasserstein distance. Strong-SDE equivalence and generator-domain justification remain separate. Sampling.lean proves the i.i.d. gradient-variance identity.

02Exponential comparison of squared energies §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Suppose EEE is continuous on [0,T][0,T][0,T], has right derivative E′(t)E'(t)E′(t) on [0,T)[0,T)[0,T), and E′(t)≤−2ρE(t)E'(t)\le-2\rho E(t)E′(t)≤−2ρE(t). Then E(t)≤E(0)e−2ρtE(t)\le E(0)e^{-2\rho t}E(t)≤E(0)e−2ρt for 0≤t≤T0\le t\le T0≤t≤T; the real number ρ\rhoρ need not be positive.

Proof. Multiplication by the integrating factor gives (e2ρtE(t))′≤0(e^{2\rho t}E(t))'\le0(e2ρtE(t))′≤0 at each right differentiation point. The right derivative comparison principle, valid for continuous functions, makes this product nonincreasing. Evaluating at zero yields e2ρtE(t)≤E(0)e^{2\rho t}E(t)\le E(0)e2ρtE(t)≤E(0). The exponential is strictly positive, so division gives the stated estimate, including the endpoints by continuity. □\square□

How the Lean proof follows this argument

energy_decay calls le_gronwallBound_of_liminf_deriv_right_le with coefficient -2 * ρ and zero forcing. The derivative hypothesis supplies the required lower limit of right difference quotients through liminf_right_slope_le. The final simplification uses gronwallBound_ε0. No differentiability at the right endpoint or nonnegativity of the energy is required by this particular scalar lemma.
