Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Full source heavy-ball criterion with constructed metric and rate

The exact source friction threshold yields explicit feasible Young, auxiliary, leakage and positive decay constants. The actual block metric is constructed. Primitive joint score monotonicity and joint Lipschitz continuity imply full law-level twisted monotonicity for the actual heavy-ball model.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume positive friction gamma and score monotonicity r, gamma>sqrt(2)L/sqrt(r), the actual joint projected field is L-Lipschitz, and constant sampler diffusion. The optimiser and sampler spaces may differ. Auxiliary dissipation, quadratic metric positivity, rate positivity and coupling-integrability estimates are derived. The criterion does not certify these global hypotheses for experiments or establish strong-SDE existence from them.
