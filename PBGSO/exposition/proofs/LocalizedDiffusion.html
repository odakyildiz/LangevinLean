Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual product-L² columns from first-hit localization

Literal raw columns masked by genuine first-hit stops have actual spatial/time L² sections, past-adaptation and total expected energy bounded by the threshold.

Assumptions and formal scope. The scalar local path energy controls the sum of original column squares and has the displayed integral, continuity, adaptation and pathwise L1 properties. No stopped coefficient curve or stochastic energy budget is supplied.
