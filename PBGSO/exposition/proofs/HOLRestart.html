01Restart existence, agreement and contraction §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Suppose the stated complete-space and global force Lipschitz assumptions hold. For each initial state zzz and restart time aaa, there is a continuous solution

rz(t)=z+∫0tf(rz(s))ds+Ba(t),r_z(t)=z+\int_0^tf(r_z(s))ds+B_a(t),rz​(t)=z+∫0t​f(rz​(s))ds+Ba​(t),

where BaB_aBa​ is the same shifted physical Brownian increment, acting only in the last sampler coordinate. The constructed solution agrees with any original solution restarted from its value at nonnegative time aaa. If a bounded linear map CCC into a real Hilbert space satisfies ⟨C(x−y),C(f(x)−f(y))⟩≤−ρ∥C(x−y)∥2\langle C(x-y),C(f(x)-f(y))\rangle\le-\rho\|C(x-y)\|^2⟨C(x−y),C(f(x)−f(y))⟩≤−ρ∥C(x−y)∥2 for every x,yx,yx,y, then for every t≥0t\ge0t≥0

∥C(rz(t)−rw(t))∥≤e−ρt∥C(z−w)∥.\|C(r_z(t)-r_w(t))\|\le e^{-\rho t}\|C(z-w)\|.∥C(rz​(t)−rw​(t))∥≤e−ρt∥C(z−w)∥.

The real rate ρ\rhoρ need not be positive for this particular statement.

Proof. The force bounds make the entire four-coordinate drift globally Lipschitz. The Brownian input is continuous in the square-integrable norm and starts at zero. Global Picard construction with this continuous additive input gives the integral solution. Subtracting two solutions with the same input leaves an ordinary integral of their drift difference, so Grönwall gives uniqueness. Subtracting the original integral equation at time aaa from that at a+ta+ta+t gives the restart equation, since Brownian increments telescope. Uniqueness proves restart agreement.

For two initial states with the same input the difference δ=rz−rw\delta=r_z-r_wδ=rz​−rw​ is continuously differentiable, with δ′=f(rz)−f(rw)\delta'=f(r_z)-f(r_w)δ′=f(rz​)−f(rw​). The chain rule and the dissipativity assumption yield (∥Cδ∥2)′=2⟨Cδ,Cδ′⟩≤−2ρ∥Cδ∥2(\|C\delta\|^2)'=2\langle C\delta,C\delta'\rangle\le-2\rho\|C\delta\|^2(∥Cδ∥2)′=2⟨Cδ,Cδ′⟩≤−2ρ∥Cδ∥2. Exponential energy comparison followed by the nonnegative square root gives the result. No contraction of the numerical update is assumed. □\square□

How the Lean proof follows this argument

higherOrderRestart chooses the actual solution from exists_higherOrder_restart; higherOrderRestart_equation extracts its integral equation. higherOrderRestart_agrees uses integral shifting and higherOrderRestartInput_increment, then the proved uniqueness result. higherOrderRestart_contracts changes the drift sign to match the shared integral-contraction theorem and applies transformed_additive_integral_contraction. Continuity and both integral identities are supplied by the constructed restart properties. The final simplification substitutes their initial states.
