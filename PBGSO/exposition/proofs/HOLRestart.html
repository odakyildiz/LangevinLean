Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed exact HOL restarts on the actual Brownian source

Global Picard solutions, their original integral equations, physical Brownian increment radii, restart identity and primitive-metric contraction are proved.

Assumptions and formal scope. Separate force Lipschitz conditions and the actual measure-preserving Brownian source suffice for construction; contraction uses primitive drift dissipativity, derived from A3 in the final client.
