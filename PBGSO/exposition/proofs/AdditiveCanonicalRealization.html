Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed additive law trajectories for every initial coupling

Each marginal process is constructed once on initial-law times noise space. Measure-preserving projections pull both onto the actual initial-coupling times noise extension, preserving their output laws. The exact initial pair law and actual quadratic weak equation give optimal optimiser–Wasserstein contraction without a realization premise.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume primitive global Lipschitz maps, autonomous response and constant diffusion compatibility, probability noise space, finite-second-moment initial laws and common continuous sampler input starting at zero. Source A3 supplies the joint metric monotonicity. Marginal tests are empty and the pair domain is exactly the quadratic singleton. The normalized model Brownian input has proved coefficient matching; adaptation, richer marginal Itô equations and frozen-target invariance remain separate.
