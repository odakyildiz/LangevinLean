Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

General closed-domain integral increments and L2L^2L2 continuity

Literal interval cuts and proved subtraction give the exact weighted second moment of every increment of the computed integral. Time-cut continuity derives its actual L2L^2L2-time continuity and zero initial value. PredictableIntegral instantiates these facts for arbitrary time-L2L^2L2 adapted columns.

Assumptions and formal scope. The bounded weight is selfadjoint and the actual primitive Driver is explicit. No diffusion time continuity, stochastic increment isometry or integral-process continuity is assumed. Simultaneous almost-sure continuous paths are separate.
