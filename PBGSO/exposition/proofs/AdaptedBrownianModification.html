An actual globally continuous adapted Brownian modification

The explicit countable-horizon past-series process has the original finite-dimensional Brownian laws and actual continuous paths, and is strictly adapted to the original filtration.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a probability source, actual pre-Brownian laws and original adaptation where requested. The construction proves the continuous modification; original raw paths are not claimed continuous.
