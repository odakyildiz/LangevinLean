PBGSO overview / PBGSO/RawLocalPullbackLocal.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Local-noise transport without global coefficient moments

First-hit stops pull back literally; actual stopped integrals obey proved probability transport and their continuous exhausted limits give one event of complete-horizon local noise equality.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only samplewise local square integrability of the original columns and actual measure-preserving, valid Driver transport enter. No global expected diffusion moment or supplied local-noise matching conclusion is required.
