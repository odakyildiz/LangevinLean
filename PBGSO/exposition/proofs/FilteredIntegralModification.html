Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Computed integrals are invariant under Brownian modification

Fixed-time almost-everywhere equality of valid Driver coordinates proves equality of actual finite sums, closed-domain integrals, observation cuts and raw predictable-curve integrals.

Assumptions and formal scope. Both Drivers must satisfy their primitive laws and independence contracts. The target filtration may enlarge the original; raw path equality and stochastic-output equality are not assumed.
