Skew Gibbs double-divergence cancellation

A finite skew array has zero double sum. Symmetry of second Fréchet derivatives then proves the exact local cancellation of the skew Gibbs correction Qe−HQe^{-H}Qe−H.

Assumptions and formal scope. The coefficient functions and HHH are C2C^2C2, and QQQ is pointwise skew-symmetric. Normalisation, integration by parts, boundary conditions, nonexplosion and the stationary-PDE-to-invariant-process bridge are separate analytic requirements.

Mathematical role and assumptions
