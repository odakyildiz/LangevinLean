Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Original raw coercivity derives global second moments

Genuine stopped equations, exact local energy and Fatou derive a uniform original second-moment budget and product-time L2 state integrability from one-sided radial work control and bounded diffusion energy.

Assumptions and formal scope. The actual raw original local equation, positive metric coercivity, initial L2 and pointwise Lyapunov/diffusion constants are explicit. No global drift moment, state moment, expected energy identity or final Fubini condition is supplied.
