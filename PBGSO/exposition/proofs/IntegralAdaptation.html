Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Adaptation derived from actual global Lipschitz integral equations

Picard is constructed inside each complete invariant past-measurable L2L^2L2 subspace. Actual common-input uniqueness identifies that trajectory with the selected original solution, proving current-past measurability. This applies to pointwise lifted particle drifts and the complete nonlinear deterministic-optimiser/L2L^2L2-sampler mean-field field. Actual Driver Brownian inputs are proved adapted.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume primitive globally Lipschitz maps, actual continuous integral equations and current-past measurable initial variable and additive input. No solution adaptedness conclusion is assumed and no conditional projection is claimed to commute with a nonlinear drift. A strongly adapted representative is constructed with a.e. equality at each time; simultaneous pathwise continuity is not established by this theorem alone.
