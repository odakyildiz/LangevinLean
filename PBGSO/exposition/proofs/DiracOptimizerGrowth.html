The explicit finite-work convention controls Dirac optimizer growth

Source A3 on one-point empirical laws and universal finite signed coupling work derive quadratic weighted Dirac-optimizer growth and compact-uniform positive sampler-work bounds.

Assumptions and formal scope. FiniteCouplingWork independently requires actual signed sampler work integrable for every joint probability law with finite quadratic weight. It is not inferred from conditional TwistedJointMonotonicity, and no optimizer Lipschitz or positive-work conclusion is assumed.
