Exact generalized minimum-eigenvalue quadratic comparison

Spectral diagonalization proves the lower bound at the actual finite minimum eigenvalue. Positive definiteness of DDD and KKK gives positivity of the whitened matrix D−1/2KD−1/2D^{-1/2}KD^{-1/2}D−1/2KD−1/2, and congruence converts its spectral estimate into K−ρDK-\rho DK−ρD positive semidefinite.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume finite nonempty real matrix coordinates and genuine positive-definite DDD and KKK. The positive square root, its invertibility and the identity between inverse square root and square root of the inverse are proved. The generalized minimum is the actual smallest eigenvalue, not a supplied scalar quadratic comparison constant.
