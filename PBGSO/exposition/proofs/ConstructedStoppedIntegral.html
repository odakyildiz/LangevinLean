Bounded stopping without a Brownian continuity premise

The proved continuous Driver modification and original-integral invariance remove the path-continuity premise from bounded stopped martingale realization and original-column energy.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only the primitive Driver, actual closed integrand and genuine bounded stopping events are supplied. No noise version, martingale, random-time realization or stochastic-isometry conclusion is assumed.

exists_constructed_bounded_stopped_martingale

Mathematical role and assumptions

Every original Brownian driver admits actual continuous original and stopped martingales, with simultaneous literal stopping and exact stopped physical-column energy. No Brownian path-continuity, noise, martingale, isometry, uniform-integrability or stochastic-limit premise remains.

Read this statement with the module assumption note above. The complete proof appears alongside it; the full source preserves every shared variable and typeclass.
