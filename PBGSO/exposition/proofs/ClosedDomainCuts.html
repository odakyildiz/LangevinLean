Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Literal closed predictable-step domains are stable under interval cuts

Finite adapted step coefficients have actual adapted interval restrictions. General predictable density and continuous mask approximation then pass the restriction through the literal closure, constructing closedCutDomain with its exact time-cut value.

Assumptions and formal scope. No extra adaptedness, time continuity or approximation sequence is required of the closed-domain member. These are deterministic domain identities used to construct one consistent stochastic integral process.
