Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual full-vector stochastic integral on represented time-L2L^2L2 columns

The actual Driver predictable sums complete in L2L^2L2 from compatible refining grids of genuine adapted random coefficient columns. The column space is the real Hilbert finite L2L^2L2 product; exact cross-grid distance gives the actual integral, convergence and isometry.

Assumptions and formal scope. IntegralDomain consists of actual time-L2L^2L2 limits of adapted step columns on the stated compatible grids. Primitive Driver properties replace any assumed stochastic-integral isometry. FilteredContinuousIntegrands derives domain membership for continuous adapted columns rather than asking the application to supply an approximation.
