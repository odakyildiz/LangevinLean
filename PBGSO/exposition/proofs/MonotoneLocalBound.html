Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual finite-dimensional monotonicity gives compact bounds

Finite test vertices bound every component of an everywhere-defined monotone field. A finite open cover derives uniform auxiliary-variable growth on compact parameter sets.

Assumptions and formal scope. Finite dimensionality, an actual orthonormal basis and the displayed pointwise monotonicity/growth data are explicit. No continuity, local boundedness or compact-uniform conclusion is assumed.
