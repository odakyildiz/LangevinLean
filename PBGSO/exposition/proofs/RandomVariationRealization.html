L2L^2L2 primitives under random square-integrable variation

A genuine random L2L^2L2 variation budget constructs spatial L2L^2L2 drift primitives and exact raw integral realizations.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only raw joint measurability, pathwise Bochner L1L^1L1 and domination of actual total variation by the given L2L^2L2 budget are supplied. No spatial L2L^2L2 drift or final primitive membership is assumed.
