Whole-noise independence from finite initial observations

Actual projective measure uniqueness proves the full original initial/path product law from independence of every finite noise observation.

Assumptions and formal scope. The time type is nonempty, original evaluations and initialization are measurable, and finite-dimensional initialization/noise independence is explicit. DriverInitialIndependence derives it for actual time-zero past variables.
