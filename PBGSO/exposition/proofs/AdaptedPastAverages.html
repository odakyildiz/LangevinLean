Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Past Bochner averaging preserves actual adaptation

Actual conditional L2L^2L2 projection fixes all past-measurable variables. Commuting this continuous linear projection with the past Bochner integral proves current-past measurability of moving means, without commuting it through a nonlinear coefficient.

Assumptions and formal scope. The curve is locally Bochner integrable and its AE-time past-measurability condition is explicit. Actual pointwise adaptation and zero values before time zero derive that condition. No density or stochastic isometry conclusion is assumed.
