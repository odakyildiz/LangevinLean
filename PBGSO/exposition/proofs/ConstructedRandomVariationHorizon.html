Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Weak quadratic balance at every original subhorizon

The original computed Brownian horizon remains fixed while actual raw quadratic balance is proved at each earlier observation time.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original random-L² variation budget, raw equations, measurability and adapted square diffusion are explicit. No noise-horizon compatibility, martingale or energy conclusion is supplied.
