Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Simultaneous pathwise integral equations for measurable drift

Joint L1L^1L1 representatives and Fubini construct one continuous sampler version satisfying the original nonlinear drift integral equation simultaneously at all horizon times, requiring only joint strong measurability of the field.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual original L2L^2L2 equations, time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2) drift, and a joint continuous raw input version are explicit. Pathwise drift integrability is derived; the selected sampler is asserted AE adapted per time rather than strictly adapted on every sample point.

Mathematical role and assumptions

Construct one jointly strongly measurable continuous path version of an actual L2L^{2}L2 integral process, with its literal original nonlinear drift equation. All pathwise assertions hold on one spatial full-measure set for every time in the finite horizon.
