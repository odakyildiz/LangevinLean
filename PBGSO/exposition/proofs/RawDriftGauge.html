Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Original pathwise drift gains a genuine local variation gauge

A common null augmentation normalizes the original almost-surely locally integrable drift and adds its actual norm to the local energy, deriving controlled original raw data.

Assumptions and formal scope. Joint drift measurability, a.s. pathwise L1, progressive norm and the original local equation are explicit. No expected variation budget, global drift moment, noise match or energy conclusion enters.

exists_data

Mathematical role and assumptions

A natural original raw equation produces the controlled localization data. Only an a.s. ordinary drift integral is required, with no spatial L² drift or expected variation assumption. The returned raw state is literal.

Read this statement with the module assumption note above. The complete proof appears alongside it; the full source preserves every shared variable and typeclass.
