Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual domination of finite-variation energy sums

Original drift total variation and actual state/noise radii bound the finite-partition work, quadratic and cross sums, supplying explicit localization domination estimates.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual raw pathwise Bochner-L1 drift and the displayed path radii are explicit. No time-L1(L2) drift or dominated-convergence conclusion is assumed; localized stochastic expectation limits remain separate.

Mathematical role and assumptions
