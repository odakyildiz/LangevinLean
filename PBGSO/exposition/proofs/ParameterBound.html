Actual parameter L² bound from stationary and particle weak laws

The target constant weak solution is built from frozen sampler stationarity, zero mean observation and the optimiser fixed point. Its optimal transport relaxation combines with the actual generator particle energy and an L² triangle estimate for the random optimiser and parameter projection.

Assumptions and formal scope. The mean-field and particle/copy weak equations, quadratic test domains, same-marginal synchronous realizations for every initial target coupling, actual iid reference marginal, response/variance and measurability contracts, zero initial particle energy and one-Lipschitz parameter projection are explicit. Strong-SDE realization and original-target model identification remain implementation obligations.
