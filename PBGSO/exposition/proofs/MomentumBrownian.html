Actual M-MFPO Gaussian half increments and Brownian coupling

Normalized actual Brownian half increments have exact d\sqrt dd​ L2L^2L2 norms. With independent coordinate grid paths, the whole innovation sequence has the iid standard multivariate Gaussian law. The sum of adjacent half increments is the scaled full Brownian increment, and its thermostat mismatch is bounded explicitly.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a probability measure, finite spatial index, real coordinate pre-Brownian processes, positive step size and nonnegative friction. The joint Gaussian path law additionally assumes coordinate grid-path independence; marginal Brownianity alone is not substituted for it. Reference SDE existence and its moment bounds are separate from these proved innovation facts.
