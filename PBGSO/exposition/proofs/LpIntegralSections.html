Actual Bochner L² integration agrees with joint pathwise integration

Finite-set integration is constructed as a bounded Hilbert linear map. Fubini and equality of all finite-set integrals identify the actual L² Bochner integral with the spatial section integral of every known joint realization. Local product integrability is derived internally.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a Bochner L¹ family of L² variables, a jointly AE strongly measurable realization and AE-time exact sections. No product-integrability premise is needed. LpJointRepresentative constructs such a joint realization on finite time/probability spaces.
