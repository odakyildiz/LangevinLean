Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Original linear-growth Model data are constructed

Original coefficient identities transfer the derived raw state and coefficient moments to the literal Model. The actual model integral equation, predictable Model Data and exact original sampler laws follow.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original local strong equation and primitive linear growth come from RawLinearGrowthState. Actual optimizer ODE/integral data, observation domain, raw coefficient measurability and coefficient realizations on one full-probability event are explicit. No stronger Data, global moment, noise compatibility or output-law premise is supplied.
