Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual law uniqueness across distinct Brownian sources

A proved initial/Wiener-path map and source pathwise uniqueness identify the optimizer and raw sampler laws of solutions on distinct original probability spaces.

Assumptions and formal scope. One canonical full-system strong-solution witness with the same initial law and source same-driver uniqueness are explicit A1 instances. No final output-law equality, common-source frozen solution or global coefficient moments are assumed.

law_unique_two_sources

Mathematical role and assumptions

A1 uniqueness on two original sources and existence on their common initial-law/Wiener-path source imply actual optimizer and marginal-law uniqueness. No common-space output coupling or output-law match is assumed.

Read this statement with the module assumption note above. The complete proof appears alongside it; the full source preserves every shared variable and typeclass.
