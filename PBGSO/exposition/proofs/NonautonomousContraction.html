Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Time-dependent drift contraction for actual integral trajectories

Common additive input cancels for an arbitrary time-dependent drift. The difference derivative is derived from the two actual integral equations, then transformed joint monotonicity gives the exponential energy and norm estimates.

Assumptions and formal scope. Supply continuous trajectories, continuity of each time-dependent drift along them, the actual common-input integral equations, a continuous linear map into a Hilbert space and the uniform transformed monotonicity inequality. Existence of solutions for arbitrary time-dependent maps and state-dependent Itô noise is not proved here.
