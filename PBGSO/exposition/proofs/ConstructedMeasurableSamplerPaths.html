Computed original sampler paths for measurable drift

The actual completed noise and Fubini path construction give one jointly measurable continuous sampler version, with the original nonlinear drift equation on one probability-one horizon event.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual computed-integral Data, coordinate Brownian continuity and horizon joint strong measurability of the original drift are explicit. Pathwise drift integrability and per-time AE adaptation are proved.

Mathematical role and assumptions

The original computed noise has a joint continuous realization. No raw noise version, matching law or continuous modification is supplied as input.
