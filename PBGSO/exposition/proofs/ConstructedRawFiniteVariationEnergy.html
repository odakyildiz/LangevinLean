Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Computed-noise quadratic balance under capped variation

The original raw computed diffusion integral derives the martingale, spatial noise moments, path radius and exact original-column weighted quadratic balance.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual raw drift pathwise L1L^1L1 and variation cap, original state/noise equation, joint coefficient measurability and product square-integrable adapted diffusion are explicit. No martingale, isometry, radius or time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2) drift is supplied.
