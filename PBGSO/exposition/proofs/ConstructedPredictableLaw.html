Optimal actual model law contraction with merely time-L2L^2L2 diffusion

Two actual canonical model trajectories with arbitrary parameter- and state-dependent adapted diffusion are aligned on every genuine initial coupling. Computed-integral transport, full joint filtration, energy balance and the actual Wasserstein infimum prove the source metric contraction factor with the true initial-law distance.

Assumptions and formal scope. Drift is Bochner L1L^1L1-time L2L^2L2 and diffusion is adapted time-L2L^2L2; actual original integral equations, coefficient realizations, initial variables and source A3 are explicit. No time continuity of diffusion, martingale, isometry, output-law equality, all-couplings realization or final energy premise remains. Identifying the paper's entire minimal A1 strong-solution class is still separate.
