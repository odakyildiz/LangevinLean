Actual adapted martingale representatives of timewise L² limits

Hilbert conditional projection is a continuous map on actual L² variables. Its martingale identity passes to every timewise L² limit; conditional expectations provide adapted representatives of the limit and prove their standard martingale property.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a filtration, actual approximating square-integrable martingales and timewise L² convergence. No limiting adaptedness or conditional-expectation identity is supplied as a premise. Constructing a consistent stochastic-integral process from these limits remains a separate step.
