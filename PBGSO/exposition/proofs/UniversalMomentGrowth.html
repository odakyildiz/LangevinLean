Universal finite-moment integrability forces pointwise growth

A genuine countable atomic probability construction proves that integrability of a field against every law with finite given weight implies a uniform pointwise weighted norm bound.

Assumptions and formal scope. The weight is at least one and the universal integrability quantifier is explicit. No growth bound, source energy inequality or solution regularity is supplied.
