PBGSO overview / PBGSO/BrownianGridIsometry.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual predictable Brownian sums on compatible time grids

All deterministic time grids use the same actual scalar Brownian path and its full natural past. Adapted square-integrable random coefficients yield actual martingale sums and exact weighted isometry. Arbitrary compatible subdivision preserves the original sum pathwise.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume actual scalar pre-Brownian laws, measurable evaluations, monotone deterministic grids and coefficients measurable in the original Brownian past with finite second moments. Coefficient independence is derived from the increment/past law, not assumed. Continuous completion and general diffusion membership are separate.
