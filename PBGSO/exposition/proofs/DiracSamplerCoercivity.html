Dirac A3 gives primitive compact drift anchors and radial coercivity

The actual weighted full Dirac field is monotone. Finite-dimensional local boundedness yields compact optimizer drift-anchor bounds, and frozen dissipation derives a uniform radial Lyapunov work bound.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual source A3, nonnegative rate and the displayed metric/projections are explicit. Universal finite work, drift norm growth, global state moments and a Lyapunov conclusion are not supplied.
