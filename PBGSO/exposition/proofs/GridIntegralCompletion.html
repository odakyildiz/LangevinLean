Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual L2L^2L2 stochastic integral on the represented step-function domain

Actual adapted Brownian sums on compatible refining grids satisfy an exact cross-grid distance identity. Cauchy convergence of the literal time-L2L^2L2 coefficients constructs an actual L2L^2L2 stochastic-integral limit and proves its norm and isometry. Dyadic grids are explicitly constructed.

Assumptions and formal scope. IntegralDomain consists precisely of time-L2L^2L2 limits represented by the actual adapted step approximations in the signature. Scalar pre-Brownian laws and measurable evaluations are explicit. Density, membership of arbitrary continuous diffusion processes, timewise martingale consistency and a full state-dependent SDE are not claimed by this completion alone.
