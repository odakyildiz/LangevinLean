Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Computed raw Brownian integration commutes with state maps

Actual finite predictable coefficients and Brownian sums, literal closed predictable domains, time cuts and raw spatial sections commute with every given bounded linear state map.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original raw field is jointly strongly measurable, adapted and square integrable on the finite horizon. Transformed measurability, square integrability and adaptation are derived. No matching coefficient curve, stochastic output or integral identity is assumed.
