Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed global additive processes and actual quadratic weak couplings

Finite Picard solutions are glued by proved uniqueness to give a global norm-continuous integral process. The optimiser ODE, actual marginal laws, second moments and observation integrability follow. Common-input cancellation derives the actual pair quadratic forward equation.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume primitive global Lipschitz maps, a common continuous sampler input starting at zero and explicit autonomous/constant-diffusion model compatibility. Marginal tests are exactly empty and the pair domain is the singleton quadratic separation test: this does not prove arbitrary marginal Itô/Fokker–Planck equations. AdditiveDiffusion constructs the correctly scaled model Brownian input; adaptation and richer generator domains remain separate.
