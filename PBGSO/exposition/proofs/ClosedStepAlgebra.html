Actual closed-domain addition and integral subtraction

Finite adapted steps are placed on a common exact refinement. Their literal coefficient embeddings and actual Brownian sums give genuine addition and subtraction identities. Closure and continuity prove domain subtraction and subtraction of the computed integral.

Assumptions and formal scope. No linearity conclusion is assumed of the completion. The initial Drivers and finite coefficient square-integrability/adaptation provide the primitive stochastic data.
