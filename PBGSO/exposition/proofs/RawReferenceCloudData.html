Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

The actual independent physical cloud solves its original local equation

One natural mean-field sampler constructs the full independently seeded physical cloud, its literal original local SDE and controlled raw representation.

Assumptions and formal scope. Primitive Borel projected drift and original one-copy raw equation are inputs. Product Driver, joint continuous representative, physical columns, coefficient/noise and state identities are proved. No supplied iid law or cloud equation is assumed.
