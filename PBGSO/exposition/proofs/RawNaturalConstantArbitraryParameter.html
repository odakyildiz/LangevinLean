The exact source4 parameter factor on arbitrary original sources

Actual cross-source law transfer, natural target relaxation with genuine initial Wasserstein distance and the raw particle floor derive the original random parameter bound.

Assumptions and formal scope. Natural original reference/target equations, A1 witness/uniqueness, A2 stationarity, A3/A4/A5 and actual physical particle/copy raw Data remain visible. No final law/energy/work or weak-coupling conclusion is assumed; the natural physical particle pairing is its separate input layer.
