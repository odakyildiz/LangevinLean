Genuine initial Wasserstein contraction under finite signed work

Every actual initial coupling receives natural canonical original trajectories and the same Brownian Driver; proved original equations and laws give the infimum contraction.

Assumptions and formal scope. Canonical actual strong-solution instances, constant diffusion, A3 and the unconditional finite signed-work convention remain explicit. Realisation, marginal laws, stochastic matching, positive work, moments and final transport estimates are derived.
