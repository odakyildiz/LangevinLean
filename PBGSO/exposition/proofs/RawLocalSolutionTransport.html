PBGSO overview / PBGSO/RawLocalSolutionTransport.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Original raw strong equations preserve their actual laws

Original sampler and optimizer local equations transport to valid common Brownian spaces with all output marginals derived. Same-driver uniqueness then gives actual cross-space solution-law equality and stationary-law transfer.

Assumptions and formal scope. The actual measure-preserving initial/noise map and valid past/evaluation compatibility are explicit. No global coefficient moment, noise matching conclusion, output-law equality or time-L1L^1L1(L2L^2L2) drift is supplied.
