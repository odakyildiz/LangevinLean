Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

HOL-MYPGD coefficients and endpoint-quadrature error

The seven coefficients and parameter-first update follow the appendix. The optimiser force and smoothed sampler force remain independent. The exact affine response to a force perturbation and the Lipschitz endpoint quadrature defect are proved.

Assumptions and formal scope. The quadrature bound assumes nonnegative step size and a uniform Lipschitz sampler force in its spatial argument. Both updates use the same innovation triple. It compares endpoint quadrature with exact line quadrature in the same affine scheme, not with the exact SDE. Gaussian covariance validity and smoothing-target bias are separate.
