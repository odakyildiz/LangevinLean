Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Original constant-diffusion contraction under finite signed A3 work

Natural original local equations, Dirac coercivity and universal finite signed work derive original moments, positive-work integrability, Fubini and exact realised RMS and pair-law contraction.

Assumptions and formal scope. FiniteCouplingWork is independent of the conditional inequality and is the stated ordinary finite-integral interpretation. Original local equations, constant columns, positive A3 rate and primitive Borel fields are explicit. No A4, response continuity, norm growth, global moment, work estimate or energy conclusion is supplied.
