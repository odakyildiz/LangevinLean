Continuous adapted approximants for general diffusion columns

Finite Hilbert columns of actual L² variables are averaged over the past. Each pointwise moving mean is proved continuous and adapted. It therefore gives a concrete represented integral-domain entry and a literal closed-domain element.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original columns need only time-L² membership and AE-time past adaptation. The exact identity with the time-L² translation average is supplied by LpIntegralSections; PredictableStepDensity then proves convergence and general domain membership.
