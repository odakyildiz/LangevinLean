Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Both proposed methods as actual general Hilbert-state models

Independent primitive Lipschitz forces construct the actual M-MFPO heavy-ball optimiser/underdamped sampler and the HOL three-coordinate sampler model. All primitive mean-field Lipschitz data and model compatibility are proved, with genuine Hilbert products. The original raw finite-empirical drifts intertwine exactly with the model drifts.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume complete real Hilbert optimiser/sampler coordinates, separate global sum-Lipschitz bounds for F and G and the displayed friction coefficients. Finite constant diffusion columns are supplied. No common potential, equal optimiser/sampler dimensions, unrelated model compatibility equality or final error bound is a premise.
