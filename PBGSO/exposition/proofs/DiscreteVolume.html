Signed Jacobian of the original B-A-B implementation

The actual kick and drift Fréchet derivatives are triangular linear shears. Their determinants are computed in a product basis, and the derivative chain rule proves determinant exactly one for the complete original interwoven B-A-B map.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a finite-dimensional complete real Hilbert position space and globally differentiable coupled force. The step and initial state are arbitrary. No joint potential, assumed Jacobian identity or C² force is required. Friction and stochastic thermostats are excluded, as in the source volume lemma.
