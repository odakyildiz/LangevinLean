The signed Jacobian of the discrete splitting

For a differentiable force in finite dimension, the derivatives of the kick and drift are triangular shear matrices. Their diagonal blocks are identities, so the determinant is exactly one, including its sign. The chain rule preserves this value for the full B–A–B map.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a finite-dimensional complete real Hilbert position space and globally differentiable coupled force. The step and initial state are arbitrary. No joint potential, assumed Jacobian identity or C2C^2C2 force is required. Friction and stochastic thermostats are excluded, as in the source volume lemma.

01Triangular derivatives and composition §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let QQQ be a finite-dimensional complete real Hilbert space with its Borel, second-countable structure, and let F:Q→QF:Q\to QF:Q→Q be differentiable everywhere. For every h∈Rh\in\mathbb Rh∈R and z∈Q×Qz\in Q\times Qz∈Q×Q, the B–A–B map has det⁡D(Kh/2∘Ah∘Kh/2)(z)=1\det D(K_{h/2}\circ A_h\circ K_{h/2})(z)=1detD(Kh/2​∘Ah​∘Kh/2​)(z)=1.

Proof. With velocity-first coordinates,

DKh(p,q)=(I−hDF(q)0I),DAh(p,q)=(I0hII).DK_h(p,q)=\begin{pmatrix}I&-hDF(q)\\0&I\end{pmatrix},\qquad DA_h(p,q)=\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\hI&I\end{pmatrix}.DKh​(p,q)=(I0​−hDF(q)I​),DAh​(p,q)=(IhI​0I​).

These expressions follow by differentiating each coordinate: the kick differentiates p−hF(q)p-hF(q)p−hF(q) and leaves qqq fixed, while the drift leaves ppp fixed and differentiates q+hpq+hpq+hp. In a product basis the matrices are block triangular. Their determinants are the products of the diagonal-block determinants, hence one, also in zero dimension. The derivative chain rule expresses the derivative of the three-stage map as the product of these three derivatives evaluated at their successive inputs. Multiplicativity of determinant gives 1⋅1⋅1=11\cdot1\cdot1=11⋅1⋅1=1. The force need not be conservative or twice differentiable. □\square□

How the Lean proof follows this argument

interwovenBAB_fderiv_det_one is the public complete-map theorem and invokes DiscreteVolume.verletStep_fderiv_det_one. Inside the namespace, upperShear_det and lowerShear_det represent the linear maps in a product basis and use the block-triangular determinant formulas. kickStep_hasFDerivAt and driftStep_hasFDerivAt compute the actual derivatives. fderiv_det_comp applies the derivative and determinant composition laws; the Verlet proof uses it twice and rewrites all three factors to one.
