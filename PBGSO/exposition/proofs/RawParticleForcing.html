Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

The original iid reference gives sharp damped-state forcing

Actual original reference-cloud variance controls residual work against any square-integrable damped vector, deriving the exact variance/N forcing.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only the original reference cloud has its iid population marginal. No independence of interacting particles or iid law for artificially damped reference coordinates is assumed. Response Lipschitz and original observation square integrability/variance are explicit.
