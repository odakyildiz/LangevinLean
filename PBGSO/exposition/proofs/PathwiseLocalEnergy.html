Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Literal local coefficient energy is continuous and adapted

The actual time integral of raw nonnegative local energy is continuous and monotone, and progressive windows prove its current-past measurability. First-hit properties follow for this literal energy.

Assumptions and formal scope. Raw joint measurability, nonnegativity, pathwise time-L1L^1L1 and actual progressive window measurability are explicit. No preexisting continuous adapted energy or stopping budget is supplied.
