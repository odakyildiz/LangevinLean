Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Canonical arbitrary-initial-coupling Brownian alignment

Every actual initial coupling carries a common full-vector Brownian source. Both pulled-back marginal natural filtrations are contained in the genuine full joint initial/past filtration. Computed marginal stochastic integrals agree on this common filtration and retain their original marginal laws.

Assumptions and formal scope. No independence between the two initial coordinates is required. The probability initial laws, actual coupling and finite noise coordinates are explicit. Full Driver properties, integral commutation and output-law identities are derived.
