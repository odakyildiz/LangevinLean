Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Stopping the actual raw stochastic integral

Original raw coefficient masks have exactly the completed stopped domain. Genuine bounded stopping and continuous computed versions then give one event of simultaneous original-noise stopping identities.

Assumptions and formal scope. Actual bounded stopping events, original product-L2L^2L2 adapted columns and computed continuous versions are explicit. No random-time output equality, stopped-domain identity or stopped SDE is supplied.
