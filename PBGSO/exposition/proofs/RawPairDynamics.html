Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual original local equations pair on the common Brownian source

The paired physical diffusion and genuine local gauge are constructed. Actual state projections of the computed local integral derive both original noise equations and their paired raw realization.

Assumptions and formal scope. Two actual original controlled local equations on the same finite Brownian Driver are inputs. No global coefficient moments, matching paired noise/output law or final energy is assumed.
