Continuous adapted Brownian modification on one horizon

The actual strict-past series defines an adapted candidate. Gaussian dyadic estimates prove its continuous paths and convergence in probability identifies it with every original fixed-time Brownian variable.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume genuine pre-Brownian laws, positive finite horizon and, for adaptation, the original process adapted to its stated filtration. No completed-filtration or raw path-continuity assumption is needed.
