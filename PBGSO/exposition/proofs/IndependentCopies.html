Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual iid reference cloud and independent complete trajectories

One complete seed per copy is sampled under a finite product law. Applying the same actual measurable one-copy variable proves the fixed-time cloud has the exact iid marginal law. The same construction proves independence of entire coordinate trajectories and genuine isometric L2L^2L2 pullbacks along the seed projections.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a probability seed law, finite copy index and actual measurable one-copy variables or measurable trajectory evaluations. Interacting particles are not asserted independent. The construction must still be identified with the intended reference equations and shared-driver interacting/copy system before discharging that full process contract.
