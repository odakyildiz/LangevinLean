Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual completed integrals agree under valid filtration enlargement

The same primitive Brownian process may be viewed in two valid Driver filtrations ordered by inclusion. Actual finite predictable sums agree and their literal coefficient values are unchanged. Completion then proves equality of the computed integrals, including continuous-input processes.

Assumptions and formal scope. Both filtrations must satisfy the genuine Driver future-past independence contract. Arbitrary anticipating enlargement is not asserted. Neither an integral-equality nor a final output-law premise is supplied.
