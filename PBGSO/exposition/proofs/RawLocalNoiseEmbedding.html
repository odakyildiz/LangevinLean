Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual local noise is unchanged by zero-padded coordinate inclusion

The original local diffusion columns embed through a genuine finite noise-index injection. Exact stopped computed integrals and actual gauge exhaustion derive one event of all local-noise identities.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only original local diffusion, the index injection and actual Driver coordinate identities are explicit. No global coefficient moment, noise-output matching or final stochastic equation is supplied.
