Constructed constant diffusion operator with exact Brownian scaling

A finite family of arbitrary diffusion columns gives an actual continuous linear map from Euclidean noise. Its normalized map exactly matches the 2\sqrt22​-scaled Brownian input constructor, so every constant-diffusion compatible model has the required columns.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume finite noise coordinates and the displayed real Hilbert sampler structure. The Brownian identity uses the supplied coordinate pre-Brownian inputs. No matrix-matching identity is a premise for the constructed normalized operator. General multiplicative Itô integration and coordinate independence remain separate.
