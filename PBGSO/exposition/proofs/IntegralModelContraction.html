Source A3 contracts actual integral trajectories with integrable coefficients

Actual optimiser integral equations, time-L¹ L² drift, time-L² diffusion columns and a genuine residual martingale give the energy balance. Literal pointwise model coefficient identities and actual joint-law pullback derive dissipation from source A3, proving energy, joint-cost RMS and marginal Wasserstein contraction.

Assumptions and formal scope. The trajectory contains original integral equations and coefficient realization, not an energy bound. Actual stochastic weighted increment isometry remains explicit at this general interface; continuous adapted coefficients have a separately constructed realization. No continuous coefficient or pointwise optimiser derivative is needed. General source A1 realization and all-initial-coupling law coherence remain separate.
