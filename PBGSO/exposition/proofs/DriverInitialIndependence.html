Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Time-zero information is independent of finite Brownian histories

The actual initial variable and all finite joint-vector Brownian evaluations have a derived independent law, including an AE time-zero measurability version.

Assumptions and formal scope. The initial variable is strongly or AE strongly measurable in the original time-zero filtration. No initial/noise independence or finite-array product-law conclusion is supplied.
