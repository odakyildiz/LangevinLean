Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Numerical metric comparisons from exact shared L2L^2L2 coordinate identities

The physical particle norm squared equals the sum of all four raw component second moments. The actual coordinate maps have norm at most two, and their inverses at most one; source A3 therefore constructs a contracting factor with explicit square-root metric bounds.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only the standard Euclidean cloud encoding and primitive finite-dimensional source metric/A3 are used. The comparison identities require no independence of the shared coordinates. No operator-norm, numerical-contraction or moment estimate is assumed.
