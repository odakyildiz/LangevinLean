Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Scalar completed Brownian integrals retain actual weighted isometry

The finite weighted moment identity passes to the actual L2L^2L2 stochastic-integral limit for every bounded symmetric metric weight. Actual represented-domain subtraction gives integral subtraction, and literal interval cuts identify increment energies.

Assumptions and formal scope. The scalar construction uses the actual adapted-step approximation domain and explicitly identified deterministic interval integrands. Stochastic weighted isometry is proved, not supplied. Membership of arbitrary diffusion processes and a full SDE are not concluded by this scalar theorem alone.
