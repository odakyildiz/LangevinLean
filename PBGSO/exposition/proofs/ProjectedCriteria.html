Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Projected primitive assumptions imply actual law-level monotonicity

Coupling marginal identities turn the two force pairings into the monotone projected score work. Separate auxiliary dissipation and constant diffusion then prove full twisted joint monotonicity with an explicit certified rate.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume pointwise U4, the separate U5 optimiser/sampler inequalities, constant diffusion, continuous linear coordinate projections with their model equalities and orthogonal norm splits, positive auxiliary coercivity and a positive residual projected rate. The relevant coupling score and auxiliary squares must be integrable. The certified rate uses min⁡{c,mlower,vlower}/Dupper\min\{c,m_{\rm lower},v_{\rm lower}\}/D_{\rm upper}min{c,mlower​,vlower​}/Dupper​; exact generalized-eigenvalue identification is not proved.
