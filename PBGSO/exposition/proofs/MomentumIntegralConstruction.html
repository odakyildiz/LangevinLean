Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual kinetic references and mesh-uniform regularity are constructed

The actual global interacting source integral process is transported to its original momentum/position coordinates. Compactness and Brownian increments derive all finite-horizon force, momentum and square-root modulus constants, and exact segmentation constructs compatible references on every admissible mesh.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume primitive separate force data, actual continuous full interacting integral process, its physical Brownian equation, canonical Euclidean particle encoding and nonnegative friction. No supplied reference, force/momentum bound, modulus or output coordinate identity remains. FullyConstructedMomentum also constructs the source process itself.
