Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual martingale energy balance with time-integrable coefficients

Exact expected quadratic increments are proved from the actual drift residual martingale and weighted diffusion-increment isometry. Bochner L1L^1L1-time L2L^2L2 drift and time-L2L^2L2 diffusion suffice for the energy balance and almost-everywhere integral Grönwall estimate, without continuous coefficients or an assumed energy derivative.

Assumptions and formal scope. The residual martingale and actual stochastic increment isometry remain explicit in this general interface; the latter is separately constructed for arbitrary time-L2L^2L2 adapted finite columns. The generic decay theorem takes a coefficient dissipation inequality; transferring source A3 and minimal source A1 into these coefficient identities is separate, without a strong-SDE equivalence claim.
