Absorption of affine local errors by an exactly contracting flow

This section contains the discrete comparison argument behind Theorems New A and New B. Two sequences in a normed space HHH are compared: the numerical chain Xn+1=An(Xn)X_{n+1}=A_n(X_n)Xn+1​=An​(Xn​) and the exact chain Yn+1=Φn(Yn)Y_{n+1}=\Phi_n(Y_n)Yn+1​=Φn​(Yn​), where Φn\Phi_nΦn​ is the exact flow of the continuous dynamics over one step and AnA_nAn​ is one step of the numerical method. Distances are measured through a bounded linear map C:H→KC:H\to KC:H→K into another normed space. Three hypotheses are made:

  • the exact maps contract: ∥C(Φnx−Φny)∥≤e−ρh∥C(x−y)∥\|C(\Phi_nx-\Phi_ny)\|\le e^{-\rho h}\|C(x-y)\|∥C(Φn​x−Φn​y)∥≤e−ρh∥C(x−y)∥;
  • the local error is affine in the state: ∥C(Anx−Φnx)∥≤hh (a+b∥Cx∥)\|C(A_nx-\Phi_nx)\|\le h\sqrt h\,(a+b\|Cx\|)∥C(An​x−Φn​x)∥≤hh​(a+b∥Cx∥);
  • the exact chain is bounded: ∥CYn∥≤R\|CY_n\|\le R∥CYn​∥≤R for all nnn.

The numerical maps are not assumed Lipschitz or contracting, and no bound on the numerical chain is assumed. The local error at the numerical state is controlled through ∥CXn∥≤∥C(Xn−Yn)∥+R\|CX_n\|\le\|C(X_n-Y_n)\|+R∥CXn​∥≤∥C(Xn​−Yn​)∥+R, and the resulting growth term hh bh\sqrt h\,bhh​b is absorbed by the contraction when bh≤ρ/4b\sqrt h\le\rho/4bh​≤ρ/4 and ρh≤1\rho h\le1ρh≤1, since then e−ρh+b hh≤1−ρh/4e^{-\rho h}+b\,h\sqrt h\le1-\rho h/4e−ρh+bhh​≤1−ρh/4. The conclusion, for chains started at the same point, is

∥Xn−Yn∥≤4(a+bR)ρ mhfor every n∈N,\|X_n-Y_n\|\le\frac{4(a+bR)}{\rho\,m}\sqrt h\qquad\text{for every }n\in\mathbb N,∥Xn​−Yn​∥≤ρm4(a+bR)​h​for every n∈N,

when m∥z∥≤∥Cz∥m\|z\|\le\|Cz\|m∥z∥≤∥Cz∥. The bound is uniform in the number of iterations; the factor h\sqrt hh​ comes from dividing the local error hhh\sqrt hhh​ by the contraction gain ρh/4\rho h/4ρh/4 per step.

Assumptions and formal scope. This generic lemma displays map updates, exact contraction, local defect and reference radius explicitly. The method and final target clients derive all these premises from primitive force regularity and source A3.
