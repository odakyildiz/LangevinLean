All-iteration error absorption under an explicit small-step condition

Exact-flow contraction and an affine local numerical defect give an absorbed recursion and a uniform square-root-step strong error. Supporting chain and geometric-radius inequalities are proved.

Assumptions and formal scope. This generic lemma displays map updates, exact contraction, local defect and reference radius explicitly. The method and final target clients derive all these premises from primitive force regularity and source A3.
