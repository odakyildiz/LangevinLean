Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

The constructed vector Itô integral is L2L^2L2-time continuous

The exact completed-integral increment norm equals the actual coefficient time-energy. Compactness of a continuous adapted finite L2L^2L2 column path supplies a horizon bound, yielding an explicit square-root increment estimate and actual L2L^2L2 time continuity of the stopped integral.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume an actual primitive Driver and continuous adapted finite L2L^2L2 columns. No stochastic-input continuity or increment modulus is supplied. This proves L2L^2L2 time continuity, without asserting a simultaneous almost-sure continuous sample-path version or the full source A1 SDE realization.
