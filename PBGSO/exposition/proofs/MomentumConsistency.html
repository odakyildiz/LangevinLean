Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

M-MFPO consistency against Euler and integral dynamics

The exact splitting map is compared with an Euler step using an explicit remainder budget. A norm integral estimate controls the Euler defect along a specified integral trajectory. This exposes the primitive force and trajectory estimates needed for a rate.

Assumptions and formal scope. The theorem headers specify force Lipschitz and force/momentum/noise bounds, an actual integral equation, noise matching, and a square-root trajectory-increment bound. Those particular moment/modulus and coupling estimates remain inputs to the method bound. IntegralModulus separately derives generic finite-horizon increments from actual integral equations and input estimates.

obabo_integral_reference_bound

Mathematical role and assumptions

New finite-horizon bound for OBABO against compatible kinetic integral solution segments. It is derived from the actual map and integral equations. RRR controls the reference square-root modulus, P,F its grid-point moments, Ξ\XiΞ the first O innovation norm, and BBB the error coupling the net O increment to the reference additive increment. These can be deterministic norms or L2L^{2}L2 norms. Establishing these assumptions for a particular stochastic construction is a separate obligation; no stationary target is assumed or concluded.
