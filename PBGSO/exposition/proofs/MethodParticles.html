Actual particle forces inside the two implementation maps

The optimiser force is the finite empirical average and the sampler force is applied separately to each particle. Their Lipschitz constants are derived in the cloud sup norm and the two full particle method maps use zero optimiser innovations.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume positive particle count and the separate nonnegative global joint sum-norm Lipschitz bounds for the original observation and sampler force. The whole coupled force constant is 2max⁡{LF,LG}2\max\{L_F,L_G\}2max{LF​,LG​} in the product Banach norm. No common potential or probability independence is needed for these deterministic map estimates.
