Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Every Driver gives a genuine initial/Wiener-path probability map

Finite-grid results derive whole-coordinate and initial/noise independence. The actual continuous Brownian modification gives a measurable initial/path map preserving exactly original-initial-law times product Wiener law.

Assumptions and formal scope. The actual initial variable is ambient measurable and AE measurable in the original time-zero past. No Brownian path continuity, whole-path independence, product-law identity or Gaussian-seed inverse is supplied. Path evaluations equal the original Driver at each fixed time almost surely; simultaneous equality to arbitrary raw pre-Brownian paths is not asserted.
