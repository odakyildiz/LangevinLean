Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Derived finite-horizon state, drift and square-root modulus constants

Compactness of the actual continuous integral trajectory derives its state and drift radii. The actual Brownian input increment bound and global Lipschitz drift then derive one square-root modulus uniformly for all mesh intervals of length at most one in the fixed horizon.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume an actual norm-continuous L² integral trajectory, primitive globally Lipschitz drift and fixed linear Brownian input from the supplied coordinate pre-Brownian laws. No moment radius or increment-modulus estimate is supplied; no uniform-in-time bound follows.

exists_brownian_mesh_regularity

Mathematical role and assumptions

Actual Brownian-driven integral trajectories have mesh-uniform state, drift and square-root increment bounds on any fixed finite horizon.

Read this statement with the module assumption note above. The complete proof appears alongside it; the full source preserves every shared variable and typeclass.
