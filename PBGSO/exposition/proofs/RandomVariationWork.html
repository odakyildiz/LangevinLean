Fubini for random-budget original drift work

The original raw equation and actual martingale supply domination for original work on the time-sample product, including random L2L^2L2 variation budgets.

Assumptions and formal scope. Raw path continuity, joint drift measurability and the literal random variation bound are explicit. No drift L2L^2L2 curve or final work-integrability premise is supplied.
