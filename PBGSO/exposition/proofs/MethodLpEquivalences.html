Actual inverse physical and raw implementation L2L^2L2 coordinates

Continuous linear joining, splitting and particle packing are proved inverse on L2L^2L2. The actual M-MFPO and HOL full drifts are identified under these maps.

Assumptions and formal scope. Primitive ParticleForceData and the original model definitions suffice; no output matching, drift identity or measure-law conclusion is supplied.
