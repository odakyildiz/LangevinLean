Actual raw and Hilbert implementation state equivalences

Explicit continuous linear equivalences and their inverses identify every shared optimiser and sampler auxiliary coordinate of the momentum and HOL implementations with the full actual particle model.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume complete real Hilbert optimiser and sampler spaces and finite particle count. These are coordinate identities, without any supplied equality of laws, drifts or error estimates; actual lifted dynamics are identified in ImplementationLpCoordinates.
