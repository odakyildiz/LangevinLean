Local diffusion data from almost-sure path integrability

One ambient null augmentation and a single common coefficient mask construct actual everywhere-defined local diffusion data while preserving original physical columns for every time on one probability-one event.

Assumptions and formal scope. Original raw columns and scalar energy are jointly strongly measurable, adapted/progressive and only AE pathwise locally integrable, with pointwise square control. No global moment or stopped stochastic process is supplied.

RawLocalDiffusion.exists_of_ae

Mathematical role and assumptions

Primitive raw progressively measurable locally-square diffusion fields produce actual data for the constructed local integral on the proved null-augmented Brownian driver. All columns and the controlling raw path energy keep their original meaning on one common event of probability one.

Read this statement with the module assumption note above. The complete proof appears alongside it; the full source preserves every shared variable and typeclass.
