Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

True optimal-law constant-noise contraction under explicit work

Every initial coupling receives an actual law-preserving common Brownian realization of the original raw equations. One-sided work gives contraction, then genuine coupling infima give the true initial-Wasserstein factor.

Assumptions and formal scope. Parameter-cross L1L^1L1 work is explicit on the constructed joint realizations. This is an additional sufficient criterion for A1(i), not a proof that the bare source constant-diffusion alternative supplies it. No final output-law or transport inequality is assumed.

Mathematical role and assumptions

Actual original raw constant-diffusion laws contract after taking the infimum over all genuine initial couplings. Parameter-cross integrability is explicit and is not claimed to follow from minimal source A1(i).

Read this statement with the module assumption note above. The complete proof appears alongside it; the full source preserves every shared variable and typeclass.
