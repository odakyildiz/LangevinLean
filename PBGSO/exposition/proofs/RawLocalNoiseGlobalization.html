Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Local noise equals global integration from actual diffusion moments

Actual stopped integral identities and horizon exhaustion prove local noise equals the original global computed raw integral whenever original diffusion expected energy is finite.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only original diffusion product-square integrability is additional. No drift growth, state moment, drift L2L^2L2 curve or local-to-global stochastic identity is supplied.
