Actual L² radii from increasing finite maxima

Monotone convergence of genuine increasing finite observation maxima constructs a square-integrable countable radius with the same uniform second-moment bound.

Assumptions and formal scope. The finite maxima and their actual uniform moments are explicit; no limiting radius membership or maximal estimate is supplied.

Mathematical role and assumptions
