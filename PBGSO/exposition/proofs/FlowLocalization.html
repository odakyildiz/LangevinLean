C1C^1C1 globally Lipschitz localization near a compact orbit

A smooth compact cutoff leaves an arbitrary C1C^1C1 field unchanged on an open neighbourhood of a given compact set. Its actual derivative has compact support, which proves a global Lipschitz constant for the localized field rather than assuming one for the original field.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a finite-dimensional real normed domain, a normed real target, C1C^1C1 field and compact set. This is a localization helper; it does not itself prove joint C1C^1C1 dependence; FlowSourceRegularity composes it with constructed cutoff trajectories to derive the full source result.
