Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual Itô integration of raw time-L2L^2L2 adapted diffusion curves

A raw finite-column curve with finite horizon time-L2L^2L2 norm and actual current-past measurability enters the literal closed domain through the proved density theorem. Its computed real-time integral has a standard martingale representative, derived L2L^2L2 continuity, zero initial value, weighted increment moments and subtraction.

Assumptions and formal scope. No time continuity of diffusion columns is required. The original actual Driver and time-integrability/adaptation assumptions remain explicit. The integral is constructed; primitive source SDE existence and simultaneous sample paths remain separate.
