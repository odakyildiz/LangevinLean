Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Natural Dirac contractivity derives original uniform sampler moments

Primitive weighted Dirac monotonicity gives compact drift anchors and coercivity. Genuine stopping, computed local energy and Fatou derive uniform original second moments and product-time square integrability.

Assumptions and formal scope. Natural original sampler equations, Borel projected drift, positive A3 rate and constant diffusion are explicit. FiniteCouplingWork, global drift moments, norm growth and an expected-energy conclusion are unnecessary.
