Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual bounded stopping times approximated by finite grids

Ceiling dyadic stops obey genuine grid stopping events, decrease to the original bounded stopping time with explicit mesh error, and preserve interval-indicator limits even at atoms.

Assumptions and formal scope. A bounded nonnegative stopping time and its primitive stopping events are inputs. No random-time stochastic identity is assumed.
