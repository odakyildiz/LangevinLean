Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

A measurable past-series candidate for continuous limits

The unconditional telescoping series of successive strict-past samples converges to the actual uniform polygon limit under the derived summable increment budget.

Assumptions and formal scope. The candidate is defined on all sample points. Continuity is proved where the eventual adjacency bounds hold; measurability and adaptation are supplied by its actual past evaluations.
