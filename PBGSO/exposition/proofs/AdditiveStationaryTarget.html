Actual frozen invariance constructs the additive stationary target

The frozen trajectory supplies actual continuous L2L^2L2 sampler dynamics, its integral equation and its constant output law. Zero mean gradient and the optimiser fixed point derive the complete constant-optimiser solution. Primitive uniqueness proves canonical target stationarity and constructs every initial-coupling target realizer.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume primitive globally Lipschitz autonomous additive model maps, constant diffusion compatibility, the actual frozen invariant trajectory, zero gradient, optimiser fixed point, positive joint monotonicity and one-Lipschitz parameter projection. The marginal test family is empty, but actual frozen invariance is a substantive input; it is not inferred from vacuous generator stationarity.
