Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual finite-grid stopped Brownian integrals

Genuine stopping events produce predictable masked coefficients, exact stopped finite sums, actual martingales and completed finite-grid random-time realization.

Assumptions and formal scope. The finite deterministic grid and genuine stopping measurability are explicit. This module alone does not assert arbitrary bounded stopping.
