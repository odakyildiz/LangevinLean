Printed HOL covariance entries and Brownian Gram identities

The six independent printed covariance entries are transcribed explicitly. Antiderivative calculations identify their kernel-product derivatives and the resulting Brownian Gram integrals in the exact statements shown below.

Assumptions and formal scope. Positive or nonzero α\alphaα and nonnegative segment widths are imposed where needed. These covariance identities concern the printed response kernels; they do not identify those kernels with the exact full linear subsystem flow, nor prove nonlinear sampler stationarity.
