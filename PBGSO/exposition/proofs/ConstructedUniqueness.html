Actual same-initial uniqueness from source A3

The constructed difference integral and source joint monotonicity imply equality of both optimizer states and sampler L2L^2L2 variables for equal initial data.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original computed-integral Data class, measurable raw drift/diffusion and actual twisted joint monotonicity are explicit. Positive contraction rate is unnecessary for uniqueness itself; minimal-A1 localization is not proved here.

Mathematical role and assumptions

One-sided joint dissipativity proves actual same-initial pathwise uniqueness in the constructed time-L1L^{1}L1-drift/time-L2L^{2}L2-diffusion data class. Positive rate is unnecessary for uniqueness itself.
