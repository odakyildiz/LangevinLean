Explicit M-MFPO small-step consistency and stability constants

For the physical exponential damping and thermostat variance, coefficient estimates give a quadratic deterministic remainder plus an hhh\sqrt hhh​ innovation response. The BAB amplification is bounded by 1+Kh1+Kh1+Kh with an explicit KKK.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a nonnegative force Lipschitz constant and friction, 0<h≤10<h\le10<h≤1, and nonnegative momentum, force and innovation bounds. Applying the resulting numerical rate to a stochastic process retains the exact reference-solution and innovation-coupling premises displayed in its theorem header.
