Countable-horizon continuous modification glue

Countably many fixed-time dyadic identities and path continuity prove simultaneous overlap equality. The explicit ceiling-horizon selector then gives one global continuous version and retains strict adaptation.

Assumptions and formal scope. The generic input consists of actual continuous horizon versions and fixed-time almost-everywhere identities. Neither global overlap nor global continuity is assumed.
