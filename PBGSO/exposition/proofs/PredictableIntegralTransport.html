Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual raw predictable-curve integrals retain canonical laws

The literal horizon column function commutes with measure-preserving source pullback. The actually computed raw predictable-curve integral also commutes with pullback and valid full Driver filtration enlargement at every observation time.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only actual horizon time-L2L^2L2 membership and AE current-past measurability are required of the raw diffusion columns. No integral equality, noise law or fixed marginal output identity is a conclusion premise. Enlarged filtrations must satisfy the genuine Driver contract.
