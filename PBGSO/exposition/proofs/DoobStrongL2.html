Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Derived strong L² maximal martingale control

Conditional expectation, a finite running maximum and Young inequality prove the factor-four strong maximal second-moment bound for actual martingales.

Assumptions and formal scope. The actual L² martingale or nonnegative L² submartingale hypotheses are explicit. No maximal-moment bound, tail-integrability or uniform-integrability conclusion is assumed.
