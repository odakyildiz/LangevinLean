Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Constructed additive-input mean-field integral dynamics

Independent time-independent globally Lipschitz observation, optimiser response and sampler maps lift to a product of a deterministic Banach coordinate and an L2L^2L2 random-variable coordinate. The complete field's Lipschitz constant is derived, then Picard constructs finite-horizon norm-continuous integral solutions with zero optimiser input.

Assumptions and formal scope. This construction assumes complete normed real spaces, a probability measure, the primitive global Lipschitz bounds, and a continuous L2L^2L2 sampler input starting at zero. It does not establish almost-sure path continuity, Brownian adaptation, an Itô integral for multiplicative noise, or all of the source's strong-SDE well-posedness assumption.
