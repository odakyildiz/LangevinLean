Dyadic Brownian tail summability and continuous polygon limits

Markov on the actual sum of adjacent fourth powers gives a geometric dyadic-grid tail budget. Borel-Cantelli and deterministic interpolation construct an almost-sure uniform continuous polygon limit on every scaled finite horizon.

Assumptions and formal scope. A probability source and genuine scalar pre-Brownian laws suffice. No grid independence, path continuity, tail or convergence conclusion is assumed.
