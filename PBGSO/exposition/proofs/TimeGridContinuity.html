Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Dyadic time-L2L^2L2 convergence derived for actual continuous integrands

Actual deterministic dyadic left-step functions converge pointwise away from interval endpoints. Compactness supplies the finite horizon radius and dominated convergence gives literal time-L2L^2L2 convergence, including deterministic interval masks.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the displayed measurable integrand, almost-everywhere continuity and finite horizon bound; the continuous-masked corollary derives the bound from compactness. No grid-approximation or stochastic-isometry premise is supplied. These are deterministic coefficient-density facts, without an SDE existence assertion.
