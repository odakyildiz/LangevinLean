Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Contraction derived from actual additive integral equations

Shared additive input cancels in two actual integral equations. The fundamental theorem of calculus gives the transformed difference derivative; monotonicity then proves exponential contraction and a bounded-forcing perturbation estimate.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume a globally Lipschitz drift on a complete real Banach space, a continuous linear map into a real Hilbert space, its displayed transformed monotonicity, continuous solution paths satisfying the integral equations, and the stated metric bounds. The forced version additionally assumes continuous forcing, its uniform transformed norm bound and matching initial states. State-dependent Itô noise is outside this result.
