Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Derived numerical parameter-law identities from actual L2L^2L2 packing

Actual shared paired pushforwards and L2L^2L2 coercion identities prove the parameter marginal law and its exact RMS norm. Concrete momentum and HOL packings retain every particle and shared optimiser in genuine WithLp 2 Hilbert coordinates, with square integrability and pointwise identification proved. Their parameter laws are exactly phase θ\thetaθ.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume the displayed Borel normed coordinates and Lipschitz parameter projection where used. No final law-compatibility equality is a premise: both laws use the same actual variables and packing. This does not identify two independently supplied process constructions; their actual reference equations must still be linked.
