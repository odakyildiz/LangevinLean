Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Explicit one-sided work criterion for constant diffusion

Source A3 yields frozen pointwise sampler dissipation through actual one-point laws. The remaining positive work is confined to parameter-cross drift; explicit sufficient integrability criteria are proved.

Assumptions and formal scope. The parameter-cross work integrability or displayed parameter-Lipschitz/linear-growth sufficient condition is an additional explicit hypothesis. This does not assert that the minimal constant-diffusion branch A1(i) alone supplies it.
