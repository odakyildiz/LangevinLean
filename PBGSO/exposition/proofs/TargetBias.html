Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Explicit hybrid-target bias from an actual sampler coupling

A strongly monotone original gradient turns a residual into a critical-point distance bound. A proved Bochner expectation comparison then gives hybrid target bias at most L epsilon/m from an actual original/surrogate L² coupling.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume positive gradient monotonicity m, a stationary original gradient at star, a spatially Lipschitz observation, the original gradient expectation identity, zero surrogate mean observation and an actual coupling radius epsilon. The code does not derive epsilon for the Moreau model or repair the improper TV prior.

hybrid_target_bias

Mathematical role and assumptions

Explicit hybrid-target bias. `X` represents the original target at `a`, `Y` its surrogate. The original gradient identity and surrogate stationarity are explicit, and the true objective's gradient must be strongly monotone. Any separately proved coupling radius epsilon gives bias at most L epsilon/m.
