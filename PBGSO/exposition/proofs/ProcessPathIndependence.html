Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Whole coordinate independence from finite Driver grids

Finite-dimensional projective uniqueness promotes actual finite-grid independence to independence of original complete raw coordinate paths. Every primitive Driver therefore has whole coordinate-path independence.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only finite-grid independence and original evaluation measurability are required by the generic theorem; DriverPathIndependence derives the finite-grid input from the Driver. No whole-path independence is supplied.
