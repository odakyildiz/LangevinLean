PBGSO overview / PBGSO/RawLocalPullback.lean
Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Original raw local diffusion fields under probability transport

Actual locally integrable physical columns and scalar energy pull along genuine probability maps into valid enlarged filtrations. The intermediate global-square noise theorem identifies their computed local output.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only primitive raw local fields, measure preservation and valid past/noise compatibility are structural inputs. The stronger noise theorem explicitly assumes global diffusion expected energy; RawLocalPullbackLocal removes that intermediate restriction.
