Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

General model contraction with merely time-L² adapted diffusion

Original optimiser and sampler integral equations driven by the computed predictable Brownian integral construct the actual model integral trajectory. Density, martingale, exact weighted stochastic isometry, state L² continuity, source A3 energy and genuine law contraction are derived for arbitrary parameter- and state-dependent diffusion.

Assumptions and formal scope. Diffusion columns need only actual horizon time-L² membership and current-past AE adaptation; drift is Bochner L¹-time L². No diffusion time continuity, stochastic isometry, residual martingale or final energy premise is supplied. Realizing the paper's entire minimal A1 strong-solution class, including localization and simultaneous sample paths, remains separate.
