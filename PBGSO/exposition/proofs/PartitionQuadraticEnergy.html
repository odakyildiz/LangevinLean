Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Finite-partition expected quadratic energy at weak drift regularity

An actual martingale, point-time L2 variables and an adapted decomposition yield the exact finite-partition quadratic energy identity, with stochastic left cross terms proved zero.

Assumptions and formal scope. Point-time L2 and the literal adapted decomposition are explicit. No time-L1(L2) drift, stochastic-isometry or energy conclusion is assumed. Riemann/variation limits and localization remain separate.

increment_orthogonal

Mathematical role and assumptions

A past-measurable L² variable is orthogonal to actual martingale increments. The zero pairing is derived by the genuine conditional L² projection.

Mathematical role and assumptions
