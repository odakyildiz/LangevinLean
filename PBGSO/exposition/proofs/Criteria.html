Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Small-gain, projected-field and hypocoercive algebra

Completed squares verify scalar small-gain conditions. Projected work inequalities combine with auxiliary dissipation. For heavy-ball dynamics, the actual quadratic metric is positive and its pairing, Young bound and quantitative dissipation are proved.

Assumptions and formal scope. The scalar work, metric-comparison, monotonicity, force-size and coefficient inequalities appear as premises. These establish the algebra behind the appendix criteria; a general automatic derivation of all law-coupling integrals and spectral constants is not asserted.
