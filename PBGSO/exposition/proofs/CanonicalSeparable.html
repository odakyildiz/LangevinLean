Separable canonical initial and Brownian sample measures

Countability of the actual scalar-time Hilbert basis proves countable generation and separability of canonical initial/noise and finite-particle sample measures.

Assumptions and formal scope. The finite noise coordinate type and the displayed Borel second-countable initial state space are explicit. No separability instance for the canonical Brownian sample law is assumed.
