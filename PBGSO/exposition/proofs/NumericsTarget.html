Actual particle numerical errors combined with a stationary target

L2L^2L2 triangle and coordinate projection estimates combine the two actual Gaussian particle implementation bounds with the proved weak-process stationary parameter bound. Compatible parameter-law equality transfers the reference RMS bound across the numerical and weak-law probability spaces.

Assumptions and formal scope. The raw-particle Lipschitz/encoding and finite-horizon integral/moment/modulus contracts remain explicit, together with the full weak-generator stationary target data and the displayed reference-parameter law equality. No final numerical error or target-rate premise substitutes for those proofs. Original/surrogate target shifts require an actual coupling-radius bias estimate; TV prior repair and the model-specific radius remain separate.
