Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Original sampler equations under a continuous Driver modification

A valid fixed-time Brownian modification preserves literal model states and actual computed noise. Continuous paths of that modified Driver then give a simultaneous continuous sampler realization of the original equations.

Assumptions and formal scope. Fixed-time coordinate equality and valid filtration enlargement are explicit in this intermediate API. ContinuousBrownianDriver constructs these inputs; no original raw Brownian path-continuity assertion is made.

Mathematical role and assumptions
