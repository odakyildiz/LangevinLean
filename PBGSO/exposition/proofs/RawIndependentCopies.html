Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual independent raw reference solutions on the product Brownian source

The finite product sample law carries a proved full-past Brownian Driver. Evaluation pullbacks preserve each original raw SDE, and the actual reference cloud has its iid marginal law and independent complete trajectories.

Assumptions and formal scope. The original raw one-copy solution and a countably generated source are explicit in this construction. Neither independence of interacting variables, a final iid output law, nor a coordinate SDE matching premise is supplied. Full physical cloud packing is separate.
