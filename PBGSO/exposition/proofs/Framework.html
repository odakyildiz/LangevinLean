How the Lean proof follows this argument

Model records the maps as independent fields. HasFiniteSecondMoment means integrability of the squared norm and does not include probability normalisation. meanGradient is the Bochner integral; optimizerField substitutes it into the optimiser. meanGradient_empirical unfolds the definitions, uses integral_smul_measure and integral_finsetSum_measure, then simplifies the Dirac integrals. empiricalLaw_probability proves total mass one using hN to justify cancellation of the particle count. Extended nonnegative scalars in a measure are written ℝ≥0∞; ordinary vector scalars are real numbers.

02Stationarity at the weak equation level §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Choose a family of twice differentiable test functions for which all integrals below are finite. Write Lθφ=Dφ[b(θ,⋅)]+12∑iD2φ[σi,σi]L_\theta\varphi=D\varphi[b(\theta,\cdot)]+\tfrac12\sum_iD^2\varphi[\sigma_i,\sigma_i]Lθ​φ=Dφ[b(θ,⋅)]+21​∑i​D2φ[σi​,σi​]. Proof. The derivative of the constant optimiser is zero, equal to Ψ(t,s⋆,Gν⋆(s⋆))\Psi(t,s_\star,G_{\nu_\star}(s_\star))Ψ(t,s⋆​,Gν⋆​​(s⋆​)) by the two vanishing assumptions. For each test, t↦∫φ dν⋆t\mapsto\int\varphi\,d\nu_\start↦∫φdν⋆​ is constant, hence has derivative zero; frozen stationarity identifies the right-hand side of its forward equation with zero. All probability, moment and integrability requirements are inherited from the assumed frozen law. Apply the assumed uniqueness to this constant solution and the given solution. Their initial optimiser states and laws agree, so they agree at every nonnegative time. □\square□

How the Lean proof follows this argument

generator uses first and second Fréchet derivatives. IsWeakSolution lists probability, moment, integrability and right derivative conditions explicitly; HasDerivWithinAt on the nonnegative half-line permits the right derivative at zero. constant_isWeakSolution fills every field, with both derivative proofs obtained from hasDerivWithinAt_const. stationary_of_frozen applies hunique to the constructed constant solution. This argument concerns the stated weak equation; existence of strong stochastic solutions is not a field of Model.
