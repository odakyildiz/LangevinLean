Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Actual HOL references and mesh-uniform regularity are constructed

The actual full interacting HOL integral process is transported into its original optimiser,x,u,v L² coordinates. State and drift compactness and Brownian increments derive mesh-uniform auxiliary, force and drift-modulus bounds. Exact segmentation and actual innovation identities construct every reference.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume primitive separate force data, actual continuous full interacting integral process and its physical Brownian integral equation, canonical Euclidean cloud encoding, alpha>0 and gamma≥0. No supplied reference equation, node moment, drift modulus, coordinate or innovation matching remains. FullyConstructedHigherOrder constructs that actual source process too.
