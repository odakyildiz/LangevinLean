Brownian time continuity in probability and limit identification

Actual fourth-moment tails prove convergence in measure along every convergent deterministic time sequence. Strict past dyadic samples therefore identify any actual almost-sure limit with the original fixed-time variable.

Assumptions and formal scope. Only pre-Brownian laws and convergence of deterministic nonnegative times are assumed. No sample-path continuity or supplied modification identity is required.
