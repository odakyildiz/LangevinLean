True initial-Wasserstein contraction of original raw linear-growth solutions

Primitive local raw Model equations and linear growth derive global moments and computed predictable realization. The true initial optimal-coupling distance contracts for the literal original raw output laws.

Assumptions and formal scope. Original canonical initial coordinates, P2\mathcal P_2P2​ initial laws, raw local strong realizations, source A3 and measurable original coefficients are explicit. No global moment, coefficient curve, noise equality, output-law or all-couplings realization premise is supplied.

Mathematical role and assumptions

Source A3 contracts the actual original raw solution laws under the source linear-growth A1 branch. No time-L1L^{1}L1(L2L^{2}L2) drift, global diffusion moment, noise matching, supplied transport coupling or output-law premise is required. The raw initial variables are the original canonical initial coordinates.
