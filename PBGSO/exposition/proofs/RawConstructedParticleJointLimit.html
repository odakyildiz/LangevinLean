Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Joint time/population convergence of the original native parameter errors

For each population and observation horizon, the original empirical solution and primitive A1/A2 witnesses form a natural family. Both source branches derive the sharp original parameter bound before the product-filter limit is taken.

Assumptions and formal scope. Uniform response Lipschitz and reference observation variance, P2\mathcal P_2P2​ laws, positive A3, primitive Borel fields, original native solutions and synchronous initialization are explicit. Family and Branch contain only equations, initial conditions, actual target stationarity and well-posedness/primitive-growth witnesses. No final error estimate, paired process, law matching, energy or work is a field; horizon-coherent global trajectories are included by restriction.
