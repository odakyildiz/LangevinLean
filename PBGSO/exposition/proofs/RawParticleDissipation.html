Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

Original particle dissipation and damping from source A3

The actual empirical coupling and source joint monotonicity derive the original full particle/copy generator bound. Literal damping retains the sharp rate after stopping while the forcing remains the original reference observation fluctuation.

Assumptions and formal scope. Positive particle count, source A3, the original coefficient identities, deterministic reference optimizer and response Lipschitz constant are explicit. No iid law for the artificial damped cloud or final dissipation conclusion is assumed.
