Stationary product-law uniqueness for the original raw equations

Actual stationary original solution families, direct A1 strong existence and same-driver uniqueness instantiate the arbitrary-source contraction bounds. Exponential decay and Wasserstein separation give equality of the optimiser states and sampler laws.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume P2\mathcal P_2P2​ stationary sampler laws, source A3 with positive rate, and the stated original stationary solution/A1 existence and uniqueness data on each finite horizon. The constant-diffusion branch additionally requires universal finite signed coupling work and primitive Borel fields; the linear-growth branch requires its canonical primitive coefficient regularity. No weak-generator realization, energy estimate or contraction conclusion is supplied.
