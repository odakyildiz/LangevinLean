Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

General model contraction from the computed continuous-column Itô integral

Original optimiser/sampler integral equations driven by the actually computed Brownian integral construct the full model integral trajectory. State continuity, the residual martingale, synchronous weighted isometry, source A3 energy and actual law contraction are derived.

Assumptions and formal scope. The drift is Bochner L1L^1L1-time L2L^2L2 and the finite diffusion columns are continuous adapted L2L^2L2 variables in this module. No stochastic-isometry or weak-generator conclusion is supplied. The intended minimal source A1 process class and localization remain separate.
