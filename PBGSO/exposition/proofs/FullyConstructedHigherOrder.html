Exact declarations · explicit assumptions

HOL-MYPGD target bound with its process and reference constructed

Primitive independent forces construct the actual physical HOL model and global interacting particles. All original L2L^2L2 coordinates, physical diffusion, Gaussian coupling, mesh-uniform moments and drift modulus, exact integral references and initial equality are derived. The final parameter bound combines discretization, relaxation and particle terms.

Assumptions and formal scope. Assume separate global joint Lipschitz bounds for FFF and GGG, canonical Euclidean cloud encoding, α>0\alpha>0α>0, γ≥0\gamma\ge0γ≥0, positive particle count and source A3 rate, initial and frozen target P2\mathcal P_2P2​ laws, an actual frozen invariant sampler trajectory, zero target mean gradient and uniform observation variance. No reference, process package, moment/modulus or law-compatibility conclusion is a premise. The optional original-target corollary requires actual coupled target observations with explicit radius and positive gradient monotonicity. This is a finite-horizon additive integral theorem, without a general multiplicative Itô claim.
