Exact primitive force values for every uniform numerical coefficient

Actual constant L2L^2L2 identities reduce lifted zero-force norms to the primitive F(0,0)F(0,0)F(0,0) and N G(0,0)\sqrt N\,G(0,0)N​G(0,0). The full raw kinetic and HOL drift norms at zero are the maximum of those two values.

Assumptions and formal scope. A probability source, positive particle count and the actual standard Euclidean cloud encoding suffice. No zero-force identity, operator norm, independence or numerical estimate is a premise.
