For a pair function WWW define the marginal integral and the marginal potential

kW(θ)=∫Rdxe−W(θ,x) dx,φW(θ)=−log⁡kW(θ).k_W(\theta)=\int_{\mathbb R^{d_x}}e^{-W(\theta,x)}\,dx,\qquad\varphi_W(\theta)=-\log k_W(\theta).kW​(θ)=∫Rdx​​e−W(θ,x)dx,φW​(θ)=−logkW​(θ).

A marginal maximizer of WWW is a maximizer of kWk_WkW​, i.e. a maximizer of the marginal likelihood in the latent-variable model with joint density proportional to e−We^{-W}e−W. This section proves: kUk_UkU​ is positive, finite and continuous and has a maximizer; φV≤φU≤φV+δ\varphi_V\le\varphi_U\le\varphi_V+\deltaφV​≤φU​≤φV​+δ; a marginal maximizer of UUU minimizes φU\varphi_UφU​; and any marginal maximizers θU\theta_UθU​ of UUU and θV\theta_VθV​ of VVV satisfy
