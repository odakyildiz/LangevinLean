02Integrate the gap before taking logarithms §

Mathematical statement and proof

For any potentials V≤U≤V+δV\le U\le V+\deltaV≤U≤V+δ, exponentiation and integration in the latent variable give

e−δkV(θ)≤kU(θ)≤kV(θ),kW(θ)=∫e−W(θ,x)dx. e^{-\delta}k_V(\theta)\le k_U(\theta)\le k_V(\theta), \qquad k_W(\theta)=\int e^{-W(\theta,x)}dx. e−δkV​(θ)≤kU​(θ)≤kV​(θ),kW​(θ)=∫e−W(θ,x)dx.

The smooth strongly convex comparison potential VVV supplies positive finite integrals and an integrable Gaussian bound. The original marginal is continuous and decays at infinity; hence it has an actual maximizer. Taking negative logarithms, which is justified by positivity, gives

FV(θ)≤FU(θ)≤FV(θ)+δ,FW=−log⁡kW. F_V(\theta)\le F_U(\theta)\le F_V(\theta)+\delta, \qquad F_W=-\log k_W. FV​(θ)≤FU​(θ)≤FV​(θ)+δ,FW​=−logkW​.

Let θU\theta_UθU​ minimize FUF_UFU​ and θV\theta_VθV​ minimize the mmm-strongly convex FVF_VFV​. Minimality and the sandwich imply FV(θU)−FV(θV)≤δF_V(\theta_U)-F_V(\theta_V)\le\deltaFV​(θU​)−FV​(θV​)≤δ. Strong-convexity growth about its minimizer therefore yields

m2∥θU−θV∥2≤δ. \frac m2\|\theta_U-\theta_V\|^2\le\delta. 2m​∥θU​−θV​∥2≤δ.

This argument never differentiates the original marginal and never integrates an asymptotic remainder.

The sharp quadratic-growth lemma uses strong convexity and global minimality, without differentiability of the marginal potential. The comparison model supplies an integrable Gaussian bound for the original weights, giving continuity and existence of an original marginal maximizer by dominated convergence and decay at infinity. Finite positive marginal integrals are established before logarithms and real conversions.

For V≤U≤V+δV\le U\le V+\deltaV≤U≤V+δ, exponentiation reverses the potential order: e−δe−V≤e−U≤e−Ve^{-\delta}e^{-V}\le e^{-U}\le e^{-V}e−δe−V≤e−U≤e−V. Integrating each latent fiber gives e−δMV(θ)≤MU(θ)≤MV(θ)e^{-\delta}M_V(\theta)\le M_U(\theta)\le M_V(\theta)e−δMV​(θ)≤MU​(θ)≤MV​(θ). Positivity and finiteness of the reference marginal then imply the same for MUM_UMU​; only after that can one use monotonicity of the logarithm to obtain FV≤FU≤FV+δF_V\le F_U\le F_V+\deltaFV​≤FU​≤FV​+δ, where F=−log⁡MF=-\log MF=−logM. These guards exclude taking logarithms of zero or infinite marginal integrals.

  • marginalPotential_sandwich: The two negative log marginal likelihoods differ by at most the same uniform potential gap. Integrability of the nonsmooth original weight is derived by domination by the smooth strongly convex comparison potential.

03Establish original-marginal existence §

Mathematical statement and proof

The inequality e−U≤e−Ve^{-U}\le e^{-V}e−U≤e−V and strong-convexity Gaussian bounds prove integrability. Continuity follows by dominated convergence on parameter neighborhoods, using the comparison potential to provide an integrable latent majorant. The same Gaussian estimate proves decay at parameter infinity. A positive continuous function that vanishes at infinity attains its maximum on a sufficiently large compact ball.

The uniform gap dominates the original latent weight by an integrable reference weight, giving finite latent integrals. Locally uniform domination also permits passage to the limit in the parameter and proves continuity of the original marginal integral. The reference's Gaussian decay in the parameter then transfers through the sandwich, so the original marginal vanishes at infinity. A continuous positive marginal attaining a positive value must achieve its maximum on a sufficiently large compact ball. This is how existence of the original center follows from finite smoothing, rather than an added optimizer-existence assumption.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • integrable_original_marginal: A continuous nonsmooth original potential has finite latent integrals when it dominates the smooth strongly convex comparison potential.
  • continuous_original_marginal: The original marginal is continuous by a Gaussian dominated convergence argument, even though its joint potential may be nonsmooth.
  • exists_original_marginalMaximizer: A maximizer of the actual nonsmooth marginal exists; existence is not an extra statistical premise added to the Moreau comparison argument.

04Strong growth controls the center shift §

Mathematical statement and proof

Positive finite marginal densities make −log⁡-\log−log order-reversing, so an original marginal maximizer minimizes FUF_UFU​. At minimizers θU,θV\theta_U,\theta_VθU​,θV​, the sandwich and minimality give FV(θU)≤FU(θU)≤FU(θV)≤FV(θV)+δF_V(\theta_U)\le F_U(\theta_U)\le F_U(\theta_V)\le F_V(\theta_V)+\deltaFV​(θU​)≤FU​(θU​)≤FU​(θV​)≤FV​(θV​)+δ. The preserved marginal strong convexity yields ∥θU−θV∥2≤2δ/m\|\theta_U-\theta_V\|^2\le2\delta/m∥θU​−θV​∥2≤2δ/m; taking the nonnegative square root gives the distance estimate.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • original_maximizer_minimizes: Marginal maximization for the actual nonsmooth model is equivalent to minimization of its finite negative log marginal likelihood.
  • marginal_center_distance_sq: A fully nonasymptotic marginal-center perturbation bound. Only the comparison model is assumed smooth. The centers maximize the actual marginal integrals; they are not joint modes.
  • marginal_center_distance: The square-root form of the uniform-gap marginal-center estimate.
