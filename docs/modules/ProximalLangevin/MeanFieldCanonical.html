Statement and proof

01Discharge every scalar Brownian law and full-path independence §

Mathematical statement and proof

The initialized source already has the true Brownian coordinate laws and independent full paths. The injective particle-coordinate selection on its finite product preserves both properties. Thus the parameter coordinates and every particle’s latent coordinates form one independent standard Brownian family, including the first-copy overlap resolved by distinct coordinate tags.

02Construct the reference once, then every particle process §

Mathematical statement and proof

Use the actual initial pair (θ0,X0)(\theta_0,X_0)(θ0​,X0​) on μ⊗QB\mu\otimes Q_Bμ⊗QB​, whose square integrability and deterministic parameter projection were proved. Substitute this source and the actual Brownian coordinate process into the Brownian comparison theorem. It constructs continuous Y,θY,\thetaY,θ, a finite C≥0C\ge0C≥0, and for every N>0N>0N>0 a continuous actual particle solution ZNZ^NZN with exact iid initialization. The equations retain latent noise 2Bx,i\sqrt2 B^{x,i}2​Bx,i and parameter noise 2/NBθ\sqrt{2/N}B^\theta2/N​Bθ, and prove

sup⁡0≤t≤T(∥ΘtN−θt∥2+∥Xti,N−Xti∥2)≤CTN.\sup_{0\le t\le T}\big(\|\Theta_t^N-\theta_t\|_2+\|X_t^{i,N}-X_t^i\|_2\big)\le\frac{C_T}{\sqrt N}.0≤t≤Tsup​(∥ΘtN​−θt​∥2​+∥Xti,N​−Xti​∥2​)≤N​CT​​.

The coefficient is selected before the quantifier over NNN. This controls the supremum of L2L^2L2 norms, without claiming an expected path supremum.

The primitive inputs are a latent probability law with finite second moment, a deterministic parameter, a globally Lipschitz joint score and a finite horizon. The initialized product source supplies all Brownian coordinate laws, their whole-path independence, and initialization/noise independence. Substitute these into the already proved Brownian comparison theorem to construct one mean-field reference, then a particle solution for every positive NNN. The output retains both integral equations and the deterministic parameter equation alongside the bound. Its uniformity is sup⁡t≤T∥Zt−Rt∥L2\sup_{t\le T}\|Z_t-R_t\|_{L^2}supt≤T​∥Zt​−Rt​∥L2​; no exchange of supremum and expectation is made.
