References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

This module imports the proximal development and asks Lean to print the axioms used by its principal analytic, algorithmic and stochastic declarations. It is an audit entry point rather than a new mathematical assumption.

01Rebuild the theorems and inspect their dependency axioms §

Mathematical statement and proof

Run lake build ProximalLangevin +ProximalLangevin.Check from the repository root. Each #print axioms command reports the dependency closure of an already checked declaration, including proximal optimality, the three actual algorithm bounds, Brownian coupling and mean-field constructions. The file introduces no new theorem or definition. The audit complements compilation; it does not strengthen any theorem’s stated assumptions or turn conditional scalar statements into algorithm convergence results.

Each #check command asks Lean to elaborate the complete theorem type, retaining every displayed assumption. Each #print axioms command reports the transitive foundational dependencies of that theorem. Compile this module after the library build to inspect that report; these commands check the existing proofs and do not supply new mathematical hypotheses or conclusions.
