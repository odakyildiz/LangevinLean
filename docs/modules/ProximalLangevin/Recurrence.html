Conditional scalar splitting recurrence and its strict endpoint

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

This file proves scalar consequences of an explicitly supplied recurrence. It does not supply the stochastic one-step law estimate for the paper’s coefficient. The independently proved PIPGLA law theorem uses the constructed coupling route.

03Treat q = 1 and equal steps honestly §

Mathematical statement and proof

At q=1q=1q=1, induction gives the finite-horizon bound en≤e0+nce_n\le e_0+ncen​≤e0​+nc; there is no finite geometric floor. When λ=γ\lambda=\gammaλ=γ and the named denominators are nonzero, direct field algebra gives

c1−q=γμ(2LDN+C).\frac c{1-q}=\frac\gamma\mu\left(\frac{2LD}{N}+C\right).1−qc​=μγ​(N2LD​+C).

This identity preserves the unscaled CCC-term and does not assert that an actual stochastic error satisfies the input recurrence.

At q=1q=1q=1, retain the finite sum: the induction gives en≤e0+nce_n\le e_0+ncen​≤e0​+nc. For λ=γ\lambda=\gammaλ=γ, cancellation in the strict regime gives c1−q=γμ(2LDN+C).\frac{c}{1-q}=\frac\gamma\mu\left(\frac{2LD}{N}+C\right).1−qc​=μγ​(N2LD​+C). The CCC term is not divided by NNN: its numerator was λ2C\lambda^2 Cλ2C, whereas the Gaussian contribution was 2λγLD/N2\lambda\gamma LD/N2λγLD/N. The nonzero assumptions in splitting_bias_equal_steps justify exactly these algebraic cancellations.

  • splitting_recurrence_endpoint: At the noncontracting endpoint, retain the linear finite-horizon bound.
  • splitting_bias_equal_steps: When lam=gamma, the corrected error floor takes this particularly simple form, with no 1/N multiplying C.
