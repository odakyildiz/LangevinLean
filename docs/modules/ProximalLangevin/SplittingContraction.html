01Jensen handles two independently averaged outputs §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply the scalar average-square inequality to the difference of the parameter outputs. Each latent difference has its exact scaled square. Thus

∥Pair⁡P(z)−Pair⁡P(w)∥2≤N−1∑i∥P(Jiz)−P(Jiw)∥2.\|\operatorname{Pair}_P(z)-\operatorname{Pair}_P(w)\|^2\le N^{-1}\sum_i\|P(J_i z)-P(J_i w)\|^2.∥PairP​(z)−PairP​(w)∥2≤N−1i∑​∥P(Ji​z)−P(Ji​w)∥2.

If PPP is nonexpansive, each summand is bounded by ∥Ji(z−w)∥2\|J_i(z-w)\|^2∥Ji​(z−w)∥2. Their sum is N∥z−w∥2N\|z-w\|^2N∥z−w∥2, proving that the actual pairwise map is also nonexpansive.

02A shared innovation cancels before proximity §

Mathematical statement and proof

The lifted smooth gradient step has strong convexity NmNmNm, gradient constant NL1NL_1NL1​, and step γ/N\gamma/Nγ/N, so its contraction factor is 1−mγ≥01-m\gamma\ge01−mγ≥0. The same Gaussian or deterministic innovation added to both inputs cancels in their difference. Composition with the actual nonexpansive averaged proximal map gives

∥Tλ(z,ξ)−Tλ(w,ξ)∥≤(1−mγ)∥z−w∥.\|T_\lambda(z,\xi)-T_\lambda(w,\xi)\|\le(1-m\gamma)\|z-w\|.∥Tλ​(z,ξ)−Tλ​(w,ξ)∥≤(1−mγ)∥z−w∥.

Induction on the displayed recursion yields factor (1−mγ)n(1-m\gamma)^n(1−mγ)n. This proves stability; a separate stationary reference and local-consistency estimate are needed for convergence to a Gibbs target.
