Full-potential PIPULA and its smoothed-center theorem

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

Assume the full nonsmooth potential UUU is mmm-strongly convex and bounded below, with m,γ>0m,\gamma>0m,γ>0. Global Lipschitzness of UUU is not required. The theorem concerns the maximizer of the marginal of UγU^\gammaUγ.

01The actual full-envelope modulus makes the step admissible §

Mathematical statement and proof

Set mγ=m/(1+mγ)>0m_\gamma=m/(1+m\gamma)>0mγ​=m/(1+mγ)>0. Since γmγ=mγ/(1+mγ)≤1\gamma m_\gamma=m\gamma/(1+m\gamma)\le1γmγ​=mγ/(1+mγ)≤1, we have γ(mγ+γ−1)≤2\gamma(m_\gamma+\gamma^{-1})\le2γ(mγ​+γ−1)≤2, equivalently γ≤2/(mγ+γ−1)\gamma\le2/(m_\gamma+\gamma^{-1})γ≤2/(mγ​+γ−1). All multiplications and divisions have positive denominators.

02The proximal/noise law is the actual IPLA law §

Mathematical statement and proof

The law is the pushforward of the exact PIPULA recursion. Set g1=0g_1=0g1​=0 and η=γ\eta=\gammaη=γ in the pointwise MYIPLA identity. Actual proximal optimality gives Pγ=I−γ∇UγP_\gamma=I-\gamma\nabla U^\gammaPγ​=I−γ∇Uγ, so the full chain equals IPLA for UγU^\gammaUγ, and hence their pushforwards agree.

03Sharp concentration and Gaussian discretization §

Mathematical statement and proof

The algebraic reduction g1=0g_1=0g1​=0, λ=γ\lambda=\gammaλ=γ is proved for the actual update and its full recursion. Together with the envelope theory, it supplies genuine proximity, smoothness, and strong-convexity facts for a full-potential scheme.

It does not reproduce v3 Corollary A.6's original-center finite-time bound. In assumption B1, the displayed proximal inner-product deficit μ\muμ gives the envelope-gradient constant μ/γ\mu/\gammaμ/γ, since the residual is divided by γ\gammaγ. The code records that exact implication. A scale-uniform PIPULA discretization theorem and an original-center estimate with the paper's rate remain unproved here; substituting a 1/γ1/\gamma1/γ smoothness bound into a generic ULA estimate alone would not establish the claimed vanishing error.

A separate proved finite-time result is supplied for the actual PIPULA chain around the maximizer of its smoothed marginal. Assume the original finite-valued potential is mmm-strongly convex and bounded below; global Lipschitzness of the strongly convex potential is not imposed. For γ>0\gamma>0γ>0, its full envelope has modulus mγ=m/(1+mγ)m_\gamma=m/(1+m\gamma)mγ​=m/(1+mγ) and gradient Lipschitz constant 1/γ1/\gamma1/γ. The actual proximal/noise recursion equals IPLA for that envelope, and the admissible-step inequality holds automatically. The resulting sharp concentration and Gaussian-chain bound are fully proved. Its conservative discretization term is (33/20)(1/(γmγ))dx+dθ/Nγ(33/20)(1/(\gamma m_\gamma))\sqrt{d_x+d_\theta/N}\sqrt\gamma(33/20)(1/(γmγ​))dx​+dθ​/N​γ​, whose coefficient diverges as γ↓0\gamma\downarrow0γ↓0. This is not a reproduction of the paper's original-center O(γ)O(\sqrt\gamma)O(γ​) convergence rate.

Writing mγ=m/(1+mγ)m_\gamma=m/(1+m\gamma)mγ​=m/(1+mγ), ΠN,γ\Pi_{N,\gamma}ΠN,γ​ for the full-envelope target and θγ\theta_\gammaθγ​ for its marginal maximizer, the exact bound is

Prove full-envelope convexity and differentiability, identify the exact algorithm law, apply the sharp IPLA theorem with gradient constant γ−1\gamma^{-1}γ−1, and use the already proved admissible-step inequality. This gives every displayed term from the actual chain.

Here the whole joint potential is smoothed, so the relevant modulus is mη=m/(1+mη)m_\eta=m/(1+m\eta)mη​=m/(1+mη), rather than mmm. The score constant of the full envelope is 1/η1/\eta1/η. The exact proximal/noise update identity reduces PIPULA to IPLA for that envelope, after which the theorem applies the sharp concentration and Gaussian discretization estimates with these constants. The admissible-step proof checks the actual inequality γ≤2/(mη+1/η)\gamma\le2/(m_\eta+1/\eta)γ≤2/(mη​+1/η); substituting the unsmoothed modulus would give a different and unjustified step restriction.
