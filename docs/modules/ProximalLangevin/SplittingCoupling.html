Lipschitz energy and the averaged lifted potential

References and publication status for this development

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

Assume ggg is convex, bounded below, and KKK-Lipschitz, with λ>0\lambda>0λ>0 and N>0N>0N>0. Proximal optimality and the value bound give the pairwise energy inequality; Jensen transfers it to the averaged particle output. The replicated-pair norm identity also proves that G=N−1∑ig(Jiz)G=N^{-1}\sum_i g(J_i z)G=N−1∑i​g(Ji​z) has the same value Lipschitz constant. Neither argument requires the averaged output to minimize a shared-state objective.

01The proximal energy inequality from value Lipschitzness §

Mathematical statement and proof

For p=Pλ(x)p=P_\lambda(x)p=Pλ​(x), test the residual support at comparator aaa to bound f(p)−f(a)f(p)-f(a)f(p)−f(a). Apply f(x)−f(p)≤K∥x−p∥f(x)-f(p)\le K\|x-p\|f(x)−f(p)≤K∥x−p∥ in the expansion of ∥x−a∥2\|x-a\|^2∥x−a∥2. Nonnegativity of (∥x−p∥−λK)2≥0(\|x-p\|-\lambda K)^2\ge0(∥x−p∥−λK)2≥0 gives ∥p−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2λ(f(x)−f(a))+λ2K2\|p-a\|^2\le\|x-a\|^2-2\lambda(f(x)-f(a))+\lambda^2K^2∥p−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2λ(f(x)−f(a))+λ2K2. Sum the pair inequalities, use Jensen for the common parameter, and divide by positive NNN. The resulting full-state remainder is λ2K2\lambda^2K^2λ2K2.

At the proximal point ppp, the residual r=(x−p)/λr=(x-p)/\lambdar=(x−p)/λ is a subgradient at ppp. Its norm is at most KKK for a KKK-Lipschitz potential. The support at ppp, bound f(x)−f(p)≤K∥x−p∥f(x)-f(p)\le K\|x-p\|f(x)−f(p)≤K∥x−p∥, and squared-distance expansion give

∥p−y∥2≤∥x−y∥2−2λ(f(x)−f(y))+λ2K2.\|p-y\|^2\le\|x-y\|^2-2\lambda(f(x)-f(y))+\lambda^2K^2.∥p−y∥2≤∥x−y∥2−2λ(f(x)−f(y))+λ2K2.

The required replacement of output energy by input energy is exactly f(x)−f(p)≤K∥x−p∥f(x)-f(p)\le K\|x-p\|f(x)−f(p)≤K∥x−p∥. Completion of a square bounds the remaining displacement expression by λ2K2\lambda^2K^2λ2K2. Thus value Lipschitzness supplies the needed numerical remainder without assuming a bounded input-subgradient selection.

02The averaged potential’s value Lipschitz constant §

Mathematical statement and proof

Put G(z)=Fg(z)/NG(z)=F_g(z)/NG(z)=Fg​(z)/N. Cauchy–Schwarz for the finite average and ∑i∥Jiv∥2=N∥v∥2\sum_i\|J_i v\|^2=N\|v\|^2∑i​∥Ji​v∥2=N∥v∥2 imply N−1∑i∥Jiv∥≤∥v∥N^{-1}\sum_i\|J_i v\|\le\|v\|N−1∑i​∥Ji​v∥≤∥v∥. Applying the pairwise value Lipschitz estimate therefore gives

∣G(z)−G(w)∣≤KN∑i∥Ji(z−w)∥≤K∥z−w∥.|G(z)-G(w)|\le\frac KN\sum_i\|J_i(z-w)\|\le K\|z-w\|.∣G(z)−G(w)∣≤NK​i∑​∥Ji​(z−w)∥≤K∥z−w∥.

The latent scaling N−1/2N^{-1/2}N−1/2 makes the average pair squared distance equal to the state squared distance. Consequently the averaged physical-time potential G=Fg/NG=F_g/NG=Fg​/N retains value Lipschitz constant KKK. This bound ensures integrability of its values at square-integrable random states.

Let fˉN(z)=N−1∑if(Aiz)\bar f_N(z)=N^{-1}\sum_i f(A_i z)fˉ​N​(z)=N−1∑i​f(Ai​z). Cauchy–Schwarz and the replication identity give 1N∑i∥Aiv∥≤(1N∑i∥Aiv∥2)1/2=∥v∥.\frac1N\sum_i\|A_i v\|\le\left(\frac1N\sum_i\|A_i v\|^2\right)^{1/2}=\|v\|.N1​i∑​∥Ai​v∥≤(N1​i∑​∥Ai​v∥2)1/2=∥v∥. Applying ∣f(a)−f(b)∣≤K∥a−b∥|f(a)-f(b)|\le K\|a-b\|∣f(a)−f(b)∣≤K∥a−b∥ to each pair and summing proves ∣fˉN(z)−fˉN(w)∣≤K∥z−w∥|\bar f_N(z)-\bar f_N(w)|\le K\|z-w\|∣fˉ​N​(z)−fˉ​N​(w)∣≤K∥z−w∥. The declarations separate the average norm estimate from the value estimate so that the particle scaling can be checked independently.

Formal correspondence: averagedPotential : The averaged lifted potential in the physical-time Langevin equation. average_joint_norm_le : Average pair distances are controlled by the scaled state norm. averagedPotential_lipschitz : Averaging in the scaled coordinates preserves the pair potential's Lipschitz constant.
