Expected energy comparison for the perturbation force

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

Let eee be convex and differentiable, assume e≤f≤e+δe\le f\le e+\deltae≤f≤e+δ, and let YtY_tYt​ have the stated stationary smoothed law, integral equation, and future-noise orthogonality. Assume the value Lipschitz bounds needed for integrability. Convex support bounds the force pairing by an envelope-energy difference. Stationarity identifies the time-dependent reference energy with the terminal energy, and the uniform gap then compares this with the original nonsmooth energy in the proximal estimate.

Statement and proof

01The expected uniform value-gap inequality §

Mathematical statement and proof

Suppose f(x)≤e(x)+δf(x)\le e(x)+\deltaf(x)≤e(x)+δ and both functions are Lipschitz. For L2L^2L2 variable XXX their values are integrable, so integral monotonicity gives Ef(X)−Ee(X)≤δ\mathbb E f(X)-\mathbb E e(X)\le\deltaEf(X)−Ee(X)≤δ. In the notation f≤e+δf\le e+\deltaf≤e+δ and an L2L^2L2 state, the mass-one calculation is Ef(X)≤Ee(X)+δP(Ω)=Ee(X)+δ\mathbb Ef(X)\le\mathbb Ee(X)+\delta P(\Omega)=\mathbb Ee(X)+\deltaEf(X)≤Ee(X)+δP(Ω)=Ee(X)+δ.

The subsequent comparison uses e≤fe\le fe≤f at the algorithm’s intermediate state and f≤e+δf\le e+\deltaf≤e+δ at the reference endpoint. The integrated gap is δ\deltaδ because the source is a probability. All expected values are finite before subtraction, and no derivative of the original nonsmooth function is used.

Formal correspondence: expectation_gap_le : A finite uniform value gap controls its actual expectation.

02The stationary convex-support and future-drift estimates §

Mathematical statement and proof

Convex support bounds ⟨S−Yh,∇e(Yt)⟩\langle S-Y_h,\nabla e(Y_t)\rangle⟨S−Yh​,∇e(Yt​)⟩ by Ee(S)−Ee(Yt)+⟨Yt−Yh,∇e(Yt)⟩\mathbb E e(S)-\mathbb E e(Y_t)+\langle Y_t-Y_h,\nabla e(Y_t)\rangleEe(S)−Ee(Yt​)+⟨Yt​−Yh​,∇e(Yt​)⟩. Stationarity gives equality of Ee(Yt)\mathbb E e(Y_t)Ee(Yt​) and Ee(Yh)\mathbb E e(Y_h)Ee(Yh​). The future-noise estimate bounds the remaining drift pairing by hGKhGKhGK. The gap e≤f≤e+δe\le f\le e+\deltae≤f≤e+δ changes the difference of the two energies by at most δ\deltaδ. Integration therefore gives

2⟨S−Yh,∫0h∇e(Yt)dt⟩−2h(Ef(S)−Ef(Yh))≤2hδ+2h2GK.2\left\langle S-Y_h,\int_0^h\nabla e(Y_t)dt\right\rangle-2h(\mathbb E f(S)-\mathbb E f(Y_h))\le2h\delta+2h^2GK.2⟨S−Yh​,∫0h​∇e(Yt​)dt⟩−2h(Ef(S)−Ef(Yh​))≤2hδ+2h2GK.

More explicitly, for bt=∇e(Yt)b_t=\nabla e(Y_t)bt​=∇e(Yt​) in L2L^2L2 the support and future-increment bounds give

⟨S−Yh,bt⟩≤Ee(S)−Ee(Yt)+(h−t)GK.\langle S-Y_h,b_t\rangle\le\mathbb Ee(S)-\mathbb Ee(Y_t)+(h-t)GK.⟨S−Yh​,bt​⟩≤Ee(S)−Ee(Yt​)+(h−t)GK.

Stationarity replaces Ee(Yt)\mathbb Ee(Y_t)Ee(Yt​) by Ee(Yh)\mathbb Ee(Y_h)Ee(Yh​). Integration over [0,h][0,h][0,h] is legitimate because btb_tbt​ is continuous and the fixed inner-product functional commutes with the Bochner integral. A valid upper bound is h[Ee(S)−Ee(Yh)]+h2GKh[\mathbb Ee(S)-\mathbb Ee(Y_h)]+h^2GKh[Ee(S)−Ee(Yh​)]+h2GK.

The decomposition S−Yh=(S−Yt)+(Yt−Yh)S-Y_h=(S-Y_t)+(Y_t-Y_h)S−Yh​=(S−Yt​)+(Yt​−Yh​) separates the convex support term from the future-increment term. Again Ee(Yt)\mathbb E e(Y_t)Ee(Yt​) equals Ee(Yh)\mathbb E e(Y_h)Ee(Yh​). At SSS use e≤fe\le fe≤f, and at YhY_hYh​ use f≤e+δf\le e+\deltaf≤e+δ. Only one additive gap δ\deltaδ remains. This is the energy comparison used to cancel the original potential difference in the splitting estimate.

The two value comparisons give [Ee(S)−Ee(Yh)]−[Ef(S)−Ef(Yh)]≤δ[\mathbb Ee(S)-\mathbb Ee(Y_h)]-[\mathbb Ef(S)-\mathbb Ef(Y_h)]\le\delta[Ee(S)−Ee(Yh​)]−[Ef(S)−Ef(Yh​)]≤δ. Multiply by 2h2h2h and combine to obtain the displayed 2hδ+2h2GK2h\delta+2h^2GK2hδ+2h2GK remainder. In Lean, hpoint is the pointwise-in-time estimate, hi its integral, and hsum the finite value-gap cancellation. The conclusion never substitutes Lip⁡(∇e)\operatorname{Lip}(\nabla e)Lip(∇e) into this remainder.

Formal correspondence: splitting_cross_energy_le : The integrated reference-gradient cross term cancels the original nonsmooth energy up to a finite value gap and a drift error of order h² . The orthogonality premise is instantiated by the actual Brownian construction in lp_langevin_future_inner_zero , not by a convergence assumption.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.13.5.
