01Minkowski and finiteness §

Mathematical statement and proof

The identity Tx−a=(Tx−b)+(b−a)Tx-a=(Tx-b)+(b-a)Tx−a=(Tx−b)+(b−a) and Minkowski yield Rμ(a)≤Rμ(b)+∥b−a∥R_\mu(a)\le R_\mu(b)+\|b-a\|Rμ​(a)≤Rμ​(b)+∥b−a∥. A constant has that L2L^2L2 norm because μ\muμ has mass one. If Rμ(b)<∞R_\mu(b)<\inftyRμ​(b)<∞, the right side is finite, hence the error about aaa is finite too.

Write Ra=∥Tx−a∥L2(μ)R_a=\|Tx-a\|_{L^2(\mu)}Ra​=∥Tx−a∥L2(μ)​. The code's heq is the pointwise identity Tx−a=(Tx−b)+(b−a)Tx-a=(Tx-b)+(b-a)Tx−a=(Tx−b)+(b−a). Since μ\muμ has mass one, the constant function b−ab-ab−a has L2L^2L2 norm exactly ∥b−a∥\|b-a\|∥b−a∥. Thus eLpNorm_add_le gives Ra≤Rb+∥b−a∥R_a\le R_b+\|b-a\|Ra​≤Rb​+∥b−a∥ even if Rb=∞R_b=\inftyRb​=∞. The second declaration uses the finite right-hand side to prove Ra<∞R_a<\inftyRa​<∞; it does not assume a second moment about the new center.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • projectedRMS_center_le: Minkowski controls the change of a parameter center, with infinite moments still represented by the extended norm.
  • projectedRMS_center_finite: A finite error about one center implies finiteness about every center.

02Pass to ordinary square-root integrals §

Mathematical statement and proof

Monotonicity of the extended-to-real conversion is valid once the upper bound is finite. This proves the real RMS inequality. Use the proved finite moments at both centers to identify the norms with ∫∥Tx−a∥2dμ\sqrt{\int\|Tx-a\|^2d\mu}∫∥Tx−a∥2dμ​ and ∫∥Tx−b∥2dμ\sqrt{\int\|Tx-b\|^2d\mu}∫∥Tx−b∥2dμ​. The resulting formula cannot hide an infinite moment through the totalized real integral.

The order of operations matters: first prove finiteness at bbb, then at aaa, then apply ENNReal.toReal_mono. Only after these guards does projectedRMSReal_eq_sqrt_integral identify both real norms with (∫∥Tx−a∥2dμ)1/2(\int\|Tx-a\|^2d\mu)^{1/2}(∫∥Tx−a∥2dμ)1/2 and (∫∥Tx−b∥2dμ)1/2(\int\|Tx-b\|^2d\mu)^{1/2}(∫∥Tx−b∥2dμ)1/2. This prevents an infinite moment from being silently converted to the real number zero.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • projectedRMSReal_center_le: The usual real-valued center-change inequality, guarded by a proved finite moment at the comparison center.
  • sqrt_integral_center_le: The integral form follows after both finite quadratic moments have been established.
