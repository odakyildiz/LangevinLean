MYIPLA about the marginal maximizer of the original nonsmooth model

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

Let U=g1+g2U=g_1+g_2U=g1​+g2​ be a pair potential on Rdθ×Rdx\mathbb R^{d_\theta}\times\mathbb R^{d_x}Rdθ​×Rdx​, where g1g_1g1​ is differentiable and mmm-strongly convex with L1L_1L1​-Lipschitz gradient (m>0m>0m>0), and g2g_2g2​ is convex, bounded below and KKK-Lipschitz. For η>0\eta>0η>0 let Vη=g1+g2ηV_\eta=g_1+g_2^\etaVη​=g1​+g2η​ be the smoothed potential and Lη=L1+η−1L_\eta=L_1+\eta^{-1}Lη​=L1​+η−1. From it we derive that the original model has a marginal maximizer θU\theta_UθU​, i.e. a maximizer of θ↦∫e−U(θ,x) dx\theta\mapsto\int e^{-U(\theta,x)}\,dxθ↦∫e−U(θ,x)dx, and that ∥θU−θη∥≤ηK2/m\|\theta_U-\theta_\eta\|\le\sqrt{\eta K^2/m}∥θU​−θη​∥≤ηK2/m​ for every marginal maximizer θη\theta_\etaθη​ of the smoothed model.

Combined with the MYIPLA estimate of the previous section and Minkowski's inequality, this gives the main bound for MYIPLA relative to the original model: under Gaussian innovations independent of a square-integrable initialization and 0<γ≤2/(m+Lη)0<\gamma\le2/(m+L_\eta)0<γ≤2/(m+Lη​),

(E∥θn−θU∥2)1/2≤ηK2m+dθNm+(1−mγ)nW2(ρ0,πN,Vη)+3320 Lηmdx+dθN γ.\Bigl(\mathbb E\|\theta_n-\theta_U\|^2\Bigr)^{1/2}\le\sqrt{\frac{\eta K^2}{m}}+\sqrt{\frac{d_\theta}{Nm}}+(1-m\gamma)^nW_2(\rho_0,\pi_{N,V_\eta})+\frac{33}{20}\,\frac{L_\eta}{m}\sqrt{d_x+\frac{d_\theta}N}\,\sqrt\gamma .(E∥θn​−θU​∥2)1/2≤mηK2​​+Nmdθ​​​+(1−mγ)nW2​(ρ0​,πN,Vη​​)+2033​mLη​​dx​+Ndθ​​​γ​.

The smoothing contribution is of order η\sqrt\etaη​. 02The distance between the original and the smoothed marginal maximizers §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let g1g_1g1​ be differentiable and mmm-strongly convex with m>0m>0m>0, let g2g_2g2​ be convex, bounded below and KKK-Lipschitz, and η>0\eta>0η>0. ∥θU−θη∥≤ηK2m.\|\theta_U-\theta_\eta\|\le\sqrt{\frac{\eta K^2}{m}} .∥θU​−θη​∥≤mηK2​​.

Proof. By (1) of the previous step, Vη≤U≤Vη+δV_\eta\le U\le V_\eta+\deltaVη​≤U≤Vη​+δ with δ=ηK2/2\delta=\eta K^2/2δ=ηK2/2. The marginal-center comparison Proximal, Section 3.4.4 states that, under exactly these conditions, any marginal maximizers satisfy ∥θU−θη∥≤2δ/m\|\theta_U-\theta_\eta\|\le\sqrt{2\delta/m}∥θU​−θη​∥≤2δ/m​. With δ=ηK2/2\delta=\eta K^2/2δ=ηK2/2, 2δ/m=ηK2/m2\delta/m=\eta K^2/m2δ/m=ηK2/m. □\square□

The bound is of order η\sqrt\etaη​. No second derivative of UUU and no Taylor expansion of the envelope are used.
