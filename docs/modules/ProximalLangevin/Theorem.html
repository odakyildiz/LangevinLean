01A finite approximation creates an actual original center §

Mathematical statement and proof

The envelope gap gives Vη≤U≤Vη+ηK2/2V_\eta\le U\le V_\eta+\eta K^2/2Vη​≤U≤Vη​+ηK2/2, where U=g1+g2U=g_1+g_2U=g1​+g2​. The smooth strongly convex comparison supplies Gaussian domination and decay of the original marginal kU(θ)=∫e−U(θ,x)dxk_U(\theta)=\int e^{-U(\theta,x)}dxkU​(θ)=∫e−U(θ,x)dx. Its continuity and vanishing at infinity imply that it attains a positive maximum. Existence of this original center is derived, rather than added as an independent model premise.

02Compare the actual marginal centers §

Mathematical statement and proof

For the actual split Moreau approximation, δ=λK2/2\delta=\lambda K^2/2δ=λK2/2, so

∥θ⋆−θλ,⋆∥≤λK2m. \|\theta_\star-\theta_{\lambda,\star}\| \le\sqrt{\frac{\lambda K^2}{m}}. ∥θ⋆​−θλ,⋆​∥≤mλK2​​.

The original and smoothed marginal maximizers exist under the primitive assumptions. The square-integrable triangle inequality then gives

(E∥θn−θ⋆∥2)1/2≤(E∥θn−θλ,⋆∥2)1/2+∥θλ,⋆−θ⋆∥. \left(\mathbb E\|\theta_n-\theta_\star\|^2\right)^{1/2} \le\left(\mathbb E\|\theta_n-\theta_{\lambda,\star}\|^2\right)^{1/2} +\|\theta_{\lambda,\star}-\theta_\star\|. (E∥θn​−θ⋆​∥2)1/2≤(E∥θn​−θλ,⋆​∥2)1/2+∥θλ,⋆​−θ⋆​∥.

The finite-moment guards are proved before identifying extended norms with real square-root integrals. Substituting the smoothed-model convergence estimate proves the original-center result in Chapter 1.

This is deliberately weaker in smoothing order than Proposition A.1's claimed linear expansion. The formal theorem requires no Hessian along a parameter fiber and no uniform Taylor domination. The weaker order is a change in the formalized result, not a silently omitted term.

The finite envelope gap is 0≤U−Vη≤δ=ηK2/20\le U-V_\eta\le\delta=\eta K^2/20≤U−Vη​≤δ=ηK2/2. Integrating the corresponding exponential inequalities in the latent variable and taking logarithms gives a parameter-marginal potential gap of the same size. If θη\theta_\etaθη​ minimizes the smoothed marginal potential, strong convexity yields m2∥θU−θη∥2≤δ,∥θU−θη∥≤ηK2/m.\frac m2\|\theta_U-\theta_\eta\|^2\le\delta,\qquad\|\theta_U-\theta_\eta\|\le\sqrt{\eta K^2/m}.2m​∥θU​−θη​∥2≤δ,∥θU​−θη​∥≤ηK2/m​. The hypotheses hmaxU and hmaxη identify the actual two marginal maximizers, so this is a bound on their displacement rather than the choice of an arbitrary comparison center.

03Move the finite error and substitute the proved bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let U=g1+g2U=g_1+g_2U=g1​+g2​ on Rdθ+dx\mathbb R^{d_\theta+d_x}Rdθ​+dx​. Assume g1g_1g1​ is differentiable, mmm-strongly convex with m>0m>0m>0, and has L1L_1L1​-Lipschitz gradient. Assume g2g_2g2​ is finite-valued, convex, bounded below, and KKK-Lipschitz. For λ>0\lambda>0λ>0, set

Vλ=g1+g2λ,Lλ=L1+λ−1. V_\lambda=g_1+g_2^\lambda,\qquad L_\lambda=L_1+\lambda^{-1}. Vλ​=g1​+g2λ​,Lλ​=L1​+λ−1.

Take N>0N>0N>0, an initial state with finite second moment, independent standard Gaussian innovations at every coordinate and time, independent of initialization, and

0<γ≤2m+Lλ. 0<\gamma\le\frac{2}{m+L_\lambda}. 0<γ≤m+Lλ​2​.

Write μn\mu_nμn​ for the law of the actual scaled MYIPLA state and Πλ,N\Pi_{\lambda,N}Πλ,N​ for its continuous Gibbs target. Let θ⋆\theta_\starθ⋆​ maximize the original latent marginal ∫e−U(θ,x)dx\int e^{-U(\theta,x)}dx∫e−U(θ,x)dx. The formal estimate is

(E∥θn−θ⋆∥2)1/2≤λK2m+dθNm+(1−mγ)nW2(μ0,Πλ,N)+3320Lλmdx+dθNγ. \begin{aligned} \left(\mathbb E\|\theta_n-\theta_\star\|^2\right)^{1/2} \le{}&\sqrt{\frac{\lambda K^2}{m}} +\sqrt{\frac{d_\theta}{Nm}}\\ &+(1-m\gamma)^n W_2(\mu_0,\Pi_{\lambda,N})\\ &+\frac{33}{20}\frac{L_\lambda}{m} \sqrt{d_x+\frac{d_\theta}{N}}\sqrt\gamma. \end{aligned} (E∥θn​−θ⋆​∥2)1/2≤​mλK2​​+Nmdθ​​​+(1−mγ)nW2​(μ0​,Πλ,N​)+2033​mLλ​​dx​+Ndθ​​​γ​.​

The target normalization, finite transport moments, stationary reference, and equilibrium concentration are derived through the existing IPLA/ULA development. Neither the displayed convergence bound nor the smoothing bias is an extra assumption. The last term retains its explicit dependence on λ\lambdaλ; no uniformity in the smoothing scale is asserted. The paper's O(λ)+O(λ2)O(\lambda)+O(\lambda^2)O(λ)+O(λ2) smoothing expression is replaced by a finite O(λ)O(\sqrt\lambda)O(λ​) guarantee, so this is a related proved theorem, not a verbatim reproduction of Theorem 4.1.

In Lean the proof first establishes that the actual MYIPLA law is probability-valued and its error about the smoothed center is square integrable. The center-change inequality then has valid finite real square-root integrals. Add the proved center displacement to the smoothed theorem, giving exactly the displayed four terms.

First apply the proved MYIPLA bound about θη\theta_\etaθη​. Its finite second moment makes the center-change inequality valid in real RMS form. Then (E∥θn−θU∥2)1/2≤(E∥θn−θη∥2)1/2+∥θη−θU∥.\bigl(\mathbb E\|\theta_n-\theta_U\|^2\bigr)^{1/2}\le\bigl(\mathbb E\|\theta_n-\theta_\eta\|^2\bigr)^{1/2}+\|\theta_\eta-\theta_U\|.(E∥θn​−θU​∥2)1/2≤(E∥θn​−θη​∥2)1/2+∥θη​−θU​∥. Substitute the preceding center displacement estimate. Thus smoothing bias, equilibrium concentration and finite-step algorithmic error enter for three different proved reasons; the original nonsmooth target is never assumed to have a Lipschitz score.
