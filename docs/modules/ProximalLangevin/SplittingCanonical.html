Construct a stationary Brownian coupling for one true transition

The generic splitting map is Th(x,ξ)=T(x−h∇f1(x)+2hξ)\mathcal T_h(x,\xi)=T(x-h\nabla f_1(x)+\sqrt{2h}\xi)Th​(x,ξ)=T(x−h∇f1​(x)+2h​ξ). The reference target is the actual Gibbs law of F=f1+eF=f_1+eF=f1​+e. Assumptions concern potentials, true energy, bounded gradients and a finite input coupling.

01Measurable update and exact gradient additivity §

Mathematical statement and proof

The map is the composition of the measurable Gaussian forward update with the Lipschitz map TTT. Differentiability proves ∇(f1+e)=∇f1+∇e\nabla(f_1+e)=\nabla f_1+\nabla e∇(f1​+e)=∇f1​+∇e. Almost everywhere representative identities then prove additivity of the corresponding L2L^2L2 drift maps, as an equality of actual L2L^2L2 elements.

02Extend any finite-cost coupling by canonical Brownian noise §

Mathematical statement and proof

Start with any coupling ccc between μ\muμ and the Gibbs target, with finite quadratic cost. Extend it by the canonical independent Brownian source. The initial reference has the exact Gibbs law and finite moments; the constructed Langevin integral solution stays stationary. Its natural-past measurability and Brownian independence prove the future orthogonality. Uniform moments give

G=2L2d/m+2K2+K,σ=2d.G=\sqrt{2L^2d/m+2K^2}+K,\qquad\sigma=\sqrt{2d}.G=2L2d/m+2K2​+K,σ=2d​.

Use the Brownian increment over [0,h][0,h][0,h] as the Gaussian update noise; its independence gives the exact Markov marginal. Apply the derived analytic one-step theorem. The explicit output pair is a finite coupling and proves

W2(μTh,π)2≤(1−mh)cost⁡(c)2+F(m,h,L,G,σ,K,δ).W_2(\mu\mathcal T_h,\pi)^2\le(1-mh)\operatorname{cost}(c)^2+\mathcal F(m,h,L,G,\sigma,K,\delta).W2​(μTh​,π)2≤(1−mh)cost(c)2+F(m,h,L,G,σ,K,δ).

The construction discharges stationarity, Gaussian laws, finite moments and orthogonality; they are not extra convergence hypotheses.
