In a Hilbert L2L^2L2 space, let f1f_1f1​ be mmm-strongly convex with LLL-Lipschitz gradient, and let the actual Lipschitz map TTT satisfy the nonsmooth energy inequality for fff. The reference solves the actual stationary smoothed integral equation for f1+ef_1+ef1​+e, where e≤f≤e+δe\le f\le e+\deltae≤f≤e+δ.

Mathematical statement and proof

For m,h>0m,h>0m,h>0, drift moment GGG, noise moment factor σ\sigmaσ, bounded nonsmooth score KKK and value gap δ\deltaδ, define

F=(1+(mh)−1)[Lh(Gh+σh)]2+2hδ+2h2GK+h2K2.\mathcal F=(1+(mh)^{-1})[Lh(Gh+\sigma\sqrt h)]^2+2h\delta+2h^2GK+h^2K^2.F=(1+(mh)−1)[Lh(Gh+σh​)]2+2hδ+2h2GK+h2K2.

The four terms are the weighted smooth local defect, the envelope value gap, the drift cross term, and the genuine proximal energy remainder.
