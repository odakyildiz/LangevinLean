01Lift value Lipschitzness and a bounded envelope score §

Mathematical statement and proof

The averaged potential is KKK-Lipschitz, so multiplying it by NNN proves Lip⁡(Fg)≤NK\operatorname{Lip}(F_g)\le NKLip(Fg​)≤NK. The envelope has the same value Lipschitz constant KKK, hence FgηF_{g^\eta}Fgη​ has constant NKNKNK too. Its actual gradient norm is therefore at most NKNKNK, uniformly over η>0\eta>0η>0.

02Sum the finite gap and preserve the split decomposition §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply 0≤g−gη≤ηK2/20\le g-g^\eta\le\eta K^2/20≤g−gη≤ηK2/2 at every joint pair and add:

0≤Fg(z)−Fgη(z)≤NηK2/2.0\le F_g(z)-F_{g^\eta}(z)\le N\eta K^2/2.0≤Fg​(z)−Fgη​(z)≤NηK2/2.

Finite sum distributivity also proves Fg1+g2η=Fg1+Fg2ηF_{g_1+g_2^\eta}=F_{g_1}+F_{g_2^\eta}Fg1​+g2η​​=Fg1​​+Fg2η​​ as an equality of functions. These exact identities supply the deterministic inputs to the stochastic splitting estimate.
