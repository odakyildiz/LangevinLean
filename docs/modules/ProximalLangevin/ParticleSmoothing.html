01The lifted value bound and envelope-gradient magnitude §

Mathematical statement and proof

The averaged potential has value Lipschitz constant KKK. Multiplication by NNN gives Lip⁡(Fg)≤NK\operatorname{Lip}(F_g)\le NKLip(Fg​)≤NK. Since the envelope retains single-pair value constant KKK, its lifted sum FgηF_{g^\eta}Fgη​ has constant NKNKNK. Differentiability therefore bounds its gradient norm by NKNKNK for every η>0\eta>0η>0.

Equivalently multiplication by NNN gives lifted value constant NKNKNK. The envelope’s single-pair constant KKK yields ∥∇VN,η(2)∥≤NK\|\nabla V_{N,\eta}^{(2)}\|\le NK∥∇VN,η(2)​∥≤NK. This uses the norm bound for the derivative of a Lipschitz function, rather than the gradient Lipschitz constant N/ηN/\etaN/η, and is consequently uniform as η↓0\eta\downarrow0η↓0. In the averaged notation the constants are KKK and NKNKNK for the full sum, giving ∥∇Fg2η∥≤NK\|\nabla F_{g_2^\eta}\|\le NK∥∇Fg2η​​∥≤NK independently of η\etaη. Rapid spatial variation of the gradient does not change this bound on its magnitude.

Earlier results used here: IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.1.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

Formal correspondence: potential_lipschitz : Lifting a globally Lipschitz pair potential to the scaled state multiplies its Lipschitz constant by the number of particles. potential_moreau_lipschitz : The lifted true Moreau approximation preserves the particle Lipschitz constant, uniformly over positive smoothing scales. potential_moreau_gradient_norm_le : The actual gradient of the lifted Moreau term is bounded by NKNKNK, independently of the smoothing scale.

potential_lipschitz: the intermediate facts include hn, hav, hh, heq, in source order; the source steps apply or rewrite LipschitzWith.of_dist_le_mul, Real.dist_eq, NNReal.coe_mul, NNReal.coe_natCast, mul_assoc. The remaining scalar rearrangements are checked by field_simp.

potential_moreau_lipschitz: the proof is the application potential_lipschitz hN (moreau_lipschitz_of_lipschitz hg hb hη hK). It specializes that result with the hypotheses and objects in the exact statement.

potential_moreau_gradient_norm_le: the proof is the application simpa only [NNReal.coe_mul, NNReal.coe_natCast] using. It specializes that result with the hypotheses and objects in the exact statement.

02The summed envelope gap and split-potential identity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply 0≤g−gη≤ηK2/20\le g-g^\eta\le\eta K^2/20≤g−gη≤ηK2/2 at each pair and sum the finite inequalities:

0≤Fg(z)−Fgη(z)≤NηK2/2.0\le F_g(z)-F_{g^\eta}(z)\le N\eta K^2/2.0≤Fg​(z)−Fgη​(z)≤NηK2/2.

Distributivity also gives the function identity Fg1+g2η=Fg1+Fg2ηF_{g_1+g_2^\eta}=F_{g_1}+F_{g_2^\eta}Fg1​+g2η​​=Fg1​​+Fg2η​​. In nonsmooth-component notation the bound 0≤g2−g2η≤ηK2/20\le g_2-g_2^\eta\le\eta K^2/20≤g2​−g2η​≤ηK2/2 has NNN summands, so

0≤VN(2)−VN,η(2)≤NηK2/2.0\le V_N^{(2)}-V_{N,\eta}^{(2)}\le N\eta K^2/2.0≤VN(2)​−VN,η(2)​≤NηK2/2.

Expanding VN(g1+g2η)V_N(g_1+g_2^\eta)VN​(g1​+g2η​) and distributing the sum gives VN(g1)+VN(g2η)V_N(g_1)+V_N(g_2^\eta)VN​(g1​)+VN​(g2η​). Each nonnegative gap is at most ηK2/2\eta K^2/2ηK2/2; summing NNN such gaps proves the displayed total gap directly. Differentiating the sum identity, under the established regularity, separates the smooth and envelope reference forces in the splitting argument.

Earlier results used here: IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.1.
