Sensitivity to the proximal parameter

For a convex bounded-below KKK-Lipschitz function, the actual minimizer satisfies ∥Pλ(x)−Pη(x)∥≤K∣λ−η∣\|P_\lambda(x)-P_\eta(x)\|\le K|\lambda-\eta|∥Pλ​(x)−Pη​(x)∥≤K∣λ−η∣. This feeds a pathwise comparison of PIPGLA chains with different positive proximal parameters.

01Bounded supports imply value Lipschitzness §

Mathematical statement and proof

Choose a genuine subgradient sxs_xsx​ at each point with ∥sx∥≤K\|s_x\|\le K∥sx​∥≤K. Its support at yyy gives f(x)−f(y)≤⟨sx,x−y⟩≤K∥x−y∥f(x)-f(y)\le\langle s_x,x-y\rangle\le K\|x-y\|f(x)−f(y)≤⟨sx​,x−y⟩≤K∥x−y∥. Reverse x,yx,yx,y to bound the absolute difference. This derives global Lipschitzness from a bounded section, without differentiability.

02Compare two actual proximal minimizers §

Mathematical statement and proof

Write q=Pη(x)q=P_\eta(x)q=Pη​(x) and s=(x−q)/ηs=(x-q)/\etas=(x−q)/η. Actual proximal optimality gives s∈∂f(q)s\in\partial f(q)s∈∂f(q) and ∥s∥≤K\|s\|\le K∥s∥≤K. ∥Pλ(x)−q∥≤∥x−(q+λs)∥=∣η−λ∣∥s∥≤K∣λ−η∣.\|P_\lambda(x)-q\|\le\|x-(q+\lambda s)\|=|\eta-\lambda|\|s\|\le K|\lambda-\eta|.∥Pλ​(x)−q∥≤∥x−(q+λs)∥=∣η−λ∣∥s∥≤K∣λ−η∣.

This proves the claimed sensitivity for every pair of positive scales.

03The averaged pair map preserves a uniform map difference §

Mathematical statement and proof

Coordinate linearity proves Pair⁡P−Q=Pair⁡P−Pair⁡Q\operatorname{Pair}_{P-Q}=\operatorname{Pair}_P-\operatorname{Pair}_QPairP−Q​=PairP​−PairQ​. If sup⁡x∥P(x)−Q(x)∥≤c\sup_x\|P(x)-Q(x)\|\le csupx​∥P(x)−Q(x)∥≤c, Jensen bounds the output square by the average of quantities at most c2c^2c2, so the output norm is at most ccc. Apply this with the single-pair proximal sensitivity to obtain the same K∣λ−η∣K|\lambda-\eta|K∣λ−η∣ bound for the actual particle map.

04Solve the pathwise parameter-perturbation recursion §

Mathematical statement and proof

Insert an intermediate update with the same parameter, then combine shared-noise contraction and the uniform parameter difference:

en+1≤(1−mγ)en+K∣λ−η∣.e_{n+1}\le(1-m\gamma)e_n+K|\lambda-\eta|.en+1​≤(1−mγ)en​+K∣λ−η∣.

05A proximal corridor gives an explicit scalar bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

If 0<mγ<10<m\gamma<10<mγ<1 and γ≤λ≤γ/(1−mγ)\gamma\le\lambda\le\gamma/(1-m\gamma)γ≤λ≤γ/(1−mγ), then ∣λ−γ∣=λ−γ≤mγ2/(1−mγ)|\lambda-\gamma|=\lambda-\gamma\le m\gamma^2/(1-m\gamma)∣λ−γ∣=λ−γ≤mγ2/(1−mγ). Divide by the positive mγm\gammamγ and multiply by KKK to get K∣λ−γ∣/(mγ)≤Kγ/(1−mγ)K|\lambda-\gamma|/(m\gamma)\le K\gamma/(1-m\gamma)K∣λ−γ∣/(mγ)≤Kγ/(1−mγ). This is a scalar consequence of the true sensitivity theorem.
