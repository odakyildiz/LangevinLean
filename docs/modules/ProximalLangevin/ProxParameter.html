The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

For a convex bounded-below KKK-Lipschitz function, the proximal map satisfies ∥Pλ(x)−Pη(x)∥≤K∣λ−η∣\|P_\lambda(x)-P_\eta(x)\|\le K|\lambda-\eta|∥Pλ​(x)−Pη​(x)∥≤K∣λ−η∣ at positive scales. The bound follows by representing one proximal point as a proximal point at the other scale with a shifted input. Jensen transfers this bound to the averaged particle map without an extra particle factor. Combined with common-innovation contraction, it gives finite-time and uniform-iteration comparisons of splitting chains at two scales.

01Value Lipschitzness from bounded supporting subgradients §

Mathematical statement and proof

Suppose a subgradient sxs_xsx​ is chosen at every point with ∥sx∥≤K\|s_x\|\le K∥sx​∥≤K. Its support evaluated at yyy and Cauchy–Schwarz give f(x)−f(y)≤⟨sx,x−y⟩≤K∥x−y∥f(x)-f(y)\le\langle s_x,x-y\rangle\le K\|x-y\|f(x)−f(y)≤⟨sx​,x−y⟩≤K∥x−y∥. Exchange x,yx,yx,y to bound the absolute difference.

Thus a subgradient section bounded by KKK gives value Lipschitz constant KKK. Every selected vector must satisfy the supporting inequality; the norm bound alone is insufficient. No derivative of the potential is required.

Formal correspondence: lipschitz_of_bounded_subgradients : A bounded genuine subgradient at every input implies global Lipschitzness. In particular, the paper's uniformly bounded minimal section supplies this hypothesis, without adding a separate Lipschitz assumption to C1.

03Uniform comparison of the averaged pair maps §

Mathematical statement and proof

If sup⁡x∥P(x)−Q(x)∥≤c\sup_x\|P(x)-Q(x)\|\le csupx​∥P(x)−Q(x)∥≤c, the averaged-square inequality bounds every summand by c2c^2c2 and hence the state norm by ccc. Under ∥P(x)−Q(x)∥≤c\|P(x)-Q(x)\|\le c∥P(x)−Q(x)∥≤c, Jensen and latent scaling give squared state difference at most N−1∑ic2=c2N^{-1}\sum_i c^2=c^2N−1∑i​c2=c2. Insert an update at the first scale and the second current state. Common-innovation contraction controls the change of state, and proximal sensitivity controls the change of scale. Thus

05A scale comparison within the specified interval §
