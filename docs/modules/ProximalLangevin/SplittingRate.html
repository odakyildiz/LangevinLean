A finite coefficient for the splitting error floor

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

The explicit local forcing is at most mh2Cmh^2Cmh2C when its potential gap is at most hK2/2hK^2/2hK2/2. Its coefficient uses curvature, smooth-gradient regularity, drift and noise moments, and the bounded perturbation-gradient magnitude. It contains no inverse envelope gradient constant. Thus the one-step squared forcing is O(h2)O(h^2)O(h2) under δ≤hK2/2\delta\le hK^2/2δ≤hK2/2, as required for a transport error tending to zero with the step.

Mathematical statement and proof

For m>0m>0m>0 and G,K≥0G,K\ge0G,K≥0 define

C(m,L,G,σ,K)=4L2m2(G2m+σ2)+2m(GK+K2).C(m,L,G,\sigma,K)=\frac{4L^2}{m^2}\left(\frac{G^2}{m}+\sigma^2\right)+\frac2m(GK+K^2).C(m,L,G,σ,K)=m24L2​(mG2​+σ2)+m2​(GK+K2).

Every squared term is nonnegative and the denominators are positive, giving C≥0C\ge0C≥0. Each summand of CCC is finite under m>0m>0m>0 and the stated nonnegative magnitude bounds. The coefficient is not asserted optimal; its explicit dependence on dimension, curvature, and particle factors is retained in the quantitative estimate.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

splittingCouplingForcing_le is the scalar estimate used after the stationary coupling has supplied its four forcing terms. hlocal bounds the squared increment by twice the sum of squares. hGscale uses hsmall to remove one time power, and hlocal' collects that estimate. hweight allows multiplication by the Young weight. The identity heq and bound htwice control its coefficient; hdelta' inserts the potential gap. Finally hC expands the proposed constant and hfirst bounds the weighted smooth-error term. Unfolding splittingCouplingForcing leaves precisely the scalar inequality stated above. The actual law recurrence is established separately in the splitting endpoint module.

Formal correspondence: splittingCouplingForcing_le : With a gap of at most hK2/2hK^2/2hK2/2, the derived one-step forcing is bounded by mh2Cmh^2Cmh2C, where CCC is independent of the smoothing parameter.
