01Curvature and gradient regularity of the smoothed sum §

Mathematical statement and proof

The convexity inequality for the envelope adds no negative curvature to that of the smooth term. Its sum with the smooth term is therefore strongly convex with modulus mmm. The triangle inequality for gradient differences gives gradient constant Lη=L1+1/ηL_\eta=L_1+1/\etaLη​=L1​+1/η. These are constants in the joint-pair space; the replicated-pair norm identity multiplies both by particle count when forming the full-state potential.

The scalar potential is Vη=g1+g2ηV_\eta=g_1+g_2^\etaVη​=g1​+g2η​. Its envelope is convex, so adding the two convex-combination inequalities preserves strong convexity mmm. Differentiability of both terms proves ∇Vη=∇g1+∇g2η\nabla V_\eta=\nabla g_1+\nabla g_2^\eta∇Vη​=∇g1​+∇g2η​; the triangle inequality then adds gradient constants. The nonnegative-real constant used by Lean equals L1+η−1L_1+\eta^{-1}L1​+η−1 when η>0\eta>0η>0.

02Equality of the recursion laws and finite parameter moments §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define ρn=Q∘Zn−1\rho_n=Q\circ Z_n^{-1}ρn​=Q∘Zn−1​ from the expanded recursion. The residual-gradient identity makes this recursion pointwise equal to IPLA for any differentiable envelope satisfying that identity. The corresponding state is an L2L^2L2 random vector. Bounded parameter projection and subtraction of a deterministic center preserve L2L^2L2.

More explicitly, a globally Lipschitz gradient has at most linear growth, so L2L^2L2 initial data and L2L^2L2 innovations give finite second moments at every finite step. The resulting parameter error belongs to L2L^2L2. Almost-sure equality of representatives gives equality of pushforward laws. These moment and law statements are established independently of the convergence estimate and justify its ordinary square-root expectation.

Formal correspondence: myiplaLaws : The law of the expanded MYIPLA recursion, for arbitrary supplied maps. myiplaLaws_eq_iplaLaws : Pointwise equivalence identifies the actual MYIPLA law with the IPLA law. myiplaLaws_parameter_memLp : Every projected MYIPLA error has a finite second moment whenever the initialization and innovations are in L2 . This is derived from the actual chain, without Gaussianity, equilibrium, or an error bound assumption.

04The theorem for the Moreau envelope §

Mathematical statement and proof

For the Moreau envelope, proximal optimality and the neighboring-objective comparisons prove convexity, differentiability, ∇g2η=η−1(I−Pη)\nabla g_2^\eta=\eta^{-1}(I-P_\eta)∇g2η​=η−1(I−Pη​), and gradient constant η−1\eta^{-1}η−1. Substitution gives the stated smoothed convergence estimate. A measurable L2L^2L2 representative pushes the initial probability space forward to a probability law.

The nonexpansive residual map gives the constant 1/η1/\eta1/η. These established envelope properties replace the general envelope assumptions. Linear growth of the update proves finite moments, and measurable pushforward proves mass one, independently of the numerical transport estimate.

For the genuine envelope, the proximal module supplies the residual identity ∇g2η=(I−Pη)/η\nabla g_2^\eta=(I-P_\eta)/\eta∇g2η​=(I−Pη​)/η, its differentiability and convexity, and the score Lipschitz constant η−1\eta^{-1}η−1. Adding the smooth part gives Vη=g1+g2ηV_\eta=g_1+g_2^\etaVη​=g1​+g2η​, modulus mmm and score constant L1+η−1L_1+\eta^{-1}L1​+η−1. The theorem therefore discharges each abstract envelope assumption before applying the sharp IPLA estimate. The associated probability and moment declarations establish that the final law has total mass one and a finite projected RMS, independently of merely writing its integral.

Formal correspondence: moreau_myipla_parameter_rms : MYIPLA with the actual proximity minimizer and its actual Moreau envelope. Every analytic envelope property used by the stochastic theorem is derived from convexity and a lower bound on g₂ . In particular, no residual-gradient identity, stationarity result, concentration bound, or convergence estimate is an additional hypothesis of this theorem. moreau_myipla_parameter_memLp : Finite moments for MYIPLA with its genuine Moreau/proximal construction. moreau_myipla_isProbability : The actual MYIPLA output is a probability law. Measurability follows from the identified L2 recursion, rather than being assumed for the map.
