01Construct the right convexity and smoothness constants §

Mathematical statement and proof

The scalar potential is Vη=g1+g2ηV_\eta=g_1+g_2^\etaVη​=g1​+g2η​. Its envelope is convex, so adding the two convex-combination inequalities preserves strong convexity mmm. Differentiability of both terms proves ∇Vη=∇g1+∇g2η\nabla V_\eta=\nabla g_1+\nabla g_2^\eta∇Vη​=∇g1​+∇g2η​; the triangle inequality then adds gradient constants. The nonnegative-real constant used by Lean equals L1+η−1L_1+\eta^{-1}L1​+η−1 when η>0\eta>0η>0.

02Identify actual laws before using convergence §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define ρn=Q∘Zn−1\rho_n=Q\circ Z_n^{-1}ρn​=Q∘Zn−1​ from the explicitly expanded chain. The pointwise chain identity gives equality of pushforward laws with IPLA for any differentiable envelope having the stated residual gradient. The IPLA recursion is an actual L2L^2L2 random variable. Projection by the bounded parameter map and subtraction of a constant preserve L2L^2L2, proving finite parameter error about every fixed center without any convergence assumption.

03Apply sharp IPLA to a general residual envelope §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Moreau envelope is convex. Therefore adding it to the mmm-strongly convex function g1g_1g1​ preserves the constant mmm. Adding their gradient-Lipschitz estimates gives Lλ=L1+1/λL_\lambda=L_1+1/\lambdaLλ​=L1​+1/λ. The replicated-pair identity ∑i∥Jiv∥2=N∥v∥2\sum_i\|J_i v\|^2=N\|v\|^2∑i​∥Ji​v∥2=N∥v∥2 then yields lifted strong convexity NmNmNm, lifted smoothness NLλNL_\lambdaNLλ​, and lifted dimension dθ+Ndxd_\theta+Nd_xdθ​+Ndx​.

Applying the existing Gaussian ULA result at step γ/N\gamma/Nγ/N produces the contraction (1−mγ)n(1-m\gamma)^n(1−mγ)n and the discretization term

The step condition γ≤2/(m+Lλ)\gamma\le2/(m+L_\lambda)γ≤2/(m+Lλ​) is the corresponding scaled ULA condition. Initial finite second moments and Gaussian-law independence are explicit. The continuous reference and finite transport quantities are constructed by the shared theorem, so no convergence estimate or invariant measure is an additional MYIPLA hypothesis.

The law-identification helpers also prove that the actual expanded recursion is probability-valued and that its parameter error about every fixed center belongs to L2L^2L2. 04Discharge the envelope and moment hypotheses §

Mathematical statement and proof

For the actual Moreau envelope, the proximal calculus proves all inputs: convexity, differentiability, ∇g2η=η−1(I−Pη)\nabla g_2^\eta=\eta^{-1}(I-P_\eta)∇g2η​=η−1(I−Pη​), and gradient Lipschitz constant η−1\eta^{-1}η−1. Substitute these into the generic smoothed theorem and the finite-moment lemma. The output is the pushforward by an almost everywhere measurable L2L^2L2 representative; mapping the whole probability space to the whole state space proves total mass one.
