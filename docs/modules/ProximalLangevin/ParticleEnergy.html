The energy inequality for averaged pairwise proximal outputs

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

The pair space is Rdθ×Rdx\mathbb R^{d_\theta}\times\mathbb R^{d_x}Rdθ​×Rdx​, and the state space is Rdθ×(Rdx)N\mathbb R^{d_\theta}\times(\mathbb R^{d_x})^NRdθ​×(Rdx​)N with points z=(θ,z1,…,zN)z=(\theta,z_1,\dots,z_N)z=(θ,z1​,…,zN​), Euclidean norm ∥z∥2=∥θ∥2+∑i∥zi∥2\|z\|^2=\|\theta\|^2+\sum_i\|z_i\|^2∥z∥2=∥θ∥2+∑i​∥zi​∥2, and physical particles xi=Nzix_i=\sqrt N z_ixi​=N​zi​. The iii-th pair of a state is Jiz=(θ,Nzi)J_iz=(\theta,\sqrt Nz_i)Ji​z=(θ,N​zi​), and the lifted potential of a pair function ggg is Fg(z)=∑i=1Ng(Jiz)F_g(z)=\sum_{i=1}^Ng(J_iz)Fg​(z)=∑i=1N​g(Ji​z). Given any map PPP of the pair space, the pairwise map P\mathcal PP applies PPP to each pair, averages the NNN parameter outputs and rescales the latent outputs:

(Pz)θ=1N∑i=1N[P(Jiz)]θ,(Pz)i=1N[P(Jiz)]x.(\mathcal Pz)_\theta=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N\bigl[P(J_iz)\bigr]_\theta,\qquad(\mathcal Pz)_i=\frac{1}{\sqrt N}\bigl[P(J_iz)\bigr]_x .(Pz)θ​=N1​i=1∑N​[P(Ji​z)]θ​,(Pz)i​=N​1​[P(Ji​z)]x​.

This section proves ∥Pz−a∥2≤N−1∑i∥P(Jiz)−Jia∥2\|\mathcal Pz-a\|^2\le N^{-1}\sum_i\|P(J_iz)-J_ia\|^2∥Pz−a∥2≤N−1∑i​∥P(Ji​z)−Ji​a∥2 for every PPP (Jensen's inequality for the averaged parameter, exact scaling for the latent blocks), and combines it with the single-pair energy inequality of the previous section. For P=PλP=P_\lambdaP=Pλ​, the proximal map of a convex, bounded-below pair function ggg with bounded subgradients, the result is

∥Pz−a∥2≤∥z−a∥2−2λN(Fg(z)−Fg(a))+λ2C.\|\mathcal Pz-a\|^2\le\|z-a\|^2-\frac{2\lambda}{N}\bigl(F_g(z)-F_g(a)\bigr)+\lambda^2C.∥Pz−a∥2≤∥z−a∥2−N2λ​(Fg​(z)−Fg​(a))+λ2C.

The averaged output is in general not a proximal point of FgF_gFg​ on the state space, so this inequality is proved directly; it is the deterministic energy estimate used for the PIPGLA splitting scheme.

01Jensen's inequality for the averaged parameter §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let N≥1N\ge1N≥1.

  1. For real numbers b1,…,bNb_1,\dots,b_Nb1​,…,bN​, N(1N∑i=1Nbi)2≤∑i=1Nbi2.(1)N\Bigl(\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^Nb_i\Bigr)^2\le\sum_{i=1}^Nb_i^2.\qquad(1)N(N1​i=1∑N​bi​)2≤i=1∑N​bi2​.(1)
  2. For every map PPP from the pair space to itself and all states z,az,az,a, ∥Pz−a∥2≤1N∑i=1N∥P(Jiz)−Jia∥2.(2)\|\mathcal Pz-a\|^2\le\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N\bigl\|P(J_iz)-J_ia\bigr\|^2.\qquad(2)∥Pz−a∥2≤N1​i=1∑N​​P(Ji​z)−Ji​a​2.(2)

(b) Write pi=P(Jiz)=(pi,θ,pi,x)p_i=P(J_iz)=(p_{i,\theta},p_{i,x})pi​=P(Ji​z)=(pi,θ​,pi,x​) and a=(aθ,a1,…,aN)a=(a_\theta,a_1,\dots,a_N)a=(aθ​,a1​,…,aN​), so that Jia=(aθ,Nai)J_ia=(a_\theta,\sqrt Na_i)Ji​a=(aθ​,N​ai​). By the definition of the Euclidean norm on the state space,

Parameter block. For each parameter coordinate jjj, the common comparison value can be moved inside the average: N−1∑ipi,θ,j−aθ,j=N−1∑i(pi,θ,j−aθ,j)N^{-1}\sum_ip_{i,\theta,j}-a_{\theta,j}=N^{-1}\sum_i(p_{i,\theta,j}-a_{\theta,j})N−1∑i​pi,θ,j​−aθ,j​=N−1∑i​(pi,θ,j​−aθ,j​). By (1) with bi=pi,θ,j−aθ,jb_i=p_{i,\theta,j}-a_{\theta,j}bi​=pi,θ,j​−aθ,j​,

N(1N∑ipi,θ,j−aθ,j)2≤∑i(pi,θ,j−aθ,j)2.N\Bigl(\frac1N\sum_ip_{i,\theta,j}-a_{\theta,j}\Bigr)^2\le\sum_i\bigl(p_{i,\theta,j}-a_{\theta,j}\bigr)^2 .N(N1​i∑​pi,θ,j​−aθ,j​)2≤i∑​(pi,θ,j​−aθ,j​)2.

Summing over jjj gives N∥N−1∑ipi,θ−aθ∥2≤∑i∥pi,θ−aθ∥2N\|N^{-1}\sum_ip_{i,\theta}-a_\theta\|^2\le\sum_i\|p_{i,\theta}-a_\theta\|^2N∥N−1∑i​pi,θ​−aθ​∥2≤∑i​∥pi,θ​−aθ​∥2.

Inserting both into (3),

N∥Pz−a∥2≤∑i=1N(∥pi,θ−aθ∥2+∥pi,x−Nai∥2)=∑i=1N∥pi−Jia∥2,N\|\mathcal Pz-a\|^2\le\sum_{i=1}^N\Bigl(\|p_{i,\theta}-a_\theta\|^2+\|p_{i,x}-\sqrt Na_i\|^2\Bigr)=\sum_{i=1}^N\|p_i-J_ia\|^2,N∥Pz−a∥2≤i=1∑N​(∥pi,θ​−aθ​∥2+∥pi,x​−N​ai​∥2)=i=1∑N​∥pi​−Ji​a∥2,

Only (1) is an inequality; the latent contributions are equalities. No property of PPP is used.

pairwiseMap_sq_dist_le is (2), for an arbitrary map P.

  1. hθ j is the parameter inequality for coordinate jjj; its hid moves the common comparison value inside the average (Finset.sum_sub_distrib, Finset.sum_const), and average_sq_le finishes.
  2. hx i j is the latent equality; hid is N(N−1/2p−a)=p−Na\sqrt N(N^{-1/2}p-a)=p-\sqrt NaN​(N−1/2p−a)=p−N​a, squared with congrArg and simplified with mul_pow and hs2 (N)2=N(\sqrt N)^2=N(N​)2=N.

02Summing the pairwise proximal energy inequalities §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let N≥1N\ge1N≥1, let ggg be a convex function on the pair space that is bounded below, let λ>0\lambda>0λ>0, let z,az,az,a be states and C∈RC\in\mathbb RC∈R. Assume that for every iii there is a subgradient sis_isi​ of ggg at JizJ_izJi​z with ∥si∥2≤C\|s_i\|^2\le C∥si​∥2≤C. Let P\mathcal PP be the pairwise map built from the proximal map PλP_\lambdaPλ​ of ggg. Then

∥Pz−a∥2≤∥z−a∥2−2λN(Fg(z)−Fg(a))+λ2C.\|\mathcal Pz-a\|^2\le\|z-a\|^2-\frac{2\lambda}{N}\bigl(F_g(z)-F_g(a)\bigr)+\lambda^2C .∥Pz−a∥2≤∥z−a∥2−N2λ​(Fg​(z)−Fg​(a))+λ2C.

Proof. Pairwise inequalities. For each iii, the energy inequality of the previous section (convexity and lower bound of ggg, λ>0\lambda>0λ>0, subgradient sis_isi​ at the input JizJ_izJi​z with ∥si∥2≤C\|s_i\|^2\le C∥si​∥2≤C, comparison point JiaJ_iaJi​a) gives

∥Pλ(Jiz)−Jia∥2≤∥Jiz−Jia∥2−2λ(g(Jiz)−g(Jia))+λ2C.(1)\|P_\lambda(J_iz)-J_ia\|^2\le\|J_iz-J_ia\|^2-2\lambda\bigl(g(J_iz)-g(J_ia)\bigr)+\lambda^2C.\qquad(1)∥Pλ​(Ji​z)−Ji​a∥2≤∥Ji​z−Ji​a∥2−2λ(g(Ji​z)−g(Ji​a))+λ2C.(1)

The norm identity. Each JiJ_iJi​ is linear, so Jiz−Jia=Ji(z−a)J_iz-J_ia=J_i(z-a)Ji​z−Ji​a=Ji​(z−a). For any state w=(wθ,w1,…,wN)w=(w_\theta,w_1,\dots,w_N)w=(wθ​,w1​,…,wN​),

∑i=1N∥Jiw∥2=∑i=1N(∥wθ∥2+N∥wi∥2)=N∥wθ∥2+N∑i=1N∥wi∥2=N∥w∥2,(2)\sum_{i=1}^N\|J_iw\|^2=\sum_{i=1}^N\bigl(\|w_\theta\|^2+N\|w_i\|^2\bigr)=N\|w_\theta\|^2+N\sum_{i=1}^N\|w_i\|^2=N\|w\|^2,\qquad(2)i=1∑N​∥Ji​w∥2=i=1∑N​(∥wθ​∥2+N∥wi​∥2)=N∥wθ​∥2+Ni=1∑N​∥wi​∥2=N∥w∥2,(2)

because the parameter block occurs in each of the NNN pairs and each latent block occurs in one pair, multiplied by N\sqrt NN​.

Summation. Summing (1) over iii and using (2) with w=z−aw=z-aw=z−a and ∑ig(Jiz)=Fg(z)\sum_ig(J_iz)=F_g(z)∑i​g(Ji​z)=Fg​(z),

By (2) of the previous step applied to P=PλP=P_\lambdaP=Pλ​, the left-hand side divided by NNN bounds ∥Pz−a∥2\|\mathcal Pz-a\|^2∥Pz−a∥2. Dividing the display by N>0N>0N>0 proves the claim. □\square□

The minimizing property of PλP_\lambdaPλ​ is used only pair by pair, in (1). The averaged output Pz\mathcal PzPz is not, in general, a minimizer of w↦Fg(w)+N∥w−z∥2/(2λ)w\mapsto F_g(w)+N\|w-z\|^2/(2\lambda)w↦Fg​(w)+N∥w−z∥2/(2λ) on the state space; an explicit two-particle example is given in Proximal, Section 3.50.4. The inequality of this step is therefore proved for the averaged map itself. A subgradient of minimal norm, when available, is one admissible choice of sis_isi​.
