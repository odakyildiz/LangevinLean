Actual splitting laws, L² realizations and iid law transfer

The pointwise splitting update applies the averaged true proximal map after a noisy smooth step. Convexity and a lower bound for g2g_2g2​, positive λ\lambdaλ, N>0N>0N>0 and a Lipschitz smooth gradient make this update measurable and preserve finite quadratic moments.

Mathematical statement and proof

Nonexpansiveness proves Lip⁡(Pair⁡Pλ)≤1\operatorname{Lip}(\operatorname{Pair}_{P_\lambda})\le1Lip(PairPλ​​)≤1. Induction proves equality of the custom pointwise chain with the generic noise-driven chain.

02Realize the actual chain inside L² §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define the algorithm law as the pushforward by the explicit chain. Define an L2L^2L2 update by applying the Lipschitz pairwise map to the L2L^2L2 noisy Euler update. Their representatives agree almost everywhere by the composition and addition identities in L2L^2L2. Inductively every iterate lies in L2L^2L2 and agrees almost everywhere with its pointwise recursion. This constructs moment control without Gaussianity or equilibrium assumptions.

The square-integrable update first applies the Euler drift/noise map and then the genuine pairwise proximal map. Both are Lipschitz, so the result is again an L2L^2L2 random variable. The update representative theorem intersects the full-measure sets for these two operations and obtains the pointwise PIPGLA formula. An induction repeats this at each fixed finite time, replacing the previous representative by the pointwise iterate on their common full-measure event. The chain's pushforward law is therefore exactly pipglaLaws, rather than merely a convenient auxiliary recursion.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • pipglaLaws: The law of the actual PIPGLA state after a supplied innovation sequence.
  • lpPipglaUpdate: The actual noisy forward step followed by the genuine pairwise proximal map, represented in L².
  • lpPipglaChain: Every iterate is an actual L² random variable.

03Pushforward equality proves mass one §

Mathematical statement and proof

Almost everywhere equal representatives have equal pushforward measures, so ρn=Q∘Xn−1\rho_n=Q\circ X_n^{-1}ρn​=Q∘Xn−1​ for the constructed L2L^2L2 iterate. Its almost everywhere measurability makes this a genuine probability law: the preimage of the whole state space has QQQ-mass one.

04Transfer any iid realization to the canonical product law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume the initial variable is independent of the whole innovation path and the innovations form an independent family with common law η\etaη. The joint input law is L(X0)⊗η⊗N\mathcal L(X_0)\otimes\eta^{\otimes\mathbb N}L(X0​)⊗η⊗N. Applying the measurable noise-driven recursion gives exactly the canonical chain law. The proof uses the actual independence and common-law hypotheses, without assuming a stochastic error estimate.

For a measurable update, the time-nnn state is a measurable function of initialization and the innovation path. Independence of initialization from that path makes their joint law a product; iid innovations make the path law η⊗N\eta^{\otimes\mathbb N}η⊗N. Pushing this product through the finite recursion proves the canonical-law equality. The assumptions hinit, hnoise, hind, hiid and hlaw respectively supply these measurability, independence and distribution facts; none is a convergence-rate premise.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • pipglaLaws_eq_canonical: Any independent iid realization has the canonical splitting law. This proves law transfer across coupling spaces without a convergence assumption.
