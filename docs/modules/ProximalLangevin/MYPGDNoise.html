Orthogonal masks and the exact removed-noise cost

Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

Let PθP_\thetaPθ​ be the orthogonal projection onto the common parameter block and Px=I−PθP_x=I-P_\thetaPx​=I−Pθ​ the complementary latent projection. For standard Gaussian ξ\xiξ, the parameter projection has mean zero and squared moment dθd_\thetadθ​. Coupling the latent-only and full-noise updates with the same Gaussian vector leaves exactly this omitted parameter block. Independence makes its mixed term with the preceding discrepancy vanish.

01Complementary parameter and latent projections §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define Pθ(θ,x)=(θ,0)P_\theta(\theta,x)=(\theta,0)Pθ​(θ,x)=(θ,0) and Px(θ,x)=(0,x)P_x(\theta,x)=(0,x)Px​(θ,x)=(0,x). These bounded linear maps preserve square integrability, so an L2L^2L2 innovation remains an L2L^2L2 random vector after either projection.

Coordinate evaluation gives Pθ+Px=IP_\theta+P_x=IPθ​+Px​=I, PθPx=0P_\theta P_x=0Pθ​Px​=0, Pθ2=PθP_\theta^2=P_\thetaPθ2​=Pθ​, and Px2=PxP_x^2=P_xPx2​=Px​. Their ranges are therefore complementary orthogonal parameter and latent subspaces. Applying PxP_xPx​ to the noise removes its parameter component and leaves the deterministic drift unchanged, which is the update difference in the MYPGD comparison.

Denote the two maps by PθP_\thetaPθ​ and Px=I−PθP_x=I-P_\thetaPx​=I−Pθ​. On a scaled particle state, Pθ(θ,z1,…,zN)=(θ,0,…,0)P_\theta(\theta,z_1,\ldots,z_N)=(\theta,0,\ldots,0)Pθ​(θ,z1​,…,zN​)=(θ,0,…,0), while Px(θ,z1,…,zN)=(0,z1,…,zN)P_x(\theta,z_1,\ldots,z_N)=(0,z_1,\ldots,z_N)Px​(θ,z1​,…,zN​)=(0,z1​,…,zN​). The four coordinate lemmas establish these formulas directly; the subtraction defining latentNoiseCLM is an equality of continuous linear maps, so all later compositions are measurable.

Formal correspondence: parameterNoiseCLM : Retain the parameter coordinates and erase the latent coordinates. latentNoiseCLM : Retain precisely the latent noise of the original particle scheme.

02Projection norms and Gaussian moments §

Mathematical statement and proof

The parameter projection satisfies ∥Pθz∥2=∑j=1dθzj2\|P_\theta z\|^2=\sum_{j=1}^{d_\theta}z_j^2∥Pθ​z∥2=∑j=1dθ​​zj2​ and hence ∥Pθz∥≤∥z∥\|P_\theta z\|\le\|z\|∥Pθ​z∥≤∥z∥. Linearity of expectation gives EPθξ=0\mathbb E P_\theta\xi=0EPθ​ξ=0; the unit second moment of each standard Gaussian coordinate gives E∥Pθξ∥2=dθ\mathbb E\|P_\theta\xi\|^2=d_\thetaE∥Pθ​ξ∥2=dθ​.

In the coordinate expansion ∑j=1dθzj2\sum_{j=1}^{d_\theta}z_j^2∑j=1dθ​​zj2​, each summand is integrable because the Gaussian vector belongs to L2L^2L2. The identities Ezj2=1\mathbb E z_j^2=1Ezj2​=1 and EPθZ=PθEZ=0\mathbb E P_\theta Z=P_\theta\mathbb EZ=0EPθ​Z=Pθ​EZ=0 therefore give E∥PθZ∥2=dθ\mathbb E\|P_\theta Z\|^2=d_\thetaE∥Pθ​Z∥2=dθ​ by a finite sum. No factor NNN occurs: the projection retains exactly dθd_\thetadθ​ scalar coordinates and their variances sum to dθd_\thetadθ​. The particle count enters only when this noise block is multiplied by amplitude 2γ/N\sqrt{2\gamma/N}2γ/N​.

03The zero mixed expectation and omitted-noise variance §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let ggg be square-integrable and independent of standard Gaussian ξ\xiξ. Expansion of ∥g+bPθξ∥2\|g+bP_\theta\xi\|^2∥g+bPθ​ξ∥2 is integrable term by term by Cauchy–Schwarz. Fubini and bilinearity identify the mixed expectation as ⟨Eg,EPθξ⟩=0\langle\mathbb E g,\mathbb E P_\theta\xi\rangle=0⟨Eg,EPθ​ξ⟩=0, so

E∥g+bPθξ∥2=E∥g∥2+b2dθ.\mathbb E\|g+bP_\theta\xi\|^2=\mathbb E\|g\|^2+b^2d_\theta.E∥g+bPθ​ξ∥2=E∥g∥2+b2dθ​.

The independence is between the fresh Gaussian and ggg; the discrepancy ggg need not be centered. In particular E⟨g,Pθξ⟩\mathbb E\langle g,P_\theta\xi\rangleE⟨g,Pθ​ξ⟩ factors into the inner product of the two means, and the Gaussian mean is zero. Either sign of the removed parameter block therefore adds exactly b2dθb^2d_\thetab2dθ​ to the squared error.

For a general g∈L2(μ)g\in L^2(\mu)g∈L2(μ), use the actual product law μ⊗N(0,I)\mu\otimes\mathcal N(0,I)μ⊗N(0,I), and expand ∥g(x)+bPθz∥2=∥g(x)∥2+2b⟨g(x),Pθz⟩+b2∥Pθz∥2.\|g(x)+bP_\theta z\|^2=\|g(x)\|^2+2b\langle g(x),P_\theta z\rangle+b^2\|P_\theta z\|^2.∥g(x)+bPθ​z∥2=∥g(x)∥2+2b⟨g(x),Pθ​z⟩+b2∥Pθ​z∥2. The facts hgsq and hzsq prove integrability of the two squares on the product; hcross proves integrability of the bilinear term, so splitting the integral is justified. Independence here is the product-measure construction itself: integral_prod_bilin factors the mixed integral as ⟨Eg,EPθZ⟩=0\langle\mathbb Eg,\mathbb E P_\theta Z\rangle=0⟨Eg,EPθ​Z⟩=0. Hence E∥g+bPθZ∥2=E∥g∥2+b2dθ\mathbb E\|g+bP_\theta Z\|^2=\mathbb E\|g\|^2+b^2d_\thetaE∥g+bPθ​Z∥2=E∥g∥2+b2dθ​, with no assumption that ggg is centered.

Formal correspondence: integral_add_parameterNoise_sq : Exact independent-noise identity, for an arbitrary square-integrable source. The mixed term vanishes by the actual product Gaussian law.
