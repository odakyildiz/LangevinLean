Orthogonal masks and the exact removed-noise cost

Let PθP_\thetaPθ​ retain the common parameter block of the scaled particle state, and Px=I−PθP_x=I-P_\thetaPx​=I−Pθ​ retain the latent blocks. For a standard Gaussian vector ξ\xiξ, the removed parameter noise has mean zero and second moment dθd_\thetadθ​.

01Define the actual complementary coordinate projections §

Mathematical statement and proof

Set Pθ(θ,x)=(θ,0)P_\theta(\theta,x)=(\theta,0)Pθ​(θ,x)=(θ,0) and Px(θ,x)=(0,x)P_x(\theta,x)=(0,x)Px​(θ,x)=(0,x). Coordinate evaluation proves the four block identities by unfolding the definitions. These are bounded linear maps, so masking an L2L^2L2 innovation remains an actual L2L^2L2 random variable.

Denote the two maps by PθP_\thetaPθ​ and Px=I−PθP_x=I-P_\thetaPx​=I−Pθ​. On a scaled particle state, Pθ(θ,z1,…,zN)=(θ,0,…,0)P_\theta(\theta,z_1,\ldots,z_N)=(\theta,0,\ldots,0)Pθ​(θ,z1​,…,zN​)=(θ,0,…,0), while Px(θ,z1,…,zN)=(0,z1,…,zN)P_x(\theta,z_1,\ldots,z_N)=(0,z_1,\ldots,z_N)Px​(θ,z1​,…,zN​)=(0,z1​,…,zN​). The four coordinate lemmas establish these formulas directly; the subtraction defining latentNoiseCLM is an equality of continuous linear maps, so all later compositions are measurable.

02Compute the projection geometry and Gaussian moments §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Euclidean square is ∥Pθz∥2=∑j=1dθzj2\|P_\theta z\|^2=\sum_{j=1}^{d_\theta}z_j^2∥Pθ​z∥2=∑j=1dθ​​zj2​, hence ∥Pθz∥≤∥z∥\|P_\theta z\|\le\|z\|∥Pθ​z∥≤∥z∥. A bounded linear map commutes with the Gaussian expectation, giving EPθξ=0\mathbb E P_\theta\xi=0EPθ​ξ=0. Each retained standard coordinate has second moment one; finite summation gives E∥Pθξ∥2=dθ\mathbb E\|P_\theta\xi\|^2=d_\thetaE∥Pθ​ξ∥2=dθ​.

The squared norm expands to ∑j=1dθzj2\sum_{j=1}^{d_\theta}z_j^2∑j=1dθ​​zj2​, with no latent terms. Every coordinate square is integrable because the standard Gaussian identity map belongs to L2L^2L2. The coordinate variance identity gives Ezj2=1\mathbb E z_j^2=1Ezj2​=1 and the continuous-linear-map integration identity gives EPθZ=PθEZ=0\mathbb E P_\theta Z=P_\theta\mathbb EZ=0EPθ​Z=Pθ​EZ=0. Interchanging only a finite sum and the integral yields E∥PθZ∥2=dθ\mathbb E\|P_\theta Z\|^2=d_\thetaE∥Pθ​Z∥2=dθ​. No factor NNN belongs in this dimension count.

03The product law cancels the mixed term exactly §

Mathematical statement and proof

For any square-integrable ggg on a probability space independent of ξ\xiξ, expand ∥g+bPθξ∥2\|g+bP_\theta\xi\|^2∥g+bPθ​ξ∥2. Fubini and bilinearity factor its mixed expectation as ⟨Eg,EPθξ⟩=0\langle\mathbb E g,\mathbb E P_\theta\xi\rangle=0⟨Eg,EPθ​ξ⟩=0. Thus

E∥g+bPθξ∥2=E∥g∥2+b2dθ.\mathbb E\|g+bP_\theta\xi\|^2=\mathbb E\|g\|^2+b^2d_\theta.E∥g+bPθ​ξ∥2=E∥g∥2+b2dθ​.

The proof establishes integrability of every term before distributing the integral.

For a general g∈L2(μ)g\in L^2(\mu)g∈L2(μ), use the actual product law μ⊗N(0,I)\mu\otimes\mathcal N(0,I)μ⊗N(0,I), and expand ∥g(x)+bPθz∥2=∥g(x)∥2+2b⟨g(x),Pθz⟩+b2∥Pθz∥2.\|g(x)+bP_\theta z\|^2=\|g(x)\|^2+2b\langle g(x),P_\theta z\rangle+b^2\|P_\theta z\|^2.∥g(x)+bPθ​z∥2=∥g(x)∥2+2b⟨g(x),Pθ​z⟩+b2∥Pθ​z∥2. The facts hgsq and hzsq prove integrability of the two squares on the product; hcross proves integrability of the bilinear term, so splitting the integral is justified. Independence here is the product-measure construction itself: integral_prod_bilin factors the mixed integral as ⟨Eg,EPθZ⟩=0\langle\mathbb Eg,\mathbb E P_\theta Z\rangle=0⟨Eg,EPθ​Z⟩=0. Hence E∥g+bPθZ∥2=E∥g∥2+b2dθ\mathbb E\|g+bP_\theta Z\|^2=\mathbb E\|g\|^2+b^2d_\thetaE∥g+bPθ​Z∥2=E∥g∥2+b2dθ​, with no assumption that ggg is centered.
