Bounded fields and positive global strong monotonicity

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

A globally bounded vector field on a nontrivial real Hilbert space cannot have a positive global strong-monotonicity constant. The Moreau gradient of a globally Lipschitz convex function is bounded. Hence the conjunction of full-potential value Lipschitzness and a positive global proximal-residual deficit is inconsistent. The conclusion concerns that literal conjunction of assumptions; it does not apply to a decomposition in which only the nonsmooth summand is Lipschitz.

01Incompatibility of boundedness and positive global monotonicity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume ∥R(x)∥≤K\|R(x)\|\le K∥R(x)∥≤K and c∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,R(x)−R(y)⟩c\|x-y\|^2\le\langle x-y,R(x)-R(y)\ranglec∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,R(x)−R(y)⟩ with c>0c>0c>0. Cauchy–Schwarz and the triangle inequality give c∥x−y∥2≤2K∥x−y∥c\|x-y\|^2\le2K\|x-y\|c∥x−y∥2≤2K∥x−y∥. A nonzero direction can be scaled to have length greater than 2K/c2K/c2K/c, contradicting this bound.

In the alternative notation ∥b(x)∥≤K\|b(x)\|\le K∥b(x)∥≤K and ⟨b(x)−b(y),x−y⟩≥q∥x−y∥2\langle b(x)-b(y),x-y\rangle\ge q\|x-y\|^2⟨b(x)−b(y),x−y⟩≥q∥x−y∥2 with q>0q>0q>0 imply q∥x−y∥≤2Kq\|x-y\|\le2Kq∥x−y∥≤2K for every distinct pair. Explicitly choose separation r>2K/cr>2K/cr>2K/c. Strong monotonicity bounds the inner product below by cr2cr^2cr2, while boundedness bounds it above by 2Kr2Kr2Kr. Division by rrr gives cr≤2Kcr\le2Kcr≤2K. A nontrivial space has arbitrarily large such separations rrr, so the assumptions cannot both hold. The nontrivial-space qualification is essential.

02The obstruction for a globally bounded proximal residual §

Mathematical statement and proof

Consider the reading of the source reduction in which g1=0g_1=0g1​=0 also applies the value-Lipschitz assumption to U=g2U=g_2U=g2​. Then UUU is globally KKK-Lipschitz, so its Moreau gradient Rγ=γ−1(I−Pγ)R_\gamma=\gamma^{-1}(I-P_\gamma)Rγ​=γ−1(I−Pγ​) satisfies ∥Rγ(x)∥≤K\|R_\gamma(x)\|\le K∥Rγ​(x)∥≤K. A positive proximal deficit gives

μγ∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,Rγ(x)−Rγ(y)⟩≤2K∥x−y∥. \frac{\mu}{\gamma}\|x-y\|^2 \le\langle x-y,R_\gamma(x)-R_\gamma(y)\rangle \le2K\|x-y\|. γμ​∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,Rγ​(x)−Rγ​(y)⟩≤2K∥x−y∥.

The preceding obstruction contradicts these simultaneous global conditions on a nontrivial space. If the Lipschitz assumption instead concerns the nonsmooth term in an independent decomposition U=g1+g2U=g_1+g_2U=g1​+g2​, it does not make the full UUU Lipschitz and the contradiction does not follow.

The residual Rγ=(I−Pγ)/γR_\gamma=(I-P_\gamma)/\gammaRγ​=(I−Pγ​)/γ is bounded in the first reading, while the deficit makes it strongly monotone with coefficient μ/γ\mu/\gammaμ/γ. Under μ>0\mu>0μ>0 these are incompatible. This is a conditional consistency result about the specified assumptions, not a general refutation of the source corollary. The separate full-potential PIPULA theorem assumes strong convexity and a lower bound without global value Lipschitzness.
