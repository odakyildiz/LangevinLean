A conditional consistency test for global assumptions

On a nontrivial real Hilbert space, a globally bounded field cannot be strongly monotone with a positive constant. This is used only for the literal specialization that also makes the full potential globally Lipschitz.

01Boundedness contradicts positive global monotonicity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume ∥R(x)∥≤K\|R(x)\|\le K∥R(x)∥≤K and c∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,R(x)−R(y)⟩c\|x-y\|^2\le\langle x-y,R(x)-R(y)\ranglec∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,R(x)−R(y)⟩ with c>0c>0c>0. Cauchy–Schwarz and the triangle inequality give c∥x−y∥2≤2K∥x−y∥c\|x-y\|^2\le2K\|x-y\|c∥x−y∥2≤2K∥x−y∥. Choose a nonzero vector and scale its norm beyond 2K/c2K/c2K/c. The two inequalities are incompatible.

02Apply the obstruction to the true proximal residual §

Mathematical statement and proof

This check is conditional on how the appendix’s reduction is read. If setting g1=0g_1=0g1​=0 also carries A3 onto U=g2U=g_2U=g2​, then UUU is globally KKK-Lipschitz. Its actual Moreau gradient Rγ=γ−1(I−Pγ)R_\gamma=\gamma^{-1}(I-P_\gamma)Rγ​=γ−1(I−Pγ​) satisfies ∥Rγ(x)∥≤K\|R_\gamma(x)\|\le K∥Rγ​(x)∥≤K. But B1 with positive deficit gives

μγ∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,Rγ(x)−Rγ(y)⟩≤2K∥x−y∥. \frac{\mu}{\gamma}\|x-y\|^2 \le\langle x-y,R_\gamma(x)-R_\gamma(y)\rangle \le2K\|x-y\|. γμ​∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,Rγ​(x)−Rγ​(y)⟩≤2K∥x−y∥.

A nontrivial real Hilbert space contains points at arbitrarily large distance, so these global premises are incompatible. This is kernel-checked for the actual proximal minimizer. If A3 is instead retained for an independent original decomposition U=g1+g2U=g_1+g_2U=g1​+g2​, it does not make the full UUU Lipschitz; that reading requires its own full-envelope analysis. The result here is therefore a conditional consistency check, not a blanket assertion that Corollary A.6 is false. The separate PIPULA endpoint assumes strong convexity of the full potential and does not impose global Lipschitzness on it.
