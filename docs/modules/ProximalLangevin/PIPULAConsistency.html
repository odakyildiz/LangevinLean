Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

Positive strong monotonicity forces an unbounded field on a nontrivial Euclidean space. A globally Lipschitz potential has a bounded Moreau gradient, so combining that property with a positive global residual deficit can be inconsistent. The conclusion applies to the literal conjunction of those global assumptions and does not invalidate different full-potential hypotheses.

On a nontrivial real Hilbert space, a globally bounded field cannot be strongly monotone with a positive constant. This is used only for the literal specialization that also makes the full potential globally Lipschitz.

01Boundedness contradicts positive global monotonicity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume ∥R(x)∥≤K\|R(x)\|\le K∥R(x)∥≤K and c∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,R(x)−R(y)⟩c\|x-y\|^2\le\langle x-y,R(x)-R(y)\ranglec∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,R(x)−R(y)⟩ with c>0c>0c>0. If ∥b(x)∥≤K\|b(x)\|\le K∥b(x)∥≤K globally but ⟨b(x)−b(y),x−y⟩≥q∥x−y∥2\langle b(x)-b(y),x-y\rangle\ge q\|x-y\|^2⟨b(x)−b(y),x−y⟩≥q∥x−y∥2 with q>0q>0q>0, Cauchy–Schwarz implies q∥x−y∥≤2Kq\|x-y\|\le2Kq∥x−y∥≤2K for every distinct pair. Along a nonzero direction one can choose arbitrarily distant points, contradicting this uniform upper bound. The second theorem applies the obstruction to a true proximal residual: a globally Lipschitz potential gives a bounded residual. Thus the stated positive global deficit condition cannot be combined with those hypotheses in positive dimension.

Formal correspondence: bounded_field_not_stronglyMonotone : A globally bounded field cannot have a positive strong-monotonicity constant on a nontrivial real Hilbert space.

Choose points separated by a distance r>2K/cr>2K/cr>2K/c. Strong monotonicity gives a lower bound cr2cr^2cr2 for the pairing, whereas boundedness gives an upper bound 2Kr2Kr2Kr. Division by positive rrr yields cr≤2Kcr\le2Kcr≤2K, contradicting the choice. A nonzero direction supplies arbitrarily large rrr, so positive dimension is essential to the obstruction.

02Apply the obstruction to the true proximal residual §

Mathematical statement and proof

This check is conditional on how the appendix’s reduction is read. If setting g1=0g_1=0g1​=0 also carries A3 onto U=g2U=g_2U=g2​, then UUU is globally KKK-Lipschitz. Its actual Moreau gradient Rγ=γ−1(I−Pγ)R_\gamma=\gamma^{-1}(I-P_\gamma)Rγ​=γ−1(I−Pγ​) satisfies ∥Rγ(x)∥≤K\|R_\gamma(x)\|\le K∥Rγ​(x)∥≤K. But B1 with positive deficit gives

μγ∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,Rγ(x)−Rγ(y)⟩≤2K∥x−y∥. \frac{\mu}{\gamma}\|x-y\|^2 \le\langle x-y,R_\gamma(x)-R_\gamma(y)\rangle \le2K\|x-y\|. γμ​∥x−y∥2≤⟨x−y,Rγ​(x)−Rγ​(y)⟩≤2K∥x−y∥.

A nontrivial real Hilbert space contains points at arbitrarily large distance, so these global premises are incompatible. This is kernel-checked for the actual proximal minimizer. If A3 is instead retained for an independent original decomposition U=g1+g2U=g_1+g_2U=g1​+g2​, it does not make the full UUU Lipschitz; that reading requires its own full-envelope analysis. The result here is therefore a conditional consistency check, not a blanket assertion that Corollary A.6 is false. The separate PIPULA endpoint assumes strong convexity of the full potential and does not impose global Lipschitzness on it.

Formal correspondence: lipschitz_full_potential_not_proximal_deficit : Under a literal full-potential globally Lipschitz reading, B1 cannot hold with positive deficit in positive dimension. The separate PIPULA theorem therefore does not assume global Lipschitzness of the full strongly convex U.

The actual residual Rγ=(I−Pγ)/γR_\gamma=(I-P_\gamma)/\gammaRγ​=(I−Pγ​)/γ is bounded by the potential's global value Lipschitz constant. The proximal deficit gives strong monotonicity with coefficient μ/γ\mu/\gammaμ/γ, including its inverse-step factor. Substitution in the general obstruction proves incompatibility when μ>0\mu>0μ>0 and the space is nontrivial. A decomposition whose nonsmooth component is Lipschitz need not make the entire potential Lipschitz, so its analysis is a different case.
