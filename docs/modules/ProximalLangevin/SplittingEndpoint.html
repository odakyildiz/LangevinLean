Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

The stationary one-step coupling gives an estimate for each input coupling. Taking the coupling infimum makes it a recurrence for the Gaussian transition laws. At equal forward and proximal scales, the potential gap meets the required linear-step bound, and the forcing is quadratic in the step with a finite step-independent coefficient. Geometric summation and the particle scaling then give the stated squared transport estimate.

For f1f_1f1​ strongly convex and smooth, a bounded convex envelope term eee, and a genuine energy map TTT, this module first proves convergence of actual Gaussian Markov laws. It then instantiates the result with the true averaged particle proximity and equal proximal/forward steps.

01The one-step transport recurrence §

Mathematical statement and proof

The admissible step and m≤Lm\le Lm≤L give mh≤1mh\le1mh≤1. For each finite-cost input coupling the stationary construction gives a finite output coupling with the same forcing. Taking the cost infimum yields

W2(ρk+1,π)2≤(1−mh)W2(ρk,π)2+F.W_2(\rho_{k+1},\pi)^2\le(1-mh)W_2(\rho_k,\pi)^2+\mathcal F.W2​(ρk+1​,π)2≤(1−mh)W2​(ρk​,π)2+F.

The forcing is nonnegative because e≤f≤e+δe\le f\le e+\deltae≤f≤e+δ implies δ≥0\delta\ge0δ≥0. In the smooth-part notation, m≤L1m\le L_1m≤L1​ and step admissibility give 0<mh≤10<mh\le10<mh≤1, hence q=1−mh≥0q=1-mh\ge0q=1−mh≥0. The transition estimate is

W2(μP,π)2≤qW2(μ,π)2+F.W_2(\mu P,\pi)^2\le qW_2(\mu,\pi)^2+\mathcal F.W2​(μP,π)2≤qW2​(μ,π)2+F.

The reference target is fixed during iteration at the chosen scale. One finite input coupling proves finite output transport, while the estimate for every coupling gives the infimum bound. The condition mh≤1mh\le1mh≤1 ensures its preceding-error coefficient is nonnegative, permitting iteration.
