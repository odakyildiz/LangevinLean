Independent Brownian coordinates for the particle input

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

On Q⊗NQ^{\otimes N}Q⊗N, select the common parameter Brownian coordinates from copy zero and latent coordinate (i,j)(i,j)(i,j) from copy iii. Independence of source rows and scalar paths gives independence of all selected full paths. Parameter and latent coordinate tags are disjoint, so the first source row may provide both parameter noise and the first particle’s latent noise without repeating any scalar coordinate.

Statement and proof

01An injective selection of source coordinates §

Mathematical statement and proof

Send parameter index jjj to (0,parameter j)(0,\mathrm{parameter}\ j)(0,parameter j) and latent index (i,j)(i,j)(i,j) to (i,latent j)(i,\mathrm{latent}\ j)(i,latent j). Different coordinate tags separate the two kinds. Within either kind equality of selected indices implies equality of original indices, so the selection is injective.

In coordinate notation parameter index jjj selects (0,θj)(0,\theta_j)(0,θj​), while (i,j)(i,j)(i,j) selects (i,xj)(i,x_j)(i,xj​). Evaluate the corresponding source Brownian path in the selected copy. A latent coordinate for particle iii comes from copy iii. Even when that copy is zero, its latent tag differs from every parameter tag. Thus no scalar Brownian path is reused.

Formal correspondence: particleBrownianIndex : Select a distinct base-copy/coordinate pair for every particle-noise coordinate. particleBrownianIndex_injective : The common parameter uses copy zero; latent blocks use their respective copies. particleBrownianFamily : The actual joint Brownian source for the interacting particle coordinates.

02The Brownian law of each selected coordinate §

Mathematical statement and proof

For each selected coordinate, product evaluation preserves the source measure. Composition with its supplied scalar path therefore preserves the standard pre-Brownian law. Every time evaluation remains measurable by composition.

These facts establish the individual process distributions and measurability. Independence of their full paths follows separately from the joint source-family argument.

Formal correspondence: particleBrownianFamily_preBrownian : Every selected coordinate is an actual standard pre-Brownian motion. particleBrownianFamily_measurable : Measurable source evaluations remain measurable after selecting a copy.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.32.1.

03Independence of the selected full paths §

Mathematical statement and proof

The product measure makes the full source rows independent, and within each row the scalar coordinate paths are jointly independent. Flattening the two indices gives a jointly independent family indexed by (i,j)(i,j)(i,j). The injective selection therefore retains independence.

This is independence of complete selected paths, including the common parameter block and the first latent block from row zero. It is stronger than independence at a single time. The constructed Brownian source supplies the row laws and the coordinate-path independence required for this construction.

First pull each whole Brownian row from the source to one coordinate of the finite product; distinct rows are independent by the product measure. Within a row the supplied coordinate paths are independent, and measure-preserving evaluation retains their joint law. The helper iIndepFun_uncurry' flattens these two levels into a jointly independent family indexed by all copy/coordinate pairs. Finally, precomposition with the injective index map selects the intended subfamily and preserves independence. This proves independence of full coordinate paths, including the parameter path and the first particle's latent path; it does not assert independence of interacting particle states.

Formal correspondence: particleBrownianFamily_paths_independent : Full coordinate-path independence is derived on the actual finite product. There is no assumption of independent particles after interaction. canonical_particleBrownianFamily : The concrete canonical Gaussian time-indicator construction supplies all selected scalar Brownian laws and their full-path independence.
