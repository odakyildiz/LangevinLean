01The envelope preserves a global value Lipschitz constant §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let p=Pλ(y)p=P_\lambda(y)p=Pλ​(y). As a competitor at xxx, use p+x−yp+x-yp+x−y, so its quadratic penalty equals that of ppp at yyy. The KKK-Lipschitz inequality for fff changes the function value by at most K∥x−y∥K\|x-y\|K∥x−y∥. Thus fλ(x)≤fλ(y)+K∥x−y∥f^\lambda(x)\le f^\lambda(y)+K\|x-y\|fλ(x)≤fλ(y)+K∥x−y∥, and reversing x,yx,yx,y proves the same global Lipschitz constant.

Choose the actual minimizer p=Pλ(y)p=P_\lambda(y)p=Pλ​(y), and test the objective at xxx with p+(x−y)p+(x-y)p+(x−y). The two quadratic penalties agree because p+(x−y)−x=p−yp+(x-y)-x=p-yp+(x−y)−x=p−y; only the potential value changes, by at most K∥x−y∥K\|x-y\|K∥x−y∥. Therefore fλ(x)≤fλ(y)+K∥x−y∥f^\lambda(x)\le f^\lambda(y)+K\|x-y\|fλ(x)≤fλ(y)+K∥x−y∥. Reversing the inputs gives the absolute-value bound. This competitor argument proves a value Lipschitz bound uniform in λ\lambdaλ, rather than using the score's larger constant 1/λ1/\lambda1/λ.

02Find a true minimum and bound the smooth force there §

Mathematical statement and proof

Strong convexity gives quadratic coercivity. Minimize FFF on a sufficiently large closed ball, with radius 2∥∇F(0)∥/m+12\|\nabla F(0)\|/m+12∥∇F(0)∥/m+1; the quadratic lower bound proves the resulting point minimizes globally. At a global minimum zzz, the local-extremum derivative vanishes. Hence ∇f(z)=−∇e(z)\nabla f(z)=-\nabla e(z)∇f(z)=−∇e(z) and ∥∇f(z)∥≤K\|\nabla f(z)\|\le K∥∇f(z)∥≤K.

The strong-convexity lower bound forces the total potential above its value at zero outside a finite ball. A minimizer on the compact ball is therefore global; this is the radius construction in exists_strongConvex_minimum. At a global minimizer of F=f+eF=f+eF=f+e, the derivative vanishes, giving ∇f(z)=−∇e(z)\nabla f(z)=-\nabla e(z)∇f(z)=−∇e(z). Thus a pointwise bound ∥∇e∥≤K\|\nabla e\|\le K∥∇e∥≤K yields ∥∇f(z)∥≤K\|\nabla f(z)\|\le K∥∇f(z)∥≤K. This is the only value of the smooth score at a specific point needed in the moment estimate.

03A bounded gradient remains bounded in L² §

Mathematical statement and proof

The norm of the derivative of a KKK-Lipschitz scalar function is at most KKK; the gradient is its Riesz representative and has the same norm. For a pointwise bounded drift ∥b(x)∥≤K\|b(x)\|\le K∥b(x)∥≤K, almost everywhere representative equality gives ∥b(Y)∥L2≤K\|b(Y)\|_{L^2}\le K∥b(Y)∥L2​≤K on a probability space.

The derivative of a globally KKK-Lipschitz scalar function has operator norm at most KKK, and the Riesz identification preserves this norm, giving ∥∇f(x)∥≤K\|\nabla f(x)\|\le K∥∇f(x)∥≤K. Separately, if ∥b(x)∥≤K\|b(x)\|\le K∥b(x)∥≤K for every input, then the representative equality for lpDrift gives ∥b(Y(ω))∥≤K\|b(Y(\omega))\|\le K∥b(Y(ω))∥≤K almost everywhere. Since PPP has mass one, its L2L^2L2 norm is at most KKK. This explains why a state-space uniform bound transfers without a dimension or measure-mass factor.

04Integrate a sharp quadratic bound about the mode §

Mathematical statement and proof

Lipschitz continuity and the mode bound give ∥∇f(x)∥≤L∥x−z∥+K\|\nabla f(x)\|\le L\|x-z\|+K∥∇f(x)∥≤L∥x−z∥+K, so ∥∇f(x)∥2≤2L2∥x−z∥2+2K2\|\nabla f(x)\|^2\le2L^2\|x-z\|^2+2K^2∥∇f(x)∥2≤2L2∥x−z∥2+2K2. Sharp Gibbs concentration supplies Eπ∥x−z∥2≤d/m\mathbb E_\pi\|x-z\|^2\le d/mEπ​∥x−z∥2≤d/m, yielding

Eπ∥∇f∥2≤2L2d/m+2K2.\mathbb E_\pi\|\nabla f\|^2\le2L^2d/m+2K^2.Eπ​∥∇f∥2≤2L2d/m+2K2.

For any YYY with actual Gibbs law π\piπ, the pushforward integral formula transfers this estimate and taking square roots gives ∥∇f(Y)∥2≤2L2d/m+2K2\|\nabla f(Y)\|_2\le\sqrt{2L^2d/m+2K^2}∥∇f(Y)∥2​≤2L2d/m+2K2​.

Let zzz minimize F=f+eF=f+eF=f+e, with FFF mmm-strongly convex. From the previous stage, ∥∇f(z)∥≤K\|\nabla f(z)\|\le K∥∇f(z)∥≤K, and score Lipschitzness gives ∥∇f(x)∥≤L∥x−z∥+K\|\nabla f(x)\|\le L\|x-z\|+K∥∇f(x)∥≤L∥x−z∥+K. Consequently ∥∇f(x)∥2≤2L2∥x−z∥2+2K2.\|\nabla f(x)\|^2\le2L^2\|x-z\|^2+2K^2.∥∇f(x)∥2≤2L2∥x−z∥2+2K2. The Gibbs second moment is integrable by strong convexity; the right side therefore supplies an integrable majorant for the score square. The facts hbound and hgrad discharge that prerequisite before integral_mono is applied.

Sharp concentration yields Eπ∥X−z∥2≤d/m\mathbb E_\pi\|X-z\|^2\le d/mEπ​∥X−z∥2≤d/m, so Eπ∥∇f(X)∥2≤2L2d/m+2K2\mathbb E_\pi\|\nabla f(X)\|^2\le2L^2d/m+2K^2Eπ​∥∇f(X)∥2≤2L2d/m+2K2. The next declaration transfers this actual Gibbs-law integral to any random variable with that law and takes its square root, obtaining the L2L^2L2 drift bound used in the splitting reference.
