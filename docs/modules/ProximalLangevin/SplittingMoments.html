Reference drift moments uniform over smoothing scales

For F=f+eF=f+eF=f+e, assume FFF is mmm-strongly convex, f,ef,ef,e differentiable, ∇f\nabla f∇f is LLL-Lipschitz, and ∥∇e∥≤K\|\nabla e\|\le K∥∇e∥≤K. The goal is a Gibbs drift bound using L,m,KL,m,KL,m,K, without an inverse smoothing scale.

01The envelope preserves a global value Lipschitz constant §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let p=Pλ(y)p=P_\lambda(y)p=Pλ​(y). As a competitor at xxx, use p+x−yp+x-yp+x−y, so its quadratic penalty equals that of ppp at yyy. The KKK-Lipschitz inequality for fff changes the function value by at most K∥x−y∥K\|x-y\|K∥x−y∥. 02Find a true minimum and bound the smooth force there §

Mathematical statement and proof

Strong convexity gives quadratic coercivity. Minimize FFF on a sufficiently large closed ball, with radius 2∥∇F(0)∥/m+12\|\nabla F(0)\|/m+12∥∇F(0)∥/m+1; the quadratic lower bound proves the resulting point minimizes globally. At a global minimum zzz, the local-extremum derivative vanishes. Hence ∇f(z)=−∇e(z)\nabla f(z)=-\nabla e(z)∇f(z)=−∇e(z) and ∥∇f(z)∥≤K\|\nabla f(z)\|\le K∥∇f(z)∥≤K.

03A bounded gradient remains bounded in L² §

Mathematical statement and proof

The norm of the derivative of a KKK-Lipschitz scalar function is at most KKK; the gradient is its Riesz representative and has the same norm. For a pointwise bounded drift ∥b(x)∥≤K\|b(x)\|\le K∥b(x)∥≤K, almost everywhere representative equality gives ∥b(Y)∥L2≤K\|b(Y)\|_{L^2}\le K∥b(Y)∥L2​≤K on a probability space.

04Integrate a sharp quadratic bound about the mode §

Mathematical statement and proof

Lipschitz continuity and the mode bound give ∥∇f(x)∥≤L∥x−z∥+K\|\nabla f(x)\|\le L\|x-z\|+K∥∇f(x)∥≤L∥x−z∥+K, so ∥∇f(x)∥2≤2L2∥x−z∥2+2K2\|\nabla f(x)\|^2\le2L^2\|x-z\|^2+2K^2∥∇f(x)∥2≤2L2∥x−z∥2+2K2. Sharp Gibbs concentration supplies Eπ∥x−z∥2≤d/m\mathbb E_\pi\|x-z\|^2\le d/mEπ​∥x−z∥2≤d/m, yielding

Eπ∥∇f∥2≤2L2d/m+2K2.\mathbb E_\pi\|\nabla f\|^2\le2L^2d/m+2K^2.Eπ​∥∇f∥2≤2L2d/m+2K2.

For any YYY with actual Gibbs law π\piπ, the pushforward integral formula transfers this estimate and taking square roots gives ∥∇f(Y)∥2≤2L2d/m+2K2\|\nabla f(Y)\|_2\le\sqrt{2L^2d/m+2K^2}∥∇f(Y)∥2​≤2L2d/m+2K2​.
