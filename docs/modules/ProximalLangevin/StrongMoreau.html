Strong convexity of the Moreau envelope of a strongly convex function

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

Let EEE be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, m≥0m\ge0m≥0, λ>0\lambda>0λ>0, and let f:E→Rf:E\to\mathbb Rf:E→R be mmm-strongly convex and bounded below. This section proves that the Moreau envelope fλ(x)=min⁡p(f(p)+∥p−x∥2/(2λ))f^\lambda(x)=\min_p\bigl(f(p)+\|p-x\|^2/(2\lambda)\bigr)fλ(x)=minp​(f(p)+∥p−x∥2/(2λ)) is m/(1+mλ)m/(1+m\lambda)m/(1+mλ)-strongly convex. The proof tests the minimization that defines fλ(ax+by)f^\lambda(ax+by)fλ(ax+by) with the convex combination aPλ(x)+bPλ(y)aP_\lambda(x)+bP_\lambda(y)aPλ​(x)+bPλ​(y) of the two proximal points, and combines the strong-convexity deficit of fff with the deficit of the quadratic penalty by completing a square. Differentiability of fff is not used.

The result is used when the whole strongly convex potential is smoothed, as in PIPULA. When only a convex summand g2g_2g2​ is smoothed and an mmm-strongly convex g1g_1g1​ is kept, the sum g1+g2λg_1+g_2^\lambdag1​+g2λ​ keeps the modulus mmm, because g2λg_2^\lambdag2λ​ is convex.

01Two quadratic identities §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let EEE be a real inner-product space and u,v∈Eu,v\in Eu,v∈E.

  1. If a,b≥0a,b\ge0a,b≥0 and a+b=1a+b=1a+b=1, then ∥au+bv∥2=a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2−ab∥u−v∥2.(1)\|au+bv\|^2=a\|u\|^2+b\|v\|^2-ab\|u-v\|^2.\qquad(1)∥au+bv∥2=a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2−ab∥u−v∥2.(1)
  2. If m≥0m\ge0m≥0 and λ>0\lambda>0λ>0, then m1+mλ∥v∥2≤m∥u∥2+∥u−v∥2λ.(2)\frac{m}{1+m\lambda}\|v\|^2\le m\|u\|^2+\frac{\|u-v\|^2}{\lambda}.\qquad(2)1+mλm​∥v∥2≤m∥u∥2+λ∥u−v∥2​.(2)

Proof. (a) Expanding the squared norms by bilinearity of the inner product,

∥au+bv∥2=a2∥u∥2+2ab⟨u,v⟩+b2∥v∥2,ab∥u−v∥2=ab∥u∥2−2ab⟨u,v⟩+ab∥v∥2.\|au+bv\|^2=a^2\|u\|^2+2ab\langle u,v\rangle+b^2\|v\|^2,\qquad ab\|u-v\|^2=ab\|u\|^2-2ab\langle u,v\rangle+ab\|v\|^2.∥au+bv∥2=a2∥u∥2+2ab⟨u,v⟩+b2∥v∥2,ab∥u−v∥2=ab∥u∥2−2ab⟨u,v⟩+ab∥v∥2.

Hence a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2−ab∥u−v∥2=a(1−b)∥u∥2+2ab⟨u,v⟩+b(1−a)∥v∥2a\|u\|^2+b\|v\|^2-ab\|u-v\|^2=a(1-b)\|u\|^2+2ab\langle u,v\rangle+b(1-a)\|v\|^2a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2−ab∥u−v∥2=a(1−b)∥u∥2+2ab⟨u,v⟩+b(1−a)∥v∥2, and since 1−b=a1-b=a1−b=a and 1−a=b1-a=b1−a=b, this equals ∥au+bv∥2\|au+bv\|^2∥au+bv∥2.

(b) Put c=1+mλc=1+m\lambdac=1+mλ. Since m≥0m\ge0m≥0 and λ>0\lambda>0λ>0, c≥1>0c\ge1>0c≥1>0. We show

m∥u∥2+∥u−v∥2λ−mc∥v∥2=∥cu−v∥2λc.(3)m\|u\|^2+\frac{\|u-v\|^2}{\lambda}-\frac{m}{c}\|v\|^2=\frac{\|cu-v\|^2}{\lambda c}.\qquad(3)m∥u∥2+λ∥u−v∥2​−cm​∥v∥2=λc∥cu−v∥2​.(3)

Multiply the left side by λc>0\lambda c>0λc>0 and expand ∥u−v∥2=∥u∥2−2⟨u,v⟩+∥v∥2\|u-v\|^2=\|u\|^2-2\langle u,v\rangle+\|v\|^2∥u−v∥2=∥u∥2−2⟨u,v⟩+∥v∥2:

mλc∥u∥2+c(∥u∥2−2⟨u,v⟩+∥v∥2)−mλ∥v∥2=c(1+mλ)∥u∥2−2c⟨u,v⟩+(c−mλ)∥v∥2=c2∥u∥2−2c⟨u,v⟩+∥v∥2=∥cu−v∥2.\begin{aligned} m\lambda c\|u\|^2+c\bigl(\|u\|^2-2\langle u,v\rangle+\|v\|^2\bigr)-m\lambda\|v\|^2 &=c(1+m\lambda)\|u\|^2-2c\langle u,v\rangle+(c-m\lambda)\|v\|^2\\ &=c^2\|u\|^2-2c\langle u,v\rangle+\|v\|^2=\|cu-v\|^2 . \end{aligned}mλc∥u∥2+c(∥u∥2−2⟨u,v⟩+∥v∥2)−mλ∥v∥2​=c(1+mλ)∥u∥2−2c⟨u,v⟩+(c−mλ)∥v∥2=c2∥u∥2−2c⟨u,v⟩+∥v∥2=∥cu−v∥2.​

Dividing by λc\lambda cλc gives (3). The right-hand side of (3) is nonnegative, because it is a square divided by the positive number λc\lambda cλc; this is (2). □\square□

For v=cuv=cuv=cu the right-hand side of (3) vanishes, so equality holds in (2) and the constant m/(1+mλ)m/(1+m\lambda)m/(1+mλ) cannot be replaced by a larger one.

The proof rewrites with norm_add_sq_real, norm_smul, Real.norm_eq_abs and abs_of_nonneg (here the signs of aaa and bbb are used to remove ∣a∣|a|∣a∣ and ∣b∣|b|∣b∣), mul_pow, real_inner_smul_left, real_inner_smul_right and norm_sub_sq_real; then obtain rfl := eq_sub_of_add_eq hab substitutes a=1−ba=1-ba=1−b and ring verifies the resulting polynomial identity.

weighted_quadratic_lower is (2) under hm : 0 ≤ m and hlam : 0 < lam. hden is c>0c>0c>0; heq is identity (3), proved by expanding both squared norms, extracting the factor ccc with norm_smul and abs_of_pos hden, and finishing with field_simp and ring; hpos is the nonnegativity of the right side of (3) (div_nonneg); rewriting hpos with ← heq and linarith give (2).

02The envelope of an mmm-strongly convex function is m/(1+mλ)m/(1+m\lambda)m/(1+mλ)-strongly convex §

Mathematical statement and proof

Recall that f:E→Rf:E\to\mathbb Rf:E→R is mmm-strongly convex if for all x,y∈Ex,y\in Ex,y∈E and all a,b≥0a,b\ge0a,b≥0 with a+b=1a+b=1a+b=1,

f(ax+by)≤af(x)+bf(y)−ab m2 ∥x−y∥2.f(ax+by)\le af(x)+bf(y)-ab\,\frac m2\,\|x-y\|^2.f(ax+by)≤af(x)+bf(y)−ab2m​∥x−y∥2.

For m=0m=0m=0 this is convexity; for m≥0m\ge0m≥0 an mmm-strongly convex function is convex.

Claim. Let EEE be finite-dimensional, m≥0m\ge0m≥0, λ>0\lambda>0λ>0, and let f:E→Rf:E\to\mathbb Rf:E→R be mmm-strongly convex and bounded below. Then fλf^\lambdafλ is mλm_\lambdamλ​-strongly convex, where

mλ=m1+mλ.m_\lambda=\frac{m}{1+m\lambda}.mλ​=1+mλm​.

Proof. Since m≥0m\ge0m≥0, fff is convex; together with the lower bound and finite dimension this gives the proximal map PλP_\lambdaPλ​ and the envelope fλf^\lambdafλ of the previous sections. Let x,y∈Ex,y\in Ex,y∈E, a,b≥0a,b\ge0a,b≥0 with a+b=1a+b=1a+b=1, and put z=ax+byz=ax+byz=ax+by, p=Pλ(x)p=P_\lambda(x)p=Pλ​(x), q=Pλ(y)q=P_\lambda(y)q=Pλ​(y). Since Pλ(z)P_\lambda(z)Pλ​(z) minimizes Qλ,zQ_{\lambda,z}Qλ,z​, the competitor ap+bqap+bqap+bq gives

fλ(z)≤f(ap+bq)+∥ap+bq−z∥22λ.(4)f^\lambda(z)\le f(ap+bq)+\frac{\|ap+bq-z\|^2}{2\lambda}.\qquad(4)fλ(z)≤f(ap+bq)+2λ∥ap+bq−z∥2​.(4)

Since ap+bq−z=a(p−x)+b(q−y)ap+bq-z=a(p-x)+b(q-y)ap+bq−z=a(p−x)+b(q−y), identity (1) of the previous step with u=p−xu=p-xu=p−x and v=q−yv=q-yv=q−y, for which u−v=(p−q)−(x−y)u-v=(p-q)-(x-y)u−v=(p−q)−(x−y), gives

∥ap+bq−z∥2=a∥p−x∥2+b∥q−y∥2−ab ∥(p−q)−(x−y)∥2.\|ap+bq-z\|^2=a\|p-x\|^2+b\|q-y\|^2-ab\,\|(p-q)-(x-y)\|^2.∥ap+bq−z∥2=a∥p−x∥2+b∥q−y∥2−ab∥(p−q)−(x−y)∥2.

Strong convexity of fff at p,qp,qp,q gives f(ap+bq)≤af(p)+bf(q)−abm2∥p−q∥2f(ap+bq)\le af(p)+bf(q)-ab\frac m2\|p-q\|^2f(ap+bq)≤af(p)+bf(q)−ab2m​∥p−q∥2. Inserting both into (4) and regrouping,

fλ(z)≤a(f(p)+∥p−x∥22λ)+b(f(q)+∥q−y∥22λ)−ab2(m∥p−q∥2+∥(p−q)−(x−y)∥2λ).f^\lambda(z)\le a\Bigl(f(p)+\frac{\|p-x\|^2}{2\lambda}\Bigr)+b\Bigl(f(q)+\frac{\|q-y\|^2}{2\lambda}\Bigr)-\frac{ab}{2}\Bigl(m\|p-q\|^2+\frac{\|(p-q)-(x-y)\|^2}{\lambda}\Bigr).fλ(z)≤a(f(p)+2λ∥p−x∥2​)+b(f(q)+2λ∥q−y∥2​)−2ab​(m∥p−q∥2+λ∥(p−q)−(x−y)∥2​).

The first two brackets are fλ(x)f^\lambda(x)fλ(x) and fλ(y)f^\lambda(y)fλ(y). By (2) of the previous step with u=p−qu=p-qu=p−q and v=x−yv=x-yv=x−y, the last bracket is at least mλ∥x−y∥2m_\lambda\|x-y\|^2mλ​∥x−y∥2; since ab≥0ab\ge0ab≥0, the last term is at most −ab2mλ∥x−y∥2-\frac{ab}{2}m_\lambda\|x-y\|^2−2ab​mλ​∥x−y∥2. Therefore

fλ(ax+by)≤afλ(x)+bfλ(y)−ab mλ2 ∥x−y∥2.f^\lambda(ax+by)\le af^\lambda(x)+bf^\lambda(y)-ab\,\frac{m_\lambda}{2}\,\|x-y\|^2 .fλ(ax+by)≤afλ(x)+bfλ(y)−ab2mλ​​∥x−y∥2.

This is mλm_\lambdamλ​-strong convexity of fλf^\lambdafλ. □\square□

No derivative of fff appears in the argument. For m=0m=0m=0 the claim reduces to convexity of the envelope.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

StrongConvexOn univ m f is Mathlib's strong convexity on the whole space: it unfolds to UniformConvexOn with modulus r↦m2r2r\mapsto\frac m2r^2r↦2m​r2, a structure whose first component is convexity of the domain and whose second component is the displayed inequality for weights a,b≥0a,b\ge0a,b≥0, a+b=1a+b=1a+b=1.

moreau_strongConvex assumes [FiniteDimensional ℝ E], hm : 0 ≤ m, hsc : StrongConvexOn univ m f, the lower bound hb and hlam : 0 < lam, and concludes StrongConvexOn univ (m / (1 + m * lam)) (moreau f lam).

  1. hf is convexity of fff: hsc.mono hm weakens the modulus from mmm to 000, and strongConvexOn_zero identifies 000-strong convexity with convexity.
  2. refine ⟨convex_univ, fun x _ y _ a b ha hb' hab => ?_⟩ introduces the two points and the weights. hmin is (4): prox_isProxPoint at a • x + b • y tested at a • p + b • q. hs is the strong convexity of fff at p,qp,qp,q.
  3. heq is ap+bq−z=a(p−x)+b(q−y)ap+bq-z=a(p-x)+b(q-y)ap+bq−z=a(p−x)+b(q−y); the rewrite with norm_convexCombo_sq (p - x) (q - y) ha hb' hab expands ∥ap+bq−z∥2\|ap+bq-z\|^2∥ap+bq−z∥2, and hd rewrites (p−x)−(q−y)(p-x)-(q-y)(p−x)−(q−y) as (p−q)−(x−y)(p-q)-(x-y)(p−q)−(x−y).
  4. hw is weighted_quadratic_lower with u=p−qu=p-qu=p−q, v=x−yv=x-yv=x−y, and hww multiplies it by ab≥0ab\ge0ab≥0. hscale, hmin' and hweight multiply the relevant inequalities by 2λ2\lambda2λ or λ\lambdaλ; field_simp clears the denominators, and nlinarith combines everything as in the final display.
