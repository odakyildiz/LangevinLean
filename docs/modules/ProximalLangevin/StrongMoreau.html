Strong convexity after full Moreau smoothing

Smoothing an entire mmm-strongly convex potential changes its modulus to mλ=m/(1+mλ)m_\lambda=m/(1+m\lambda)mλ​=m/(1+mλ). This proof needs m≥0m\ge0m≥0, λ>0\lambda>0λ>0, convex minimizer existence and a lower bound, without differentiability of the original function.

01Two exact quadratic identities §

Mathematical statement and proof

For a+b=1a+b=1a+b=1, expand inner products to prove ∥au+bv∥2=a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2−ab∥u−v∥2\|au+bv\|^2=a\|u\|^2+b\|v\|^2-ab\|u-v\|^2∥au+bv∥2=a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2−ab∥u−v∥2. The needed effective modulus follows from the exact square

m∥u∥2+∥u−v∥2λ−m∥v∥21+mλ=∥(1+mλ)u−v∥2λ(1+mλ)≥0.m\|u\|^2+\frac{\|u-v\|^2}{\lambda}-\frac{m\|v\|^2}{1+m\lambda}=\frac{\|(1+m\lambda)u-v\|^2}{\lambda(1+m\lambda)}\ge0.m∥u∥2+λ∥u−v∥2​−1+mλm∥v∥2​=λ(1+mλ)∥(1+mλ)u−v∥2​≥0.

02Compare actual minimizers §

Mathematical statement and proof

If UUU is mmm-strongly convex, its actual full Moreau envelope is

mλ=m1+mλ-strongly convex. m_\lambda=\frac{m}{1+m\lambda}\text{-strongly convex}. mλ​=1+mλm​-strongly convex.

The proof evaluates the envelope at the convex combination of its two actual proximal minimizers, applies strong convexity to UUU, and uses the exact square identity for that combination. The remaining comparison is the weighted quadratic inequality

m1+mλ∥v∥2≤m∥u∥2+∥u−v∥2λ. \frac{m}{1+m\lambda}\|v\|^2 \le m\|u\|^2+\frac{\|u-v\|^2}{\lambda}. 1+mλm​∥v∥2≤m∥u∥2+λ∥u−v∥2​.

Completing the square proves this inequality with the displayed sharp modulus. No differentiability of UUU is needed. In contrast, smoothing only g2g_2g2​ preserves the modulus of the unsmoothed g1g_1g1​; the two constructions must not share a convexity constant by notation alone.
