Strong convexity after full Moreau smoothing

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

Full Moreau smoothing reduces the curvature by factor 1+mλ1+m\lambda1+mλ. Specifically an mmm-strongly convex potential has envelope curvature mλ=m/(1+mλ)m_\lambda=m/(1+m\lambda)mλ​=m/(1+mλ) under m≥0m\ge0m≥0 and λ>0\lambda>0λ>0. Compare the envelope at a convex combination of inputs with the objective at the corresponding combination of proximal minimizers. Combining the potential and quadratic-penalty deficits and completing a square proves the result. Existence of those minimizers follows from finite-dimensional convexity and the lower bound; differentiability of the original potential is not required.

01Quadratic identities for envelope curvature §

Mathematical statement and proof

For a+b=1a+b=1a+b=1, inner-product expansion gives ∥au+bv∥2=a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2−ab∥u−v∥2\|au+bv\|^2=a\|u\|^2+b\|v\|^2-ab\|u-v\|^2∥au+bv∥2=a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2−ab∥u−v∥2. The same expansion and completion of a square give

m∥u∥2+∥u−v∥2λ−m∥v∥21+mλ=∥(1+mλ)u−v∥2λ(1+mλ)≥0.m\|u\|^2+\frac{\|u-v\|^2}{\lambda}-\frac{m\|v\|^2}{1+m\lambda}=\frac{\|(1+m\lambda)u-v\|^2}{\lambda(1+m\lambda)}\ge0.m∥u∥2+λ∥u−v∥2​−1+mλm∥v∥2​=λ(1+mλ)∥(1+mλ)u−v∥2​≥0.

The denominator λ(1+mλ)\lambda(1+m\lambda)λ(1+mλ) is positive under λ>0\lambda>0λ>0 and m≥0m\ge0m≥0. Dropping the nonnegative square therefore bounds a quadratic in the input difference by the sum of a quadratic in the proximal-output difference and a quadratic in the residual difference. This is the precise coefficient needed in the convexity comparison.

02Strong convexity from the proximal minimizer comparison §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let UUU be mmm-strongly convex. Its full Moreau envelope is

mλ=m1+mλ-strongly convex. m_\lambda=\frac{m}{1+m\lambda}\text{-strongly convex}. mλ​=1+mλm​-strongly convex.

Use the convex combination of the two proximal minimizers as a competitor. Strong convexity of UUU and the quadratic-combination identity give two nonnegative deficits. Their required bound is

m1+mλ∥v∥2≤m∥u∥2+∥u−v∥2λ. \frac{m}{1+m\lambda}\|v\|^2 \le m\|u\|^2+\frac{\|u-v\|^2}{\lambda}. 1+mλm​∥v∥2≤m∥u∥2+λ∥u−v∥2​.

The completed-square identity proves this coefficient without differentiability of UUU. By comparison, smoothing only g2g_2g2​ and adding it to an unsmoothed strongly convex g1g_1g1​ preserves the curvature of the latter term.

In detail, put p=Pλ(x)p=P_\lambda(x)p=Pλ​(x), q=Pλ(y)q=P_\lambda(y)q=Pλ​(y), and z=ax+byz=ax+byz=ax+by, and test at ap+bqap+bqap+bq for a+b=1a+b=1a+b=1. The potential deficit is mab2∥p−q∥2\frac{mab}{2}\|p-q\|^22mab​∥p−q∥2 and the penalty deficit is ab2λ∥(p−q)−(x−y)∥2\frac{ab}{2\lambda}\|(p-q)-(x-y)\|^22λab​∥(p−q)−(x−y)∥2. Their sum is at least ab2m1+mλ∥x−y∥2\frac{ab}{2}\frac{m}{1+m\lambda}\|x-y\|^22ab​1+mλm​∥x−y∥2 because λ(1+mλ)\lambda(1+m\lambda)λ(1+mλ) is positive. Equivalently, with u=p−qu=p-qu=p−q and v=x−yv=x-yv=x−y, the two quadratic expressions are m∥u∥2m\|u\|^2m∥u∥2 and ∥u−v∥2/λ\|u-v\|^2/\lambda∥u−v∥2/λ; their sum is bounded below by m∥v∥2/(1+mλ)m\|v\|^2/(1+m\lambda)m∥v∥2/(1+mλ). This is exactly the strong-convexity correction at the original inputs.
