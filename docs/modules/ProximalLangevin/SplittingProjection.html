01Establish state and projected second moments §

Mathematical statement and proof

The law equals the pushforward of the constructed L2L^2L2 chain. The map-measure criterion then proves z↦z∈L2(ρn)z\mapsto z\in L^2(\rho_n)z↦z∈L2(ρn​). Apply the bounded linear parameter map and subtract a constant to get θ(z)−a∈L2(ρn)\theta(z)-a\in L^2(\rho_n)θ(z)−a∈L2(ρn​) for every center aaa.

02Combine transport, equilibrium concentration and center displacement §

Mathematical statement and proof

Sharp marginal concentration gives target RMS at most dθ/(Nm)\sqrt{d_\theta/(Nm)}dθ​/(Nm)​, and the actual uniform potential gap gives ∥θU−θη∥≤ηK2/m\|\theta_U-\theta_\eta\|\le\sqrt{\eta K^2/m}∥θU​−θη​∥≤ηK2/m​. Nonexpansive parameter projection and the center triangle inequality yield

RMS⁡ρn(θU)≤ηK2/m+dθ/(Nm)+W2(ρn,ΠN,η).\operatorname{RMS}_{\rho_n}(\theta_U)\le\sqrt{\eta K^2/m}+\sqrt{d_\theta/(Nm)}+W_2(\rho_n,\Pi_{N,\eta}).RMSρn​​(θU​)≤ηK2/m​+dθ​/(Nm)​+W2​(ρn​,ΠN,η​).
