01Expectation is a bounded linear map from L² §

Mathematical statement and proof

The norm-of-integral inequality and ∥X∥L1≤∥X∥L2\|X\|_{L^1}\le\|X\|_{L^2}∥X∥L1​≤∥X∥L2​ on a probability space give ∥EQX∥≤∥X∥2\|\mathbb E_Q X\|\le\|X\|_2∥EQ​X∥≤∥X∥2​.
