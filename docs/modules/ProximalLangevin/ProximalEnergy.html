The nonsmooth single-pair energy estimate

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

For convex fff, a true proximal minimizer p=Pη(x)p=P_\eta(x)p=Pη​(x), positive η\etaη, and a genuine input subgradient s∈∂f(x)s\in\partial f(x)s∈∂f(x), proximity controls both squared distance and the original nonsmooth energy.

Statement and proof

01Expand supports and complete a square §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let p=Pλ(y)p=P_\lambda(y)p=Pλ​(y), s∈∂g2(y)s\in\partial g_2(y)s∈∂g2​(y), and aaa be any comparator. Proximal optimality supplies the residual subgradient at ppp. Combine its support inequality at aaa with the support inequality for sss at ppp, expand the squared distances, and use the nonnegative square ∥(y−p)−λs∥2\|(y-p)-\lambda s\|^2∥(y−p)−λs∥2. The result is v3 Lemma A.8:

∥p−a∥2≤∥y−a∥2−2λ(g2(y)−g2(a))+λ2∥s∥2. \|p-a\|^2\le\|y-a\|^2 -2\lambda\bigl(g_2(y)-g_2(a)\bigr)+\lambda^2\|s\|^2. ∥p−a∥2≤∥y−a∥2−2λ(g2​(y)−g2​(a))+λ2∥s∥2.

If ∥s∥2≤C\|s\|^2\le C∥s∥2≤C, replace the last term by λ2C\lambda^2Cλ2C. The proof works with any genuine subgradient satisfying that bound; it does not assume an energy estimate or a convergence statement. Taking the minimal section in C1 is one way to supply such a vector.

02Instantiate the chosen point and bound the forcing §

Mathematical statement and proof

The existence theorem proves that the chosen Pη(x)P_\eta(x)Pη​(x) meets the variational premise. Substitution gives ∥Pη(x)−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2∥s∥2\|P_\eta(x)-a\|^2\le\|x-a\|^2-2\eta(f(x)-f(a))+\eta^2\|s\|^2∥Pη​(x)−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2∥s∥2. If ∥s∥2≤C\|s\|^2\le C∥s∥2≤C, multiply by the nonnegative η2\eta^2η2 to replace the final term by η2C\eta^2 Cη2C. The vector must satisfy the true support inequality; a merely named subgradient is insufficient.

Expand ∥p−y∥2=∥x−y∥2−2⟨x−p,x−y⟩+∥x−p∥2\|p-y\|^2=\|x-y\|^2-2\langle x-p,x-y\rangle+\|x-p\|^2∥p−y∥2=∥x−y∥2−2⟨x−p,x−y⟩+∥x−p∥2, and use the residual subgradient at ppp to replace its pairing with the potential difference. A supplied subgradient sx∈∂f(x)s_x\in\partial f(x)sx​∈∂f(x) bounds the remaining difference f(x)−f(p)f(x)-f(p)f(x)−f(p) by ⟨sx,x−p⟩\langle s_x,x-p\rangle⟨sx​,x−p⟩. Completing the square gives 2λ⟨sx,x−p⟩−∥x−p∥2≤λ2∥sx∥22\lambda\langle s_x,x-p\rangle-\|x-p\|^2\le\lambda^2\|s_x\|^22λ⟨sx​,x−p⟩−∥x−p∥2≤λ2∥sx​∥2. Thus a bound ∥sx∥2≤C\|s_x\|^2\le C∥sx​∥2≤C produces the forcing λ2C\lambda^2Cλ2C. This algebra is why that forcing has a squared proximal scale.
