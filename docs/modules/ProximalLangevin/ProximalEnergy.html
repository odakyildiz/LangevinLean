A distance–energy inequality for one proximal step

References and publication status for this development

Published source; unpublished corrected proofs

The algorithms are from the published paper below; the correspondence audit uses arXiv:2406.14292v3 (29 May 2025). The corrected MYIPLA, PIPGLA, and MYPGD bounds and proofs presented here are unpublished. The audit records their differences from the paper’s printed rates and constants, and the claims that remain outside the formalization.

  • Paula Cordero Encinar, Francesca Romana Crucinio, and Omer Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Proximal Interacting Particle Langevin Algorithms. Proceedings of the Forty-first Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 286, 1220–1265. Audited version: arXiv:2406.14292v3

Let EEE be a real inner-product space, f:E→Rf:E\to\mathbb Rf:E→R convex, η>0\eta>0η>0 and x∈Ex\in Ex∈E, and let ppp be a proximal point of fff at xxx with parameter η\etaη, i.e. a global minimizer of f(⋅)+∥⋅−x∥2/(2η)f(\cdot)+\|\cdot-x\|^2/(2\eta)f(⋅)+∥⋅−x∥2/(2η). If sss is a subgradient of fff at the input point xxx, then for every comparison point a∈Ea\in Ea∈E

∥p−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2∥s∥2.\|p-a\|^2\le\|x-a\|^2-2\eta\bigl(f(x)-f(a)\bigr)+\eta^2\|s\|^2.∥p−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2∥s∥2.

Thus one proximal step decreases the squared distance to any point aaa by 2η2\eta2η times the energy excess f(x)−f(a)f(x)-f(a)f(x)−f(a), up to the error η2∥s∥2\eta^2\|s\|^2η2∥s∥2. This is the deterministic inequality used in Lemma A.8 of Cordero Encinar, Crucinio and Akyildiz (arXiv:2406.14292v3); a bound ∥s∥2≤C\|s\|^2\le C∥s∥2≤C turns the error into η2C\eta^2Cη2C. Any subgradient at xxx may be used. The inequality is applied pairwise to the particle updates in the next section and, with a random comparison point, in the energy estimate of the splitting scheme.

01The energy inequality for an arbitrary proximal point §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let f:E→Rf:E\to\mathbb Rf:E→R be convex, η>0\eta>0η>0, x,p,s,a∈Ex,p,s,a\in Ex,p,s,a∈E. Assume that ppp is a proximal point of fff at xxx with parameter η\etaη and that s∈∂f(x)s\in\partial f(x)s∈∂f(x), i.e. f(x)+⟨s,y−x⟩≤f(y)f(x)+\langle s,y-x\rangle\le f(y)f(x)+⟨s,y−x⟩≤f(y) for every y∈Ey\in Ey∈E. Then

∥p−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2∥s∥2.\|p-a\|^2\le\|x-a\|^2-2\eta\bigl(f(x)-f(a)\bigr)+\eta^2\|s\|^2.∥p−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2∥s∥2.

Proof. Put d=x−pd=x-pd=x−p. Four facts are combined.

Support at the output. Since fff is convex and ppp is a proximal point, the residual η−1d\eta^{-1}dη−1d is a subgradient of fff at ppp. Testing it at aaa gives f(p)+η−1⟨d,a−p⟩≤f(a)f(p)+\eta^{-1}\langle d,a-p\rangle\le f(a)f(p)+η−1⟨d,a−p⟩≤f(a); multiplying by η>0\eta>0η>0 and using a−p=−(p−a)a-p=-(p-a)a−p=−(p−a),

ηf(p)−⟨d,p−a⟩≤ηf(a).(1)\eta f(p)-\langle d,p-a\rangle\le\eta f(a).\qquad(1)ηf(p)−⟨d,p−a⟩≤ηf(a).(1)

Support at the input. Testing s∈∂f(x)s\in\partial f(x)s∈∂f(x) at y=py=py=p and using p−x=−dp-x=-dp−x=−d,

f(x)−⟨s,d⟩≤f(p).(2)f(x)-\langle s,d\rangle\le f(p).\qquad(2)f(x)−⟨s,d⟩≤f(p).(2)

Expansion of the distance. Since x−a=d+(p−a)x-a=d+(p-a)x−a=d+(p−a),

∥x−a∥2=∥d∥2+2⟨d,p−a⟩+∥p−a∥2.(3)\|x-a\|^2=\|d\|^2+2\langle d,p-a\rangle+\|p-a\|^2.\qquad(3)∥x−a∥2=∥d∥2+2⟨d,p−a⟩+∥p−a∥2.(3)

A completed square. Expanding ∥d−ηs∥2≥0\|d-\eta s\|^2\ge0∥d−ηs∥2≥0,

2η⟨s,d⟩−∥d∥2≤η2∥s∥2.(4)2\eta\langle s,d\rangle-\|d\|^2\le\eta^2\|s\|^2.\qquad(4)2η⟨s,d⟩−∥d∥2≤η2∥s∥2.(4)

Now, by (3), then (1) multiplied by 222, then (2) multiplied by 2η>02\eta>02η>0, and finally (4),

∥p−a∥2=∥x−a∥2−∥d∥2−2⟨d,p−a⟩≤∥x−a∥2−∥d∥2+2η(f(a)−f(p))≤∥x−a∥2−∥d∥2+2η(f(a)−f(x))+2η⟨s,d⟩≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2∥s∥2.□\begin{aligned} \|p-a\|^2&=\|x-a\|^2-\|d\|^2-2\langle d,p-a\rangle\\ &\le\|x-a\|^2-\|d\|^2+2\eta\bigl(f(a)-f(p)\bigr)\\ &\le\|x-a\|^2-\|d\|^2+2\eta\bigl(f(a)-f(x)\bigr)+2\eta\langle s,d\rangle\\ &\le\|x-a\|^2-2\eta\bigl(f(x)-f(a)\bigr)+\eta^2\|s\|^2. \qquad\square \end{aligned}∥p−a∥2​=∥x−a∥2−∥d∥2−2⟨d,p−a⟩≤∥x−a∥2−∥d∥2+2η(f(a)−f(p))≤∥x−a∥2−∥d∥2+2η(f(a)−f(x))+2η⟨s,d⟩≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2∥s∥2.□​

Convexity enters only through the residual subgradient (1), and the subgradient sss at the input only through (2) and (4). The point aaa is arbitrary and need not be related to xxx or ppp.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

isProxPoint_energy_le has the hypotheses hf : ConvexOn ℝ univ f, hη : 0 < η, hp : IsProxPoint f η x p and hs : IsSubgradient f x s, and an arbitrary comparison point a : E; the space need not be finite-dimensional.

  1. hres is the residual support subgradient_of_isProxPoint hf hη hp tested at a; hneg and the simplification with real_inner_smul_left and inner_neg_right put it in the form of (1) divided by η\etaη. hscaled multiplies it by η\etaη (mul_le_mul_of_nonneg_left), and hinv, ηη−1=1\eta\eta^{-1}=1ηη−1=1 (mul_inv_cancel₀), cancels the factor; the result is (1).
  2. hsub := hs p, rewritten with p−x=−(x−p)p-x=-(x-p)p−x=−(x−p) and inner_neg_right, is (2).
  3. hdist is (3), from norm_add_sq_real after writing x−a=(x−p)+(p−a)x-a=(x-p)+(p-a)x−a=(x−p)+(p−a).
  4. hyoung is ∥(x−p)−ηs∥2≥0\|(x-p)-\eta s\|^2\ge0∥(x−p)−ηs∥2≥0 expanded with norm_sub_sq_real, norm_smul, abs_of_pos hη, real_inner_smul_right and real_inner_comm, which is (4).
  5. nlinarith, supplied with (2) multiplied by η\etaη, performs the final chain of inequalities.

02The inequality for the proximal map and under a subgradient bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let EEE be finite-dimensional, f:E→Rf:E\to\mathbb Rf:E→R convex and bounded below, η>0\eta>0η>0, x,s,a∈Ex,s,a\in Ex,s,a∈E with s∈∂f(x)s\in\partial f(x)s∈∂f(x), and let PηP_\etaPη​ be the proximal map of fff with parameter η\etaη. Then

∥Pη(x)−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2∥s∥2.(1)\|P_\eta(x)-a\|^2\le\|x-a\|^2-2\eta\bigl(f(x)-f(a)\bigr)+\eta^2\|s\|^2.\qquad(1)∥Pη​(x)−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2∥s∥2.(1)

If moreover ∥s∥2≤C\|s\|^2\le C∥s∥2≤C for a real number CCC, then

∥Pη(x)−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2C.(2)\|P_\eta(x)-a\|^2\le\|x-a\|^2-2\eta\bigl(f(x)-f(a)\bigr)+\eta^2C.\qquad(2)∥Pη​(x)−a∥2≤∥x−a∥2−2η(f(x)−f(a))+η2C.(2)

Proof. Under these hypotheses Pη(x)P_\eta(x)Pη​(x) is a proximal point of fff at xxx (existence of proximal points), so (1) is the previous step with p=Pη(x)p=P_\eta(x)p=Pη​(x). Since η2≥0\eta^2\ge0η2≥0, ∥s∥2≤C\|s\|^2\le C∥s∥2≤C implies η2∥s∥2≤η2C\eta^2\|s\|^2\le\eta^2Cη2∥s∥2≤η2C, and (2) follows from (1). □\square□

A numerical bound on ∥s∥2\|s\|^2∥s∥2 controls only the last term; sss must still be a subgradient at xxx. For a convex KKK-Lipschitz function every subgradient satisfies ∥s∥≤K\|s\|\le K∥s∥≤K, so (2) holds with C=K2C=K^2C=K2.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

prox_energy_le adds [FiniteDimensional ℝ E] and the lower bound hb : ∃ b : ℝ, ∀ z, b ≤ f z, which are the hypotheses of prox_isProxPoint; its proof is the one-line application isProxPoint_energy_le hf hη (prox_isProxPoint hf hb hη x) hs a. prox_energy_le_of_subgradient_bound adds hC : ‖s‖ ^ 2 ≤ C; it chains prox_energy_le with the monotonicity step η2∥s∥2≤η2C\eta^2\|s\|^2\le\eta^2Cη2∥s∥2≤η2C, which gcongr proves from hC and η2≥0\eta^2\ge0η2≥0.

The last remark of the mathematical text (subgradients of a KKK-Lipschitz convex function have norm at most KKK) is not part of these statements; the bounds on subgradients that are actually used are proved where they are needed.
