02Let the compact-time modulus shrink §

Mathematical statement and proof

The drift along the continuous integral solution is uniformly continuous on [0,TTT]. Choose a drift tolerance from the requested path error, then refine the mesh below its continuity radius. The preceding estimate proves simultaneous convergence of all Euler grid points.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.16.1.

03Derive uniform state and score moments §

Mathematical statement and proof

The true continuous path has bounded norm on the compact time interval. Eventually all Euler grid points are within distance one of this path. Lipschitz growth then bounds every drift value by LLL times this common state radius plus ∥g(0)∥\|g(0)\|∥g(0)∥. In the L2L^{2}L2 application these are actual uniform second-moment bounds.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.16.1.

04Prove the uniform left-sum estimate §

Mathematical statement and proof

On each partition cell, integrate the difference between the left-endpoint value and the actual continuous integrand. A modulus ε\varepsilonε bounds its norm by hεh\varepsilonhε. The adjacent-interval identity and the norm of a finite sum give total error at most TεT\varepsilonTε. Compact-time uniform continuity then proves convergence of the uniform Riemann sums.

05Pass observations of Euler states to time integrals §

Mathematical statement and proof

Uniform continuity of an observation sends the simultaneous Euler grid error to one common observation error. Sum this error over the partition, and combine it with the continuous path's left Riemann-sum limit. No differentiability of the Brownian path is required.
