Proposition 1: scalar warmup, variable steps and iteration count

References and publication status for this development

Supplied manuscript; checked sampling proofs and separate source mistakes

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. Verified counterexamples to the false Gaussian-design proposition, its empirical fourth-moment event and an intermediate SGLD transient comparison have separate pages linked from the top of the overview. The complete source inventory distinguishes this main-theorem review scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (undated manuscript). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. Supplied Arxiv_version_May_26.tex; publication status not established.

05Verify the accuracy ceiling and conclude the conditional rate §

Mathematical statement and proof

The exact iteration threshold is kε=k0+⌈max⁡{aε2−κ,0}⌉.k_\varepsilon=k_0+\left\lceil\max\left\{\frac a{\varepsilon^2}-\kappa,0\right\}\right\rceil.kε​=k0​+⌈max{ε2a​−κ,0}⌉. The arithmetic envelope theorem proves this implication without a recurrence. The final accuracy theorem combines it with the preceding conditional scalar Proposition 1 to obtain rk≤ε2r_k\le\varepsilon^2rk​≤ε2. It proves no independent assertion that actual LMC errors obey the required recurrence.
