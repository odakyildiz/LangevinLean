The exact third-order LMC theorem for the actual Gaussian algorithm

References and publication status for this development

Supplied manuscript; checked main sampling proofs

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. The complete source inventory distinguishes this main-theorem review scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (undated manuscript). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. Supplied Arxiv_version_May_26.tex; publication status not established.

Statement and proof

01Pass the quadratic-plus-linear estimate to the transport infimum §

Mathematical statement and proof

For each positive ε, the definition of Wasserstein distance supplies a finite initial coupling with cost at most W₂+ε. Nonnegative quadratic and linear coefficients allow both powers of that cost to be replaced by W₂+ε. The resulting polynomial upper bound is continuous as ε decreases to zero. Passing to this limit proves the exact quadratic-plus-linear estimate in the actual Wasserstein distance without assuming that any optimal coupling exists.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.17.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.17.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4; IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.1.

02Instantiate the error recurrence with actual marginal laws §

Mathematical statement and proof

Finite initial transport propagates along the actual constant-step Gaussian LMC laws. At every step, use the canonical coupling estimate and the quadratic-plus-linear infimum theorem to obtain the scalar recurrence for their actual Wasserstein errors. Strong convexity and coordinate smoothness give m≤Mav≤L. Split √(Mav p) into √Mav√p and apply the proved recurrence with A=√Mav, δ=Mδ and S=√p. Its invariant floor is exactly (2L√Mav+Mδ)h√p/m, while its transient is (1−mh)ᵏ times the true initial distance. The constant-schedule law identity converts the recursive marginals to iteration of the actual Gaussian transition.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.7.

03Include arbitrary initial probability laws exactly §

Mathematical statement and proof

The manuscript uses the strict step range hLhLhL<1. Together with m≤L this makes 1−mh strictly positive, so its kth power is positive for every k. An infinite initial extended Wasserstein distance therefore gives an infinite right side, proving that case immediately. For finite initial distance, the actual-law theorem and derived finite output transport convert back to the extended nonnegative inequality. Thus the final statement imposes no independent initial second-moment assumption.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.7.
