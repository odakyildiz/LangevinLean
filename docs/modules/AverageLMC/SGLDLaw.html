01Define the update and propagate probability laws §

Mathematical statement and proof

The proposal subtracts h times n times the difference between the selected component gradients at the current state and the anchor, then adds the Gaussian innovation. Mapping the product measure defines one transition; iteration defines all marginal laws. Algebra separates the full-gradient proposal and the centered estimator error. Lipschitz gradients give measurability, and the positive finite index count gives normalization of all these laws.

02Derive the required second moments §

Mathematical statement and proof

A Lipschitz score acts on every L2 input, including its nonzero value at the origin. The Gaussian proposal is a sum of the input, its scaled score and independent Gaussian noise, so it lies in L2. Adding the finite-index estimator error proves a second moment for the actual SGLD output law.

03Apply the constructed centered-perturbation coupling §

Mathematical statement and proof

Instantiate the general variance-addition theorem on initialization times Gaussian innovation. The perturbation is minus h times the estimator error, whose index mean is zero by the proved unbiasedness identity. Pulling h squared through its norm integral gives the actual SGLD transport bound. Fubini removes the unused Gaussian variable and identifies the remaining variance as estimator energy minus full-gradient energy.

04Recover the manuscript's component-energy term §

Mathematical statement and proof

The exact uniform-index identity makes estimator energy equal to n times the sum of squared component-gradient increments. Each increment is square integrable by its Lipschitz score bound, justifying interchange of the finite sum and the initial-law integral.
