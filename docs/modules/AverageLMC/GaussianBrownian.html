Statement and proof

01Center and measure future Brownian noise §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Langevin additive noise is √2 times a Brownian increment. Its expectation vanishes at every time, so the expectation of a future difference also vanishes. Algebraically, the difference between the noises at h and t is the additive noise over the segment starting at t with length h−t. The existing exact Brownian L² norm formula therefore gives its squared norm as 2(h−t)d. This uses the true elapsed-time Gaussian variance and covers the endpoint t=h.

02Derive the orthogonality of the actual error §

Mathematical statement and proof

The complete initial state and stopped Brownian past are independent of the future Brownian increment. Projection onto the initial-state and current-noise coordinates gives zero L² inner product with each of them because the future increment is centered. For Y(t), the natural-past measurability of the actual integral solution has already been proved by the invariant-subspace construction; applying it to the identity vector field gives the same zero inner product. Subtract and add the three identities to prove that X+B(t)−Y(t) is orthogonal to B(h)−B(t). No stochastic-error orthogonality condition is assumed in the final LMC theorem.
