Bounded weighted observations are uniformly continuous on L2L^{2}L2

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; checked main sampling proofs

The correspondence uses arXiv:2605.31413v1, identical to the manuscript audited here. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3C^3C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. The source audit distinguishes this main-theorem scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (2026). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. arXiv preprint arXiv:2605.31413, version 1 (29 May 2026). Full paper

The observation is the actual probability integral EEE[www F(X)]F(X)]F(X)]. Its continuity is proved from boundedness and a second-moment tail estimate, allowing weak Euler limits to be taken on the L2L^{2}L2 state space.

The actual weighted observation is

Ow,F(X)=∫w(a)F(X(a)) P(da).\mathcal O_{w,F}(X)=\int w(a)F(X(a))\,P(da).Ow,F​(X)=∫w(a)F(X(a))P(da).

02Make the continuity estimate explicit §

Mathematical statement and proof

If FFF oscillates by at most ε\varepsilonε at distance at most δ\deltaδ, then everywhere ∣F(x)−F(y)∣≤ε+(2C/δ2)∥x−y∥2|F(x)-F(y)|\le \varepsilon +(2C/\delta ^{2})\|x-y\|^{2}∣F(x)−F(y)∣≤ε+(2C/δ2)∥x−y∥2. Integrate against the bounded weight and identify the squared L2L^{2}L2 norm with the squared-distance integral. The resulting bound is A[ε+(2C/δ2)∥X−Y∥2]A[\varepsilon +(2C/\delta ^{2})\|X-Y\|^{2}]A[ε+(2C/δ2)∥X−Y∥2].

03Transfer uniform continuity from state tests §

Mathematical statement and proof

Choose a small test oscillation first, then a sufficiently small L2L^{2}L2 radius to control the quadratic tail term. This proves uniform continuity of X↦E[wF(X)]X\mapsto\mathbb E[wF(X)]X↦E[wF(X)] for every bounded uniformly continuous FFF.
