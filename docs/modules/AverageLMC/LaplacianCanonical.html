The canonical stationary coupling for the third-order LMC estimate

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; checked main sampling proofs

The correspondence uses arXiv:2605.31413v1, identical to the manuscript audited here. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3C^3C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. The source audit distinguishes this main-theorem scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

Mathematical statement and proof

Extend each finite-cost coupling by the canonical Brownian probability space and construct the continuous integral solution started at its Gibbs coordinate. Stationarity, exact Brownian moments and standard Gaussian innovations follow from their existing constructions. The deterministic gradient-step difference Ψ\PsiΨ and the initial reference coordinate are a measurable function of the coupled initial pair. The product measure therefore makes this pair independent of every full Brownian Euler innovation sequence; almost-everywhere L2L^{2}L2 representatives preserve that independence. Apply the stationary gradient pairing theorem on each restricted interval [0,ttt] to obtain the cross-term bound with the true constant LMavp+MΔpL\sqrt{M_{\mathrm{av}}p}+M_\Delta\sqrt pLMav​p​+MΔ​p​. The local one-step energy theorem supplies the contracted error, exact cross coefficient and total-defect square. The endpoint Euler random vector is proved to have the ordinary Gaussian LMC law, while the reference endpoint has the Gibbs law. Their actual L2L^{2}L2 coupling bounds Wasserstein distance. The exact initial-coupling cost identity then gives the claimed quadratic-plus-linear estimate for every finite initial coupling.
