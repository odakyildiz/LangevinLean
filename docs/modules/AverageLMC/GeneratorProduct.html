Statement and proof

01Differentiate the product once and twice §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Fréchet product rule, evaluated on a fixed direction, gives Dᵥ(χψ)=χDᵥψ+ψDᵥχ. The corresponding addition rule allows this expression to be differentiated once more. For C² factors their first directional derivatives are differentiable, so another product rule gives Dᵥ²(χψ)=χDᵥ²ψ+ψDᵥ²χ+2DᵥχDᵥψ. The cross coefficient is exactly two.

02Sum the directional generator product rules §

Mathematical statement and proof

Subtract the first-order gradient drift from the second directional derivative product identity. Algebra separates the two factors’ directional generators and retains the cross term 2DᵥχDᵥψ. Summing over the standard orthonormal basis produces the full Langevin generator product rule. The sum of the products of the directional derivatives equals the inner product of the two actual gradients by the orthonormal-basis inner-product identity.

03Identify the coordinate score generator §

Mathematical statement and proof

A coordinate score is the continuous linear projection of the C² vector field ∇f. Projection commutes with its vector Laplacian, and the proved C³ commutation identity turns this into the corresponding coordinate of ∇Δf. The Fréchet chain rule identifies its derivative along ∇f with the same coordinate of D(∇f)∇f. Subtracting the drift term therefore gives exactly that coordinate of the actual vector gradientGenerator.
