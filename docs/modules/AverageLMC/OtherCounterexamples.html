01A design row defeats the proposed uniform fourth-moment event §

Mathematical statement and proof

Choose the unit direction parallel to a nonzero design row. Its own summand gives the fourth power of its norm, and all other summands are nonnegative. A uniform fourth-moment bound of order p would imply a cubic norm bound of order p with a fixed constant. The proved Gaussian cubic-norm probability estimate shows that this event has probability below one half in sufficiently large square dimensions. This is a separate proof-level failure from the actual Hessian counterexample.

02Check a concrete failure of the displayed reciprocal transient comparison §

Mathematical statement and proof

A finite exponential Taylor sum proves log(200) at most 16/3. Thus a=1/3, epsilon=1/10 and k=16 meet the immediately displayed threshold k at least a inverse log(2/epsilon squared). Exact rational arithmetic shows that (1+a) to the power minus k still exceeds 0.6 epsilon squared. The later stricter precision-dependent step choice is a separate claim.
