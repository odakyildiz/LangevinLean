01Force the accumulated weak residual to zero §

Mathematical statement and proof

Suppose every positive ε permits an eventual bound C[ε+K√(T/(n+1))], with K allowed to depend on ε. First fix ε small relative to the desired error; then refine the mesh so its square-root term is equally small. This proves that the actual scalar residual converges to zero.

02Identify the unique limiting residual §

Mathematical statement and proof

Uniform continuity sends the terminal matching-noise Euler state to the terminal test observation. Uniform mesh convergence sends its generator sum to the time integral. Their difference therefore converges to the proposed Dynkin residual, while the summed weak remainder converges to zero. Uniqueness of real limits gives the exact identity.
