Regularity of compact C² Langevin tests and generators

References and publication status for this development

Supplied manuscript; checked main sampling proofs

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. The complete source inventory distinguishes this main-theorem review scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (undated manuscript). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. Supplied Arxiv_version_May_26.tex; publication status not established.

The drift is the paper's Lipschitz gradient, and tests have only two continuous derivatives. Both the test and its actual Langevin generator yield bounded uniformly continuous observations needed in the Euler limit.

For every compactly supported C2C^2C2 test and Lipschitz score, its actual Langevin generator remains continuous and compactly supported:

Lfϕ=Δϕ−Dϕ[∇f],ϕ∈Cc2 ⟹ Lfϕ∈Cc0.\mathcal L_f\phi=\Delta\phi-D\phi[\nabla f],\qquad \phi\in C_c^2\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathcal L_f\phi\in C_c^0.Lf​ϕ=Δϕ−Dϕ[∇f],ϕ∈Cc2​ ⟹ Lf​ϕ∈Cc0​.

Consequently both scalar observations admit global finite bounds and uniform continuity, and hence

X⟼E[wϕ(X)],X⟼E[wLfϕ(X)]are uniformly continuous on L2(P)(w∈L∞(P)).X\longmapsto\mathbb E[w\phi(X)],\qquad X\longmapsto\mathbb E[w\mathcal L_f\phi(X)] \quad\text{are uniformly continuous on }L^2(P) \quad (w\in L^\infty(P)).X⟼E[wϕ(X)],X⟼E[wLf​ϕ(X)]are uniformly continuous on L2(P)(w∈L∞(P)).

Statement and proof

01Control the actual generator §

Mathematical statement and proof

Continuity of the Hessian makes the Laplacian continuous. The drift derivative term is continuous by continuity of the derivative and the Lipschitz gradient. Derivatives of compactly supported tests vanish off their support, so the generator also has compact support.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.46.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.46.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.46.8; ULA-SC, Section 3.46.9.

02Obtain global bounds and uniform continuity §

Mathematical statement and proof

A continuous compactly supported test is bounded and uniformly continuous. Apply the same facts to its generator, using the proved generator continuity and compact support. No extra regularity of the potential is introduced.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.46.2.

03Build the L² observation interface §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply the weighted-test uniform continuity theorem to a compact C² test and its generator. These are the precise observation functions appearing in the abstract matching-noise Euler limit.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.46.2.
