The third-order one-step energy estimate for the actual Euler coupling

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; checked main sampling proofs

The correspondence uses arXiv:2605.31413v1, identical to the manuscript audited here. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3C^3C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. The source audit distinguishes this main-theorem scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (2026). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. arXiv preprint arXiv:2605.31413, version 1 (29 May 2026). Full paper

The proof retains the cancellation in the defect cross term while bounding the complete defect by the existing first-order Brownian estimate. All gradient contraction and energy algebra concern the actual lifted gradient and actual integral solution.

Mathematical statement and proof

Let Ψ\PsiΨ be the difference between the deterministic gradient steps of XXX and Y(0)Y(0)Y(0), and let DDD be the exact drift integral omitted by Euler. The integral equation gives the exact output error Ψ+D\Psi +DΨ+D. On the admissible range hM≤1hM\le1hM≤1 and m≤Mm\le Mm≤M, the actual smooth strongly convex gradient step contracts by q=1−mh≥0q=1-mh\ge 0q=1−mh≥0, so ∥Ψ∥≤q∥X−Y(0)∥\|\Psi \|\le q\|X-Y(0)\|∥Ψ∥≤q∥X−Y(0)∥. The first-order total-defect theorem gives ∥D∥≤(3/2)Mhhd\|D\|\le (3/2)Mh\sqrt h\sqrt d∥D∥≤(3/2)Mhh​d​. The separately proved weak-Dynkin pairing inequality bounds the pairing with the instantaneous gradient increment by t∥Ψ∥Rgent\|\Psi \|R_{\mathrm{gen}}t∥Ψ∥Rgen​. Continuous linear integration then bounds ⟨Ψ,D⟩\langle \Psi ,D\rangle⟨Ψ,D⟩ by h2∥Ψ∥Rgen/2h^{2}\|\Psi \|R_{\mathrm{gen}}/2h2∥Ψ∥Rgen​/2. Expanding ∥Ψ+D∥2\|\Psi +D\|^{2}∥Ψ+D∥2 and substituting the three bounds yields q2q^{2}q2 times the original error squared, the exact cross term h2q∥X−Y(0)∥Rgenh^{2}q\|X-Y(0)\|R_{\mathrm{gen}}h2q∥X−Y(0)∥Rgen​, and the squared total-defect bound. The pairing premise is an intermediate analytic consequence of the actual stationary diffusion, discharged by the Dynkin and cutoff construction.
