Supplied manuscript; checked main sampling proofs

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. The complete source inventory distinguishes this main-theorem review scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

01Derive the unrestricted one-step claim §

Mathematical statement and proof

Combine the actual average-smoothness LMC inequality with variance addition. The exact variance identity subtracts one full-gradient energy from estimator energy. The leftover coefficient is at most h squared times (hMhMhM-2)/(1+mh), because the discarded term is nonpositive. This gives Proposition 3's first estimate for every nonnegative step, with its actual estimator-energy term.

02Obtain the RMS recurrence and propagate finite transport §

Mathematical statement and proof

When hMhMhM is at most two, drop the nonpositive score term. The exact component RMS bounds estimator energy by R squared times the initial squared radius about the minimizer. The radius estimate is twice the squared transport error plus 2p/m, yielding exactly the second claim's coefficients. Actual proposals preserve L2 and probability, so finite quadratic transport propagates along every iteration.

03Prove the constant-step main theorem §

Mathematical statement and proof

The full gradient has Lipschitz constant at most the exact R=n MrmsM_{\rm rms}Mrms​, and strong convexity gives m at most R. The paper's step restriction therefore ensures the local stability condition. Apply the proved scalar recurrence to the actual SGLD marginal laws. The result has the reciprocal contraction and the precise persistent error A+2m+2 R squared/m. Substituting m=n mbar and dividing the full average by n produces the printed theorem in average-potential units.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

04Remove a finite-moment hypothesis from the main statement §

Mathematical statement and proof

The extended endpoint applies the positive-affine transport extension. It has the same contraction, step restriction and average-potential constants for every probability initialization. For infinite initial distance its right side is infinite; otherwise all moments used by the real proof are derived from finite transport to the Gibbs target. The source-facing paper endpoint uses the literal coordinate average of the divided potential f/n. Gradient linearity rescales every coordinate Taylor quotient, and the proved homogeneity of their exact suprema identifies this constant with the full average divided by n, without a chosen replacement bound.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.
