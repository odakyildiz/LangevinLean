Weighted weak consistency of the Euler chain

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; checked main sampling proofs

The correspondence uses arXiv:2605.31413v1, identical to the manuscript audited here. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3C^3C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition.
