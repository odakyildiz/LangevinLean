01Integrate a fresh Gaussian step with its retained past weight §

Mathematical statement and proof

Bounded observations times a bounded weight are integrable. The joint product law of weight, state and next innovation lets the update expectation be rewritten as its actual conditional Gaussian integral, by mapping measures and applying Fubini. Applying the uniform pointwise C2 expansion gives a weighted one-step residual with the same K at every state and mesh.

02Retain the weight through the real Euler recursion §

Mathematical statement and proof

Adjoin the initial weight as a passive coordinate of the noise-driven chain. The ordinary iid recursion theorem supplies its product joint law with the next innovation. Passive-coordinate retention and the almost-everywhere representation of the L2 Euler chain transfer that law to the actual quotient-valued process. The generator formula and the exact L2 drift norm then give the genuine one-step weak residual.

03Sum a uniform local error over the full time horizon §

Mathematical statement and proof

The signed local residuals telescope to final expectation minus initial expectation minus the generator Riemann sum. The triangle inequality bounds the sum by the number of steps times a common local bound. A single L2 drift radius controls every mesh state, so the resulting error is A times the horizon times (epsilon+K sqrt(h)) times (1+R squared). This supplies the discrete stochastic input for the deterministic Euler-to-Dynkin limit.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.19.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.46.2.
