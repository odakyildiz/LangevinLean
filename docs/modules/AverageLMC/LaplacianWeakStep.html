Uniform weak consistency for compact smooth tests

References and publication status for this development

Supplied manuscript; checked main sampling proofs

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3C^3C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. The complete source inventory distinguishes this main-theorem review scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (undated manuscript). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. Supplied Arxiv_version_May_26.tex; publication status not established.

This smooth-test development supplies the Gaussian calculus and drift-noise separation used by the later C2C^{2}C2 extension. Smoothness is imposed only on auxiliary compact tests; the final potential keeps the paper's C3C^{3}C3 hypothesis.

Drift and noise are separated by the deterministic estimate

∣ϕ(x+w+d)−ϕ(x+w)−Dϕ(x)[d]∣≤L2∥d∥2+L∥w∥ ∥d∥.\left|\phi(x+w+d)-\phi(x+w)-D\phi(x)[d]\right| \le\frac L2\|d\|^2+L\|w\|\,\|d\|.∣ϕ(x+w+d)−ϕ(x+w)−Dϕ(x)[d]∣≤2L​∥d∥2+L∥w∥∥d∥.

Mathematical statement and proof

02Control a deterministic drift increment §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply the smooth upper bound to φ\varphiφ and −φ-\varphi−φ to obtain an absolute first-order remainder bounded by L∥d∥2/2L\|d\|^{2}/2L∥d∥2/2. Apply it at x+wx+wx+w and compare the two derivatives by gradient Lipschitz continuity, obtaining the additional L∥w∥∥d∥L\|w\|\|d\|L∥w∥∥d∥ term. This separates a potentially large random drift from the small Gaussian increment.

03Average the true Gaussian update §

Mathematical statement and proof

Use the compact smooth Taylor remainder for the noise alone and the shifted first-order estimate for the drift. Gaussian first and third moments are finite. For 0≤h≤10\le h\le 10≤h≤1, absorb the deterministic h2h^{2}h2 term into hhh\sqrt hhh​ and obtain one uniform constant KKK times hh(1+∥v∥2)h\sqrt h(1+\|v\|^{2})hh​(1+∥v∥2), for arbitrary starting point and drift.
