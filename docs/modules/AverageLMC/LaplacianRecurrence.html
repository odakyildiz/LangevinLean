The scalar quadratic recurrence and its invariant floor

References and publication status for this development

Supplied manuscript; checked main sampling proofs

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. The complete source inventory distinguishes this main-theorem review scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (undated manuscript). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. Supplied Arxiv_version_May_26.tex; publication status not established.

This module isolates arithmetic used after the actual LMC one-step estimate is proved. Its recurrence premise is an intermediate numerical interface, not a replacement for the stochastic theorem.

Statement and proof

01Keep the quadratic cross term §

Mathematical statement and proof

Induct on the iteration number with the comparison q^k e₀+F. The inequalities C≤(1−q)F and 2qCF+B²≤(1−q²)F² make this comparison invariant. Nonnegativity allows comparison of squares without replacing the local error by a triangle bound.

02Split the floor into two contributions §

Mathematical statement and proof

Choose a drift floor a and a variance floor b. From C≤(1−q)a and B²≤(1−q²)b², expand the square of a+b and verify the invariant-floor hypotheses. This retains the order h accumulation of the quadratic defect.

03Recover the manuscript coefficient §

Mathematical statement and proof

Set q=1−mh, C=h²(MA+δ)S/2 and use the manuscript-style local-defect coefficient. The conditions m≤M and hMhMhM≤1 control all square roots. Explicit floors add to (2MA+δ)hShShS/m, giving the claimed iteration bound with A=√MavM_{\rm av}Mav​ and S=√p.
