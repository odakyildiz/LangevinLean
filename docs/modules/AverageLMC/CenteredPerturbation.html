Preprint source; checked main sampling proofs

The correspondence uses arXiv:2605.31413v1, identical to the manuscript audited here. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3C^3C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. The source audit distinguishes this main-theorem scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

This section proves the variance-addition inequality for quadratic transport that underlies the stochastic-gradient bound. Let sss be a random input with law PPP on a measurable space SSS, let iii be an independent random index with law QQQ on a measurable space III, let F(s)F(s)F(s) be an output in a Hilbert space EEE (for instance Rp\mathbb R^pRp), and let D(s,i)D(s,i)D(s,i) be a perturbation whose mean over the index vanishes for every fixed input. Then, for every probability measure π\piπ on EEE with finite second moment,

W22(L(F(s)+D(s,i)),π)≤W22(L(F(s)),π)+E∥D(s,i)∥2,\mathsf W_2^2\bigl(\mathcal L(F(s)+D(s,i)),\pi\bigr)\le\mathsf W_2^2\bigl(\mathcal L(F(s)),\pi\bigr)+\mathbb E\|D(s,i)\|^2,W22​(L(F(s)+D(s,i)),π)≤W22​(L(F(s)),π)+E∥D(s,i)∥2,

where L\mathcal LL denotes the law under P⊗QP\otimes QP⊗Q. For SGLD, s=(ϑ,ξ)s=(\vartheta,\xi)s=(ϑ,ξ), F(s)=ϑ−h∇f(ϑ)+2h ξF(s)=\vartheta-h\nabla f(\vartheta)+\sqrt{2h}\,\xiF(s)=ϑ−h∇f(ϑ)+2h​ξ is the full-gradient Langevin output, i=Ii=Ii=I is the uniform data index and D=−h(gI(ϑ)−∇f(ϑ))D=-h(g_I(\vartheta)-\nabla f(\vartheta))D=−h(gI​(ϑ)−∇f(ϑ)) is the stochastic-gradient error.
