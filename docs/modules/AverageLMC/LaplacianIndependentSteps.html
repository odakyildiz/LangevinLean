The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. Verified counterexamples to the false Gaussian-design proposition, its empirical fourth-moment event and an intermediate SGLD transient comparison have separate pages linked from the top of the overview. The weak consistency proof needs the actual joint law of the current recursive state and its next innovation, including a passive initial weight. These results obtain that law from independence of initialization and an iid noise path.

For iid innovations of law η\etaη, independent of initialization, the actual recursion has the fresh joint law

Xk+1=F(Xk,Wk),Law⁡(Xk,Wk)=Law⁡(Xk)⊗η.X_{k+1}=F(X_k,W_k),\qquad \operatorname{Law}(X_k,W_k)=\operatorname{Law}(X_k)\otimes\eta.Xk+1​=F(Xk​,Wk​),Law(Xk​,Wk​)=Law(Xk​)⊗η.

Copying a passive initial coordinate through each update retains it exactly:

01Identify the current-state and next-noise law §

Mathematical statement and proof

The iid family has the infinite product path law. Independence makes its joint law with the initialization a product. Push this identity through the measurable recursion and use freshness of the canonical product chain to obtain the product of the actual current-state law and the innovation law.

02Retain the initial weight exactly §

Mathematical statement and proof

Run the recursion on a pair whose first coordinate is copied at each step. Induction proves that this coordinate remains the original value while the second coordinate is precisely the ordinary noise-driven chain. This allows a bounded initial weight to be included in the freshness argument.
