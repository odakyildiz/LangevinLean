Mathematical statement and proof

The restriction 3R2h≤m3R^2h\le m3R2h≤m, together with m2≤R2m^2\le R^2m2≤R2, implies mh≤1/3mh\le1/3mh≤1/3. Multiplying by the positive denominator 1+mh1+mh1+mh bounds the actual contraction by its reciprocal. The same step restriction bounds the persistent-error coefficient by the precise expression A+2m+2R2/mA+2m+2R^2/mA+2m+2R2/m, divided by 1+mh1+mh1+mh.
