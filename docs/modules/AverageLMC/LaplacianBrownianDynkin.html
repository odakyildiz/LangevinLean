Weighted Dynkin identity for the constructed Brownian Langevin solution

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; checked main sampling proofs

The correspondence uses arXiv:2605.31413v1, identical to the manuscript audited here. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3C^3C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. The source audit distinguishes this main-theorem scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (2026). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. arXiv preprint arXiv:2605.31413, version 1 (29 May 2026). Full paper

This module completes the stochastic bridge for compact C2C^{2}C2 tests. All formulas are derived from the actual Brownian innovations, the continuous L2L^{2}L2 integral solution, and finite Gaussian Euler chains.

The actual Brownian Euler chains have an eventual uniform score bound on every time partition:

∃R≥0 ∃N ∀n≥N ∀k≤n+1:∥∇f(Ek(n))∥L2(P)≤R.\exists R\ge0\ \exists N\ \forall n\ge N\ \forall k\le n+1: \quad\|\nabla f(E_k^{(n)})\|_{L^2(P)}\le R.∃R≥0 ∃N ∀n≥N ∀k≤n+1:∥∇f(Ek(n)​)∥L2(P)​≤R.

Actual Gaussian weak consistency and the mesh limit prove, for bounded initial weights independent of the innovations and ϕ∈Cc2\phi\in C_c^2ϕ∈Cc2​,

E[wϕ(YT)]−E[wϕ(Y0)]=∫0TE[wLfϕ(Yt)] dt.\mathbb E[w\phi(Y_T)]-\mathbb E[w\phi(Y_0)] =\int_0^T\mathbb E[w\mathcal L_f\phi(Y_t)]\,dt.E[wϕ(YT​)]−E[wϕ(Y0​)]=∫0T​E[wLf​ϕ(Yt​)]dt.

Statement and proof

01Bound all actual Brownian Euler scores §

Mathematical statement and proof

Use the deterministic uniform grid bound in L2L^{2}L2, with the lifted gradient as drift and the actual Brownian noise path. The established equality between additive matching-noise Euler states and the Euler chain in the Hilbert space of square-integrable random vectors identifies the objects used in the probabilistic weak-step proof.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.13.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.19.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.29.7; ULA-SC, Section 3.42.5.

02Derive weighted Dynkin without an assumed formula §

Mathematical statement and proof

For positive TTT, actual Brownian coordinate increments give independent standard Gaussian vector innovations on every refining partition. Keep the bounded initial weight paired with the initialization, so each next innovation is fresh. Apply compact C2C^{2}C2 Gaussian weak consistency, sum its residual using the uniform score moment, and pass to the limit with bounded uniformly continuous test and generator observations. The zero-time case follows directly from the integral equation. The conclusion is E[wφ(YT)]−E[wφ(Y0)]=∫0TE[wLφ(Yt)] dt\mathbb E[w\varphi(Y_T)]-\mathbb E[w\varphi(Y_0)]=\int_0^T\mathbb E[w\mathcal L\varphi(Y_t)]\,dtE[wφ(YT​)]−E[wφ(Y0​)]=∫0T​E[wLφ(Yt​)]dt.
