References and publication status for this development

Supplied manuscript; incomplete formalization and source obstruction

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Deterministic smoothness and gradient lemmas and conditional scalar recurrences are checked. An allowed quadratic-plus-sine GLM contradicts the dimension independent isotropic Hessian Lipschitz bound in Proposition 2. The Gaussian probability obstruction is checked using exact moments. The actual-law LMC, Laplacian-smooth LMC and SGLD convergence theorems remain unproved; a full formalization with the printed hypotheses and conclusions is impossible without correcting Proposition 2.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (undated manuscript). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. Supplied Arxiv_version_May_26.tex; publication status not established.

The audit imports the library and prints the axioms used by each central deterministic, scalar and counterexample theorem. Only the standard Lean mathematical foundations are expected. A successful audit does not discharge the unproved stochastic endpoints or certify the false source proposition.
