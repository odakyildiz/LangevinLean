01Define actual marginal laws and propagate finite moments §

Mathematical statement and proof

The recursive law at step k+1 is exactly ulaLawStep at the deterministic step h(k), applied to the current law. The zero and successor identities expose this recursion. A pushforward of a product of probability laws remains a probability law, proving normalization at every k. For each nonnegative step, the already constructed L² Brownian realization gives finite output transport whenever initial transport is finite; the zero-step identity covers h=0. Induction gives finite moments for every law of an arbitrary nonnegative deterministic schedule.

02Remove the nonpositive score correction and iterate a constant step §

Mathematical statement and proof

When hL≤1, the score correction coefficient is nonpositive and the score second moment is nonnegative, so the exact one-step theorem implies the two-term affine recurrence. A constant schedule agrees by induction with iteration of the actual Gaussian transition. Strong convexity and coordinate smoothness imply m≤Mav≤L, hence 0≤mh≤1. Apply the scalar recurrence theorem with t=mh and forcing coefficient (Mav+m)p/m². Its exponential contraction becomes exp(−2mkh), and its fixed point becomes exactly (Mav+m)hp/(2m). This proves Theorem 1’s global claim for actual LMC laws.

03Normalize the variable schedule without changing its constants §

Mathematical statement and proof

Multiplying the proved stable one-step inequality by m/p gives the dimensionless recurrence for r(k)=m W₂²/p with a=Mav/m+1. The schedule is initially written as variableStep(L/m,k₀,k)/m; elementary division shows it is exactly 1/(L+m(k−k₀)₊), as displayed in Proposition 1. The scalar warmup proof is extended to zero initial error: then k₀=0 and the harmonic tail applies immediately. For nonzero initial error, its absolute value has the same square and the same warmup, so the existing positive-parameter scalar theorem applies without adding a sign restriction.

04Prove the entire variable-step proposition for the actual chain §

Mathematical statement and proof

The coordinate comparison gives κ=L/m≥1 and a>0. The chosen schedule is positive and satisfies h(k)L≤1 at every step. Use the actual-law normalized estimate and the derived finite moments to discharge every recurrence premise. The warmup and harmonic-tail theorem then gives r(k)≤a/(κ+k−k₀) for all k≥k₀. Multiply back by p/m to recover the exact paper envelope. Finally, the explicit positive-part ceiling in variableIterations makes this envelope at most ε²; multiplying by p/m proves the stated sufficient iteration threshold for the actual algorithm.

05Remove separate initial-moment assumptions from the global statements §

Mathematical statement and proof

The exponential contraction factor is strictly positive. If the initial extended Wasserstein distance is infinite, the extended global constant-step inequality has infinite right side and follows immediately. If it is finite, the proved real-valued estimate and finite-output transport convert back to the extended nonnegative inequality. For the variable-step proposition, its stated finite initial squared-distance bound already forces finite initial distance; taking real values gives exactly the initial-error inequality used above. Applying the actual-law variable envelope and accuracy theorems therefore requires no independent finite-moment premise.
