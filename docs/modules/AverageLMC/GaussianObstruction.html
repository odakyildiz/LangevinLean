The actual Gaussian norm probability obstruction

References and publication status for this development

Supplied manuscript; incomplete formalization and source obstruction

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Deterministic smoothness and gradient lemmas and conditional scalar recurrences are checked. An allowed quadratic-plus-sine GLM contradicts the dimension independent isotropic Hessian Lipschitz bound in Proposition 2. The Gaussian probability obstruction is checked using exact moments. The actual-law LMC, Laplacian-smooth LMC and SGLD convergence theorems remain unproved; a full formalization with the printed hypotheses and conclusions is impossible without correcting Proposition 2.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (undated manuscript). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. Supplied Arxiv_version_May_26.tex; publication status not established.

This module uses the actual standard Gaussian measure in dimension p, with no assumed concentration event. It proves the exact variance of its squared norm and rules out a dimension independent order-p bound on its cubed norm with probability one half. This is the probability ingredient for the GLM counterexample.

01Compute the squared-norm variance from exact Gaussian moments §

Mathematical statement and proof

The standard Gaussian second moment is ppp, and the shared fourth-moment theorem gives p2+2pp^2+2pp2+2p. Gaussian integrability of every finite moment proves square integrability of the squared norm before using the variance formula. Subtracting the squared mean gives Var⁡(∥Z∥2)=2p\operatorname{Var}(\|Z\|^2)=2pVar(∥Z∥2)=2p. Thus no finite-moment premise is supplied externally.

02Apply Chebyshev to the small-norm event §

Mathematical statement and proof

If ∥Z∥2<p/2\|Z\|^2<p/2∥Z∥2<p/2, its deviation from its mean p is at least p/2. Chebyshev bounds the probability by 2p/(p/2)2=8/p2p/(p/2)^2=8/p2p/(p/2)2=8/p. The proof uses a set inclusion and the actual Gaussian law, retaining the extended nonnegative probability values.

03Disprove every uniform cubic norm bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let C≥0C\ge0C≥0 and p>8C2p>8C^2p>8C2. If ∥Z∥2≥p/2\|Z\|^2\ge p/2∥Z∥2≥p/2, then ∥Z∥6≥p3/8>C2p2\|Z\|^6\ge p^3/8>C^2p^2∥Z∥6≥p3/8>C2p2, so ∥Z∥3>Cp\|Z\|^3>Cp∥Z∥3>Cp. For every proposed C choose a natural dimension larger than both thresholds; its probability contradicts the proposed universal one-half lower bound.
