Exact scaling of coordinate smoothness constants

References and publication status for this development

Supplied manuscript; checked sampling proofs and separate source mistakes

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition.   • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (undated manuscript). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. Supplied Arxiv_version_May_26.tex; publication status not established.

This module proves that the literal supremum constants commute with nonnegative rescaling of the potential. In particular the barred average-coordinate constant of the SGLD average potential f/n is exactly the full-potential constant divided by n. The proof does not replace either quantity by an arbitrary upper bound.

For a differentiable potential with Lipschitz gradient, nonnegative multiplication commutes with the literal coordinate Taylor suprema:

c≥0⟹Mj(cf)=cMj(f),Mav(cf)=cMav(f).c\ge0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad M_j(cf)=cM_j(f),\qquad M_{\rm av}(cf)=cM_{\rm av}(f).c≥0⟹Mj​(cf)=cMj​(f),Mav​(cf)=cMav​(f).

In particular the SGLD barred constant is exactly the constant of the average potential:

n≥1⟹Mˉav=Mav(f/n)=Mav(f)n.n\ge1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \bar M_{\rm av}=M_{\rm av}(f/n)=\frac{M_{\rm av}(f)}{n}.n≥1⟹Mˉav​=Mav​(f/n)=nMav​(f)​.

Statement and proof

01Rescale the actual gradient and coordinate Taylor quotients §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Frechet derivative of c times f is c times the derivative of f. The Riesz representation defining the gradient commutes with this real scalar, so the true gradient is c times the original gradient. Substitution in the exact coordinate Taylor quotient factors out c. Consequently the entire quotient set of the rescaled potential is the image of the original set under multiplication by c.

02Transport the least upper bound through nonnegative multiplication §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Lipschitz-gradient assumption supplies an upper bound on the original Taylor-quotient set, and that set is nonempty. Its supremum therefore is a genuine least upper bound. Nonnegative multiplication transports that least upper bound to the image set, including scalar zero. This proves exact homogeneity of every coordinate supremum. A finite sum and division by the dimension prove the same identity for MavM_{\rm av}Mav​.

03Identify the paper's barred average-potential constant §

Mathematical statement and proof

Choose the scalar to be the reciprocal of the finite component count. It is nonnegative, and real division is multiplication by this reciprocal. The proved homogeneity yields MavM_{\rm av}Mav​(x ↦ f(x)/n)=MavM_{\rm av}Mav​(f)/n. Thus the source-facing SGLD theorem can state its discretization term with the literal constant of the average potential.
