Expected potential and gradient-step energies on L2

References and publication status for this development

Supplied manuscript; checked sampling proofs and separate source mistakes

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. Verified counterexamples to the false Gaussian-design proposition, its empirical fourth-moment event and an intermediate SGLD transient comparison have separate pages linked from the top of the overview. The complete source inventory distinguishes this main-theorem review scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (undated manuscript). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. Supplied Arxiv_version_May_26.tex; publication status not established.

The deterministic gradient-step and descent inequalities extend to the actual Hilbert space of square-integrable random variables. Potential integrability is proved from quadratic growth, and the lifted drift is identified almost everywhere with the pointwise gradient. These estimates retain the full gradient energy, including its sign-sensitive correction in the sampler one-step bound.

01Integrate the strong gradient-step inequality §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply the integrated strong supporting-plane inequality to Y and X. Reverse the displacement Y-X to X-Y and multiply by nonnegative h. Expand the squared L2 distance after subtracting h times the lifted gradient. The mixed inner product is bounded by the potential difference and strong convexity, giving

∥X−h∇f(X)−Y∥L22≤(1−mh)∥X−Y∥L22+2h(Ef(Y)−Ef(X))+h2∥∇f(X)∥L22.\|X-h\nabla f(X)-Y\|_{L^2}^2\le(1-mh)\|X-Y\|_{L^2}^2+2h(\mathbb Ef(Y)-\mathbb Ef(X))+h^2\|\nabla f(X)\|_{L^2}^2.∥X−h∇f(X)−Y∥L22​≤(1−mh)∥X−Y∥L22​+2h(Ef(Y)−Ef(X))+h2∥∇f(X)∥L22​.

The norms are genuine L2 norms and every expectation is finite.

02Integrate deterministic descent without dropping the score term §

Mathematical statement and proof

The pointwise deterministic descent theorem holds outside a null set where the lifted state arithmetic and lifted gradient agree with their ordinary representatives. Both potential terms are integrable by smooth quadratic growth, and the squared score is integrable by the Lipschitz drift construction. Integrate the pointwise estimate, use linearity, and identify the integral of the squared lifted gradient with its squared L2 norm. The result is

2(Ef(X−h∇f(X))−Ef(X))≤−h(2−hM)∥∇f(X)∥L22.2(\mathbb Ef(X-h\nabla f(X))-\mathbb Ef(X))\le-h(2-hM)\|\nabla f(X)\|_{L^2}^2.2(Ef(X−h∇f(X))−Ef(X))≤−h(2−hM)∥∇f(X)∥L22​.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.13.3; IPLA-SC, Section 3.11.1; Proximal, Section 3.15.2.
