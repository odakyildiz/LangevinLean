Concentration around a minimizer without differentiability

References and publication status for this development

Paper references

The two step-size regimes and the coefficient 1.65 follow Theorem 1(a)–(b) of Dalalyan and Karagulyan (2019). Dalalyan (2017), Theorem 1 and Section 6, gives the earlier synchronous-coupling argument.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv
  • Arnak Dalalyan (2017). Further and stronger analogy between sampling and optimization: Langevin Monte Carlo and gradient descent. Proceedings of the 2017 Conference on Learning Theory, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 65, 678–689.

For a continuous qqq-strongly convex potential FFF, q>0q>0q>0, and an explicitly supplied minimizer zzz, this module proves the equilibrium radius ∫∥x−z∥2dπF≤2d/q\int\|x-z\|^2d\pi_F\le2d/q∫∥x−z∥2dπF​≤2d/q. The proof uses dilation and convexity, so it also applies to the nonsmooth potentials used by proximal algorithms.

03integrable sq dist mul exp neg of continuous strongConvex mode §

Mathematical statement and proof

Multiply the translated Gaussian bound by ∥x−z∥2\|x-z\|^2∥x−z∥2. Translation preserves Lebesgue measure, and a quadratic times a decaying Gaussian is integrable. Lean transports that integrability through x↦x−zx\mapsto x-zx↦x−z and then applies the nonnegative domination theorem to the desired weighted moment.
