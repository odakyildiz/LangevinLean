The Langevin update and cancellation of shared noise

We now work in finite-dimensional Euclidean space. A ULA step adds the same scaled noise vector to a gradient step. When two starting points use this same noise, it cancels exactly from their difference. The deterministic estimates can therefore be applied pointwise, before introducing any probability law.

01The Euclidean state space §

02One unadjusted Langevin step §

Mathematical statement and proof

For a potential U:Ed→RU:E_d\to\mathbb RU:Ed​→R, a real step size hhh, a state x∈Edx\in E_dx∈Ed​, and a noise vector z∈Edz\in E_dz∈Ed​, define

The update consists of the current state, the drift increment −h∇U(x)-h\nabla U(x)−h∇U(x), and the noise increment 2h z\sqrt{2h}\,z2h​z.

03Shared noise cancels §

Mathematical statement and proof

For every potential U:Ed→RU:E_d\to\mathbb RU:Ed​→R, h∈Rh\in\mathbb Rh∈R, and vectors x,y,z∈Edx,y,z\in E_dx,y,z∈Ed​,

Thz(x)−Thz(y)=(x−h∇U(x))−(y−h∇U(y)).T_h^z(x)-T_h^z(y)=(x-h\nabla U(x))-(y-h\nabla U(y)).Thz​(x)−Thz​(y)=(x−h∇U(x))−(y−h∇U(y)).

Expand the two updates. The two copies of 2h z\sqrt{2h}\,z2h​z subtract to zero:

(x−h∇U(x)+2h z)−(y−h∇U(y)+2h z)=(x−h∇U(x))−(y−h∇U(y)).(x-h\nabla U(x)+\sqrt{2h}\,z)-(y-h\nabla U(y)+\sqrt{2h}\,z)=(x-h\nabla U(x))-(y-h\nabla U(y)).(x−h∇U(x)+2h​z)−(y−h∇U(y)+2h​z)=(x−h∇U(x))−(y−h∇U(y)).

04The initial shared-noise estimate §

Mathematical statement and proof

Suppose U:Ed→RU:E_d\to\mathbb RU:Ed​→R is differentiable and mmm-strongly convex, ∇U\nabla U∇U is LLL-Lipschitz with L≥0L\ge0L≥0, and h≥0h\ge0h≥0. For every x,y,z∈Edx,y,z\in E_dx,y,z∈Ed​,

∥Thz(x)−Thz(y)∥≤1−2hm+h2L2 ∥x−y∥.\|T_h^z(x)-T_h^z(y)\|\le\sqrt{1-2hm+h^2L^2}\,\|x-y\|.∥Thz​(x)−Thz​(y)∥≤1−2hm+h2L2​∥x−y∥.

For h>0h>0h>0 this factor is strictly below one whenever hL2<2mhL^2<2mhL2<2m.

Cancel the common noise. The left-hand side becomes the distance between the two deterministic gradient steps.

Strong convexity gives

m∥x−y∥2≤⟨∇U(x)−∇U(y),x−y⟩,m\|x-y\|^2\le\langle\nabla U(x)-\nabla U(y),x-y\rangle,m∥x−y∥2≤⟨∇U(x)−∇U(y),x−y⟩,

and the Lipschitz assumption gives ∥∇U(x)−∇U(y)∥≤L∥x−y∥\|\nabla U(x)-\nabla U(y)\|\le L\|x-y\|∥∇U(x)−∇U(y)∥≤L∥x−y∥. Insert these two inequalities into the Euler-step square-root bound.
