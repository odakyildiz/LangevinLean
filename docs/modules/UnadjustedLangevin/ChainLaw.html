A noise-driven recursion has a law independent of its realization

For measurable F(x,z)F(x,z)F(x,z), the recursion Xn+1=F(Xn,Zn)X_{n+1}=F(X_n,Z_n)Xn+1​=F(Xn​,Zn​) has a law determined by the initial law and the full noise law when the initialization and noise are independent.

01Define the recursion and its canonical law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Set Φ0(x,w)=x\Phi_0(x,w)=xΦ0​(x,w)=x, Φn+1(x,w)=F(Φn(x,w),wn)\Phi_{n+1}(x,w)=F(\Phi_n(x,w),w_n)Φn+1​(x,w)=F(Φn​(x,w),wn​). Induction proves measurability: the next step composes measurable FFF with the previous state and coordinate evaluation. Define chainLaw⁡n=Φn#(μ⊗Q)\operatorname{chainLaw}_n=\Phi_{n\#}(\mu\otimes Q)chainLawn​=Φn#​(μ⊗Q). For probability noise law QQQ, the time-zero law is μ\muμ.

02Use independence to identify any realization §

Mathematical statement and proof

If X0X_0X0​ is independent of the noise path WWW, then Law⁡(X0,W)=Law⁡(X0)⊗Law⁡(W)\operatorname{Law}(X_0,W)=\operatorname{Law}(X_0)\otimes\operatorname{Law}(W)Law(X0​,W)=Law(X0​)⊗Law(W). Push this equality through Φn\Phi_nΦn​. For iid noise with marginal η\etaη, the path law is η⊗N\eta^{\otimes\mathbb N}η⊗N. Hence two realizations with equal initial law, equal noise marginals, iid noise and the stated initial independence have identical output laws at every finite time.

03Specialize to pointwise and L² ULA §

Mathematical statement and proof

With F(x,z)=x−h∇U(x)+2hzF(x,z)=x-h\nabla U(x)+\sqrt{2h}zF(x,z)=x−h∇U(x)+2h​z, induction identifies the generic recursion with ulaChain. Gradient Lipschitzness gives measurability of FFF. The generic iid law theorem therefore applies to ULA; its L2L^2L2 version first uses the almost-everywhere identity between the lifted chain and the pointwise recursion, which leaves pushforward laws unchanged.
