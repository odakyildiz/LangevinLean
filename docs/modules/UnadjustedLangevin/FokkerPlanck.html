The weak Fokker–Planck equation is formulated by integrating Lφ\mathcal L\varphiLφ against time-dependent probability measures. The final theorem in this module assumes uniqueness of this weak equation explicitly; the main ULA development proves stationarity by the separate Metropolis approximation route.

01IsWeakFokkerPlanckSolution §

Mathematical statement and proof

A curve ρt\rho_tρt​ is in this weak solution class when it is probability valued, has the stated moment and integrability properties, and satisfies the integrated generator equation against every compactly supported C2C^2C2 test. The structure fields keep the analytic conditions visible, preventing a formal real integral from substituting for an unproved integrable quantity.

02constant gibbs isWeakFokkerPlanckSolution §

Mathematical statement and proof

Use the proved Gibbs normalization and second moment for the probability and moment fields. Compact-support generator integrability supplies the test-integral fields. Finally ∫Lφ dπU=0\int L\varphi\,d\pi_U=0∫LφdπU​=0 makes the time integral vanish, while the constant curve has zero change in ∫φ dρt\int\varphi\,d\rho_t∫φdρt​. These verify each field of the weak equation for the stationary candidate.

03WeakFokkerPlanckUnique §

Mathematical statement and proof

This proposition quantifies over two curves in the stated weak solution class with the same initial measure and requires their equality throughout [0,T][0,T][0,T]. It is a named analytic uniqueness obligation. Defining the proposition does not prove it, and no axiom or automatic instance makes it available.

04gibbs stationary of weakFokkerPlanckUniqueness §

Mathematical statement and proof

Compare the supplied weak solution ρ\rhoρ with the constant Gibbs weak solution constructed above. Their time-zero measures agree by hypothesis. The explicit hunique premise therefore identifies them at every time. Applying this conditional bridge to a stochastic process still requires its law curve to satisfy the weak equation and requires this uniqueness premise.
