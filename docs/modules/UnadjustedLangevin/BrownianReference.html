Assemble continuous Brownian reference segments

Construct consecutive L2L^2L2 Langevin segments from actual Brownian-coordinate noise and package them as an analytic reference. At this intermediate stage Gibbs stationarity remains an explicit premise, later discharged in CanonicalULA.

01Choose segments and link their endpoints §

Mathematical statement and proof

For the nnn-th interval, choose the continuous integral solution driven by NnhN_{nh}Nnh​ from a supplied start. Its specification records continuity, the initial value, the integral equation and the exact noise norm. Recursively define knots K0=XK_0=XK0​=X, Kn+1=Sn(h)K_{n+1}=S_n(h)Kn+1​=Sn​(h), with SnS_nSn​ starting from KnK_nKn​. Then Sn(0)=KnS_n(0)=K_nSn​(0)=Kn​, and S0(0)=XS_0(0)=XS0​(0)=X; no gap is introduced at consecutive endpoints.

02Match the discrete innovation and package the reference §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define Zn=Nnh(h)/2hZ_n=N_{nh}(h)/\sqrt{2h}Zn​=Nnh​(h)/2h​. For h>0h>0h>0, multiplication by 2h\sqrt{2h}2h​ recovers the exact noise increment. A reference package uses the constructed segments and increments, the supplied stationarity identities Law⁡(Sn(t))=π\operatorname{Law}(S_n(t))=\piLaw(Sn​(t))=π, the Gibbs score bound ∥∇U(Sn(t))∥2≤Ld\|\nabla U(S_n(t))\|_2\le\sqrt{Ld}∥∇U(Sn​(t))∥2​≤Ld​, and the noise bound ∥Nnh(t)∥2=2dt\|N_{nh}(t)\|_2=\sqrt{2d}\sqrt t∥Nnh​(t)∥2​=2d​t​. The strong-convexity wrapper additionally derives finite Gibbs normalization.
