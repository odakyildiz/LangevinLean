Assemble continuous Brownian reference segments

Construct consecutive L2L^2L2 Langevin segments from actual Brownian-coordinate noise and package them as an analytic reference. At this intermediate stage Gibbs stationarity remains an explicit premise, later discharged in CanonicalULA.

03brownianKnots §

Mathematical statement and proof

Set the zeroth knot to the prescribed initial variable. Define the next knot to be the endpoint at local time hhh of the segment started at the current knot. This recursion ensures consecutive segments meet exactly as L2L^2L2 classes.

09brownianReference §

Mathematical statement and proof

Populate L2Reference with the constructed segments. Their specification proves continuity and the integral equation; recursive knots prove endpoint compatibility; normalized noise proves endpoint matching; Gibbs-score moment transfer proves the drift bound. The supplied all-time Gibbs-law premise remains visible and is later discharged by the stationarity construction.
