Construct an isonormal Gaussian family from product coordinates

An explicit product of standard normal coordinates realizes a linear isometry I:H→L2I:H\to L^2I:H→L2 with I(x)∼N(0,∥x∥2)I(x)\sim\mathcal N(0,\|x\|^2)I(x)∼N(0,∥x∥2) and covariance E[I(x)I(y)]=⟨x,y⟩\mathbb E[I(x)I(y)]=\langle x,y\rangleE[I(x)I(y)]=⟨x,y⟩.
