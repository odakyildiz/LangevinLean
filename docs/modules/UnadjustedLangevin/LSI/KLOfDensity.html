Identify density entropy and Fisher integrals

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound follows Theorem 2, Section 3.1, and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019). The Lean development supplies the regularity and endpoint arguments for the actual Gaussian ULA laws.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

For a strictly positive relative density, with the stated differentiability and integrability, the actual likelihood ratio equals log⁡r\log rlogr. This identifies the formal KL divergence with ∫rlog⁡r dν\int r\log r\,d\nu∫rlogrdν, proves its finiteness and identifies the real Fisher value with its score-energy integral.

01llr tiltedMeasure ae eq log §

Mathematical statement and proof

The with-density measure is absolutely continuous with respect to ν\nuν, and its Radon–Nikodym derivative equals ofReal r ν\nuν-almost everywhere. Absolute continuity transfers that equality to ρr\rho_rρr​-almost everywhere. Taking the real logarithm gives the actual log-likelihood ratio log⁡r\log rlogr.

02klDiv tiltedMeasure ne top §

Mathematical statement and proof

Absolute continuity follows from the with-density construction. Integrability of rlog⁡rr\log rrlogr gives integrability of log⁡r\log rlogr under ρr\rho_rρr​, and the log-likelihood identity identifies that with the KL integrand. The standard finite-KL criterion therefore proves KL⁡(ρr∣ν)<∞\operatorname{KL}(\rho_r\mid\nu)<\inftyKL(ρr​∣ν)<∞.

03densityEntropy eq integral §

Mathematical statement and proof

For normalized ν\nuν and ρr\rho_rρr​, the finite-KL integral formula has mass correction ν(E)−ρr(E)=0\nu(E)-\rho_r(E)=0ν(E)−ρr​(E)=0. Replace the log-likelihood ratio by log⁡r\log rlogr, then change density to obtain Hν(r)=∫rlog⁡r dνH_\nu(r)=\int r\log r\,d\nuHν​(r)=∫rlogrdν. The integrability premise is used before invoking that formula.

04relativeFisher toReal eq integral §

Mathematical statement and proof

Nonnegativity and integrability identify the real value of the extended Fisher integral with the ordinary integral under ρr\rho_rρr​. The weighted-measure formula then rewrites it as Iν(r)R=∫r∥∇log⁡r∥2dνI_\nu(r)_{\mathbb R}=\int r\|\nabla\log r\|^2d\nuIν​(r)R​=∫r∥∇logr∥2dν. Both representations refer to the same supplied density.

05integral tiltedMeasure §

Mathematical statement and proof

For measurable r≥0r\ge0r≥0, the Bochner with-density formula gives ∫g dρr=∫r(x)g(x)dν\int g\,d\rho_r=\int r(x)g(x)d\nu∫gdρr​=∫r(x)g(x)dν. The embedded density is finite pointwise and has real value rrr, so the theorem's extended-to-real scalar action simplifies to ordinary multiplication.

06integrable log tiltedMeasure iff §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply the with-density integrability criterion to g=log⁡rg=\log rg=logr. Since the density is measurable, finite, and nonnegative, it yields log⁡r∈L1(ρr)  ⟺  rlog⁡r∈L1(ν)\log r\in L^1(\rho_r)\iff r\log r\in L^1(\nu)logr∈L1(ρr​)⟺rlogr∈L1(ν). This is the exact entropy-integrability bridge used before converting KL to real values.

07relativeFisher ne top §

Mathematical statement and proof

The squared score is nonnegative. Its ordinary integrability implies finiteness of its extended nonnegative integral, by the hasFiniteIntegral_iff_ofReal criterion. That extended integral is exactly relativeFisher.
