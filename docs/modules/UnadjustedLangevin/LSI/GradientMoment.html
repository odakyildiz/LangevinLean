A gradient moment bound from a coupling

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound is Theorem 2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019); its proof is in Section 3.1 and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2. The proof here establishes Gaussian density regularity, entropy differentiation at positive times, and the limit as elapsed time decreases to zero.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

Let (X,Y)(X,Y)(X,Y) couple μ\muμ and ν\nuν, with finite squared distance, and suppose the gradient is LLL-Lipschitz. The inequality ∥∇U(X)∥≤L∥X−Y∥+∥∇U(Y)∥\|\nabla U(X)\|\le L\|X-Y\|+\|\nabla U(Y)\|∥∇U(X)∥≤L∥X−Y∥+∥∇U(Y)∥ gives a second-moment bound under μ\muμ when that moment is finite under ν\nuν. For a normalized Gibbs target, the latter is at most nLnLnL. An explicitly assumed entropy bound on the coupling cost then gives the stated entropy-dependent gradient estimate. The final ULA entropy theorem instead obtains its gradient moment directly from exponential integrability of the Gibbs score.

01Integrability and the coupling estimate §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let ccc have marginals μ,ν\mu,\nuμ,ν, and assume ∫∥x−y∥2dc<∞\int\|x-y\|^2dc<\infty∫∥x−y∥2dc<∞ and ∫∥∇U∥2dν<∞\int\|\nabla U\|^2d\nu<\infty∫∥∇U∥2dν<∞. Lipschitzness and (a+b)2≤2a2+2b2(a+b)^2\le2a^2+2b^2(a+b)2≤2a2+2b2 give the pointwise inequality

∥∇U(x)∥2≤2L2∥x−y∥2+2∥∇U(y)∥2.\|\nabla U(x)\|^2\le2L^2\|x-y\|^2+2\|\nabla U(y)\|^2.∥∇U(x)∥2≤2L2∥x−y∥2+2∥∇U(y)∥2.

The right-hand side is integrable under ccc. Measurability and domination imply integrability of the first-coordinate gradient square, hence of ∥∇U∥2\|\nabla U\|^2∥∇U∥2 under μ\muμ. Integration and the marginal identities give

∫∥∇U∥2dμ≤2L2∫∥x−y∥2dc(x,y)+2∫∥∇U∥2dν.\int\|\nabla U\|^2d\mu\le2L^2\int\|x-y\|^2dc(x,y)+2\int\|\nabla U\|^2d\nu.∫∥∇U∥2dμ≤2L2∫∥x−y∥2dc(x,y)+2∫∥∇U∥2dν.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.1.

02The estimate for a Gibbs target §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let ν=Z−1e−Udx\nu=Z^{-1}e^{-U}dxν=Z−1e−Udx be a Gibbs probability measure, with differentiable UUU and LLL-Lipschitz gradient. Probability normalization supplies a finite positive partition function. The Gibbs score estimate proves square-integrability and ∫∥∇U∥2dν≤nL\int\|\nabla U\|^2d\nu\le nL∫∥∇U∥2dν≤nL, without strong convexity. Substituting these facts in the coupling estimate gives

∫∥∇U∥2dμ≤2L2∫∥x−y∥2dc+2nL.\int\|\nabla U\|^2d\mu\le2L^2\int\|x-y\|^2dc+2nL.∫∥∇U∥2dμ≤2L2∫∥x−y∥2dc+2nL.

The same argument proves gradient square-integrability under μ\muμ; it is not assumed before this conclusion.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.27.6; ULA-SC, Section 3.27.7.

03Substitution of an entropy bound on the coupling cost §

Mathematical statement and proof

Suppose the given finite-cost coupling also satisfies

∫∥x−y∥2dc(x,y)≤2αKL⁡(μ∣ν),α>0,KL⁡(μ∣ν)<∞.\int\|x-y\|^2dc(x,y)\le\frac2\alpha\operatorname{KL}(\mu\mid\nu),\qquad \alpha>0,\quad \operatorname{KL}(\mu\mid\nu)<\infty.∫∥x−y∥2dc(x,y)≤α2​KL(μ∣ν),α>0,KL(μ∣ν)<∞.

Multiply this inequality by 2L2≥02L^2\ge02L2≥0 and substitute it in the preceding gradient estimate. Since 2L2(2/α)=4L2/α2L^2(2/\alpha)=4L^2/\alpha2L2(2/α)=4L2/α,

∫∥∇U∥2dμ≤4L2αKL⁡(μ∣ν)+2nL.\int\|\nabla U\|^2d\mu\le\frac{4L^2}{\alpha}\operatorname{KL}(\mu\mid\nu)+2nL.∫∥∇U∥2dμ≤α4L2​KL(μ∣ν)+2nL.

The cost-to-entropy estimate is an explicit hypothesis for the supplied coupling. This result does not prove a transport inequality from LSI.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.
