Construct spatial smoothness, finite Fisher information and finite actual entropy

For t>0t>0t>0, the actual frozen Gaussian update Yt=X−t∇U(X)+2t ZY_t=X-t\nabla U(X)+\sqrt{2t}\,ZYt​=X−t∇U(X)+2t​Z has the positive mixture density constructed in InterpolationDensity.lean. This module proves its spatial C1C^1C1 regularity by differentiating the displayed Gaussian integral. Bayes' posterior formula and Jensen's inequality then give the universal Lebesgue Fisher bound ∫∥∇log⁡pt∥2pt≤d/(2t)\int\|\nabla\log p_t\|^2p_t\le d/(2t)∫∥∇logpt​∥2pt​≤d/(2t). The target LSI consequently proves finite actual KL at every positive time. No initial density or second moment of the state is assumed.
