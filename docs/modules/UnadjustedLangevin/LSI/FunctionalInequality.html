01tiltedMeasure §

Mathematical statement and proof

For a real relative density rrr, define ρr(dx)=r(x)ν(dx)\rho_r(dx)=r(x)\nu(dx)ρr​(dx)=r(x)ν(dx) using withDensity (ENNReal.ofReal ∘ r). Positivity and normalization are later hypotheses, rather than part of this raw measure definition. A normalized positive rrr is the density of the current law relative to the target.

04Why finite KL and Fisher information are separate obligations §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply the extended LSI to the supplied positive C1C^1C1 probability density. Both KL and Fisher are assumed finite, so ENNReal.toReal_le_toReal preserves the inequality. Nonnegativity of 2α2\alpha2α identifies the real value of its embedding, giving 2αHν(r)≤Iν(r)R2\alpha H_\nu(r)\le I_\nu(r)_{\mathbb R}2αHν​(r)≤Iν​(r)R​.
