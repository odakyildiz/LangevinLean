Fisher dissipation and the frozen-gradient contribution to entropy production

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound is Theorem 2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019); its proof is in Section 3.1 and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2. The proof here establishes Gaussian density regularity, entropy differentiation at positive times, and the limit as elapsed time decreases to zero.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

The entropy chain rule gives a locally absolutely continuous entropy whose derivative is the Gaussian flux almost everywhere. The component-score averaging identity and the relative-score decomposition rewrite this flux as

Qt=−It+E⟨∇U(Yt)−∇U(X),∇log⁡rt(Yt)⟩.Q_t=-I_t+\mathbb E\langle\nabla U(Y_t)-\nabla U(X),\nabla\log r_t(Y_t)\rangle.Qt​=−It​+E⟨∇U(Yt​)−∇U(X),∇logrt​(Yt​)⟩.

All pairings are integrable by the square-energy estimates on the independent initial/noise space. Young's inequality bounds the error pairing by It/4I_t/4It​/4 plus its squared-gradient discrepancy. The latter is at most L2(t2G0+2td)L^2(t^2G_0+2td)L2(t2G0​+2td). Thus Ht′≤−3It/4+t2L2G0+2tdL2H'_t\le-3I_t/4+t^2L^2G_0+2tdL^2Ht′​≤−3It​/4+t2L2G0​+2tdL2 almost everywhere at positive times. A countable family of positive compact intervals gives one full-measure time set on which the derivative conclusion holds, without a derivative assertion at zero.

01Justify inner-product integrability from two L2L^2L2 fields §

Mathematical statement and proof

For vector fields F,G∈L2(P)F,G\in L^2(P)F,G∈L2(P), Hölder's inequality puts ∥F∥∥G∥\|F\|\|G\|∥F∥∥G∥ in L1(P)L^1(P)L1(P). Since ∣⟨F,G⟩∣≤∥F∥∥G∥|\langle F,G\rangle|\le\|F\|\|G\|∣⟨F,G⟩∣≤∥F∥∥G∥, their inner product is integrable.

The initial gradient, current gradient, Gaussian component score, and relative score all have the square energies proved above. Each needed pairing is therefore integrable by Cauchy–Schwarz. Their signed integrals may consequently be split and recombined in the entropy-flux algebra.

Cauchy–Schwarz proves absolute integrability of the pairing on the common independent-noise probability. This permits the exact entropy flux to be split into its Fisher and discrepancy contributions as ordinary integrals.

03Match the measurable state-space score field to the same finite Fisher value §

Mathematical statement and proof

∫∥St(y)∥2 dρt(y)=It.\int\|S_t(y)\|^2\,d\rho_t(y)=I_t.∫∥St​(y)∥2dρt​(y)=It​.

The raw entropy flux is −E⟨g(X)−2tZ/(2t),St⟩-\mathbb E\langle g(X)-\sqrt{2t}Z/(2t),S_t\rangle−E⟨g(X)−2t​Z/(2t),St​⟩. The averaged Gaussian score identity gives

E⟨−2t2tZ,St⟩=∫⟨∇log⁡pt,∇log⁡rt⟩ dρt.\mathbb E\left\langle-\frac{\sqrt{2t}}{2t}Z,S_t\right\rangle=\int\langle\nabla\log p_t,\nabla\log r_t\rangle\,d\rho_t.E⟨−2t2t​​Z,St​⟩=∫⟨∇logpt​,∇logrt​⟩dρt​.

Qt=−It+E⟨∇U(Yt)−∇U(X),St⟩.Q_t=-I_t+\mathbb E\langle\nabla U(Y_t)-\nabla U(X),S_t\rangle.Qt​=−It​+E⟨∇U(Yt​)−∇U(X),St​⟩.

The pulled-back initial gradient, current gradient, Gaussian component score, and relative score are all square integrable on the common probability space. Thus every inner product used in these sums and differences is integrable. Pushforward change of variables identifies the output-space pairings with these expectations. 06The entropy flux dissipation bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

Qt=−It+E⟨Dt,St⟩≤−34It+t2L2G0+2tdL2.Q_t=-I_t+\mathbb E\langle D_t,S_t\rangle\le-\frac34I_t+t^2L^2G_0+2tdL^2.Qt​=−It​+E⟨Dt​,St​⟩≤−43​It​+t2L2G0​+2tdL2.

The proof rewrites the flux with the decomposition of entropy flux into Fisher dissipation and the drift-discrepancy pairing, obtains the already justified numerical cross-term bound, and collects the scalar terms. The target's probability normalization and finite initial gradient energy suffice for this pointwise flux estimate. LSI is needed later to relate Fisher information to entropy, rather than to justify Young absorption.

Subtracting the absorbed quarter from the full negative Fisher term gives −3It/4-3I_t/4−3It​/4. The remaining discrepancy energy is at most t2L2G0+2tdL2t^2L^2G_0+2tdL^2t2L2G0​+2tdL2, giving the desired scalar forcing.
