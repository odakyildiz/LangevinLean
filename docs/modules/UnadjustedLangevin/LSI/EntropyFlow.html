Relative entropy along an identified density path

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound follows Theorem 2, Section 3.1, and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019). The Lean development supplies the regularity and endpoint arguments for the actual Gaussian ULA laws.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

Density entropy is the real conversion of the actual KL divergence of the tilted measure. With finite-entropy guards, LSI and a supplied dissipation inequality give exponential entropy decay along this path.

For an admissible density path, the exact entropy identity H′=−IH'=-IH′=−I and the inequality I≥2αHI\ge2\alpha HI≥2αH imply H′≤−2αHH'\le-2\alpha HH′≤−2αH. Multiplication by e2αte^{2\alpha t}e2αt then shows that the weighted entropy is nonincreasing. This continuous-flow calculation explains the role of LSI. A frozen-drift numerical interpolation has an additional error pairing, so its later decay rate and bias require a separate estimate rather than this unforced identity alone.

Statement and proof

01Define actual density entropy with its finiteness guard §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define the real entropy of a specified relative density as Hν(r)=KL⁡(rν∣ν)H_\nu(r)=\operatorname{KL}(r\nu\mid\nu)Hν​(r)=KL(rν∣ν). It uses the same the probability law specified by a relative density and actual relative entropy as the LSI definition. Applications must separately establish finite KL before using it as an ordinary entropy value.

For a normalized density, the familiar entropy integral agrees with KL only after the needed integrability is established. The present definition retains the actual divergence as its starting point. Consequently later derivative or comparison statements must first identify a finite entropy integral with this quantity, rather than differentiate an arbitrary real-converted extended value.

02Apply the finite real LSI at every time §

Mathematical statement and proof

At each interpolation time, positivity, C1C^1C1 regularity, normalization, and finite KL/Fisher allow the inequality 2αH≤I2\alpha H\le I2αH≤I to give 2αH(t)≤I(t)2\alpha H(t)\le I(t)2αH(t)≤I(t). Combine this with the explicit right-dissipation identity and entropy continuity in the integrating-factor comparison for H′≤−2αHH\prime\le-2\alpha HH′≤−2αH. The conclusion is decay at rate 2α2\alpha2α for that admissible density path; identifying an actual evolution with the path remains an application obligation.

At each time LSI is applied to the same density defining the law of the path. Together with the supplied dissipation identity it gives H′+2αH≤0H'+2\alpha H\le0H′+2αH≤0. Integration of (e2αtH(t))′≤0(e^{2\alpha t}H(t))'\le0(e2αtH(t))′≤0 yields the displayed decay. The argument proves a scalar consequence for the admissible path; establishing that a particular stochastic evolution has this path is a separate law-identification step.
