Entropy decay from a dissipation identity

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound is Theorem 2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019); its proof is in Section 3.1 and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2. The proof here establishes Gaussian density regularity, entropy differentiation at positive times, and the limit as elapsed time decreases to zero.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

Let rtνr_t\nurt​ν be a specified path of probability measures. Suppose the densities are strictly positive and C1C^1C1, entropy and Fisher information are finite, entropy is continuous in time, and its right derivative equals minus the Fisher information. If ν\nuν satisfies LSI with constant α>0\alpha>0α>0, then H(t)≤e−2αtH(0)H(t)\le e^{-2\alpha t}H(0)H(t)≤e−2αtH(0). The proof concerns any path satisfying these assumptions. A ULA interpolation has an additional drift-error term and requires a separate estimate.

Mathematical statement and proof

For ρr=rν\rho_r=r\nuρr​=rν, write Hν(r)=KL⁡(ρr∣ν)H_\nu(r)=\operatorname{KL}(\rho_r\mid\nu)Hν​(r)=KL(ρr​∣ν) whenever the divergence is finite. This notation refers to the divergence of the two measures, not to an independent error function. Under the appropriate integrability assumption it equals ∫rlog⁡r dν\int r\log r\,d\nu∫rlogrdν. That equality must be established before differentiating the integral. In particular, differentiation of an entropy integral requires both identification with the divergence and control of its integrand.

02Exponential entropy decay §

Mathematical statement and proof

For each t∈[0,T]t\in[0,T]t∈[0,T], apply the finite LSI to the density rtr_trt​. It gives 2αH(t)≤I(t)2\alpha H(t)\le I(t)2αH(t)≤I(t). The assumed right-dissipation identity therefore implies H′(t)≤−2αH(t)H'(t)\le-2\alpha H(t)H′(t)≤−2αH(t) for t<Tt<Tt<T. The integrating factor gives

(e2αtH(t))′≤0.(e^{2\alpha t}H(t))'\le0.(e2αtH(t))′≤0.

The right-derivative comparison, together with continuity of HHH on the closed interval, shows that the weighted entropy is nonincreasing. Evaluating at zero yields H(t)≤e−2αtH(0)H(t)\le e^{-2\alpha t}H(0)H(t)≤e−2αtH(0). Positivity, differentiability and normalization of the densities are used at the LSI step; the time regularity is used in the scalar comparison.
