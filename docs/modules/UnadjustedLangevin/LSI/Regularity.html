Local regularity needed for actual entropy calculus

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound follows Theorem 2, Section 3.1, and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019). The Lean development supplies the regularity and endpoint arguments for the actual Gaussian ULA laws.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

A LocalEntropyRegularity package supplies positive densities, time derivatives, and local integrable domination. Its consequences prove finite KL, continuity, derivatives and a production inequality for the identified density entropy; existence of these packages is retained as a premise.

01LocalEntropyRegularity §

Mathematical statement and proof

At a fixed time ttt, store a common time neighborhood, an integrable spatial envelope for r˙u(log⁡ru+1)\dot r_u(\log r_u+1)r˙u​(logru​+1), local pointwise positivity/differentiability, and measurability/integrability of the entropy integrands. These are sufficient data for dominated differentiation. The structure contains no derivative of the entropy integral and no entropy-production estimate.

05densityEntropy continuousOn of regular §

Mathematical statement and proof

At every point of the closed interval, dominated differentiation makes the ordinary entropy integral differentiable and hence continuous. Normalization and the finite-entropy density formula identify that integral with actual KL entropy at every interval point. ContinuousOn.congr transfers continuity to t↦KL⁡(ρt∣ν)Rt\mapsto\operatorname{KL}(\rho_t\mid\nu)_{\mathbb R}t↦KL(ρt​∣ν)R​.

07Express the production bound using relative Fisher information §

Mathematical statement and proof
