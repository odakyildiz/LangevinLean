Local regularity needed for actual entropy calculus

A LocalEntropyRegularity package supplies positive densities, time derivatives, and local integrable domination. Its consequences prove finite KL, continuity, derivatives and a production inequality for the identified density entropy; existence of these packages is retained as a premise.

03Transfer finiteness, continuity and the right derivative to actual KL §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume ν\nuν is a probability and each density on [0,T][0,T][0,T] is positive and C1C^1C1. Local entropy regularity supplies integrability of rtlog⁡rtr_t\log r_trt​logrt​, which proves finite actual KL of rtνr_t\nurt​ν. 04Express the production bound using relative Fisher information §

Mathematical statement and proof

Under the same normalized regular path assumptions, suppose the squared score is integrable against rtνr_t\nurt​ν and the forced weak-equation conditions hold. The with-density integrability equivalence converts score integrability to integrability of rt∥∇log⁡rt∥2r_t\|\nabla\log r_t\|^2rt​∥∇logrt​∥2 against ν\nuν. The Fisher formula identifies this integral with the finite real value J(t)J(t)J(t). Combine the preceding right derivative and the integral production bound to get

H′+(t)≤−34J(t)+E(t),E(t)=∫rt∥et∥2dν.H\prime_+(t)\le-\tfrac34J(t)+\mathcal E(t),\qquad\mathcal E(t)=\int r_t\|e_t\|^2d\nu.H′+​(t)≤−43​J(t)+E(t),E(t)=∫rt​∥et​∥2dν.

This module derives entropy calculus. A quantitative bound for E\mathcal EE, and construction of these regular density data for the actual Gaussian interpolation, remain separate requirements.
