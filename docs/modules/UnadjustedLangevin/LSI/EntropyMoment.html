Finite entropy supplies the gradient moment directly

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound follows Theorem 2, Section 3.1, and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019). The Lean development supplies the regularity and endpoint arguments for the actual Gaussian ULA laws.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

A finite initial KL divergence already controls the squared Gibbs score when ∇U\nabla U∇U is Lipschitz. Gaussian averaging of the directional score MGF gives Eνe∥∇U∥2/(4L)≤ed/2\mathbb E_\nu e^{\|\nabla U\|^2/(4L)}\le e^{d/2}Eν​e∥∇U∥2/(4L)≤ed/2. The entropy inequality then proves both integrability and Gμ≤4LH(μ∣ν)+2dLG_\mu\le4L H(\mu\mid\nu)+2dLGμ​≤4LH(μ∣ν)+2dL. This direct argument closes the moment input to ULA without assuming an entropy-cost coupling or a transport inequality.

02integrable of finite kl of integrable exp §

Mathematical statement and proof

Write a=dμ/dνa=d\mu/d\nua=dμ/dν. Finite KL supplies absolute continuity μ≪ν\mu\ll\nuμ≪ν and integrability of the actual log likelihood; equivalently Φ(a)\Phi(a)Φ(a) is integrable under ν\nuν. For a measurable nonnegative observable fff, the Young inequality gives 0≤af≤Φ(a)+ef0\le af\le\Phi(a)+e^f0≤af≤Φ(a)+ef. Both terms on the right are integrable by the finite-KL and exponential-moment hypotheses.

Domination proves af∈L1(ν)af\in L^1(\nu)af∈L1(ν). The Radon-Nikodym weighted-integrability equivalence then gives f∈L1(μ)f\in L^1(\mu)f∈L1(μ). In particular, the moment conclusion is derived before any ordinary expectation is manipulated; it is not an extra assumption hidden in the entropy inequality.

08gibbs score exponential moment §

Mathematical statement and proof

Convert the nonnegative integral back to its real expectation. Integrability is inherited from the stronger direct theorem. Compatibility α≤L\alpha\le Lα≤L is proved from LSI in a separate module.
