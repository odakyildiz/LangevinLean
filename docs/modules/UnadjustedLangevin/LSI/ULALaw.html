Actual Gaussian ULA laws and their frozen-drift interpolation

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound follows Theorem 2, Section 3.1, and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019). The Lean development supplies the regularity and endpoint arguments for the actual Gaussian ULA laws.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

Define the frozen-drift law ρk,t=Law⁡(Xk−t∇U(Xk)+2tZ)\rho_{k,t}=\operatorname{Law}(X_k-t\nabla U(X_k)+\sqrt{2t}Z)ρk,t​=Law(Xk​−t∇U(Xk​)+2t​Z), with independent standard Gaussian ZZZ, and ρk+1=ρk,h\rho_{k+1}=\rho_{k,h}ρk+1​=ρk,h​. The endpoint and probability identities connect the entropy argument to the actual algorithm.

01ulaEntropy §

Mathematical statement and proof

Set Hk=KL⁡(ρk∣ν)RH_k=\operatorname{KL}(\rho_k\mid\nu)_{\mathbb R}Hk​=KL(ρk​∣ν)R​. The definition uses the actual extended KL of the ULA law, followed by toReal. Separate theorems prove finite KL wherever this is used as ordinary real entropy; the definition alone would not exclude infinity.

03interpolationLaw §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define ρt=(Tt)∗(μ⊗γ)\rho_t=(T_t)_*(\mu\otimes\gamma)ρt​=(Tt​)∗​(μ⊗γ), where Tt(x,z)=x−t∇U(x)+2tzT_t(x,z)=x-t\nabla U(x)+\sqrt{2t}zTt​(x,z)=x−t∇U(x)+2t​z. The product encodes independence. This is an alias of the actual ulaLawStep; its frozen-drift Gaussian interpolation interpretation applies to t≥0t\ge0t≥0.

04ulaLaws §

Mathematical statement and proof

Iterate the actual independent-Gaussian transition at fixed step hhh: ρk=Phkμ\rho_k=P_h^k\muρk​=Phk​μ. The definition is on measures, while the separate chain-law theorems identify independent iid implementations with these same laws.

05interpolationLaw endpoint §

Mathematical statement and proof

At elapsed time hhh, the interpolation from ρk\rho_kρk​ is PhρkP_h\rho_kPh​ρk​. The preceding successor identity therefore proves ρk,h=ρk+1\rho_{k,h}=\rho_{k+1}ρk,h​=ρk+1​. This is the law equality used to turn a continuous interpolation bound into the discrete entropy step.

06ulaLaws isProbability §

Mathematical statement and proof

Induct from the initial probability μ\muμ. At the successor rewrite the iterate as one interpolation step at time hhh, then apply probability preservation. Every algorithm law is therefore a probability before applying KL and LSI identities.

07interpolationLaw zero §

Mathematical statement and proof

10interpolationLaw isProbability §

Mathematical statement and proof

Lipschitzness makes the gradient continuous and the joint update measurable. The product μ⊗γ\mu\otimes\gammaμ⊗γ is a probability, and its pushforward has the same total mass. Evaluating the mapped measure on the whole space proves normalization of ρt\rho_tρt​.
