The KL bound for ULA under explicit interpolation hypotheses

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound is Theorem 2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019); its proof is in Section 3.1 and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2. The proof here establishes Gaussian density regularity, entropy differentiation at positive times, and the limit as elapsed time decreases to zero.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices
