Conditional LSI theorem for the actual ULA laws

The actual iterated Gaussian ULA laws satisfy Hk≤e−αhkH0+8hdL2/αH_k\le e^{-\alpha hk}H_0+8hdL^2/\alphaHk​≤e−αhkH0​+8hdL2/α, once the displayed interpolation identification, finite KL, entropy/Fisher dissipation, LSI, and moment hypotheses are supplied. This endpoint does not construct all those analytic data from LSI and smoothness alone.

The full actual Gaussian ULA endpoint in UnconditionalTheorem.lean derives all necessary analytic estimates from smoothness, LSI, and finite initial KL. This module retains a reusable interface for explicitly specified interpolation data.

02Why finite KL and Fisher information are separate obligations §

Mathematical statement and proof

At time zero the supplied relative-density law identity gives rk,0ν=ρkr_{k,0}\nu=\rho_krk,0​ν=ρk​, because interpolationLaw_zero returns its input law. Substitute this equality into finite KL of the time-zero density path. It yields finite actual KL of the algorithm law at every index.

04The direct endpoint constructs the recurrence from the path hypotheses §

Mathematical statement and proof

Identify the entropy path's endpoints using interpolationLaw_zero and interpolationLaw_endpoint: its time-zero entropy is HkH_kHk​, and its time-hhh entropy is Hk+1H_{k+1}Hk+1​. The law identity and probability preservation prove that every intermediate relative density is normalized.

Apply the real LSI inequality using the explicit positive smooth density and finite KL/Fisher guards. The scalar one_step_entropy_of_interpolation then combines it with the supplied entropy derivative, production, and gradient-moment estimates. Rewriting the two endpoints yields Hk+1≤e−αhHk+6h2dL2H_{k+1}\le e^{-\alpha h}H_k+6h^2dL^2Hk+1​≤e−αhHk​+6h2dL2. Those analytic interpolation premises remain explicit in this theorem.
