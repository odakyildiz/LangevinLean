This endpoint does not construct all those analytic data from LSI and smoothness alone.

02Why finite KL and Fisher information are separate obligations §

Mathematical statement and proof

The entropy is Mathlib's InformationTheory.klDiv, not a function chosen solely to satisfy a recurrence. This divergence and the Fisher information take values in [0,∞][0,\infty][0,∞]. Lean's real-valued comparisons use ENNReal.toReal; that operation sends infinity to zero, so a real inequality alone would not establish a meaningful finite-entropy estimate.

IsLogSobolev.entropy_le_fisher therefore retains proofs that both extended quantities are finite before giving 2αH≤J2\alpha H\leq J2αH≤J over the reals. The endpoint also retains finite KL along every identified interpolation path. ula_kl_finite_of_interpolation uses its zero-time identification to prove finite KL for the corresponding ULA law, including initialization. These are the guards that make densityEntropy and ulaEntropy faithful real representations of entropy in their applications.

04The direct endpoint constructs the recurrence from the path hypotheses §

Mathematical statement and proof

Supply a family rk,tr_{k,t}rk,t​ with rk,tν=Ptρkr_{k,t}\nu=P_t\rho_krk,t​ν=Pt​ρk​ for every kkk and t∈[0,h]t\in[0,h]t∈[0,h]. Finite KL and Fisher information make the real LSI inequality legitimate. Continuity, right derivatives, the production bound, and the per-step gradient-moment bound supply the scalar theorem's remaining inputs.

The zero-time and endpoint law identities turn H(0)H(0)H(0) and H(h)H(h)H(h) into the actual HkH_kHk​ and Hk+1H_{k+1}Hk+1​. ula_kl_one_step_of_interpolation derives the coefficient-666 estimate. ula_kl_bound_of_interpolation then applies the proved recurrence bound and rewrites the zero-step entropy as Hν(μ)H_\nu(\mu)Hν​(μ). No discrete entropy recurrence is among the endpoint's assumptions.

The potential differentiability and squared-gradient integrability premises remain explicit in this interface, alongside the quantified gradient-moment estimate. Their presence should not be read as a proof of that estimate.
