Entropy convergence under interpolation assumptions

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound is Theorem 2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019); its proof is in Section 3.1 and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2. The proof here establishes Gaussian density regularity, entropy differentiation at positive times, and the limit as elapsed time decreases to zero.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

Let ρk\rho_kρk​ be the ULA laws, let ν\nuν satisfy a log-Sobolev inequality with constant α>0\alpha>0α>0, and let ∇U\nabla U∇U be LLL-Lipschitz. This result assumes a positive differentiable relative density for each interpolation, finite entropy and Fisher information, an entropy derivative estimate, and a bound on the initial gradient moment. Under α≤L\alpha\le Lα≤L and 0<h≤α/(4L2)0<h\le\alpha/(4L^2)0<h≤α/(4L2), these assumptions imply Hk≤e−αhkH0+8hdL2/αH_k\le e^{-\alpha hk}H_0+8hdL^2/\alphaHk​≤e−αhkH0​+8hdL2/α, where Hk=KL⁡(ρk∣ν)H_k=\operatorname{KL}(\rho_k\mid\nu)Hk​=KL(ρk​∣ν).

For one step put H(t)=KL⁡(ρk,t∣ν)H(t)=\operatorname{KL}(\rho_{k,t}\mid\nu)H(t)=KL(ρk,t​∣ν), I(t)=Iν(ρk,t)I(t)=I_\nu(\rho_{k,t})I(t)=Iν​(ρk,t​), and Gk=∫∥∇U∥2dρkG_k=\int\|\nabla U\|^2d\rho_kGk​=∫∥∇U∥2dρk​. The assumed inequalities are H′≤−3I/4+t2L2Gk+2tdL2H'\le-3I/4+t^2L^2G_k+2tdL^2H′≤−3I/4+t2L2Gk​+2tdL2, I≥2αHI\ge2\alpha HI≥2αH, and Gk≤(4L2/α)Hk+2dLG_k\le(4L^2/\alpha)H_k+2dLGk​≤(4L2/α)Hk​+2dL. The equalities ρk,0=ρk\rho_{k,0}=\rho_kρk,0​=ρk​ and ρk,h=ρk+1\rho_{k,h}=\rho_{k+1}ρk,h​=ρk+1​ then give a recurrence for HkH_kHk​. 02Finiteness of the entropy of each iterate §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume the interpolating law has finite relative entropy at every elapsed time in [0,h][0,h][0,h], including zero. At elapsed time zero the equality of laws is rk,0ν=ρkr_{k,0}\nu=\rho_krk,0​ν=ρk​. Therefore

KL⁡(ρk∣ν)=KL⁡(rk,0ν∣ν)<∞.\operatorname{KL}(\rho_k\mid\nu)=\operatorname{KL}(r_{k,0}\nu\mid\nu)<\infty.KL(ρk​∣ν)=KL(rk,0​ν∣ν)<∞.

This argument applies to every index kkk. It establishes finiteness before the divergences are treated as real numbers in the recurrence. Finiteness of Fisher information at a positive time has a different use: together with LSI it bounds the entropy at that time.

04The one-step entropy inequality §

Mathematical statement and proof

The interpolation identities imply H(0)=HkH(0)=H_kH(0)=Hk​ and H(h)=Hk+1H(h)=H_{k+1}H(h)=Hk+1​. Its laws are probability measures, so each positive normalized relative density is admissible for LSI. H′(t)≤−3α2H(t)+t2L2Gk+2tdL2.H'(t)\le-\frac{3\alpha}{2}H(t)+t^2L^2G_k+2tdL^2.H′(t)≤−23α​H(t)+t2L2Gk​+2tdL2.

Insert the assumed bound on GkG_kGk​. The step restriction bounds the part proportional to HkH_kHk​ by αHk/4\alpha H_k/4αHk​/4, and bounds the remaining part by (5/2)hdL2(5/2)hdL^2(5/2)hdL2. Hk+1≤e−αhHk+6h2dL2.H_{k+1}\le e^{-\alpha h}H_k+6h^2dL^2.Hk+1​≤e−αhHk​+6h2dL2.

The differentiability, continuity and finiteness hypotheses justify this integration; they are assumptions of the present result.
