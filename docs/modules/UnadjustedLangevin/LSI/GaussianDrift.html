Gaussian displacement and frozen-gradient error energy

For Yt=X−t∇U(X)+2tZY_t=X-t\nabla U(X)+\sqrt{2t}ZYt​=X−t∇U(X)+2t​Z, independent Gaussian noise makes the cross term vanish, giving E∥Yt−X∥2=t2E∥∇U(X)∥2+2td\mathbb E\|Y_t-X\|^2=t^2\mathbb E\|\nabla U(X)\|^2+2tdE∥Yt​−X∥2=t2E∥∇U(X)∥2+2td. Gradient Lipschitzness bounds the corresponding frozen-gradient error by L2L^2L2 times this moment.

Mathematical statement and proof

05frozen gradient error energy §

Mathematical statement and proof

Lipschitzness gives ∥∇U(Tt)−∇U(X)∥2≤L2∥Tt−X∥2\|\nabla U(T_t)-\nabla U(X)\|^2\le L^2\|T_t-X\|^2∥∇U(Tt​)−∇U(X)∥2≤L2∥Tt​−X∥2 pointwise. Convert integrability of the squared gradient to L2L^2L2 membership, so the displacement has an integrable squared norm.

Continuity of the proposal and gradient proves measurability of the squared discrepancy. Domination by the integrable displacement energy proves its integrability; integral_mono and the exact displacement moment then give L2(t2G0+2td)L^2(t^2G_0+2td)L2(t2G0​+2td). This is a joint-law estimate; a state-space conditional drift is constructed separately.
