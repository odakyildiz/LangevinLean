Relative scores turn the weak equation into entropy production

The chain rule ∇log⁡r=∇r/r\nabla\log r=\nabla r/r∇logr=∇r/r converts the entropy test log⁡r+1\log r+1logr+1 into the relative score. The weak identity supplied for this test then gives Fisher dissipation, with an extra score/error pairing when drift forcing is present.

01gradient log of pos §

Mathematical statement and proof

The logarithm chain rule gives Dlog⁡r(x)=r(x)−1Dr(x)D\log r(x)=r(x)^{-1}Dr(x)Dlogr(x)=r(x)−1Dr(x) when r(x)>0r(x)>0r(x)>0. The real Hilbert-space gradient is the Riesz representative of the derivative; linearity of that identification gives ∇log⁡r(x)=r(x)−1∇r(x)\nabla\log r(x)=r(x)^{-1}\nabla r(x)∇logr(x)=r(x)−1∇r(x).

02gradient log add one of pos §

Mathematical statement and proof

Adding a constant to a scalar function leaves its Fréchet derivative unchanged. Apply the gradient's derivative-based definition to obtain ∇(log⁡r+1)=∇log⁡r\nabla(\log r+1)=\nabla\log r∇(logr+1)=∇logr. The explicit differentiability/positivity arguments align the statement with the entropy-test calculus.

03inner gradient entropy test §

Mathematical statement and proof

Rearrange the positive-density score identity to ∇r=r∇log⁡r\nabla r=r\nabla\log r∇r=r∇logr. Pair this with ∇(log⁡r+1)=∇log⁡r\nabla(\log r+1)=\nabla\log r∇(logr+1)=∇logr and use ⟨s,s⟩=∥s∥2\langle s,s\rangle=\|s\|^2⟨s,s⟩=∥s∥2. The Dirichlet integrand is therefore ⟨∇r,∇(log⁡r+1)⟩=r∥∇log⁡r∥2\langle\nabla r,\nabla(\log r+1)\rangle=r\|\nabla\log r\|^2⟨∇r,∇(logr+1)⟩=r∥∇logr∥2.

04entropy production of weak identity §

Mathematical statement and proof

The strictly positive C1C^1C1 density makes ψ=log⁡r+1\psi=\log r+1ψ=logr+1 a C1C^1C1 test. The entropy derivative integrability is supplied, and the pointwise gradient identity converts its Dirichlet integrability to the supplied weighted Fisher integrability. Substitute this admissible test into the explicit weak equation to obtain ∫r˙(log⁡r+1)dν=−∫r∥∇log⁡r∥2dν\int\dot r(\log r+1)d\nu=-\int r\|\nabla\log r\|^2d\nu∫r˙(logr+1)dν=−∫r∥∇logr∥2dν.

05Test the weak equation with a noncompact function §

Mathematical statement and proof

Use the same C1C^1C1 entropy test in the forced weak equation. Rewrite the Dirichlet term by inner_gradient_entropy_test and the forcing gradient by gradient_log_add_one_of_pos. The three explicit integrability conditions justify the substitution, giving entropy production −I+∫r⟨e,∇log⁡r⟩dν-I+\int r\langle e,\nabla\log r\rangle d\nu−I+∫r⟨e,∇logr⟩dν. Admissibility of this noncompact test remains part of the weak-equation premise.
