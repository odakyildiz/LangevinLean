A reusable regular-interpolation interface

The RegularInterpolationData structure bundles density paths identifying the actual ULA interpolation, regularity, weak-equation and error controls. Its methods derive path probability, finite KL, the one-step recurrence and the iterated entropy bound. Existence of this package is not asserted by its definition.

The full actual Gaussian ULA endpoint in UnconditionalTheorem.lean derives all necessary analytic estimates from smoothness, LSI, and finite initial KL. This module retains a reusable interface for explicitly specified interpolation data.

01Specify the actual laws, density regularity and energy data §

Mathematical statement and proof

Package the relative densities rk,tr_{k,t}rk,t​, their time derivatives, and drift fields together with the exact law identity, positivity, spatial regularity, local entropy domination, finite score/error energies, admissible weak equation, drift-energy bound, and gradient-moment bound. These remain explicit mathematical inputs. No entropy derivative, entropy recurrence, or convergence estimate is a structure field.

02RegularInterpolationData.path probability §

Mathematical statement and proof

The data's law field identifies the tilted density with the actual interpolation from ρk\rho_kρk​. Probability preservation proves that interpolation is normalized. Rewrite by the law identity to obtain normalization of each density along the supplied path.

Mathematical statement and proof

04RegularInterpolationData.one step §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define Hk′(t)H'_k(t)Hk′​(t) by the differentiated entropy integral. The regular weak-equation theorem proves its right-derivative identity and Fisher/drift production bound. Data fields supply probability, KL/Fisher finiteness, continuity, numerical error-energy and gradient bounds. Feed these proved ingredients to ula_kl_one_step_of_interpolation to derive the recurrence rather than assuming it in the data.

Mathematical statement and proof

Its already proved differentiation, one-step estimate, and geometric iteration give the full displayed KL bound for the actual ULA laws represented by the data.
