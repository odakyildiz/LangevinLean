A reusable regular-interpolation interface

The RegularInterpolationData structure bundles density paths identifying the actual ULA interpolation, regularity, weak-equation and error controls. Its methods derive path probability, finite KL, the one-step recurrence and the iterated entropy bound. Existence of this package is not asserted by its definition.

01Specify the actual laws, density regularity and energy data §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let EEE be a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space, d=dim⁡Ed=\dim Ed=dimE, U:E→RU:E\to\mathbb RU:E→R, ν=πU\nu=\pi_Uν=πU​, and μ\muμ an initial law. 02Derive path probability and finite algorithm KL §

Mathematical statement and proof

If μ\muμ is a probability and ∇U\nabla U∇U is Lipschitz, the Gaussian transition is measurable and preserves probability. The density-law identity therefore makes rk,tνr_{k,t}\nurk,t​ν a probability. If ν\nuν is also a probability, local regularity supplies integrability of rk,tlog⁡rk,tr_{k,t}\log r_{k,t}rk,t​logrk,t​; the change-of-measure formula proves finite actual KL. At time zero, P0ρk=ρkP_0\rho_k=\rho_kP0​ρk​=ρk​, so this gives KL⁡(ρk∥ν)<∞\operatorname{KL}(\rho_k\|\nu)<\inftyKL(ρk​∥ν)<∞ for every kkk, including initialization.
