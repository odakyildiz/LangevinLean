Paper reference

The entropy bound is Theorem 2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019); its proof is in Section 3.1 and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2. The proof here establishes Gaussian density regularity, entropy differentiation at positive times, and the limit as elapsed time decreases to zero.

01A square bound for the sum of two vectors §

Mathematical statement and proof

The triangle inequality gives ∥x+y∥≤∥x∥+∥y∥\|x+y\|\le\|x\|+\|y\|∥x+y∥≤∥x∥+∥y∥. Square both nonnegative sides and use 2∥x∥∥y∥≤∥x∥2+∥y∥22\|x\|\|y\|\le\|x\|^2+\|y\|^22∥x∥∥y∥≤∥x∥2+∥y∥2, which follows from (∥x∥−∥y∥)2≥0(\|x\|-\|y\|)^2\ge0(∥x∥−∥y∥)2≥0. This proves ∥x+y∥2≤2∥x∥2+2∥y∥2\|x+y\|^2\le2\|x\|^2+2\|y\|^2∥x+y∥2≤2∥x∥2+2∥y∥2, the pointwise estimate used to split relative Fisher energy.

Expand ∥a+b∥2\|a+b\|^2∥a+b∥2, bound the mixed term by 2∥a∥∥b∥2\|a\|\|b\|2∥a∥∥b∥, and use 2uv≤u2+v22uv\le u^2+v^22uv≤u2+v2. This gives the factor two without requiring any relation between the vectors.

02Splitting the relative-score energy §

Mathematical statement and proof

The jointly measurable score field agrees at positive time with the relative score. Its decomposition is st=∇U+∇log⁡pts_t=\nabla U+\nabla\log p_tst​=∇U+∇logpt​. The propagated gradient is square integrable, and Gaussian smoothing gives Eρt∥∇log⁡pt∥2≤d/(2t)\mathbb E_{\rho_t}\|\nabla\log p_t\|^2\le d/(2t)Eρt​​∥∇logpt​∥2≤d/(2t). These facts also prove integrability of the relative-score square.

Apply the squared sum bound pointwise and integrate: It≤2Gt+2Eρt∥∇log⁡pt∥2≤2Gt+d/tI_t\le2G_t+2\mathbb E_{\rho_t}\|\nabla\log p_t\|^2\le2G_t+d/tIt​≤2Gt​+2Eρt​​∥∇logpt​∥2≤2Gt​+d/t. The proof keeps the integrability witnesses for all terms before using integral monotonicity.

Split the relative score into the Lebesgue score and potential gradient. The preceding square inequality bounds its energy by twice each component energy, which are estimated by Gaussian smoothing and propagated score moments.

05Spacetime integrability of relative-score energy §

Mathematical statement and proof

The relative density and the chosen relative-score field are jointly measurable in time and space. Their weighted energy is nonnegative. On a positive interval [a,b][a,b][a,b], the section at every time is integrable by the density-change theorem.

Since 0<a≤t≤b0<a\le t\le b0<a≤t≤b, the quantitative estimate is bounded uniformly by 4(1+L2b2)G0+8bdL2+d/a4(1+L^2b^2)G_0+8bdL^2+d/a4(1+L2b2)G0​+8bdL2+d/a. The integral of the section norm is exactly its nonnegative energy, so this finite scalar bound makes it integrable in time. The product criterion yields rt∥st∥2∈L1(dt dν)r_t\|s_t\|^2\in L^1(dt\,d\nu)rt​∥st​∥2∈L1(dtdν) on the interval. This is the spacetime dominating function required to pass from bounded entropy tests to the full relative Fisher term.

The explicit time bound is a sum of powers of time and 1/t1/t1/t. On a positive compact interval each is integrable, proving spacetime square integrability of the measurable relative-score field.

09The kernel representation of gradient-error energy §

Mathematical statement and proof

The pair law is identified with Kt(x,y) μ(dx)dyK_t(x,y)\,\mu(dx)dyKt​(x,y)μ(dx)dy by the previously proved Gaussian proposal pair-density theorem. The proof specializes its measurable center to x−t∇U(x)x-t\nabla U(x)x−t∇U(x); the precision and step-size versions of the kernel agree pointwise.

Rewrite the pair-law energy theorem using this exact density identity. The weighted-density integrability equivalence gives Kt∥∇U(y)−∇U(x)∥2∈L1(μ⊗dy)K_t\|\nabla U(y)-\nabla U(x)\|^2\in L^1(\mu\otimes dy)Kt​∥∇U(y)−∇U(x)∥2∈L1(μ⊗dy), and its integral formula gives the sharp bound ∫Kt∥∇U(y)−∇U(x)∥2dμ dy≤L2(t2G0+2td)\int K_t\|\nabla U(y)-\nabla U(x)\|^2d\mu\,dy\le L^2(t^2G_0+2td)∫Kt​∥∇U(y)−∇U(x)∥2dμdy≤L2(t2G0​+2td). The change of measure preserves the constants in this error-energy bound, which are used in the entropy recurrence.

Rewriting the pair measure using its conditional Gaussian density gives the kernel-weighted energy. Thus the same sharp bound is available in the weak PDE's Lebesgue integral representation.
