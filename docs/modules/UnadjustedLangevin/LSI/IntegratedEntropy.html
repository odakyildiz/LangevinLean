Integrating entropy dissipation with almost-everywhere derivatives

The actual entropy chain rule yields local absolute continuity and a derivative inequality almost everywhere. These scalar lemmas use precisely that regularity, together with a continuous one-sided upper envelope at time zero. They do not require pointwise derivatives at every time.

02Send the positive initial time to zero §

Mathematical statement and proof

Suppose HHH is absolutely continuous on every [s,T][s,T][s,T], 0<s<T0<s<T0<s<T, and H(s)≤M(s)H(s)\le M(s)H(s)≤M(s) for all sufficiently small positive sss. If MMM is continuous at zero, the preceding estimate gives
