Differentiation of the entropy integral

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound is Theorem 2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019); its proof is in Section 3.1 and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2. The proof here establishes Gaussian density regularity, entropy differentiation at positive times, and the limit as elapsed time decreases to zero.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

By the formula Hν(rtν)=∫rtlog⁡rt dνH_\nu(r_t\nu)=\int r_t\log r_t\,d\nuHν​(rt​ν)=∫rt​logrt​dν for normalized relative densities, the time derivative of the relative entropy along a path of densities (rt)(r_t)(rt​) is the derivative of a parameter-dependent integral. This section computes it under the hypotheses of the theorem on differentiation under the integral sign. The pointwise derivative is ddt(rtlog⁡rt)=r˙t(log⁡rt+1)\frac{d}{dt}\bigl(r_t\log r_t\bigr)=\dot r_t(\log r_t+1)dtd​(rt​logrt​)=r˙t​(logrt​+1) where rt>0r_t>0rt​>0, and if these derivatives are bounded, uniformly for ttt in a neighbourhood of t0t_0t0​, by a ν\nuν-integrable function, then

ddt∣t=t0∫rtlog⁡rt dν=∫r˙t0(log⁡rt0+1) dν.\frac{d}{dt}\Big|_{t=t_0}\int r_t\log r_t\,d\nu=\int\dot r_{t_0}\bigl(\log r_{t_0}+1\bigr)\,d\nu.dtd​​t=t0​​∫rt​logrt​dν=∫r˙t0​​(logrt0​​+1)dν.

This is the first step of the entropy computation in Vempala and Wibisono (2019), Lemma 3, written in relative-density form. No evolution equation for rtr_trt​ and no integration by parts are used here; they enter later, when ∫r˙t(log⁡rt+1) dν\int\dot r_t(\log r_t+1)\,d\nu∫r˙t​(logrt​+1)dν is expressed through the Fisher information.

The proof applies Mathlib's hasDerivAt_integral_of_dominated_loc_of_deriv_le, the theorem on differentiation under the integral sign, with FFF and F′F'F′ as in the proof. Its proof in Mathlib is the argument above: it extracts a ball of radius ε\varepsilonε inside SSS, derives the Lipschitz bound (1) from the derivative bound by the mean value inequality on the convex ball, and passes to the limit in the difference quotients by dominated convergence.
