Differentiation of the entropy integral

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound is Theorem 2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019); its proof is in Section 3.1 and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2. The proof here establishes Gaussian density regularity, entropy differentiation at positive times, and the limit as elapsed time decreases to zero.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

For a positive differentiable function rrr, the scalar derivative is (rlog⁡r)′=r′(log⁡r+1)(r\log r)'=r'(\log r+1)(rlogr)′=r′(logr+1). Applying this identity to a density depending on time gives a derivative of its entropy integral when the differentiated integrands have a common integrable bound on a time neighborhood. This bound and the relevant measurability and integrability assumptions are hypotheses of the result.

01The derivative of a positive function times its logarithm §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume rrr is differentiable at ttt and r(t)>0r(t)>0r(t)>0. The product rule and the derivative of the logarithm give (rlog⁡r)′(t)=r′(t)log⁡r(t)+r(t)r′(t)/r(t)(r\log r)'(t)=r'(t)\log r(t)+r(t)r'(t)/r(t)(rlogr)′(t)=r′(t)logr(t)+r(t)r′(t)/r(t). Positivity implies r(t)≠0r(t)\ne0r(t)=0, so cancellation yields r′(t)(log⁡r(t)+1)r'(t)(\log r(t)+1)r′(t)(logr(t)+1). The constant term is retained at this stage. Its contribution can be removed from an entropy calculation only after the derivative of total mass has been shown to vanish.

Mathematical statement and proof

The scalar chain rule identifies the pointwise derivative, and the dominated differentiation theorem gives

The same bound proves integrability of the derivative integrand. Uniformity on a time neighborhood, rather than a bound at one time alone, is what justifies exchanging differentiation and integration.
