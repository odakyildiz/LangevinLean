Averaging the Gaussian component score

The Gaussian component score St,x(y)=−(y−x+t∇U(x))/(2t)S_{t,x}(y)=-(y-x+t\nabla U(x))/(2t)St,x​(y)=−(y−x+t∇U(x))/(2t) averages to the actual mixture score. This module establishes the vector identity pt∇log⁡pt=∫KtSt,xdμp_t\nabla\log p_t=\int K_tS_{t,x}d\mupt​∇logpt​=∫Kt​St,x​dμ, proves all joint integrability needed for pairing it with a continuous L2(ρt)L^2(\rho_t)L2(ρt​) vector field, and transfers the resulting identity to the fixed independent initial/noise space used by entropy production.

02frozenGaussianScore memLp §

Mathematical statement and proof

04integrable frozenGaussianScore pairing §

Mathematical statement and proof

The component score and the state field V(y)V(y)V(y) are both square integrable under the actual pair law. Holder's inequality makes the product of their norms integrable, and ∣⟨S,V⟩∣≤∥S∥∥V∥|\langle S,V\rangle|\le\|S\|\|V\|∣⟨S,V⟩∣≤∥S∥∥V∥ proves integrability of their inner product. The exact pair-density identity then rewrites this law as Kt(x,y)μ(dx)dyK_t(x,y)\mu(dx)dyKt​(x,y)μ(dx)dy. The with-density equivalence gives joint integrability of Kt⟨St,x,V(y)⟩K_t\langle S_{t,x},V(y)\rangleKt​⟨St,x​,V(y)⟩ under μ⊗dy\mu\otimes dyμ⊗dy, which is the hypothesis needed for Fubini in the averaging calculation.

05interpolationLebesgueDensity weighted score §

Mathematical statement and proof

The already constructed spatial derivative of the Gaussian mixture identifies its score as the posterior mean of the component score. The posterior density in xxx is Kt(x,y)/pt(y)K_t(x,y)/p_t(y)Kt​(x,y)/pt​(y), where pt(y)=∫Kt(x,y)dμ(x)>0p_t(y)=\int K_t(x,y)d\mu(x)>0pt​(y)=∫Kt​(x,y)dμ(x)>0. Substitute this explicit with-density formula into the posterior integral and multiply by pt(y)p_t(y)pt​(y). The common denominator cancels pointwise, giving pt(y)∇log⁡pt(y)=∫Kt(x,y)St,x(y)dμ(x)p_t(y)\nabla\log p_t(y)=\int K_t(x,y)S_{t,x}(y)d\mu(x)pt​(y)∇logpt​(y)=∫Kt​(x,y)St,x​(y)dμ(x). The proof uses the actual Gaussian normalizers in both precision and step-size notation, rather than assuming a formal Bayes identity.

07integral frozenGaussianScore pairing §

Mathematical statement and proof

For almost every yyy, vector-section integrability permits the linear functional w↦⟨w,V(y)⟩w\mapsto\langle w,V(y)\ranglew↦⟨w,V(y)⟩ to commute with integration. The pointwise weighted-score identity then gives ∫Kt(x,y)⟨St,x(y),V(y)⟩dμ(x)=pt(y)⟨∇log⁡pt(y),V(y)⟩\int K_t(x,y)\langle S_{t,x}(y),V(y)\rangle d\mu(x)=p_t(y)\langle\nabla\log p_t(y),V(y)\rangle∫Kt​(x,y)⟨St,x​(y),V(y)⟩dμ(x)=pt​(y)⟨∇logpt​(y),V(y)⟩.

The scalar pairing is jointly integrable by the preceding Holder argument, so Fubini exchanges the initial-state and output integrals. Integrate the displayed identity in yyy. Finally pt(y)dy=ρt(dy)p_t(y)dy=\rho_t(dy)pt​(y)dy=ρt​(dy), with its explicit measurable finite density, changes the weighted Lebesgue integral into the actual-law integral. Thus ∫Kt⟨St,x,V⟩dμ dy=∫⟨∇log⁡pt,V⟩dρt\int K_t\langle S_{t,x},V\rangle d\mu\,dy=\int\langle\nabla\log p_t,V\rangle d\rho_t∫Kt​⟨St,x​,V⟩dμdy=∫⟨∇logpt​,V⟩dρt​.

08integral frozenGaussianScore pairing independent §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply the measurable pair map Tt(x,z)=(x,Yt(x,z))T_t(x,z)=(x,Y_t(x,z))Tt​(x,z)=(x,Yt​(x,z)) to the continuous pairing ⟨St,x(y),V(y)⟩\langle S_{t,x}(y),V(y)\rangle⟨St,x​(y),V(y)⟩. The map-integral theorem expresses its actual pair-law integral as an integral over the fixed independent initial/noise space. Substitute Yt=ct(x)+2tzY_t=c_t(x)+\sqrt{2t}zYt​=ct​(x)+2t​z in the component score.
