Mathematical statement and proof

Run lake env lean UnadjustedLangevin/LSI/Check.lean after the library build. The audit prints the axioms of the chain

relative density calculus  ⟶  H′≤−34J+E  ⟶  Hk≤e−αhkH0+8hdL2/α.\text{relative density calculus}\;\longrightarrow\;H\prime\le-\tfrac34J+\mathcal E\;\longrightarrow\;H_k\le e^{-\alpha hk}H_0+8hdL^2/\alpha.relative density calculus⟶H′≤−43​J+E⟶Hk​≤e−αhkH0​+8hdL2/α.

The desired reports contain standard logical axioms and no sorryAx. This is compatible with a conditional theorem: interpolation densities, regularity, weak identities, drift-error control and the stated gradient-moment bridge remain premises wherever present. Inspect the theorem types alongside their dependency reports.

The #check commands print the exact formal types of the entropy endpoints, and #print axioms audits their transitive proof dependencies. Compiling this file verifies the imported proofs and exposes their hypotheses, including the regular-interpolation premises. The mathematical derivations are in the density, calculus, one-step and recurrence modules.
