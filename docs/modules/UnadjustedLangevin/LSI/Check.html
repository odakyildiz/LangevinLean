Assumptions of the ULA entropy results

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound is Theorem 2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019); its proof is in Section 3.1 and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2. The proof here establishes Gaussian density regularity, entropy differentiation at positive times, and the limit as elapsed time decreases to zero.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

The final Gaussian ULA entropy theorem assumes a differentiable potential with a globally Lipschitz gradient, LSI for its Gibbs probability measure, the positive step restriction, and finite initial relative entropy. It proves the needed gradient moment, Gaussian density regularity, finite Fisher information, entropy absolute continuity, and the estimate near elapsed time zero. The conditional interpolation results retain their additional density and weak-equation assumptions.

Mathematical statement and proof

The mathematical implication is

H′≤−34I+E⟹Hk+1≤e−αhHk+6h2dL2⟹Hk≤e−αhkH0+8hdL2/α.H'\le-\tfrac34I+E\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H_{k+1}\le e^{-\alpha h}H_k+6h^2dL^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H_k\le e^{-\alpha hk}H_0+8hdL^2/\alpha.H′≤−43​I+E⟹Hk+1​≤e−αhHk​+6h2dL2⟹Hk​≤e−αhkH0​+8hdL2/α.

The first implication also uses LSI and the specified gradient and discrepancy bounds. The second uses the step restriction and geometric summation. The final theorem proves the required analytic estimates for the Gaussian interpolation. Conditional variants require those estimates as explicit hypotheses.

The audit prints the logical axioms used by the proofs of both the conditional interpolation results and the final Gaussian ULA theorem.
