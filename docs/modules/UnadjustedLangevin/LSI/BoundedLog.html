Bounded logarithmic tests and their entropy primitives

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound is Theorem 2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019); its proof is in Section 3.1 and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2. The proof here establishes Gaussian density regularity, entropy differentiation at positive times, and the limit as elapsed time decreases to zero.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

For M=m+1M=m+1M=m+1, define χm(z)=Marctan⁡(z/M)\chi_m(z)=M\arctan(z/M)χm​(z)=Marctan(z/M) and wm(z)=(1+(z/M)2)−1w_m(z)=(1+(z/M)^2)^{-1}wm​(z)=(1+(z/M)2)−1. Then χm\chi_mχm​ is smooth and bounded, χm′=wm\chi_m^{\prime}=w_mχm′​=wm​, 0≤wm≤10\le w_m\le10≤wm​≤1, and ∣χm(z)∣≤∣z∣|\chi_m(z)|\le|z|∣χm​(z)∣≤∣z∣. For each fixed zzz, the test and its derivative converge to zzz and 1 respectively.

For a positive density value rrr, the primitive Φm(r)=∫1rχm(log⁡u)du\Phi_m(r)=\int_1^r\chi_m(\log u)duΦm​(r)=∫1r​χm​(logu)du has derivative χm(log⁡r)\chi_m(\log r)χm​(logr), is nonnegative, and is bounded by rlog⁡r−r+1r\log r-r+1rlogr−r+1. Its pointwise limit is this entropy integrand. These estimates justify bounded tests in the weak equation, their time chain rules, and dominated convergence to the entropy identity under finite relative entropy.

Mathematical statement and proof

03Recover the logarithm and its primitive §

Mathematical statement and proof

As m→∞m\to\inftym→∞, z/(m+1)→0z/(m+1)\to0z/(m+1)→0. The derivative of arctangent at zero is one; its difference quotient therefore gives χm(z)→z\chi_m(z)\to zχm​(z)→z. At z=0z=0z=0 the claim is exact at every index. Continuity of inversion at the positive denominator one gives wm(z)→1w_m(z)\to1wm​(z)→1.

The function u↦χm(log⁡u)u\mapsto\chi_m(\log u)u↦χm​(logu) is measurable and bounded, hence integrable on every finite interval, including intervals containing zero. 04Dominate the entropy endpoints §

Mathematical statement and proof

We have Φm(1)=0\Phi_m(1)=0Φm​(1)=0. For r≥1r\ge1r≥1, both log⁡u\log ulogu and its test are nonnegative on [1,r][1,r][1,r], giving Φm(r)≥0\Phi_m(r)\ge0Φm​(r)≥0. For 0<r≤10<r\le10<r≤1, the test is nonpositive on [r,1][r,1][r,1], and reversing the interval again gives nonnegativity.

0≤Φm(r)≤∫1rlog⁡u du=rlog⁡r−r+1.0\le\Phi_m(r)\le\int_1^r\log u\,du=r\log r-r+1.0≤Φm​(r)≤∫1r​logudu=rlogr−r+1.

The entropy integrand rlog⁡r−r+1r\log r-r+1rlogr−r+1 is nonnegative and dominates the bounded primitives. For a probability density its integral equals KL, so finite relative entropy supplies the endpoint majorant for dominated convergence.

05Identify the spatial gradient of the test §

Mathematical statement and proof

For a differentiable scalar field fff, the Fréchet chain rule gives D(χm∘f)=wm(f)DfD(\chi_m\circ f)=w_m(f)DfD(χm​∘f)=wm​(f)Df. Applying the Hilbert-space identification of linear derivatives with gradients yields

∇(χm∘f)(x)=wm(f(x))∇f(x),∥∇(χm∘f)(x)∥≤∥∇f(x)∥.\nabla(\chi_m\circ f)(x)=w_m(f(x))\nabla f(x),\qquad\|\nabla(\chi_m\circ f)(x)\|\le\|\nabla f(x)\|.∇(χm​∘f)(x)=wm​(f(x))∇f(x),∥∇(χm​∘f)(x)∥≤∥∇f(x)∥.

The inequality uses 0≤wm≤10\le w_m\le10≤wm​≤1. With f=log⁡rtf=\log r_tf=logrt​, it supplies square-integrability of the bounded log test gradient from the Fisher information bound.

Applying the spatial chain rule multiplies the relative score by wm(log⁡r)w_m(\log r)wm​(logr). Since this weight is at most one, the gradient's squared energy is bounded by Fisher information, proving admissibility of every bounded test.
