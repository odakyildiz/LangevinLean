An entropy upper bound at the initial time

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound is Theorem 2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019); its proof is in Section 3.1 and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2. The proof here establishes Gaussian density regularity, entropy differentiation at positive times, and the limit as elapsed time decreases to zero.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

Let H0<∞H_0<\inftyH0​<∞ be the initial relative entropy and G0=∫∥∇U∥2dμG_0=\int\|\nabla U\|^2d\muG0​=∫∥∇U∥2dμ, finite by the preceding entropy-moment estimate. With δ(t)=1−4tL\delta(t)=1-4tLδ(t)=1−4tL, define

B(t)=H0−d2log⁡δ(t)+t4δ(t)[2(1+L2t2)G0+4tdL2].B(t)=H_0-\frac d2\log\delta(t)+\frac{t}{4\delta(t)}\left[2(1+L^2t^2)G_0+4tdL^2\right].B(t)=H0​−2d​logδ(t)+4δ(t)t​[2(1+L2t2)G0​+4tdL2].

The updated Gibbs density comparison, data processing, and propagated gradient bound give finite interpolation entropy Ht≤B(t)H_t\le B(t)Ht​≤B(t) for sufficiently small positive ttt. The denominator tends to one, so BBB is continuous at zero and B(0)=H0B(0)=H_0B(0)=H0​. This upper bound supplies the initial value in the scalar comparison. No initial Fisher information, position second moment, or continuity assumption on the entropy path at zero is needed.

02The majorant at zero §

Mathematical statement and proof

At t=0t=0t=0, the logarithm has argument δ(0)=1\delta(0)=1δ(0)=1, hence is zero. The coefficient t/(4δ(t))t/(4\delta(t))t/(4δ(t)) is zero as well. Both correction terms vanish exactly, proving B(0)=H0B(0)=H_0B(0)=H0​. This is a direct algebraic evaluation of the constructed scalar function. No limit of the entropy path is assumed.

At zero the logarithmic term is log⁡1=0\log1=0log1=0, and the entire energy correction has a factor ttt. Hence the value is precisely the initial entropy, even when the initial law has no smooth density.

03Continuity of the majorant at zero §

Mathematical statement and proof

The denominator δ(t)\delta(t)δ(t) has value one at zero, so it stays nonzero locally. The logarithm is continuous at that same positive value. All other terms in BBB are polynomial or constant scalar operations. Their continuity proves B(t)→B(0)=H0B(t)\to B(0)=H_0B(t)→B(0)=H0​ as t→0t\to0t→0. The initial gradient integral is an already defined scalar; its finiteness in the entropy application is established by the finite-KL moment theorem.

The denominator tends to one and is nonzero near zero. Continuity of the logarithm at one, polynomial factors, and reciprocal then proves continuity of the full majorant at the endpoint.

04The interpolation entropy lies below the upper bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

For positive ttt with δ=1−4tL>0\delta=1-4tL>0δ=1−4tL>0, choose the comparison Gaussian time s=t/δs=t/\deltas=t/δ. Since s/t=δ−1s/t=\delta^{-1}s/t=δ−1, this is exp⁡[−(d/2)log⁡δ+(s/4)∥∇U(y)∥2]\exp[-(d/2)\log\delta+(s/4)\|\nabla U(y)\|^2]exp[−(d/2)logδ+(s/4)∥∇U(y)∥2].

The change-of-reference entropy estimate then gives finite HtH_tHt​ and Ht≤H0−(d/2)log⁡δ+(s/4)GtH_t\le H_0-(d/2)\log\delta+(s/4)G_tHt​≤H0​−(d/2)logδ+(s/4)Gt​. The density-ratio comparison bounds output entropy by the majorant whenever 1−4tL>01-4tL>01−4tL>0. The finite score moment supplies all integrability required for the likelihood decomposition.

05The entropy majorant on a positive neighborhood §

Mathematical statement and proof

For all sufficiently small positive ttt, specifically t<1/(8L)t<1/(8L)t<1/(8L), one has 1−4tL>01-4tL>01−4tL>0. The previous positive-time entropy bound therefore applies eventually in the right neighborhood of zero, giving Ht≤B(t)H_t\le B(t)Ht​≤B(t). Combined with continuity of BBB and B(0)=H0B(0)=H_0B(0)=H0​, this supplies the upper initial boundary used in scalar comparison. It does not insert an assumption that HtH_tHt​ is differentiable or continuous at zero.

A sufficiently small positive neighborhood satisfies the denominator condition. Thus the entropy upper bound is available along every approach to zero through positive times, exactly the endpoint control used in scalar comparison.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.
