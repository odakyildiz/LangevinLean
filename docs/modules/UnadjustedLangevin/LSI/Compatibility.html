05Weighted Gibbs integration §

Unfold ν=Z−1(e−Udx)\nu=Z^{-1}(e^{-U}dx)ν=Z−1(e−Udx). Scalar multiplication of a measure multiplies its real Bochner integral by (Z−1)(Z^{-1})(Z−1).
