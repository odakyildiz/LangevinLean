Mathematical statement and proof

The pointwise Young inequality used here is

⟨e,s⟩≤14∥s∥2+∥e∥2. \langle e,s\rangle\leq\frac14\|s\|^2+\|e\|^2. ⟨e,s⟩≤41​∥s∥2+∥e∥2.

It follows from Cauchy–Schwarz and (∥e∥−∥s∥/2)2≥0(\|e\|-\|s\|/2)^2\geq0(∥e∥−∥s∥/2)2≥0. Multiplication by r≥0r\geq0r≥0 and integration yield

H′(t)≤−34J(t)+E(t),E(t)=∫rt∥et∥2 dν. H'(t)\leq-\frac34J(t)+\mathcal E(t), \qquad \mathcal E(t)=\int r_t\|e_t\|^2\,d\nu. H′(t)≤−43​J(t)+E(t),E(t)=∫rt​∥et​∥2dν.

The coefficient 3/43/43/4 is deliberate: one quarter of the negative Fisher term pays for the drift-score interaction, leaving enough dissipation for the scalar contraction estimate. Lean also proves an absolute-inner-product version. With measurability and finite score and error energies, that version establishes integrability of the cross term; cross-term integrability is not silently inferred from a formal expression.
