Spend one quarter under Fisher information on drift error

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound follows Theorem 2, Section 3.1, and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019). The Lean development supplies the regularity and endpoint arguments for the actual Gaussian ULA laws.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

Young’s inequality ⟨a,b⟩≤14∥a∥2+∥b∥2\langle a,b\rangle\le\frac14\|a\|^2+\|b\|^2⟨a,b⟩≤41​∥a∥2+∥b∥2 bounds the error pairing in entropy production. Weighted energy integrability yields an actual integral inequality H′≤−34J+EH\prime\le-\frac34J+\mathcal EH′≤−43​J+E.

The error term in entropy production pairs the drift discrepancy eee with the relative score sss. The weighted square identity (∥e∥−∥s∥/2)2≥0(\|e\|-\|s\|/2)^2\ge0(∥e∥−∥s∥/2)2≥0 gives ∣⟨e,s⟩∣≤∥e∥2+∥s∥2/4|\langle e,s\rangle|\le\|e\|^2+\|s\|^2/4∣⟨e,s⟩∣≤∥e∥2+∥s∥2/4. Its absolute-value form first proves that the pairing is integrable from the two energy bounds. Its signed form then absorbs one quarter of Fisher information, leaving −3I/4-3I/4−3I/4 as a strictly negative dissipation contribution.

01Young absorption preserving three quarters of Fisher information §

Mathematical statement and proof

Cauchy–Schwarz gives ⟨e,s⟩≤∥e∥∥s∥\langle e,s\rangle\le\|e\|\|s\|⟨e,s⟩≤∥e∥∥s∥. Expand the nonnegative square (∥e∥−∥s∥/2)2≥0(\|e\|-\|s\|/2)^2\ge0(∥e∥−∥s∥/2)2≥0 to get ∥e∥∥s∥≤∥e∥2+∥s∥2/4\|e\|\|s\|\le\|e\|^2+\|s\|^2/4∥e∥∥s∥≤∥e∥2+∥s∥2/4. This is the Young coefficient that preserves three quarters of Fisher information.

Expanding the square gives ∥e∥2−∥e∥∥s∥+∥s∥2/4≥0\|e\|^2-\|e\|\|s\|+\|s\|^2/4\ge0∥e∥2−∥e∥∥s∥+∥s∥2/4≥0. Thus the quarter coefficient is exact for this chosen absorption, and leaves a unit coefficient on the discrepancy energy.

02Integrability of the weighted error-score pairing §

Mathematical statement and proof

Multiply the absolute Young bound by the nonnegative weight rrr. It dominates ∣r⟨e,s⟩∣|r\langle e,s\rangle|∣r⟨e,s⟩∣ by r∥s∥2/4+r∥e∥2r\|s\|^2/4+r\|e\|^2r∥s∥2/4+r∥e∥2. Both energy functions are integrable, so the assumed measurability and integrable domination prove integrability of the cross term.

The absolute-value domination is established before integrating the signed pairing. Measurability plus the two finite energy integrals therefore gives a legitimate ordinary cross-term integral.

03The forced entropy-production inequality §

Mathematical statement and proof

First derive cross-term integrability from the two finite energies and its measurability. The forced weak identity tested by log⁡r+1\log r+1logr+1 gives production −I+∫r⟨e,s⟩-I+\int r\langle e,s\rangle−I+∫r⟨e,s⟩. Insert the integrated Young bound to obtain −34I+∫r∥e∥2-\frac34I+\int r\|e\|^2−43​I+∫r∥e∥2. The entropy differential inequality is therefore proved from the stated spatial weak identity.

Insert the admissible entropy test only after its forcing pairing has been proved integrable. The resulting inequality is derived from the spatial weak equation and finite energies, not postulated as a scalar property of the entropy curve.

04Absolute-value Young domination §

Mathematical statement and proof

Use the absolute Cauchy–Schwarz inequality ∣⟨e,s⟩∣≤∥e∥∥s∥|\langle e,s\rangle|\le\|e\|\|s\|∣⟨e,s⟩∣≤∥e∥∥s∥, followed by the same square expansion. The absolute-value version controls integrability of the signed cross term before its integral is estimated.

Replacing the pairing by its absolute value is used for domination and Fubini. The same nonnegative-square calculation supplies the upper bound, so no additional moment beyond the two square energies is needed.

05The integrated weighted Young inequality §

Mathematical statement and proof

Multiply the pointwise Young inequality by r≥0r\ge0r≥0, then integrate. The supplied integrability permits splitting the upper integral and pulling out 1/41/41/4. Thus ∫r⟨e,s⟩≤14∫r∥s∥2+∫r∥e∥2\int r\langle e,s\rangle\le\frac14\int r\|s\|^2+\int r\|e\|^2∫r⟨e,s⟩≤41​∫r∥s∥2+∫r∥e∥2. No probability normalization of the weight is needed.

Nonnegativity of rrr preserves the pointwise upper bound. Integral linearity then separates the two finite energies, retaining exactly the Fisher fraction required for the subsequent LSI argument.
