Derive entropy calculus from regular interpolation hypotheses

This wrapper replaces a supplied entropy derivative estimate with explicit regular density data, a forced weak identity and an error-energy bound. It then invokes the conditional ULA entropy endpoint. Constructing those regular interpolation data remains a displayed premise.

The actual Gaussian law identification gives probability normalization. Local regularity proves finite KL and dominated entropy differentiation; score integrability proves finite Fisher information. The KL density formula transfers continuity and the right derivative to Hk(t)=KL⁡(rk,tν∥ν)H_k(t)=\operatorname{KL}(r_{k,t}\nu\|\nu)Hk​(t)=KL(rk,t​ν∥ν). Testing the weak equation and applying Young’s inequality then proves

Hk′+(t)≤−34Jk(t)+t2L2Gk+2tdL2.H_k\prime_+(t)\le-\tfrac34J_k(t)+t^2L^2G_k+2tdL^2.Hk​′+​(t)≤−43​Jk​(t)+t2L2Gk​+2tdL2.

These are all hypotheses of the direct interpolation theorem, so it derives the one-step recurrence and its iteration:

KL⁡(ρk∥ν)≤e−αhkKL⁡(μ∥ν)+8hdL2α.\operatorname{KL}(\rho_k\|\nu)\le e^{-\alpha hk}\operatorname{KL}(\mu\|\nu)+\frac{8hdL^2}{\alpha}.KL(ρk​∥ν)≤e−αhkKL(μ∥ν)+α8hdL2​.

The wrapper therefore derives the entropy derivative and production estimate rather than assuming them. Existence of the density paths, their local regularity, the admissible weak equation, error estimate and gradient-moment estimate remain explicit premises; they are not constructed from LSI and smoothness alone.
