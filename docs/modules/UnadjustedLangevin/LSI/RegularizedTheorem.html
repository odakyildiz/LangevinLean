Derive entropy calculus from regular interpolation hypotheses

References and publication status for this development

Paper reference

The entropy bound follows Theorem 2, Section 3.1, and Appendices B.2.1–B.2.2 of Vempala and Wibisono (2019). The Lean development supplies the regularity and endpoint arguments for the actual Gaussian ULA laws.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices

This wrapper replaces a supplied entropy derivative estimate with explicit regular density data, a forced weak identity and an error-energy bound. It then invokes the conditional ULA entropy endpoint. Constructing those regular interpolation data remains a displayed premise.

The full actual Gaussian ULA endpoint in UnconditionalTheorem.lean derives all necessary analytic estimates from smoothness, LSI, and finite initial KL. This module retains a reusable interface for explicitly specified interpolation data.

Mathematical statement and proof

First use the density/law identity to prove probability normalization along each interpolation. Local regularity proves finite actual KL, score integrability proves finite Fisher, and densityEntropy_continuousOn_of_regular gives continuity of actual entropy.

Define the candidate derivative Hk′(t)=∫r˙k,t(log⁡rk,t+1)dπUH'_k(t)=\int\dot r_{k,t}(\log r_{k,t}+1)d\pi_UHk′​(t)=∫r˙k,t​(logrk,t​+1)dπU​. The regular weak-equation theorem proves that this is the actual right derivative and that it is bounded by −3I/4+E-3I/4+E−3I/4+E. Insert the supplied drift-energy bound E≤t2L2Gk+2tdL2E\le t^2L^2G_k+2tdL^2E≤t2L2Gk​+2tdL2, then invoke ula_kl_bound_of_interpolation.

The final estimate is therefore derived without assuming an entropy derivative, a production inequality, or a discrete recurrence. This wrapper still explicitly requires the regular density construction, its admissible weak equation, its drift-energy estimate, and the entropy-to-gradient moment bound; the wrapper does not itself construct them from LSI.
