The full Gaussian ULA entropy theorem

For a differentiable potential with an LLL-Lipschitz gradient, its normalized Gibbs target satisfying LSI with constant α>0\alpha>0α>0, and a finite-KL initial probability law, this module proves the actual ULA bound

KL(ρk∣ν)≤e−αhkKL(ρ0∣ν)+8hdL2/α,0<h≤α/(4L2).\mathrm{KL}(\rho_k\mid\nu)\le e^{-\alpha hk}\mathrm{KL}(\rho_0\mid\nu)+8hdL^2/\alpha,\quad0<h\le\alpha/(4L^2).KL(ρk​∣ν)≤e−αhkKL(ρ0​∣ν)+8hdL2/α,0<h≤α/(4L2).

The final conclusion includes finiteness of the actual divergence. All score moments, density calculus, weak equations, entropy endpoints, and numerical compatibility estimates are supplied by the preceding modules. The theorem applies also in dimension zero.

01Handle the singleton state space and a zero smoothness parameter §

Mathematical statement and proof

On a singleton state space every probability measure is equal: any measurable set either contains the unique point and is the whole space, or is empty. Measure extensionality and probability normalization therefore identify any two laws. In particular their KL divergence is zero.

Independently of dimension, a positive admissible step forces L>0L>0L>0. If L=0L=0L=0, Lean’s total-field convention gives α/(4L2)=0\alpha/(4L^2)=0α/(4L2)=0, contradicting 0<h≤00<h\le00<h≤0. This elementary guard permits the score-exponential estimates to divide by LLL. It is derived from the step condition, not assumed in the final theorem.
