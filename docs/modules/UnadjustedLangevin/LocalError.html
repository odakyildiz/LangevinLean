The local Euler defect

Let HHH be a real Banach space, with norm ∥⋅∥\|\cdot\|∥⋅∥. All Lipschitz constants LLL below are nonnegative. All time integrals are Bochner integrals. For the Langevin application we will take H=L2(P;Rd)H=L^2(P;\mathbb R^d)H=L2(P;Rd); the norms below then measure random variables in mean square. The scalar defect estimate permits a nonnegative real parameter ddd; its application uses the natural-number dimension, viewed as a real number. Begin with a Lipschitz drift ggg, a reference curve YYY, and its additive noise BBB. We first derive the error of freezing the drift over one time step directly from the integral equation.

The analytic calculation is conditional on the displayed integral equation and norm bounds. It neither constructs a stationary Langevin diffusion nor proves its score moment. The Gaussian and Brownian noise moments used later are proved separately.

localDefect is a vector-valued Bochner interval integral. It exists as a totalized expression before integrability is proved; all norm and linearity steps below explicitly establish the continuous or integrable hypotheses needed for their ordinary mathematical meaning. The interval 0..h0..h0..h is oriented, while the error estimates assume h≥0h\ge0h≥0.

03An exact step is Euler minus its defect §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume the preceding drift integrability and the endpoint integral equation

Yh−Y0=−∫0hg(Yt) dt+Bh.Y_h-Y_0=-\int_0^h g(Y_t)\,dt+B_h.Yh​−Y0​=−∫0h​g(Yt​)dt+Bh​.

Solve the assumed integral equation for YhY_hYh​, then replace the defect by the difference between the exact drift integral and its frozen approximation.

Cancel the two frozen-drift terms. The resulting equality is precisely the Euler update with the signed remainder −Dh-D_h−Dh​.

04Continuity supplies drift integrability §
