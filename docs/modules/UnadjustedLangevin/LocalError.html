Let HHH be a real Banach space, let g:H→Hg:H\to Hg:H→H be a map (the drift), and let Y,B:R→HY,B:\mathbb R\to HY,B:R→H be curves. Integrals of HHH-valued functions of time are Bochner integrals with respect to Lebesgue measure, written as oriented integrals ∫ab\int_a^b∫ab​ (for b<ab<ab<a, ∫ab=−∫ba\int_a^b=-\int_b^a∫ab​=−∫ba​); the integrands below are assumed integrable on the intervals in question, which holds in the application because they are continuous. We use linearity, the formula ∫abc dt=(b−a)c\int_a^bc\,dt=(b-a)c∫ab​cdt=(b−a)c for a constant vector ccc, and the norm estimate ∥∫abϕ(t) dt∥≤∫abψ(t) dt\|\int_a^b\phi(t)\,dt\|\le\int_a^b\psi(t)\,dt∥∫ab​ϕ(t)dt∥≤∫ab​ψ(t)dt for a≤ba\le ba≤b whenever ∥ϕ(t)∥≤ψ(t)\|\phi(t)\|\le\psi(t)∥ϕ(t)∥≤ψ(t) on (a,b](a,b](a,b] and ψ\psiψ is integrable.

The local defect of one Euler step of length hhh is

Dh(g,Y)=∫0h(g(Yt)−g(Y0)) dt.D_h(g,Y)=\int_0^h\bigl(g(Y_t)-g(Y_0)\bigr)\,dt.Dh​(g,Y)=∫0h​(g(Yt​)−g(Y0​))dt.

If YYY solves the integral equation Yt−Y0=−∫0tg(Ys) ds+BtY_t-Y_0=-\int_0^tg(Y_s)\,ds+B_tYt​−Y0​=−∫0t​g(Ys​)ds+Bt​ on [0,h][0,h][0,h], then Yh=Y0−hg(Y0)+Bh−Dh(g,Y)Y_h=Y_0-hg(Y_0)+B_h-D_h(g,Y)Yh​=Y0​−hg(Y0​)+Bh​−Dh​(g,Y): the exact value after time hhh is one Euler step with the same additive input BhB_hBh​, minus the defect. If moreover ggg is LLL-Lipschitz, ∥g(Yt)∥≤G\|g(Y_t)\|\le G∥g(Yt​)∥≤G and ∥Bt∥≤Nt\|B_t\|\le N\sqrt t∥Bt​∥≤Nt​ for 0≤t≤h0\le t\le h0≤t≤h, then

and with G=LdG=\sqrt{Ld}G=Ld​, N=2dN=\sqrt{2d}N=2d​ and Lh≤2Lh\le2Lh≤2 this becomes ∥Dh(g,Y)∥≤3320Lhhd\|D_h(g,Y)\|\le\frac{33}{20}Lh\sqrt{hd}∥Dh​(g,Y)∥≤2033​Lhhd​, where 3320\frac{33}{20}2033​ is a rational upper bound for 762\frac76\sqrt267​2​. 01Definition of the local defect §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. For g:H→Hg:H\to Hg:H→H, Y:R→HY:\mathbb R\to HY:R→H and h∈Rh\in\mathbb Rh∈R,

Dh(g,Y)=∫0h(g(Yt)−g(Y0)) dt∈H.D_h(g,Y)=\int_0^h\bigl(g(Y_t)-g(Y_0)\bigr)\,dt\in H.Dh​(g,Y)=∫0h​(g(Yt​)−g(Y0​))dt∈H.

The integrand vanishes at t=0t=0t=0; the defect measures how much the drift along the curve differs from its value at the initial point, which is the value used by one Euler step.

The variable declaration makes HHH a real Banach space ([NormedAddCommGroup H] [NormedSpace ℝ H] [CompleteSpace H]). Mathlib assigns the value 000 to the integral of a function that is not integrable; the hypotheses of the following results make the relevant integrands integrable whenever this matters.

02The defect as exact minus frozen drift §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let g:H→Hg:H\to Hg:H→H, Y:R→HY:\mathbb R\to HY:R→H and h∈Rh\in\mathbb Rh∈R, and assume that t↦g(Yt)t\mapsto g(Y_t)t↦g(Yt​) is integrable on the interval between 000 and hhh. Then

Dh(g,Y)=∫0hg(Yt) dt−h g(Y0).D_h(g,Y)=\int_0^hg(Y_t)\,dt-h\,g(Y_0).Dh​(g,Y)=∫0h​g(Yt​)dt−hg(Y0​).

Proof. A constant function is integrable on every bounded interval. By linearity of the integral of the two integrable functions t↦g(Yt)t\mapsto g(Y_t)t↦g(Yt​) and t↦g(Y0)t\mapsto g(Y_0)t↦g(Y0​),

03An exact step is an Euler step minus its defect §

Mathematical statement and proof

04Continuity gives integrability of the drift §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let HHH be a normed space, L≥0L\ge0L≥0, let g:H→Hg:H\to Hg:H→H be LLL-Lipschitz, i.e. ∥g(a)−g(b)∥≤L∥a−b∥\|g(a)-g(b)\|\le L\|a-b\|∥g(a)−g(b)∥≤L∥a−b∥, and let Y:R→HY:\mathbb R\to HY:R→H be continuous. Then for every h∈Rh\in\mathbb Rh∈R the function t↦g(Yt)t\mapsto g(Y_t)t↦g(Yt​) is integrable on the interval between 000 and hhh.

Proof. A Lipschitz map is continuous, so t↦g(Yt)t\mapsto g(Y_t)t↦g(Yt​) is continuous. On the compact interval III with endpoints 000 and hhh it is therefore bounded, and it is strongly measurable: its image of III is compact, hence separable, and the function is a uniform limit on III of step functions by uniform continuity. A bounded strongly measurable function on a set of finite measure is Bochner integrable. □\square□

In the application HHH is a space of square-integrable random vectors and continuity of YYY is continuity in the root-mean-square norm.
