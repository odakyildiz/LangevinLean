Couple ULA to an invariant Metropolis chain

A common Gaussian proposal couples unadjusted ULA with MALA. Under a stable small step, their expected distance grows by at most the Gibbs-average rejected displacement at each step. This is an auxiliary approximation for stationarity.

02Identify the two marginals §

Mathematical statement and proof

The accepted and rejected weights sum to one. Thus the first marginal of the coupled step is exactly the ULA step applied to the old first marginal. The second is exactly MALA applied to the old second marginal. The first marginal is a probability measure, so the whole coupled measure is one. Starting on the diagonal X0=Y0X_0=Y_0X0​=Y0​ and iterating gives first marginal PhnμP_h^n\muPhn​μ; if π\piπ is MALA invariant, the second marginal remains π\piπ at every time.

03A pointwise distance estimate §

Mathematical statement and proof

Strong convexity and h≤2/(m+L)h\le2/(m+L)h≤2/(m+L) make shared-noise proposals nonexpansive. On acceptance the distance is at most ∥x−y∥\|x-y\|∥x−y∥. On rejection, the triangle inequality adds only the MALA proposal displacement. Averaging these two alternatives yields

a∥ph(x,ξ)−ph(y,ξ)∥+(1−a)∥ph(x,ξ)−y∥≤∥x−y∥+Dh(y,ξ).a\|p_h(x,\xi)-p_h(y,\xi)\|+(1-a)\|p_h(x,\xi)-y\|\le\|x-y\|+D_h(y,\xi).a∥ph​(x,ξ)−ph​(y,ξ)∥+(1−a)∥ph​(x,ξ)−y∥≤∥x−y∥+Dh​(y,ξ).
