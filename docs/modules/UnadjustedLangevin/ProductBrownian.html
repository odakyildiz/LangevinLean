01Measure-preserving pullback preserves Brownian laws §

Mathematical statement and proof

If fff is measure preserving, composing every finite Brownian coordinate vector with fff preserves its law. The L2L^2L2 class of the pulled-back coordinate process is the measure-preserving composition of the original class: almost-everywhere representative equalities prove this. Linearity of composition then proves the analogous identity for normalized Euler noise.

02Recover the actual normalized source innovations §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define source innovations as representatives of the Brownian Euler L2L^2L2 classes. They automatically belong to L2L^2L2. Expanding scalar multiplication and subtraction of representatives shows each coordinate equals

Zn,i=Bi((n+1)h)−Bi(nh)halmost everywhere.Z_{n,i}=\frac{B_i((n+1)h)-B_i(nh)}{\sqrt h}\quad\text{almost everywhere}.Zn,i​=h​Bi​((n+1)h)−Bi​(nh)​almost everywhere.

The real/nonnegative-real versions check the time coercions. The normalized-increment law gives Zn,i∼N(0,1)Z_{n,i}\sim\mathcal N(0,1)Zn,i​∼N(0,1); coordinate grid-path independence and Brownian time-increment independence give joint independence over (n,i)(n,i)(n,i).

03Transfer innovations and independence to the product §

Mathematical statement and proof

The local product probability instance follows from total mass γ(H2)Q(S)=1\gamma(H^2)Q(S)=1γ(H2)Q(S)=1, since a coupling of a probability has mass one. Its noise projection is measure preserving, so the pulled-back coordinate processes remain pre-Brownian. The product innovation classes equal the Brownian Euler classes constructed directly on that product. Their scalar laws and coordinate-time independence transfer by pullback and almost-everywhere equality. Product independence makes both initial coordinates independent of the full innovation sequence, including after passing to their L2L^2L2 representatives.

04Assemble the analytic product reference §

Mathematical statement and proof

Strong convexity gives the Gibbs L2L^2L2 moment. With the displayed stationarity premise for the constructed segments, BrownianReference supplies every L2L^2L2 reference field: continuity, endpoint compatibility, integral equation, score norm and noise norm. The product-noise identity makes its innovations exactly the fixed source innovations. This provides a ProductReference for every finite initial coupling, and the product-reference theorem yields the small-step Dalalyan bound with exact initial W2W_2W2​. This intermediate endpoint still assumes stationarity.
