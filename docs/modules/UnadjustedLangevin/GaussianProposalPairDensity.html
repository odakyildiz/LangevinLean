Mathematical statement and proof

For X∼μX\sim\muX∼μ and independent Z∼N(0,I)Z\sim\mathcal N(0,I)Z∼N(0,I), fix xxx and apply the proved proposal-density identity to c(x)+2hZc(x)+\sqrt{2h}Zc(x)+2h​Z. It gives conditional integration against qh(c(x),y)dyq_h(c(x),y)dyqh​(c(x),y)dy.

To obtain equality of joint measures, evaluate a measurable set A⊂H×HA\subset H\times HA⊂H×H. Tonelli writes both sides as integrals over xxx of their yyy-sections. The fixed-center density theorem identifies each section, hence the measures agree. Measurability of ccc supplies measurability of the pair update and joint density; no probability or moment condition on μ\muμ is added.
