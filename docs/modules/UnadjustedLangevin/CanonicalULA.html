The two step-size regimes and the coefficient 1.651.651.65 follow Theorem 1(a)–(b) of Dalalyan and Karagulyan (2019). Dalalyan (2017), Theorem 1 and Section 6, gives the earlier synchronous-coupling argument.

Let U:Rd→RU:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb RU:Rd→R be differentiable and mmm-strongly convex with m>0m>0m>0, with LLL-Lipschitz gradient. Suppose the initial probability has finite W2W_2W2​ distance from Gibbs. Independent normalized Brownian increments give the Gaussian transition PhP_hPh​; the numerical marginal at index kkk is PhkμP_h^k\muPhk​μ.

For every finite-cost initial coupling, the product Brownian construction gives a stationary integral reference with the same innovations. Applying the mean-square comparison and taking the infimum of the initial coupling costs yields the Wasserstein estimate. In the small-step range its factor is 1−mh1-mh1−mh and its additive bound is (33/20)(L/m)hd(33/20)(L/m)\sqrt{hd}(33/20)(L/m)hd​. In the other stated stable range the factor is Lh−1Lh-1Lh−1 and the additive bound is (33/20)Lhhd/(2−Lh)(33/20)Lh\sqrt{hd}/(2-Lh)(33/20)Lhhd​/(2−Lh). The reference existence and stationarity used in these estimates are proved from the stated potential assumptions.

01The output law of the product construction §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Brownian source provides jointly independent standard Gaussian innovations; the product extension makes initialization independent of the entire sequence. The realization/Markov-law theorem identifies its output with PhkμP_h^k\muPhk​μ. The product output-law identity removes the chosen finite coupling from this law equality.

The Brownian-generated innovations have the full iid standard vector Gaussian law. Their product construction makes the initial coordinate independent of the complete path. Thus the measurable recursion has law PhkμP_h^k\muPhk​μ. The product output definition removes the auxiliary reference coordinate, showing this identity is common to every finite initial coupling and depends only on the prescribed initial marginal.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

productBrownianOutput_eq_ulaLawStep puts the finite-cost coupling on the independent extension. W is the lifted Euler innovation and hW its measurability. hcoord and hind provide its scalar Gaussian laws and independence. product_chain_outputLaw identifies the product construction with its square-integrable recursion; hilbert_lpChain_law_eq_ulaLawStep identifies that recursion with the Gaussian transition iterate. The final rewrite initialExtension_lpInitial_law restores the prescribed initial marginal. The resulting law no longer contains the chosen auxiliary coupling.

02A product reference from independent Brownian coordinates §

Mathematical statement and proof

On each finite-coupling extension, pull back the independent Brownian coordinates and use the product independence theorems for the reference initialization. Construct a global L2L^2L2 integral solution over the required finite horizon.

The proved MALA/Euler stationarity theorem gives Gibbs law for that global solution at every needed absolute time. Segment/global uniqueness identifies each recursively constructed reference segment with that solution. Consequently the reference segments on the independent initial-coupling and Brownian product space have Gibbs law, and all reference hypotheses follow from the stated source and potential assumptions.

For each finite coupling, solve the global Langevin integral equation from the target-distributed reference coordinate with independent Brownian forcing. The invariant-approximation argument proves its Gibbs law at every fixed time. Uniqueness then identifies the recursively chosen local segments with this global solution on their intervals. Hence all segment target-law premises are proved for the references used in the coupling family.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

productReference_of_independent_brownian starts with productReference_of_constructed_brownian and proves its remaining segment-law obligation. For an arbitrary initial coupling, W pulls Brownian coordinates to the product extension and X is its target-distributed initial reference. The global integral solution comes from exists_lp_langevin_integral_of_brownian; brownianSegments_eq_global identifies each segment with that solution. The claims hscalar, hind and hinitial provide exactly the independence and initial-law inputs of lp_langevin_gibbs_stationary, which proves the required stationary segment law.

03The innovations of the fixed Brownian construction §

Mathematical statement and proof

Specialize the source Euler noise to the explicitly constructed independent coordinate Brownian source. This fixes the same standard Gaussian innovation sequence for every initial coupling in the law theorem.

Fix the explicitly constructed independent Brownian coordinate source once and normalize its grid increments. For any h>0h>0h>0, the resulting coordinates are standard Gaussian and independent over step and coordinate. The innovation sequence is thus fixed independently of the input law and initial coupling.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

canonicalEulerNoise specializes sourceEulerNoise to CanonicalBrownian.vectorProbability and the proved coordinate Brownian processes. It is an abbreviation, so its value is the previously defined normalized grid-increment function. The positive-step Gaussian laws are supplied by the corresponding source-noise theorems; they are not extra fields hidden in this definition.

04The reference on the independent product space §

Mathematical statement and proof

The independent Brownian construction supplies the coordinate Brownian laws and independence of their grid paths for every positive step. Apply the product reference with Brownian noise independent of the initial pair. The reference existence and stationary law are therefore consequences of the hypotheses.

The independent Brownian source supplies its Brownian finite-dimensional laws and full-path coordinate independence. Product extension supplies independence from the initial coupling. The stationary integral solution and segment uniqueness then provide all reference target laws. Thus the analytic reference family is obtained entirely from the potential and initial transport assumptions, because existence and invariance have been proved.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

canonicalProductReference applies productReference_of_independent_brownian to the explicit Brownian coordinate family. CanonicalBrownian.coordinate_isPreBrownian supplies each coordinate law and CanonicalBrownian.coordinate_grid_paths_independent supplies independence on every positive grid. productGibbsMemLp provides the target second moment from the potential assumptions. The returned reference therefore includes its proved stationarity and moment fields.

05The output law equals the Gaussian transition iterate §

Mathematical statement and proof

Finite initial transport provides a finite-cost coupling, and Gibbs has a finite second moment. Apply the general Brownian output-law identity on that extension with the independent Brownian source. It proves that the product construction has ULA law PhkμP_h^k\muPhk​μ.

Finite initial transport supplies a finite-cost coupling, and target Gaussian tails supply the reference second moment needed on its extension. The innovation law theorem identifies the numerical output with PhkμP_h^k\muPhk​μ. Since the output is common to all such coupling extensions, this identity does not retain any chosen coupling or auxiliary sample-space information.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

canonicalOutput_eq_ulaLawStep uses exists_finiteCost_coupling to choose an arbitrary finite-cost initial coupling. It then applies productBrownianOutput_eq_ulaLawStep to the canonical Brownian coordinates, using their grid-path independence and the proved Gibbs second moment. This is an existence-based law identification; optimality of the chosen coupling is unnecessary.

06Finiteness of the output transport distance §

Mathematical statement and proof

The constructed product reference supplies a finite-cost output coupling for every iteration whenever initial transport is finite. Its output law is PhkμP_h^k\muPhk​μ. Substituting this law proves W2(Phkμ,πU)<∞W_2(P_h^k\mu,\pi_U)<\inftyW2​(Phk​μ,πU​)<∞, so the Wasserstein distances in the estimates are finite real numbers.

The constructed numerical and stationary reference states lie in L2L^2L2 on any one finite-cost extension. Their joint law is therefore a finite-cost coupling at every iteration. The numerical marginal is PhkμP_h^k\muPhk​μ and the reference marginal is πU\pi_UπU​. This proves output transport finiteness before passing to real-valued distances in the quantitative convergence bounds.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

dalalyan_output_finite invokes the output_finite field of canonicalProductReference with the finite initial transport premise. canonicalOutput_eq_ulaLawStep then rewrites that finite output as the ordinary Gaussian transition iterate. This proves finiteness before the real-valued distance is used in quantitative estimates.

07The small-step Wasserstein bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construct the product reference from differentiability, mmm-strong convexity, and LLL-Lipschitz gradient. Nontriviality of the finite coordinate space proves m≤Lm\le Lm≤L; the finite-coupling product theorem gives the small-step bound with bias (33/20)(L/m)hd(33/20)(L/m)\sqrt{hd}(33/20)(L/m)hd​.

Replace the product output by PhkμP_h^k\muPhk​μ using the identification of the product-space chain law with the iterated Gaussian transition. The resulting inequality applies to the Gaussian ULA laws. Their transport finiteness and the existence of the stationary reference have already been proved.

Apply the synchronous finite-coupling estimate to the fully constructed reference family, then take the initial cost infimum. The small-step factor is q=1−mhq=1-mhq=1−mh, with gap mh>0mh>0mh>0. The local forcing (33/20)Lhhd(33/20)Lh\sqrt{hd}(33/20)Lhhd​ divided by that gap gives (33/20)(L/m)hd(33/20)(L/m)\sqrt{hd}(33/20)(L/m)hd​. Rewriting the common output as the Gaussian transition iterate proves the stated bound for these probability laws.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

dalalyan obtains hb from dalalyan_small_step_of_product_reference. strongConvex_le_lipschitz supplies the order of curvature and smoothness in the nonempty coordinate space. The target moment, canonical innovations and reference are all constructed arguments of that theorem. The last rewrite canonicalOutput_eq_ulaLawStep changes the auxiliary output law into the stated Gaussian iterate. Thus the theorem retains only the potential, initial-law, finite-transport and positive stable-step assumptions.

08The large-step Wasserstein bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

Use the same constructed reference in the large-step product theorem. The step assumptions give 0≤Lh−1<10\le Lh-1<10≤Lh−1<1 and 2−Lh>02-Lh>02−Lh>0, so the bias is (33/20)Lhhd/(2−Lh)(33/20)Lh\sqrt{hd}/(2-Lh)(33/20)Lhhd​/(2−Lh). Rewrite its output law as the iterated Gaussian ULA law.

The same stationary reference and local defect estimate also apply in the second stable step regime. Here the deterministic factor is q=Lh−1q=Lh-1q=Lh−1, so the positive gap is 2−Lh2-Lh2−Lh. Geometric summation gives bias (33/20)Lhhd/(2−Lh)(33/20)Lh\sqrt{hd}/(2-Lh)(33/20)Lhhd​/(2−Lh). The output-law identity transfers this inequality to the Gaussian transition iterates without changing either the target or initial Wasserstein distance.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

dalalyan_large_step uses the same constructed target moment, innovation sequence and stationary reference as the small-step result. It applies dalalyan_large_step_of_product_reference with the lower threshold hlarge and strict upper stability premise hstep. The latter makes the denominator in the bias positive. Rewriting with canonicalOutput_eq_ulaLawStep identifies its output with the Gaussian transition iterate.
