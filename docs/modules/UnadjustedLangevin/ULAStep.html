02One unadjusted Langevin step §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. For U:Ed→RU:E_d\to\mathbb RU:Ed​→R, h∈Rh\in\mathbb Rh∈R and x,z∈Edx,z\in E_dx,z∈Ed​, the ULA update is

Thz(x)=x−h∇U(x)+2h z,T_h^z(x)=x-h\nabla U(x)+\sqrt{2h}\,z,Thz​(x)=x−h∇U(x)+2h​z,

where 2h=0\sqrt{2h}=02h​=0 if h<0h<0h<0.

The update consists of the gradient step x−h∇U(x)x-h\nabla U(x)x−h∇U(x) and the noise increment 2h z\sqrt{2h}\,z2h​z. The definition is a deterministic function of (x,z)(x,z)(x,z); probabilistic statements arise only when xxx and zzz are replaced by random vectors. No sign condition on hhh is imposed here; the estimates below assume h≥0h\ge0h≥0.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

ulaUpdate U h x z is defined as x - h • gradient U x + Real.sqrt (2 * h) • z; • is scalar multiplication and Real.sqrt is 000 on negative arguments. gradient U x is the Riesz representative of the derivative of UUU at xxx; Mathlib sets it to 000 at points where UUU is not differentiable, but every theorem below assumes that UUU is differentiable, so this convention never intervenes. The definition is outside the namespace ULA.

03Shared noise cancels §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For every U:Ed→RU:E_d\to\mathbb RU:Ed​→R, h∈Rh\in\mathbb Rh∈R and x,y,z∈Edx,y,z\in E_dx,y,z∈Ed​,

Thz(x)−Thz(y)=(x−h∇U(x))−(y−h∇U(y)).T_h^z(x)-T_h^z(y)=\bigl(x-h\nabla U(x)\bigr)-\bigl(y-h\nabla U(y)\bigr).Thz​(x)−Thz​(y)=(x−h∇U(x))−(y−h∇U(y)).

Proof. With a=x−h∇U(x)a=x-h\nabla U(x)a=x−h∇U(x), b=y−h∇U(y)b=y-h\nabla U(y)b=y−h∇U(y) and c=2h zc=\sqrt{2h}\,zc=2h​z, the two updates are a+ca+ca+c and b+cb+cb+c, and (a+c)−(b+c)=a−b(a+c)-(b+c)=a-b(a+c)−(b+c)=a−b in any vector space. □\square□

The cancellation requires the same vector zzz in both updates. With two different noise vectors z,z′z,z'z,z′, the additional term 2h(z−z′)\sqrt{2h}(z-z')2h​(z−z′) would remain.

04The shared-noise estimate from strong monotonicity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let U:Ed→RU:E_d\to\mathbb RU:Ed​→R be differentiable at every point and mmm-strongly convex, where m∈Rm\in\mathbb Rm∈R, and let ∇U\nabla U∇U be LLL-Lipschitz with L≥0L\ge0L≥0. Let h≥0h\ge0h≥0. Then for all x,y,z∈Edx,y,z\in E_dx,y,z∈Ed​,

∥Thz(x)−Thz(y)∥≤1−2hm+h2L2  ∥x−y∥.\|T_h^z(x)-T_h^z(y)\|\le\sqrt{1-2hm+h^2L^2}\;\|x-y\|.∥Thz​(x)−Thz​(y)∥≤1−2hm+h2L2​∥x−y∥.

Proof. By the previous step, the left-hand side equals ∥(x−h∇U(x))−(y−h∇U(y))∥\|(x-h\nabla U(x))-(y-h\nabla U(y))\|∥(x−h∇U(x))−(y−h∇U(y))∥. Apply the square-root Euler-step bound of the previous section with gx=∇U(x)g_x=\nabla U(x)gx​=∇U(x) and gy=∇U(y)g_y=\nabla U(y)gy​=∇U(y). Its two hypotheses hold: m∥x−y∥2≤⟨∇U(x)−∇U(y),x−y⟩m\|x-y\|^2\le\langle\nabla U(x)-\nabla U(y),x-y\ranglem∥x−y∥2≤⟨∇U(x)−∇U(y),x−y⟩ is the strong monotonicity of the gradient, proved for differentiable mmm-strongly convex functions on the Hilbert space EdE_dEd​; and ∥∇U(x)−∇U(y)∥≤L∥x−y∥\|\nabla U(x)-\nabla U(y)\|\le L\|x-y\|∥∇U(x)−∇U(y)∥≤L∥x−y∥ is the Lipschitz assumption. □\square□

The factor is less than one exactly when h(hL2−2m)<0h(hL^2-2m)<0h(hL2−2m)<0, that is, when h>0h>0h>0 and hL2<2mhL^2<2mhL2<2m. The sharp contraction of the next section improves it: if 0<m≤L0<m\le L0<m≤L, then (1−mh)2≤1−2hm+h2L2(1-mh)^2\le1-2hm+h^2L^2(1−mh)2≤1−2hm+h2L2 and (1−Lh)2≤1−2hm+h2L2(1-Lh)^2\le1-2hm+h^2L^2(1−Lh)2≤1−2hm+h2L2, because m2≤L2m^2\le L^2m2≤L2 and −2hL≤−2hm-2hL\le-2hm−2hL≤−2hm, so the sharp factor max⁡{∣1−mh∣,∣1−Lh∣}\max\{|1-mh|,|1-Lh|\}max{∣1−mh∣,∣1−Lh∣} is never larger.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

ula_shared_noise_contract takes hdiff : Differentiable ℝ U, hsc : StrongConvexOn Set.univ m U, hsm : LipschitzWith L (gradient U) and hh : 0 ≤ h. After rw [ulaUpdate_sub] (the noise cancellation of the previous step), the goal is closed by ULA.gradient_step_contract applied to gradient U x and gradient U y. Its monotonicity hypothesis is ULA.strong_gradient_monotone hdiff hsc x y; its Lipschitz hypothesis is hsm.dist_le_mul x y, with distances rewritten as norms of differences by dist_eq_norm.
