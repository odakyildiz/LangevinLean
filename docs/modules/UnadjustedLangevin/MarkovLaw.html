Identify recursive sampling laws with iterated Markov transitions

The path-space law of an iid-noise recursion agrees with the repeated one-step transition μ↦F#(μ⊗η)\mu\mapsto F_\#(\mu\otimes\eta)μ↦F#​(μ⊗η). The proof explicitly establishes independence of the current state and the fresh innovation.

Statement and proof

01Only the finite prefix affects the current state §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define the state after nnn updates using only a vector of nnn innovations. Induction proves equality with the full-path recursion and measurability of this finite-prefix map. Product-coordinate independence makes (Z0,…,Zn−1)(Z_0,\ldots,Z_{n-1})(Z0​,…,Zn−1​) independent of ZnZ_nZn​. This is the probabilistic fact needed to justify the Markov transition formula, rather than just an informal reading of the recursion.

02Derive the one-step law identity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define Kμ=F#(μ⊗η)K\mu=F_\#(\mu\otimes\eta)Kμ=F#​(μ⊗η), μ0=μ\mu_0=\muμ0​=μ, μn+1=Kμn\mu_{n+1}=K\mu_nμn+1​=Kμn​. The independent initialization and finite-prefix/fresh independence give Law⁡(Xn,Zn)=Law⁡(Xn)⊗η\operatorname{Law}(X_n,Z_n)=\operatorname{Law}(X_n)\otimes\etaLaw(Xn​,Zn​)=Law(Xn​)⊗η. Push through FFF to derive chainLaw⁡n+1=K(chainLaw⁡n)\operatorname{chainLaw}_{n+1}=K(\operatorname{chainLaw}_n)chainLawn+1​=K(chainLawn​). Starting at the common initial law and inducting gives equality with the iterated Markov laws.

03Preserve probability and transfer to an arbitrary source §

Mathematical statement and proof

A product of probability measures has total mass one, and a measurable pushforward preserves that mass. Thus every KμK\muKμ and every iterated law is a probability measure. The realization theorem from ChainLaw then identifies a recursion on any probability space with these same iterated laws, under iid noise and independence of its initialization.
