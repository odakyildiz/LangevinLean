Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let L≥0L\ge0L≥0 and let U:H→RU:H\to\mathbb RU:H→R be differentiable with LLL-Lipschitz gradient. Then for all x,v∈Hx,v\in Hx,v∈H,

Proof. A differentiable function with LLL-Lipschitz gradient satisfies the descent inequality ULA-SC, Section 3.9.1
