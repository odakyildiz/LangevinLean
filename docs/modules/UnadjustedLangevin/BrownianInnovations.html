Normalized Brownian increments are independent standard Gaussian innovations

Let (Bt)t≥0(B_t)_{t\ge0}(Bt​)t≥0​ be a pre-Brownian motion on a measure space (Ω,F,P)(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)(Ω,F,P): for every finite set F⊆[0,∞)F\subseteq[0,\infty)F⊆[0,∞) the vector (Bt)t∈F(B_t)_{t\in F}(Bt​)t∈F​ has the centred Gaussian law ΓF\Gamma_FΓF​ with covariance matrix (min⁡(s,t))s,t∈F(\min(s,t))_{s,t\in F}(min(s,t))s,t∈F​ (see ULA-SC, Section 3.8.15). For a step h>0h>0h>0, the normalized increments over the time grid {nh:n∈N}\{nh:n\in\mathbb N\}{nh:n∈N} are

ξn=B(n+1)h−Bnhh,n∈N.\xi_n=\frac{B_{(n+1)h}-B_{nh}}{\sqrt h},\qquad n\in\mathbb N.ξn​=h​B(n+1)h​−Bnh​​,n∈N.

We prove that each ξn\xi_nξn​ has law N(0,1)\mathcal N(0,1)N(0,1), is square integrable with ∫ξn2 dP=1\int\xi_n^2\,dP=1∫ξn2​dP=1, and that (ξn)n∈N(\xi_n)_{n\in\mathbb N}(ξn​)n∈N​ is an independent family. For a family (B(i))i∈ι(B^{(i)})_{i\in\iota}(B(i))i∈ι​ of pre-Brownian motions whose grid paths (Bnh(i))n∈N(B^{(i)}_{nh})_{n\in\mathbb N}(Bnh(i)​)n∈N​ are independent over iii, the whole array (ξn(i))(i,n)∈ι×N(\xi^{(i)}_n)_{(i,n)\in\iota\times\mathbb N}(ξn(i)​)(i,n)∈ι×N​ is independent, and for countable ι\iotaι its joint law is the product measure ⨂(i,n)∈ι×NN(0,1)\bigotimes_{(i,n)\in\iota\times\mathbb N}\mathcal N(0,1)⨂(i,n)∈ι×N​N(0,1).

The proof of joint independence combines independence across coordinates with independence across time within each coordinate. A general lemma on arrays of random variables, proved here, shows that these two kinds of independence together give independence of the whole array. These results identify the Brownian increments that drive an Euler scheme with the independent standard Gaussian innovations of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm.
