02normalizedBrownianIncrement hasLaw §

Mathematical statement and proof

A Brownian increment over an interval of length hhh is N(0,h)\mathcal N(0,h)N(0,h). Scaling by 1/h1/\sqrt h1/h​ multiplies its variance by 1/h1/h1/h, producing N(0,1)\mathcal N(0,1)N(0,1). 03normalizedBrownianIncrement memLp §

04normalizedBrownianIncrement second moment §

Mathematical statement and proof

Transfer the integral of x2x^2x2 along the standard Gaussian law of ξn\xi_nξn​. Its mean is zero and variance one, hence Eξn2=1\mathbb E\xi_n^2=1Eξn2​=1. This is an exact second-moment identity for the normalized increment.

05normalizedBrownianIncrement independent §

Mathematical statement and proof

Brownian increments on the consecutive grid intervals are independent. Normalizing each by the same deterministic scalar is a coordinatewise measurable transformation and preserves independence. The formulation permits h=0h=0h=0, where every increment is the constant zero and the independence assertion remains valid.

06iIndepFun uncurry nat of aemeasurable §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume the row-valued random maps (Xi,n)n(X_{i,n})_n(Xi,n​)n​ are independent and each row is internally independent. Replace the almost-everywhere measurable coordinates by measurable versions; countability of N\mathbb NN lets all replacements in a row hold on one full-measure set. Apply independence of independent families, then transfer the result back across the coordinatewise almost-everywhere equalities.

07normalizedBrownianIncrements independent §

Mathematical statement and proof

Each grid increment depends measurably on two entries of its coordinate grid path. Independence of those paths gives independence across coordinate rows, while Brownian independent increments gives independence within each row. Apply iIndepFun_uncurry_nat_of_aemeasurable to obtain joint independence of all (i,n)(i,n)(i,n) innovations.

08normalizedBrownianIncrements independent of paths §

Mathematical statement and proof

Restrict each independent full coordinate path to the grid nhnhnh, preserving independence under composition. The preceding theorem then yields joint coordinate-time independence of the normalized increments. This avoids requiring a separate measurable map into an uncountable path space.

09The entire innovation law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Every coordinate-time innovation has law N(0,1)\mathcal N(0,1)N(0,1), and the family is jointly independent. The countable product-law theorem therefore identifies the whole innovation map with ⨂(i,n)N(0,1)\bigotimes_{(i,n)}\mathcal N(0,1)⨂(i,n)​N(0,1). Countability supplies measurability of the path-valued map from coordinatewise almost-everywhere measurability.
