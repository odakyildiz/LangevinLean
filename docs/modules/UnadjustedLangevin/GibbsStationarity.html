Prove Gibbs stationarity by refining invariant Metropolis chains

A Brownian Langevin integral solution started from Gibbs stays Gibbs. The proof uses convergent Euler approximations and exactly invariant MALA chains, with no weak-PDE uniqueness assumption.

01hilbert lpChain coe §

Mathematical statement and proof

Induct over the chain recursion. The representative of the lifted gradient equals the pointwise gradient almost everywhere, and the representative formulas for addition and scalar multiplication give the same Euler successor. Intersect the finite set of null-set complements needed at each index to identify the L2L^2L2 chain with the ordinary recursion.
