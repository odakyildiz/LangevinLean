Characteristic functions are continuous in L²

At each fixed frequency, the characteristic function of the law of a real L2L^2L2 variable depends continuously on that variable. Consequently centered Gaussian laws are closed under L2L^2L2 convergence, with variance given by the squared norm.

02Pass Gaussian identities to the limit §

Mathematical statement and proof

Lipschitz composition and the continuous L2L^2L2 inner product make X↦Law⁡(X)^(t)X\mapsto\widehat{\operatorname{Law}(X)}(t)X↦Law(X)​(t) continuous. The Gaussian characteristic function e−∥X∥22t2/2e^{-\|X\|_2^2t^2/2}e−∥X∥22​t2/2 is also continuous in XXX. If a convergent family is eventually centered Gaussian with variance ∥Xi∥22\|X_i\|_2^2∥Xi​∥22​, its two characteristic functions agree eventually. Uniqueness of limits gives agreement at the limit for every ttt, and uniqueness of characteristic functions identifies the limiting Gaussian law. The everywhere-Gaussian version supplies the eventual premise automatically.
