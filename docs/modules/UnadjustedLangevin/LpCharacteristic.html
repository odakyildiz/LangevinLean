Characteristic functions are continuous in L²

References and publication status for this development

Paper references

The two step-size regimes and the coefficient 1.65 follow Theorem 1(a)–(b) of Dalalyan and Karagulyan (2019). Dalalyan (2017), Theorem 1 and Section 6, gives the earlier synchronous-coupling argument.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv
  • Arnak Dalalyan (2017). Further and stronger analogy between sampling and optimization: Langevin Monte Carlo and gradient descent. Proceedings of the 2017 Conference on Learning Theory, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 65, 678–689.

At each fixed frequency, the characteristic function of the law of a real L2L^2L2 variable depends continuously on that variable. Consequently centered Gaussian laws are closed under L2L^2L2 convergence, with variance given by the squared norm.

Mathematical statement and proof

04characteristicLp §

05characteristicLp coe §

Mathematical statement and proof

Combine the almost-everywhere representative identities for lifted composition, L2L^2L2 addition, and the constant class. Their intersection has full measure; there the representative is (eitX−1)+1=eitX(e^{itX}-1)+1=e^{itX}(eitX−1)+1=eitX. This connects quotient-space operations with the random function used in the characteristic integral.

07continuous charFun map Lp §

Mathematical statement and proof

Lipschitz composition is continuous on L2L^2L2; adding the constant one preserves continuity. Pairing with one is a continuous linear functional. The preceding integral identity rewrites their composition as X↦PX^(t)X\mapsto\widehat{P_X}(t)X↦PX​​(t), proving continuity of the actual-law characteristic function at every fixed frequency.

09hasLaw gaussianReal of tendsto Lp eventually §

Mathematical statement and proof

At each frequency, L2L^2L2 convergence makes the actual characteristic functions converge. It also makes the centered Gaussian characteristic functions with variances ∥Xi∥22\|X_i\|_2^2∥Xi​∥22​ converge to variance ∥Y∥22\|Y\|_2^2∥Y∥22​. Eventual Gaussianity identifies the two nets eventually, so uniqueness of limits identifies their limits. Measure.ext_of_charFun then gives the claimed Gaussian law of YYY; a nontrivial filter is required for uniqueness.
