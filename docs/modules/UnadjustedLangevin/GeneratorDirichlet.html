01The derivative of a weighted test §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let U,φ:H→RU,\varphi:H\to\mathbb RU,φ:H→R be differentiable. Then for all x,v∈Hx,v\in Hx,v∈H,

∂v(e−Uφ)(x)=e−U(x)(∂vφ(x)−⟨∇U(x),v⟩ φ(x)).\partial_v\bigl(e^{-U}\varphi\bigr)(x)=e^{-U(x)}\Bigl(\partial_v\varphi(x)-\langle\nabla U(x),v\rangle\,\varphi(x)\Bigr).∂v​(e−Uφ)(x)=e−U(x)(∂v​φ(x)−⟨∇U(x),v⟩φ(x)).

Proof. By the chain rule, D(e−U)(x)=−e−U(x)DU(x)D(e^{-U})(x)=-e^{-U(x)}DU(x)D(e−U)(x)=−e−U(x)DU(x). By the product rule,

D(e−Uφ)(x)=e−U(x)Dφ(x)+φ(x) D(e−U)(x)=e−U(x)Dφ(x)−φ(x)e−U(x)DU(x).D\bigl(e^{-U}\varphi\bigr)(x)=e^{-U(x)}D\varphi(x)+\varphi(x)\,D(e^{-U})(x)=e^{-U(x)}D\varphi(x)-\varphi(x)e^{-U(x)}DU(x).D(e−Uφ)(x)=e−U(x)Dφ(x)+φ(x)D(e−U)(x)=e−U(x)Dφ(x)−φ(x)e−U(x)DU(x).

Applying this linear map to vvv, using DU(x)v=⟨∇U(x),v⟩DU(x)v=\langle\nabla U(x),v\rangleDU(x)v=⟨∇U(x),v⟩ and factoring out e−U(x)e^{-U(x)}e−U(x) gives the claim. □\square□

02Weighted directional integration by parts §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let L≥0L\ge0L≥0, let U:H→RU:H\to\mathbb RU:H→R be differentiable with LLL-Lipschitz gradient, let φ,ψ:H→R\varphi,\psi:H\to\mathbb Rφ,ψ:H→R be twice continuously differentiable with compact support, and let v∈Hv\in Hv∈H. Then

∫He−Uφ Lvψ dx=−∫He−U ∂vφ ∂vψ dx.\int_He^{-U}\varphi\,\mathcal L_v\psi\,dx=-\int_He^{-U}\,\partial_v\varphi\,\partial_v\psi\,dx.∫H​e−UφLv​ψdx=−∫H​e−U∂v​φ∂v​ψdx.

Proof. Put a=e−Uφa=e^{-U}\varphia=e−Uφ and g=∂vψg=\partial_v\psig=∂v​ψ.

Regularity and integrability. As in the section on the generator, ggg is continuously differentiable with compact support, ∂vg\partial_vg∂v​g and ∂vφ\partial_v\varphi∂v​φ are continuous, aaa is continuous with compact support, and by the previous step ∂va=e−U(∂vφ−⟨∇U,v⟩φ)\partial_va=e^{-U}(\partial_v\varphi-\langle\nabla U,v\rangle\varphi)∂v​a=e−U(∂v​φ−⟨∇U,v⟩φ), which is continuous because ∇U\nabla U∇U is continuous. The functions (∂va)g(\partial_va)g(∂v​a)g, a ∂vga\,\partial_vga∂v​g, agagag, e−Uφ Lvψe^{-U}\varphi\,\mathcal L_v\psie−UφLv​ψ and e−U∂vφ ∂vψe^{-U}\partial_v\varphi\,\partial_v\psie−U∂v​φ∂v​ψ are continuous and vanish outside the support of φ\varphiφ or of ψ\psiψ, so they are Lebesgue integrable.

Integration by parts. The integration by parts formula along vvv (proved in the section on the generator ULA-SC, Section 3.46.6) gives

∫Ha ∂vg dx=−∫H(∂va) g dx.(1)\int_Ha\,\partial_vg\,dx=-\int_H(\partial_va)\,g\,dx.\qquad(1)∫H​a∂v​gdx=−∫H​(∂v​a)gdx.(1)

Algebra. By the previous step and the definition Lvψ=∂vg−⟨∇U,v⟩g\mathcal L_v\psi=\partial_vg-\langle\nabla U,v\rangle gLv​ψ=∂v​g−⟨∇U,v⟩g,

a ∂vg+(∂va) g=e−Uφ ∂vg+e−U(∂vφ−⟨∇U,v⟩φ)g=e−Uφ Lvψ+e−U∂vφ ∂vψ.a\,\partial_vg+(\partial_va)\,g=e^{-U}\varphi\,\partial_vg+e^{-U}\bigl(\partial_v\varphi-\langle\nabla U,v\rangle\varphi\bigr)g=e^{-U}\varphi\,\mathcal L_v\psi+e^{-U}\partial_v\varphi\,\partial_v\psi.a∂v​g+(∂v​a)g=e−Uφ∂v​g+e−U(∂v​φ−⟨∇U,v⟩φ)g=e−UφLv​ψ+e−U∂v​φ∂v​ψ.

Integrating, using linearity for the integrable summands and then (1),

∫He−Uφ Lvψ dx+∫He−U∂vφ ∂vψ dx=∫Ha ∂vg dx+∫H(∂va) g dx=0,\int_He^{-U}\varphi\,\mathcal L_v\psi\,dx+\int_He^{-U}\partial_v\varphi\,\partial_v\psi\,dx=\int_Ha\,\partial_vg\,dx+\int_H(\partial_va)\,g\,dx=0,∫H​e−UφLv​ψdx+∫H​e−U∂v​φ∂v​ψdx=∫H​a∂v​gdx+∫H​(∂v​a)gdx=0,

which is the claim. □\square□

Mathematical statement and proof

∫Hφ Lvψ dπU=−∫H∂vφ ∂vψ dπU.\int_H\varphi\,\mathcal L_v\psi\,d\pi_U=-\int_H\partial_v\varphi\,\partial_v\psi\,d\pi_U.∫H​φLv​ψdπU​=−∫H​∂v​φ∂v​ψdπU​.

Proof. By the weighted Gibbs integral formula ULA-SC, Section 3.46.3, each side is ZU−1Z_U^{-1}ZU−1​ times the corresponding Lebesgue integral with weight e−Ue^{-U}e−U. By the previous step,

∫φ Lvψ dπU=ZU−1∫e−Uφ Lvψ dx=ZU−1(−∫e−U∂vφ ∂vψ dx)=−∫∂vφ ∂vψ dπU.□\int\varphi\,\mathcal L_v\psi\,d\pi_U=Z_U^{-1}\int e^{-U}\varphi\,\mathcal L_v\psi\,dx=Z_U^{-1}\Bigl(-\int e^{-U}\partial_v\varphi\,\partial_v\psi\,dx\Bigr)=-\int\partial_v\varphi\,\partial_v\psi\,d\pi_U.\qquad\square∫φLv​ψdπU​=ZU−1​∫e−UφLv​ψdx=ZU−1​(−∫e−U∂v​φ∂v​ψdx)=−∫∂v​φ∂v​ψdπU​.□

07Symmetry of the generator §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Under the hypotheses of the previous step,

∫Hφ Lψ dπU=∫Hψ Lφ dπU.\int_H\varphi\,\mathcal L\psi\,d\pi_U=\int_H\psi\,\mathcal L\varphi\,d\pi_U.∫H​φLψdπU​=∫H​ψLφdπU​.

Proof. Apply the Dirichlet identity to (φ,ψ)(\varphi,\psi)(φ,ψ) and to (ψ,φ)(\psi,\varphi)(ψ,φ): the two sides equal −∫⟨∇φ,∇ψ⟩ dπU-\int\langle\nabla\varphi,\nabla\psi\rangle\,d\pi_U−∫⟨∇φ,∇ψ⟩dπU​ and −∫⟨∇ψ,∇φ⟩ dπU-\int\langle\nabla\psi,\nabla\varphi\rangle\,d\pi_U−∫⟨∇ψ,∇φ⟩dπU​, which agree because the real inner product is symmetric. □\square□

Thus L\mathcal LL is a symmetric operator in L2(πU)L^2(\pi_U)L2(πU​) on twice continuously differentiable functions with compact support.

08The Dirichlet energy as a quadratic form §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let L≥0L\ge0L≥0, let U:H→RU:H\to\mathbb RU:H→R be differentiable with LLL-Lipschitz gradient, and let φ\varphiφ be twice continuously differentiable with compact support. Then
