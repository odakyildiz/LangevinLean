Gibbs Dirichlet form, symmetry and dissipation

For compactly supported C2C^2C2 tests φ,ψ\varphi,\psiφ,ψ, the generator obeys ∫φLψ dπ=−∫⟨∇φ,∇ψ⟩dπ\int\varphi\mathcal L\psi\,d\pi=-\int\langle\nabla\varphi,\nabla\psi\rangle d\pi∫φLψdπ=−∫⟨∇φ,∇ψ⟩dπ. This provides the symmetric Dirichlet form at the test-function level.

01The weighted product derivative §

Mathematical statement and proof

The product and chain rules give Dv(e−Uφ)=e−U(Dvφ−⟨∇U,v⟩φ)D_v(e^{-U}\varphi)=e^{-U}(D_v\varphi-\langle\nabla U,v\rangle\varphi)Dv​(e−Uφ)=e−U(Dv​φ−⟨∇U,v⟩φ). Integrate this derivative against DvψD_v\psiDv​ψ, using compact support to justify integration by parts. The drift terms cancel, leaving

∫e−UφLvψ dx=−∫e−UDvφDvψ dx.\int e^{-U}\varphi\mathcal L_v\psi\,dx=-\int e^{-U}D_v\varphi D_v\psi\,dx.∫e−UφLv​ψdx=−∫e−UDv​φDv​ψdx.

The Gibbs integration formula divides both sides by the same partition function.

02Sum the directional forms §

Mathematical statement and proof

Continuity and compact support make each product DvφDvψD_v\varphi D_v\psiDv​φDv​ψ and φLvψ\varphi\mathcal L_v\psiφLv​ψ integrable; for the integrability lemmas it is enough that φ\varphiφ has compact support. Sum over an orthonormal basis and use Parseval to identify ∑iDeiφDeiψ=⟨∇φ,∇ψ⟩\sum_iD_{e_i}\varphi D_{e_i}\psi=\langle\nabla\varphi,\nabla\psi\rangle∑i​Dei​​φDei​​ψ=⟨∇φ,∇ψ⟩. For the integration-by-parts identity both tests carry the stated compact-support hypotheses.

03Symmetry and nonpositive energy §

Mathematical statement and proof

The inner product is symmetric, so interchanging φ\varphiφ and ψ\psiψ leaves ∫φLψ dπ\int\varphi\mathcal L\psi\,d\pi∫φLψdπ unchanged. Setting ψ=φ\psi=\varphiψ=φ gives ∫φLφ dπ=−∫∥∇φ∥2dπ≤0\int\varphi\mathcal L\varphi\,d\pi=-\int\|\nabla\varphi\|^2d\pi\le0∫φLφdπ=−∫∥∇φ∥2dπ≤0. Positivity of the squared norm supplies the last inequality.
