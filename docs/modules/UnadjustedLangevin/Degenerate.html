Zero-dimensional state spaces have zero transport cost

On a subsingleton state space every two states coincide, so every coupling has zero quadratic difference cost. Probability laws on such a space therefore have zero Wasserstein distance.

Mathematical statement and proof

Subsingleton equality gives x−y=0x-y=0x−y=0 for every pair, so the L2L^2L2 coupling cost is zero for every joint measure. For two probability measures, their product is a coupling; its zero cost bounds the nonnegative Wasserstein infimum above by zero. The real-valued form follows by conversion. This handles the degenerate state-space branch without invoking m≤Lm\le Lm≤L, which requires a nontrivial space.
