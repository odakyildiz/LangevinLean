Couplings and quadratic transport

References and publication status for this development

Paper references

The two step-size regimes and the coefficient 1.65 follow Theorem 1(a)–(b) of Dalalyan and Karagulyan (2019). Dalalyan (2017), Theorem 1 and Section 6, gives the earlier synchronous-coupling argument.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv
  • Arnak Dalalyan (2017). Further and stronger analogy between sampling and optimization: Langevin Monte Carlo and gradient descent. Proceedings of the 2017 Conference on Learning Theory, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 65, 678–689.

Let HHH be a normed additive group with its Borel measurable structure and a second countable topology. Let PPP be a measure on a measurable space Ω\OmegaΩ, and write LP(X)=X#P\mathcal L_P(X)=X_\#PLP​(X)=X#​P. The symbol ∥Z∥2,P\|Z\|_{2,P}∥Z∥2,P​ denotes the extended L2L^2L2 seminorm, which may be infinite. In the probability setting the following definitions give the usual quadratic transport cost.

Coupling stores a joint measure with exact first and second pushforward marginals. couplingCost is its extended L2L^2L2 displacement norm, and wasserstein2 is the infimum over all such couplings. Finiteness guards precede toReal conversions; no existence of an optimal coupling is assumed.

06The joint law of two random vectors §

Mathematical statement and proof

If X,Y:Ω→HX,Y:\Omega\to HX,Y:Ω→H are measurable, then (X,Y)#P(X,Y)_\#P(X,Y)#​P is a coupling of X#PX_\#PX#​P and Y#PY_\#PY#​P.

Set γ=(ω↦(X(ω),Y(ω)))#P\gamma=(\omega\mapsto(X(\omega),Y(\omega)))_\#Pγ=(ω↦(X(ω),Y(ω)))#​P.

Compose the pushforward with the first projection. Since pr⁡1(X(ω),Y(ω))=X(ω)\operatorname{pr}_1(X(\omega),Y(\omega))=X(\omega)pr1​(X(ω),Y(ω))=X(ω), the first marginal is X#PX_\#PX#​P.

The same calculation with the second projection gives the second marginal Y#PY_\#PY#​P.

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume q≥0q\ge0q≥0, AAA is almost everywhere strongly measurable, X′−Y′=A−VX'-Y'=A-VX′−Y′=A−V and ∥A∥≤q∥X−Y∥\|A\|\le q\|X-Y\|∥A∥≤q∥X−Y∥ almost everywhere. If X−Y,V∈L2(P;H)X-Y,V\in L^2(P;H)X−Y,V∈L2(P;H), then
