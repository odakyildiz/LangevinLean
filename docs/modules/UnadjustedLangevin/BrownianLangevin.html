Construct the L² Langevin equation from Brownian-coordinate laws

Coordinate pre-Brownian laws produce the continuous L2L^2L2 additive noise needed by the deterministic integral-equation existence theorem. The exact noise norm is 2dt\sqrt{2d}\sqrt t2d​t​.

01Define a shifted Brownian noise path §

Mathematical statement and proof

For a starting time aaa, put Na(t)=2(Wa+tR−WaR)N_a(t)=\sqrt2(\mathsf W_{a+t}^{\mathbb R}-\mathsf W_a^{\mathbb R})Na​(t)=2​(Wa+tR​−WaR​). The previously proved L2L^2L2 continuity of W\mathsf WW makes NaN_aNa​ continuous, and subtraction gives Na(0)=0N_a(0)=0Na​(0)=0. When a,t≥0a,t\ge0a,t≥0, the Brownian coordinate moment calculation gives ∥Na(t)∥2=2dt\|N_a(t)\|_2=\sqrt{2d}\sqrt t∥Na​(t)∥2​=2d​t​. No independence between coordinates is needed for this norm identity.

02Insert the noise into the existence theorem §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply existence in the complete space L2(P;Rd)L^2(P;\mathbb R^d)L2(P;Rd) to the lifted gradient and NaN_aNa​. It returns a continuous path satisfying Yt−Y0=−∫0t∇U(Ys)ds+Na(t)Y_t-Y_0=-\int_0^t\nabla U(Y_s)ds+N_a(t)Yt​−Y0​=−∫0t​∇U(Ys​)ds+Na​(t), together with the already proved exact noise norm. One wrapper uses Lh≤1/2Lh\le1/2Lh≤1/2; the other uses finite-horizon splicing and needs only h≥0h\ge0h≥0. These are existence and moment statements, without a stationary-law conclusion.
