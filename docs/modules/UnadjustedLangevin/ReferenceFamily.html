An abstract family of compatible finite-coupling references

This interface isolates the passage from a bound for each initial coupling to a theorem with the exact initial Wasserstein distance. A concrete product construction later supplies this interface without assuming an optimal plan.

01Record only finite initial couplings §

Mathematical statement and proof

FiniteCoupling consists of couplings γ\gammaγ with cost⁡(γ)<∞\operatorname{cost}(\gamma)<\inftycost(γ)<∞. A ReferenceFamily assigns to every such coupling an L2L^2L2 initial variable, innovations and stationary analytic reference. It requires an initial discrepancy at most that coupling cost, and the same output law νn\nu_nνn​ for every choice. One finite coupling already realizes a finite L2L^2L2 discrepancy at each time, proving W2(νn,π)<∞W_2(\nu_n,\pi)<\inftyW2​(νn​,π)<∞.

02Pass the analytic bound through the infimum §

Mathematical statement and proof

For every finite initial coupling the reference theorem gives W2(νn,π)≤qncost⁡(γ)+BW_2(\nu_n,\pi)\le q^n\operatorname{cost}(\gamma)+BW2​(νn​,π)≤qncost(γ)+B, with q=1−mhq=1-mhq=1−mh and B=3320(L/m)hdB=\frac{33}{20}(L/m)\sqrt{hd}B=2033​(L/m)hd​. The small-step assumptions and m≤Lm\le Lm≤L ensure q≥0q\ge0q≥0. Apply the finite-coupling infimum theorem to replace the initial cost by W2(ν0,π)W_2(\nu_0,\pi)W2​(ν0​,π). Common output law is crucial: all these inequalities bound the same left side.
