A reference for every initial coupling with one output law

This construction turns the analytic reference estimate into an exact initial Wasserstein term by realizing each finite initial coupling on a product with one fixed noise source.

02Package compatible stationary references §

Mathematical statement and proof

A ProductReference supplies an analytic stationary L2L^2L2 reference for every finite coupling, with its time-zero state equal to the second coupled initial coordinate. Selecting one finite coupling already bounds the output Wasserstein distance by an actual finite L2L^2L2 discrepancy. Therefore output transport is finite before any conversion to real-valued distance.

03Take the infimum of finite initial costs §
