From scalar coordinate laws to independent Gaussian vectors

Assume the scalar innovation array is jointly independent and each entry has law N(0,1)\mathcal N(0,1)N(0,1). Group the finitely many spatial entries at time nnn into a vector WnW_nWn​. Its coordinate product law is the standard Euclidean Gaussian law N(0,I)\mathcal N(0,I)N(0,I). Grouping disjoint subsets of an independent array preserves independence, so the vectors WnW_nWn​ are independent over time.

The resulting vector-sequence law is the product of these standard Gaussian laws. This conclusion uses joint independence of the scalar array, not only its coordinate marginal distributions.

Statement and proof

01The law of the scalar innovation array §

Mathematical statement and proof

Regroup the jointly independent scalar coordinates indexed by (n,i)(n,i)(n,i) into vector blocks WnW_nWn​. A finite block with independent standard Gaussian coordinates has the standard Euclidean Gaussian law. Product-measure identification and reindexing then show that the full vector path has law ⨂nN(0,I)\bigotimes_n\mathcal N(0,I)⨂n​N(0,I).

The path-level product law specifies every finite array of innovation coordinates. It therefore determines the joint law of their sequences, including dependence across time.

02The standard Gaussian vector law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Project the established whole-path product law to its nnn-th vector coordinate. Product marginals give P∘Wn−1=N(0,I)P\circ W_n^{-1}=\mathcal N(0,I)P∘Wn−1​=N(0,I). This derives the multivariate law from the scalar coordinate assumptions rather than adding it as an algorithm hypothesis.

At a fixed index, the independent scalar standard Gaussian coordinates form the Euclidean standard Gaussian vector. The product characterization identifies the vector law.

gaussianInnovations_vectorLaw: the intermediate fact is hp, in source order; the source steps apply or rewrite AEMeasurable.map_map_of_aemeasurable, Measure.infinitePi_map_eval. The calc block checks the successive equalities or inequalities in the argument.

03Independence of the vector innovations §

Mathematical statement and proof

The coordinate maps of the vector product law are independent. Pull that independence back through the innovation-path map using the product law of the complete scalar innovation array. Thus the vector-valued WnW_nWn​ are independent over time, not just uncorrelated.

Disjoint time-index groups of an independent scalar array remain independent after assembly into vectors. Hence the vector innovations form an independent family with the required common Gaussian law.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

gaussianInnovations_vectorIndependent: the source steps apply or rewrite iIndepFun_iff_map_fun_eq_infinitePi_map₀'.
