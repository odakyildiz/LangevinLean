From scalar coordinate laws to independent Gaussian vectors

For finitely many spatial coordinates, independent standard normal scalar innovations assemble into independent standard Gaussian vectors. Scalar normal laws without joint independence would not suffice for this conclusion.

01Reindex the infinite product §

Mathematical statement and proof

Flatten WnW_nWn​ into coordinates indexed by N×I\mathbb N\times\mathcal IN×I. Its assumed scalar independence and normal marginals identify its law as the standard-normal product. The measurable equivalence (N×I→R)≅(N→RI)(\mathbb N\times\mathcal I\to\mathbb R)\cong(\mathbb N\to\mathbb R^{\mathcal I})(N×I→R)≅(N→RI), followed by the Euclidean-space identification, converts this to ⨂nN(0,I)\bigotimes_n\mathcal N(0,I)⨂n​N(0,I). Evaluation at time nnn gives each vector law, and the product-law characterization of independence gives independence of the vector family.
