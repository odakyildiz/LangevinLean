The conditional common-noise convergence theorem

Combine the actual chain, propagation of finite moments, the transport bridge, and the scalar recurrence. This theorem takes the stationary reference updates and defect bounds as inputs. The subsequent integral-reference result derives those defect bounds from a continuous equation.

The theorem combines the established local-defect estimate and contraction recurrence for the supplied analytic dynamics. Its stochastic reference requirements are visible in the formal hypotheses. The canonical law theorem later instantiates them and identifies the resulting output with independent-Gaussian ULA.
