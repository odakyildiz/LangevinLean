Exact Gibbs invariance of the Metropolis-adjusted step

The Gaussian proposal density identifies the MALA pair flow. Its reversed-to-forward density ratio is precisely the exponential of the implemented log ratio, so the Metropolis balance theorem proves exact Gibbs invariance.

01malaProposalJoint §

Mathematical statement and proof

Push μ⊗γ\mu\otimes\gammaμ⊗γ through (x,ξ)↦(x,Th(x,ξ))(x,\xi)\mapsto(x,T_h(x,\xi))(x,ξ)↦(x,Th​(x,ξ)). Its first coordinate retains the current state, and its second records the actual Gaussian proposal. This joint measure is the input to the accepted-flow detailed-balance theorem.

02malaJointWeight §

Mathematical statement and proof

Set wh(x,y)=exp⁡(−U(x)−∥y−x+h∇U(x)∥2/(4h))w_h(x,y)=\exp(-U(x)-\|y-x+h\nabla U(x)\|^2/(4h))wh​(x,y)=exp(−U(x)−∥y−x+h∇U(x)∥2/(4h)). The Gibbs and Gaussian normalization constants are kept in a separate symmetric base measure, so whw_hwh​ is strictly positive and its reverse/forward ratio is easy to compute.

03malaJointWeight ratio §

Mathematical statement and proof

Divide the two positive exponentials and use ea/eb=ea−be^a/e^b=e^{a-b}ea/eb=ea−b. Expanding the definition of whw_hwh​ shows the exponent difference is exactly malaLogRatio. Thus wh(y,x)/wh(x,y)=eRh(x,y)w_h(y,x)/w_h(x,y)=e^{R_h(x,y)}wh​(y,x)/wh​(x,y)=eRh​(x,y).

04malaOneStep eq metropolisOutput §

Mathematical statement and proof

Transport acceptance and rejection weights through the measurable pair map using map_weighted_comp. Projecting the accepted pair to its second coordinate gives the proposal; projecting rejected pairs to the first gives the unchanged state. These are precisely the two branches defining malaOneStep.

06malaOneStep gibbs invariant §

Mathematical statement and proof

The joint proposal measure has a positive density whw_hwh​ against a symmetric base. Its acceptance rule is min⁡(1,wh(y,x)/wh(x,y))\min(1,w_h(y,x)/w_h(x,y))min(1,wh​(y,x)/wh​(x,y)) by malaJointWeight_ratio. Hence its accepted density is the symmetric minimum min⁡(wh(x,y),wh(y,x))\min(w_h(x,y),w_h(y,x))min(wh​(x,y),wh​(y,x)).

Apply metropolisOutput_density_eq_initial and the exact step/output identity. The initial marginal of the proposal pair is Gibbs, since projecting πU⊗γ\pi_U\otimes\gammaπU​⊗γ to its state returns πU\pi_UπU​. Thus MhπU=πUM_h\pi_U=\pi_UMh​πU​=πU​ is proved with no stationarity input.
