Exact Gibbs invariance of the Metropolis-adjusted step

The Gaussian proposal density identifies the MALA pair flow. Its reversed-to-forward density ratio is precisely the exponential of the implemented log ratio, so the Metropolis balance theorem proves exact Gibbs invariance.

02Apply symmetric minimum-density balance §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Gaussian pair-density theorem and the Gibbs density show that the proposal joint law is w(x,y)w(x,y)w(x,y) times the symmetric base Z−1Nh dx dyZ^{-1}N_h\,dx\,dyZ−1Nh​dxdy. The weight is measurable and strictly positive, and swapping x,yx,yx,y leaves the base unchanged. The Metropolis theorem therefore preserves the first marginal. Projecting the joint proposal back to its first coordinate recovers Gibbs, proving Mhπ=πM_h\pi=\piMh​π=π for h>0h>0h>0. This density-level identity needs differentiability and Lipschitzness as displayed; strong convexity is used elsewhere to make Gibbs a probability with moments.
