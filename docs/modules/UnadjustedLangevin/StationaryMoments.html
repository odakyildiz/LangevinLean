Let (Ω,F,P)(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)(Ω,F,P) be a probability space and HHH a real inner product space of finite dimension ddd. We write L2(P;H)L^2(P;H)L2(P;H) for the space of (classes up to almost sure equality of) measurable maps Y:Ω→HY:\Omega\to HY:Ω→H with E∥Y∥2=∫Ω∥Y∥2 dP<∞\mathbb E\|Y\|^2=\int_\Omega\|Y\|^2\,dP<\inftyE∥Y∥2=∫Ω​∥Y∥2dP<∞, normed by ∥Y∥L2=(E∥Y∥2)1/2\|Y\|_{L^2}=(\mathbb E\|Y\|^2)^{1/2}∥Y∥L2​=(E∥Y∥2)1/2. The law of YYY is the image measure P∘Y−1P\circ Y^{-1}P∘Y−1, A↦P(Y∈A)A\mapsto P(Y\in A)A↦P(Y∈A). For a potential UUU with LLL-Lipschitz gradient and Y∈L2(P;H)Y\in L^2(P;H)Y∈L2(P;H), the random vector ∇U(Y):ω↦∇U(Y(ω))\nabla U(Y):\omega\mapsto\nabla U(Y(\omega))∇U(Y):ω↦∇U(Y(ω)) again belongs to L2(P;H)L^2(P;H)L2(P;H), because ∥∇U(y)∥≤∥∇U(0)∥+L∥y∥\|\nabla U(y)\|\le\|\nabla U(0)\|+L\|y\|∥∇U(y)∥≤∥∇U(0)∥+L∥y∥.

This section shows that if the law of YYY is the Gibbs measure πU\pi_UπU​, then

∥∇U(Y)∥L2=(E∥∇U(Y)∥2)1/2≤Ld,\|\nabla U(Y)\|_{L^2}=\bigl(\mathbb E\|\nabla U(Y)\|^2\bigr)^{1/2}\le\sqrt{Ld},∥∇U(Y)∥L2​=(E∥∇U(Y)∥2)1/2≤Ld​,

by transferring the Gibbs gradient-energy bound ∫∥∇U∥2 dπU≤Ld\int\|\nabla U\|^2\,d\pi_U\le Ld∫∥∇U∥2dπU​≤Ld through the change of variables formula for image measures. The hypotheses are those of that bound (differentiability, LLL-Lipschitz gradient and finite partition function), and the finite partition function is automatic under strong convexity. The Gibbs law of YYY is a hypothesis: no stationarity of any process is proved here. The estimate is applied to the stationary Langevin process, whose law is πU\pi_UπU​ at every time, to control its drift in the local error analysis of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm.

01The reference drift norm from its Gibbs law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let L≥0L\ge0L≥0, let U:H→RU:H\to\mathbb RU:H→R be differentiable with LLL-Lipschitz gradient, and assume ZU<∞Z_U<\inftyZU​<∞. Let (Ω,F,P)(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)(Ω,F,P) be a probability space and let Y∈L2(P;H)Y\in L^2(P;H)Y∈L2(P;H) have law P∘Y−1=πUP\circ Y^{-1}=\pi_UP∘Y−1=πU​. Then the random vector ∇U(Y)∈L2(P;H)\nabla U(Y)\in L^2(P;H)∇U(Y)∈L2(P;H) satisfies

∥∇U(Y)∥L2≤Ld.\|\nabla U(Y)\|_{L^2}\le\sqrt{Ld}.∥∇U(Y)∥L2​≤Ld​.

Proof. For a measurable map Y:Ω→HY:\Omega\to HY:Ω→H and a Borel function f:H→Rf:H\to\mathbb Rf:H→R, the change of variables formula for the image measure states

∫Hf d(P∘Y−1)=∫Ωf(Y(ω)) P(dω),\int_Hf\,d(P\circ Y^{-1})=\int_\Omega f(Y(\omega))\,P(d\omega),∫H​fd(P∘Y−1)=∫Ω​f(Y(ω))P(dω),

whenever either side is defined (for indicator functions f=1Af=1_Af=1A​ this is the definition P∘Y−1(A)=P(Y−1(A))P\circ Y^{-1}(A)=P(Y^{-1}(A))P∘Y−1(A)=P(Y−1(A)); it extends to simple functions by linearity, to nonnegative functions by monotone convergence, and to integrable functions by splitting into positive and negative parts). We apply it to f(x)=∥∇U(x)∥2f(x)=\|\nabla U(x)\|^2f(x)=∥∇U(x)∥2, which is continuous since ∇U\nabla U∇U is Lipschitz, and use P∘Y−1=πUP\circ Y^{-1}=\pi_UP∘Y−1=πU​:

∫Ω∥∇U(Y(ω))∥2 P(dω)=∫H∥∇U(x)∥2 πU(dx)≤Ld,(1)\int_\Omega\|\nabla U(Y(\omega))\|^2\,P(d\omega)=\int_H\|\nabla U(x)\|^2\,\pi_U(dx)\le Ld,\qquad(1)∫Ω​∥∇U(Y(ω))∥2P(dω)=∫H​∥∇U(x)∥2πU​(dx)≤Ld,(1)

where the inequality is the Gibbs gradient-energy bound ULA-SC, Section 3.27.6, valid under the present hypotheses. The squared L2L^2L2 norm of ∇U(Y)\nabla U(Y)∇U(Y) is the left side of (1) ULA-SC, Section 3.13.9, so ∥∇U(Y)∥L22≤Ld\|\nabla U(Y)\|_{L^2}^2\le Ld∥∇U(Y)∥L22​≤Ld. Since ∥∇U(Y)∥L2≥0\|\nabla U(Y)\|_{L^2}\ge0∥∇U(Y)∥L2​≥0 and the square root is increasing on [0,∞)[0,\infty)[0,∞), ∥∇U(Y)∥L2≤Ld\|\nabla U(Y)\|_{L^2}\le\sqrt{Ld}∥∇U(Y)∥L2​≤Ld​. □\square□

The bound concerns the L2L^2L2 norm of a random vector, not its pointwise values, and it uses only the law of YYY: no independence and no information on the joint law of YYY with other random variables is involved.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

In lpDrift_norm_le_of_gibbs_law, P : Measure Ω carries the instance [IsProbabilityMeasure P], and Y : Lp H 2 P is an element of Mathlib's space Lp H 2 P of equivalence classes of square-integrable maps Ω→H\Omega\to HΩ→H; writing Y ω uses a fixed representative. The hypothesis hlaw : P.map Y = gibbsMeasure U states that the image measure P.map Y of PPP under YYY is πU\pi_UπU​. The random vector ∇U(Y)\nabla U(Y)∇U(Y) is lpDrift P (gradient U) hLip Y, the element of Lp H 2 P constructed in the module on the lifted drift, which agrees almost surely with ω↦∇U(Y(ω))\omega\mapsto\nabla U(Y(\omega))ω↦∇U(Y(ω)); its norm ‖…‖ is the L2L^2L2 norm. The conclusion bounds it by Real.sqrt ((L : ℝ) * (Module.finrank ℝ H : ℝ)), that is Ld\sqrt{Ld}Ld​.

  1. he is the change of variables of (1), the Mathlib lemma integral_map. 02The drift moment under strong convexity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let m>0m>0m>0 and L≥0L\ge0L≥0, let U:H→RU:H\to\mathbb RU:H→R be differentiable and mmm-strongly convex with LLL-Lipschitz gradient, let (Ω,F,P)(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)(Ω,F,P) be a probability space, and let Y∈L2(P;H)Y\in L^2(P;H)Y∈L2(P;H) have law πU\pi_UπU​. Then ∥∇U(Y)∥L2≤Ld\|\nabla U(Y)\|_{L^2}\le\sqrt{Ld}∥∇U(Y)∥L2​≤Ld​.

Proof. Strong convexity with m>0m>0m>0 and differentiability give ZU<∞Z_U<\inftyZU​<∞ ULA-SC, Section 3.26.10. All hypotheses of the previous step hold, and it gives the claim. □\square□

As in the Gibbs gradient-energy bound, the constant mmm serves only to guarantee normalization and does not appear in the estimate.
