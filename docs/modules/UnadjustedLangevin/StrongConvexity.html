Only first derivatives occur. The quadratic lower bound is used in the sharp contraction estimate for the gradient step and in the tail bounds for the Gibbs measure; strong monotonicity is used in the first one-step estimate for the unadjusted Langevin algorithm.

01The supporting-plane inequality §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let HHH be a real inner-product space and let f:H→Rf:H\to\mathbb Rf:H→R be convex on HHH and differentiable at every point of HHH. Then for all x,y∈Hx,y\in Hx,y∈H,

f(x)+Df(x)[y−x]≤f(y).f(x)+Df(x)[y-x]\le f(y).f(x)+Df(x)[y−x]≤f(y).

Proof. Fix x,y∈Hx,y\in Hx,y∈H. Define the affine map ℓ:R→H\ell:\mathbb R\to Hℓ:R→H, ℓ(t)=x+t(y−x)\ell(t)=x+t(y-x)ℓ(t)=x+t(y−x), and the real function φ=f∘ℓ\varphi=f\circ\ellφ=f∘ℓ, so that φ(0)=f(x)\varphi(0)=f(x)φ(0)=f(x) and φ(1)=f(y)\varphi(1)=f(y)φ(1)=f(y).

(a) φ\varphiφ is convex. Let s,t∈Rs,t\in\mathbb Rs,t∈R and a,b≥0a,b\ge0a,b≥0 with a+b=1a+b=1a+b=1. Because ℓ\ellℓ is affine and a+b=1a+b=1a+b=1, ℓ(as+bt)=x+(as+bt)(y−x)=a ℓ(s)+b ℓ(t)\ell(as+bt)=x+(as+bt)(y-x)=a\,\ell(s)+b\,\ell(t)ℓ(as+bt)=x+(as+bt)(y−x)=aℓ(s)+bℓ(t). Convexity of fff gives

φ(as+bt)=f(a ℓ(s)+b ℓ(t))≤af(ℓ(s))+bf(ℓ(t))=aφ(s)+bφ(t).\varphi(as+bt)=f\bigl(a\,\ell(s)+b\,\ell(t)\bigr)\le af(\ell(s))+bf(\ell(t))=a\varphi(s)+b\varphi(t).φ(as+bt)=f(aℓ(s)+bℓ(t))≤af(ℓ(s))+bf(ℓ(t))=aφ(s)+bφ(t).

(b) φ\varphiφ is differentiable at 000 with φ′(0)=Df(x)[y−x]\varphi'(0)=Df(x)[y-x]φ′(0)=Df(x)[y−x]. For s≠0s\ne0s=0 the difference quotient of ℓ\ellℓ is (ℓ(t+s)−ℓ(t))/s=y−x(\ell(t+s)-\ell(t))/s=y-x(ℓ(t+s)−ℓ(t))/s=y−x, so ℓ\ellℓ is differentiable with ℓ′(t)=y−x\ell'(t)=y-xℓ′(t)=y−x for every ttt; moreover ℓ(0)=x\ell(0)=xℓ(0)=x. φ′(0)=Df(x)[ℓ′(0)]=Df(x)[y−x].\varphi'(0)=Df(x)[\ell'(0)]=Df(x)[y-x].φ′(0)=Df(x)[ℓ′(0)]=Df(x)[y−x].

(c) The derivative at 000 is at most the slope of the secant over [0,1][0,1][0,1]. Let 0<t≤10<t\le10<t≤1. Writing t=(1−t)⋅0+t⋅1t=(1-t)\cdot0+t\cdot1t=(1−t)⋅0+t⋅1 and applying (a) with a=1−ta=1-ta=1−t, b=tb=tb=t,

Subtracting φ(0)\varphi(0)φ(0) and dividing by t>0t>0t>0 gives

φ(t)−φ(0)t≤φ(1)−φ(0)(0<t≤1).(1)\frac{\varphi(t)-\varphi(0)}{t}\le\varphi(1)-\varphi(0)\qquad(0<t\le1).\qquad(1)tφ(t)−φ(0)​≤φ(1)−φ(0)(0<t≤1).(1)

As t↓0t\downarrow0t↓0, the left-hand side of (1) converges to φ′(0)\varphi'(0)φ′(0) by (b), while the right-hand side does not depend on ttt. Passing to the limit in (1) yields φ′(0)≤φ(1)−φ(0)\varphi'(0)\le\varphi(1)-\varphi(0)φ′(0)≤φ(1)−φ(0).

(d) Conclusion. Substituting φ′(0)=Df(x)[y−x]\varphi'(0)=Df(x)[y-x]φ′(0)=Df(x)[y−x], φ(0)=f(x)\varphi(0)=f(x)φ(0)=f(x) and φ(1)=f(y)\varphi(1)=f(y)φ(1)=f(y) gives Df(x)[y−x]≤f(y)−f(x)Df(x)[y-x]\le f(y)-f(x)Df(x)[y−x]≤f(y)−f(x), which is the claim after adding f(x)f(x)f(x) to both sides. □\square□

02The quadratic lower bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let HHH be a real Hilbert space, let m∈Rm\in\mathbb Rm∈R, and let f:H→Rf:H\to\mathbb Rf:H→R be differentiable at every point and mmm-strongly convex on HHH. Then for all x,y∈Hx,y\in Hx,y∈H,

f(x)+⟨∇f(x),y−x⟩+m2∥y−x∥2≤f(y).f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle+\frac m2\|y-x\|^2\le f(y).f(x)+⟨∇f(x),y−x⟩+2m​∥y−x∥2≤f(y).

Proof. Define the shifted function g(v)=f(v)−m2∥v∥2g(v)=f(v)-\frac m2\|v\|^2g(v)=f(v)−2m​∥v∥2 on HHH.

(a) ggg is convex. We first verify the identity

a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2−∥au+bv∥2=ab∥u−v∥2(u,v∈H, a+b=1).(1)a\|u\|^2+b\|v\|^2-\|au+bv\|^2=ab\|u-v\|^2\qquad(u,v\in H,\ a+b=1).\qquad(1)a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2−∥au+bv∥2=ab∥u−v∥2(u,v∈H, a+b=1).(1)

Now let a,b≥0a,b\ge0a,b≥0 with a+b=1a+b=1a+b=1. By strong convexity and then by (1),

g(au+bv)=f(au+bv)−m2∥au+bv∥2≤af(u)+bf(v)−m2(ab∥u−v∥2+∥au+bv∥2)=af(u)+bf(v)−m2(a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2)=ag(u)+bg(v).\begin{aligned}g(au+bv)&=f(au+bv)-\tfrac m2\|au+bv\|^2\\&\le af(u)+bf(v)-\tfrac m2\bigl(ab\|u-v\|^2+\|au+bv\|^2\bigr)\\&=af(u)+bf(v)-\tfrac m2\bigl(a\|u\|^2+b\|v\|^2\bigr)=ag(u)+bg(v).\end{aligned}g(au+bv)​=f(au+bv)−2m​∥au+bv∥2≤af(u)+bf(v)−2m​(ab∥u−v∥2+∥au+bv∥2)=af(u)+bf(v)−2m​(a∥u∥2+b∥v∥2)=ag(u)+bg(v).​

03Strong monotonicity of the gradient §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let HHH be a real Hilbert space, m∈Rm\in\mathbb Rm∈R, and let f:H→Rf:H\to\mathbb Rf:H→R be differentiable at every point and mmm-strongly convex on HHH. Then for all x,y∈Hx,y\in Hx,y∈H,

m∥x−y∥2≤⟨∇f(x)−∇f(y),x−y⟩.m\|x-y\|^2\le\langle\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y),x-y\rangle.m∥x−y∥2≤⟨∇f(x)−∇f(y),x−y⟩.

Proof. Let g(v)=f(v)−m2∥v∥2g(v)=f(v)-\frac m2\|v\|^2g(v)=f(v)−2m​∥v∥2. By parts (a) and (b) of the previous proof, ggg is convex and differentiable with Dg(v)[u]=⟨∇f(v),u⟩−m⟨v,u⟩Dg(v)[u]=\langle\nabla f(v),u\rangle-m\langle v,u\rangleDg(v)[u]=⟨∇f(v),u⟩−m⟨v,u⟩. Since y−x=−(x−y)y-x=-(x-y)y−x=−(x−y), linearity of the inner product gives

⟨∇f(x),y−x⟩+⟨∇f(y),x−y⟩=−⟨∇f(x)−∇f(y),x−y⟩,⟨x,y−x⟩+⟨y,x−y⟩=−⟨x−y,x−y⟩=−∥x−y∥2.\begin{aligned}\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle+\langle\nabla f(y),x-y\rangle&=-\langle\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y),x-y\rangle,\\\langle x,y-x\rangle+\langle y,x-y\rangle&=-\langle x-y,x-y\rangle\\&=-\|x-y\|^2.\end{aligned}⟨∇f(x),y−x⟩+⟨∇f(y),x−y⟩⟨x,y−x⟩+⟨y,x−y⟩​=−⟨∇f(x)−∇f(y),x−y⟩,=−⟨x−y,x−y⟩=−∥x−y∥2.​

Substituting both identities into (1) gives −⟨∇f(x)−∇f(y),x−y⟩+m∥x−y∥2≤0-\langle\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y),x-y\rangle+m\|x-y\|^2\le0−⟨∇f(x)−∇f(y),x−y⟩+m∥x−y∥2≤0, which is the claim. □\square□

Equivalently, the claim is the sum of the quadratic lower bounds of the previous step at (x,y)(x,y)(x,y) and at (y,x)(y,x)(y,x). For m=0m=0m=0 it is the monotonicity ⟨∇f(x)−∇f(y),x−y⟩≥0\langle\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y),x-y\rangle\ge0⟨∇f(x)−∇f(y),x−y⟩≥0 of the gradient of a differentiable convex function.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

strong_gradient_monotone has the same hypotheses as the previous step ([CompleteSpace H], hdiff, hsc) and the conclusion m * ‖x - y‖ ^ 2 ≤ inner ℝ (gradient f x - gradient f y) (x - y). Its proof does not call strong_first_order; it repeats the construction of ggg and argues as above.

  1. g, hc, hg and hgd are the shifted function, its convexity (from strongConvexOn_iff_convex), its derivative formula and its differentiability, exactly as in the previous step.
  2. hxy := convex_first_order hc hgd x y and hyx := convex_first_order hc hgd y x are the two supporting-plane inequalities. The rewrites with (hg x).fderiv and (hg y).fderiv insert the derivative formula, and the simp only call with ← inner_gradient_left expresses all derivatives through gradients.
  3. rw [inner_sub_left, inner_sub_right, inner_sub_right] expands the inner product in the goal, norm_sub_sq_real expands ∥x−y∥2\|x-y\|^2∥x−y∥2, and the second simp only normalises all inner products (real_inner_self_eq_norm_sq, real_inner_comm). nlinarith then adds hxy and hyx; this is inequality (1) followed by the two identities.
