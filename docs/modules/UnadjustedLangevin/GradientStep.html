A first Euler-step estimate

Before using the full lower and upper models together, it is useful to record the estimate obtained directly from strong monotonicity and a Lipschitz bound. This estimate applies to arbitrary vectors gx,gyg_x,g_ygx​,gy​; they need not arise as gradients.

The formal inputs gx and gy are arbitrary vectors satisfying the displayed monotonicity and Lipschitz inequalities. Thus gradient_step_sq_bound is an algebraic Hilbert-space result; the potential's actual gradients are substituted later. gradient_step_contract takes a nonnegative square root and does not require a separate sign assumption on the scalar coefficient.

∥(x−hgx)−(y−hgy)∥2≤(1−2hm+h2L2)∥x−y∥2.\|(x-hg_x)-(y-hg_y)\|^2\le(1-2hm+h^2L^2)\|x-y\|^2.∥(x−hgx​)−(y−hgy​)∥2≤(1−2hm+h2L2)∥x−y∥2.

02Taking the square root §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let HHH be a real inner-product space, x,y,gx,gy∈Hx,y,g_x,g_y\in Hx,y,gx​,gy​∈H, m∈Rm\in\mathbb Rm∈R, L≥0L\ge0L≥0, and h≥0h\ge0h≥0. Assume

m∥x−y∥2≤⟨gx−gy,x−y⟩,∥gx−gy∥≤L∥x−y∥.m\|x-y\|^2\le\langle g_x-g_y,x-y\rangle,\qquad\|g_x-g_y\|\le L\|x-y\|.m∥x−y∥2≤⟨gx​−gy​,x−y⟩,∥gx​−gy​∥≤L∥x−y∥.

Then

∥(x−hgx)−(y−hgy)∥≤1−2hm+h2L2 ∥x−y∥.\|(x-hg_x)-(y-hg_y)\|\le\sqrt{1-2hm+h^2L^2}\,\|x-y\|.∥(x−hgx​)−(y−hgy​)∥≤1−2hm+h2L2​∥x−y∥.

No separate sign assumption on 1−2hm+h2L21-2hm+h^2L^21−2hm+h2L2 is required; Lean uses the real square root, which is zero on nonpositive inputs.

Apply the preceding squared estimate. With A=∥(x−hgx)−(y−hgy)∥A=\|(x-hg_x)-(y-hg_y)\|A=∥(x−hgx​)−(y−hgy​)∥, r=∥x−y∥r=\|x-y\|r=∥x−y∥, and c=1−2hm+h2L2c=1-2hm+h^2L^2c=1−2hm+h2L2, it says A2≤cr2A^2\le cr^2A2≤cr2.

Since A≥0A\ge0A≥0, take square roots and use r2≥0r^2\ge0r2≥0:

A≤cr2=c r2=c r.A\le\sqrt{cr^2}=\sqrt c\,\sqrt{r^2}=\sqrt c\,r.A≤cr2​=c​r2​=c​r.

The square-root product identity is valid here even without a sign hypothesis on ccc, because the second factor r2r^2r2 is nonnegative.
