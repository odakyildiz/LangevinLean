The noise-driven iterates

Write Th(x)=x−h∇U(x)T_h(x)=x-h\nabla U(x)Th​(x)=x−h∇U(x), so one Euler update is Th(x)+2h zT_h(x)+\sqrt{2h}\,zTh​(x)+2h​z. The noise vectors remain explicit. Common-noise algebra works for any supplied noise sequence; identifying the algorithm with Gaussian ULA also requires independent standard Gaussian noise hypotheses.

ulaChain recursively applies ulaUpdate to a supplied sequence of vectors. Its zero and successor equations are definitional. ulaChain_measurable proves measurable dependence on the sample by induction, combining the current iterate, the Lipschitz gradient's measurability, and the next supplied measurable innovation. No independence or Gaussian-law assumption is needed for this measurability assertion.

04Measurability of every iterate §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let Ω\OmegaΩ be a measurable space. If ∇U\nabla U∇U is LLL-Lipschitz, the initial vector X0:Ω→RdX_0:\Omega\to\mathbb R^dX0​:Ω→Rd is measurable, and every ξk:Ω→Rd\xi_k:\Omega\to\mathbb R^dξk​:Ω→Rd is measurable, then XnX_nXn​ is measurable for every nnn.

A Lipschitz gradient is continuous and therefore measurable.

Induct on nnn. The initial iterate is measurable by hypothesis. For the successor step, assume XnX_nXn​ is measurable.

The composition ∇U∘Xn\nabla U\circ X_n∇U∘Xn​ is measurable. Subtracting its scalar multiple from XnX_nXn​, then adding 2h ξn\sqrt{2h}\,\xi_n2h​ξn​, preserves measurability. Hence Xn+1X_{n+1}Xn+1​ is measurable.
