X0=x0,Xk+1=Thξk(Xk)=Xk−h∇U(Xk)+2h ξk(k≥0).X_0=x_0,\qquad X_{k+1}=T_h^{\xi_k}(X_k)=X_k-h\nabla U(X_k)+\sqrt{2h}\,\xi_k\qquad(k\ge0).X0​=x0​,Xk+1​=Thξk​​(Xk​)=Xk​−h∇U(Xk​)+2h​ξk​(k≥0).

The iterate XnX_nXn​ depends only on x0x_0x0​ and ξ0,…,ξn−1\xi_0,\dots,\xi_{n-1}ξ0​,…,ξn−1​. When x0x_0x0​ and the ξk\xi_kξk​ are random vectors, with the ξk\xi_kξk​ independent standard Gaussian vectors independent of x0x_0x0​, this is the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. The recursion itself is deterministic and is defined for every input sequence; the coupling estimates of the later sections hold for every common noise sequence. This section records the defining identities and proves that every iterate is a measurable function of the randomness when the gradient is Lipschitz and the inputs are measurable, so that the law of each iterate is defined.

04Measurability of every iterate §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let Ω\OmegaΩ be a measurable space, L≥0L\ge0L≥0, and U:Ed→RU:E_d\to\mathbb RU:Ed​→R with LLL-Lipschitz gradient. Let x0:Ω→Edx_0:\Omega\to E_dx0​:Ω→Ed​ and ξk:Ω→Ed\xi_k:\Omega\to E_dξk​:Ω→Ed​ (k≥0k\ge0k≥0) be measurable, where EdE_dEd​ carries its Borel σ\sigmaσ-algebra. Then for every nnn, the map ω↦Xn(ω)\omega\mapsto X_n(\omega)ω↦Xn​(ω), the nnn-th iterate with initial vector x0(ω)x_0(\omega)x0​(ω) and noise (ξk(ω))k(\xi_k(\omega))_k(ξk​(ω))k​, is measurable.

Proof. A Lipschitz map is continuous, and continuous maps between metric spaces are Borel measurable; so ∇U\nabla U∇U is measurable. We argue by induction on nnn. For n=0n=0n=0, X0=x0X_0=x_0X0​=x0​ is measurable by hypothesis. Assume XnX_nXn​ is measurable. Then ∇U∘Xn\nabla U\circ X_n∇U∘Xn​ is measurable as a composition of measurable maps. Scalar multiples, differences and sums of measurable EdE_dEd​-valued maps are measurable (the vector operations are continuous and EdE_dEd​ is separable, so the Borel σ\sigmaσ-algebra of Ed×EdE_d\times E_dEd​×Ed​ is the product σ\sigmaσ-algebra). Consequently the law (Xn)#P(X_n)_\#P(Xn​)#​P of every iterate is defined for every measure PPP on Ω\OmegaΩ.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

In ulaChain_measurable the hypotheses are hLip : LipschitzWith L (gradient U), hinitial : Measurable initial and hnoise : ∀ n, Measurable (noise n). hg is measurability of the gradient, hLip.continuous.measurable. The proof is induction n: the zero case is hinitial; in the succ case simp only [ulaChain_succ, ulaUpdate] unfolds one step, and the term (ih.sub ((hg.comp ih).const_smul h)).add ((hnoise n).const_smul (Real.sqrt (2 * h))) assembles measurability from the induction hypothesis ih by the rules for composition, scalar multiplication, subtraction and addition.
