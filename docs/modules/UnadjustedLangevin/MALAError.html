A finite O(h³ᐟ²) rejected-displacement bound

The Metropolis-adjusted Langevin proposal provides an exactly invariant approximation to the diffusion. This module proves that its mean rejected displacement under Gibbs initialization is at most ChhCh\sqrt hChh​, with a finite constant derived from the potential assumptions.
