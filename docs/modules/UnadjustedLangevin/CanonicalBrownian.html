An explicit Gaussian construction of pre-Brownian coordinates

The isonormal family on the time Hilbert space constructs a Gaussian process with covariance min⁡(s,t)\min(s,t)min(s,t). Independent product copies supply the Brownian coordinates required by the canonical ULA theorem. The construction proves pre-Brownian laws; continuous sample paths are not asserted or needed, because continuity is established in L2L^2L2.

01TimeHilbert §

Mathematical statement and proof

03timeIndicator inner §

Mathematical statement and proof

The product of the two indicators is the indicator of [0,min⁡(s,t)][0,\min(s,t)][0,min(s,t)] almost everywhere. Integrating it gives ⟨es,et⟩=min⁡(s,t)\langle e_s,e_t\rangle=\min(s,t)⟨es​,et​⟩=min(s,t). The proof evaluates the Lebesgue measure of that finite interval, producing the Brownian covariance before introducing randomness.

19coordinate isPreBrownian §

Mathematical statement and proof
