01The Hilbert space of time functions §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. Let λ\lambdaλ be Lebesgue measure on R\mathbb RR. The time Hilbert space is H=L2(R,λ)\mathcal H=L^2(\mathbb R,\lambda)H=L2(R,λ), the space of classes, modulo equality λ\lambdaλ-almost everywhere, of Borel functions f:R→Rf:\mathbb R\to\mathbb Rf:R→R with ∫f2 dλ<∞\int f^2\,d\lambda<\infty∫f2dλ<∞. With the inner product ⟨f,g⟩=∫fg dλ\langle f,g\rangle=\int fg\,d\lambda⟨f,g⟩=∫fgdλ it is a real Hilbert space (Riesz–Fischer theorem).

The space H\mathcal HH carries no randomness: its elements are deterministic functions of time. It serves as the domain of the isonormal map, and it must not be confused with the space L2(Q)L^2(Q)L2(Q) of random variables on the Gaussian source.

04The index set of a Hilbert basis of time functions §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. Every real Hilbert space has a Hilbert basis, that is, an orthonormal family whose linear span is dense. (By Zorn's lemma there is a maximal orthonormal family; if its closed span were a proper closed subspace, a unit vector orthogonal to that subspace could be added, contradicting maximality.) Fix a Hilbert basis of H\mathcal HH whose members are the elements of a subset W⊆HW\subseteq\mathcal HW⊆H, each element indexing itself. The set WWW is the index set of the construction.

No property of WWW other than being the index set of a Hilbert basis is used. In particular WWW is countable, because H\mathcal HH is separable, but countability is not needed: the product measure of the next steps exists for every index set.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

Mathlib's exists_hilbertBasis states that there exist a set w of vectors and a Hilbert basis b : HilbertBasis w 𝕜 E indexed by w whose vectors are the elements of w. Here it is applied with (𝕜 := ℝ) and (E := TimeHilbert). Classical.choose selects a witness of an existential statement using the axiom of choice; basisIndex is the selected set WWW, of type Set TimeHilbert.
