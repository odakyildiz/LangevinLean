Detailed balance through accepted and rejected flows

The Metropolis correction preserves the initial marginal whenever the accepted proposal flow is invariant under swapping its two states. A positive proposal-pair density makes this symmetry explicit.

01acceptedFlow §

Mathematical statement and proof

Weight the current/proposal joint measure ν(dx,dy)\nu(dx,dy)ν(dx,dy) by acceptance probability a(x,y)a(x,y)a(x,y). Thus A(dx,dy)=a(x,y)ν(dx,dy)A(dx,dy)=a(x,y)\nu(dx,dy)A(dx,dy)=a(x,y)ν(dx,dy), with ofReal embedding its real nonnegative weight. The bounds 0≤a≤10\le a\le10≤a≤1 are supplied in the conservation theorems below.

03metropolisOutput §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define the output as snd⁡∗A+fst⁡∗R\operatorname{snd}_*A+\operatorname{fst}_*Rsnd∗​A+fst∗​R. Accepted moves output yyy, while rejected moves output xxx. This is the complete transition measure and requires no renormalization of accepted moves.

04acceptedFlow add rejectedFlow §

Mathematical statement and proof

Since 0≤a≤10\le a\le10≤a≤1, the two nonnegative weights add to one after embedding in extended scalars. Additivity of with-density measures then gives A+R=νA+R=\nuA+R=ν. The proof checks both nonnegativity guards before combining ofReal values.

05marginals eq of swap invariant §

Mathematical statement and proof

The identity fst⁡∘swap⁡=snd⁡\operatorname{fst}\circ\operatorname{swap}=\operatorname{snd}fst∘swap=snd and pushforward composition give snd⁡∗ν=fst⁡∗(swap⁡∗ν)\operatorname{snd}_*\nu=\operatorname{fst}_*(\operatorname{swap}_*\nu)snd∗​ν=fst∗​(swap∗​ν). If the pair measure is swap invariant, this is fst⁡∗ν\operatorname{fst}_*\nufst∗​ν, proving equality of its marginals.

06metropolisOutput eq initial §

Mathematical statement and proof

Detailed balance makes the two marginals of the accepted flow equal. Replace its second marginal by its first, then combine the accepted and rejected first marginals using A+R=νA+R=\nuA+R=ν. The output is therefore fst⁡∗ν\operatorname{fst}_*\nufst∗​ν, exactly the original state law.

09metropolisAcceptance §

Mathematical statement and proof

For positive joint density w(x,y)w(x,y)w(x,y), define a(x,y)=min⁡(1,w(y,x)/w(x,y))a(x,y)=\min(1,w(y,x)/w(x,y))a(x,y)=min(1,w(y,x)/w(x,y)). The ratio is reverse flow divided by forward flow; positivity ensures both its meaning and nonnegative acceptance.

10weight mul metropolisAcceptance §

11acceptedFlow density §

Mathematical statement and proof

Two successive changes of density multiply their weights. Combine the original positive density www and acceptance aaa, then apply the weight/minimum identity. The accepted flow becomes the base measure weighted by min⁡(w,w∘swap⁡)\min(w,w\circ\operatorname{swap})min(w,w∘swap), a visibly symmetric density.

12metropolisOutput density eq initial §

Mathematical statement and proof

The base measure is swap invariant, and the accepted minimum density is unchanged by swapping its arguments. The weight-transport theorem therefore proves accepted-flow detailed balance. The general accepted/rejected conservation theorem then identifies the output with the initial marginal.
