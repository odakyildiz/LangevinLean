Detailed balance through accepted and rejected flows

The Metropolis correction preserves the initial marginal whenever the accepted proposal flow is invariant under swapping its two states. A positive proposal-pair density makes this symmetry explicit.

01Split the proposal flow §

02Weights commute with measurable pushforward §

Mathematical statement and proof

For measurable fff and weight www, integrating indicators and using the pushforward formula proves f#[(w∘f)μ]=w(f#μ)f_\#[(w\circ f)\mu]=w(f_\#\mu)f#​[(w∘f)μ]=w(f#​μ). The measurable-equivalence version specializes this identity. It allows an accept/reject rule defined in proposal-pair coordinates to be moved through a proposal-generating transformation.

03The Metropolis minimum is symmetric §

Mathematical statement and proof

For a strictly positive density w(x,y)w(x,y)w(x,y), set a(x,y)=min⁡{1,w(y,x)/w(x,y)}a(x,y)=\min\{1,w(y,x)/w(x,y)\}a(x,y)=min{1,w(y,x)/w(x,y)}. Then

w(x,y)a(x,y)=min⁡{w(x,y),w(y,x)}.w(x,y)a(x,y)=\min\{w(x,y),w(y,x)\}.w(x,y)a(x,y)=min{w(x,y),w(y,x)}.

Thus the accepted flow has the symmetric minimum density. If the base measure is swap invariant, so is this weighted flow. Apply the accepted/rejected decomposition to conclude exact preservation of the initial marginal. Positivity of www justifies the ratio algebra.
