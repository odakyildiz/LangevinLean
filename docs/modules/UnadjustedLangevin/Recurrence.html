Iteration of the scalar error estimate

References and publication status for this development

Paper references

The two step-size regimes and the coefficient 1.651.651.65 follow Theorem 1(a)–(b) of Dalalyan and Karagulyan (2019). Dalalyan (2017), Theorem 1 and Section 6, gives the earlier synchronous-coupling argument.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv
  • Arnak Dalalyan (2017). Further and stronger analogy between sampling and optimization: Langevin Monte Carlo and gradient descent. Proceedings of the 2017 Conference on Learning Theory, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 65, 678–689.

This section studies real sequences (en)n≥0(e_n)_{n\ge0}(en​)n≥0​ satisfying a one-step inequality en+1≤q en+be_{n+1}\le q\,e_n+ben+1​≤qen​+b, where q≥0q\ge0q≥0 and bbb are fixed real numbers. Every convergence proof in this report ends with an inequality of this kind: ene_nen​ is an error after nnn iterations of an algorithm, qqq is the factor by which one iteration contracts the error, and bbb is the error newly introduced by each iteration. We prove, in increasing order of simplification, the three bounds

en≤qne0+b∑j=0n−1qj,en≤qne0+b(1−qn)1−q,en≤qne0+b1−q,e_n\le q^ne_0+b\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}q^j,\qquad e_n\le q^ne_0+\frac{b(1-q^n)}{1-q},\qquad e_n\le q^ne_0+\frac{b}{1-q},en​≤qne0​+bj=0∑n−1​qj,en​≤qne0​+1−qb(1−qn)​,en​≤qne0​+1−qb​,

the second for 0≤q<10\le q<10≤q<1 and the third when in addition b≥0b\ge0b≥0. We then show that the right-hand side of the third bound converges to b/(1−q)b/(1-q)b/(1−q) as n→∞n\to\inftyn→∞, and deduce that every tolerance ε>b/(1−q)\varepsilon>b/(1-q)ε>b/(1−q) is eventually met by the sequence. No probability enters this section: the one-step inequality for the random algorithm is proved elsewhere, and only the scalar calculation is carried out here.

01Iteration gives a geometric sum §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let (en)n≥0(e_n)_{n\ge0}(en​)n≥0​ be a sequence of real numbers and let q,b∈Rq,b\in\mathbb Rq,b∈R with q≥0q\ge0q≥0. Suppose that

ek+1≤q ek+bfor every k∈N.e_{k+1}\le q\,e_k+b\qquad\text{for every }k\in\mathbb N.ek+1​≤qek​+bfor every k∈N.

Then for every n∈Nn\in\mathbb Nn∈N,

en≤qne0+b Sn,whereSn=∑j=0n−1qj,e_n\le q^ne_0+b\,S_n,\qquad\text{where}\quad S_n=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}q^j,en​≤qne0​+bSn​,whereSn​=j=0∑n−1​qj,

with the convention that the empty sum S0S_0S0​ equals 000.

Proof. We argue by induction on nnn.

Base case n=0n=0n=0. Since q0=1q^0=1q0=1 and S0=0S_0=0S0​=0, the right-hand side equals e0e_0e0​, and e0≤e0e_0\le e_0e0​≤e0​.

Inductive step. Fix nnn and assume the induction hypothesis en≤qne0+bSne_n\le q^ne_0+bS_nen​≤qne0​+bSn​. Because q≥0q\ge0q≥0, multiplying both sides of this inequality by qqq preserves its direction:

q en≤qn+1e0+b qSn.(1)q\,e_n\le q^{n+1}e_0+b\,qS_n.\qquad(1)qen​≤qn+1e0​+bqSn​.(1)

Next we compute qSnqS_nqSn​. Multiplying the finite sum term by term and shifting the index,

qSn=∑j=0n−1qj+1=∑j=1nqj=Sn−1+qn,qS_n=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}q^{j+1}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}q^{j}=S_n-1+q^n,qSn​=j=0∑n−1​qj+1=j=1∑n​qj=Sn​−1+qn,

because the last sum contains every term of SnS_nSn​ except q0=1q^0=1q0=1, together with the additional term qnq^nqn. Hence qSn+1=Sn+qn=Sn+1qS_n+1=S_n+q^n=S_{n+1}qSn​+1=Sn​+qn=Sn+1​. Multiplying this identity by bbb gives

b qSn+b=b Sn+1.(2)b\,qS_n+b=b\,S_{n+1}.\qquad(2)bqSn​+b=bSn+1​.(2)

Now apply the hypothesis with k=nk=nk=n, then (1), then (2):

en+1≤q en+b≤qn+1e0+b qSn+b=qn+1e0+b Sn+1.e_{n+1}\le q\,e_n+b\le q^{n+1}e_0+b\,qS_n+b=q^{n+1}e_0+b\,S_{n+1}.en+1​≤qen​+b≤qn+1e0​+bqSn​+b=qn+1e0​+bSn+1​.

This is the claim for n+1n+1n+1, which completes the induction. □\square□

Written out, the bound reads en≤qne0+b(1+q+⋯+qn−1)e_n\le q^ne_0+b(1+q+\dots+q^{n-1})en​≤qne0​+b(1+q+⋯+qn−1): the initial error is multiplied by qqq once per iteration, and the defect bbb introduced at iteration jjj has been multiplied by qqq once for each of the n−1−jn-1-jn−1−j later iterations.

02The finite geometric-sum bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. In the setting of the previous step, assume in addition that q<1q<1q<1, so that 0≤q<10\le q<10≤q<1. Then for every n∈Nn\in\mathbb Nn∈N,

en≤qne0+b (1−qn)1−q.e_n\le q^ne_0+\frac{b\,(1-q^n)}{1-q}.en​≤qne0​+1−qb(1−qn)​.

Proof. By the previous step, en≤qne0+bSne_n\le q^ne_0+bS_nen​≤qne0​+bSn​ with Sn=∑j=0n−1qjS_n=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}q^jSn​=∑j=0n−1​qj. It remains to evaluate SnS_nSn​. The sum telescopes after multiplication by q−1q-1q−1:

(q−1)Sn=∑j=0n−1(qj+1−qj)=qn−1.(q-1)S_n=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\bigl(q^{j+1}-q^{j}\bigr)=q^n-1.(q−1)Sn​=j=0∑n−1​(qj+1−qj)=qn−1.

Since q<1q<1q<1, we have q−1≠0q-1\neq0q−1=0, and dividing gives the finite geometric series formula Sn=(qn−1)/(q−1)S_n=(q^n-1)/(q-1)Sn​=(qn−1)/(q−1). Multiplying numerator and denominator by −1-1−1,

Sn=1−qn1−q,S_n=\frac{1-q^n}{1-q},Sn​=1−q1−qn​,

a quotient whose denominator 1−q1-q1−q is positive. Substituting this expression into the bound of the previous step and writing b⋅1−qn1−q=b(1−qn)1−qb\cdot\frac{1-q^n}{1-q}=\frac{b(1-q^n)}{1-q}b⋅1−q1−qn​=1−qb(1−qn)​ proves the claim. □\square□

Since 0≤qn≤10\le q^n\le10≤qn≤1, the factor 1−qn1-q^n1−qn lies in [0,1][0,1][0,1]. It records that only nnn defects have been introduced by iteration nnn; for n=0n=0n=0 the bound reduces to e0≤e0e_0\le e_0e0​≤e0​.

03A uniform additive error bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If 0≤q<10\le q<10≤q<1, b≥0b\ge0b≥0 and ek+1≤qek+be_{k+1}\le qe_k+bek+1​≤qek​+b for all kkk, then for every n∈Nn\in\mathbb Nn∈N,

en≤qne0+b1−q.e_n\le q^ne_0+\frac{b}{1-q}.en​≤qne0​+1−qb​.

Proof. Start from the bound of the previous step. Since q≥0q\ge0q≥0, we have qn≥0q^n\ge0qn≥0 and hence 1−qn≤11-q^n\le11−qn≤1. Multiplying this inequality by b≥0b\ge0b≥0 gives b(1−qn)≤bb(1-q^n)\le bb(1−qn)≤b. Dividing both sides by the positive number 1−q1-q1−q preserves the inequality:

b(1−qn)1−q≤b1−q.\frac{b(1-q^n)}{1-q}\le\frac{b}{1-q}.1−qb(1−qn)​≤1−qb​.

Adding qne0q^ne_0qne0​ to both sides and combining with the previous step by transitivity of ≤\le≤ gives the claim. □\square□

The bound consists of the term qne0q^ne_0qne0​, which tends to zero as n→∞n\to\inftyn→∞, and the constant b/(1−q)b/(1-q)b/(1−q), which does not depend on nnn.
