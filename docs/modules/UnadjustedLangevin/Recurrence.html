Iteration of the scalar error estimate

Once coupling gives ek+1≤qek+be_{k+1}\le qe_k+bek+1​≤qek​+b, the remaining calculation is deterministic. We retain the exact geometric sum before obtaining the simpler bias-floor bound. The upper envelope approaches b/(1−q)b/(1-q)b/(1−q); this does not assert that the actual error converges to that value.

The scalar sequence need not have an algorithmic interpretation for these lemmas. The proofs separate the exact geometric sum from its uniform bias-floor envelope and explicitly require the contraction factor in [0,1)[0,1)[0,1). Later the coupling construction proves that the actual ULA error satisfies this recurrence.
