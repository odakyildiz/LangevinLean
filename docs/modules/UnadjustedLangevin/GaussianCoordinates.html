01gaussian coordinates memLp §

Mathematical statement and proof

Each centered Gaussian coordinate has a finite second moment. In finite Euclidean dimension, ∥X∥2=∑iXi2\|X\|^2=\sum_iX_i^2∥X∥2=∑i​Xi2​, so the finite sum is integrable. Coordinatewise measurability also assembles to vector measurability, giving X∈L2X\in L^2X∈L2. Coordinate independence is unnecessary for this moment statement.

02gaussian coordinates second moment §

Mathematical statement and proof

Use ∥X∥2=∑iXi2\|X\|^2=\sum_iX_i^2∥X∥2=∑i​Xi2​ and finite-sum integral linearity. The coordinate law Xi∼N(0,v)X_i\sim\mathcal N(0,v)Xi​∼N(0,v) gives EXi2=v\mathbb EX_i^2=vEXi2​=v, hence E∥X∥2=∣ι∣v\mathbb E\|X\|^2=|\iota|vE∥X∥2=∣ι∣v.
