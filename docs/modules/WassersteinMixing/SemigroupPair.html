The compression of stationary observations depends only on their true two-time joint law. In particular equal pair laws give equal operators, and reversal symmetry of the pair proves self-adjointness.

Statement and proof

01From actual pair laws to operator equality §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The evaluation pairing is the integral of the actual pointwise observable pairing under the joint state law. Thus equality of pair laws identifies the compressed operators. If that law is unchanged by coordinate swap, the operator is self-adjoint.

Proof. Compare the exact evaluation representatives, then change variables under the measurable pair map. Equal pair laws give equal integrals against every pair of square-integrable observables. The adjoint correlation identity and separation by inner products yield operator equality. Swapping the pair exchanges the two observable arguments and gives the symmetric pairing identity required for self-adjointness.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

evaluation_inner_pair proves the actual pushforward integral identity. compression_eq_of_pair_law uses inner-product extensionality. compression_isSelfAdjoint_of_pair_swap applies the proved pair reversal and the bounded symmetric-operator criterion.
