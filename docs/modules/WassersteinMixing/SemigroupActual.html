The actual Gibbs diffusion evaluations and contractions

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The canonical Brownian Langevin solution, constructed from the potential, is stationary for the Gibbs law. Evaluating observables on its grid and interior states therefore gives actual isometries and actual contractive transition compressions, with strong continuity along each segment.

01Actual operators and their true two-time correlations §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. Let Unf=f(Ynh)U_nf=f(Y_{nh})Un​f=f(Ynh​) and Un,sf=f(Ynh+s)U_{n,s}f=f(Y_{nh+s})Un,s​f=f(Ynh+s​). Their Gibbs marginal laws make them isometries. Compress against time-zero evaluation to obtain the actual transition. Its norm is at most one, its value at zero is the identity, and

⟨Pnf,g⟩=∫⟨f(Ynh),g(Y0)⟩ dQ.\langle P_nf,g\rangle=\int\langle f(Y_{nh}),g(Y_0)\rangle\,dQ.⟨Pn​f,g⟩=∫⟨f(Ynh​),g(Y0​)⟩dQ.

Proof. Apply the general compression identities to the actual stationary evaluation maps. The representative theorem identifies the abstract pairing with the displayed actual two-time integral.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The definitions use the actual reference state and its proved stationary law. Norm and zero identities specialize the compression results. actualEvaluation_coe and actualTransition_correlation compare exact pullback representatives.

02Strong continuity from the actual continuous reference path §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Every square-integrable target observable has norm-continuous evaluation along an actual reference segment; its compressed transition orbit is also continuous.

Proof. The reference segment is continuous in L2L^2L2 and has the same Gibbs law throughout the interval. The stationary-observable continuity theorem applies to every observable, not just bounded continuous ones. The fixed initial evaluation adjoint is bounded and hence preserves this convergence after compression.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

continuous_actualSegmentEvaluation applies StationaryObservables.evaluation_tendsto to the constructed continuous segment. continuous_actualSegmentTransition composes with the continuous adjoint of time-zero evaluation.
