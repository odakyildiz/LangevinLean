The exact anchored law of the actual random MALA path

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The finite-time pair of the actual initial state and sampled MALA endpoint has law π⊗Kn\pi\otimes K^nπ⊗Kn, with the explicit fresh-noise kernel. This identifies actual random endpoint pairs with the proved reversible kernel powers.

01Induction on the actual anchored law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim.

Law⁡(X0,XnMALA)=π⊗Kn,Law⁡(X0,XnMALA)=Law⁡(XnMALA,X0).\operatorname{Law}(X_0,X_n^{\mathrm{MALA}})=\pi\otimes K^n,\qquad \operatorname{Law}(X_0,X_n^{\mathrm{MALA}})=\operatorname{Law}(X_n^{\mathrm{MALA}},X_0).Law(X0​,XnMALA​)=π⊗Kn,Law(X0​,XnMALA​)=Law(XnMALA​,X0​).

Proof. At time zero the anchor/endpoint law is the diagonal Gibbs law, exactly π⊗K0\pi\otimes K^0π⊗K0. Both the sampled anchored recursion and the explicit kernel-power pair advance by the same parallel-kernel update. Induction gives equality of the full pair laws. The actual product initial/innovation law transfers this equality to the canonical sampled path. Reversal follows from the proved explicit-power detailed balance.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.20.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.20.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.20.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.33.6; ULA-SC, Section 3.33.9.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

anchored_chainLaw compares the zero-step and successor laws. recursion_law applies the actual sampled-input law. anchored_state_law identifies the true canonical pair and anchored_state_swap substitutes the proved explicit-power swap identity.
