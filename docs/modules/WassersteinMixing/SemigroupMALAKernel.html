The explicit actual Gaussian Metropolis kernel

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The transition kernel samples an actual fresh standard Gaussian, maps accepted mass to the Langevin proposal and rejected mass to the current state. Its action on every initial law equals the existing Gaussian MALA law step exactly.

Statement and proof

01Constructing the actual fresh-noise transition §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. At state xxx, take the product δx⊗γ\delta_x\otimes\gammaδx​⊗γ, where γ\gammaγ is standard Gaussian. Weight by actual Metropolis acceptance and rejection probabilities and map to the proposed or unchanged state. The resulting explicit kernel at xxx is exactly the MALA law step applied to δx\delta_xδx​.

Proof. The product kernel is Markov. Measurability of the actual gradient proposal and acceptance permits weighted mapping. Evaluating at a state and substituting the Dirac initial law yields the same accepted/rejected formula as the actual MALA step.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.34.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.6; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.8.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

gaussianPairKernel is the product of the identity and constant-Gaussian kernels. explicitKernel is the sum of its actual weighted mapped kernels. explicitKernel_apply compares their event masses with the Dirac-start MALA step.

02The exact law action and mass normalization §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For every sigma-finite initial law μ\muμ, the explicit kernel action is the actual MALA output law. It has total mass one at every state.

Proof. Composing the fresh-Gaussian pair kernel with μ\muμ gives μ⊗γ\mu\otimes\gammaμ⊗γ. Mapping and weighting commute with actual measure/kernel composition, so the explicit output is exactly the accepted/rejected law formula for μ\muμ. For a Dirac input, acceptance plus rejection partition the entire Gaussian mass, proving the Markov normalization.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.34.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.6; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.8.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

gaussianPairKernel_comp identifies the actual independent-product sampling law. explicitKernel_comp uses map, density and sum composition identities. explicitKernel_isMarkov proves unit mass from the true accepted/rejected partition.
