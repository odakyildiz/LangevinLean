01Semigroup positivity, order and integrability §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The transition operators commute, 0⪯Pt⪯I0\preceq P_t\preceq I0⪯Pt​⪯I, and Pt⪯PsP_t\preceq P_sPt​⪯Ps​ when 0≤s≤t0\le s\le t0≤s≤t. Every orbit is integrable on a compact time interval.

Proof. The semigroup law and commutativity of addition give commutation. Since Pt=Pt/2∗Pt/2P_t=P_{t/2}^*P_{t/2}Pt​=Pt/2∗​Pt/2​, it is positive. Contractivity and Cauchy–Schwarz bound its quadratic form by ∥x∥2\|x\|^2∥x∥2, giving Pt⪯IP_t\preceq IPt​⪯I. The positive commuting product Ps(I−Pt−s)P_s(I-P_{t-s})Ps​(I−Pt−s​) is Ps−PtP_s-P_tPs​−Pt​. Strong continuity supplies compact-interval Bochner integrability.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

ReversibleSemigroup packages operator properties that must be proved for a concrete process. The order lemmas derive positivity and monotonicity from those fields; orbit_integrable restricts the strong continuity theorem to the compact interval. The private inner functional and integrability helper justify the later pairing integrals.

02The integrated defect and its telescoping order bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The actual integral Dhx=∫0h(x−Ptx) dtD_hx=\int_0^h(x-P_tx)\,dtDh​x=∫0h​(x−Pt​x)dt is a bounded linear map, is symmetric, and commutes with every PsP_sPs​. Moreover

0⪯PsDh⪯h(Ps−Ps+h).0\preceq P_sD_h\preceq h(P_s-P_{s+h}).0⪯Ps​Dh​⪯h(Ps​−Ps+h​).

Proof. The integrand has norm at most 2∥x∥2\|x\|2∥x∥, giving boundedness and allowing linearity to pass through the integral. Bounded linear operators and inner-product functionals commute with that integral. Semigroup commutation gives the operator commutation, and self-adjointness gives symmetry. Finally PsDhx=∫0h(Ps−Ps+t)x dtP_sD_hx=\int_0^h(P_s-P_{s+t})x\,dtPs​Dh​x=∫0h​(Ps​−Ps+t​)xdt. Time monotonicity bounds the integrand between zero and Ps−Ps+hP_s-P_{s+h}Ps​−Ps+h​, proving the order estimate.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

defect is a genuine vector-valued interval integral; defectOp is its constructed bounded linear form. The commutation and symmetry proofs move continuous linear maps through the justified integrals. shiftedDefect_bounds integrates nonnegative quadratic forms and their uniform upper bound.

03Constructing the corrector by bounded monotone convergence §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The finite sums are increasing and satisfy

0⪯GN⪯h(I−PNh)⪯hI.0\preceq G_N\preceq h(I-P_{Nh})\preceq hI.0⪯GN​⪯h(I−PNh​)⪯hI.

They converge strongly to a bounded positive operator GhG_hGh​, with ∥Gh∥≤h\|G_h\|\le h∥Gh​∥≤h.

Proof. Each summand is positive by the previous order bound. Summing that bound telescopes the consecutive semigroup differences. Thus the positive sums are increasing and uniformly bounded by hIhIhI. The proved monotone-operator convergence theorem constructs their bounded linear pointwise limit, and positivity and the upper bound pass to it.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

partialCorrector contains only finite operator sums. The successive-sum, positivity and telescoping lemmas supply all hypotheses of exists_monotone_operator_limit. corrector chooses that proved operator, while partialCorrector_tendsto and corrector_order retain its actual convergence and order properties.

04The exact Poisson equation and Dirichlet estimate §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The constructed corrector commutes with the semigroup and satisfies
