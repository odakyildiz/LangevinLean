Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. Mathematical statement and proof

Definitions and claims. Set D=dx+dθ/N≥0D=d_x+d_\theta/N\ge0D=dx​+dθ​/N≥0. The new and Project 03 discretization terms are

Proof. Cancel the positive particle number in the condition number. Since dθ+Ndx=NDd_\theta+Nd_x=NDdθ​+Ndx​=ND, the expression under the remaining square root is N2(L/m)LDN^2(L/m)LDN2(L/m)LD. How the Lean proof follows this argument

The first three definitions retain the literal dimension and the two constants. iplaEffectiveDimension_nonneg proves the dimension sign. ipla_lifted_bias_eq factors the radicand as the square of the particle number times the reduced radicand, then uses the square-root product identity. ipla_linear_bias_scale invokes the proved curvature scaling identity. ipla_concentration_scale combines positive square roots before exact cancellation.

05An exact regime where the discretization estimate improves Project 03 §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For m>0m>0m>0, L,D,γ≥0L,D,\gamma\ge0L,D,γ≥0,

mγ≤1211600 ⟹ B(γ)≤B03(γ),m\gamma\le\frac{121}{1600}\ \Longrightarrow\ B(\gamma)\le B_{03}(\gamma),mγ≤1600121​ ⟹ B(γ)≤B03​(γ), mγ≤1216400 ⟹ 2B(γ)≤B03(γ).\quad m\gamma\le\frac{121}{6400}\ \Longrightarrow\ 2B(\gamma)\le B_{03}(\gamma).mγ≤6400121​ ⟹ 2B(γ)≤B03​(γ).

Proof. Factor the new term as

B(γ)=(6γm)(LmD).B(\gamma)=\bigl(6\gamma\sqrt m\bigr)\left(\frac Lm\sqrt D\right).B(γ)=(6γm​)(mL​D​).

The second factor is nonnegative and is shared with the Project 03 expression. Comparing the first factors reduces to 6γm≤(33/20)γ6\gamma\sqrt m\le(33/20)\sqrt\gamma6γm​≤(33/20)γ​; squaring the nonnegative sides and using the displayed bound on mγm\gammamγ proves it. Replacing 666 by 121212 gives the second threshold and the doubled-bias result. No division by γ\gammaγ or DDD is required, so their zero cases are included.

The primary application attains a single bias with either discretized-invariant or exact-Gibbs initialization, so its sharp complete bound uses the first threshold. The doubled statement records a valid conservative alternative. Thus the improved discretization order is linear in the step, within the explicitly stated regularity and step regime, rather than a claim that every Project 03 hypothesis or every allowed step is covered.
