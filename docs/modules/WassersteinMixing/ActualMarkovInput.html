Stationary independent inputs for the actual future dynamics

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

At each deterministic time, the actual state has the Gibbs law and is measurable from the past. Its pair with the whole independent future Brownian path therefore has a product law, and this pair law is independent of the cut time.

Statement and proof

01The state and future form an actual product input §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. At a≥0a\ge0a≥0, the state YaY_aYa​ is past-measurable, is independent of WaW_aWa​, and

Law⁡(Ya,Wa)=π⊗Law⁡(Wa).\operatorname{Law}(Y_a,W_a)=\pi\otimes\operatorname{Law}(W_a).Law(Ya​,Wa​)=π⊗Law(Wa​).

Proof. Apply natural-past adaptation to a finite horizon ending at aaa, then identify the endpoint with the global solution. Independence of the whole future from the past implies independence from this measurable state. The pair-law factorization follows from independence and the already proved Gibbs stationarity.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.25.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.8.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

actualState_adapted_pastAt applies the actual integral-solution adaptation theorem. actualState_future_independent descends future/past independence to the state. actualState_future_joint_law uses the exact product pushforward identity.

02Equality of whole future-input laws §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The shifted Brownian path has the same law at any two cut times, so Law⁡(Ya,Wa)=Law⁡(Yb,Wb)\operatorname{Law}(Y_a,W_a)=\operatorname{Law}(Y_b,W_b)Law(Ya​,Wa​)=Law(Yb​,Wb​).

Proof. Finite collections of shifted Brownian coordinates have the same centered Gaussian law, with covariance determined solely by their elapsed times. Equality on finite coordinate cylinders gives equality of the full path laws. The actual vector source is a product of independent coordinate sources; equality therefore transfers to the vector path. Substitute this equality into the preceding product-input factorization.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.22.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.11; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.16; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.17; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.19; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.20; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.21.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

preBrownian_shift_path_law proves equality of path measures from finite-dimensional Gaussian data. actualBrownianFuture_law_eq applies it to the actual coordinate product. The final theorem rewrites both exact product input laws.
