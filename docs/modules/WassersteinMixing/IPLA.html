New linear-step parameter rates for actual Gaussian IPLA

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

01Displaying every structural assumption and deriving the lifted contract §

Mathematical statement and proof

Setting. Let N,dθ,dxN,d_\theta,d_xN,dθ​,dx​ be nonnegative integers with N>0N>0N>0. Assume U:Rdθ+dx→RU:\mathbb R^{d_\theta+d_x}\to\mathbb RU:Rdθ​+dx​→R belongs to C2C^2C2, is mmm-strongly convex, and has an LLL-Lipschitz joint gradient, where 0<m≤L0<m\le L0<m≤L. Let 0<γ0<\gamma0<γ satisfy γL≤1\gamma L\le1γL≤1.

Claim and proof. For the actual scaled potential VN(z)=∑iU(θ,Nzi)V_N(z)=\sum_iU(\theta,\sqrt N z_i)VN​(z)=∑i​U(θ,N​zi​), the previous lifting theorems give C2C^2C2 regularity, curvature NmNmNm, gradient Lipschitz constant NLNLNL, dimension dθ+Ndxd_\theta+Nd_xdθ​+Ndx​, and ULA step γ/N\gamma/Nγ/N. In particular the required small-step condition reduces exactly to γL≤1\gamma L\le1γL≤1. These are consequences of the joint assumptions, not extra analytic hypotheses on a lifted reference process.

The regularity and step range are explicit additions relative to the full scope of Project 03: that theorem needs differentiability and a Lipschitz gradient and permits γ≤2/(m+L)\gamma\le2/(m+L)γ≤2/(m+L). The improved rate is asserted within the regularity class and step interval displayed here.

03Projecting the finite transport estimate to the parameter §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let θ⋆\theta^\starθ⋆ maximize the latent-integrated density ∫e−U(θ,x) dx\int e^{-U(\theta,x)}\,dx∫e−U(θ,x)dx. For a finite-second-moment probability initialization,

(Eμk∥θ−θ⋆∥2)1/2≤W2(μk,πN)+CN.\left(\mathbb E_{\mu_k}\|\theta-\theta^\star\|^2\right)^{1/2}\le W_2(\mu_k,\pi_N)+C_N.(Eμk​​∥θ−θ⋆∥2)1/2≤W2​(μk​,πN​)+CN​.

Proof. The established IPLA concentration theorem gives (EπN∥θ−θ⋆∥2)1/2≤CN(\mathbb E_{\pi_N}\|\theta-\theta^\star\|^2)^{1/2}\le C_N(EπN​​∥θ−θ⋆∥2)1/2≤CN​ from the same joint strong convexity. The parameter projection has operator norm at most one. For any coupling of μk\mu_kμk​ and πN\pi_NπN​, the L2L^2L2 triangle inequality bounds the projected distance to θ⋆\theta^\starθ⋆ by its coupling displacement plus that target radius. Taking the coupling infimum gives the displayed inequality. The coordinate convergence-finiteness theorem proves finite output transport first; this and concentration prove a finite projected second moment before converting the extended moment to the ordinary square-root integral.

07The sharp rate for the original independent Gaussian recursion §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Take a square-integrable scaled initial state on an actual probability space and an innovation sequence satisfying IPLA's original coordinatewise standard-Gaussian laws, independence across time and coordinates, and independence from initialization. Under the displayed C2C^2C2, joint curvature and step assumptions, the original noise-driven IPLA laws satisfy

(E∥θk−θ⋆∥2)1/2≤2dθNm+(1−mγ)kW2(Law⁡(Z0),πN)+6γ(L/m)L(dx+dθ/N).\left(\mathbb E\|\theta_k-\theta^\star\|^2\right)^{1/2}\le\sqrt{\frac{2d_\theta}{Nm}}+(1-m\gamma)^kW_2(\operatorname{Law}(Z_0),\pi_N)+6\gamma\sqrt{(L/m)L(d_x+d_\theta/N)}.(E∥θk​−θ⋆∥2)1/2≤Nm2dθ​​​+(1−mγ)kW2​(Law(Z0​),πN​)+6γ(L/m)L(dx​+dθ​/N)​.

Proof. Assemble the structural setting from the displayed potential, dimensions and numerical constants. The pushforward law of the square-integrable initial state has finite second moment by the mapped-measure characterization of square integrability. Apply the already proved sharp target-based parameter theorem to this initial law. The exact identity of the Gaussian recursion laws replaces its kernel-iterate law by the law of the original recursion. This proves the same improved bound in the original algorithm, with all potential assumptions and all distributional requirements displayed explicitly.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4; IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.1; IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.6; IPLA-SC, Section 3.3.1; IPLA-SC, Section 3.3.2; IPLA-SC, Section 3.3.3.

ipla_gaussian_parameter_error has the full original-style probability-space, initialization and innovation binders. It constructs IPLASetting from those structural inputs, derives MemLp id 2 of the initial pushforward using Lp.memLp, and applies ipla_parameter_error_target. Finally IPLASetting.law_eq_gaussian rewrites the actual output law. Neither an auxiliary reference process nor an assumed transport estimate appears among the theorem inputs.
