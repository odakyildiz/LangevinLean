The actual conditional Markov identity on the Brownian past

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The true diffusion Markov identity is derived from whole-future independence, measurable finite Euler maps, strong Euler convergence, bounded observable convergence and density. It concerns the full initialization/Brownian past and every square-integrable target observable.

01Actual observable representatives and adaptation §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Evaluation of a bounded continuous target function is its actual bounded composition with the canonical state. Evaluation of every square-integrable target observable at time aaa is measurable from the actual past at aaa.

Proof. The exact Gibbs state law transports the target observable representative through evaluation. The canonical state is past-measurable; compose its measurable version with the strongly measurable observable and transport back through almost-sure equality.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.4.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The bounded-continuous helper compares exact pullback and bounded-composition representatives. actualEvaluation_adapted_pastAt combines actual state adaptation with measurable composition and the measure-preserving evaluation representative.

02Proving the conditional mean is a function of the actual current state §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. E[f(Ya+t)∣Fa]\mathbb E[f(Y_{a+t})\mid\mathcal F_a]E[f(Ya+t​)∣Fa​] is measurable from YaY_aYa​ alone.

Proof. First take bounded continuous fff. The finite shifted Euler endpoint is a measurable function of (Ya,Wa)(Y_a,W_a)(Ya​,Wa​), and the entire future path is independent of the actual past. Conditional averaging therefore makes each finite Euler conditional mean a function of YaY_aYa​. The actual Euler endpoint converges strongly to Ya+tY_{a+t}Ya+t​; bounded-composition convergence and closedness of the conditional state-measurability property pass this fact to the diffusion. For arbitrary L2(π)L^2(\pi)L2(π) observables, use density of bounded continuous functions, continuity of evaluation and conditional expectation, and the same closed measurable subspace.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.16.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.5.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

actual_conditional_state_measurable applies the independent-input result to pointwiseEulerMap, uses actual shifted Euler convergence, then applies condExpL2_state_measurable_of_tendsto. A closed-preimage argument and bounded-continuous density extend the result to every target observable.

03The exact actual transition in the conditional identity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim.

E[f(Ya+t)∣Fa]=(Ptf)(Ya),a,t≥0.\mathbb E[f(Y_{a+t})\mid\mathcal F_a]=(P_tf)(Y_a),\qquad a,t\ge0.E[f(Ya+t​)∣Fa​]=(Pt​f)(Ya​),a,t≥0.

Proof. The preceding state-measurability result and square-integrable Doob–Dynkin factorization put the conditional mean in the range of actual current-state evaluation. Since this evaluation is adapted, its adjoint compression identifies that conditional mean exactly. Actual time homogeneity identifies the compression from aaa to a+ta+ta+t with the canonical time-zero operator PtP_tPt​. Substitution gives the displayed identity, without an assumed Markov property.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.5.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

actualEvaluation_markov_pastAt uses observable_range_of_state_measurable, the actual evaluation adaptation theorem, conditional_eq_evaluation_adjoint and shifted_compression_eq_op. Every premise is a previously proved fact about the constructed Brownian solution.
