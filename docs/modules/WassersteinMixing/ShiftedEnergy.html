Curvature, shifted errors and genuine conditional centering

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

This section derives the deterministic weighted interpolation inequality and uses the actual conditional-expectation projection in L2L^2L2 to remove the martingale cross term. These general inequalities take an exact error recursion, curvature bounds and conditional centering as hypotheses. The actual averaged-Hessian construction, conditional defect mean and realized stationary comparison prove all those hypotheses for the genuine Langevin and ULA processes.

01Positive contractions and weighted interpolation §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let TTT be a self-adjoint linear operator on a real inner product space with 0≤⟨x,Tx⟩≤∥x∥20\le\langle x,Tx\rangle\le\|x\|^20≤⟨x,Tx⟩≤∥x∥2. Then ∥Tx∥2≤⟨x,Tx⟩\|Tx\|^2\le\langle x,Tx\rangle∥Tx∥2≤⟨x,Tx⟩. If additionally a∥x∥2≤⟨x,Tx⟩≤b∥x∥2a\|x\|^2\le\langle x,Tx\rangle\le b\|x\|^2a∥x∥2≤⟨x,Tx⟩≤b∥x∥2, then

∥u−Tu+Tv∥2≤(1−a)∥u∥2+b∥v∥2.\|u-Tu+Tv\|^2\le(1-a)\|u\|^2+b\|v\|^2.∥u−Tu+Tv∥2≤(1−a)∥u∥2+b∥v∥2.

Proof. The quadratic function F(x)=⟨x,Tx⟩/2F(x)=\langle x,Tx\rangle/2F(x)=⟨x,Tx⟩/2 has gradient TxTxTx. Positivity and the upper quadratic bound give its convex lower model and unit-smooth upper model. The resulting gradient inequality yields ∥Tx∥2≤⟨x,Tx⟩\|Tx\|^2\le\langle x,Tx\rangle∥Tx∥2≤⟨x,Tx⟩. Expanding ∥u+T(v−u)∥2\|u+T(v-u)\|^2∥u+T(v−u)∥2, applying this inequality to v−uv-uv−u, and using symmetry gives the sharper upper bound ∥u∥2−⟨u,Tu⟩+⟨v,Tv⟩\|u\|^2-\langle u,Tu\rangle+\langle v,Tv\rangle∥u∥2−⟨u,Tu⟩+⟨v,Tv⟩. The two quadratic-form bounds finish the proof.

For T=hAT=hAT=hA, where AAA is self-adjoint with αI⪯A⪯βI\alpha I\preceq A\preceq\beta IαI⪯A⪯βI, the restriction hβ≤1h\beta\le1hβ≤1 supplies 0⪯T⪯I0\preceq T\preceq I0⪯T⪯I. Taking a=αha=\alpha ha=αh, b=βhb=\beta hb=βh gives

∥u−hAu+hAv∥2≤(1−αh)∥u∥2+hβ∥v∥2.\|u-hAu+hAv\|^2\le(1-\alpha h)\|u\|^2+h\beta\|v\|^2.∥u−hAu+hAv∥2≤(1−αh)∥u∥2+hβ∥v∥2.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.10.1.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

positive_contraction_cocoercive derives the gradient inequality from explicit quadratic models using the shared ULA result. weighted_interpolation expands the squared norm and applies the two quadratic-form estimates. curvature_weighted_interpolation constructs the scaled operator and checks all required signs from Parameters.

02The shifted identity and the martingale residual constant §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If z+=z−hAz+Dz^+=z-hAz+Dz+=z−hAz+D and D=g−g++MD=g-g^++MD=g−g++M, then

z++g+=(z+g)−hA(z+g)+hAg+M.z^++g^+=(z+g)-hA(z+g)+hAg+M.z++g+=(z+g)−hA(z+g)+hAg+M.

This follows by substituting the two equalities and cancelling the added terms. If ⟨U,M⟩=0\langle U,M\rangle=0⟨U,M⟩=0, expanding the square gives ∥U+M∥2=∥U∥2+∥M∥2\|U+M\|^2=\|U\|^2+\|M\|^2∥U+M∥2=∥U∥2+∥M∥2.

For any real ccc, if ∥D∥2≤3c\|D\|^2\le3c∥D∥2≤3c and ∥J∥2≤6c\|J\|^2\le6c∥J∥2≤6c, then

∥D−J∥2≤18c.\|D-J\|^2\le18c.∥D−J∥2≤18c.

Indeed the triangle inequality and 2ab≤a2+b22ab\le a^2+b^22ab≤a2+b2 give ∥D−J∥2≤2∥D∥2+2∥J∥2≤18c\|D-J\|^2\le2\|D\|^2+2\|J\|^2\le18c∥D−J∥2≤2∥D∥2+2∥J∥2≤18c. This is the source's residual constant, derived from its two separate estimates.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

shifted_error_identity proves the vector identity by linearity and module arithmetic. centered_energy_identity expands the actual Hilbert norm. martingale_residual_bound squares the triangle inequality and combines it with the nonnegative square of the difference of the two norms.

03Conditional expectation supplies the orthogonality §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let U,M∈L2(Q;H)U,M\in L^2(Q;H)U,M∈L2(Q;H), let G\mathcal GG be a sub-σ\sigmaσ-algebra, suppose UUU is G\mathcal GG-measurable and E[M∣G]=0\mathbb E[M\mid\mathcal G]=0E[M∣G]=0. Then ⟨U,M⟩L2=0\langle U,M\rangle_{L^2}=0⟨U,M⟩L2​=0. In particular, if ∥U∥L22≤qE+ℓ\|U\|_{L^2}^2\le qE+\ell∥U∥L22​≤qE+ℓ, then

∥U+M∥L22≤qE+ℓ+∥M∥L22.\|U+M\|_{L^2}^2\le qE+\ell+\|M\|_{L^2}^2.∥U+M∥L22​≤qE+ℓ+∥M∥L22​.

Proof. Conditional expectation is the orthogonal projection onto the closed subspace of G\mathcal GG-measurable square-integrable vectors. Hence ⟨M,U⟩=⟨E[M∣G],U⟩=0\langle M,U\rangle=\langle\mathbb E[M\mid\mathcal G],U\rangle=0⟨M,U⟩=⟨E[M∣G],U⟩=0. Symmetry of the real inner product gives the claimed orientation. Substitute this equality into the squared-norm expansion and apply the predictable energy bound.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

conditional_centering_orthogonal uses inner_condExpL2_eq_inner_fun for the actual conditional-expectation projection. conditional_shifted_energy applies this orthogonality and the exact energy identity before using the predictable estimate. It introduces no new axiom or substitute conditional-expectation operation.
