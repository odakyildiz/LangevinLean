The fresh-noise anchored MALA pair and its actual powers

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The fresh-noise pair retains the current state next to the actual accepted or rejected output. Its exact kernel identity links the concrete random update to the already proved reversible Gaussian pair, and hence to reversible anchored finite-step laws.

01Identifying the actual sampled one-step pair §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The fresh pair kernel is x↦δx⊗K(x,⋅)x\mapsto\delta_x\otimes K(x,\cdot)x↦δx​⊗K(x,⋅), where KKK is the actual explicit MALA kernel. For any initial law, its integrated pair is the accepted Metropolis flow plus rejected diagonal mass. In particular π⊗K\pi\otimes Kπ⊗K is exactly the proved reversible Gibbs pair.

Proof. Direct accepted/rejected partition gives first marginal δx\delta_xδx​; mapping the second coordinate gives the actual explicit kernel. A pair whose first marginal is a Dirac measure has deterministic first coordinate almost surely, so it equals the corresponding product with its second marginal. Integrate this identity. Moving the actual proposal map through the weighted measures identifies the result with the Gaussian Metropolis pair construction.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.34.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.34.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.7; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.6; ULA-SC, Section 3.39.1.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

freshJointKernel and freshJointMeasure keep the input coordinate in the actual weighted output. The deterministic-first-coordinate lemma proves their product form. explicitKernel_pair_eq is valid for every sigma-finite initial law; explicitKernel_gibbs_pair_eq gives the exact stationary identification.

02The actual anchored endpoint-update recurrence §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim.

μ⊗Kn+1=(I⊗K)∗(μ⊗Kn),μ⊗K0=(x↦(x,x))∗μ.\mu\otimes K^{n+1}=(I\otimes K)_*(\mu\otimes K^n),\qquad\mu\otimes K^0=(x\mapsto(x,x))_*\mu.μ⊗Kn+1=(I⊗K)∗​(μ⊗Kn),μ⊗K0=(x↦(x,x))∗​μ.

Proof. Associativity proves that the recursive kernel power can advance by its final transition: Kn+1=K∘KnK^{n+1}=K\circ K^nKn+1=K∘Kn. Composing the product pair with the kernel that keeps its first coordinate and updates its second gives precisely that power. The zero-step identity is the diagonal law of the identity kernel.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

kernelPower_succ_last proves the alternate composition order by induction. anchored_pair_succ uses the exact parallel-kernel composition identity, and anchored_pair_zero uses the identity-kernel product law.

03Actual reversible finite-step endpoint pairs §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The actual explicit anchored pair Jn=π⊗KnJ_n=\pi\otimes K^nJn​=π⊗Kn is a probability law, is invariant under swapping, has both marginals π\piπ, and follows the endpoint-update recurrence above.

Proof. The explicit kernel is Markov. Its one-step pair is exactly the proved reversible Gaussian pair, so generic kernel-power reversal applies. First-marginal preservation and swap symmetry give both Gibbs marginals. The generic anchored successor theorem supplies the actual update recursion.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.5.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

explicitPowerJointLaw uses the explicit fresh-Gaussian kernel directly, avoiding a conditional-kernel identification. Its probability, swap, marginal and successor theorems specialize the proved actual kernel identities.
