References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

01The numerical parameters and their elementary bounds §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Under the displayed restrictions, β>0\beta>0β>0, κ≥1\kappa\ge1κ≥1, 0≤q<10\le q<10≤q<1, h≤1/βh\le1/\betah≤1/β, and B≥0B\ge0B≥0.

Proof. Since β≥α>0\beta\ge\alpha>0β≥α>0, division by α\alphaα preserves β≥α\beta\ge\alphaβ≥α and gives κ≥1\kappa\ge1κ≥1. Multiplication by h≥0h\ge0h≥0 gives αh≤βh≤1\alpha h\le\beta h\le1αh≤βh≤1, so q≥0q\ge0q≥0. The strict inequality αh>0\alpha h>0αh>0 gives q<1q<1q<1. Dividing hβ≤1h\beta\le1hβ≤1 by β>0\beta>0β>0 gives the step restriction. Finally h>0h>0h>0 and the nonnegativity of the square root imply B≥0B\ge0B≥0.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

Parameters records all numerical hypotheses. condition, contraction and bias define their literal expressions. The remaining declarations divide only by a proved positive curvature constant; the contraction inequalities use the supplied step restriction and positive step.

02The first-order potential contract and the original Hessian bounds §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. On a real inner product space, the potential contract requires V∈C2V\in C^2V∈C2, α\alphaα-strong convexity and a β\betaβ-Lipschitz gradient:

V(y)≥V(x)+⟨∇V(x),y−x⟩+α2∥y−x∥2,∥∇V(y)−∇V(x)∥≤β∥y−x∥.V(y)\ge V(x)+\langle\nabla V(x),y-x\rangle+\frac\alpha2\|y-x\|^2,\qquad\|\nabla V(y)-\nabla V(x)\|\le\beta\|y-x\|.V(y)≥V(x)+⟨∇V(x),y−x⟩+2α​∥y−x∥2,∥∇V(y)−∇V(x)∥≤β∥y−x∥.

The original source Hessian bounds imply this contract with exactly the same constants, as proved in the Hessian-assumption argument. The contract contains no invariant law, semigroup, spectral basis or comparison conclusion; those objects and properties are constructed separately.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

PotentialAssumptions is a proposition-valued structure containing ContDiff ℝ 2 V, StrongConvexOn and a LipschitzWith gradient condition. None of its fields asserts an invariant measure, a generator realization or a spectral basis.
