Constructing the actual ULA invariant probability law on an independent Gaussian past

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

A contracting additive-noise update has an invariant probability law constructed from a fixed random vector on an independent infinite noise sequence. The operator acts on the complete space L2L^2L2; a Banach fixed point supplies existence and a finite second moment. Independence of the most recent noise from the older past proves invariance for the original transition. Applied to the actual Gaussian ULA update, this gives an invariant law and convergence from every finite-second-moment initialization without an invariant-law premise.

01The independent past, its tail and its newest innovation §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. For an innovation probability law η\etaη, put Q=η⊗NQ=\eta^{\otimes\mathbb N}Q=η⊗N on sequences ω=(ω0,ω1,…)\omega=(\omega_0,\omega_1,\ldots)ω=(ω0​,ω1​,…). The tail map is τω=(ω1,ω2,…)\tau\omega=(\omega_1,\omega_2,\ldots)τω=(ω1​,ω2​,…).

Claim. The map τ\tauτ preserves QQQ, and the tail is independent of ω0\omega_0ω0​.

Proof. Every finite set of coordinates has its product law. Reindexing a finite cylinder by the injective successor map therefore leaves its probability unchanged; the product-measure identity gives measure preservation. Independence follows because every finite set of tail coordinates has indices disjoint from {0}\{0\}{0}. Independence of the full tail process follows from these finite-coordinate independence statements.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

probability is the actual infinite product measure and has a probability instance. tail_measurePreserving applies the injective reindexing identity for Measure.infinitePi. tail_independent_head starts from the independent coordinate family, proves the finite index sets disjoint and extends finite-coordinate independence to the whole process.

02A contraction on the complete space of square-integrable past functions §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction and claim. Suppose F:E→EF:E\to EF:E→E is qqq-Lipschitz with 0≤q<10\le q<10≤q<1, the innovation has finite second moment, and a∈Ra\in\mathbb Ra∈R. On L2(Q;E)L^2(Q;E)L2(Q;E) define

Φ(X)=F(X∘τ)+aω0.\Phi(X)=F(X\circ\tau)+a\omega_0.Φ(X)=F(X∘τ)+aω0​.

The tail pullback is a linear isometry because τ\tauτ preserves QQQ; the head coordinate is square-integrable because its law is η\etaη. A Lipschitz map has at most linear growth, so composition with FFF preserves square integrability on the probability space. The common additive noise cancels in a difference, giving

∥Φ(X)−Φ(Y)∥L2≤q∥X∘τ−Y∘τ∥L2=q∥X−Y∥L2.\|\Phi(X)-\Phi(Y)\|_{L^2}\le q\|X\circ\tau-Y\circ\tau\|_{L^2} =q\|X-Y\|_{L^2}.∥Φ(X)−Φ(Y)∥L2​≤q∥X∘τ−Y∘τ∥L2​=q∥X−Y∥L2​.

Thus Φ\PhiΦ is a strict contraction on a complete space.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.13.5.

03The fixed random variable and its invariant law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. There is a square-integrable random vector X∗X_*X∗​ with

X∗(ω)=F(X∗(τω))+aω0Q-almost everywhere.X_*(\omega)=F(X_*(\tau\omega))+a\omega_0\qquad Q\text{-almost everywhere}.X∗​(ω)=F(X∗​(τω))+aω0​Q-almost everywhere.

Its law ν\nuν is a probability law with finite second moment and is invariant under the independent-noise transition x↦F(x)+aξx\mapsto F(x)+a\xix↦F(x)+aξ, ξ∼η\xi\sim\etaξ∼η.

Proof. Banach's fixed-point theorem applied to Φ\PhiΦ constructs X∗∈L2X_*\in L^2X∗​∈L2. Equality in L2L^2L2 yields the displayed almost-everywhere recursion after choosing representatives. The pushforward law is a probability measure, and square integrability transfers to it. Since the tail preserves QQQ, X∗∘τX_*\circ\tauX∗​∘τ has law ν\nuν. Since the tail is independent of ω0\omega_0ω0​, the pair (X∗∘τ,ω0)(X_*\circ\tau,\omega_0)(X∗​∘τ,ω0​) has product law ν⊗η\nu\otimes\etaν⊗η. Taking laws in the recursion therefore gives Tν=νT\nu=\nuTν=ν for the original independent-noise transition.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.13.3.

04Specializing the construction to the actual Gaussian ULA transition §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For the potential contract and numerical parameters, take F(x)=x−h∇V(x)F(x)=x-h\nabla V(x)F(x)=x−h∇V(x), q=1−αhq=1-\alpha hq=1−αh, η=N(0,I)\eta=\mathcal N(0,I)η=N(0,I) and a=2ha=\sqrt{2h}a=2h​. The resulting law π^\widehat\piπ is a probability measure with finite second moment and satisfies the actual Gaussian ULA invariance equality Tπ^=π^T\widehat\pi=\widehat\piTπ=π. For every probability initialization μ\muμ with finite second moment,

W2(Tkμ,π^)≤qkW2(μ,π^),W2(Tkμ,π^)⟶0.W_2(T^k\mu,\widehat\pi)\le q^kW_2(\mu,\widehat\pi), \qquad W_2(T^k\mu,\widehat\pi)\longrightarrow0.W2​(Tkμ,π)≤qkW2​(μ,π),W2​(Tkμ,π)⟶0.

Proof. Common-noise gradient-step contraction proves the required Lipschitz bound for FFF, and 0≤q<10\le q<10≤q<1 is already established. Standard Gaussian innovations have finite second moment, so the general construction applies. Its invariance equality is exactly the existing Gaussian law transition. Both initial laws have finite second moments, so their transport distance is finite. The actual ULA contraction gives the geometric estimate. Finally qk→0q^k\to0qk→0 and the distance is nonnegative, so squeezing proves attraction.
