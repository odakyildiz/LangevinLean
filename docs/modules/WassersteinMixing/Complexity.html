The exact scale-invariant step and logarithmic iteration count

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

This section proves scalar accuracy arithmetic. For α,β,d,ε>0\alpha,\beta,d,\varepsilon>0α,β,d,ε>0, define

h=1βmin⁡(1,ε12d),K=βαmax⁡(1,12dε).h=\frac1\beta\min\left(1,\frac{\varepsilon}{12\sqrt d}\right), \qquad K=\frac\beta\alpha\max\left(1,\frac{12\sqrt d}{\varepsilon}\right).h=β1​min(1,12d​ε​),K=αβ​max(1,ε12d​​).

The constants 666 and 121212, the maximum, minimum and logarithm are the literal quantities of Corollary 1.1. A convergence estimate for actual sampling laws is not established by this scalar argument.

01The chosen step and its reciprocal §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Put m=max⁡(1,12d/ε)m=\max(1,12\sqrt d/\varepsilon)m=max(1,12d​/ε). Then h=1/(βm)>0h=1/(\beta m)>0h=1/(βm)>0, h≤1/βh\le1/\betah≤1/β, and αhK=1\alpha hK=1αhK=1.

Proof. If ε≤12d\varepsilon\le12\sqrt dε≤12d​, the minimum in the step is its second term and the maximum is 12d/ε12\sqrt d/\varepsilon12d​/ε. Otherwise they both select their first terms. In either case multiplication verifies h=1/(βm)h=1/(\beta m)h=1/(βm). Positivity follows from β>0\beta>0β>0, m≥1m\ge1m≥1, and the upper bound follows by replacing the minimum by 111. Finally substitute K=βm/αK=\beta m/\alphaK=βm/α and cancel the positive factors.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

paperStep and iterationPrefactor retain the exact minimum and maximum. paperStep_eq_inv checks the two branches explicitly. The positivity, stability and reciprocal-product lemmas clear only nonzero factors established from the positive hypotheses.

02Allocating half of the accuracy to the bias §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For α>0\alpha>0α>0, β,d≥0\beta,d\ge0β,d≥0,

α(β/α)βd=βd.\sqrt\alpha\sqrt{(\beta/\alpha)\beta d}=\beta\sqrt d.α​(β/α)βd​=βd​.

Both sides are nonnegative and their squares agree after cancellation of α\alphaα, which proves the identity. For the selected step and d>0d>0d>0, it follows that

α 6h(β/α)βd=6dm≤ε2.\sqrt\alpha\,6h\sqrt{(\beta/\alpha)\beta d} =\frac{6\sqrt d}{m}\le\frac\varepsilon2.α​6h(β/α)βd​=m6d​​≤2ε​.

The last inequality is precisely 12d≤εm12\sqrt d\le\varepsilon m12d​≤εm, supplied by the second term in the maximum.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

sqrt_curvature_scale proves equality by equality of squares and nonnegativity. paperStep_bias_le substitutes this identity and the reciprocal step formula, then uses the maximum's lower bound to prove the half-budget inequality.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

geometric_transient_le bounds the geometric factor by an exponential, uses monotonicity of the exponential to invert the logarithmic bound and checks the final quotient. paper_complexity_bound proves the admissible dimensionless step, invokes the reciprocal-product identity and adds the two half-budgets. No mixing hypothesis is hidden in these scalar statements.
