Moments and coupling error of the actual random MALA path

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The canonical sampled MALA recursion is converted to a genuine L2L^2L2 random variable. Its marginal remains exactly Gibbs, and its mean distance from the synchronous Euler coordinate satisfies the proved cumulative rejection estimate.

Statement and proof

01Actual representatives, Gibbs laws and square integrability §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The sampled input and paired states are measurable. The first coordinate is the actual Euler recursion; the second has Gibbs law and finite second moment, hence defines an actual L2L^2L2 variable with that same law.

Proof. Compose the measurable sampled update through finite recursion. Projecting its exact law gives the established MALA marginal, which is Gibbs by invariance at each finite step. Pull the Gibbs finite second moment through this exact pushforward to obtain square integrability; passing to the L2L^2L2 class preserves its almost-everywhere representative and law.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.19.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.20.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.20.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.23.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.17; ULA-SC, Section 3.39.6; ULA-SC, Section 3.42.1.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The initial measurability lemmas justify the sampled pushforwards. state_snd_law and state_snd_memLp establish the true marginal and moment before defining mala. mala_coe and mala_law retain the representative and exact law.

02The actual accumulated rejection distance §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Under the stated small-step conditions, the expected distance between the actual MALA state and the lifted Euler state is at most nCrrnCr\sqrt rnCrr​, where CCC is the proved Gaussian rejection constant for the potential.

Proof. Both coordinates are square integrable, so their difference norm is integrable. The actual joint law equals the established coupled-chain law. Transfer its cumulative rejection-cost estimate through that exact law and through the almost-everywhere representatives of the L2L^2L2 states.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.34.22; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.25; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.26; ULA-SC, Section 3.39.6.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

distance_integrable uses the two true L2L^2L2 coordinates. distance_integral_le rewrites the actual expectation under state_law and invokes the proved coupled MALA/Euler rejection estimate.
