An actual common-randomness Euler and Metropolis update

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

Independent Gaussian proposals and independent uniform decisions are combined into a single innovation law. A deterministic paired update advances Euler and MALA states with common Gaussian input, and its actual law is the established coupled-step law.

Statement and proof

01The paired actual sampling map §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. At paired states (x,y)(x,y)(x,y), update the Euler coordinate by x−h∇V(x)+2h ξx-h\nabla V(x)+\sqrt{2h}\,\xix−h∇V(x)+2h​ξ. In the second coordinate use the actual Gaussian proposal from yyy, accepted by an independent uniform threshold and otherwise leave yyy unchanged. The map is measurable and its first coordinate is exactly the Euler update, including along the full recursion.

Proof. The innovation measure is the product of standard Gaussian and unit-interval probability. Continuity of the actual proposal and measurability of its acceptance event give measurable branches; projection to the first coordinate removes the acceptance decision.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.20.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.20.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.34.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.6.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

innovationLaw is the actual Gaussian/uniform product. update is the paired threshold update. update_fst and chain_fst identify the actual Euler coordinate; update_measurable combines proposal and threshold measurability.

02Identifying every actual paired Markov law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The sampled paired update has exactly the coupled Euler/MALA law, and its iterated laws agree at every finite time.

Proof. Integrate the uniform decision using the proved acceptance/rejection identity. The resulting weighted Gaussian maps are precisely the coupled-step construction. Starting from the same paired initial law, substitute that exact one-step equality at each recursion step to obtain equality of all iterated laws.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.20.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.33.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.33.6; ULA-SC, Section 3.33.9; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.14; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.15; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.6; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.9.
