The filtration at step nnn contains the actual initialization and all additive Brownian input up to nhnhnh. Adaptation of the diffusion is obtained from its integral construction; adaptation of the Euler chain follows from its recursion. The actual curvature consequently preserves this same past.

Statement and proof

01Identifying the common initialization and path §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The Euler and global-diffusion initial variables agree, and the actual grid reference equals the endpoint of any canonical solution whose horizon reaches that grid time.

Proof. The diagonal initial coupling has zero squared difference, so its two L2L^2L2 variables agree. Uniqueness of the Langevin integral equation identifies the segmented reference with the canonical finite-horizon solution. The additive Brownian input is the actual noise measured from time zero.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.22.23; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.29.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.29.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.44.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.17.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

actualInitial_eq_semigroupInitial uses the exact diagonal coupling cost. actualReference_eq_solutionOn invokes uniqueness for the actual Brownian integral equation, so no path-identification premise is added.

02Constructing the increasing filtration §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. Let Fn=σ(Y0,Bs:0≤s≤nh)\mathcal F_n=\sigma(Y_0,B_s:0\le s\le nh)Fn​=σ(Y0​,Bs​:0≤s≤nh), interpreted with the actual additive Brownian representatives. Inclusion of time intervals gives Fn⊆Fn+1\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal F_{n+1}Fn​⊆Fn+1​. The constructed reference belongs to L2(Fn)L^2(\mathcal F_n)L2(Fn​).

Proof. The initial variable and each permitted Brownian evaluation generate the stated sub-sigma-algebra. The Picard solution remains in the closed subspace of random variables measurable with respect to that past; uniqueness identifies it with the reference endpoint.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.25.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.8.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

actualPast specializes the natural-past construction. actualPast_mono proves inclusion of every generating time evaluation and actualFiltration packages it. actualReference_adapted uses the proved natural-past theorem for the actual integral solution.

03Adaptation of innovations, chain and curvature §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The normalized innovation is the difference of the actual Brownian inputs at (n+1)h(n+1)h(n+1)h and nhnhnh, divided by 2h\sqrt{2h}2h​, hence is measurable by Fn+1\mathcal F_{n+1}Fn+1​. The Euler state and synchronous error are Fn\mathcal F_nFn​-measurable, and AnA_nAn​ maps L2(Fn)L^2(\mathcal F_n)L2(Fn​) into itself.

Proof. Use the exact increment identity. Induct through the Euler update using continuity of the gradient and the increasing filtration. Subtracting adapted states gives the adapted error. The curvature-preservation theorem then applies because both of its endpoint states are adapted.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.32.13; ULA-SC, Section 3.44.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.6; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.7.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The proof works inside the closed measurable submodule lpMeas, whose closure under sums and scalar multiplication handles the recursion. actualCurvature_preserves_past supplies the derived adaptation of both actual endpoint states to the actual random-curvature theorem.
