Reversal symmetry of actual anchored kernel powers

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

One-step detailed balance implies reversal of every actual finite-step pair μ(dx)Kn(x,dy)\mu(dx)K^n(x,dy)μ(dx)Kn(x,dy). The proof uses nonnegative observable correlations and actual kernel integration, so the original initial coordinate remains present throughout.

01Iterating the actual detailed-balance correlation §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. With (Kf)(x)=∫f(y)K(x,dy)(Kf)(x)=\int f(y)K(x,dy)(Kf)(x)=∫f(y)K(x,dy), one-step pair reversal gives

∫f Kg dμ=∫Kf g dμ,∫f Kng dμ=∫Knf g dμ\int f\,Kg\,d\mu=\int Kf\,g\,d\mu,\qquad\int f\,K^ng\,d\mu=\int K^nf\,g\,d\mu∫fKgdμ=∫Kfgdμ,∫fKngdμ=∫Knfgdμ

for measurable nonnegative observables.

Proof. Product-kernel integration expresses the first pairing as the actual two-time integral. Swap invariance exchanges the two coordinates. For the iterated identity, move one averaging operator to the other observable by one-step balance, then apply the induction hypothesis. Kernel integration preserves measurability at every iterate.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

average is actual kernel integration. Its measurability lemmas justify each iteration. average_balance changes variables under the swap law and average_iterate_balance proves the iterated pairing by induction.

02Actual powers, rectangles and their marginals §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Define K0K^0K0 as the identity kernel and use actual kernel composition for successors. The pair law μ⊗Kn\mu\otimes K^nμ⊗Kn is swap invariant and has both marginals μ\muμ.

Proof. Kernel integration identifies the averaging action of the power with the iterated averaging function. Pair rectangle masses are indicator correlations, so the proved iterated balance gives equality with their reversed rectangles. Equality on rectangles proves full measure reversal. The first marginal is μ\muμ because the kernel is Markov; swapping gives the second.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.35.5.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

kernelPower is the literal recursive kernel composition. kernelPower_average and kernelPower_rectangle connect it with the actual correlations. kernelPower_pair_swap uses product-measure extensionality. Its marginal theorems derive the exact laws.

03Applying the power construction to the proved MALA pair §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. Disintegrate the actual reversible Gibbs MALA pair to obtain a Markov kernel. Its initial-law product is exactly the original pair. The actual kernel-power pair is therefore a probability measure, is reversible, and has both Gibbs marginals.

Proof. The disintegration theorem identifies the original pair with its first marginal times its conditional kernel. Its first marginal is already proved to be Gibbs. Apply the generic power reversal and marginal theorems to this exact one-step identity.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

kernel is the actual conditional kernel of jointLaw, not a postulated transition. kernel_pair_eq applies disintegration. powerJointLaw and its law theorems instantiate the proved generic kernel-power results.
