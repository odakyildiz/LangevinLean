Strong limits of bounded increasing positive operators

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

A sequence of positive operators with 0≤An≤An+1≤cI0\le A_n\le A_{n+1}\le cI0≤An​≤An+1​≤cI has a bounded linear strong limit. This supplies an actual corrector operator from increasing finite sums, without choosing an eigenbasis.

01The quadratic estimate behind convergence §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If 0≤A≤cI0\le A\le cI0≤A≤cI, then ∥Ax∥2≤c⟨Ax,x⟩R\|Ax\|^2\le c\langle Ax,x\rangle_{\mathbb R}∥Ax∥2≤c⟨Ax,x⟩R​.

Proof. Positivity implies self-adjointness. The positive operators cI−AcI-AcI−A and AAA commute, so their product is positive. Its quadratic form is c⟨Ax,x⟩−⟨A2x,x⟩c\langle Ax,x\rangle-\langle A^2x,x\ranglec⟨Ax,x⟩−⟨A2x,x⟩. Self-adjointness identifies the last term with ∥Ax∥2\|Ax\|^2∥Ax∥2. Rearrangement yields the bound.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The local scalar restriction gives the real part of the complex inner product. positive_operator_square_le uses positivity of the commuting product and the proved symmetric pairing identity.

02Constructing the genuine bounded strong limit §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. There is a bounded linear GGG with Anx→GxA_nx\to GxAn​x→Gx for every xxx, ∥G∥≤c\|G\|\le c∥G∥≤c, and 0≤G≤cI0\le G\le cI0≤G≤cI.

Proof. The increasing real numbers ⟨Anx,x⟩\langle A_nx,x\rangle⟨An​x,x⟩ are bounded by c∥x∥2c\|x\|^2c∥x∥2, hence are Cauchy. For m≥nm\ge nm≥n, apply the preceding estimate to Am−AnA_m-A_nAm​−An​; its scalar quadratic difference tending to zero forces AnxA_nxAn​x to be Cauchy. Completeness gives the pointwise limit. Passing sums and scalar multiples through limits proves linearity, and the uniform norm bound proves continuity. Symmetry and the lower and upper quadratic inequalities pass to the limit.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

monotone_operator_cauchy uses monotone convergence of the scalar quadratic forms. exists_monotone_operator_limit constructs the linear map from the actual pointwise limits. strong_limit_order transfers symmetry and both form inequalities through those limits.

03A commuting operator preserves the sharp energy factor §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If GGG is self-adjoint, ∥G∥≤h\|G\|\le h∥G∥≤h, B≥0B\ge0B≥0, and GB=BGGB=BGGB=BG, then

⟨Gx,BGx⟩R≤h2⟨x,Bx⟩R.\langle Gx,BGx\rangle_{\mathbb R}\le h^2\langle x,Bx\rangle_{\mathbb R}.⟨Gx,BGx⟩R​≤h2⟨x,Bx⟩R​.

Proof. The operator h2I−G2h^2I-G^2h2I−G2 is positive because its quadratic form is h2∥x∥2−∥Gx∥2h^2\|x\|^2-\|Gx\|^2h2∥x∥2−∥Gx∥2. It commutes with BBB, so their product is positive. Its nonnegative quadratic form, together with symmetry and commutation, is exactly the displayed difference.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

commuting_energy_bound constructs the positive squared-norm deficit, proves its commutation with the energy operator and expands the resulting positive product pairing.
