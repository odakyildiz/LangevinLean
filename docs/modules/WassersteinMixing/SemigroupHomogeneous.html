Time homogeneity of the actual diffusion pair law

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The actual state/future-path input law is the same at all deterministic cut times. Finite Euler maps preserve that input-law equality; passing their genuine strong limits proves time homogeneity of the actual two-time diffusion law and its observable compression.

Statement and proof

01Equal actual input laws pass to diffusion pair laws §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For a,b,t≥0a,b,t\ge0a,b,t≥0,

Law⁡(Ya,Ya+t)=Law⁡(Yb,Yb+t),Ua∗Ua+t=Pt.\operatorname{Law}(Y_a,Y_{a+t})=\operatorname{Law}(Y_b,Y_{b+t}),\qquad U_a^*U_{a+t}=P_t.Law(Ya​,Ya+t​)=Law(Yb​,Yb+t​),Ua∗​Ua+t​=Pt​.

Proof. Each shifted finite Euler endpoint is the same measurable function of its current state and future Brownian path. Those actual paired inputs have equal laws at all cuts, so their anchored Euler output laws agree. The actual Euler endpoints converge strongly to their respective diffusion endpoints. Their pair laws consequently converge weakly, and uniqueness of the weak limit gives equality of the actual diffusion pair laws. Equality of pair laws determines the observable compression; take the second cut to be zero.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.16.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

shiftedEuler_pair_law maps the proved equality of state/future input laws through the same actual Euler map. state_pair_law passes the actual Euler limits to their pair-law limits. shifted_compression_eq_op invokes equality of compression from actual pair laws.
