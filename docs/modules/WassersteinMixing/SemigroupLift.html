The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. Appending an independent coordinate to the probability space changes neither the Langevin dynamics nor their laws. Measure-preserving pullback commutes with the true drift, Bochner time integral, Euler recursion and stationary observable evaluation.

Statement and proof

01Lifting genuine solutions and observables §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Pulling a Langevin solution and its additive noise through the first projection of an independent product gives the same integral equation on the extended space. Euler endpoints and stationary evaluations lift in the same way, and their laws are unchanged.

Proof. The first projection is measure-preserving because the added measure is a probability. Its L2L^2L2 pullback is a linear isometry. The actual Lipschitz drift commutes with this pullback by comparing representatives. Bounded linear pullback commutes with the Bochner integral; induction gives the Euler identity. Exact map composition and measure preservation give the law and observable-evaluation identities.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.13.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.16.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.16.3; Kinetic, Section 3.14.1; Kinetic, Section 3.14.2.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

langevin_integral_sourceLift transports the actual integral equation through the pullback isometry. additiveEulerChain_sourceLift inducts on its recursion. sourceLift_law and stationaryEvaluation_sourceLift use exact measure-preserving composition and representative equality.
