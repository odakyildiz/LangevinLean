Actual shifted Langevin Euler convergence

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

Subtracting the actual integral equation at two times yields the shifted equation started from the actual current state. Applying deterministic additive Euler convergence to this genuine L2L^2L2 equation gives both fixed-terminal and moving-grid limits.

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For a,t≥0a,t\ge0a,t≥0,

Ya+t−Ya=−∫0t∇V(Ya+s) ds+2(Ba+t−Ba).Y_{a+t}-Y_a=-\int_0^t\nabla V(Y_{a+s})\,ds+\sqrt2(B_{a+t}-B_a).Ya+t​−Ya​=−∫0t​∇V(Ya+s​)ds+2​(Ba+t​−Ba​).

Euler recursion started from YaY_aYa​ with these actual future increments converges in L2L^2L2 to Ya+tY_{a+t}Ya+t​, including along variable steps and counts whose elapsed time tends to ttt.

Proof. Subtract the global integral equations at a+ta+ta+t and aaa. Additivity and translation of the integrals give the shifted drift integral, while the exact Brownian increment theorem gives the shifted noise. The actual gradient map on L2L^2L2 is Lipschitz and the global path is continuous, so the proved additive Euler convergence theorem applies.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.13.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.16.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.16.9; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.44.2.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

shifted_state_integral derives the actual restarted equation by interval-integral subtraction. shiftedEuler_tendsto uses equal elapsed subdivisions; shiftedEuler_variable_tendsto applies the general moving-step theorem to the actual Lipschitz drift and shifted Brownian input.
