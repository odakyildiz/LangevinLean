Real-range preservation by the actual Langevin operator

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The canonical Brownian diffusion evaluations are actual measure-preserving observations. Their naturality identifies the complex transition on real observables with the actual real transition at every time. The real-range condition needed by the corrector is consequently derived.

Statement and proof

01Exact intertwining of the actual real and complex transitions §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For every pointwise real isometry iii, every actual time ttt and real observable fff,

PtC(if)=i(PtRf).P_t^{\mathbb C}(if)=i(P_t^{\mathbb R}f).PtC​(if)=i(PtR​f).

Proof. Gibbs stationarity makes evaluation at zero and at ttt isometric pullbacks. Apply the exact compression embedding theorem to these two actual observations. This proves range preservation and applies in particular to the concrete orthonormal-coordinate inclusion, with no norm or dimension loss.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

op_mapLp specializes compression_mapLp to the actual canonical state evaluations and their proved Gibbs laws. op_mem_real_range gives the explicit range witness. op_coordinateLp uses the concrete coordinate embedding of the physical state space.

02The corrector extraction is the actual real transition §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. A semigroup assembled from these actual canonical operators preserves the real coordinate image. Its extracted real transition i∗Ptii^*P_tii∗Pt​i equals the actual real canonical transition itself.

Proof. The preceding intertwining identity puts every output in the embedded real range, which is exactly the projection-fixed condition. Apply the embedding adjoint to the same identity and use i∗i=Ii^*i=Ii∗i=I to identify the extracted transition.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

preservesReal_of_op_eq rewrites the supplied operator identification and applies the proved actual range theorem. realOp_eq_op rewrites through op_mapLp and cancels the isometric embedding with adjoint_embed.
