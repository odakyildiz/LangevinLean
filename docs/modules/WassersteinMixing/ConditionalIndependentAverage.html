Conditional averaging over an independent path input

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

Let XXX be measurable from a past sigma-algebra F\mathcal FF, and let ZZZ be independent of all of F\mathcal FF. For an integrable measurable vector function FFF, conditioning F(X,Z)F(X,Z)F(X,Z) on the past integrates only the independent input. The input space may be a whole path space.

Statement and proof

01Factorization after restriction to a past event §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For every A∈FA\in\mathcal FA∈F, the joint law under the restricted measure satisfies

Law⁡Q∣A(X,Z)=Law⁡Q∣A(X)⊗Law⁡Q(Z).\operatorname{Law}_{Q|_A}(X,Z)=\operatorname{Law}_{Q|_A}(X)\otimes\operatorname{Law}_{Q}(Z).LawQ∣A​​(X,Z)=LawQ∣A​​(X)⊗LawQ​(Z).

Proof. Evaluate both sides on a measurable rectangle. Its preimage is the intersection of a past event, obtained from XXX and AAA, with an event determined by ZZZ. Independence factorizes its mass. Equality on all rectangles determines the product measure.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

independent_input_restrict_joint_map uses product-measure extensionality and the definition of independence on the two preimage events. The independent input retains its unrestricted marginal.

02The genuine conditional mean depends only on the current state §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim.

E[F(X,Z)∣F]=∫F(X,z) Law⁡(Z)(dz).\mathbb E[F(X,Z)\mid\mathcal F]=\int F(X,z)\,\operatorname{Law}(Z)(dz).E[F(X,Z)∣F]=∫F(X,z)Law(Z)(dz).

Proof. Product integrability gives integrability of the averaged function and its measurable dependence on XXX. On every past event, the restricted pair-law factorization and Fubini identify its integral with the integral of F(X,Z)F(X,Z)F(X,Z). These are the defining properties of conditional expectation. If XXX is only almost surely past-measurable, replace it by a measurable version, apply the result and transport back through almost-sure equality. The conditional mean is then measurable from XXX alone.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

condExp_independent_input_average applies the set-integral characterization of conditional expectation. The final theorem uses the measurable version furnished by AEStronglyMeasurable and proves measurability for the sigma-algebra generated by the original state.
