From stationary comparison to the actual invariant-law bounds

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

These reusable conclusions identify the comparison target with the actual Gibbs law and its chain with actual Gaussian ULA iterates. The constructed invariant law, its proved attraction and the genuine transport triangle give the exact bias, convergence and accuracy bounds once the concrete comparison is supplied.

01Actual law identifications and finite transport §

Mathematical statement and proof

Set-up. Identify the stationary reference law with π\piπ, the chain law with KhnπK_h^n\piKhn​π, and the parameter dimension with the physical dimension. The proved stationary comparison then bounds W2(π,Khnπ)W_2(\pi,K_h^n\pi)W2​(π,Khn​π) uniformly and proves finiteness. Gaussian ULA iteration preserves probability, and finite second moments imply finite transport from any such iterate to the constructed invariant law πh\pi_hπh​.

Proof. Substitute the exact law identifications into the comparison coupling. Iteration is the actual Gaussian Markov recursion. Its proved contraction transports the finite initial second-moment distance to the invariant law.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.21.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.33.11; ULA-SC, Section 3.34.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.7.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

ULAPoissonComparison adds only dimension and actual-law equalities to the generic comparison. The stationary-start theorems substitute these equalities. Probability and finiteness of iterates use the actual Gaussian Markov law and the independently constructed invariant law.

02The exact invariant bias and finite-time convergence §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim.

W2(π,πh)≤6hκβd,W2(Khnμ,π)≤(1−αh)nW2(μ,πh)+6hκβd,κ=β/α.W_2(\pi,\pi_h)\le6h\sqrt{\kappa\beta d},\qquad W_2(K_h^n\mu,\pi)\le(1-\alpha h)^nW_2(\mu,\pi_h)+6h\sqrt{\kappa\beta d},\quad\kappa=\beta/\alpha.W2​(π,πh​)≤6hκβd​,W2​(Khn​μ,π)≤(1−αh)nW2​(μ,πh​)+6hκβd​,κ=β/α.

Proof. Apply the transport triangle through KhnπK_h^n\piKhn​π. Its first leg has the uniform stationary-start bound; its second tends to zero by actual invariant attraction. Taking this proved limit yields the bias. For the finite-time statement, apply the triangle through πh\pi_hπh​, bound its first leg by the proved ULA contraction and its second by the bias. All laws and finite distances have already been established.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.21.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.13; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.15; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.7.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

bias_from_comparison passes the actual invariant-attraction limit through a uniform triangle bound. convergence_from_comparison combines invariantLaw_mixing, the proved transport triangle and that bias. Invariant existence, contraction and limit convergence are not comparison fields.

03The source accuracy scale §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. With the source step choice and its logarithmic iteration threshold, α W2(Khnμ,π)≤ε\sqrt\alpha\,W_2(K_h^n\mu,\pi)\le\varepsilonα​W2​(Khn​μ,π)≤ε.

Proof. Multiply the actual convergence bound by α\sqrt\alphaα​. The step choice makes the bias contribution at most ε/2\varepsilon/2ε/2; the iteration threshold makes the geometric transient at most ε/2\varepsilon/2ε/2. These exact numerical estimates are proved independently in the complexity calculation. Positive step together with the displayed step formula excludes the zero-dimensional exceptional branch when this accuracy statement is used.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.7.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

accuracy_from_comparison applies the exact numerical theorem paper_complexity_bound to the actual initial distance from the constructed invariant law. The proof derives the needed positive dimension from the positive step and its prescribed formula.
