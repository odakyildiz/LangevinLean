Stationary evaluation of the constructed Poisson corrector

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

A real observable transition and its genuine bounded Poisson corrector can be evaluated along a stationary Markov process. The resulting corrector increments, exact coboundary decomposition and centered martingale residual inherit the same constants, including the residual coefficient 181818.

01The stationary Markov evaluation identity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For stationary isometric evaluations UnU_nUn​ and transition PPP, with E[Un+1f∣Fn]=UnPf\mathbb E[U_{n+1}f\mid\mathcal F_n]=U_nPfE[Un+1​f∣Fn​]=Un​Pf,

∥Un+1f−Unf∥22=2⟨f,(I−P)f⟩.\|U_{n+1}f-U_nf\|_2^2=2\langle f,(I-P)f\rangle.∥Un+1​f−Un​f∥22​=2⟨f,(I−P)f⟩.

Proof. Adaptation makes current evaluation fixed by conditional expectation. Its orthogonal-projection pairing identity replaces the two-time cross term by ⟨Unf,UnPf⟩\langle U_nf,U_nPf\rangle⟨Un​f,Un​Pf⟩. The evaluation isometry transfers this pairing and both square norms to the observable space. Expanding the square gives the formula.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

PoissonProcess packages a transition, the constructed-corrector properties and actual evaluation/Markov data for reuse. condExp_eval_self uses measurability of current evaluations. eval_increment_norm_sq applies the actual conditional-expectation inner-product identity.

02The evaluated corrector and its increment energy §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For gn=UnGhfg_n=U_nG_hfgn​=Un​Gh​f,

∥gn∥2≤h∥f∥,∥gn+1−gn∥22≤h2∥Un+1f−Unf∥22.\|g_n\|_2\le h\|f\|,\qquad \|g_{n+1}-g_n\|_2^2\le h^2\|U_{n+1}f-U_nf\|_2^2.∥gn​∥2​≤h∥f∥,∥gn+1​−gn​∥22​≤h2∥Un+1​f−Un​f∥22​.

Proof. The norm bound is the corrector operator bound transported through the isometry. For the increment, use the preceding stationary increment formula for GhfG_hfGh​f, then apply the proved corrector Dirichlet estimate and the same formula for fff. Thus an actual score-increment bound 6hβ2d6h\beta^2d6hβ2d implies the corrector-increment bound 6h3β2d6h^3\beta^2d6h3β2d. Adaptation follows from the actual evaluation property.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

correctorAt evaluates the existing corrector. Its norm and adaptation lemmas use the corresponding constructed properties. correctorAt_increment_norm_sq_le rewrites both increments as energy pairings; the final energy lemma inserts the primitive score increment estimate.

03The true centered residual and coefficient eighteen §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For a local defect DnD_nDn​, define Mn=Dn−(gn−gn+1)M_n=D_n-(g_n-g_{n+1})Mn​=Dn​−(gn​−gn+1​). Then Dn=gn−gn+1+MnD_n=g_n-g_{n+1}+M_nDn​=gn​−gn+1​+Mn​. If its conditional mean is the evaluated Poisson source, E[Mn∣Fn]=0\mathbb E[M_n\mid\mathcal F_n]=0E[Mn​∣Fn​]=0, and MnM_nMn​ is orthogonal to every current adapted square-integrable vector. If ∥Dn∥22≤3h3β2d\|D_n\|_2^2\le3h^3\beta^2d∥Dn​∥22​≤3h3β2d, then ∥Mn∥22≤18h3β2d\|M_n\|_2^2\le18h^3\beta^2d∥Mn​∥22​≤18h3β2d.

Proof. The decomposition is algebraic. Conditional expectation of the corrector difference is the Poisson source by the Markov and Poisson identities, giving cancellation. Orthogonality follows from the conditional-expectation projection. Finally ∥a−b∥2≤2∥a∥2+2∥b∥2\|a-b\|^2\le2\|a\|^2+2\|b\|^2∥a−b∥2≤2∥a∥2+2∥b∥2 combines the independent constants 333 and 666.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

martingaleAt defines the actual residual. The centering proof uses linearity of condExpL2, adaptation, the Markov identity and the proved Poisson equation. The energy theorem combines the previously proved defect and corrector-increment bounds; no residual-energy conclusion is a primitive input.
