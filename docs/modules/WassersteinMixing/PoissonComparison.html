The stationary comparison and the sharp shifted-energy constants

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

Combine a stationary evaluated Poisson corrector with a synchronous error update, an actual curvature operator and primitive score increments. The defect, martingale, shifted energy and transport bounds are all derived. The constants 333, 181818, 191919 and 666 appear as conclusions.

Mathematical statement and proof

Set-up. Let en=Yn−Xne_n=Y_n-X_nen​=Yn​−Xn​, Dn=∫0hIn(s) dsD_n=\int_0^h I_n(s)\,dsDn​=∫0h​In​(s)ds, and let gng_ngn​ be the evaluated corrector. 02Deriving the component bounds and adaptation §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim.

∥gn∥2≤h2βd,∥Dn∥2≤3h3β2d,∥Mn∥2≤18h3β2d.\|g_n\|^2\le h^2\beta d,\quad\|D_n\|^2\le3h^3\beta^2d,\quad\|M_n\|^2\le18h^3\beta^2d.∥gn​∥2≤h2βd,∥Dn​∥2≤3h3β2d,∥Mn​∥2≤18h3β2d.

Proof. Use the corrector norm bound and score moment for the first estimate. Time Cauchy–Schwarz and integration of the primitive increment bound give the second. The endpoint increment identifies the actual score difference, so the process corrector-increment bound and squared triangle inequality give the third. The shifted drift is adapted because the error and corrector are adapted and the actual curvature preserves the current measurable subspace.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The component energy theorems invoke the constructed process and local integral estimates. score_increment_energy uses the exact endpoint identity. predictable_adapted proves closure inside the measurable submodule under the actual curvature action, sums and scalar multiplication.

03The coefficient nineteen and the final constant six §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. With un=∥en+gn∥2u_n=\|e_n+g_n\|^2un​=∥en​+gn​∥2,

un+1≤(1−αh)un+19h3β2d,∥en∥≤6h(β/α)βd.u_{n+1}\le(1-\alpha h)u_n+19h^3\beta^2d,\qquad \|e_n\|\le6h\sqrt{(\beta/\alpha)\beta d}.un+1​≤(1−αh)un​+19h3β2d,∥en​∥≤6h(β/α)βd​.

Proof. The exact coboundary decomposition gives en+1+gn+1=Zn+Mne_{n+1}+g_{n+1}=Z_n+M_nen+1​+gn+1​=Zn​+Mn​. Conditional centering makes their cross term zero. Weighted curvature interpolation bounds ∥Zn∥2\|Z_n\|^2∥Zn​∥2 by (1−αh)un+hβ∥gn∥2(1-\alpha h)u_n+h\beta\|g_n\|^2(1−αh)un​+hβ∥gn​∥2. Adding the martingale bound gives the coefficient 1+18=191+18=191+18=19. Equal initial states bound u0u_0u0​ by the corrector budget. The scalar affine recurrence gives a uniform energy floor. Removing the corrector by triangle inequality and the proved numerical comparison gives the final constant 666.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

shifted_equation applies the exact algebraic shifted identity. shifted_energy_step combines weighted interpolation and actual martingale orthogonality. The recurrence and final norm theorems use the independently proved scalar recurrence results.

04Actual pushforward transport from the synchronous coupling §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The reference initial law and the actual chain law satisfy W2(Law⁡(Y0),Law⁡(Xn))≤6h(β/α)βdW_2(\operatorname{Law}(Y_0),\operatorname{Law}(X_n))\le6h\sqrt{(\beta/\alpha)\beta d}W2​(Law(Y0​),Law(Xn​))≤6h(β/α)βd​, and this transport distance is finite.

Proof. Stationarity replaces the initial reference law by the current reference law. The actual joint random variables (Yn,Xn)(Y_n,X_n)(Yn​,Xn​) are an admissible coupling. Their L2L^2L2 difference bounds its transport cost by the preceding error estimate. The same actual coupling proves finiteness before converting the extended-valued distance to a real number.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.12; ULA-SC, Section 3.17.13; ULA-SC, Section 3.17.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

stationary_transport_bound uses the actual pushforward coupling cost theorem. stationary_transport_finite obtains a finite extended-valued bound from the same L2L^2L2 coupling, preventing loss of information during real conversion.
