Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The integer mesh count Nr(t)=⌊t/r⌋N_r(t)=\lfloor t/r\rfloorNr​(t)=⌊t/r⌋ gives actual finite-step approximants at elapsed times tending to ttt. Its sum at two target times has the analogous bound and converges to their sum.

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For r>0r>0r>0 and t≥0t\ge0t≥0,

Nr(t)r≤t,0≤t−Nr(t)r<r.N_r(t)r\le t,\qquad 0\le t-N_r(t)r<r.Nr​(t)r≤t,0≤t−Nr​(t)r<r.

Hence Nr(t)r→tN_r(t)r\to tNr​(t)r→t as r↓0r\downarrow0r↓0. For two nonnegative times, (Nr(s)+Nr(t))r≤s+t(N_r(s)+N_r(t))r\le s+t(Nr​(s)+Nr​(t))r≤s+t and these elapsed sums tend to s+ts+ts+t.

Proof. The defining floor inequalities bound the ratio t/rt/rt/r between its natural integer floor and the next integer. Multiply by the positive step to get the elapsed-time and remainder estimates. Squeeze the remainder between zero and the step, then add the two convergent elapsed times.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

meshCount is the actual natural floor of the time/step ratio. The floor lower and strict upper inequalities prove the bounds. The elapsed-time limit is a squeeze argument; the sum lemmas combine the exact count arithmetic and the two limits.
