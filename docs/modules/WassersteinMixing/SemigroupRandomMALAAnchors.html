References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

A sampled MALA update is identified with its explicit transition kernel. Extending the state by an unchanged initial coordinate yields an actual anchored recursion, whose law advances by the identity kernel on the anchor and the actual MALA kernel on the endpoint.

Statement and proof

01Actual sampled updates are kernel composition §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Sampling an independent innovation and applying a measurable update is exactly composition with its sampled transition kernel. For the scalar MALA update, this kernel is the explicit Gaussian accepted/rejected kernel.

Proof. Product integration identifies the sampled Markov law with integration of the output kernel over the current state law. For MALA, integrate the independent uniform threshold exactly; its weighted Gaussian output agrees with the explicit transition formula.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.33.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.12; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.9.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

sampled_markovStep_eq_comp proves the generic equality by integration on measurable events. malaUpdate is the actual scalar uniform-threshold update; malaUpdate_law applies the exact uniform-decision law to identify its kernel.

02The actual anchored update and recursion §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Updating (x0,x)(x_0,x)(x0​,x) to (x0,MALA⁡(x,ξ,U))(x_0,\operatorname{MALA}(x,\xi,U))(x0​,MALA(x,ξ,U)) preserves the anchor and has kernel I⊗KI\otimes KI⊗K. The resulting deterministic recursion equals the pair of its original initial value and the scalar sampled MALA recursion.

Proof. The first output coordinate is deterministic and the second has the proved explicit MALA law, so the sampled pair law is the Dirac anchor times that transition. Integrating over the current pair gives the parallel-kernel action. Inducting the deterministic recursion proves the exact anchor/endpoint identity.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.20.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.20.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.33.5.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

anchoredUpdate_law maps the exact scalar update law while keeping the first coordinate. anchored_markovStep applies the generic sampled composition result. anchored_chain_eq is induction on the actual update recursion.
