A genuine integral estimate for the local defect

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

Let HHH be a complete real normed space and J:[0,h]→HJ:[0,h]\to HJ:[0,h]→H a square-integrable increment. The local defect is the actual Bochner integral D=∫0hJ(s) dsD=\int_0^hJ(s)\,dsD=∫0h​J(s)ds. Time Cauchy–Schwarz yields the source constant 333 from the primitive square-increment constant 666.

Statement and proof

01Scalar Cauchy–Schwarz through the square-integrable Hilbert space §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If f,gf,gf,g are square-integrable real functions on a measure space, then

∫fg≤(∫f2)1/2(∫g2)1/2.\int fg\le\left(\int f^2\right)^{1/2}\left(\int g^2\right)^{1/2}.∫fg≤(∫f2)1/2(∫g2)1/2.

Proof. The squared norm of the equivalence class of fff in L2L^2L2 is ∫f2\int f^2∫f2, and likewise for ggg. Their real inner product is ∫fg\int fg∫fg. Apply the Hilbert-space bound for the inner product and replace each norm by the square root of its squared integral. The identifications hold almost everywhere and therefore preserve the integrals.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

toLp_norm_sq uses the real L2.inner_def and the almost-everywhere representative equality. real_cauchy_schwarz identifies the integral product with the genuine inner product and applies real_inner_le_norm.

02Time Cauchy–Schwarz for the Bochner integral §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For h≥0h\ge0h≥0 and square-integrable JJJ on (0,h](0,h](0,h],

∥∫0hJ(s) ds∥2≤h∫0h∥J(s)∥2 ds.\left\|\int_0^hJ(s)\,ds\right\|^2\le h\int_0^h\|J(s)\|^2\,ds.​∫0h​J(s)ds​2≤h∫0h​∥J(s)∥2ds.

Proof. The norm of the Bochner integral is at most ∫0h∥J(s)∥ ds\int_0^h\|J(s)\|\,ds∫0h​∥J(s)∥ds. Apply scalar Cauchy–Schwarz to ∥J∥\|J\|∥J∥ and the constant function 111. The latter has squared integral hhh. Both resulting sides are nonnegative, so squaring gives the stated inequality. This also defines the local defect DDD as the genuine integral, with all square-integrability hypotheses retained.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

time_integral_norm_sq combines intervalIntegral.norm_integral_le_integral_norm with the preceding scalar bound for the restricted interval measure. It evaluates the measure of the interval and uses the nonnegative square-root identities before squaring. stationaryLocalDefect is the Bochner interval integral itself.

03The exact local defect constant and the displacement polynomial §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If ∥J(s)∥2≤6sβ2d\|J(s)\|^2\le6s\beta^2d∥J(s)∥2≤6sβ2d on (0,h](0,h](0,h], then

∥D∥2≤3h3β2d.\|D\|^2\le3h^3\beta^2d.∥D∥2≤3h3β2d.

Proof. Square integrability makes ∥J∥2\|J\|^2∥J∥2 integrable. Integrate its upper bound and use the preceding time estimate:

∥D∥2≤h∫0h6sβ2d ds=h⋅3h2β2d.\|D\|^2\le h\int_0^h6s\beta^2d\,ds =h\cdot3h^2\beta^2d.∥D∥2≤h∫0h​6sβ2dds=h⋅3h2β2d.

For the auxiliary source displacement polynomial, 0≤s≤h0\le s\le h0≤s≤h, β≥0\beta\ge0β≥0 and hβ≤1h\beta\le1hβ≤1 imply sβ≤1s\beta\le1sβ≤1, hence 2s2β+4s≤6s2s^2\beta+4s\le6s2s2β+4s≤6s. This last simplification supplies no independent probabilistic displacement estimate.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

stationaryLocalDefect_energy_le proves integrability of the squared norm from MemLp, applies monotonicity of the interval integral and evaluates the linear polynomial exactly. displacement_polynomial_le isolates the arithmetic consequence of the stable-step restriction.
