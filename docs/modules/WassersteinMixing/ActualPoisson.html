The actual Langevin Poisson comparison process

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The actual reversible canonical semigroup, its proved real-coordinate preservation, its bounded constructed corrector and the actual Brownian filtration combine into a genuine Poisson process for the physical real score observables. All analytic components are derived from the potential.

Statement and proof

01The concrete complex observable space and actual real extraction §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. Embed the physical real observable space isometrically in complex coordinate L2(π)L^2(\pi)L2(π), and use the actual canonical reversible semigroup there. The actual transition preserves the real image, and its adjoint extraction equals the true real canonical transition.

Proof. The complex coordinate inclusion is an exact isometry. Actual evaluation compression intertwines it with the real operator, proving preservation. The extraction identity follows by canceling the isometric embedding with its adjoint. Thus the corrector constructed on the complex space acts on genuine physical real observables.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

actualObservableSemigroup is the proved canonical semigroup instance, and actualRealEmbedding is the actual coordinate isometry. The preservation and extraction theorems instantiate the already proved actual real-range bridge.

02The actual filtration, evaluations and Markov law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Actual grid evaluations are adapted to the actual Brownian filtration and satisfy the exact one-step conditional Markov identity with the extracted transition. They therefore form a Poisson process with the constructed corrector, source identity, norm bound and energy domination.

Proof. Specialize the actual arbitrary-time adaptation and Markov theorems to nhnhnh and step hhh. The true grid/global evaluation identity identifies both endpoints, and the extracted transition is already equal to the actual real operator. Supply these derived facts to the semigroup-corrector process construction.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

actualGridEvaluation_adapted and actualGridEvaluation_markov specialize the checked actual arbitrary-time theorems. actualPoissonProcess invokes semigroupPoissonProcess with only constructed objects and derived analytic properties.
