Euler convergence with a moving step and endpoint

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

For a Lipschitz drift and continuous additive input, Euler approximations converge to the actual integral solution when the step tends to zero and the elapsed grid time tends to the desired time. The step and iteration count may vary independently along any filter subject to a fixed finite horizon bound.

Statement and proof

01Uniform finite-horizon control gives the moving-time limit §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If ri→0r_i\to0ri​→0, niri≤Tn_ir_i\le Tni​ri​≤T and niri→tn_ir_i\to tni​ri​→t, then the actual additive Euler endpoint converges to YtY_tYt​.

Proof. The deterministic finite-horizon Euler estimate bounds the endpoint error relative to YniriY_{n_ir_i}Yni​ri​​ by a constant times the modulus of continuity of the drift along the solution over intervals of length rir_iri​. Uniform continuity on the compact horizon makes that modulus tend to zero. Continuity of YYY makes Yniri→YtY_{n_ir_i}\to Y_tYni​ri​​→Yt​. The triangle inequality combines these limits. The case of a zero Lipschitz constant is included in the same finite-horizon estimate.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.16.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.16.7.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

additiveEulerChain_variable_tendsto applies the proved uniform additive Euler error estimate and compact-horizon uniform continuity. It separates the Euler error at the actual elapsed grid time from the moving-endpoint continuity error, then combines their limits.
