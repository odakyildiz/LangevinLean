02Ordering the curvature constants and the one-point space §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If dθ+dx>0d_\theta+d_x>0dθ​+dx​>0 and a differentiable joint potential UUU is mmm-strongly convex with an LLL-Lipschitz gradient, then m≤Lm\le Lm≤L. On a one-point measurable space, any two probability measures are equal.

Proof. Take a unit coordinate vector vvv. Strong monotonicity of the gradient, the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality and the Lipschitz bound give

m=m∥v∥2≤⟨∇U(v)−∇U(0),v⟩≤∥∇U(v)−∇U(0)∥≤L.m=m\|v\|^2\le\langle\nabla U(v)-\nabla U(0),v\rangle\le\|\nabla U(v)-\nabla U(0)\|\le L.m=m∥v∥2≤⟨∇U(v)−∇U(0),v⟩≤∥∇U(v)−∇U(0)∥≤L.

On a one-point space every set is empty or the whole space, where both probability measures take the values 000 and 111.
