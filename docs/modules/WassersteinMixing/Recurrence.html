The shifted energy floor and the coefficient six

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

01The budgets and their exact fixed point §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Under the numerical parameter restrictions, C≥0C\ge0C≥0, E∗≥0E_*\ge0E∗​≥0, C≤E∗C\le E_*C≤E∗​, and qE∗+F=E∗qE_*+F=E_*qE∗​+F=E∗​.

Proof. The budgets are products of nonnegative factors. Since κ≥1\kappa\ge1κ≥1, E∗=19κC≥CE_*=19\kappa C\ge CE∗​=19κC≥C. For the fixed point, (1−q)E∗=αh⋅19h2(β/α)βd=19h3β2d=F(1-q)E_*=\alpha h\cdot19h^2(\beta/\alpha)\beta d=19h^3\beta^2d=F(1−q)E∗​=αh⋅19h2(β/α)βd=19h3β2d=F. Division by α\alphaα is valid because α>0\alpha>0α>0.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The three parameter definitions retain the exact constants. The sign lemmas use positive curvature and nonnegative dimension. energy_fixed_point clears the nonzero denominator and proves the polynomial identity. correctorBudget_le_floor rewrites the floor as 19κ19\kappa19κ times the budget.

02Uniform and finite-time energy envelopes §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Suppose En+1≤qEn+FE_{n+1}\le qE_n+FEn+1​≤qEn​+F. If E0≤CE_0\le CE0​≤C, then En≤E∗E_n\le E_*En​≤E∗​ for every nnn. Without the initial restriction,

En≤qnE0+(1−qn)E∗.E_n\le q^nE_0+(1-q^n)E_*.En​≤qnE0​+(1−qn)E∗​.

Proof. The uniform bound follows by induction. Initially E0≤C≤E∗E_0\le C\le E_*E0​≤C≤E∗​. Multiplication of the induction hypothesis by q≥0q\ge0q≥0, followed by the recurrence and fixed-point identity, gives En+1≤qE∗+F=E∗E_{n+1}\le qE_*+F=E_*En+1​≤qE∗​+F=E∗​. For the finite-time bound, iterating the affine recurrence yields its geometric sum. Substitute F=(1−q)E∗F=(1-q)E_*F=(1−q)E∗​; since q<1q<1q<1, cancellation of 1−q1-q1−q gives the displayed expression.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.15.2.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

shifted_energy_uniform is an explicit natural-number induction. shifted_energy_finite invokes the shared exact affine-recurrence formula and substitutes the proved fixed point. These are scalar implications. The stationary comparison derives their recurrence from the constructed corrector, actual martingale centering and genuine curvature bounds; the realized comparison supplies all primitive inputs from the actual potential dynamics.

03Converting the two squared budgets to the literal bias constant §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If ∥z+g∥2≤E∗\|z+g\|^2\le E_*∥z+g∥2≤E∗​ and ∥g∥2≤C\|g\|^2\le C∥g∥2≤C, then

∥z∥≤6hκβd.\|z\|\le6h\sqrt{\kappa\beta d}.∥z∥≤6hκβd​.

Proof. Put R=hκβd≥0R=h\sqrt{\kappa\beta d}\ge0R=hκβd​≥0. Then E∗=19R2E_*=19R^2E∗​=19R2, while C≤R2C\le R^2C≤R2 because κ≥1\kappa\ge1κ≥1. Hence ∥z+g∥≤5R\|z+g\|\le5R∥z+g∥≤5R, using 19≤2519\le2519≤25, and ∥g∥≤R\|g\|\le R∥g∥≤R. The triangle inequality for z=(z+g)−gz=(z+g)-gz=(z+g)−g gives ∥z∥≤6R\|z\|\le6R∥z∥≤6R. This proves the source's coefficient 666 directly, including a zero-dimensional budget.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

shifted_norm_le_bias proves the squared identities for the nonnegative radius, compares each squared norm using sq_le_sq₀, and applies norm_sub_le. The proof does not use an unproved manipulation of a limit superior.
