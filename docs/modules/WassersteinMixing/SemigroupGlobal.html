A global actual Gibbs diffusion and its continuous operators

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

Finite-horizon Langevin solutions driven by the same actual Brownian source agree on overlaps by uniqueness. Their endpoints define a global canonical path. It has the Gibbs law at every nonnegative time and yields strongly continuous actual observable contractions.

01Actual Brownian input and finite-horizon solutions §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. Use the coordinate Brownian source on the independent Gibbs extension and the actual Gibbs initial variable. For each finite horizon, Picard existence gives a continuous L2L^2L2 solution of

Yt−Y0=−∫0t∇V(Ys) ds+2Bt.Y_t-Y_0=-\int_0^t\nabla V(Y_s)\,ds+\sqrt2 B_t.Yt​−Y0​=−∫0t​∇V(Ys​)ds+2​Bt​.

The initial law is the actual normalized Gibbs measure; Brownian coordinates retain their actual pre-Brownian laws on the product space.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.22.20; ULA-SC, Section 3.22.22; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.6; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.32.12; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.17; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.19; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.20.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

brownian and initial select the actual source and initialization. solutionOn chooses a solution only from the proved existence theorem. solutionOn_spec retains continuity, the exact initial value and the actual integral equation.

02Consistency, continuity and identification with the reference path §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Define the global endpoint at t≥0t\ge0t≥0 using horizon ttt, and hold it constant before zero. It agrees with every longer-horizon solution, is continuous, satisfies the same integral equation and coincides with the actual comparison segment and grid reference.

Proof. Uniqueness of solutions with the same initial state and Brownian input identifies overlapping finite horizons. Near any time choose one fixed larger horizon; the global path equals that continuous solution locally, including the clipped-zero presentation. Substituting consistency into the finite-horizon integral equation gives the global one. The actual segmented reference solves that same equation, so uniqueness gives its segment and grid identification.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.43.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.44.5.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

state_eq_solutionOn proves horizon independence by integral-solution uniqueness. The continuity lemmas use local equality with a longer fixed solution. state_integral, state_eq_actualSegment and state_grid carry that exact identification to the global and comparison representations.

03Gibbs stationarity and the global observable contraction §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Every global state has law π\piπ. Thus Utf=f(Yt)U_tf=f(Y_t)Ut​f=f(Yt​) is an isometry, Pt=U0∗UtP_t=U_0^*U_tPt​=U0∗​Ut​ is a contraction, P0=IP_0=IP0​=I, and every orbit is strongly continuous for t≥0t\ge0t≥0.

Proof. The actual Euler innovations have the independent coordinate and initialization laws required by the proved Brownian Gibbs-stationarity theorem. Apply it to the global finite-horizon solution to obtain the marginal law. Actual evaluation and compression then give the operator and norm identities. Continuity of the global state together with stationarity implies strong continuity of every observable evaluation; the fixed adjoint preserves it.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.29.7; ULA-SC, Section 3.32.13; ULA-SC, Section 3.32.15; ULA-SC, Section 3.32.17; ULA-SC, Section 3.42.7; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.17; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.19; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.20; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.22.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The two independence helpers specialize the actual Brownian product source. state_law invokes the proved Langevin Gibbs-stationarity result. evaluation and op are actual pullback and compression constructions; continuousOn_op uses the stationary-observable continuity theorem.
