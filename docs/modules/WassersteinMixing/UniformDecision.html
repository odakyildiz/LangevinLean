References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

An independent uniform variable on [0,1][0,1][0,1] realizes acceptance probability a(x)a(x)a(x) exactly. This connects a concrete random Metropolis update with the weighted accepted and rejected measures used in detailed balance.

01The actual acceptance decision and its law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let 0≤a≤10\le a\le10≤a≤1, and let UUU be independent and uniform on [0,1][0,1][0,1]. Output f(x)f(x)f(x) when U≤a(x)U\le a(x)U≤a(x), and g(x)g(x)g(x) otherwise. Its law under μ⊗Unif⁡[0,1]\mu\otimes\operatorname{Unif}[0,1]μ⊗Unif[0,1] is

f∗(aμ)+g∗((1−a)μ).f_*(a\mu)+g_*((1-a)\mu).f∗​(aμ)+g∗​((1−a)μ).

Proof. The threshold event is measurable. For a target event, integrate first over the uniform coordinate. Depending on whether each output lies in the event, its section is the entire interval, an initial interval of length a(x)a(x)a(x), a terminal interval of length 1−a(x)1-a(x)1−a(x), or empty. These four masses give precisely the displayed sum after integrating over xxx.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

uniformDecision is the actual threshold conditional. uniformDecision_measurable checks its measurable branches and decision event. uniformDecision_law proves equality on measurable events using the exact unit-interval masses and the weighted-measure formulas.
