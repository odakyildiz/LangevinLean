Independence of the entire Brownian future and past

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. Statement and proof

01Regrouping independent pairs into independent families §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Suppose the rows (Xi,Yi)(X_i,Y_i)(Xi​,Yi​) are mutually independent and, within each row, XiX_iXi​ and YiY_iYi​ are independent. Then the entire families (Xi)i(X_i)_i(Xi​)i​ and (Yi)i(Y_i)_i(Yi​)i​ are independent.

Proof. Regard each row as a two-coordinate independent family. Independence of the rows gives independence of the family indexed by the product of the row set and the two-point coordinate set. The two collections occupy disjoint index sets, so their generated sigma-algebras are independent. This argument does not require countability of the row set.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The Boolean helper expands all finite subsets of a two-point index. indepFun_families_of_independent_pairs combines row and within-row independence by uncurry, then applies independence of sigma-algebras generated by disjoint index subsets.

02Whole-path independence on the actual product source §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The actual Brownian future at aaa is independent of the initial state together with the entire Brownian past.

Proof. For each coordinate, independent Brownian increments give independence of the shifted future path and the path restricted to [0,a][0,a][0,a]. Compose the latter with time clipping. Coordinate paths are mutually independent, so the preceding regrouping result gives vector whole-path independence. Finally take the product with the independent initialization: integrating rectangle events over the initial coordinate leaves the same future marginal and factorizes their masses.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.22.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.11; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.16; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.17; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.19; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.20; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.21.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

canonicalBrownian_future_past_independent combines the actual coordinate pre-Brownian shift theorem with coordinate-path independence. indepFun_future_initial_past proves the product extension directly by product integration. The final theorem specializes these facts to actualProbability.
