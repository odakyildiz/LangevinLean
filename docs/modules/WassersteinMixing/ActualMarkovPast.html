The actual future sigma-algebra and the natural past

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The actual L2L^2L2 Brownian noise has an explicit coordinate representative. This identifies the natural past sigma-algebra with information contained in the initial state and raw Brownian past, allowing whole-path independence to pass to the actual conditioning filtration.

01Representatives and future information §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. For t≥0t\ge0t≥0, the additive noise equals 2Bt\sqrt2 B_t2​Bt​ almost surely. Define the future path at aaa by Wa(t)=Ba+t−BaW_a(t)=B_{a+t}-B_aWa​(t)=Ba+t​−Ba​, and let its generated sigma-algebra be the future information. Each coordinate evaluation is measurable, hence the whole path and its generated information are measurable on the original probability space.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.24.15; ULA-SC, Section 3.24.21; ULA-SC, Section 3.24.23; ULA-SC, Section 3.24.8; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.11.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

actualBrownianNoise_coe identifies the actual noise representative with the coordinate Brownian vector. actualBrownianFuture uses raw coordinate increments, and its measurability is proved coordinatewise. actualFutureInformation is the corresponding comap sigma-algebra.

02Independence from the actual conditioning past §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The future information at aaa is independent of σ(Y0,2Bs:0≤s≤a)\sigma(Y_0,\sqrt2 B_s:0\le s\le a)σ(Y0​,2​Bs​:0≤s≤a), and therefore independent of the grid filtration at a=nha=nha=nh.

Proof. Whole-path independence has already been proved for the raw source. Replace each additive-noise evaluation by its almost-sure coordinate representative and the initialization by its actual initial-coordinate representative. Every generator of the natural past is measurable from the raw past and initialization. Independence descends to this generated sub-sigma-algebra; the grid statement is its specialization.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.22.17; ULA-SC, Section 3.24.8; ULA-SC, Section 3.54.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.17.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The arbitrary-time theorem compares the generating comap sigma-algebras using their almost-everywhere representatives. The grid theorem substitutes the nonnegative time n * P.step and unfolds actualPast.
