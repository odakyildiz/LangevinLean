The random averaged Hessian on square-integrable vectors

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

For actual random states X,Y∈L2(Q;H)X,Y\in L^2(Q;H)X,Y∈L2(Q;H), the field ω↦A‾(X(ω),Y(ω))\omega\mapsto\overline A(X(\omega),Y(\omega))ω↦A(X(ω),Y(ω)) is measurable and bounded by β\betaβ. Multiplication by this field defines a bounded symmetric operator on L2(Q;H)L^2(Q;H)L2(Q;H), with the original curvature constants.

Statement and proof

01Constructing multiplication by the actual field §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. Set (AU)(ω)=A‾(X(ω),Y(ω))U(ω)(\mathcal A U)(\omega)=\overline A(X(\omega),Y(\omega))U(\omega)(AU)(ω)=A(X(ω),Y(ω))U(ω). Joint continuity of the averaged Hessian gives measurable coefficients; its uniform operator bound gives an essentially bounded field. The estimate ∥AU(ω)∥≤β∥U(ω)∥\|\mathcal A U(\omega)\|\le\beta\|U(\omega)\|∥AU(ω)∥≤β∥U(ω)∥ proves square integrability and a bounded linear action on equivalence classes.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

averagedHessianField_memLp establishes membership at exponent infinity. randomCurvature uses bounded bilinear evaluation and the Hölder multiplication construction. randomCurvature_coe proves the exact almost-everywhere pointwise representative.

02Integrated forms, exact secants and adaptation §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The constructed operator satisfies ∥A∥≤β\|\mathcal A\|\le\beta∥A∥≤β, is symmetric, and obeys

α∥U∥22≤⟨U,AU⟩L2≤β∥U∥22,A(X−Y)=∇V(X)−∇V(Y).\alpha\|U\|_2^2\le\langle U,\mathcal A U\rangle_{L^2}\le\beta\|U\|_2^2,\qquad \mathcal A(X-Y)=\nabla V(X)-\nabla V(Y).α∥U∥22​≤⟨U,AU⟩L2​≤β∥U∥22​,A(X−Y)=∇V(X)−∇V(Y).

Proof. Integrate the pointwise norm and quadratic-form estimates using the exact representative. Pointwise Hessian symmetry gives symmetry of the integral pairing; the secant identity holds almost everywhere. If X,Y,UX,Y,UX,Y,U are measurable with respect to a sub-sigma-algebra, continuity of the field and evaluation makes their product measurable there too.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.13.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The norm and pairing lemmas use the pointwise representative and the integral expression for the real square norm. randomCurvature_secant invokes the actual segment secant identity. randomCurvature_adapted proves measurability under the supplied sub-sigma-algebra, without an adaptation field on the operator.
