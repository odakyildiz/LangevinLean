The source Wasserstein estimates from the original Hessian bounds

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The displayed endpoints concern the actual normalized Gibbs law, the constructed invariant law of ordinary independent-Gaussian ULA and its true finite-time iterates. Their potential input is exactly a twice continuously differentiable potential with the source Hessian bounds; the analytic comparison is constructed internally.

01The exact invariant bias estimate §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For V∈C2(Rd)V\in C^2(\mathbb R^d)V∈C2(Rd), αI⪯∇2V⪯βI\alpha I\preceq\nabla^2V\preceq\beta IαI⪯∇2V⪯βI, 0<α≤β0<\alpha\le\beta0<α≤β, 0<h≤1/β0<h\le1/\beta0<h≤1/β, and κ=β/α\kappa=\beta/\alphaκ=β/α,

W2(π,πh)≤6hκβd.W_2(\pi,\pi_h)\le6h\sqrt{\kappa\beta d}.W2​(π,πh​)≤6hκβd​.

Proof. The original Hessian bounds imply the first-order potential contract with unchanged constants. From that contract the actual reversible Langevin semigroup, bounded corrector, score and synchronous comparison are constructed. Apply the proved comparison-to-invariant theorem to this constructed comparison and the genuinely constructed Gaussian ULA invariant law. No spectral representation, conditional mean, curvature operator or comparison estimate is an additional input.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

02The actual finite-time Gaussian ULA estimate §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For an initial probability law μ\muμ with finite second moment,

W2(Khnμ,π)≤(1−αh)nW2(μ,πh)+6hκβd.W_2(K_h^n\mu,\pi)\le(1-\alpha h)^nW_2(\mu,\pi_h)+6h\sqrt{\kappa\beta d}.W2​(Khn​μ,π)≤(1−αh)nW2​(μ,πh​)+6hκβd​.

Proof. Instantiate the general convergence theorem with the constructed actual comparison. The invariant law exists and attracts Gaussian ULA iterates by its own contraction construction. The true transport triangle, actual ULA contraction and preceding bias estimate yield the displayed two-term bound. The initial moment hypothesis supplies finiteness of every transport distance used in this argument.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.7.

03The source scale-invariant mixing complexity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. With the source step size

h=β−1min⁡{1,ε12d}h=\beta^{-1}\min\left\{1,\frac{\varepsilon}{12\sqrt d}\right\}h=β−1min{1,12d​ε​}

and an integer iteration count at least

κmax⁡{1,12dε}log⁡(1+2α W2(μ,πh)ε),\kappa\max\left\{1,\frac{12\sqrt d}{\varepsilon}\right\}\log\left(1+\frac{2\sqrt\alpha\,W_2(\mu,\pi_h)}{\varepsilon}\right),κmax{1,ε12d​​}log(1+ε2α​W2​(μ,πh​)​),

one has α W2(Khnμ,π)≤ε\sqrt\alpha\,W_2(K_h^n\mu,\pi)\le\varepsilonα​W2​(Khn​μ,π)≤ε.

Proof. Apply the proved exact complexity arithmetic to the actual finite-time convergence bound. The chosen step reduces the scaled bias to at most half the tolerance, and the logarithmic threshold controls the scaled geometric transient by the other half. Positive step and this formula imply the positive-dimension condition needed for its displayed numerical form.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.17.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.7.
