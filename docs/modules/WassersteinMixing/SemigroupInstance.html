Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. Statement and proof

01Assembling the actual operator properties §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The canonical transition family is a reversible strongly continuous contraction semigroup on complex L2(π)L^2(\pi)L2(π).

Proof. The actual conditional Markov theorem and compression calculation prove the real composition law. Scalar restriction identifies the real and complex operators, so that law transfers to complex observables. Reversibility follows from actual reversible MALA approximants and their strong limit; contraction, the time-zero identity and strong continuity follow from actual stationary evaluation. Each defining property is therefore a theorem about the same canonical Brownian operator family.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

op_add_real instantiates the actual Markov composition theorem. The local real structure and op_restrictScalars transfer it in op_add. semigroup fills every field of ReversibleSemigroup with the proved canonical operator results, and semigroup_op records its definitional identification.
