Real observables and the adjoints of actual evaluations

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

For an actual measure-preserving observation, pointwise real embedding and pointwise adjoint extraction commute with evaluation. Taking adjoints therefore proves that the complex transition compression of an embedded real observable is exactly the embedding of its real transition.

01Scalar restriction and pointwise adjoint extraction §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Complex adjunction agrees with real adjunction after restricting scalars. On L2L^2L2, the real inner product is the integral of the pointwise real inner product and equals the real part of the complex pairing. The adjoint of the lifted embedding is the lift of its pointwise adjoint.

Proof. Test scalar-restricted adjoints against arbitrary vectors and take the real part of the complex adjoint identity. For square-integrable functions, the same identity follows by integrating their pointwise pairings. The real-part functional commutes with the integrable complex pairing; the defining pointwise adjoint equality then identifies the lifted adjoint.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The local scalar restrictions specify the underlying real inner products. adjoint_restrictScalars uses uniqueness of adjoints. real_inner_Lp_eq_re uses the integral of the real part. mapLp_adjoint tests against arbitrary real observables and compares exact almost-everywhere representatives.

02Naturality of actual evaluation and its adjoint §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If ϕ∗ν=μ\phi_*\nu=\muϕ∗​ν=μ, then pointwise embedding and extraction commute with pullback by ϕ\phiϕ. The adjoint pullback satisfies the corresponding embedding identity.

Proof. Both sides have the same almost-everywhere representative, obtained by composing the pointwise map with ϕ\phiϕ. A measure-preserving map transports the exceptional null sets correctly. Express extraction as the proved lifted adjoint and take adjoints of its commuting diagram. Scalar-restriction compatibility and the double-adjoint identity give the asserted adjoint-evaluation diagram.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The two composition theorems use Lp.ext and transport representative equalities through the measure-preserving map. evaluation_adjoint_mapLp takes adjoints of the exact extraction diagram and applies adjoint_restrictScalars.

03Actual transition compression preserves the real image §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let U0,U1U_0,U_1U0​,U1​ be evaluation isometries of two observations with the same marginal. Their complex compression U0∗U1U_0^*U_1U0∗​U1​ sends the embedded real observable ififif to i(U0,R∗U1,Rf)i(U_{0,\mathbb R}^*U_{1,\mathbb R}f)i(U0,R∗​U1,R​f), hence preserves the actual real image.

Proof. Move embedding through the second evaluation using pointwise naturality, then through the first adjoint using the previous theorem. The resulting vector is explicitly the embedding of the genuine real compression, which supplies its witness in the real range.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

compression_mapLp composes the evaluation and adjoint-evaluation identities. compression_mem_range exhibits the actual real compression as the preimage, so range preservation is proved from the observation maps rather than introduced as an assumption.
