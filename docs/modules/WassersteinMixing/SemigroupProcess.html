References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The bounded corrector of a reversible semigroup, its derived real-range preservation and actual stationary Markov evaluations supply a real Poisson comparison process. Corrector norms, Dirichlet domination and the exact integrated source are consequences of the construction.

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. Extract the real corrector GhRG_h^{\mathbb R}GhR​ and real transition PhRP_h^{\mathbb R}PhR​, and define the source operator by (I−PhR)GhR(I-P_h^{\mathbb R})G_h^{\mathbb R}(I−PhR​)GhR​. The already proved Poisson identity identifies this operator with f↦∫0h(f−PtRf) dtf\mapsto\int_0^h(f-P_t^{\mathbb R}f)\,dtf↦∫0h​(f−PtR​f)dt.

Proof. The real-extraction theorems provide the exact integral identity, the norm bound hhh and energy factor h2h^2h2. Combine them with the supplied actual evaluation isometries, their adaptation and their conditional Markov identity. The resulting process uses the constructed corrector itself and retains all these proved estimates.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

semigroupPoissonSource is the bounded operator difference. semigroupPoissonSource_eq_integral invokes realCorrector_poisson. semigroupPoissonProcess fills every corrector field from the proved real-extraction theorems, leaving only actual evaluation and Markov facts to the concrete process construction.
