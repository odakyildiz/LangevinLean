01Accepted flow and rejected diagonal mass §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. Given proposal-pair law JJJ and acceptance a(x,y)a(x,y)a(x,y), retain the accepted weighted pair aJaJaJ, and send the rejected pair to (x,x)(x,x)(x,x). Its first marginal is the original first marginal, since the weights aaa and 1−a1-a1−a sum to one; its second marginal is the actual Metropolis output.

Proof of reversal. Rejected diagonal mass is unchanged by swapping. For a swap-invariant base measure and forward density www, Metropolis weighting gives accepted density min⁡{w(x,y),w(y,x)}\min\{w(x,y),w(y,x)\}min{w(x,y),w(y,x)}, which is symmetric. Thus the entire output pair is reversible.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.35.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.11; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.8; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.9.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

metropolisJoint uses the actual accepted measure and mapped rejected measure. Its marginal lemmas use exact weighted partition and map composition. acceptedFlow_swap_of_density proves the symmetric minimum-density identity, and metropolisJoint_swap combines accepted reversal with the diagonal rejection map.

02The actual Gibbs-start Gaussian pair §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For positive step size, the Gaussian MALA pair has both marginals equal to π\piπ, is swap invariant, and is a probability law.

Proof. The actual proposal joint law has the Gibbs/Gaussian density with respect to the product reference measure. The logarithmic acceptance ratio is the actual ratio of its backward and forward densities, so the preceding minimum-density argument applies. The first marginal is Gibbs by exact acceptance/rejection partition. Swap invariance makes the second marginal equal to the first; total mass then equals one. Measurability follows from continuity of the potential and its Lipschitz gradient.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.34.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.35.9; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.36.8; ULA-SC, Section 3.37.12; ULA-SC, Section 3.39.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.39.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.39.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.39.4; ULA-SC, Section 3.39.5.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

jointLaw specializes the generic Metropolis pair to malaProposalJoint. The acceptance helper proves measurability from the true potential and gradient. jointLaw_swap uses the actual proposal density theorem; its marginal and probability consequences are derived from that measure identity.

03The actual self-adjoint contraction and its powers §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The transition compressed from this actual stationary pair is a self-adjoint contraction. Every integer power is self-adjoint and contractive. Actual bounded-continuous two-time correlations are symmetric.

Proof. Apply the stationary-pair construction to the proved marginals and swap identity. Powers of a self-adjoint operator remain self-adjoint; induction applies the contraction bound at each factor. The correlation symmetry follows by mapping the integrable pair function through the actual swap-invariant law.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

operator instantiates the generic stationary-pair adjoint compression over either real or complex targets. Its bounds and self-adjointness use proved law properties. The power bounds are inductive consequences; the final real correlation lemma uses actual pair-law reversal directly.
