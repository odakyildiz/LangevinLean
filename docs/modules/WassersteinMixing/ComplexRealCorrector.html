An isometric real embedding i:R→Hi:R\to Hi:R→H into a complex Hilbert space has orthogonal projection ii∗ii^*ii∗ onto its closed real range. If a semigroup preserves this range, its actual integrated defect, finite corrector sums and strong limit preserve it too. Extraction transfers the same Poisson identity and constants to the real space.

01The genuine closed real subspace §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The real adjoint satisfies i∗i=Ii^*i=Ii∗i=I, and Π=ii∗\Pi=ii^*Π=ii∗ fixes exactly i(R)i(R)i(R). This image is closed.

Proof. The adjoint identity follows from preservation of the real inner product by an isometry. Hence Π(ix)=ix\Pi(ix)=ixΠ(ix)=ix. Conversely a fixed vector yyy equals i(i∗y)i(i^*y)i(i∗y), so it belongs to the image. The image is therefore the equalizer of the continuous maps Π\PiΠ and the identity, which is closed.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

The local real inner product is the real part of the complex one. projection is the actual adjoint composition. projection_fixed_iff characterizes its fixed vectors, and isClosed_range uses continuity of the two equalizer maps.

02Preservation passes through integrals, sums and limits §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If Pti(R)⊆i(R)P_ti(R)\subseteq i(R)Pt​i(R)⊆i(R), then Dhi(R)⊆i(R)D_hi(R)\subseteq i(R)Dh​i(R)⊆i(R), GNi(R)⊆i(R)G_Ni(R)\subseteq i(R)GN​i(R)⊆i(R) and Ghi(R)⊆i(R)G_hi(R)\subseteq i(R)Gh​i(R)⊆i(R).

Proof. Apply the projection to ix−Ptixix-P_tixix−Pt​ix; it fixes each integrand. Continuity of the projection lets it pass through the Bochner integral. Induction on the finite-sum recursion gives the same property for every GNG_NGN​. Taking the strong limit preserves the projection equality, so the constructed corrector also lies in the genuine closed real image.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

PreservesReal expresses only a semigroup range property. defect_fixed commutes the projection through the justified integral. partialCorrector_fixed uses the finite recursion, and corrector_fixed uses uniqueness of limits. The actual diffusion proves the range property separately.

03The exact real corrector and its unchanged constants §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. Define PtR=i∗PtiP_t^{\mathbb R}=i^*P_tiPtR​=i∗Pt​i and GhR=i∗GhiG_h^{\mathbb R}=i^*G_hiGhR​=i∗Gh​i. Range preservation gives iPtR=PtiiP_t^{\mathbb R}=P_tiiPtR​=Pt​i and iGhR=GhiiG_h^{\mathbb R}=G_hiiGhR​=Gh​i. Consequently

∥GhR∥≤h,(I−PhR)GhRx=∫0h(x−PtRx) dt.\|G_h^{\mathbb R}\|\le h,\qquad (I-P_h^{\mathbb R})G_h^{\mathbb R}x=\int_0^h(x-P_t^{\mathbb R}x)\,dt.∥GhR​∥≤h,(I−PhR​)GhR​x=∫0h​(x−PtR​x)dt.

Proof. Apply the isometry to the extracted operators and use the fixed projection identities. Norm preservation transfers the bound without a dimension loss. Adjoint extraction commutes with the integral. Injectivity of the embedding transfers the Poisson equation; preservation of its real inner product transfers the full Dirichlet energy estimate with factor h2h^2h2.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

realOp, realCorrector and realDefect are explicit adjoint extractions of constructed objects. The embedding lemmas identify them exactly. realCorrector_poisson and realCorrector_energy_le transfer the already proved complex identities through the isometry.

04Actual coordinate embeddings on square-integrable observables §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construction. A pointwise real linear isometry induces an isometry on L2L^2L2 by composition. For a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space, use an orthonormal basis, take its real coordinates and include them in complex coordinate space.

Proof. The pointwise norm equality gives equality of the integral square norms, so the lifted map is a genuine isometry of equivalence classes. The coordinate representation is an isometry by the orthonormal basis theorem; real-to-complex inclusion preserves the sum of squared coordinate norms. Composing these maps and then lifting to L2L^2L2 preserves every observable norm exactly.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

mapLp uses actual continuous linear pointwise composition and proves its norm equality by almost-everywhere representative comparison. euclideanOfReal, coordinate and coordinateLp construct the explicit finite-dimensional embedding; coordinateLp_norm records its exact norm equality.
