Self-adjointness of the actual Langevin transition

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

Actual anchored random MALA pairs are reversible. Their corresponding Gibbs observable compressions converge strongly to the actual Langevin operator. Passing the symmetric pairing identity to that genuine limit proves actual diffusion reversibility.

01The true reversible approximants and their diffusion limit §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The actual canonical Langevin operator PtP_tPt​ is self-adjoint for t≥0t\ge0t≥0.

Proof. The zero-time diffusion variable is the actual initial coordinate. The exact anchored sampled MALA pair law therefore identifies its initial/endpoint pair with the reversible explicit kernel-power pair. The pair-law theorem makes each actual MALA compression self-adjoint. Choose the floor-mesh counts approaching ttt. The actual MALA operator approximation theorem gives strong convergence to PtP_tPt​; self-adjointness passes to that strong limit by testing all pairings against fixed vectors.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

mala_pair_swap uses the exact canonical anchored law and actual initial representative. malaOp_isSelfAdjoint applies pair-law reversal. op_isSelfAdjoint combines the floor-mesh convergence, actual operator approximation and the proved strong-limit self-adjointness theorem.
