Actual MALA approximations converge to the Langevin operators

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

On the genuine Brownian/uniform extension, Euler converges strongly to the lifted actual diffusion, while the mean MALA/Euler discrepancy is bounded by accumulated rejections. Stationary observable continuity then upgrades actual MALA convergence in measure to strong convergence of all L2L^2L2 transition orbits.

01The actual lifted diffusion and Euler limit §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The actual diffusion lifted to the uniform extension retains its Gibbs law and continuity. The Euler path from the same Brownian innovations converges strongly to it along any bounded moving mesh with ri→0r_i\to0ri​→0 and niri→tn_ir_i\to tni​ri​→t.

Proof. The source-lifting isometry preserves laws and continuity. Identify the actual Gaussian innovations with the normalized Brownian increments, then identify the Euler recursion with the actual additive-noise Euler construction. Apply its moving-step convergence theorem and lift the result through the isometry. The auxiliary mean-norm bound follows from Cauchy–Schwarz on a probability space.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.16.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.25.1; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.29.7; ULA-SC, Section 3.32.13; ULA-SC, Section 3.42.5; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.17; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.19; ULA-SC, Section 3.8.20; Kinetic, Section 3.14.1.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

stateLift is actual product-projection pullback and diffusion lifts the canonical global state. The Gaussian/Brownian and Euler/additive identities identify the concrete recursions before invoking moving-step Euler convergence.

02Rejections vanish on a bounded elapsed horizon §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. The expected MALA/Euler distance is at most nCrrnCr\sqrt rnCrr​. For bounded elapsed time nr≤Tnr\le Tnr≤T, it tends to zero as r→0r\to0r→0, so the actual MALA endpoint converges in measure to the actual diffusion endpoint.

Proof. Transfer the proved cumulative rejection bound to the genuine canonical random coupling. Its upper bound is at most TCrTC\sqrt rTCr​. The actual Euler endpoint converges strongly, hence in mean and in measure, to the diffusion. Add the two mean-distance estimates and use the integral criterion for convergence in measure. Required small-step restrictions hold eventually as the mesh tends to zero.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.34.22; ULA-SC, Section 3.34.23.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

mala_euler_integral_le specializes the actual random-coupling estimate. mala_variable_tendstoInMeasure proves eventual step restrictions, controls cumulative rejections by the elapsed horizon, combines the Euler limit and applies the mean-norm convergence criterion.

03Strong convergence of the actual observable operators §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For every square-integrable Gibbs observable fff, actual MALA evaluations converge in L2L^2L2 to diffusion evaluation; their compressed operators converge strongly to PtfP_tfPt​f.

Proof. Every MALA endpoint has the exact Gibbs law. The actual diffusion limit has that same law, so stationary-observable convergence upgrades convergence in measure of states to strong convergence of every evaluated observable. Diffusion evaluation on the extension is exactly the source-lift of original evaluation. The common lift is an isometry, so compression removes it. Boundedness of the fixed initial adjoint then passes evaluation convergence to the actual compressed operators.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

malaEvaluation and diffusionEvaluation are actual stationary pullbacks. The evaluation-limit theorem applies the proved stationary convergence result. diffusionEvaluation_eq_lift and diffusion_compression remove the independent extension. malaOp_variable_tendsto passes the limit through the fixed adjoint compression.
