The exact conditional mean of the actual Euler defect

References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

  • Francesco Pedrotti and Peter A. Whalley (2026). Wasserstein mixing time of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm. arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.02430, version 1 (3 August 2026). Paper HTML · Full paper

The actual score is the true target gradient evaluated on the actual diffusion. The proved conditional Markov identity identifies the mean of every genuine score increment, and integration gives the precise semigroup source for the actual local Euler defect.

01Identifying the true score evaluations §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For the actual target observable f=∇Vf=\nabla Vf=∇V, Utf=∇V(Yt)U_tf=\nabla V(Y_t)Ut​f=∇V(Yt​), and the actual local increment is In(s)=Unhf−Unh+sfI_n(s)=U_{nh}f-U_{nh+s}fIn​(s)=Unh​f−Unh+s​f.

Proof. Transport the actual gradient representative through the measure-preserving Gibbs state map, and compare it with the true L2L^2L2 drift representative. The proved global/segment path identity then identifies the two actual score evaluations defining the local increment.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2; ULA-SC, Section 3.13.3; ULA-SC, Section 3.26.3.

02The actual conditional source integral §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim.

E[In(s)∣Fn]=Unh(f−Psf),E[Dn∣Fn]=Unh∫0h(f−Psf) ds.\mathbb E[I_n(s)\mid\mathcal F_n]=U_{nh}(f-P_sf),\qquad \mathbb E[D_n\mid\mathcal F_n]=U_{nh}\int_0^h(f-P_sf)\,ds.E[In​(s)∣Fn​]=Unh​(f−Ps​f),E[Dn​∣Fn​]=Unh​∫0h​(f−Ps​f)ds.

Proof. The current evaluation is fixed by conditioning. Apply the actual conditional Markov theorem to the later score evaluation and subtract, obtaining the increment mean. The actual increment curve is continuous and hence integrable on the compact step interval. Commute the conditional projection and evaluation through its true integral to obtain the defect mean.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

actualScoreIncrement_conditionalMean applies the actual Markov theorem at the true grid time, including its zero-time identity. actualDefect_conditionalMean invokes conditionalDefect_eq_evaluation_integral with the actual continuous score-increment curve.
