References and publication status for this development

Preprint source; original statements proved by a modified argument

The supplied manuscript is audited by exact source hashes and compared with arXiv:2608.02430v1. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.1 are proved under their original C2C^2C2 Hessian assumptions, with the same constants, using a modified proof. The actual Langevin dynamics, reversible semigroup, corrector, Gaussian ULA invariant law and comparison estimates are constructed. The paper's generator/eigenbasis construction and Gaussian-smoothing passage are not formalized. The separate IPLA application derives new bounds by combining this result with Project 03's particle geometry and parameter concentration.

Statement and proof

01Isometric cancellation and strong-limit algebra §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Replacing both observation maps by their common isometric lift leaves their adjoint compression unchanged. A strong limit of self-adjoint operators is self-adjoint. If two strongly convergent operator families are contractions, their compositions converge strongly to the composition of their limits.

Proof. The lift cancels because its adjoint times itself is the identity. For self-adjointness, pass to the limit in the two inner-product pairings against fixed vectors. For composition, split the error into the first contraction applied to the second orbit error and the first-family error evaluated at the fixed limiting vector. Both terms tend to zero. Uniqueness of limits then identifies any separately known composition limit.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

compression_isometricLift uses the exact adjoint composition identities. strongLimit_isSelfAdjoint passes symmetric pairings through limits. strongLimit_comp uses the contraction norm bound and a two-term difference; strongLimit_comp_eq applies uniqueness of the resulting vector limits.
