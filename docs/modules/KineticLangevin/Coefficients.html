01Definition, continuity, and derivatives of the response coefficients §

Mathematical statement and proof

The response at index zero is the exponential, and each successor response is the time integral of the preceding one. Continuity follows by induction: exponential continuity handles zero, while continuity of the primitive handles the successor. The fundamental theorem of calculus then proves ψk+1′(t)=ψk(t)\psi'_{k+1}(t)=\psi_k(t)ψk+1′​(t)=ψk​(t), using interval integrability and continuity of the integrand. The separate zero-index derivative is ψ0′=−γψ0\psi'_0=-\gamma\psi_0ψ0′​=−γψ0​, obtained from the exponential chain rule; algebraic rewriting and simplification reconcile its scalar expression with the recursive notation.

The first three responses have a direct dynamical interpretation. The exponential propagates an initial velocity, its first primitive propagates velocity into position, and its second primitive propagates a constant force into position. The recursive notation also makes all higher-order time integrations unambiguous.

06Definition and factorization of the Hessian response §

Mathematical statement and proof

The damped response φ\varphiφ assigns zero to index zero as an explicit convention and defines positive indices by exponential convolution. The defining formulas distinguish these cases.
