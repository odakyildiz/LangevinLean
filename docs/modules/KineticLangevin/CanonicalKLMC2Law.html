The genuine canonical KLMC2 Gaussian Markov law and coupling bridge

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The second-order sampler is now a single actual Gaussian Markov law, not a law depending on an analysis coupling. Its four-noise stream is built from the genuine canonical Brownian kernels. The module proves the exact complete initial/noise product law, identifies the original pointwise output with the correlated-Gaussian Markov iterates, and identifies every actual random-Hessian L² coupling construction with that same output.

01Define the independent-source actual second-order law §

Mathematical statement and proof

The source contains independent standard Gaussian velocity and a vector Brownian coordinate. The implemented four-kernel noise reads that Brownian coordinate. Its finite second moments follow from the actual convolution construction and measure-preserving source projection. The pointwise original KLMC2 chain on initial-position times source defines the canonical output law, which is a probability measure by the proved original-step measurability.

02Prove the complete actual Gaussian Markov identification §

Mathematical statement and proof

On phase-times-Brownian source, the initial phase is independent of the entire actual iid four-kernel stream. Reassociation of independent initial position and standard Gaussian velocity pulls this exact law back to the sampler’s source. The resulting initial-phase/whole-stream product law determines the Markov iterates through the general random-recursion theorem. Projecting to position proves the independently defined canonical Brownian sampler law equals exactly the implemented correlated-Gaussian Markov output.

03Identify every actual L² coupling construction §

Mathematical statement and proof

The directly lifted Brownian convolutions and the generic independent-source noise extension have the same original source representatives, hence are equal in L² coordinate by coordinate. The actual numerical chain uses the original gradient lift, the actual bounded random Hessian and those same four convolutions. The general coupling-independent output theorem therefore gives its exact canonical law. The zero and successor identities expose the genuine initial state and recurrence needed by the stochastic convergence proof.

04Establish finite transport and the exact initial cost §

Mathematical statement and proof

A finite-cost initial coupling and the finite target second moment construct an actual L² numerical chain. Its output-law equality transfers this finite second moment to the single canonical sampler law. Existence of a finite-cost initial plan therefore proves the true output Wasserstein distance is finite. Finally, the initial numerical/reference position discrepancy is exactly the root-mean-square cost of the supplied initial coupling. These facts permit the final convergence estimate to take the actual infimum over initial couplings without assuming an optimizer or hiding infinite distances through real conversion.
