The OBABO composition and its difference identities

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The comparison uses the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. The results prove Wasserstein convergence of the kinetic diffusion, invariance of its Gibbs measure, KLMC and KLMC2 error estimates for correlated Gaussian increments, their Cholesky representations, and explicit iteration counts for a prescribed error. The corrected proofs are unpublished. The reference table records the correction to the continuous prefactor and the stochastic Hessian-difference terms retained in the second-order comparison. The KLMC2 estimate retains the exact fourth Gaussian moment in every dimension; the coarser displayed simplification assumes p≥2p\ge2p≥2. The stronger inverse-step-square exponential term is also proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L2L^2L2 on finite time intervals. Almost-sure continuity of sample paths is not proved here.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

Let V\mathcal VV be a real vector space (no norm is needed), F:V→VF:\mathcal V\to\mathcal VF:V→V a force field and phase points z=(v,x)∈V×Vz=(v,x)\in\mathcal V\times\mathcal Vz=(v,x)∈V×V, velocity first. The OBABO integrator is the composition of five substeps: an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck velocity update OOO with damping δ\deltaδ, noise coefficient σ\sigmaσ and a supplied noise vector ξ\xiξ; a half kick Bh/2B_{h/2}Bh/2​, which changes the velocity by −(h/2)F(x)-(h/2)F(x)−(h/2)F(x); a drift AhA_hAh​, which moves the position by hvhvhv; a second half kick; and a second Ornstein–Uhlenbeck update with a new noise vector ξ′\xi'ξ′. The three middle substeps form the velocity Verlet step.

This section proves pathwise identities for these maps, for arbitrary supplied vectors: the expanded formula of one OBABO step; the inverse of each kick and drift; the conjugation of kicks and drifts by velocity reversal; the identity V−h∘Vh=idV_{-h}\circ V_h=\mathrm{id}V−h​∘Vh​=id for the Verlet step VhV_hVh​; the triviality of OBABO for h=0h=0h=0, δ=1\delta=1δ=1, σ=0\sigma=0σ=0; the difference of two OBABO steps driven by the same noises, in which both noises cancel; and, for the standard damping δ=e−γh/2\delta=e^{-\gamma h/2}δ=e−γh/2, the identity δ2u+(u(1−δ2))2=u\delta^2u+\bigl(\sqrt{u(1-\delta^2)}\bigr)^2=uδ2u+(u(1−δ2)​)2=u, which expresses that each Ornstein–Uhlenbeck half step preserves the velocity variance uuu. Probability laws of the noises and convergence of the scheme are treated in later sections.

03The Verlet step has an exact inverse §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. The velocity Verlet step is Vh=Bh/2∘Ah∘Bh/2V_h=B_{h/2}\circ A_h\circ B_{h/2}Vh​=Bh/2​∘Ah​∘Bh/2​.

Claim. (i) For all hhh and zzz, V−h(Vhz)=zV_{-h}(V_hz)=zV−h​(Vh​z)=z.
(ii) For every FFF, zzz, ξ\xiξ, ξ′\xi'ξ′, the OBABO step with h=0h=0h=0, δ=1\delta=1δ=1, σ=0\sigma=0σ=0 is the identity: Ψ(z;ξ,ξ′)=z\Psi(z;\xi,\xi')=zΨ(z;ξ,ξ′)=z.

Proof. (i) Since (−h)/2=−(h/2)(-h)/2=-(h/2)(−h)/2=−(h/2),

V−h∘Vh=B−h/2∘A−h∘B−h/2∘Bh/2∘Ah∘Bh/2.V_{-h}\circ V_h=B_{-h/2}\circ A_{-h}\circ B_{-h/2}\circ B_{h/2}\circ A_h\circ B_{h/2}.V−h​∘Vh​=B−h/2​∘A−h​∘B−h/2​∘Bh/2​∘Ah​∘Bh/2​.

By (i) of the previous step the innermost pair B−h/2∘Bh/2B_{-h/2}\circ B_{h/2}B−h/2​∘Bh/2​ is the identity, then A−h∘AhA_{-h}\circ A_hA−h​∘Ah​, then B−h/2∘Bh/2B_{-h/2}\circ B_{h/2}B−h/2​∘Bh/2​. (ii) With h=0h=0h=0 the kicks and the drift are the identity, and with δ=1\delta=1δ=1, σ=0\sigma=0σ=0 so are both Ornstein–Uhlenbeck substeps. □\square□

The Verlet step is palindromic, which is why running it with the negated step undoes it exactly. Combined with (iii) of the previous step it gives R∘Vh∘R=V−h=Vh−1R\circ V_h\circ R=V_{-h}=V_h^{-1}R∘Vh​∘R=V−h​=Vh−1​, the reversibility used for Metropolis corrections of Hamiltonian proposals.

  3. obaboStep_zero is closed by simp after unfolding the five substeps.

04Synchronous noises cancel in the difference of two steps §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let z=(v,x)z=(v,x)z=(v,x), w=(v′,x′)w=(v',x')w=(v′,x′), h,δ,σ∈Rh,\delta,\sigma\in\mathbb Rh,δ,σ∈R and ξ,ξ′∈V\xi,\xi'\in\mathcal Vξ,ξ′∈V. Put

Proof. By the expanded formula of the first step, the half-step velocities are v1/2=δv+σξ−h2F(x)v_{1/2}=\delta v+\sigma\xi-\frac h2F(x)v1/2​=δv+σξ−2h​F(x) and v1/2′=δv′+σξ−h2F(x′)v'_{1/2}=\delta v'+\sigma\xi-\frac h2F(x')v1/2′​=δv′+σξ−2h​F(x′); their difference is aaa, the noise σξ\sigma\xiσξ cancelling. The new positions are x+=x+hv1/2x^+=x+hv_{1/2}x+=x+hv1/2​ and y+=x′+hv1/2′y^+=x'+hv'_{1/2}y+=x′+hv1/2′​, whose difference is x−x′+hax-x'+hax−x′+ha. The new velocities are δ(v1/2−h2F(x+))+σξ′\delta(v_{1/2}-\frac h2F(x^+))+\sigma\xi'δ(v1/2​−2h​F(x+))+σξ′ and δ(v1/2′−h2F(y+))+σξ′\delta(v'_{1/2}-\frac h2F(y^+))+\sigma\xi'δ(v1/2′​−2h​F(y+))+σξ′; subtracting, σξ′\sigma\xi'σξ′ cancels and the difference is δ(a−h2(F(x+)−F(y+)))\delta(a-\frac h2(F(x^+)-F(y^+)))δ(a−2h​(F(x+)−F(y+))). □\square□

Under a synchronous coupling both thermostat noises disappear from the difference, which therefore evolves deterministically; but the second kick still involves the forces at the new positions x+x^+x+, y+y^+y+, which depend on the noise ξ\xiξ. The contraction analysis of OBABO starts from this identity.

05The standard damping and the preservation of the velocity variance §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definitions. For γ,h∈R\gamma,h\in\mathbb Rγ,h∈R put δ=e−γh/2\delta=e^{-\gamma h/2}δ=e−γh/2 and ς2=1−δ2\varsigma^2=1-\delta^2ς2=1−δ2.

Interpretation: if a velocity coordinate has variance uuu and ξ\xiξ is an independent standard Gaussian, then δv+u(1−δ2) ξ\delta v+\sqrt{u(1-\delta^2)}\,\xiδv+u(1−δ2)​ξ again has variance δ2u+u(1−δ2)=u\delta^2u+u(1-\delta^2)=uδ2u+u(1−δ2)=u. With uuu the inverse mass, this is the choice σ=u(1−e−γh)\sigma=\sqrt{u(1-e^{-\gamma h})}σ=u(1−e−γh)​ of the noise coefficient for which each half thermostat preserves the Gaussian velocity marginal; the identity itself is a statement about real numbers.
