The OBABO splitting and its exact pathwise identities

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

OBABO brackets a kick–drift–kick Hamiltonian step between two Ornstein–Uhlenbeck half steps. The module proves the composition and its reversal and cancellation identities for arbitrary supplied vectors. The Gaussian-law and convergence analysis is separate. Its thermostat coefficient is chosen so that the damping and fresh noise exactly preserve a target scalar velocity variance.

The five-stage chronology determines both deterministic reversibility and the placement of the two independent refresh noises. Its expanded identities are the starting point for the weighted contraction calculation.

01Define each subflow and compose O–B–A–B–O §

Mathematical statement and proof

In velocity–position ordering, O(v,x)=(δv+σξ,x)O(v,x)=(\delta v+\sigma\xi,x)O(v,x)=(δv+σξ,x), Bh(v,x)=(v−hg(x),x)B_h(v,x)=(v-hg(x),x)Bh​(v,x)=(v−hg(x),x), and Ah(v,x)=(v,x+hv)A_h(v,x)=(v,x+hv)Ah​(v,x)=(v,x+hv). the OBABO update composes O,Bh/2,Ah,Bh/2,OO,B_{h/2},A_h,B_{h/2},OO,Bh/2​,Ah​,Bh/2​,O, using distinct noise arguments in the two thermostat steps. Expanding the nested definitions gives v1/2=δv+σξ−hg(x)/2v_{1/2}=\delta v+\sigma\xi-hg(x)/2v1/2​=δv+σξ−hg(x)/2, x+=x+hv1/2x^+=x+hv_{1/2}x+=x+hv1/2​, and v+=δ(v1/2−hg(x+)/2)+σξ′v^+=\delta(v_{1/2}-hg(x^+)/2)+\sigma\xi'v+=δ(v1/2​−hg(x+)/2)+σξ′. the expanded five-stage OBABO formula is proved by the defining formulas because this formula follows directly from the composition; the new-position force is structurally enforced.

The first and last thermostat stages are half steps, as are the two force kicks; the position drift is a full step. Keeping this chronology explicit matters because the second force evaluation occurs after the position update. The composition is symmetric in its deterministic stages.

02Reverse the deterministic kick and drift §

Mathematical statement and proof

The kick leaves position fixed, so subtracting hg(x)hg(x)hg(x) and then adding it cancels exactly. The drift leaves velocity fixed, so adding hvhvhv and then subtracting it also cancels. Their inverse formulas hold for arbitrary real hhh and arbitrary force, with no smoothness assumption. velocity reversal negates velocity and keeps position; applying it twice is the identity. Conjugating a kick or drift by this reversal changes hhh to −h-h−h. The proofs use pair extensionality and simplification of scalar signs, making the time-reversal algebra explicit.

A kick changes velocity while holding position fixed, so reversing its step size gives its inverse. A drift changes position while holding velocity fixed and has the same property. These triangular maps are the elementary reversible pieces used to build the Hamiltonian proposal.

03The palindromic Hamiltonian composition has an exact inverse §

Mathematical statement and proof

the velocity-Verlet map is Bh/2AhBh/2B_{h/2}A_hB_{h/2}Bh/2​Ah​Bh/2​. To invert it, reverse the order and invert each factor. Since the composition is palindromic, this is precisely B−h/2A−hB−h/2B_{-h/2}A_{-h}B_{-h/2}B−h/2​A−h​B−h/2​. The proof expands the two compositions, rewrites (−h)/2=−(h/2)(-h)/2=-(h/2)(−h)/2=−(h/2), and cancels the three adjacent inverse pairs with the preceding lemmas. The OBABO zero-time theorem separately checks that h=0,δ=1,σ=0h=0,\delta=1,\sigma=0h=0,δ=1,σ=0 makes every substep the identity. Reversal of the deterministic map alone does not assert reversibility of a stochastic transition with a particular probability density.

Velocity reversal changes the sign of the drift direction and interchanges forward and backward kicks. The palindrome of the Verlet composition therefore yields an exact algebraic inverse. This reversibility identity holds for any force; conservation of the true Hamiltonian is a separate question.

04Synchronous noises cancel while the new forces remain §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply two OBABO steps with the same ξ,ξ′\xi,\xi'ξ,ξ′. The difference of half velocities is a=δΔv−h(g(x)−g(y))/2a=\delta\Delta v-h(g(x)-g(y))/2a=δΔv−h(g(x)−g(y))/2. Thus Δx+=Δx+ha\Delta x^+=\Delta x+haΔx+=Δx+ha. The final shared refresh cancels too, leaving Δv+=δ(a−h(g(x+)−g(y+))/2)\Delta v^+=\delta(a-h(g(x^+)-g(y^+))/2)Δv+=δ(a−h(g(x+)−g(y+))/2). The proof unfolds all subflows, proves the velocity and position identities as a conjunction, and normalizes vector expressions with vector-space algebra. Although additive noises disappear explicitly from the difference, the new positions still depend on the common first noise. The identity preserves their force evaluations instead of treating them as old-position gradients.

The two trajectories use the same first thermostat innovation and the same second thermostat innovation. Their differences still contain forces at their respective updated positions. Thus cancellation of noise simplifies the comparison but does not linearize the nonlinear force.

05Choose damping and prove the thermal variance identity §

Mathematical statement and proof

The OU half-step damping is δ=e−γh/2>0\delta=e^{-\gamma h/2}>0δ=e−γh/2>0. The exponential addition identity gives δ2=e−γh\delta^2=e^{-\gamma h}δ2=e−γh. With γ,h≥0\gamma,h\ge0γ,h≥0, this is at most one, so s2=1−δ2≥0s^2=1-\delta^2\ge0s2=1−δ2≥0. For a target velocity variance u≥0u\ge0u≥0, set σ=u(1−δ2)\sigma=\sqrt{u(1-\delta^2)}σ=u(1−δ2)​. Then δ2u+σ2=u\delta^2u+\sigma^2=uδ2u+σ2=u. One proves positivity directly from positivity of the exponential, rewrites the square with the exponential addition formula, uses the nonpositive exponent to bound the exponential, and finally applies the square-root identity followed by expansion of the scalar polynomials. For independent centered input velocity and fresh Gaussian noise this is the scalar covariance balance; Gaussian-law preservation additionally uses those distributional facts.

With δ=e−γh/2\delta=e^{-\gamma h/2}δ=e−γh/2, the deterministic variance contribution is δ2\delta^2δ2. An independent standard Gaussian scaled by 1−δ2\sqrt{1-\delta^2}1−δ2​ supplies the missing variance. Nonnegative γh\gamma hγh ensures that the square root is real and that thermostat is contractive.
