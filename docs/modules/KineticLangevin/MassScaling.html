Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. 01Normalize the true phase drift §

02Transform the integral and its actual Brownian amplitude §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply the bounded linear map to the solution identity at t/ct/ct/c, pass it through the Bochner integral, and substitute r=s/cr=s/cr=s/c. The transformed equation has friction γ/c\gamma/cγ/c and inverse mass one. The additive input becomes c−1Bt/cc^{-1}B_{t/c}c−1Bt/c​. For the original thermal amplitude 2γc2\sqrt{2\gamma c^2}2γc2​, the coefficient identity gives c−12γc2=2γ/ccc^{-1}\sqrt{2\gamma c^2}=\sqrt{2\gamma/c}\sqrt cc−12γc2​=2γ/c​c​. The separately proved changed-clock Brownian process is cWt/c\sqrt c W_{t/c}c​Wt/c​, so the resulting equation has exactly the required fluctuation-dissipation coefficient. Positivity of ccc supplies both the interval inclusion and the square-root cancellations.
