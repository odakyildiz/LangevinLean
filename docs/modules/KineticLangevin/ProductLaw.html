KLMC output laws on independent extensions of initial couplings

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The comparison uses the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. The results prove Wasserstein convergence of the kinetic diffusion, invariance of its Gibbs measure, KLMC and KLMC2 error estimates for correlated Gaussian increments, their Cholesky representations, and explicit iteration counts for a prescribed error. The corrected proofs are unpublished. The reference table records the correction to the continuous prefactor and the stochastic Hessian-difference terms retained in the second-order comparison. The KLMC2 estimate retains the exact fourth Gaussian moment in every dimension; the coarser displayed simplification assumes p≥2p\ge2p≥2. The stronger inverse-step-square exponential term is also proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L2L^2L2 on finite time intervals. Almost-sure continuity of sample paths is not proved here.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

05Independence of the output law from the coupling §

Mathematical statement and proof

Recall that a map FFF is measurable PPP-almost everywhere if it agrees PPP-almost everywhere with a measurable map; image measures under such maps are defined through that measurable version.

Claim. Let ccc be a coupling of the probability measure μ\muμ and of π\piπ.

  1. If F:H×S→HF:H\times S\to HF:H×S→H is measurable (μ⊗Q)(\mu\otimes Q)(μ⊗Q)-almost everywhere, then the image of PcP_cPc​ under ((x,y),s)↦F(x,s)((x,y),s)\mapsto F(x,s)((x,y),s)↦F(x,s) equals the image of μ⊗Q\mu\otimes Qμ⊗Q under FFF.
  2. If ∇f\nabla f∇f is MMM-Lipschitz and V,Wk(1),Wk(2)V,W_k^{(1)},W_k^{(2)}V,Wk(1)​,Wk(2)​ are square integrable, then Φn\Phi_nΦn​ is measurable (μ⊗Q)(\mu\otimes Q)(μ⊗Q)-almost everywhere.

The image of a product measure under a product of measurable maps is the product of the images; since ccc has first marginal μ\muμ, ρ\rhoρ maps Pc=c⊗QP_c=c\otimes QPc​=c⊗Q to μ⊗Q\mu\otimes Qμ⊗Q. Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. If ∇f\nabla f∇f is Lipschitz and V,Wk(1),Wk(2)V,W_k^{(1)},W_k^{(2)}V,Wk(1)​,Wk(2)​ are square integrable, then νn\nu_nνn​ is a probability measure for every nnn.
