The exact Brownian equations on every numerical time interval

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The numerical method uses fresh correlated noise on each interval (a,a+t](a,a+t](a,a+t]. The preceding module constructs those noises on the same canonical Brownian source and proves independence across disjoint intervals. Here the actual noises also satisfy the integrated kinetic equations, so their relationship to the continuous diffusion is an equality in L² rather than a matching-law assumption.

03Prove the actual damped Brownian integral equation §

The exact coefficient identity ψ0+γψ1=1\psi_0+\gamma\psi_1=1ψ0​+γψ1​=1 gives Vta+γPta=WtaV^a_t+\gamma P^a_t=W^a_tVta​+γPta​=Wta​. 04Recover the ordinary Brownian convolution formula §

Mathematical statement and proof

The position response also equals Pta=∫0te−γ(t−s)WsadsP^a_t=\int_0^te^{-\gamma(t-s)}W^a_sdsPta​=∫0t​e−γ(t−s)Wsa​ds, hence Vta=Wta−γ∫0te−γ(t−s)WsadsV^a_t=W^a_t-\gamma\int_0^te^{-\gamma(t-s)}W^a_sdsVta​=Wta​−γ∫0t​e−γ(t−s)Wsa​ds. To prove this for the exact translated construction, Lean pulls the established unshifted formula back through the injective isonormal isometry, then sends the deterministic identity through the translated continuous linear map. Continuity ensures Bochner integrability. Commuting that map with the integral and its scalar factors yields the formula; the velocity version follows by the balance identity. These are exact integration-by-parts identities on the constructed Brownian source.
