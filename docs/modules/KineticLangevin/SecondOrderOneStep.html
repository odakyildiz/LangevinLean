The actual second-order one-step coupling recurrence

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

These results connect the implemented four-kernel KLMC2 update to the true stationary kinetic segment and combine the genuine local and repaired comparison estimates. Every moment and error constant has already been derived from actual path equations and Gaussian/Gibbs marginals.

01Recover the local friction restriction from the source step size §

Mathematical statement and proof

The restriction 5 gamma M h at most m, together with m at most M and M positive, implies 5 gamma h at most one. Cancelling the positive M gives gamma h at most one fifth. This is precisely the small-time restriction used to convert the cubic and quartic local component estimates into the transformed quarter-bound.

02Bound the local error of the actual implemented step §

Mathematical statement and proof

Use the actual corrected-step identity for all four correlated Brownian kernels. The initial velocity and fresh filtered noise define the complete free-OU path. The actual stationary reference has the exact mild and kinematic equations, with Gaussian-times-Gibbs time marginals. The proved local theorem therefore bounds the transformed norm of the implemented step minus the exact endpoint by t^3 times [M2 sqrt(p^2+2p)+M sqrt(Mp)] divided by four. The exact fourth-moment dimension remains valid in every dimension.

03Combine local defect and repaired synchronous comparison §

Mathematical statement and proof

Insert the numerical update starting at the stationary reference into the difference between the actual numerical update and exact reference endpoint. The transformed triangle inequality splits this into comparison of two actual numerical steps and the actual reference local defect. Proposition 3 controls the former, including both fresh stochastic Hessian terms. The preceding local theorem controls the latter. The result is the source affine one-step inequality with factor 1-mt/(2 gamma)+M2 sqrt(alpha)t^2/gamma, Gaussian tail sqrt(2)t^2(M-m)exp(-(alpha-p)/8), and cubic local term. Its remaining path and law premises are supplied by the constructed canonical segment in the final sampler theorem.
