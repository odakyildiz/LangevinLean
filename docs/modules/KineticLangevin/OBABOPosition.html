The original OBABO position laws satisfy the full sampling bound

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The actual initial phase law is standard Gaussian velocity independent of the specified position law. Coupling these velocities identically costs nothing. The explicit inverse weighted coordinate map then projects the proved full Markov theorem to the actual position laws, with factor √2 and finite output transport established separately.

01Project the contracting geometry to the physical position §

Mathematical statement and proof

The projection is the position coordinate of the explicitly verified inverse transform. The metric lower bound gives ∥x−y∥2≤2∥T(v,x)−T(w,y)∥2\|x-y\|^2\le2\|T(v,x)-T(w,y)\|^2∥x−y∥2≤2∥T(v,x)−T(w,y)∥2. Both sides are nonnegative, so the square comparison yields the global √2 Lipschitz coefficient. Composing this projection with the original weighted transform is exactly the ordinary position projection; this is proved from the inverse identity, not supplied as a law equivalence.

02Adjoin the same Gaussian velocity to every initial position coupling §

Mathematical statement and proof

For every coupling of two position laws, append one common independent Gaussian velocity. Their phase difference has zero velocity, so its weighted energy is exactly the squared original position distance. The cross-space random-map theorem takes the actual coupling infimum and yields W2,E(N⊗μ,N⊗ν)≤W2(μ,ν)W_{2,E}(\mathcal N\otimes\mu,\mathcal N\otimes\nu)\le W_2(\mu,\nu)W2,E​(N⊗μ,N⊗ν)≤W2​(μ,ν). Product-measure swapping proves that the actual pushforward initialization is the stated independent Gaussian-times-position law. No optimal position coupling is assumed.

03Obtain the unconditional actual position estimate and expose finite transport §

Mathematical statement and proof

Start from N(0,Id)⊗μ\mathcal N(0,I_d)\otimes\muN(0,Id​)⊗μ, use the actual independent Gaussian-pair OBABO transition at every step, and let νn\nu_nνn​ be its actual position marginal. Under the original strong-convexity, smoothness, friction and step-size restrictions, the theorem proves both finite W2(νn,π)W_2(\nu_n,\pi)W2​(νn​,π) and

W2(νn,π)≤2 rnW2(μ,π)+62γmhd(4M+Mh2),r=1−mh3γ.W_2(\nu_n,\pi)\le\sqrt2\,r^nW_2(\mu,\pi)+\frac{6\sqrt2\gamma}{m}h\sqrt d\left(4\sqrt M+\frac{Mh}{2}\right),\qquad r=\sqrt{1-\frac{mh}{3\gamma}}.W2​(νn​,π)≤2​rnW2​(μ,π)+m62​γ​hd​(4M​+2Mh​),r=1−3γmh​​.

The initial weighted distance is bounded by the actual initial position distance using the preceding shared-velocity construction. The full weighted theorem and its finite-output counterpart apply to the implemented Markov laws. The √2 Lipschitz transport theorem projects both actual output measures; the target projection is the already proved normalized Gibbs marginal. Output finiteness is proved before the real-valued inequality is used, so this endpoint can safely imply RMS consequences. The finite-dimensional index type is arbitrary, allowing direct reuse for the scaled particle configuration.
