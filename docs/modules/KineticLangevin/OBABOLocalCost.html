The integrated common-noise local error for OBABO

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The comparison uses the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. The results prove Wasserstein convergence of the kinetic diffusion, invariance of its Gibbs measure, KLMC and KLMC2 error estimates for correlated Gaussian increments, their Cholesky representations, and explicit iteration counts for a prescribed error. The corrected proofs are unpublished. The reference table records the correction to the continuous prefactor and the stochastic Hessian-difference terms retained in the second-order comparison. The KLMC2 estimate retains the exact fourth Gaussian moment in every dimension; the coarser displayed simplification assumes p≥2p\ge2p≥2. The stronger inverse-step-square exponential term is also proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L2L^2L2 on finite time intervals. Almost-sure continuity of sample paths is not proved here.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

This section turns the L2L^{2}L2 Hamiltonian local comparison into the integrated cost of the original finite-dimensional maps, retaining the same initial state and both noise variables on one probability space.

The local kernel discrepancy is a common-noise transport cost. It is obtained by integrating the deterministic Verlet error against a stationary Hamiltonian comparator in the contracting phase metric.

01The explicit local-error bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

The local-error bound is bh=2h2(Md+Md+hMMd/4)b_h=2h^2(\sqrt{Md}+\sqrt M\sqrt d+h\sqrt M\sqrt{Md}/4)bh​=2h2(Md​+M​d​+hM​Md​/4). Its nonnegativity follows directly from h≥0h\ge0h≥0 and square-root nonnegativity. It is O(h2d)O(h^{2}\sqrt d)O(h2d​) with coefficients depending only on MMM and the allowed step-size range. The following calculation proves that this expression bounds the numerical error.

The local-error bound retains the explicit step size, friction, smoothness, and dimension factors obtained from the Hamiltonian comparator. Its order in the step is quadratic. This order is needed because geometric accumulation will later divide by a contraction gap of first order.

02The full common-noise defect before taking its cost §

Mathematical statement and proof

Construct the L2L^{2}L2 Hamiltonian path at the first thermostat output. The strengthened pointwise Hamiltonian lifting theorem identifies its endpoint almost everywhere with the finite-dimensional exact Hamiltonian flow at that same initial state. The lifted OBABO theorem and final-thermostat representative theorem therefore identify the two outputs jointly, including both original noise variables. Subtracting the phase representatives and applying the weighted-transform L2L^{2}L2 identity gives the exact integrated weighted cost of the original vector map discrepancy. The stationary-reference local estimate then proves that this cost is at most bhb_hbh​. The only reference-law input is the law of the first thermostat output; normalized target initialization and exact thermostat invariance verify that hypothesis in the full sampler theorem.

Both updates use exactly the same thermostat noises. The weighted random-variable discrepancy is therefore a coupling cost for their output measures. Integrating the previously derived deterministic local defect under the stationary reference yields the stated kernel error.

Earlier results used here: ULA-SC, Section 3.13.2.
