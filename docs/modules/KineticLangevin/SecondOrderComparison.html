Proposition 3 with the full stochastic Hessian difference

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The printed comparison omits the difference of the two Hessians multiplying the fresh phi2 and phi3 innovations. The formal comparison retains them. They combine with the initial reference velocity into the complete free-OU response, whose actual Gaussian marginals admit the cutoff estimate without independence of the Hessian.

01Use the actual transformed phase geometry §

Mathematical statement and proof

Negating both phase coordinates negates their transformed coordinates and preserves their Euclidean norm. The centered force sector, when squared and expanded, is exactly the ordinary strong-smooth interpolation inequality. This lets the already proved centered-gradient bound in L2 supply the interpolation hypothesis of the frozen-linear theorem.

02Project the cutoff displacement with the exact coefficient §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Hessian cutoff contains M2 sqrt(alpha) times the position difference norm. The phase projection contributes sqrt(2)/gamma. The complete free-OU correction carries sqrt(2)t^2/2. Multiplication cancels sqrt(2) squared divided by two, leaving M2 sqrt(alpha)t^2/gamma times the initial transformed norm. The Gaussian tail retains sqrt(2)t^2 B exp(-(alpha-p)/8). This scalar lemma checks the cancellation separately from the probability-space construction.

03Keep all four innovations on the same source §

Mathematical statement and proof

klmc2SourceInnovation applies the actual continuous linear Brownian-source lift to the four kernels psi0, psi1, phi2, phi3. klmc2SourceStep writes the actual update using the corresponding free-OU velocity and its complete Hessian responses. With the lift scaled by sqrt(2 gamma), direct linear algebra proves this is exactly Integrator.klmc2Step at the unscaled four-kernel innovations. These are actual coupled Gaussian-response variables.

04Derive the corrected difference identity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Subtract the two source steps at z and w with common innovations. The common psi noise cancels. Freeze the linear part at the Hessian of the second starting position; its data are the actual velocity, position and force differences. The remainder is minus the Hessian difference applied to the complete phi2 and phi3 free-OU corrections built from the first reference velocity. This exact identity includes both fresh stochastic Hessian terms absent from the printed algebra.

05Supply the frozen-linear estimate from the actual potential §

Mathematical statement and proof

The actual L2 gradient retains its Lipschitz constant and centered strong-smooth sector. The actual random Hessian has operator norm at most M. The frozen-linear map therefore contracts by 1-mt/(2 gamma) under gamma^2 at least m+M and 5 gamma M t at most m. The checked deterministic theorem is applied in the Hilbert space of actual square-integrable random vectors.

07Prove the repaired source comparison inequality §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let A be the difference of the two actual random Hessian operators. Each actual free-OU velocity is standard Gaussian. The genuine Hessian difference bound M-m and Hessian Lipschitz constant M2 give the cutoff norm M2 sqrt(alpha) times the L2 position difference plus 2(M-m)exp(-(alpha-p)/8), for alpha at least 5p. Integrating the complete response yields the sharp quadratic time factor. Combining this with frozen-linear contraction and position projection proves transformed error at most [1-mt/(2 gamma)+M2 sqrt(alpha)t^2/gamma] times the initial transformed error, plus sqrt(2)t^2(M-m)exp(-(alpha-p)/8). The Gaussian premise is an actual source law and is discharged by the canonical past/future construction.
