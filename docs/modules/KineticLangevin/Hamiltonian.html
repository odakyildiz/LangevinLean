Exact Hamiltonian energy and volume-preserving Verlet geometry

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The Hamiltonian subflow is the kinetic equation with zero friction and zero noise. Its total energy is conserved by an exact cancellation between force work and kinetic-energy change. This module proves conservation for the actual Picard-constructed integral flow and proves that the explicit kick/drift/Verlet maps preserve product Haar volume. These are genuine prerequisites for Gibbs stationarity; energy conservation alone and volume preservation of a numerical map do not yet establish invariance of the full kinetic diffusion.

01Differentiate total energy along the actual Hamiltonian equations §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define H(v,x)=f(x)+∥v∥2/2H(v,x)=f(x)+\|v\|^2/2H(v,x)=f(x)+∥v∥2/2. If x′=vx'=vx′=v and v′=−∇f(x)v'=-\nabla f(x)v′=−∇f(x), the chain rule gives (f(x))′=⟨∇f(x),v⟩(f(x))'=\langle\nabla f(x),v\rangle(f(x))′=⟨∇f(x),v⟩, while the squared-norm derivative gives (∥v∥2/2)′=−⟨v,∇f(x)⟩(\|v\|^2/2)'=-\langle v,\nabla f(x)\rangle(∥v∥2/2)′=−⟨v,∇f(x)⟩. Symmetry of the real inner product makes their sum zero. Lean composes the actual Fréchet derivative of fff with the position derivative, uses the gradient representation of that derivative, and combines it with the Hilbert squared-norm derivative. The statement uses right derivatives within [t,∞)[t,\infty)[t,∞), allowing the initial endpoint of an integral solution.

02Turn vanishing right derivatives into exact conservation §

Mathematical statement and proof

A continuous scalar function with zero right derivative on [0,T)[0,T)[0,T) is constant on [0,T][0,T][0,T], including the final endpoint. The proof applies the scalar Gronwall comparison with coefficient and forcing both zero, first to FFF and then to −F-F−F. These give F(t)≤F(0)F(t)\le F(0)F(t)≤F(0) and −F(t)≤−F(0)-F(t)\le-F(0)−F(t)≤−F(0). Their combination proves equality. This avoids assuming a derivative of the chosen extension beyond the terminal time or on negative time.

04Build the reversible proposal and preserve the Hamiltonian under reversal §

Mathematical statement and proof

Velocity reversal R(v,x)=(−v,x)R(v,x)=(-v,x)R(v,x)=(−v,x) conjugates every kick and drift to its negative-time inverse. Applying this to the palindromic kick–drift–kick composition gives R Vh R=V−hR\,V_h\,R=V_{-h}RVh​R=V−h​. Since V−hVh=idV_{-h}V_h=\mathrm{id}V−h​Vh​=id, the proposal Jh=R VhJ_h=R\,V_hJh​=RVh​ satisfies Jh2=idJ_h^2=\mathrm{id}Jh2​=id. Lean rewrites the three component conjugacies and then uses the proved inverse Verlet identity. Negating velocity leaves its squared norm unchanged, so H(Rz)=H(z)H(Rz)=H(z)H(Rz)=H(z). The exact involution will give symmetric old/new proposal graph measures in the Metropolis argument.

06Prove the elementary subflows preserve Haar volume §

Mathematical statement and proof

A drift (v,x)↦(v,x+hv)(v,x)\mapsto(v,x+hv)(v,x)↦(v,x+hv) is a skew product whose second component is a translation for each fixed velocity. Translation invariance of Haar measure therefore proves preservation of the product measure. A kick (v,x)↦(v−hg(x),x)(v,x)\mapsto(v-hg(x),x)(v,x)↦(v−hg(x),x) is the same construction after swapping the two phase coordinates; measurability of ggg suffices. Lean applies the measure-preserving skew-product theorem to translations, then conjugates the kick by the measure-preserving swap. Composition proves the complete Verlet map preserves product Haar volume. This derives the Jacobian-one property without presupposing differentiability of the force.

07The reversed Verlet proposal is a measure-preserving involution §

Mathematical statement and proof

For a Haar factor also invariant under negation, velocity reversal preserves product measure: apply the negation-preserving map in the velocity coordinate and identity in the position coordinate. Composing this with the proved volume-preserving Verlet map gives volume preservation of the explicit proposal JhJ_hJh​. Together with Jh2=idJ_h^2=\mathrm{id}Jh2​=id, this supplies the two exact properties used by deterministic Metropolis detailed balance. Neither property asserts that the uncorrected finite-step Verlet map itself preserves the Gibbs weight.
