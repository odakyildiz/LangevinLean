Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. The contracting metric factor is r=1−mh/(3γ)r=\sqrt{1-mh/(3\gamma)}r=1−mh/(3γ)​. These scalar lemmas prove its strict contraction and a lower bound on its gap, then simplify the genuinely derived local budget into an explicit first-order bias coefficient.

01Quantify the contraction gap §

Mathematical statement and proof

For positive m, gamma and h, the squared rate is strictly below one, so the nonnegative square root r is also below one. When its square is nonnegative, 1−r2=mh/(3γ)1-r^2=mh/(3\gamma)1−r2=mh/(3γ). The nonnegative square (1−r)2(1-r)^2(1−r)2 gives 2(1−r)≥1−r22(1-r)\ge1-r^22(1−r)≥1−r2, hence 1−r≥mh/(6γ)1-r\ge mh/(6\gamma)1−r≥mh/(6γ). All division steps retain their positive denominator hypotheses.

02Simplify the actual local budget and divide by the proved gap §

Mathematical statement and proof

The nonnegative square-root product identity yields bh=h2d(4M+Mh/2)b_h=h^2\sqrt d(4\sqrt M+Mh/2)bh​=h2d​(4M​+Mh/2). Dividing the actual local budget by the proved lower bound on 1−r1-r1−r gives

bh1−r≤6γmhd(4M+Mh/2).\frac{b_h}{1-r}\le\frac{6\gamma}{m}h\sqrt d(4\sqrt M+Mh/2).1−rbh​​≤m6γ​hd​(4M​+Mh/2).

Thus the accumulated bias is O(h√d), and restricting h to its source upper bound makes its coefficient depend only on m, M and gamma. This is arithmetic applied to an independently proved local map error and contraction rate.
