Accumulation of quadratic local errors under OBABO contraction

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The comparison uses the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. The results prove Wasserstein convergence of the kinetic diffusion, invariance of its Gibbs measure, KLMC and KLMC2 error estimates for correlated Gaussian increments, their Cholesky representations, and explicit iteration counts for a prescribed error. The corrected proofs are unpublished. The reference table records the correction to the continuous prefactor and the stochastic Hessian-difference terms retained in the second-order comparison. The KLMC2 estimate retains the exact fourth Gaussian moment in every dimension; the coarser displayed simplification assumes p≥2. The stronger inverse-step-square exponential term is also proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite time intervals. Almost-sure continuity of sample paths is not proved here.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The contracting metric factor is r=1−mh/(3γ)r=\sqrt{1-mh/(3\gamma)}r=1−mh/(3γ)​. These scalar lemmas prove its strict contraction and a lower bound on its gap, then simplify the proved local-error bound into an explicit first-order bias coefficient.

The contraction gap is of first order in the step, while local kernel error is of second order. Their ratio produces the explicit linear-step global bias, with all friction and dimension factors visible.

02Simplify the local-error bound and divide by the proved gap §

Mathematical statement and proof

The nonnegative square-root product identity yields bh=h2d(4M+Mh/2)b_h=h^2\sqrt d(4\sqrt M+Mh/2)bh​=h2d​(4M​+Mh/2). Dividing the local-error bound by the proved lower bound on 1−r1-r1−r gives

bh1−r≤6γmhd(4M+Mh/2).\frac{b_h}{1-r}\le\frac{6\gamma}{m}h\sqrt d(4\sqrt M+Mh/2).1−rbh​​≤m6γ​hd​(4M​+Mh/2).

Thus the accumulated bias is O(hd)O(h\sqrt d)O(hd​). Restricting hhh to its stated upper bound makes the remaining coefficient depend only on m,M,γm,M,\gammam,M,γ. This is arithmetic applied to an independently proved local map error and contraction rate.

The local cost is proportional to h2dh^2\sqrt dh2d​, while the contraction gap is bounded below by a constant times mh/γmh/\gammamh/γ. Dividing cancels one power of hhh. The remaining terms give the stated linear-step bias with all physical constants retained.
