The L² splitting is the actual vector algorithm almost everywhere

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The deterministic convergence theorem applies to a Banach space of square-integrable random variables. To connect that analysis to the intended stochastic method, this module checks every elementary stage on actual representatives. The lifted force is the original force almost everywhere, and the supplied noise is retained unchanged. Induction then identifies the entire L² recursion with the vector recursion.

01Expose the chain driven by explicit filtered innovations §

Mathematical statement and proof

The innovation chain starts at its supplied phase state and recursively applies the explicit Verlet–OU step with innovation NkN_kNk​. The earlier continuous-input chain is exactly this chain with Nk=Nkh,h(B)N_k=N_{kh,h}(B)Nk​=Nkh,h​(B). Lean proves this equality by induction, unfolding the two actual recursions at the successor step. This representation lets the probability layer identify the innovation array law independently of the deterministic input analysis.

02Check force kicks on actual L² representatives §

Mathematical statement and proof

The lifted force GGG is constructed from the actual Lipschitz force ggg, and the shared lifting theorem gives G(X)(ω)=g(X(ω))G(X)(\omega)=g(X(\omega))G(X)(ω)=g(X(ω)) almost everywhere. The representative identities for subtraction and scalar multiplication therefore give [V−hG(X)](ω)=V(ω)−hg(X(ω))[V-hG(X)](\omega)=V(\omega)-hg(X(\omega))[V−hG(X)](ω)=V(ω)−hg(X(ω)). Position is unchanged. Lean intersects the three almost-everywhere events and proves equality of the two phase coordinates. No new vector-field or output-law assumption is introduced.

03Check free transport and the full supplied thermostat noise §

Mathematical statement and proof

The representative of X+hVX+hVX+hV is X(ω)+hV(ω)X(\omega)+hV(\omega)X(ω)+hV(ω), proving the free drift identity. Likewise the representative of δV+σΞ\delta V+\sigma\XiδV+σΞ is δV(ω)+σΞ(ω)\delta V(\omega)+\sigma\Xi(\omega)δV(ω)+σΞ(ω), proving the OU stage identity. Lean explicitly intersects the addition and scalar-multiplication representative events and retains the exact supplied innovation Ξ\XiΞ. These are pathwise stage identities and do not presume any Gaussian distribution.

04Compose the checked stages into the actual Verlet–OU map §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Verlet step is the kick–drift–kick composition. Apply the checked representative identity at each actual intermediate L² state, intersect the resulting almost-everywhere events, and substitute them in order. The complete splitting then composes this checked Verlet map with the checked OU stage, using exactly the damping e−γhe^{-\gamma h}e−γh and supplied filtered innovation. Lean performs these successive substitutions, so the state at which the second force is evaluated remains the updated position.

05Identify every numerical iterate with the original vector chain §

Mathematical statement and proof

The initial phase representatives agree by definition. Assuming agreement at step nnn, the checked full-step identity and the induction hypothesis give agreement at step n+1n+1n+1, driven by the same innovation representative Nn(ω)N_n(\omega)Nn​(ω). Lean intersects these two almost-everywhere events and rewrites the recursive definitions. Hence every L² iterate analyzed by the convergence theorem is the actual vector splitting algorithm on the same probability source.
