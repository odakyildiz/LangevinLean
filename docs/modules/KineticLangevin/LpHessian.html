The actual frozen random Hessian and its Taylor remainder

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The Hessian in KLMC2 is evaluated at the actual random starting position. It acts on the original coupled L² variables through the previously constructed random-operator map. Combining its pointwise Taylor remainder with actual fourth displacement moments gives Proposition 2’s quadratic Taylor forcing.

01Freeze the true Hessian at a random position §

Mathematical statement and proof

The field ω↦H(Y(ω)) is strongly measurable because H is Lipschitz and Y has a strongly measurable L² representative. The force Lipschitz bound controls its derivative: ‖H(y)‖≤M follows from HasFDerivAt g (H(y)) y and the global Lipschitz property of g. The actual random-operator construction then produces a continuous linear action on L². Its representative is exactly H(Y(ω))X(ω), and its operator norm is at most M.

02Lift the genuine Taylor estimate §

Mathematical statement and proof

For actual X,Y, expand the representatives of gLp(X)−gLp(Y)−H(Y)(X−Y). Almost everywhere this is the ordinary pointwise Taylor remainder of g. The deterministic Hessian remainder bound is M₂‖X−Y‖²/2. If the displacement has fourth norm at most C, the quadratic-to-L² estimate gives a remainder norm at most M₂C²/2. Every variable stays on the same probability space.

03Obtain the exact dimension factor from stationary velocities §

Mathematical statement and proof

The kinematic equation and actual Gaussian velocity marginals give C=t(p²+2p)^(1/4). Squaring this radius yields t²√(p²+2p). Consequently the actual L² Taylor forcing is at most M₂t²√(p²+2p)/2. Lean verifies the fractional-power identity using nonnegativity and the real power multiplication rule. No independence of position, Hessian, or velocity is assumed.
