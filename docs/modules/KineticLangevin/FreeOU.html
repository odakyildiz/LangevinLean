The Gaussian marginal and primitive of the free Ornstein–Uhlenbeck velocity

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The comparison uses the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. The results prove Wasserstein convergence of the kinetic diffusion, invariance of its Gibbs measure, KLMC and KLMC2 error estimates for correlated Gaussian increments, their Cholesky representations, and explicit iteration counts for a prescribed error. The corrected proofs are unpublished. The reference table records the correction to the continuous prefactor and the stochastic Hessian-difference terms retained in the second-order comparison. The KLMC2 estimate retains the exact fourth Gaussian moment in every dimension; the coarser displayed simplification assumes p≥2p\ge2p≥2. The stronger inverse-step-square exponential term is also proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L2L^2L2 on finite time intervals. Almost-sure continuity of sample paths is not proved here.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The free Ornstein–Uhlenbeck velocity is the solution of the kinetic velocity equation without force: started at VVV and driven by 2γ dW\sqrt{2\gamma}\,dW2γ​dW, it equals

Ut=ψ0(t)V+Nt,ψ0(t)=e−γt,Nt=2γ∫0te−γ(t−s) dWs.U_t=\psi_0(t)V+N_t,\qquad\psi_0(t)=e^{-\gamma t},\qquad N_t=\sqrt{2\gamma}\int_0^te^{-\gamma(t-s)}\,dW_s.Ut​=ψ0​(t)V+Nt​,ψ0​(t)=e−γt,Nt​=2γ​∫0t​e−γ(t−s)dWs​.

It appears in the second-order algorithm, where the Hessian at the initial position multiplies ∫0t∫0sUr dr ds\int_0^t\int_0^sU_r\,dr\,ds∫0t​∫0s​Ur​drds, the part of the displacement that is linear in the initial velocity and in the fresh noise. This section treats UUU as a path in L2(P;E)L^2(\mathbb P;E)L2(P;E) for a given initial velocity VVV and a given continuous innovation path NNN. We prove: its primitive is ∫0tUs ds=ψ1(t)V+∫0tNs ds\int_0^tU_s\,ds=\psi_1(t)V+\int_0^tN_s\,ds∫0t​Us​ds=ψ1​(t)V+∫0t​Ns​ds with ψ1(t)=∫0tψ0\psi_1(t)=\int_0^t\psi_0ψ1​(t)=∫0t​ψ0​; and if V∼N(0,I)V\sim\mathcal N(0,I)V∼N(0,I) and NtN_tNt​ is independent of VVV with law N(0,(1−ψ0(t)2)I)\mathcal N(0,(1-\psi_0(t)^2)I)N(0,(1−ψ0​(t)2)I), then Ut∼N(0,I)U_t\sim\mathcal N(0,I)Ut​∼N(0,I). The independence hypothesis is verified later for the Brownian reference process, from the independence of past and future Brownian increments.

01An independent thermal innovation preserves the standard Gaussian law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let EEE be a finite-dimensional real inner product space with its Borel σ\sigmaσ-algebra and standard Gaussian law N=N(0,I)\mathcal N=\mathcal N(0,I)N=N(0,I). Let a∈Ra\in\mathbb Ra∈R with a2≤1a^2\le1a2≤1, and let Nc\mathcal N_cNc​ be the image of N\mathcal NN under z↦czz\mapsto czz↦cz, c=1−a2c=\sqrt{1-a^2}c=1−a2​. Then the image of N⊗Nc\mathcal N\otimes\mathcal N_cN⊗Nc​ under (x,y)↦ax+y(x,y)\mapsto ax+y(x,y)↦ax+y is N\mathcal NN. In terms of random vectors: if Z∼NZ\sim\mathcal NZ∼N and Y∼NcY\sim\mathcal N_cY∼Nc​ are independent, then aZ+Y∼NaZ+Y\sim\mathcal NaZ+Y∼N.

Proof. The product of two image measures is the image of the product measure under the product map: N⊗Nc\mathcal N\otimes\mathcal N_cN⊗Nc​ is the image of N⊗N\mathcal N\otimes\mathcal NN⊗N under (z,z′)↦(z,cz′)(z,z')\mapsto(z,cz')(z,z′)↦(z,cz′). Composing the two maps, the measure in question is the image of N⊗N\mathcal N\otimes\mathcal NN⊗N under (z,z′)↦az+cz′(z,z')\mapsto az+cz'(z,z′)↦az+cz′. Since a2+c2=a2+(1−a2)=1a^2+c^2=a^2+(1-a^2)=1a2+c2=a2+(1−a2)=1, the earlier linear-combination lemma for independent standard Gaussians applies: for independent Z,Z′∼NZ,Z'\sim\mathcal NZ,Z′∼N and a2+c2=1a^2+c^2=1a2+c2=1, aZ+cZ′∼NaZ+cZ'\sim\mathcal NaZ+cZ′∼N. (Its proof compares characteristic functions: E ei⟨ξ,aZ+cZ′⟩=e−a2∥ξ∥2/2e−c2∥ξ∥2/2=e−∥ξ∥2/2\mathbb E\,e^{i\langle\xi,aZ+cZ'\rangle}=e^{-a^2\|\xi\|^2/2}e^{-c^2\|\xi\|^2/2}=e^{-\|\xi\|^2/2}Eei⟨ξ,aZ+cZ′⟩=e−a2∥ξ∥2/2e−c2∥ξ∥2/2=e−∥ξ∥2/2, and a law on EEE is determined by its characteristic function.) □\square□

The conclusion is an identity of probability measures, not a statement about covariances only.

02The free Ornstein–Uhlenbeck path and its primitive §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. Let (Ω,P)(\Omega,\mathbb P)(Ω,P) be a measure space, EEE a real normed space, γ∈R\gamma\in\mathbb Rγ∈R, V∈L2(P;E)V\in L^2(\mathbb P;E)V∈L2(P;E) and N:R→L2(P;E)N:\mathbb R\to L^2(\mathbb P;E)N:R→L2(P;E). The free Ornstein–Uhlenbeck velocity is the path

Ut=ψ0(t) V+Nt∈L2(P;E),ψ0(t)=e−γt.U_t=\psi_0(t)\,V+N_t\in L^2(\mathbb P;E),\qquad\psi_0(t)=e^{-\gamma t}.Ut​=ψ0​(t)V+Nt​∈L2(P;E),ψ0​(t)=e−γt.

Claim. (a) If NNN is continuous, then t↦Utt\mapsto U_tt↦Ut​ is continuous. (b) If moreover EEE is complete, then for every t∈Rt\in\mathbb Rt∈R,

∫0tUs ds=ψ1(t) V+∫0tNs ds,ψ1(t)=∫0tψ0(s) ds,\int_0^tU_s\,ds=\psi_1(t)\,V+\int_0^tN_s\,ds,\qquad\psi_1(t)=\int_0^t\psi_0(s)\,ds,∫0t​Us​ds=ψ1​(t)V+∫0t​Ns​ds,ψ1​(t)=∫0t​ψ0​(s)ds,

with Bochner integrals in L2(P;E)L^2(\mathbb P;E)L2(P;E) (oriented integrals when t<0t<0t<0).

Proof. (a) t↦ψ0(t)t\mapsto\psi_0(t)t↦ψ0​(t) is continuous, scalar multiplication R×L2→L2\mathbb R\times L^2\to L^2R×L2→L2 is continuous, and the sum of continuous paths is continuous.

(b) Both s↦ψ0(s)Vs\mapsto\psi_0(s)Vs↦ψ0​(s)V and s↦Nss\mapsto N_ss↦Ns​ are continuous, hence integrable on the interval between 000 and ttt. By linearity of the integral, ∫0tUs ds=∫0tψ0(s)V ds+∫0tNs ds\int_0^tU_s\,ds=\int_0^t\psi_0(s)V\,ds+\int_0^tN_s\,ds∫0t​Us​ds=∫0t​ψ0​(s)Vds+∫0t​Ns​ds. For a fixed vector VVV, ∫0tψ0(s)V ds=(∫0tψ0(s) ds)V=ψ1(t)V\int_0^t\psi_0(s)V\,ds=\bigl(\int_0^t\psi_0(s)\,ds\bigr)V=\psi_1(t)V∫0t​ψ0​(s)Vds=(∫0t​ψ0​(s)ds)V=ψ1​(t)V, which is the definition of ψ1\psi_1ψ1​. □\square□

For the Brownian reference process, Nt=2γ∫0te−γ(t−s)dWsN_t=\sqrt{2\gamma}\int_0^te^{-\gamma(t-s)}dW_sNt​=2γ​∫0t​e−γ(t−s)dWs​ and the second term is the position innovation 2γ∫0tψ1(t−s) dWs\sqrt{2\gamma}\int_0^t\psi_1(t-s)\,dW_s2γ​∫0t​ψ1​(t−s)dWs​. The initial-velocity part and the noise part of the displacement are thus integrated on the same Brownian interval.

03The Gaussian marginal of the free velocity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let EEE be a finite-dimensional real inner product space, (Ω,P)(\Omega,\mathbb P)(Ω,P) a measure space, γ≥0\gamma\ge0γ≥0, t≥0t\ge0t≥0, V∈L2(P;E)V\in L^2(\mathbb P;E)V∈L2(P;E) and N:R→L2(P;E)N:\mathbb R\to L^2(\mathbb P;E)N:R→L2(P;E). Suppose the pair (V,Nt)(V,N_t)(V,Nt​) has the law

N(0,I)⊗(image of N(0,I) under z↦1−ψ0(t)2 z),\mathcal N(0,I)\otimes\bigl(\text{image of }\mathcal N(0,I)\text{ under }z\mapsto\sqrt{1-\psi_0(t)^2}\,z\bigr),N(0,I)⊗(image of N(0,I) under z↦1−ψ0​(t)2​z),

that is, V∼N(0,I)V\sim\mathcal N(0,I)V∼N(0,I), Nt∼N(0,(1−ψ0(t)2)I)N_t\sim\mathcal N(0,(1-\psi_0(t)^2)I)Nt​∼N(0,(1−ψ0​(t)2)I), and VVV, NtN_tNt​ are independent. Then Ut=ψ0(t)V+NtU_t=\psi_0(t)V+N_tUt​=ψ0​(t)V+Nt​ has law N(0,I)\mathcal N(0,I)N(0,I).

Proof. Since γ≥0\gamma\ge0γ≥0 and t≥0t\ge0t≥0, 0≤ψ0(t)=e−γt≤10\le\psi_0(t)=e^{-\gamma t}\le10≤ψ0​(t)=e−γt≤1, so ψ0(t)2≤1\psi_0(t)^2\le1ψ0​(t)2≤1. By the first step with a=ψ0(t)a=\psi_0(t)a=ψ0​(t), the image of the pair law under (x,y)↦ψ0(t)x+y(x,y)\mapsto\psi_0(t)x+y(x,y)↦ψ0​(t)x+y is N(0,I)\mathcal N(0,I)N(0,I). The law of a measurable function of the pair (V,Nt)(V,N_t)(V,Nt​) is the image of the pair law under that function, so ψ0(t)V+Nt\psi_0(t)V+N_tψ0​(t)V+Nt​, computed pointwise, has law N(0,I)\mathcal N(0,I)N(0,I). Finally, the L2L^2L2 class UtU_tUt​ is represented almost everywhere by ω↦ψ0(t)V(ω)+Nt(ω)\omega\mapsto\psi_0(t)V(\omega)+N_t(\omega)ω↦ψ0​(t)V(ω)+Nt​(ω), and almost everywhere equal random vectors have the same law. □\square□

The hypothesis involves only the joint law of (V,Nt)(V,N_t)(V,Nt​). In the second-order comparison, VVV is the stationary velocity at the start of a step and NtN_tNt​ is built from the Brownian increments after that time; the required independence follows from the independence of past and future Brownian increments, proved later. No independence between UtU_tUt​ and the position discrepancy of the coupled algorithm is needed.
