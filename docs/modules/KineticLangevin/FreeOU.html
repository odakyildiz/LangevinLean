The free OU velocity has its actual stationary Gaussian marginal

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The homogeneous OU velocity retains the actual fresh filtered Brownian innovation. Its Gaussian marginal is derived from the actual joint initial-velocity/innovation law, and its continuity and primitive are identities in the actual L² space. The later canonical source theorem proves the needed past/future joint law.

01An independent thermal innovation restores the exact standard Gaussian law §

Mathematical statement and proof

If the initial velocity is standard Gaussian and the independent additive innovation has law 1−a2 N(0,I)\sqrt{1-a^2}\,\mathcal N(0,I)1−a2​N(0,I), with a2≤1a^2\le1a2≤1, their sum aV+NaV+NaV+N is exactly standard Gaussian. Lean first rewrites the true product innovation law as the pushforward of two actual independent standard Gaussian measures. It composes the addition map with that product pushforward, then applies the previously proved Gaussian linear-combination theorem using a2+(1−a2)2=1a^2+(\sqrt{1-a^2})^2=1a2+(1−a2​)2=1. The conclusion is an exact measure identity, rather than a covariance assumption.

02Retain the actual free OU path and its actual position primitive §

Mathematical statement and proof

For an initial L² velocity VVV and the actual scaled filtered innovation path NtN_tNt​, define Ut=ψ0(γ,t)V+NtU_t=\psi_0(\gamma,t)V+N_tUt​=ψ0​(γ,t)V+Nt​. This is the precise free OU velocity used to combine the initial-velocity and fresh-noise Hessian correction terms. Its L² continuity follows from continuity of ψ0\psi_0ψ0​ and of the actual innovation path. Its exact primitive is ∫0tUs ds=ψ1(γ,t)V+∫0tNs ds.\int_0^tU_s\,ds=\psi_1(\gamma,t)V+\int_0^tN_s\,ds.∫0t​Us​ds=ψ1​(γ,t)V+∫0t​Ns​ds. 03Derive the Gaussian marginal from the actual initial/fresh-noise product law §

Mathematical statement and proof

For γ,t≥0\gamma,t\ge0γ,t≥0, the damping lies between zero and one, so ψ0(γ,t)2≤1\psi_0(\gamma,t)^2\le1ψ0​(γ,t)2≤1. This premise states the actual initial/fresh-noise probability identity; canonical past/future independence proves it for the diffusion reference. Lean applies the proved Gaussian addition identity to this actual pair law. It then rewrites the almost-everywhere representatives of the L² scalar multiplication and sum, obtaining Law⁡(Ut)=N(0,I)\operatorname{Law}(U_t)=\mathcal N(0,I)Law(Ut​)=N(0,I) for the actual L² element. The result can be used in Gaussian truncation even when the initial position and velocity are dependent, since no independence from that position appears in the cutoff theorem.
