One actual KLMC2 output law for every initial coupling

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The stochastic analysis may choose any initial transport coupling. The implemented sampler has one fixed law, defined independently of this choice. This module proves the two constructions coincide exactly, including the random Hessian and all four source noises. It supplies the law identity needed to pass a uniform coupling estimate to the true Wasserstein infimum.

01Lift the actual four-noise source §

Mathematical statement and proof

The initial coupling and its independent source extension are probability measures. Each square-integrable original noise coordinate is composed with the source projection and represented in L². Intersecting the four representative identities proves that these lifted variables retain precisely the original four-coordinate noise value.

02Define the fixed-source actual sampler law §

Mathematical statement and proof

The pointwise original KLMC2 recursion starts at the common source velocity and the independent initial position. Its terminal position defines the output map and its product-source pushforward defines the actual law. The force and Hessian are continuous, so the complete four-noise step is measurable. Induction over the original recursion proves the output map is almost everywhere measurable from the square-integrable source data.

03Identify the L² analysis with that single law §

Mathematical statement and proof

The pointwise correspondence theorem for the genuine random-Hessian L² chain identifies every iterate with the original vector recursion. The initial velocity, initial coupling coordinate and all countably many four-noise values have exactly their source representatives. Substituting these identities proves the L² output equals the original fixed-source output map composed with the initial/source projection. That projection has exactly the independent initial-position/source product law. Pushforward composition therefore proves the actual output law is independent of the chosen transport coupling. Its measurability also confirms it is a probability measure.
