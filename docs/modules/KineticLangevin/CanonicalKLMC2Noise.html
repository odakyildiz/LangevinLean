The actual four-kernel correlated Gaussian KLMC2 noise

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The second-order method uses four responses of the same Brownian interval: the two exponential-integrator kernels and the two Hessian correction kernels. Their correlations are essential. This module constructs those four responses simultaneously in every spatial coordinate, proves their exact Gram Gaussian law and iid time stream, and lifts the same random variables to arbitrary initial coupling extensions.

Statement and proof

01Pack the exact scalar covariance into spatial vectors §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Euclidean space uses an arbitrary finite spatial coordinate set. Packing transposes the array of scalar four-dimensional blocks into four full spatial vectors. Every coordinate projection and Euclidean packing map is measurable. Pushing the product of the actual four-dimensional Gram Gaussians through this pack map defines the exact vector innovation law, which is a probability measure.

02Construct the actual Brownian innovation stream §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply all four deterministic kernels to the same Brownian interval and pack all independent spatial copies. Brownian intervals have disjoint supports, so these full correlated vectors are independent across time. At each step their law is the packed product of the exact scalar Gram Gaussian laws. The independent-family product-law theorem therefore proves the complete implemented stream has the actual iid Gaussian distribution.

03Identify the L² variables with the original sampler noise §

Mathematical statement and proof

The four L² innovations are directly the vector Brownian convolutions, so no separate Gaussian realization is substituted. Their representative theorem identifies every coordinate with the corresponding implemented packed response on the same source. Finite second moments follow from the L² construction and this exact almost-everywhere identity.

04Keep the actual noise on every initial coupling extension §

Mathematical statement and proof

Pull the same Brownian convolutions onto the initial-position-coupling/Gaussian-velocity/Brownian product. The Brownian source projection preserves measure. The resulting representatives are exactly the original innovation values at that coordinate. A countable almost-everywhere intersection identifies the entire lifted stream with the original stream, and source projection preserves its exact iid law.
