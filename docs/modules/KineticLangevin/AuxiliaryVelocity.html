The exact auxiliary velocity estimate in the first-order proof

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The source velocity lemma is exposed as an explicit L² theorem, derived from the synchronous kinetic integral equations and the Gaussian reference law.

The auxiliary exact velocity is bounded using the stationary Gaussian reference and full transformed contraction. This keeps its local velocity radius tied to the current discrepancy rather than repeatedly charging the initial error.

Mathematical statement and proof

Let A,RA,RA,R be the two continuous kinetic solutions with the same forcing, and put e0=∥P−1(A(0)−R(0))∥L2e_0=\|P^{-1}(A(0)-R(0))\|_{L^2}e0​=∥P−1(A(0)−R(0))∥L2​. The synchronous contraction gives ∥P−1(A(t)−R(t))∥L2≤e−mt/γe0≤e0\|P^{-1}(A(t)-R(t))\|_{L^2}\le e^{-mt/\gamma}e_0\le e_0∥P−1(A(t)−R(t))∥L2​≤e−mt/γe0​≤e0​ when m,t≥0m,t\ge0m,t≥0. The velocity projection has norm at most the transformed phase norm. The standard Gaussian law of the reference velocity gives ∥RV(t)∥L2=p\|R_V(t)\|_{L^2}=\sqrt p∥RV​(t)∥L2​=p​. The argument applies the triangle inequality to AV(t)=(AV(t)−RV(t))+RV(t)A_V(t)=(A_V(t)-R_V(t))+R_V(t)AV​(t)=(AV​(t)−RV​(t))+RV​(t), substitutes these proved estimates, and obtains ∥AV(t)∥L2≤p+e0\|A_V(t)\|_{L^2}\le\sqrt p+e_0∥AV​(t)∥L2​≤p​+e0​. This is the source auxiliary-velocity lemma, valid without an additional small-step restriction.

The auxiliary and reference exact paths receive the same input, so their full transformed discrepancy contracts from the step’s starting error. Velocity projection has coefficient one in this geometry. Adding the stationary reference velocity radius therefore bounds the auxiliary velocity uniformly throughout the local interval.
