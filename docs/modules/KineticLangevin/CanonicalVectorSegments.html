05Adjoining an independent Gaussian initial velocity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. The source of the KLMC sampler in dimension ppp is the probability space S=Rp×Ω1pS=\mathbb R^p\times\Omega_1^{p}S=Rp×Ω1p​ with Q=N(0,Ip)⊗PQ=\mathcal N(0,I_p)\otimes\mathbb PQ=N(0,Ip​)⊗P, where N(0,Ip)\mathcal N(0,I_p)N(0,Ip​) is the standard Gaussian measure on Rp\mathbb R^pRp. A point of SSS is written (u,ω)(u,\omega)(u,ω); the initial velocity is V(u,ω)=uV(u,\omega)=uV(u,ω)=u and the innovations are Wn(u,ω)=Ξn(ω)W_n(u,\omega)=\Xi_n(\omega)Wn​(u,ω)=Ξn​(ω).

Claim.

  1. QQQ is a probability measure, VVV has law N(0,Ip)\mathcal N(0,I_p)N(0,Ip​) under QQQ, and VVV is square integrable.
  2. Each WnW_nWn​ has the law of the paper's innovation (as in the previous step), the WnW_nWn​ are mutually independent, and both components of each WnW_nWn​ are square integrable under QQQ.
  3. VVV is independent of the whole sequence (Wn)n∈N(W_n)_{n\in\mathbb N}(Wn​)n∈N​.

Proof. (1) A product of probability measures is a probability measure. The first projection maps QQQ to N(0,Ip)\mathcal N(0,I_p)N(0,Ip​) because the second factor has mass one; so VVV has law N(0,Ip)\mathcal N(0,I_p)N(0,Ip​), and ∫∥V∥2 dQ=∫∥u∥2 N(0,Ip)(du)=p<∞\int\|V\|^2\,dQ=\int\|u\|^2\,\mathcal N(0,I_p)(du)=p<\infty∫∥V∥2dQ=∫∥u∥2N(0,Ip​)(du)=p<∞ (Gaussian measures have finite moments of all orders).

(2) The second projection (u,ω)↦ω(u,\omega)\mapsto\omega(u,ω)↦ω maps QQQ to P\mathbb PP and is measurable. Since Wn=Ξn∘pr2W_n=\Xi_n\circ\mathrm{pr}_2Wn​=Ξn​∘pr2​, the law of WnW_nWn​ under QQQ equals the law of Ξn\Xi_nΞn​ under P\mathbb PP; the mutual independence transfers by the first step; and square integrability transfers because composition with a measure-preserving map preserves L2L^2L2 norms.

(3) Under a product measure μ⊗ν\mu\otimes\nuμ⊗ν, a measurable function of the first coordinate and a measurable function of the second coordinate are independent, because (μ⊗ν)((A×Ω1p)∩(Rp×C))=(μ⊗ν)(A×C)=μ(A) ν(C)(\mu\otimes\nu)\bigl((A\times\Omega_1^p)\cap(\mathbb R^p\times C)\bigr)=(\mu\otimes\nu)(A\times C)=\mu(A)\,\nu(C)(μ⊗ν)((A×Ω1p​)∩(Rp×C))=(μ⊗ν)(A×C)=μ(A)ν(C) for measurable A⊆RpA\subseteq\mathbb R^pA⊆Rp and C⊆Ω1pC\subseteq\Omega_1^pC⊆Ω1p​. Here VVV depends only on uuu, and the sequence (Wn)n(W_n)_n(Wn​)n​, viewed as one random element of a countable product space, depends only on ω\omegaω and is measurable because each of its coordinates is. □\square□

Independence of VVV from the entire innovation sequence, rather than from each WnW_nWn​ separately, is what is needed for the KLMC chain to be a Markov chain started from the product law N(0,Ip)⊗ν0\mathcal N(0,I_p)\otimes\nu_0N(0,Ip​)⊗ν0​ of the paper.
