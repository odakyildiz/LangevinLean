Spectral cancellation and pathwise exponential decay

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

This module proves a deterministic theorem for a kinetic difference equation v′=−γv−Htxv'=-\gamma v-H_txv′=−γv−Ht​x, x′=vx'=vx′=v. The continuous linear operator HtH_tHt​ is allowed to vary with time but must be self-adjoint and satisfy quadratic-form bounds mI⪯Ht⪯MImI\preceq H_t\preceq MImI⪯Ht​⪯MI. The theorem derives energy decay from this differential equation. Constructing stochastic solutions whose difference has these properties and taking an infimum over probability couplings are separate layers.

01Write the derivative and prove exact spectral cancellation §

Mathematical statement and proof

The transformed derivatives are y′=−av−Hxy'=-av-Hxy′=−av−Hx, z′=(γ−a)v+Hxz'=(\gamma-a)v+Hxz′=(γ−a)v+Hx. Thus E′=2⟨y,y′⟩+2⟨z,z′⟩\mathcal E'=2\langle y,y'\rangle+2\langle z,z'\rangleE′=2⟨y,y′⟩+2⟨z,z′⟩, which energyDerivative records. Multiplying by d=γ−2ad=\gamma-2ad=γ−2a gives

The proof expands linear-map applications, scalar multiplication and inner products. Self-adjointness rewrites ⟨v,Hx⟩\langle v,Hx\rangle⟨v,Hx⟩ as ⟨x,Hv⟩\langle x,Hv\rangle⟨x,Hv⟩, so cross terms cancel. ring verifies the remaining scalar polynomial identity. 07Include the initial time through right derivatives §

Mathematical statement and proof

Finite-horizon integral solutions naturally provide a derivative within [t,∞)[t,\infty)[t,∞), including at t=0t=0t=0. The coordinate differentiation proof works unchanged with HasDerivWithinAt: add the two right derivatives, normalize their vector coefficients, apply the squared-norm derivative rule, and sum. The right-slope Grönwall theorem therefore gives the same exponential energy bound under the spectral hypotheses, without imposing a differentiable extension of the stochastic paths to negative time.
