Gaussian invariance of the velocity thermostat

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The comparison uses the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. The results prove Wasserstein convergence of the kinetic diffusion, invariance of its Gibbs measure, KLMC and KLMC2 error estimates for correlated Gaussian increments, their Cholesky representations, and explicit iteration counts for a prescribed error. The corrected proofs are unpublished. The reference table records the correction to the continuous prefactor and the stochastic Hessian-difference terms retained in the second-order comparison. The KLMC2 estimate retains the exact fourth Gaussian moment in every dimension; the coarser displayed simplification assumes p≥2. The stronger inverse-step-square exponential term is also proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite time intervals. Almost-sure continuity of sample paths is not proved here.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The O substep removes velocity variance through damping and restores exactly that variance through independent Gaussian noise. This section proves an equality of probability measures, not only a matching second moment. Invariance of this substep is one component of kinetic stationarity; the Hamiltonian evolution and the full diffusion require separate arguments.

The invariant Gaussian temperature is determined by balancing the variance lost through damping against the variance supplied by fresh independent noise. This exact identity is one of the invariant building blocks for kinetic stationarity.

01Independent Gaussian linear combinations §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let G,G′G,G\primeG,G′ be independent standard Gaussian vectors in a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space, and suppose a2+b2=1a^2+b^2=1a2+b2=1. For a continuous linear functional LLL, the characteristic function of aG+bG′aG+bG\primeaG+bG′ is

exp⁡(−a2∥L∥2/2)exp⁡(−b2∥L∥2/2)=exp⁡(−∥L∥2/2).\exp(-a^2\|L\|^2/2)\exp(-b^2\|L\|^2/2)=\exp(-\|L\|^2/2).exp(−a2∥L∥2/2)exp(−b2∥L∥2/2)=exp(−∥L∥2/2).

Independence is represented by the product of two standard Gaussian measures. The argument constructs the continuous linear map A(v,w)=av+bwA(v,w)=av+bwA(v,w)=av+bw, uses the characteristic function of a linear pushforward to pull the test functional back through AAA, and uses factorization of characteristic functions on independent products to factor its characteristic function. The restrictions of L∘AL\circ AL∘A to the two factors are aLaLaL and bLbLbL, as follows by evaluation at arbitrary vectors in each factor. The norm of a scalar multiple supplies the squared coefficients. The relation a2+b2=1a^2+b^2=1a2+b2=1 then simplifies the exponent, and uniqueness of characteristic functions proves the distributional identity.

For independent centered Gaussian vectors VVV and GGG, the characteristic function of aV+bGaV+bGaV+bG factors into the product of the two Gaussian characteristic functions. Its covariance is (a2+b2)I(a^2+b^2)I(a2+b2)I. Independence is essential: an unspecified cross covariance would contribute an additional term.

02Apply the fluctuation-dissipation balance §

Mathematical statement and proof

For nonnegative friction and time step, put δ=e−γh/2\delta=e^{-\gamma h/2}δ=e−γh/2 and s=1−δ2s=\sqrt{1-\delta^2}s=1−δ2​. The variance under the square root is nonnegative, so s2=1−δ2s^2=1-\delta^2s2=1−δ2. Hence δ2+s2=1\delta^2+s^2=1δ2+s2=1, and the preceding theorem gives δG+sG′∼N(0,I)\delta G+sG\prime\sim\mathcal N(0,I)δG+sG′∼N(0,I). The argument applies the general linear-combination theorem, rewrites the square root squared using its proved nonnegativity, unfolds the variance, and finishes with a polynomial identity. This is precisely the standard-temperature O half-step used in OBABO.

Take a=e−γta=e^{-\gamma t}a=e−γt and b=1−e−2γtb=\sqrt{1-e^{-2\gamma t}}b=1−e−2γt​. Their squared sum is one, so a standard Gaussian velocity remains standard Gaussian after thermostat. Position is unchanged, hence the full Gaussian-times-position target is preserved whenever the refresh noise is independent.
