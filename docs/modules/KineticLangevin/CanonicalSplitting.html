Splitting convergence on the actual constructed vector Brownian source

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The audit follows the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. Actual canonical Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both correlated-Gaussian KLMC/KLMC2 Wasserstein bounds, literal Cholesky sampling, and explicit accuracy guarantees are proved. The corrected proofs checked here are unpublished: the audit records the continuous prefactor correction, the stationary local reference, and the retained stochastic Hessian-difference terms. KLMC2 keeps the exact fourth-moment factor in every dimension and states p≥2 for the printed coarser simplification; its sharper inverse-step-square tail is proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L² on finite horizons; no separate almost-surely continuous sample-path modification theorem is claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

The deterministic input in the splitting theorem is now identified with the actual canonical vector Brownian motion in L². Its filtered increments are exactly the constructed Gaussian segment convolutions, including the fluctuation-dissipation scaling. Consequently the innovation-driven numerical chain and the continuous-input chain coincide on the same source, and the actual L² chain converges to its kinetic integral solution.

01Keep scaling and source lifts exact §

Mathematical statement and proof

The OU input construction is linear in the additive input. Multiplying BBB by a real coefficient ccc multiplies both its matching increment and its exponential convolution by ccc. More generally, a continuous linear map LLL commutes with this construction: it commutes with subtraction, scalar multiplication, and the Bochner interval integral of the continuous integrand. Lean proves these exact identities by integral linearity and continuous-linear integral commutation. The latter applies, in particular, to measure-preserving pullbacks that extend the Brownian source by an independent initial state.

02Construct the actual continuous L² Brownian input §

Mathematical statement and proof

The input is the already constructed vector Brownian increment from time zero, evaluated at t+=max⁡{t,0}t_+=\max\{t,0\}t+​=max{t,0}. This supplies a globally defined real-time function, anchored at zero. The proved continuity of vector Brownian increments in L² and continuity of t↦t+t\mapsto t_+t↦t+​ prove continuity of this input. At zero the underlying increment is the difference of a Brownian value with itself, so it is exactly zero. Lean unfolds the actual source definitions to check this anchor.

03Identify actual input increments and OU segment responses §

Mathematical statement and proof

The difference between the anchored values at a+ta+ta+t and aaa cancels their common time-zero value, leaving the actual canonical Brownian increment on (a,a+t](a,a+t](a,a+t]. The independently proved segment convolution identity gives Za,t=ΔBa,t−γ∫0te−γ(t−s)ΔBa,s ds.Z_{a,t}=\Delta B_{a,t}-\gamma\int_0^t e^{-\gamma(t-s)}\Delta B_{a,s}\,ds.Za,t​=ΔBa,t​−γ∫0t​e−γ(t−s)ΔBa,s​ds. This is exactly the deterministic ouInputIncrement used in the splitting theorem. Lean rewrites the endpoint increment, compares the actual interval integrands, and uses nonnegativity of the integration time to remove its real-to-nonnegative-time clamp. Scaling by any ccc, including 2γ\sqrt{2\gamma}2γ​, follows from the checked linearity identity. The equality is of actual L² variables, stronger than merely matching marginal Gaussian laws.

04Identify the entire canonical innovation chain §

Mathematical statement and proof

On a mesh of nonnegative size hhh, the continuous-input chain uses the increment Nkh,hN_{kh,h}Nkh,h​. The preceding actual identity replaces this by cZkh,hcZ_{kh,h}cZkh,h​ for every grid index. The explicit-innovation recursion therefore agrees exactly with the continuous-input recursion. Lean first uses the proved equality between the two chain representations and then substitutes the actual OU increment identity at each nonnegative grid time.

05Conclude actual L² convergence on the canonical source §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply the generic Banach splitting theorem to the continuous canonical input cBcBcB, its proved zero initial value, and the actual continuous kinetic integral solution YYY driven by that same input. Rewrite the resulting continuous-input chain by the proved canonical innovation-chain identity. This yields convergence of the explicit chain with actual Gaussian convolution innovations on the mesh T/(n+1)T/(n+1)T/(n+1) to YTY_TYT​. Lean checks the nonnegative-time casts in the mesh and grid endpoints. The integral-solution premise is the actual equation constructed elsewhere by Picard existence; no stationarity or numerical convergence is supplied as a contract.
