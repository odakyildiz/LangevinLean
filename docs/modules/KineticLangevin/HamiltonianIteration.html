Accumulation of bounded-test errors and invariance of a limiting law

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The comparison uses the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. The results prove Wasserstein convergence of the kinetic diffusion, invariance of its Gibbs measure, KLMC and KLMC2 error estimates for correlated Gaussian increments, their Cholesky representations, and explicit iteration counts for a prescribed error. The corrected proofs are unpublished. The reference table records the correction to the continuous prefactor and the stochastic Hessian-difference terms retained in the second-order comparison. The KLMC2 estimate retains the exact fourth Gaussian moment in every dimension; the coarser displayed simplification assumes p≥2p\ge2p≥2. The stronger inverse-step-square exponential term is also proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L2L^2L2 on finite time intervals. Almost-sure continuity of sample paths is not proved here.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

Corrected invariance can be used entirely through bounded tests. Composing a test with more deterministic proposal steps preserves its bound. Each telescoping increment is therefore controlled under the same fixed measure, giving a linear accumulation of rejection mass. Pointwise convergence and vanishing accumulated error then identify the limiting law exactly by dual characteristic functions.

Telescoping bounded-test errors produces linear accumulation over a fixed horizon. Once the numerical maps converge, characteristic-function uniqueness identifies the invariant law of the limiting Hamiltonian flow.

01Telescope tests composed with deterministic iterates §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let Ak=∫F(Vkz) dπA_k=\int F(V^kz)\,d\piAk​=∫F(Vkz)dπ. Apply the one-step weak estimate to G=F∘VkG=F\circ V^kG=F∘Vk. Measurability of iterates proves measurability of GGG, and the same bound ∥G∥≤C\|G\|\le C∥G∥≤C holds. Thus ∥Ak+1−Ak∥≤2C∫(1−a) dπ\|A_{k+1}-A_k\|\le2C\int(1-a)\,d\pi∥Ak+1​−Ak​∥≤2C∫(1−a)dπ. The triangle inequality and induction give ∥An−A0∥≤2Cn∫(1−a) dπ.\|A_n-A_0\|\le2Cn\int(1-a)\,d\pi.∥An​−A0​∥≤2Cn∫(1−a)dπ. The argument explicitly rewrites the successor iterate as Vk∘VV^k\circ VVk∘V, applies the one-step result, and uses that the successor step count is one more than the preceding count. Every increment is integrated against the fixed measure π\piπ; no law of an unadjusted intermediate chain is assumed invariant.

Write the difference between the nnn-step test integral and its invariant value as a sum of nnn one-step differences. Composition with deterministic iterates preserves the test’s uniform bound. Each term is therefore bounded by the same local rejection estimate, giving linear rather than exponential accumulation.

02The limiting pushforward through characteristic tests §

Mathematical statement and proof

Suppose measurable maps YnY_nYn​ converge pointwise to a measurable map YYY, and the bounded-test error is eventually at most a real sequence δn→0\delta_n\to0δn​→0. For every continuous real linear functional LLL, use F(z)=exp⁡(iLz)F(z)=\exp(iLz)F(z)=exp(iLz), whose norm is one. Dominated convergence under the finite measure gives ∫F(Ynz) dπ→∫F(Yz) dπ\int F(Y_nz)\,d\pi\to\int F(Yz)\,d\pi∫F(Yn​z)dπ→∫F(Yz)dπ. The error bound gives convergence of the same sequence to ∫F dπ\int F\,d\pi∫Fdπ. Uniqueness of limits equates the dual characteristic functions of Y#πY_\#\piY#​π and π\piπ; the uniqueness theorem for finite measures gives Y#π=πY_\#\pi=\piY#​π=π. The argument uses dual characteristic functions, so the result applies to Banach phase space with its usual product norm and does not silently require that norm to arise from an inner product.

Characteristic functions determine probability laws in finite-dimensional Euclidean space. If the numerical maps converge to the Hamiltonian flow and the accumulated test error vanishes, bounded convergence identifies the limiting pushforward with the target. This gives invariance of the flow.
