The exponential integrator and its Hessian correction

References and publication status for this development

Published source; corrected checked proofs

The comparison uses the supplied mainDR_dec_18.tex manuscript. The results prove Wasserstein convergence of the kinetic diffusion, invariance of its Gibbs measure, KLMC and KLMC2 error estimates for correlated Gaussian increments, their Cholesky representations, and explicit iteration counts for a prescribed error. The corrected proofs are unpublished. The reference table records the correction to the continuous prefactor and the stochastic Hessian-difference terms retained in the second-order comparison. The KLMC2 estimate retains the exact fourth Gaussian moment in every dimension; the coarser displayed simplification assumes p≥2p\ge2p≥2. The stronger inverse-step-square exponential term is also proved. Continuous solutions are continuous in L2L^2L2 on finite time intervals. Almost-sure continuity of sample paths is not proved here.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv

Φh((v,x),(ξ,ξ′))=(ψ0(h)v−ψ1(h)F(x)+ξ,  x+ψ1(h)v−ψ2(h)F(x)+ξ′),\Phi_h\bigl((v,x),(\xi,\xi')\bigr)=\bigl(\psi_0(h)v-\psi_1(h)F(x)+\xi,\;x+\psi_1(h)v-\psi_2(h)F(x)+\xi'\bigr),Φh​((v,x),(ξ,ξ′))=(ψ0​(h)v−ψ1​(h)F(x)+ξ,x+ψ1​(h)v−ψ2​(h)F(x)+ξ′),

06The numerical chains for prescribed innovations §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. Given an initial phase point y0y_0y0​ and innovations (ηk)k∈N(\eta_k)_{k\in\mathbb N}(ηk​)k∈N​ in X×X\mathcal X\times\mathcal XX×X, the KLMC chain is defined recursively by

y0=the initial point,yk+1=KLMC step(yk,ηk),k∈N.y_0=\text{the initial point},\qquad y_{k+1}=\text{KLMC step}(y_k,\eta_k),\qquad k\in\mathbb N .y0​=the initial point,yk+1​=KLMC step(yk​,ηk​),k∈N.

The second-order chain is defined in the same way from the second-order step and innovations (ξk(1),…,ξk(4))k∈N(\xi_k^{(1)},\dots,\xi_k^{(4)})_{k\in\mathbb N}(ξk(1)​,…,ξk(4)​)k∈N​.

Claim. The KLMC chain satisfies y0=y_0=y0​= the initial point and yk+1=KLMC step(yk,ηk)y_{k+1}=\text{KLMC step}(y_k,\eta_k)yk+1​=KLMC step(yk​,ηk​) for every kkk.

Proof. These are the defining equations of the recursion. □\square□

For a fixed innovation sequence the chain is a deterministic sequence. It becomes the KLMC (respectively KLMC2) Markov chain of the paper when the initial point is random and the innovations are independent Gaussian vectors with the prescribed covariance, independent of the initial point.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

klmcChain force γ h initial noise : ℕ → PhaseSpace E and klmc2Chain force hessian γ h initial noise are defined by structural recursion on the step index, with the innovation sequences noise : ℕ → PhaseSpace E and noise : ℕ → Fin 4 → E; note that the step from kkk to k+1k+1k+1 uses noise k. klmcChain_zero and klmcChain_succ restate the two defining equations and are proved by rfl.
