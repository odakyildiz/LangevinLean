Pointwise recursion and the L² algorithm law

The formal convergence proof uses equivalence classes in L2L^2L2. This module proves that their pushforward laws equal those of the displayed coordinate algorithm, including the exact square-root noise coefficients.

01Recover the original pairs from scaled coordinates §

Mathematical statement and proof

For every particle iii, checking parameter and latent coordinates gives Aiz=pair⁡(pθz,N block⁡iz)A_i z=\operatorname{pair}(p_\theta z,\sqrt N\,\operatorname{block}_i z)Ai​z=pair(pθ​z,N​blocki​z). Substitution into the finite sum proves

VN(z)=∑iU(pθz,N block⁡iz).V_N(z)=\sum_iU\bigl(p_\theta z,\sqrt N\,\operatorname{block}_iz\bigr).VN​(z)=i∑​U(pθ​z,N​blocki​z).

This identifies the abstract lifted potential with the sum of the original pair potentials at the physical particles.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • joint_eq_pair: A joint extraction is exactly the original parameter-particle pair after unscaling its particle.
  • potential_eq_pair_sum: The lifted potential is the sum of the original pair potential, with physical particles equal to sqrt N times the scaled particle blocks.

03Induction identifies the L² and pointwise algorithms §

Mathematical statement and proof

At time zero, the representative of the L2L^2L2 chain is the initialization. Suppose its representative equals the pointwise chain almost surely at time kkk. The shared update representative theorem gives the Euler formula almost surely; intersect that full-measure event with the induction event, substitute the previous-state equality, and rewrite 2(γ/N)=2γ/N2(\gamma/N)=2\gamma/N2(γ/N)=2γ/N. This proves almost-sure equality at k+1k+1k+1. Finally, almost-everywhere equal measurable maps have equal pushforward measures, hence

ρk=P∘[ω↦scaledChain⁡(U,γ,Z0(ω),ξ(ω),k)]−1.\rho_k=P\circ\bigl[\omega\mapsto\operatorname{scaledChain}(U,\gamma,Z_0(\omega),\xi(\omega),k)\bigr]^{-1}.ρk​=P∘[ω↦scaledChain(U,γ,Z0​(ω),ξ(ω),k)]−1.

The representative induction uses equality almost everywhere at each fixed finite time. In the successor case the intersection of the update's representative event and the previous induction event still has full measure, so substitution is legitimate. Once the random variables agree almost everywhere, Measure.map_congr gives equality of their laws. This step is what permits results proved for the Hilbert-space recursion to be presented as results for the explicit coordinate algorithm displayed above.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • lpChain_coe: The L² chain represents the explicit IPLA recursion almost everywhere. This includes the exact identity between sqrt (2 * (gamma/N)) and the noise coefficient sqrt (2*gamma/N) in the displayed IPLA update.
  • laws_eq_scaledChain: The laws appearing in the final theorem are exactly the laws of the explicit, noise-driven, pointwise IPLA recursion.
