The pointwise particle recursion and its square-integrable realization

References and publication status for this development

Published source and unpublished extension

Source: Theorem 1, equation (34), and Appendix B of arXiv:2409.07101v2 (27 May 2025), using the ULA estimate of Dalalyan and Karagulyan. The independent parameter- and latent-dimension extension and its proof presented here are unpublished; equal dimensions recover the paper’s displayed bound. The assumption audit explains the joint-potential smoothness condition.

  • Alex Glyn-Davies, Connor Duffin, Ieva Kazlauskaite, Mark Girolami, and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Statistical Finite Elements via Interacting Particle Langevin Dynamics. SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification 13(3), 1200–1227. Audited version: arXiv:2409.07101v2
  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv

The iterates of the interacting particle algorithm were defined in the previous section as square-integrable random vectors, that is, as equivalence classes of measurable maps Ω→Z\Omega\to\mathcal ZΩ→Z modulo equality almost surely. This section relates them to the explicit recursion computed pointwise: given an initial state z0∈Zz_0\in\mathcal Zz0​∈Z and a sequence of innovation vectors ξ0,ξ1,⋯∈Z\xi_0,\xi_1,\dots\in\mathcal Zξ0​,ξ1​,⋯∈Z, put zk+1=Tγ(zk,ξk)z_{k+1}=T_\gamma(z_k,\xi_k)zk+1​=Tγ​(zk​,ξk​), where TγT_\gammaTγ​ is the scaled update of the geometry section. In coordinates this recursion is

θk+1=θk−γN∑i=1N∇θU(θk,xki)+2γN ξkθ,xk+1i=xki−γ∇xU(θk,xki)+2γ ξki,\theta_{k+1}=\theta_k-\frac\gamma N\sum_{i=1}^N\nabla_\theta U(\theta_k,x_k^i)+\sqrt{\frac{2\gamma}N}\,\xi_k^\theta,\qquad x_{k+1}^i=x_k^i-\gamma\nabla_xU(\theta_k,x_k^i)+\sqrt{2\gamma}\,\xi_k^i,θk+1​=θk​−Nγ​i=1∑N​∇θ​U(θk​,xki​)+N2γ​​ξkθ​,xk+1i​=xki​−γ∇x​U(θk​,xki​)+2γ​ξki​,

with xki=Nzk,ix_k^i=\sqrt N z_{k,i}xki​=N​zk,i​. We prove that the lifted potential is the sum of the pair potentials at the physical coordinates, that the pointwise recursion satisfies these two coordinate formulas, and that, evaluated at the initial state and innovations, it agrees almost surely with the square-integrable recursion at every iteration. Consequently the laws ρk\rho_kρk​ in the convergence theorem are the laws of the explicit particle algorithm.

01The lifted potential in physical coordinates §

Mathematical statement and proof

Recall that Pθz=θP_\theta z=\thetaPθ​z=θ is the parameter projection, ziz_izi​ the iii-th scaled latent block and JizJ_izJi​z the iii-th pair extraction.

Claim. For every N∈NN\in\mathbb NN∈N, every particle iii and every z∈Zz\in\mathcal Zz∈Z,

Jiz=(Pθz, N zi)andVN(z)=∑i=1NU(Pθz, N zi).J_iz=\bigl(P_\theta z,\ \sqrt N\,z_i\bigr)\qquad\text{and}\qquad V_N(z)=\sum_{i=1}^NU\bigl(P_\theta z,\ \sqrt N\,z_i\bigr).Ji​z=(Pθ​z, N​zi​)andVN​(z)=i=1∑N​U(Pθ​z, N​zi​).

Proof. Two elements of the pair space are equal when all coordinates agree. A parameter coordinate jjj of JizJ_izJi​z is θj\theta_jθj​, which is coordinate jjj of PθzP_\theta zPθ​z. A latent coordinate jjj of JizJ_izJi​z is Nzi,j\sqrt N z_{i,j}N​zi,j​, which is coordinate jjj of the vector Nzi\sqrt N z_iN​zi​. This proves the first identity. The second follows by inserting the first into each summand of VN(z)=∑iU(Jiz)V_N(z)=\sum_iU(J_iz)VN​(z)=∑i​U(Ji​z). □\square□

With xi=Nzix^i=\sqrt Nz_ixi=N​zi​ the second identity reads VN(z)=∑iU(θ,xi)V_N(z)=\sum_iU(\theta,x^i)VN​(z)=∑i​U(θ,xi): every evaluation of the potential or of its gradient in the scaled recursion is an evaluation of the original joint potential at the common parameter and at a physical particle.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

joint_eq_pair states joint i z = pair (parameterCLM z) (Real.sqrt N • block z i), the first identity, with parameterCLM the projection PθP_\thetaPθ​ and block z i the block ziz_izi​. Its proof compares coordinates: ext a, cases a (parameter or latent index) and simp [joint, pair, block] unfolds the three definitions. potential_eq_pair_sum is the second identity, proved by simp only [potential, joint_eq_pair], i.e. by unfolding potential and rewriting every summand with joint_eq_pair.

02The explicit recursion and its coordinate formulas §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. Let γ∈R\gamma\in\mathbb Rγ∈R, z0∈Zz_0\in\mathcal Zz0​∈Z and let (ξk)k≥0(\xi_k)_{k\ge0}(ξk​)k≥0​ be a sequence in Z\mathcal ZZ. The pointwise recursion is

N zk+1,(i,j)=N zk,(i,j)−γ(∇U(Jizk))(x,j)+2γ  ξk,(i,j).\sqrt N\,z_{k+1,(i,j)}=\sqrt N\,z_{k,(i,j)}-\gamma\bigl(\nabla U(J_iz_k)\bigr)_{(x,j)}+\sqrt{2\gamma}\;\xi_{k,(i,j)} .N​zk+1,(i,j)​=N​zk,(i,j)​−γ(∇U(Ji​zk​))(x,j)​+2γ​ξk,(i,j)​.

Part (a) is the parameter formula of the scaled update, and part (b) is its physical latent formula, both proved in the geometry section, applied with step h=γh=\gammah=γ, state zkz_kzk​ and innovation ξk\xi_kξk​; part (b) is where N>0N>0N>0 is needed. □\square□

In vector notation (a) and (b) are the two displayed formulas of the summary: the parameter moves along the average of the NNN pair forces with noise of variance 2γ/N2\gamma/N2γ/N per coordinate, and each physical particle performs a Langevin step of size γ\gammaγ in its own pair potential at the current parameter. All forces are evaluated at the state zkz_kzk​, and both formulas use the coordinates of the same innovation vector ξk\xi_kξk​.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

scaledChain U γ initial noise is defined by structural recursion on the iteration number: the case 0 returns initial and the case k + 1 returns scaledUpdate U γ (scaledChain U γ initial noise k) (noise k). The two defining equations are recorded as the simplification lemmas scaledChain_zero and scaledChain_succ, both proved by rfl.

scaledChain_parameter is (a); its proof term is scaledUpdate_parameter hU γ _ _ j, the parameter formula with the state and innovation left for Lean to infer. scaledChain_particle is (b) under hN : 0 < N, with proof term scaledUpdate_particle hN hU γ _ _ i j. Both theorems hold by definitional unfolding of scaledChain at k + 1, so no further argument is needed.

03The square-integrable chain represents the pointwise recursion §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let P\mathbb PP be a probability measure on Ω\OmegaΩ, let UUU be differentiable with LLL-Lipschitz gradient for some L≥0L\ge0L≥0, let γ∈R\gamma\in\mathbb Rγ∈R, and let Z0,ξ0,ξ1,…Z_0,\xi_0,\xi_1,\dotsZ0​,ξ0​,ξ1​,… be square-integrable random vectors in Z\mathcal ZZ, each given by a fixed measurable representative. Laws.
