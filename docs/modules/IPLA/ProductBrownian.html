01Brownian source, product pullback and Gaussian innovations §

Mathematical statement and proof

Pulling a Brownian coordinate through the measure-preserving source projection preserves its Brownian law. Define the source Euler coordinates by

ξk,a=Ba((k+1)h)−Ba(kh)h,h>0.\xi_{k,a}=\frac{B_a((k+1)h)-B_a(kh)}{\sqrt h},\qquad h>0.ξk,a​=h​Ba​((k+1)h)−Ba​(kh)​,h>0.

Gaussian increment laws give unit variance, and coordinate independence plus independent disjoint increments give independence in both kkk and aaa. Product independence makes the entire source path independent of initialization. The shared proofs also identify the source noise with the Brownian endpoint noise used in the integral equation, prove square integrability and produce the product Gibbs initial state. The reference-building theorems retain their explicit stationarity assumptions at this intermediate layer; the complete canonical ULA construction proves the required Gibbs law.

The export commands expose the already proved declarations under the IPLA namespace. Their definitions and proofs are unchanged, so the mathematical argument is the one in the imported source modules; the dependency diagram below opens those full proofs. This file itself makes no additional analytic assumption and contains no replacement proof.
