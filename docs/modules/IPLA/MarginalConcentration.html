01The norm of a parameter–latent pair §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. For θ∈Rdθ\theta\in\mathbb R^{d_\theta}θ∈Rdθ​ and x∈Rdxx\in\mathbb R^{d_x}x∈Rdx​, the pair (θ,x)∈J(\theta,x)\in\mathcal J(θ,x)∈J has parameter coordinates θj\theta_jθj​ and latent coordinates xjx_jxj​, and

∥(θ,x)∥2=∥θ∥2+∥x∥2.(1)\|(\theta,x)\|^2=\|\theta\|^2+\|x\|^2 .\qquad(1)∥(θ,x)∥2=∥θ∥2+∥x∥2.(1)

Proof. The coordinate formulas hold by definition of the pair. By (1), a Gaussian factor in the joint norm splits into a parameter factor and a latent factor, and continuity of UUU gives continuity of (θ,x)↦U(θ,x)(\theta,x)\mapsto U(\theta,x)(θ,x)↦U(θ,x) and of its slices.
