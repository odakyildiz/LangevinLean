Scaled particle geometry and exact coordinate updates

Fix a particle count NNN, independent dimensions dθ,dxd_\theta,d_xdθ​,dx​, and a joint potential UUU. Write z=(θ,z1,…,zN)z=(\theta,z_1,\ldots,z_N)z=(θ,z1​,…,zN​), where the physical particles are xi=Nzix_i=\sqrt N z_ixi​=N​zi​. The lifted potential is VN(z)=∑iU(θ,Nzi)V_N(z)=\sum_iU(\theta,\sqrt N z_i)VN​(z)=∑i​U(θ,N​zi​). This module derives its dimension, strong convexity, smoothness and exact Euler coordinates; none is an additional hypothesis.

01The coordinate spaces and their dimensions §

Mathematical statement and proof

The scaled state is the Euclidean space indexed by Fin(dθ)⊔(Fin(N)×Fin(dx))\mathrm{Fin}(d_\theta)\sqcup(\mathrm{Fin}(N)\times\mathrm{Fin}(d_x))Fin(dθ​)⊔(Fin(N)×Fin(dx​)), and a joint pair is indexed by Fin(dθ)⊔Fin(dx)\mathrm{Fin}(d_\theta)\sqcup\mathrm{Fin}(d_x)Fin(dθ​)⊔Fin(dx​). Counting these orthogonal coordinates proves

dim⁡(State)=dθ+Ndx,dim⁡(Joint)=dθ+dx.\dim(\mathrm{State})=d_\theta+Nd_x,\qquad \dim(\mathrm{Joint})=d_\theta+d_x.dim(State)=dθ​+Ndx​,dim(Joint)=dθ​+dx​.

The block map extracts ziz_izi​, while Aiz=joint⁡i(z)=(θ,Nzi)A_i z=\operatorname{joint}_i(z)=(\theta,\sqrt N z_i)Ai​z=jointi​(z)=(θ,N​zi​) restores the physical particle. The two coordinate evaluation identities follow directly from this definition. The definition of VNV_NVN​ then sums the original potential over these pairs.

02Replication preserves a precise quadratic identity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Each AiA_iAi​ is linear: checking a parameter coordinate and a latent coordinate separately proves preservation of addition, scalar multiplication and subtraction. Every parameter coordinate occurs NNN times, while each latent coordinate is multiplied by N\sqrt NN​ once. Therefore

∑i∥Aiz∥2=N∥θ∥2+N∑i∥zi∥2=N∥z∥2.\sum_i\|A_i z\|^2=N\|\theta\|^2+N\sum_i\|z_i\|^2=N\|z\|^2.i∑​∥Ai​z∥2=N∥θ∥2+Ni∑​∥zi​∥2=N∥z∥2.

The proof expands squared Euclidean norms into finite sums and uses (N)2=N(\sqrt N)^2=N(N​)2=N, which is valid also when N=0N=0N=0. This exact identity supplies the constants in both the convexity and smoothness arguments.

03Joint strong convexity lifts to N times the constant §

Mathematical statement and proof

If UUU is mmm-strongly convex, then for a,b≥0a,b\ge0a,b≥0, a+b=1a+b=1a+b=1, each pair satisfies

U(aAix+bAiy)≤aU(Aix)+bU(Aiy)−mab2∥Ai(x−y)∥2.U(aA_ix+bA_iy)\le aU(A_ix)+bU(A_iy)-\frac{mab}{2}\|A_i(x-y)\|^2.U(aAi​x+bAi​y)≤aU(Ai​x)+bU(Ai​y)−2mab​∥Ai​(x−y)∥2.

Sum over iii, use linearity to identify the arguments with Ai(ax+by)A_i(ax+by)Ai​(ax+by), and use the quadratic replication identity on x−yx-yx−y. The result is the strong-convexity inequality for VNV_NVN​ with constant NmNmNm. The continuous linear map AiA_iAi​ is obtained from its finite-dimensional linear map; its evaluation theorem is definitional. If UUU is differentiable, the finite sum of U∘AiU\circ A_iU∘Ai​ is differentiable by the chain rule.

04The gradient chain rule and the lifted Lipschitz constant §

Mathematical statement and proof

Differentiating in a direction www gives

⟨∇VN(z),w⟩=∑i⟨∇U(Aiz),Aiw⟩.\langle\nabla V_N(z),w\rangle=\sum_i\langle\nabla U(A_i z),A_iw\rangle.⟨∇VN​(z),w⟩=i∑​⟨∇U(Ai​z),Ai​w⟩.

Cauchy–Schwarz in the particle index, followed by the quadratic replication identity, proves ∑i∥Aiv∥∥Aiw∥≤N∥v∥∥w∥\sum_i\|A_iv\|\|A_iw\|\le N\|v\|\|w\|∑i​∥Ai​v∥∥Ai​w∥≤N∥v∥∥w∥. If ∇U\nabla U∇U is LLL-Lipschitz, subtract the two gradient identities to obtain

⟨∇VN(x)−∇VN(y),w⟩≤NL∥x−y∥∥w∥.\langle\nabla V_N(x)-\nabla V_N(y),w\rangle\le NL\|x-y\|\|w\|.⟨∇VN​(x)−∇VN​(y),w⟩≤NL∥x−y∥∥w∥.

Choose w=∇VN(x)−∇VN(y)w=\nabla V_N(x)-\nabla V_N(y)w=∇VN​(x)−∇VN​(y). If its norm is zero the desired bound is immediate; otherwise cancel its positive norm. Thus ∇VN\nabla V_N∇VN​ is NLNLNL-Lipschitz, with the same particle factor as the convexity constant.

To track the constants without a coordinatewise overestimate, test the score difference against an arbitrary www. The chain rule gives a sum of pair-gradient differences paired with AiwA_iwAi​w. Smoothness bounds it by L∑i∥Ai(x−y)∥∥Aiw∥L\sum_i\|A_i(x-y)\|\|A_iw\|L∑i​∥Ai​(x−y)∥∥Ai​w∥, and Cauchy–Schwarz over particles makes this NL∥x−y∥∥w∥NL\|x-y\|\|w\|NL∥x−y∥∥w∥. Taking www equal to the score difference and cancelling its norm, with the zero case separate, proves the exact NLNLNL Lipschitz constant. This is the content of inner_gradient_potential, sum_joint_norm_mul and the final norm comparison.

06The parameter projection is a contraction §

Mathematical statement and proof

The continuous linear map pθz=θp_\theta z=\thetapθ​z=θ simply selects the parameter coordinates. Expanding squared norms gives ∥pθz∥2≤∥z∥2\|p_\theta z\|^2\le\|z\|^2∥pθ​z∥2≤∥z∥2, since the omitted latent squared coordinates are nonnegative. Both norms are nonnegative, so taking square roots proves ∥pθz∥≤∥z∥\|p_\theta z\|\le\|z\|∥pθ​z∥≤∥z∥. The definition of operator norm then gives ∥pθ∥≤1\|p_\theta\|\le1∥pθ​∥≤1. The coordinate evaluation theorem identifies each output coordinate directly. These inequalities permit quadratic transport to control the parameter RMS.

07One ULA step is exactly one physical IPLA step §

Mathematical statement and proof

Define the scaled update with physical step hhh by

z′=z−hN∇VN(z)+2h/N ξ.z'=z-\frac hN\nabla V_N(z)+\sqrt{2h/N}\,\xi.z′=z−Nh​∇VN​(z)+2h/N​ξ.

Its parameter coordinate is θ′=θ−(h/N)∑i∇θU(θ,xi)+2h/N ξθ\theta'=\theta-(h/N)\sum_i\nabla_\theta U(\theta,x_i)+\sqrt{2h/N}\,\xi_\thetaθ′=θ−(h/N)∑i​∇θ​U(θ,xi​)+2h/N​ξθ​. For N>0N>0N>0, multiply the iii-th scaled latent coordinate by N\sqrt NN​. The drift coefficient becomes (N)2h/N=h(\sqrt N)^2h/N=h(N​)2h/N=h, and N2h/N=2h\sqrt N\sqrt{2h/N}=\sqrt{2h}N​2h/N​=2h​. Consequently

xi′=xi−h∇xU(θ,xi)+2h ξi.x_i'=x_i-h\nabla_xU(\theta,x_i)+\sqrt{2h}\,\xi_i.xi′​=xi​−h∇x​U(θ,xi​)+2h​ξi​.

The square-root identity follows by multiplying radicands and cancelling positive NNN; Lean's real square-root convention makes the algebraic identity valid for the stated arbitrary real hhh as well.

The score-block lemmas are obtained by testing the derivative against single basis vectors: a parameter basis vector is replicated across all pairs, while a latent basis vector appears in one pair with factor N\sqrt NN​. In the particle update, multiplying the scaled coordinate by N\sqrt NN​ turns the drift coefficient (γ/N)NN(\gamma/N)\sqrt N\sqrt N(γ/N)N​N​ into γ\gammaγ, and the noise coefficient into 2γ\sqrt{2\gamma}2γ​. The parameter block keeps the averaged force and noise coefficient 2γ/N\sqrt{2\gamma/N}2γ/N​. Positive NNN protects exactly these unscaling cancellations.
