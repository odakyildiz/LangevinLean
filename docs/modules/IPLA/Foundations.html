01The named target is the normalized Gibbs measure §

The definitions give gibbsTarget⁡(U)=gibbsMeasure⁡(VN)\operatorname{gibbsTarget}(U)=\operatorname{gibbsMeasure}(V_N)gibbsTarget(U)=gibbsMeasure(VN​). If N>0N>0N>0 and m>0m>0m>0, then Nm>0Nm>0Nm>0. 02Finite transport supplies square integrability of initialization §

Mathematical statement and proof

Suppose a measurable initialization XXX has W2(X#P,ΠN)<∞W_2(X_\#P,\Pi_N)<\inftyW2​(X#​P,ΠN​)<∞. The target already has a finite second moment. For any finite-cost coupling, ∥x∥2≤2∥x−y∥2+2∥y∥2\|x\|^2\le2\|x-y\|^2+2\|y\|^2∥x∥2≤2∥x−y∥2+2∥y∥2; integrating transfers the target moment and coupling cost to the initial law. The shared transport-moment theorem formalizes this argument and proves X∈L2(P)X\in L^2(P)X∈L2(P). Thus this alternative initialization interface introduces no independent moment assumption.

03The lifted Gibbs score moment has the correct dimension §

Mathematical statement and proof

The shared score estimate for a strongly convex potential with ℓ\ellℓ-Lipschitz gradient is ∫∥∇V∥2dπ≤ℓdim⁡H\int\|\nabla V\|^2d\pi\le\ell\dim H∫∥∇V∥2dπ≤ℓdimH. Substitute V=VNV=V_NV=VN​, ℓ=NL\ell=NLℓ=NL, and dim⁡H=dθ+Ndx\dim H=d_\theta+Nd_xdimH=dθ​+Ndx​ to obtain

∫∥∇VN(z)∥2dΠN(z)≤NL(dθ+Ndx).\int\|\nabla V_N(z)\|^2d\Pi_N(z)\le NL(d_\theta+Nd_x).∫∥∇VN​(z)∥2dΠN​(z)≤NL(dθ​+Ndx​).

The lifted smoothness and convexity hypotheses follow from the joint ones. The proof only rewrites the common Gibbs definition and exact dimension; it adds no score-moment or Hessian premise.
