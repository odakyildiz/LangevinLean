Normalization, moments and the score bound for the lifted Gibbs target

References and publication status for this development

Published source and unpublished extension

Source: Theorem 1, equation (34), and Appendix B of arXiv:2409.07101v2 (27 May 2025), using the ULA estimate of Dalalyan and Karagulyan. The independent parameter- and latent-dimension extension and its proof presented here are unpublished; equal dimensions recover the paper’s displayed bound. The assumption audit explains the joint-potential smoothness condition.

  • Alex Glyn-Davies, Connor Duffin, Ieva Kazlauskaite, Mark Girolami, and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Statistical Finite Elements via Interacting Particle Langevin Dynamics. SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification 13(3), 1200–1227. Audited version: arXiv:2409.07101v2
  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv

Let UUU be differentiable and mmm-strongly convex on the pair space with m>0m>0m>0, and let N≥1N\ge1N≥1. By the geometry section, the lifted potential VN(z)=∑iU(Jiz)V_N(z)=\sum_iU(J_iz)VN​(z)=∑i​U(Ji​z) on Z\mathcal ZZ is differentiable and NmNmNm-strongly convex, with Nm>0Nm>0Nm>0; if ∇U\nabla U∇U is LLL-Lipschitz, then ∇VN\nabla V_N∇VN​ is NLNLNL-Lipschitz. This section applies three general results on Gibbs measures πW∝e−W\pi_W\propto e^{-W}πW​∝e−W of strongly convex potentials to W=VNW=V_NW=VN​, and obtains:

  • the normalizing constant ZN=∫e−VNZ_N=\int e^{-V_N}ZN​=∫e−VN​ satisfies 0<ZN<∞0<Z_N<\infty0<ZN​<∞, so the target ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ is a probability measure, and ∫∥z∥2 dΠN(z)<∞\int\|z\|^2\,d\Pi_N(z)<\infty∫∥z∥2dΠN​(z)<∞;
  • an initial state whose law is at finite quadratic Wasserstein distance from ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ is square integrable;
  • the score of the target satisfies ∫∥∇VN∥2 dΠN≤NL D\int\|\nabla V_N\|^2\,d\Pi_N\le NL\,D∫∥∇VN​∥2dΠN​≤NLD, where D=dθ+Ndx=dim⁡ZD=d_\theta+Nd_x=\dim\mathcal ZD=dθ​+Ndx​=dimZ.

None of these properties is assumed in the main theorem; they are consequences of the hypotheses on the joint potential. The first justifies the use of ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ as a target law, the second the representation of the initial state as a square-integrable random vector, and the third is the moment bound on the drift of the stationary reference process in the convergence proof.

01Normalization and second moment of the target §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. (a) For every UUU, the target ΠN=ZN−1e−VNdz\Pi_N=Z_N^{-1}e^{-V_N}dzΠN​=ZN−1​e−VN​dz is the Gibbs measure πW=ZW−1e−Wdz\pi_W=Z_W^{-1}e^{-W}dzπW​=ZW−1​e−Wdz of the potential W=VNW=V_NW=VN​, with the same extended-valued normalization. (b) Let N≥1N\ge1N≥1, m>0m>0m>0, and let UUU be differentiable and mmm-strongly convex. Then ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ is a probability measure and

∫Z∥z∥2 dΠN(z)<∞.\int_{\mathcal Z}\|z\|^2\,d\Pi_N(z)<\infty .∫Z​∥z∥2dΠN​(z)<∞.

Proof. (a) Both measures are defined by the same formula, so they are equal.

(b) Put W=VNW=V_NW=VN​ and κ=Nm\kappa=Nmκ=Nm. By the geometry section WWW is differentiable and κ\kappaκ-strongly convex, and κ>0\kappa>0κ>0 because N≥1N\ge1N≥1 and m>0m>0m>0. We use the general normalization and moment theorems for Gibbs measures ULA-SC, Section 3.26.13 and ULA-SC, Section 3.26.15, whose proof runs as follows.

Quadratic lower bound. Applying the definition of κ\kappaκ-strong convexity with the points 0,z0,z0,z and weights 1−t,t1-t,t1−t,t for t∈(0,1)t\in(0,1)t∈(0,1) gives W(tz)≤(1−t)W(0)+tW(z)−t(1−t)κ2∥z∥2W(tz)\le(1-t)W(0)+tW(z)-t(1-t)\frac\kappa2\|z\|^2W(tz)≤(1−t)W(0)+tW(z)−t(1−t)2κ​∥z∥2. Subtract W(0)W(0)W(0), divide by t>0t>0t>0 and let t→0+t\to0^+t→0+; differentiability of WWW at 000 gives

W(z)≥W(0)+⟨∇W(0),z⟩+κ2∥z∥2.W(z)\ge W(0)+\langle\nabla W(0),z\rangle+\frac\kappa2\|z\|^2 .W(z)≥W(0)+⟨∇W(0),z⟩+2κ​∥z∥2.

Finiteness. The Gaussian function e−κ4∥z∥2e^{-\frac\kappa4\|z\|^2}e−4κ​∥z∥2 and the function ∥z∥2e−κ4∥z∥2\|z\|^2e^{-\frac\kappa4\|z\|^2}∥z∥2e−4κ​∥z∥2 are Lebesgue integrable on the finite-dimensional space Z\mathcal ZZ. Conclusion. Since 0<ZW<∞0<Z_W<\infty0<ZW​<∞, πW(Z)=ZW−1∫e−W=ZW−1ZW=1\pi_W(\mathcal Z)=Z_W^{-1}\int e^{-W}=Z_W^{-1}Z_W=1πW​(Z)=ZW−1​∫e−W=ZW−1​ZW​=1, and ∫∥z∥2dπW=ZW−1∫∥z∥2e−W(z)dz<∞\int\|z\|^2d\pi_W=Z_W^{-1}\int\|z\|^2e^{-W(z)}dz<\infty∫∥z∥2dπW​=ZW−1​∫∥z∥2e−W(z)dz<∞. 02Square integrability from finite transport distance §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definitions. A coupling of two measures μ,ν\mu,\nuμ,ν on Z\mathcal ZZ is a measure ccc on Z×Z\mathcal Z\times\mathcal ZZ×Z whose first and second marginals are μ\muμ and ν\nuν. Its quadratic cost is (∫∥x−y∥2 dc(x,y))1/2∈[0,∞]\bigl(\int\|x-y\|^2\,dc(x,y)\bigr)^{1/2}\in[0,\infty](∫∥x−y∥2dc(x,y))1/2∈[0,∞], and the quadratic Wasserstein distance is

W2(μ,ν)=inf⁡{(∫∥x−y∥2 dc(x,y))1/2: c a coupling of μ and ν}∈[0,∞].W_2(\mu,\nu)=\inf\Bigl\{\Bigl(\int\|x-y\|^2\,dc(x,y)\Bigr)^{1/2}:\ c\text{ a coupling of }\mu\text{ and }\nu\Bigr\}\in[0,\infty].W2​(μ,ν)=inf{(∫∥x−y∥2dc(x,y))1/2: c a coupling of μ and ν}∈[0,∞].

Claim. Let N≥1N\ge1N≥1, m>0m>0m>0, and let UUU be differentiable and mmm-strongly convex. Let X:Ω→ZX:\Omega\to\mathcal ZX:Ω→Z be almost everywhere measurable with W2(X#P,ΠN)<∞W_2(X_\#\mathbb P,\Pi_N)<\inftyW2​(X#​P,ΠN​)<∞. Then XXX is square integrable: ∫∥X∥2 dP<∞\int\|X\|^2\,d\mathbb P<\infty∫∥X∥2dP<∞.

Proof. An infimum in [0,∞][0,\infty][0,∞] is finite only if some element of the set is finite, so there is a coupling ccc of μ=X#P\mu=X_\#\mathbb Pμ=X#​P and ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ with ∫∥x−y∥2 dc<∞\int\|x-y\|^2\,dc<\infty∫∥x−y∥2dc<∞; attainment of the infimum is not needed. By the triangle inequality and (a+b)2≤2a2+2b2(a+b)^2\le2a^2+2b^2(a+b)2≤2a2+2b2,

∥x∥2≤(∥x−y∥+∥y∥)2≤2∥x−y∥2+2∥y∥2.\|x\|^2\le\bigl(\|x-y\|+\|y\|\bigr)^2\le2\|x-y\|^2+2\|y\|^2 .∥x∥2≤(∥x−y∥+∥y∥)2≤2∥x−y∥2+2∥y∥2.

Integrating against ccc and using that the second marginal of ccc is ΠN\Pi_NΠN​,

∫∥x∥2 dc(x,y)≤2∫∥x−y∥2 dc+2∫∥y∥2 dΠN(y)<∞,\int\|x\|^2\,dc(x,y)\le2\int\|x-y\|^2\,dc+2\int\|y\|^2\,d\Pi_N(y)<\infty,∫∥x∥2dc(x,y)≤2∫∥x−y∥2dc+2∫∥y∥2dΠN​(y)<∞,

where the last integral is finite by the previous step. The first marginal of ccc is μ\muμ, so the left side equals ∫∥x∥2 dμ(x)\int\|x\|^2\,d\mu(x)∫∥x∥2dμ(x), and by the change-of-variables formula for image measures ∫∥x∥2 dμ=∫∥X∥2 dP\int\|x\|^2\,d\mu=\int\|X\|^2\,d\mathbb P∫∥x∥2dμ=∫∥X∥2dP. □\square□

Thus the main theorem needs no separate moment assumption on the initial state: finiteness of the initial transport distance already provides it, and conversely a square-integrable initial state is at finite distance because both laws have finite second moments.

03The second moment of the target score §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let N≥1N\ge1N≥1, m>0m>0m>0, L≥0L\ge0L≥0, and let UUU be differentiable, mmm-strongly convex, with LLL-Lipschitz gradient. Then, with D=dθ+NdxD=d_\theta+Nd_xD=dθ​+Ndx​,

∫Z∥∇VN(z)∥2 dΠN(z)≤NL D.\int_{\mathcal Z}\|\nabla V_N(z)\|^2\,d\Pi_N(z)\le NL\,D .∫Z​∥∇VN​(z)∥2dΠN​(z)≤NLD.

Proof. We use the general score bound for Gibbs measures ULA-SC, Section 3.27.8: if WWW is differentiable and κ\kappaκ-strongly convex with κ>0\kappa>0κ>0 on a finite-dimensional inner-product space HHH, and ∇W\nabla W∇W is KKK-Lipschitz, then ∫∥∇W∥2dπW≤Kdim⁡H\int\|\nabla W\|^2d\pi_W\le K\dim H∫∥∇W∥2dπW​≤KdimH. Its proof is as follows. Strong convexity gives ZW<∞Z_W<\inftyZW​<∞ (previous step). For a fixed vector vvv, the descent inequality W(x+v)≤W(x)+⟨∇W(x),v⟩+K2∥v∥2W(x+v)\le W(x)+\langle\nabla W(x),v\rangle+\frac K2\|v\|^2W(x+v)≤W(x)+⟨∇W(x),v⟩+2K​∥v∥2 (a consequence of the KKK-Lipschitz gradient) gives

Apply this with H=ZH=\mathcal ZH=Z, W=VNW=V_NW=VN​, κ=Nm>0\kappa=Nm>0κ=Nm>0 and K=NLK=NLK=NL: VNV_NVN​ is differentiable, NmNmNm-strongly convex and has NLNLNL-Lipschitz gradient by the geometry section, πVN=ΠN\pi_{V_N}=\Pi_NπVN​​=ΠN​ by the first step, and dim⁡Z=dθ+Ndx\dim\mathcal Z=d_\theta+Nd_xdimZ=dθ​+Ndx​ by the dimension formula. □\square□
