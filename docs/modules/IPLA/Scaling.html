Particle and time scaling of the transport estimate

References and publication status for this development

Published source and unpublished extension

Source: Theorem 1, equation (34), and Appendix B of arXiv:2409.07101v2 (27 May 2025), using the ULA estimate of Dalalyan and Karagulyan. The independent parameter- and latent-dimension extension and its proof presented here are unpublished; equal dimensions recover the paper’s displayed bound. The assumption audit explains the joint-potential smoothness condition.

  • Alex Glyn-Davies, Connor Duffin, Ieva Kazlauskaite, Mark Girolami, and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Statistical Finite Elements via Interacting Particle Langevin Dynamics. SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification 13(3), 1200–1227. Audited version: arXiv:2409.07101v2
  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv

Substitution of the lifted curvature, gradient constant, dimension, and step into the Euclidean Langevin estimate gives the particle estimate. The dimension factor reduces to D/N=dx+dθ/ND/N=d_x+d_\theta/ND/N=dx​+dθ​/N because the scaled step is the physical step divided by the particle count. All substitutions below are equalities, so the numerical coefficient is preserved.

The lifted ULA problem has mN=Nmm_N=NmmN​=Nm, LN=NLL_N=NLLN​=NL, step h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N, and dimension D=dθ+NdxD=d_\theta+Nd_xD=dθ​+Ndx​. This module performs the exact scalar substitutions into the shared Dalalyan theorem.

01Step restriction and square-root bias §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume N>0N>0N>0. Division by NNN preserves inequalities, and Nm+NL=N(m+L)Nm+NL=N(m+L)Nm+NL=N(m+L). Hence γ/N≤2/(Nm+NL)\gamma/N\le2/(Nm+NL)γ/N≤2/(Nm+NL) is equivalent to γ≤2/(m+L)\gamma\le2/(m+L)γ≤2/(m+L). The regularity ratio NL/(Nm)NL/(Nm)NL/(Nm) equals L/mL/mL/m, while the dimension–step product satisfies

γN(dθ+Ndx)=γ(dx+dθN).\frac\gamma N(d_\theta+Nd_x)=\gamma\left(d_x+\frac{d_\theta}{N}\right).Nγ​(dθ​+Ndx​)=γ(dx​+Ndθ​​).

Nonnegativity of the dimensions and step permits the square-root product rule, giving

3320NLNmγND=3320Lmdx+dθ/Nγ.\frac{33}{20}\frac{NL}{Nm}\sqrt{\frac\gamma N D}=\frac{33}{20}\frac Lm\sqrt{d_x+d_\theta/N}\sqrt\gamma.2033​NmNL​Nγ​D​=2033​mL​dx​+dθ​/N​γ​.

In terms of the lifted quantities mN=Nmm_N=NmmN​=Nm, LN=NLL_N=NLLN​=NL, h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N, and D=dθ+NdxD=d_\theta+Nd_xD=dθ​+Ndx​, positivity of NNN gives LN/mN=L/mL_N/m_N=L/mLN​/mN​=L/m and

hD=γ(dx+dθ/N).hD=\gamma(d_x+d_\theta/N).hD=γ(dx​+dθ​/N).

The square-root identity is therefore hD=dx+dθ/Nγ\sqrt{hD}=\sqrt{d_x+d_\theta/N}\sqrt\gammahD​=dx​+dθ​/N​γ​. Every cancellation of NNN uses N>0N>0N>0. The latent contribution is proportional to dxd_xdx​, whereas the parameter contribution is divided by NNN. Thus particle count reduces the parameter contribution to discretization error; at a fixed physical step, the latent contribution persists. The admissible physical step does not depend on particle count.

02The transport estimate after scaling §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume a family of comparison processes is available for every finite-cost initial coupling and that the initial W2W_2W2​ distance is finite. The Langevin transport theorem gives

W2(νk,π)≤(1−mNh)kW2(ν0,π)+3320LNmNhD.W_2(\nu_k,\pi)\le(1-m_Nh)^kW_2(\nu_0,\pi)+\frac{33}{20}\frac{L_N}{m_N}\sqrt{hD}.W2​(νk​,π)≤(1−mN​h)kW2​(ν0​,π)+2033​mN​LN​​hD​.

The assumptions m≤Lm\le Lm≤L, m>0m>0m>0, γ>0\gamma>0γ>0, and the physical step restriction imply the lifted hypotheses by multiplication and division by positive NNN. The identity Nm(γ/N)=mγNm(\gamma/N)=m\gammaNm(γ/N)=mγ simplifies the contraction, and the preceding square-root identity simplifies the dimension term. In particular (1−mNh)k=(1−mγ)k(1-m_Nh)^k=(1-m\gamma)^k(1−mN​h)k=(1−mγ)k. The initial transport distance is already measured in the scaled state norm and acquires no additional particle factor. This proves the finite-time particle estimate under the stated comparison-process premise; the Gaussian construction establishes that premise for the algorithm laws.

The shared Hilbert-space theorem first gives W2(νk,π)≤(1−mNh)kW2(ν0,π)+(33/20)(LN/mN)hDW_2(\nu_k,\pi)\le(1-m_Nh)^kW_2(\nu_0,\pi)+(33/20)(L_N/m_N)\sqrt{hD}W2​(νk​,π)≤(1−mN​h)kW2​(ν0​,π)+(33/20)(LN​/mN​)hD​. The local facts hNL and hs verify its compatibility and step premises from m≤Lm\le Lm≤L and the physical step restriction. Substituting mNh=mγm_Nh=m\gammamN​h=mγ and the exact bias identity yields the displayed IPLA rate. This module expects an explicit stationary reference family; later modules construct that family before the public theorem invokes the estimate.
