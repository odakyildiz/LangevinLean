The law of the Gaussian particle algorithm depends only on the initial law

References and publication status for this development

Published source and unpublished extension

Source: Theorem 1, equation (34), and Appendix B of arXiv:2409.07101v2 (27 May 2025), using the ULA estimate of Dalalyan and Karagulyan. The independent parameter- and latent-dimension extension and its proof presented here are unpublished; equal dimensions recover the paper’s displayed bound. The assumption audit explains the joint-potential smoothness condition.

  • Alex Glyn-Davies, Connor Duffin, Ieva Kazlauskaite, Mark Girolami, and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Statistical Finite Elements via Interacting Particle Langevin Dynamics. SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification 13(3), 1200–1227. Audited version: arXiv:2409.07101v2
  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv

Let III be the index set of the coordinates of the scaled state space Z\mathcal ZZ and K=N×IK=\mathbb N\times IK=N×I the countable set of pairs (iteration, coordinate). This section shows that the Gaussian innovation hypotheses determine the joint law of the whole innovation sequence: it is the image G\mathcal GG of the product measure ⨂(k,a)∈KN(0,1)\bigotimes_{(k,a)\in K}\mathcal N(0,1)⨂(k,a)∈K​N(0,1) on RK\mathbb R^KRK under the map that groups the scalar coordinates of each iteration into a vector of Z\mathcal ZZ. Together with the independence of the initial state, the law ρk\rho_kρk​ of the kkk-th iterate is therefore

ρk=(Φk)#(μ0⊗G),\rho_k=(\Phi_k)_\#\bigl(\mu_0\otimes\mathcal G\bigr),ρk​=(Φk​)#​(μ0​⊗G),

where μ0\mu_0μ0​ is the law of the initial state and Φk(z0,(un)n)\Phi_k(z_0,(u_n)_n)Φk​(z0​,(un​)n​) is the kkk-th iterate of the deterministic recursion zn+1=Tγ(zn,un)z_{n+1}=T_\gamma(z_n,u_n)zn+1​=Tγ​(zn​,un​). This recursion is identical to the Euler scheme of the unadjusted Langevin algorithm for the lifted potential VNV_NVN​ with step γ/N\gamma/Nγ/N. In particular two realizations of the algorithm with the same initial law have the same laws ρk\rho_kρk​ at all iterations, even if they are defined on different probability spaces. This is what allows the convergence proof to replace a given realization by a convenient one built on a product space that also carries a Brownian reference process.

01The canonical Gaussian innovation law §

Under G\mathcal GG, the vectors u0,u1,…u_0,u_1,\dotsu0​,u1​,… of a path (uk)k(u_k)_k(uk​)k​ are independent standard Gaussian vectors in Z\mathcal ZZ: all their coordinates, at all times, are independent N(0,1)\mathcal N(0,1)N(0,1) variables. The construction uses a single countable family of scalar Gaussians, so it does not presuppose any grouping of the coordinates into independent blocks.

02The innovation hypotheses determine the path law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Assume the Gaussian innovation hypotheses for an initial state Z0Z_0Z0​ and innovations ξ0,ξ1,…\xi_0,\xi_1,\dotsξ0​,ξ1​,… on (Ω,F,P)(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)(Ω,F,P). Then

(ω↦(ξk,a(ω))(k,a)∈K)#P=γKand(ω↦(ξk(ω))k∈N)#P=G.\bigl(\omega\mapsto(\xi_{k,a}(\omega))_{(k,a)\in K}\bigr)_\#\mathbb P=\gamma_K\qquad\text{and}\qquad\bigl(\omega\mapsto(\xi_k(\omega))_{k\in\mathbb N}\bigr)_\#\mathbb P=\mathcal G .(ω↦(ξk,a​(ω))(k,a)∈K​)#​P=γK​and(ω↦(ξk​(ω))k∈N​)#​P=G.

Proof. Write Ξ(ω)=(ξj(ω))j∈K∈RK\Xi(\omega)=(\xi_j(\omega))_{j\in K}\in\mathbb R^KΞ(ω)=(ξj​(ω))j∈K​∈RK. Each coordinate ξj\xi_jξj​ has law N(0,1)\mathcal N(0,1)N(0,1) and is in particular almost everywhere measurable. Since KKK is countable, Ξ\XiΞ is then almost everywhere measurable for the product σ\sigmaσ-algebra (modify each coordinate on a null set; a countable union of null sets is null). For a finite set F⊆KF\subseteq KF⊆K and Borel sets AjA_jAj​, mutual independence of the family and the coordinate laws give

P(ξj∈Aj ∀j∈F)=∏j∈FP(ξj∈Aj)=∏j∈FN(0,1)(Aj).\mathbb P\bigl(\xi_j\in A_j\ \forall j\in F\bigr)=\prod_{j\in F}\mathbb P(\xi_j\in A_j)=\prod_{j\in F}\mathcal N(0,1)(A_j).P(ξj​∈Aj​ ∀j∈F)=j∈F∏​P(ξj​∈Aj​)=j∈F∏​N(0,1)(Aj​).

Thus the probability measure Ξ#P\Xi_\#\mathbb PΞ#​P agrees with γK\gamma_KγK​ on the cylinder sets {w:wj∈Aj ∀j∈F}\{w:w_j\in A_j\ \forall j\in F\}{w:wj​∈Aj​ ∀j∈F}. These sets are closed under finite intersections and generate the product σ\sigmaσ-algebra, so the uniqueness theorem for measures (Dynkin's π\piπ–λ\lambdaλ theorem) gives Ξ#P=γK\Xi_\#\mathbb P=\gamma_KΞ#​P=γK​. This is the first identity.

For the second, the path map ω↦(ξk(ω))k\omega\mapsto(\xi_k(\omega))_kω↦(ξk​(ω))k​ equals pack⁡∘Ξ\operatorname{pack}\circ\Xipack∘Ξ, because (pack⁡Ξ(ω))k(\operatorname{pack}\Xi(\omega))_k(packΞ(ω))k​ has coordinates ξk,a(ω)\xi_{k,a}(\omega)ξk,a​(ω), a∈Ia\in Ia∈I. For a measurable map fff and an almost everywhere measurable map ggg, (f∘g)#P=f#(g#P)(f\circ g)_\#\mathbb P=f_\#(g_\#\mathbb P)(f∘g)#​P=f#​(g#​P). With f=pack⁡f=\operatorname{pack}f=pack (measurable by the previous step) and g=Ξg=\Xig=Ξ,

((ξk)k)#P=pack⁡#(Ξ#P)=pack⁡#γK=G.□\bigl((\xi_k)_k\bigr)_\#\mathbb P=\operatorname{pack}_\#\bigl(\Xi_\#\mathbb P\bigr)=\operatorname{pack}_\#\gamma_K=\mathcal G .\qquad\square((ξk​)k​)#​P=pack#​(Ξ#​P)=pack#​γK​=G.□

The conclusion concerns the joint law of the whole sequence, across all times as well as within each vector. Knowing only that each ξk\xi_kξk​ is a standard Gaussian vector would not determine it, because the vectors at different times could be dependent.

03The particle update is the Langevin update for the lifted potential §

No reference diffusion, stationarity or coupling enters this argument. Identity (1) is used in two ways: it shows that ρk\rho_kρk​ is the law of the Langevin algorithm for VNV_NVN​ at step γ/N\gamma/Nγ/N, and it allows the convergence proof to work with a realization on a product probability space that also carries a stationary reference process.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

ULA.chainLaw F μ Q n is defined in the Langevin library as (μ.prod Q).map (fun p ↦ noiseDrivenChain F p.1 p.2 n), the measure law⁡n(F,μ,Q)\operatorname{law}_n(F,\mu,Q)lawn​(F,μ,Q); μ.prod Q is the product measure.

  1. laws_eq_canonicalChainLaw is (1): laws P U γ L hdiff hLip initial noise k = ULA.chainLaw (pointwiseUpdate (potential U) (γ / N)) (P.map initial) (canonicalNoisePathLaw N dθ dx) k, under [IsProbabilityMeasure P] and hGaussian. The proof is a chain of rewrites:
    • laws_eq_scaledChain replaces the law of the square-integrable chain by the image of the pointwise recursion;
    • simp_rw [← noiseDrivenChain_eq_scaledChain] writes that recursion as the noise-driven chain of pointwiseUpdate;
    • ULA.noiseDrivenChain_law_of_indep is the library theorem that, for a measurable update map, an almost everywhere measurable initial state XXX and an almost everywhere measurable path WWW that are independent, the law of the chain at time nnn is the chain law of X#PX_\#\mathbb PX#​P and W#PW_\#\mathbb PW#​P; its proof is the product-law and composition argument above (it uses IndepFun.map_prod_eq_prod_map_map and AEMeasurable.map_map_of_aemeasurable, and the measurability of every iterate). Its hypotheses are discharged by pointwiseUpdate_measurable applied to the Lipschitz gradient potential_gradient_lipschitz hdiff hLip with constant (N : ℝ≥0) * L, by Lp.aestronglyMeasurable for the representatives of initial and of each noise n (combined with aemeasurable_pi_iff for the path), and by the field hGaussian.independentInitial;
    • hGaussian.noisePath_law P initial noise replaces the path law by G\mathcal GG.
  2. laws_eq_of_initialLaw_eq is the consequence for two realizations on P and P' (possibly different sample spaces Ω and Ω'), under hGaussian, hGaussian' and hinitial : P.map initial = P'.map initial'. It rewrites both sides with laws_eq_canonicalChainLaw and then with hinitial.
