Canonical Gaussian path law and realization independence

References and publication status for this development

Published source and unpublished extension

Source: Theorem 1, equation (34), and Appendix B of arXiv:2409.07101v2 (27 May 2025), using the ULA estimate of Dalalyan and Karagulyan. The independent parameter- and latent-dimension extension and its proof presented here are unpublished; equal dimensions recover the paper’s displayed bound. The assumption audit explains the joint-potential smoothness condition.

  • Alex Glyn-Davies, Connor Duffin, Ieva Kazlauskaite, Mark Girolami, and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Statistical Finite Elements via Interacting Particle Langevin Dynamics. SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification 13(3), 1200–1227. Audited version: arXiv:2409.07101v2
  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv

Once the initial law and the independent Gaussian path law are fixed, the algorithm law is obtained by applying a deterministic measurable recursion to their product. This gives an realization-independent meaning to the finite-time output distribution. It also permits coupling constructions on auxiliary probability spaces without changing the law of the algorithm being analyzed.

The scalar Gaussian coordinate hypotheses uniquely determine the entire countable noise path. Independence of initialization then determines every output law, even across different underlying probability spaces.

01Construct the independent Gaussian noise path §

Mathematical statement and proof

A noise coordinate is a pair (k,a)(k,a)(k,a) consisting of iteration and state coordinate. Put the countable product law ⨂(k,a)N(0,1)\bigotimes_{(k,a)}\mathcal N(0,1)⨂(k,a)​N(0,1) on scalar paths www. Packing sets (pack⁡w)k(a)=w(k,a)(\operatorname{pack}w)_k(a)=w(k,a)(packw)k​(a)=w(k,a); its measurability follows because every output coordinate is a measurable product projection, followed by the measurable Euclidean coordinate identification. The canonical vector path law is the pushforward of that scalar product law by packing.

The two probability instances are proved as well: each scalar Gaussian factor has mass one, hence the infinite product is a probability measure; pushing it through measurable packing preserves total mass. These are canonicalFlatNoiseLaw_isProbabilityMeasure and canonicalNoisePathLaw_isProbabilityMeasure, whose source appears in the complete-module panel.

Formal correspondence: NoiseCoordinate : A coordinate records the iteration and the parameter or particle block. canonicalFlatNoiseLaw : The canonical scalar noise source: independent standard real Gaussians. packNoisePath : Group scalar coordinates into the Euclidean noise vector at each time. packNoisePath_measurable : Packing the countable coordinate source is measurable. canonicalNoisePathLaw : The canonical vector-valued Gaussian noise-path law.

A cylinder event specifies only finitely many scalar coordinates and has probability equal to the product of their standard Gaussian probabilities. These cylinder probabilities determine the countable product law. Grouping the coordinates into vectors changes their representation but does not change any event or distributional relationship. The resulting time-indexed vectors therefore retain both coordinate independence and independence between distinct time slices.

02The algorithm hypotheses identify the entire path law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Mutual independence of the scalar family implies that its joint map has law equal to the infinite product of its individual coordinate laws. Since each law is N(0,1)\mathcal N(0,1)N(0,1),

L((ξk,a)k,a)=⨂k,aN(0,1).\mathcal L((\xi_{k,a})_{k,a})=\bigotimes_{k,a}\mathcal N(0,1).L((ξk,a​)k,a​)=k,a⨂​N(0,1).

Composing this map with measurable packing and using the composition rule for pushforwards gives L((ξk)k)=G\mathcal L((\xi_k)_k)=\mathcal GL((ξk​)k​)=G. The argument proves independence of vector blocks from scalar independence and never assumes a grouping principle without justification.

The canonical flat source is the product of one scalar Gaussian for each time/coordinate pair. The packing map merely groups these coordinates into a vector at each time; coordinate evaluation proves its measurability. The supplied innovation structure has the same finite-coordinate Gaussian product laws by mutual independence, so the product-law uniqueness theorem identifies its whole flat path law. Applying the measurable packing map gives equality of the full vector path law, including all cross-time relations.

Formal correspondence: GaussianInnovations.flatNoisePath_law : The Gaussian coordinate hypotheses identify the complete flattened path law. GaussianInnovations.noisePath_law : The vector-valued path law is derived from the scalar Gaussian hypotheses, including every coordinate and every iteration.

The joint distribution theorem for an independent countable family applies to all time-coordinate pairs at once. It is stronger than knowing the correct vector law separately at each time: separate laws alone would permit temporal dependence. After identifying this full scalar law, composition with coordinate grouping proves the vector-path identity. This is the exact distributional input used when iterating a Markov transition.

03Identify the common update and recursion §

Mathematical statement and proof

The generic pointwise ULA update for VNV_NVN​ and step γ/N\gamma/Nγ/N is z−(γ/N)∇VN(z)+2(γ/N)ξz-(\gamma/N)\nabla V_N(z)+\sqrt{2(\gamma/N)}\xiz−(γ/N)∇VN​(z)+2(γ/N)​ξ. Reassociating multiplication inside the square root makes it the scaled IPLA update. The generic noise-driven chain and scaledChain therefore agree by induction: their initial values match and each successor uses the same map on the same preceding state and noise vector.

Formal correspondence: pointwiseUpdate_eq_scaledUpdate : The generic ULA update for U_N is exactly the scaled IPLA update. noiseDrivenChain_eq_scaledChain : Exact identification of the generic noise recursion with the IPLA recursion.

Write the common update as T(z,u)=z−(γ/N)∇VN(z)+2γ/N uT(z,u)=z-(\gamma/N)\nabla V_N(z)+\sqrt{2\gamma/N}\,uT(z,u)=z−(γ/N)∇VN​(z)+2γ/N​u. Equality of updates gives equality of the zero-step chains immediately. If two chains agree after kkk steps, applying the same function TTT to the equal states and the same kkk-th innovation proves agreement after k+1k+1k+1 steps. No distributional premise is required for this deterministic induction.

04A common initial law gives common output laws §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let Fk(z0,ξ)F_k(z_0,\xi)Fk​(z0​,ξ) be the measurable map that iterates the update kkk times. Initialization–path independence gives the joint input law μ0⊗G\mu_0\otimes\mathcal Gμ0​⊗G, where G\mathcal GG is the canonical Gaussian path law. Hence

ρk=(Fk)#(μ0⊗G).\rho_k=(F_k)_\#(\mu_0\otimes\mathcal G).ρk​=(Fk​)#​(μ0​⊗G).

The pointwise/L² identity identifies this measure with laws. The right-hand side depends only on U,γ,μ0U,\gamma,\mu_0U,γ,μ0​, so two Gaussian realizations with the same initial law give identical output laws. This identity needs no stationary diffusion or reference coupling.

The exact update identity matches the generic noise-driven recursion to the scaled IPLA recursion. Independence then makes the joint initialization/path law the product of the initial pushforward and the canonical noise path law. Push this common source through the measurable time-kkk recursion. Two realizations on different probability spaces therefore have the same output law whenever their initial laws agree; the conclusion is about the actual entire algorithm law at the specified time.

Formal correspondence: laws_eq_canonicalChainLaw : The law in the IPLA convergence statement is the canonical law of the actual Gaussian algorithm. No reference process is involved in this result. laws_eq_of_initialLaw_eq : Two Gaussian realizations of IPLA with the same initial law have the same output law, even when they use different underlying probability spaces.

The map FkF_kFk​ depends only on the initialization and the first kkk vector innovations. Its measurability follows by composing the measurable update a finite number of times. Independence then factors the input law before any recursion is evaluated. Thus a coupling construction is free to alter the auxiliary probability space, provided it preserves these input laws and their independence; the algorithm's output marginal remains fixed.
