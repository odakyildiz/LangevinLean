The physical-time generator and the infinitesimal invariance of the target

References and publication status for this development

Published source and unpublished extension

Source: Theorem 1, equation (34), and Appendix B of arXiv:2409.07101v2 (27 May 2025), using the ULA estimate of Dalalyan and Karagulyan. In physical time the interacting particle algorithm discretizes the diffusion

dZt=−1N∇VN(Zt) dt+2N dBtdZ_t=-\frac1N\nabla V_N(Z_t)\,dt+\sqrt{\frac2N}\,dB_tdZt​=−N1​∇VN​(Zt​)dt+N2​​dBt​

on the scaled state space Z\mathcal ZZ: its Euler scheme with step γ\gammaγ is the update Zk+1=Zk−γN∇VN(Zk)+2γ/N ξkZ_{k+1}=Z_k-\frac\gamma N\nabla V_N(Z_k)+\sqrt{2\gamma/N}\,\xi_kZk+1​=Zk​−Nγ​∇VN​(Zk​)+2γ/N​ξk​. This diffusion is the Langevin diffusion dYs=−∇VN(Ys) ds+2 dBsdY_s=-\nabla V_N(Y_s)\,ds+\sqrt2\,dB_sdYs​=−∇VN​(Ys​)ds+2​dBs​ run at the slower clock s=t/Ns=t/Ns=t/N, so its generator is AN=1NLVN\mathcal A_N=\frac1N\mathcal L_{V_N}AN​=N1​LVN​​, where LVφ=Δφ−⟨∇V,∇φ⟩\mathcal L_V\varphi=\Delta\varphi-\langle\nabla V,\nabla\varphi\rangleLV​φ=Δφ−⟨∇V,∇φ⟩ is the Langevin generator. This section defines AN\mathcal A_NAN​, proves the displayed formula for C2C^2C2 test functions, and proves that the target ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ is infinitesimally invariant: for every compactly supported C2C^2C2 function φ\varphiφ, ANφ\mathcal A_N\varphiAN​φ is ΠN\Pi_NΠN​-integrable and ∫ANφ dΠN=0\int\mathcal A_N\varphi\,d\Pi_N=0∫AN​φdΠN​=0. Only the hypotheses of the main theorem on the joint potential UUU are used; no second derivative of UUU appears. The result is not used in the proof of the main theorem, where the stationarity of the Langevin reference process is obtained by a different argument.

Statement and proof

01The generator in physical time §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. Let e1,…,eDe_1,\dots,e_De1​,…,eD​ be an orthonormal basis of Z\mathcal ZZ, D=dθ+NdxD=d_\theta+Nd_xD=dθ​+Ndx​. For W,φ:Z→RW,\varphi:\mathcal Z\to\mathbb RW,φ:Z→R and z∈Zz\in\mathcal Zz∈Z, the Langevin generator is

LWφ(z)=∑r=1D(∂er(∂erφ)(z)−⟨∇W(z),er⟩ ∂erφ(z)),\mathcal L_W\varphi(z)=\sum_{r=1}^D\Bigl(\partial_{e_r}\bigl(\partial_{e_r}\varphi\bigr)(z)-\langle\nabla W(z),e_r\rangle\,\partial_{e_r}\varphi(z)\Bigr),LW​φ(z)=r=1∑D​(∂er​​(∂er​​φ)(z)−⟨∇W(z),er​⟩∂er​​φ(z)),

where ∂v\partial_v∂v​ is the directional derivative D(⋅)vD(\cdot)vD(⋅)v. The physical-time generator of the particle diffusion is ANφ(z)=1N LVNφ(z)\mathcal A_N\varphi(z)=\frac1N\,\mathcal L_{V_N}\varphi(z)AN​φ(z)=N1​LVN​​φ(z), with the convention 10=0\frac10=001​=0. Here ∇VN(z)\nabla V_N(z)∇VN​(z) denotes the gradient of VNV_NVN​ at zzz (taken to be 000 at a point where VNV_NVN​ is not differentiable; in all applications VNV_NVN​ is differentiable).

Claim. For every UUU, every twice continuously differentiable φ:Z→R\varphi:\mathcal Z\to\mathbb Rφ:Z→R and every zzz,

ANφ(z)=1N(Δφ(z)−⟨∇VN(z),∇φ(z)⟩),Δφ(z)=∑r=1DD2φ(z)(er,er).\mathcal A_N\varphi(z)=\frac1N\Bigl(\Delta\varphi(z)-\bigl\langle\nabla V_N(z),\nabla\varphi(z)\bigr\rangle\Bigr),\qquad\Delta\varphi(z)=\sum_{r=1}^DD^2\varphi(z)(e_r,e_r).AN​φ(z)=N1​(Δφ(z)−⟨∇VN​(z),∇φ(z)⟩),Δφ(z)=r=1∑D​D2φ(z)(er​,er​).

Proof. Since φ\varphiφ is C2C^2C2, the map z↦Dφ(z)z\mapsto D\varphi(z)z↦Dφ(z) is differentiable and the derivative of z↦Dφ(z)erz\mapsto D\varphi(z)e_rz↦Dφ(z)er​ in direction ere_rer​ is the second derivative D2φ(z)(er,er)D^2\varphi(z)(e_r,e_r)D2φ(z)(er​,er​). Summing over rrr gives the Laplacian Δφ(z)\Delta\varphi(z)Δφ(z), the trace of the second derivative (which does not depend on the choice of orthonormal basis). For the first-order terms, ∂erφ(z)=⟨∇φ(z),er⟩\partial_{e_r}\varphi(z)=\langle\nabla\varphi(z),e_r\rangle∂er​​φ(z)=⟨∇φ(z),er​⟩ by the definition of the gradient, and by Parseval's identity in the orthonormal basis

∑r=1D⟨∇VN(z),er⟩⟨∇φ(z),er⟩=⟨∇VN(z),∇φ(z)⟩.\sum_{r=1}^D\langle\nabla V_N(z),e_r\rangle\langle\nabla\varphi(z),e_r\rangle=\langle\nabla V_N(z),\nabla\varphi(z)\rangle .r=1∑D​⟨∇VN​(z),er​⟩⟨∇φ(z),er​⟩=⟨∇VN​(z),∇φ(z)⟩.

Subtracting and multiplying by 1N\frac1NN1​ gives the claim. □\square□

The first-order term is an algebraic identity for the vector ∇VN(z)\nabla V_N(z)∇VN​(z), so no regularity of UUU is needed for the formula. In physical coordinates the drift term is 1N⟨∑i∇θU(θ,xi),∇θφ⟩+∑i⟨∇xU(θ,xi),1N∇ziφ⟩\frac1N\langle\sum_i\nabla_\theta U(\theta,x^i),\nabla_\theta\varphi\rangle+\sum_i\langle\nabla_xU(\theta,x^i),\frac1{\sqrt N}\nabla_{z_i}\varphi\rangleN1​⟨∑i​∇θ​U(θ,xi),∇θ​φ⟩+∑i​⟨∇x​U(θ,xi),N​1​∇zi​​φ⟩, the generator of the parameter moving along the average force with diffusion coefficient 2/N2/N2/N and of each particle performing a Langevin diffusion in its pair potential.

02Infinitesimal invariance of the Gibbs target §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let N≥1N\ge1N≥1, m>0m>0m>0, L≥0L\ge0L≥0, and let the joint potential U=UyU=U_yU=Uy​ (for a fixed datum yyy) be differentiable and mmm-strongly convex with LLL-Lipschitz gradient. Then ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ is a probability measure, and for every twice continuously differentiable φ:Z→R\varphi:\mathcal Z\to\mathbb Rφ:Z→R with compact support,

ANφ∈L1(ΠN)and∫ZANφ dΠN=0.\mathcal A_N\varphi\in L^1(\Pi_N)\qquad\text{and}\qquad\int_{\mathcal Z}\mathcal A_N\varphi\,d\Pi_N=0 .AN​φ∈L1(ΠN​)and∫Z​AN​φdΠN​=0.

Proof. The probability property was proved in the section on foundations. Put W=VNW=V_NW=VN​; it is differentiable and ∇W\nabla W∇W is NLNLNL-Lipschitz, in particular continuous, by the geometry section. Let KKK be the compact support of φ\varphiφ and fix a basis vector v=erv=e_rv=er​.

Integrability. The function fv=e−W(∂v∂vφ−⟨∇W,v⟩∂vφ)f_v=e^{-W}\bigl(\partial_v\partial_v\varphi-\langle\nabla W,v\rangle\partial_v\varphi\bigr)fv​=e−W(∂v​∂v​φ−⟨∇W,v⟩∂v​φ) vanishes outside KKK, because all derivatives of φ\varphiφ vanish on the open complement of the support. On KKK it is continuous, hence bounded. A bounded measurable function with compact support is Lebesgue integrable, so fv∈L1(dz)f_v\in L^1(dz)fv​∈L1(dz). Since ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ has density e−W/ZNe^{-W}/Z_Ne−W/ZN​ with 0<ZN<∞0<Z_N<\infty0<ZN​<∞, ∫∣LWφ∣ dΠN≤ZN−1∑r∫∣fer∣ dz<∞\int|\mathcal L_W\varphi|\,d\Pi_N\le Z_N^{-1}\sum_r\int|f_{e_r}|\,dz<\infty∫∣LW​φ∣dΠN​≤ZN−1​∑r​∫∣fer​​∣dz<∞, and multiplying by 1N\frac1NN1​ preserves integrability.

Integration by parts. The function gv=e−W∂vφg_v=e^{-W}\partial_v\varphigv​=e−W∂v​φ is differentiable with compact support in KKK, and by the product and chain rules

∂vgv=e−W∂v∂vφ−e−W⟨∇W,v⟩∂vφ=fv.\partial_vg_v=e^{-W}\partial_v\partial_v\varphi-e^{-W}\langle\nabla W,v\rangle\partial_v\varphi=f_v .∂v​gv​=e−W∂v​∂v​φ−e−W⟨∇W,v⟩∂v​φ=fv​.

Choose orthonormal coordinates with vvv as the first axis. By Fubini's theorem (applicable since fvf_vfv​ is integrable) and the fundamental theorem of calculus on each line parallel to vvv, ∫fv dz=∫(∫R∂tgv(z′+tv) dt)dz′=0\int f_v\,dz=\int\bigl(\int_{\mathbb R}\partial_tg_v(z'+tv)\,dt\bigr)dz'=0∫fv​dz=∫(∫R​∂t​gv​(z′+tv)dt)dz′=0, because t↦gv(z′+tv)t\mapsto g_v(z'+tv)t↦gv​(z′+tv) vanishes for ∣t∣|t|∣t∣ large. Summing over rrr gives ∫e−WLWφ dz=0\int e^{-W}\mathcal L_W\varphi\,dz=0∫e−WLW​φdz=0.

Conclusion. ∫LWφ dΠN=ZN−1∫e−WLWφ dz=0\int\mathcal L_W\varphi\,d\Pi_N=Z_N^{-1}\int e^{-W}\mathcal L_W\varphi\,dz=0∫LW​φdΠN​=ZN−1​∫e−WLW​φdz=0, and therefore ∫ANφ dΠN=1N⋅0=0\int\mathcal A_N\varphi\,d\Pi_N=\frac1N\cdot0=0∫AN​φdΠN​=N1​⋅0=0. □\square□

The integrability statement is part of the conclusion, so the vanishing integral is a genuine computation. Only the first derivative of UUU, through ∇VN\nabla V_N∇VN​, enters; no Hessian of UUU is assumed. Infinitesimal invariance concerns the generator on test functions; that the law of a solution of the diffusion started at ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ stays equal to ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ is a different statement, proved for the Langevin reference process in the Langevin library.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

gibbsTarget_infinitesimalInvariant is stated for a family of potentials U : Data → Joint dθ dx → ℝ at a fixed datum y, with hypotheses hN : 0 < N, hm : 0 < m, hdiff, hsc and hLip on U y. The conclusion is the conjunction (∧) of IsProbabilityMeasure (@gibbsTarget N dθ dx (U y)) and, for every φ with ContDiff ℝ 2 φ and HasCompactSupport φ, of Integrable (diffusionGenerator (U y) φ) (gibbsTarget (U y)) and the vanishing integral.

  1. refine ⟨gibbsTarget_isProbability hN (U y) m hm hdiff hsc, ?_⟩ proves the first conjunct.
  2. After intro φ hφ hc and rw [gibbsTarget_eq_gibbsMeasure], constructor splits the two remaining claims.
  3. Integrability: the library theorem ULA.integrable_langevinGenerator_gibbsMeasure, applied to potential (U y) with the Lipschitz constant (N : ℝ≥0) * L (hypotheses potential_differentiable hdiff and potential_gradient_lipschitz hdiff hLip), gives integrability of LVNφ\mathcal L_{V_N}\varphiLVN​​φ; .const_mul _ multiplies by 1/N1/N1/N.
  4. Vanishing integral: simp only [diffusionGenerator, integral_const_mul, ULA.integral_langevinGenerator_gibbs_eq_zero ..., mul_zero] pulls the constant 1/N1/N1/N out of the integral (integral_const_mul), replaces ∫LVNφ dΠN\int\mathcal L_{V_N}\varphi\,d\Pi_N∫LVN​​φdΠN​ by 000 using the library's integration-by-parts theorem, and simplifies 1N⋅0=0\frac1N\cdot0=0N1​⋅0=0.

Mathlib's Bochner integral of a nonintegrable function is 000 by convention; the explicit integrability conjunct excludes that the identity holds for this trivial reason.
