01The marginal likelihood and its maximizer §

Mathematical statement and proof

The pairing map forms pair⁡(θ,x)=(θ,x)\operatorname{pair}(\theta,x)=(\theta,x)pair(θ,x)=(θ,x) in the orthogonal joint space. The unnormalized parameter marginal is

M(θ)=∫Rdxe−U(θ,x) dx.M(\theta)=\int_{\mathbb R^{d_x}}e^{-U(\theta,x)}\,dx.M(θ)=∫Rdx​​e−U(θ,x)dx.

The definition uses the nonnegative extended integral, so it remains meaningful before finiteness has been established. A marginal maximizer is a point θ∗\theta_*θ∗​ such that M(θ)≤M(θ∗)M(\theta)\le M(\theta_*)M(θ)≤M(θ∗​) for every θ\thetaθ. If U=−log⁡pθ(x,y)U=-\log p_\theta(x,y)U=−logpθ​(x,y), this is maximum marginal likelihood; a prior included in UUU gives the corresponding MAP interpretation. The deprecated name is an abbreviation for exactly the same predicate.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • pair: The original parameter/latent pair (theta,x), parameter first. For fixed observations y, the standard IPLA potential is U y (pair theta x) = -log p_theta(x,y). The geometry and dynamics work with the fixed slice U y.
  • marginalDensity: Unnormalized marginal density of the parameter, integrating out the latent variable. For U_y(theta,x) = -log p_theta(x,y), this is the marginal likelihood p_theta(y). A parameter prior may instead be included in U_y.
  • IsMarginalMaximizer: The parameter maximizes the integrated joint density. This is maximum likelihood in standard IPLA notation, and MAP when the parameter prior is included in U. Its existence is not asserted by this definition.
  • IsMarginalMAP: Compatibility name for the prior-inclusive formulation.

02The actual Gibbs target and all Gaussian assumptions §

Mathematical statement and proof

For VNV_NVN​ defined by the geometry module, the target is ΠN=ZN−1e−VN dz\Pi_N=Z_N^{-1}e^{-V_N}\,dzΠN​=ZN−1​e−VN​dz, with ZN=∫e−VN dzZ_N=\int e^{-V_N}\,dzZN​=∫e−VN​dz. Both normalization and density are initially extended-valued; this definition alone asserts no probability property. The Gaussian innovation structure asks that every coordinate ξk,a\xi_{k,a}ξk,a​ have law N(0,1)\mathcal N(0,1)N(0,1), that all coordinates at all times be mutually independent, and that the entire noise path be independent of the initialization. These are explicit distributional hypotheses on the supplied random variables.

The Gaussian structure concerns the entire countable collection (k,a)↦ξk,a(k,a)\mapsto\xi_{k,a}(k,a)↦ξk,a​, not only coordinate independence at one time. The separate initialization field states independence of Z0Z_0Z0​ from the whole innovation path. Together these conditions imply that every transition receives a fresh full state-space standard Gaussian. They are the probabilistic assumptions later used to equate a supplied implementation with the canonical Markov laws; moment membership in L2L^2L2 alone would not establish either the Gaussian distribution or independence.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • gibbsTarget: The normalized density in (49), with U_N the actual lifted potential. The integral and scalar are extended-valued. gibbsTarget_isProbability derives positive finite normalization from the original joint strong convexity, before any conversion to real numbers.
  • GaussianInnovations: The actual update noise is independent standard Gaussian in every coordinate and time, and independent of initialization. These are distributional hypotheses, not a claim to have constructed a random sequence.

03The law of the L² recursion §

Mathematical statement and proof

A square-integrable initial state and square-integrable noise vectors lie in L2(P)L^2(P)L2(P). With h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N, define the random sequence through the shared ULA recursion

Zk+1=Zk−h∇VN(Zk)+2h ξk,ρk=(Zk)#P.Z_{k+1}=Z_k-h\nabla V_N(Z_k)+\sqrt{2h}\,\xi_k,\qquad \rho_k=(Z_k)_\#P.Zk+1​=Zk​−h∇VN​(Zk​)+2h​ξk​,ρk​=(Zk​)#​P.

The Lipschitz estimate for ∇VN\nabla V_N∇VN​ ensures the drift update stays in L2L^2L2. The definition of laws is precisely this pushforward. The dynamics module proves that its representatives agree almost everywhere with the ordinary pointwise IPLA recursion, so the eventual theorem concerns this actual algorithm.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • laws: The laws of the actual noise-driven IPLA recursion, in scaled coordinates. The coordinate identities in Geometry.lean show that this is precisely the usual average-gradient parameter step and ordinary particle Langevin steps.
