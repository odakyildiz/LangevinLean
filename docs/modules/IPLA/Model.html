The statistical target, the Gibbs measure and the Gaussian innovations

References and publication status for this development

Published source and unpublished extension

Source: Theorem 1, equation (34), and Appendix B of arXiv:2409.07101v2 (27 May 2025), using the ULA estimate of Dalalyan and Karagulyan. The independent parameter- and latent-dimension extension and its proof presented here are unpublished; equal dimensions recover the paper’s displayed bound. The assumption audit explains the joint-potential smoothness condition.

This section fixes the objects that appear in the convergence theorem for the interacting particle Langevin algorithm. For fixed data yyy let U=Uy:Rdθ×Rdx→RU=U_y:\mathbb R^{d_\theta}\times\mathbb R^{d_x}\to\mathbb RU=Uy​:Rdθ​×Rdx​→R be the joint potential, in the standard model Uy(θ,x)=−log⁡pθ(x,y)U_y(\theta,x)=-\log p_\theta(x,y)Uy​(θ,x)=−logpθ​(x,y). The statistical target is a maximizer of the marginal density

ℓ(θ)=∫Rdxe−U(θ,x) dx∈[0,∞],\ell(\theta)=\int_{\mathbb R^{d_x}}e^{-U(\theta,x)}\,dx\in[0,\infty],ℓ(θ)=∫Rdx​​e−U(θ,x)dx∈[0,∞],

which is the marginal likelihood pθ(y)p_\theta(y)pθ​(y) in the standard model, or the unnormalized posterior density when a prior is included in UUU. The sampling target is the Gibbs measure ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ on the scaled state space Z=Rdθ×(Rdx)N\mathcal Z=\mathbb R^{d_\theta}\times(\mathbb R^{d_x})^NZ=Rdθ​×(Rdx​)N with density proportional to e−VNe^{-V_N}e−VN​, where VN(z)=∑iU(θ,Nzi)V_N(z)=\sum_iU(\theta,\sqrt Nz_i)VN​(z)=∑i​U(θ,N​zi​) is the lifted potential of the previous section. The algorithm is driven by an initial state and innovations whose scalar coordinates are independent standard Gaussian variables, independent of the initial state; its iterates are defined as square-integrable random vectors and their laws ρk\rho_kρk​ are the measures compared with ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ in the main theorem.

Only definitions are made here. That ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ is a probability measure, that a maximizer of ℓ\ellℓ exists and is unique, and that the laws ρk\rho_kρk​ are those of the explicit particle recursion are proved in later sections.

01The marginal density and its maximizers §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definitions. For θ∈Rdθ\theta\in\mathbb R^{d_\theta}θ∈Rdθ​ and x∈Rdxx\in\mathbb R^{d_x}x∈Rdx​, (θ,x)(\theta,x)(θ,x) denotes the element of the pair space J=Rdθ×Rdx\mathcal J=\mathbb R^{d_\theta}\times\mathbb R^{d_x}J=Rdθ​×Rdx​ with parameter block θ\thetaθ and latent block xxx. Let U:J→RU:\mathcal J\to\mathbb RU:J→R be any function. The marginal density of UUU is

ℓ(θ)=∫Rdxe−U(θ,x) dx ∈[0,∞],\ell(\theta)=\int_{\mathbb R^{d_x}}e^{-U(\theta,x)}\,dx\ \in[0,\infty],ℓ(θ)=∫Rdx​​e−U(θ,x)dx ∈[0,∞],

the integral of a nonnegative function with respect to Lebesgue measure on Rdx\mathbb R^{d_x}Rdx​, taken in the extended half-line [0,∞][0,\infty][0,∞]. (For a function that is not measurable this is the lower integral, the supremum of the integrals of nonnegative simple measurable functions below it; for continuous UUU it is the ordinary Lebesgue integral.) No finiteness or positivity of ℓ\ellℓ is assumed. A parameter θ⋆\theta_\starθ⋆​ is a marginal maximizer if

ℓ(θ)≤ℓ(θ⋆)for every θ∈Rdθ,\ell(\theta)\le\ell(\theta_\star)\qquad\text{for every }\theta\in\mathbb R^{d_\theta},ℓ(θ)≤ℓ(θ⋆​)for every θ∈Rdθ​,

the comparison taking place in [0,∞][0,\infty][0,∞].

Interpretation. If U(θ,x)=−log⁡pθ(x,y)U(\theta,x)=-\log p_\theta(x,y)U(θ,x)=−logpθ​(x,y) for a joint density pθ(x,y)p_\theta(x,y)pθ​(x,y) of latent variables and data, then ℓ(θ)=∫pθ(x,y) dx=pθ(y)\ell(\theta)=\int p_\theta(x,y)\,dx=p_\theta(y)ℓ(θ)=∫pθ​(x,y)dx=pθ​(y) is the marginal likelihood and a marginal maximizer is a maximum marginal likelihood estimator. If UUU also contains a negative log prior −log⁡p(θ)-\log p(\theta)−logp(θ), then ℓ(θ)=p(θ)pθ(y)\ell(\theta)=p(\theta)p_\theta(y)ℓ(θ)=p(θ)pθ​(y) is proportional to the posterior density of θ\thetaθ and a marginal maximizer is a maximum a posteriori estimator. In both cases the latent variable is integrated out; a marginal maximizer is in general different from the parameter component of a minimizer of (θ,x)↦U(θ,x)(\theta,x)\mapsto U(\theta,x)(θ,x)↦U(θ,x).

The definition does not assert that a marginal maximizer exists. Under the hypotheses of the main theorem (differentiability, joint strong convexity and a Lipschitz gradient of UUU) it is shown later that 0<ℓ<∞0<\ell<\infty0<ℓ<∞, that −log⁡ℓ-\log\ell−logℓ is strongly convex and continuous, and hence that a marginal maximizer exists and is unique.

03The laws of the square-integrable particle recursion §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definition. Let P\mathbb PP be a probability measure, γ∈R\gamma\in\mathbb Rγ∈R, L≥0L\ge0L≥0, and let UUU be differentiable with LLL-Lipschitz gradient.
