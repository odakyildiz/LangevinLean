01Join integral solutions and identify their Euler noises §

Mathematical statement and proof

For each interval [kh,(k+1)h][kh,(k+1)h][kh,(k+1)h], solve the Lipschitz-drift integral equation in L2L^2L2, starting from the preceding endpoint:

Yk,t−Yk,0=−∫0t∇V(Yk,s)ds+2(Bkh+t−Bkh).Y_{k,t}-Y_{k,0}=-\int_0^t\nabla V(Y_{k,s})ds+\sqrt2\bigl(B_{kh+t}-B_{kh}\bigr).Yk,t​−Yk,0​=−∫0t​∇V(Yk,s​)ds+2​(Bkh+t​−Bkh​).

The construction proves continuity, the initial value and interval joining. Normalize each Brownian endpoint by 2h\sqrt{2h}2h​ to obtain the same Euler noise vector, and use Gaussian second moments to get the noise norm 2dt\sqrt{2d}\sqrt t2d​t​. Given the explicit all-time Gibbs-law premise, the Gibbs score bound yields ∥∇V(Yk,t)∥L2≤Ld\|\nabla V(Y_{k,t})\|_{L^2}\le\sqrt{Ld}∥∇V(Yk,t​)∥L2​≤Ld​, assembling the shared integral-reference structure. This layer keeps stationarity visible as a premise, rather than claiming the construction alone proves it.
