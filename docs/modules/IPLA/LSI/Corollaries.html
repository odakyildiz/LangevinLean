Conditional entropy bounds for the actual parameter marginal

References and publication status for this development

Unpublished proof

The IPLA LSI proof presented here is unpublished. It derives the joint entropy bound, per-particle normalization, and parameter-marginal consequence by applying the shared ULA entropy theorem to the actual lifted IPLA target. The papers below are background references for that construction.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices
  • Alex Glyn-Davies, Connor Duffin, Ieva Kazlauskaite, Mark Girolami, and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Statistical Finite Elements via Interacting Particle Langevin Dynamics. SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification 13(3), 1200–1227. Audited version: arXiv:2409.07101v2

Combine the finite joint entropy result with measurable parameter projection. Every conclusion retains the lifted-target LSI and the explicit regular interpolation data. The initialization term remains the full joint initial divergence.

01The actual parameter divergence is finite §

Mathematical statement and proof

The particle-law probability theorem proves ρk\rho_kρk​ is a probability, and the LSI hypothesis proves ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ is a probability. The supplied interpolation data prove finite actual joint KL for γ≥0\gamma\ge0γ≥0. Apply the Gibbs-specific finiteness transfer to obtain KL⁡(pθ#ρk∥ΠNθ)<∞\operatorname{KL}(p_{\theta\#}\rho_k\Vert\Pi_N^\theta)<\inftyKL(pθ#​ρk​∥ΠNθ​)<∞. This is an extended-valued assertion, proved before any real-valued inequality.

Under N>0N>0N>0, β>0\beta>0β>0, β≤NL\beta\le NLβ≤NL, and 0<γ≤β/(4NL2)0<\gamma\le\beta/(4NL^2)0<γ≤β/(4NL2), the joint theorem gives Hk≤e−βγk/NH0+8γNDL2/βH_k\le e^{-\beta\gamma k/N}H_0+8\gamma ND L^2/\betaHk​≤e−βγk/NH0​+8γNDL2/β. The finite joint guard and the Gibbs projection bound yield

03Retain N when using the normalized linear-LSI bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

If the actual lifted LSI constant is NαN\alphaNα, the normalized joint theorem gives

HkN≤e−αγkH0N+8γL2α(dx+dθN).\frac{H_k}{N}\le e^{-\alpha\gamma k}\frac{H_0}{N}+\frac{8\gamma L^2}{\alpha}\left(d_x+\frac{d_\theta}{N}\right).NHk​​≤e−αγkNH0​​+α8γL2​(dx​+Ndθ​​).

The per-particle transfer lemma multiplies this complete upper bound by positive NNN, so the unscaled parameter divergence is at most

N[e−αγkH0N+8γL2α(dx+dθN)].N\left[e^{-\alpha\gamma k}\frac{H_0}{N}+\frac{8\gamma L^2}{\alpha}\left(d_x+\frac{d_\theta}{N}\right)\right].N[e−αγkNH0​​+α8γL2​(dx​+Ndθ​​)].

The proof obtains finite joint KL separately and then applies this guarded transfer. This entropy estimate compares two parameter probability laws; no concentration at a marginal-likelihood maximizer is asserted.

The proof first installs probability instances for the actual particle law and Gibbs target, using kernel mass preservation and the LSI hypothesis's normalization field. It next supplies the proved finite joint KL to the real data-processing theorem. In the general case it transfers the full joint bound directly. In the NαN\alphaNα case it transfers the normalized bound by multiplying its entire right side by NNN: KL⁡(p#ρk∥p#ΠN)≤N[e−αγkH0N+8γL2α(dx+dθ/N)].\operatorname{KL}(p_\#\rho_k\Vert p_\#\Pi_N)\le N\left[e^{-\alpha\gamma k}\frac{H_0}{N}+\frac{8\gamma L^2}{\alpha}(d_x+d_\theta/N)\right].KL(p#​ρk​∥p#​ΠN​)≤N[e−αγkNH0​​+α8γL2​(dx​+dθ​/N)]. The lifted LSI and regular interpolation hypotheses remain exactly those of the joint theorem.
