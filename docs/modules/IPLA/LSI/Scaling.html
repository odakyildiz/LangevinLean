Exact entropy scaling with an explicit lifted LSI constant

References and publication status for this development

Unpublished proof

The IPLA LSI proof presented here is unpublished. It derives the joint entropy bound, per-particle normalization, and parameter-marginal consequence by applying the shared ULA entropy theorem to the actual lifted IPLA target. The papers below are background references for that construction.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices
  • Alex Glyn-Davies, Connor Duffin, Ieva Kazlauskaite, Mark Girolami, and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Statistical Finite Elements via Interacting Particle Langevin Dynamics. SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification 13(3), 1200–1227. Audited version: arXiv:2409.07101v2

Keep the LSI constant β\betaβ of the actual lifted target explicit. Substitute h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N, ℓ=NL\ell=NLℓ=NL, D=dθ+NdxD=d_\theta+Nd_xD=dθ​+Ndx​ into the shared ULA entropy argument. The regime β=Nα\beta=N\alphaβ=Nα is an additional lifted-target assumption, with its own scalar specializations.

01Translate the numerical step and compatibility conditions §

Mathematical statement and proof

For N>0N>0N>0, L>0L>0L>0, factor the denominator and cancel positive NNN to get

γN≤β4(NL)2⟺γ≤β4NL2.\frac\gamma N\le\frac\beta{4(NL)^2}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\gamma\le\frac\beta{4NL^2}.Nγ​≤4(NL)2β​⟺γ≤4NL2β​.

If β=Nα\beta=N\alphaβ=Nα, multiplication by positive NNN gives Nα≤NL⟺α≤LN\alpha\le NL\Longleftrightarrow\alpha\le LNα≤NL⟺α≤L, and cancellation gives Nα/(4NL2)=α/(4L2)N\alpha/(4NL^2)=\alpha/(4L^2)Nα/(4NL2)=α/(4L2). The forward compatibility lemma simply uses the equivalence. These are exact ordered-field identities; they do not derive an LSI for the lifted model from any pair-model LSI.

The general lifted theorem uses h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N, score constant LN=NLL_N=NLLN​=NL, dimension D=dθ+NdxD=d_\theta+Nd_xD=dθ​+Ndx​, and an LSI constant β\betaβ for the actual lifted Gibbs target. Thus h≤β/(4LN2)h\le\beta/(4L_N^2)h≤β/(4LN2​) becomes γ≤β/(4NL2)\gamma\le\beta/(4NL^2)γ≤β/(4NL2). Only after additionally assuming β=Nα\beta=N\alphaβ=Nα can the step restriction simplify to γ≤α/(4L2)\gamma\le\alpha/(4L^2)γ≤α/(4L2). Positive NNN justifies these cancellations; no product tensorization is used, since the pair potentials share a parameter.

02Rewrite the contraction exponent §

Mathematical statement and proof

Associativity of real multiplication and division gives −β(γ/N)k=−βγk/N-\beta(\gamma/N)k=-\beta\gamma k/N−β(γ/N)k=−βγk/N, so the corresponding exponentials agree. When β=Nα\beta=N\alphaβ=Nα and N>0N>0N>0, cancellation yields

e−βγk/N=e−αγk,e−(Nα)(γ/N)k=e−αγk.e^{-\beta\gamma k/N}=e^{-\alpha\gamma k},\qquad e^{-(N\alpha)(\gamma/N)k}=e^{-\alpha\gamma k}.e−βγk/N=e−αγk,e−(Nα)(γ/N)k=e−αγk.

The second version composes the first two identities. The rate becomes independent of NNN only under this explicitly supplied linear scaling of the actual lifted LSI constant.

03Rewrite full and normalized entropy biases §

Mathematical statement and proof

The shared ULA bias is 8hDℓ2/β8hD\ell^2/\beta8hDℓ2/β. Substitute the lifted values and cancel one particle factor:

8(γ/N)D(NL)2β=8γNDL2β,1N8γNDL2β=8γDL2β.\frac{8(\gamma/N)D(NL)^2}{\beta}=\frac{8\gamma ND L^2}{\beta},\qquad \frac1N\frac{8\gamma ND L^2}{\beta}=\frac{8\gamma DL^2}{\beta}.β8(γ/N)D(NL)2​=β8γNDL2​,N1​β8γNDL2​=β8γDL2​.

The dimension identity D/N=dx+dθ/ND/N=d_x+d_\theta/ND/N=dx​+dθ​/N follows by distributing division over the sum. If β=Nα\beta=N\alphaβ=Nα, the normalized bias becomes

8γDL2Nα=8γL2α(dx+dθN).\frac{8\gamma DL^2}{N\alpha}=\frac{8\gamma L^2}{\alpha}\left(d_x+\frac{d_\theta}{N}\right).Nα8γDL2​=α8γL2​(dx​+Ndθ​​).

The formal algebra handles α=0\alpha=0α=0 separately because real division is total; applications use α>0\alpha>0α>0. For any bound H≤qH0+8γNDL2/βH\le qH_0+8\gamma ND L^2/\betaH≤qH0​+8γNDL2/β, divide by positive NNN, distribute, and use the second identity to obtain the normalized entropy bound.

The full-state bias is 8hDLN2/β=8γNDL2/β8hDL_N^2/\beta=8\gamma NDL^2/\beta8hDLN2​/β=8γNDL2/β; dividing entropy by NNN removes exactly one particle factor. Under β=Nα\beta=N\alphaβ=Nα, its intensive form becomes (8γL2/α)(dx+dθ/N)(8\gamma L^2/\alpha)(d_x+d_\theta/N)(8γL2/α)(dx​+dθ​/N). The initial entropy is divided by NNN at the same time. The one-step forcing is different: 6h2DLN2=6γ2DL26h^2DL_N^2=6\gamma^2DL^26h2DLN2​=6γ2DL2, so its normalized form is 6γ2L2(dx+dθ/N)6\gamma^2L^2(d_x+d_\theta/N)6γ2L2(dx​+dθ​/N). Keeping these full and intensive quantities separate avoids losing a factor of NNN.

04Rewrite the one-step forcing and normalized recurrence §

Mathematical statement and proof

The squared step cancels both particle factors in the squared smoothness:

6(γ/N)2D(NL)2=6γ2DL2.6(\gamma/N)^2D(NL)^2=6\gamma^2DL^2.6(γ/N)2D(NL)2=6γ2DL2.

Dividing by NNN and substituting the intensive dimension gives 6γ2DL2/N=6γ2L2(dx+dθ/N)6\gamma^2DL^2/N=6\gamma^2L^2(d_x+d_\theta/N)6γ2DL2/N=6γ2L2(dx​+dθ​/N). Thus any supplied lifted recurrence normalizes to

Hk+1N≤e−βγ/NHkN+6γ2DL2N.\frac{H_{k+1}}N\le e^{-\beta\gamma/N}\frac{H_k}N+\frac{6\gamma^2DL^2}{N}.NHk+1​​≤e−βγ/NNHk​​+N6γ2DL2​.

This scalar helper explicitly assumes its recurrence. Its proof divides by positive NNN, rewrites the exponential argument, and applies the forcing identity. Under β=Nα\beta=N\alphaβ=Nα, the rate and intensive-dimension identities give the specialized recurrence with rate e−αγe^{-\alpha\gamma}e−αγ and forcing 6γ2L2(dx+dθ/N)6\gamma^2L^2(d_x+d_\theta/N)6γ2L2(dx​+dθ​/N). The analytic theorem derives the recurrence separately from interpolation data.
