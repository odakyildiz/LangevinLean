Particle-entropy convergence from the lifted logarithmic Sobolev inequality

References and publication status for this development

Unpublished proof

The IPLA LSI proof presented here is unpublished. It applies the ULA entropy theorem to the joint particle Gibbs measure, proves the joint entropy estimate and its normalization by particle number, and bounds the parameter-marginal entropy by data processing. The papers below provide the ULA and IPLA results used in the argument.

  • Santosh S. Vempala and Andre Wibisono (2019). Rapid Convergence of the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm: Isoperimetry Suffices. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32, 8094–8106. Full paper with appendices
  • Alex Glyn-Davies, Connor Duffin, Ieva Kazlauskaite, Mark Girolami, and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Statistical Finite Elements via Interacting Particle Langevin Dynamics. SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification 13(3), 1200–1227. Audited version: arXiv:2409.07101v2

The Gaussian interpolation theorem derives the required density, differentiation, and integrability properties from the entropy assumptions. For the particle model, substitute VNV_NVN​, step γ/N\gamma/Nγ/N, gradient constant NLNLNL, and dimension D=dθ+NdxD=d_\theta+Nd_xD=dθ​+Ndx​. The lifted-target logarithmic Sobolev inequality, positive admissible step, and finite initial joint entropy then imply finite output entropy and the quantitative estimate. Division by particle count and parameter projection give the stated corollaries.

01Finite joint entropy and its convergence estimate §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let N>0N>0N>0, D=dθ+NdxD=d_\theta+Nd_xD=dθ​+Ndx​, and VN(z)=∑iU(θ,Nzi)V_N(z)=\sum_iU(\theta,\sqrt N z_i)VN​(z)=∑i​U(θ,N​zi​). Assume differentiability of UUU with LLL-Lipschitz gradient, a logarithmic Sobolev inequality of constant β>0\beta>0β>0 for ΠN\Pi_NΠN​, a positive physical step satisfying γ≤β/(4NL2)\gamma\le\beta/(4NL^2)γ≤β/(4NL2), and finite initial joint entropy H0H_0H0​. Every particle law then has finite divergence and satisfies

Hk≤e−βγk/NH0+8γNDL2β.H_k\le e^{-\beta\gamma k/N}H_0+\frac{8\gamma ND L^2}{\beta}.Hk​≤e−βγk/NH0​+β8γNDL2​.

The lifted potential is differentiable with NLNLNL-Lipschitz gradient, and its Gaussian Euler law at step h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N is the particle law. The physical restriction is equivalent to h≤β/(4(NL)2)h\le\beta/(4(NL)^2)h≤β/(4(NL)2). Thus the Gaussian interpolation theorem applies with h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N, dimension DDD, and gradient constant NLNLNL. Its rate is e−βγk/Ne^{-\beta\gamma k/N}e−βγk/N and its error is 8γNDL2/β8\gamma ND L^2/\beta8γNDL2/β. The admissible positive step also gives positive LLL before any division.

In detail, the squared gradient constant is ℓ2=N2L2\ell^2=N^2L^2ℓ2=N2L2 and the step is h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N. These substitutions give restriction γ≤β/(4NL2)\gamma\le\beta/(4NL^2)γ≤β/(4NL2) and error term 8γNDL2/β8\gamma NDL^2/\beta8γNDL2/β. The Gaussian theorem supplies the interpolation regularity and the compatibility of constants, so neither is an additional premise here. The finite-divergence conclusion is retained alongside the real inequality, justifying each real-valued entropy in the formula.

Earlier results used here: ULA-LSI, Section 3.1.3; ULA-LSI, Section 3.29.7; IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.1; IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.3; IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.4; IPLA-SC, Section 3.3.2.

No regular-interpolation data is an additional premise here.

02The entropy bound divided by particle count §

Mathematical statement and proof

Divide the finite joint entropy inequality by positive NNN. Division preserves order and gives

HkN≤e−βγk/NH0N+8γDL2β.\frac{H_k}{N}\le e^{-\beta\gamma k/N}\frac{H_0}{N}+\frac{8\gamma D L^2}{\beta}.NHk​​≤e−βγk/NNH0​​+β8γDL2​.

Because N>0N>0N>0, every term is divided by the same finite positive number. The exponential is unchanged, and its initial factor is the initial joint entropy per particle. This is a numerical normalization of the joint divergence, not the divergence of the parameter marginal.

Earlier results used here: ULA-LSI, Section 3.1.3; IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.1; IPLA-SC, Section 3.3.2.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

particle_kl_bound_normalized_of_finite_kl obtains the finite joint divergence hfinite and full bound hb from particle_kl_bound_of_finite_kl. It keeps the former as the first component of its conclusion and applies normalize_entropy_bound to the latter. Thus every term of the full inequality, including the initial entropy, is divided by the same positive particle count.

The helper normalize_entropy_bound divides both the initial contribution and the bias, canceling one particle factor in the latter. The same finite-KL witness is retained for the unnormalized divergence. This quantity is the entropy of the full state divided by particle number; parameter projection is handled separately.

03A linearly scaled lifted logarithmic Sobolev constant §

Mathematical statement and proof

Assume the lifted Gibbs target has logarithmic Sobolev constant β=Nα\beta=N\alphaβ=Nα with α>0\alpha>0α>0. Positivity of particle count gives β>0\beta>0β>0, and the restriction γ≤β/(4NL2)\gamma\le\beta/(4NL^2)γ≤β/(4NL2) becomes γ≤α/(4L2)\gamma\le\alpha/(4L^2)γ≤α/(4L2). Apply the normalized joint theorem with this constant. Its rate is e−αγke^{-\alpha\gamma k}e−αγk. The dimension equality D/N=dx+dθ/ND/N=d_x+d_\theta/ND/N=dx​+dθ​/N gives error term (8γL2/α)(dx+dθ/N)(8\gamma L^2/\alpha)(d_x+d_\theta/N)(8γL2/α)(dx​+dθ​/N), so

HkN≤e−αγkH0N+8γL2α(dx+dθN).\frac{H_k}{N}\le e^{-\alpha\gamma k}\frac{H_0}{N}+\frac{8\gamma L^2}{\alpha}\left(d_x+\frac{d_\theta}{N}\right).NHk​​≤e−αγkNH0​​+α8γL2​(dx​+Ndθ​​).

The functional-inequality premise concerns ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ itself. The relation D/N=dx+dθ/ND/N=d_x+d_\theta/ND/N=dx​+dθ​/N separates the intensive latent and parameter contributions. Since the particles share a parameter, no product tensorization of independent pair targets is used to obtain the assumed constant.

Earlier results used here: ULA-LSI, Section 3.1.3; ULA-LSI, Section 3.29.1; IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.1; IPLA-SC, Section 3.3.2.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

particle_kl_bound_linear_lsi_of_finite_kl proves positive smoothness using ULA.LSI.positive_smoothness_of_step, then rewrites the step assumption with linear_lsi_step_iff. It applies the normalized theorem at the supplied linearly scaled lifted-target constant. The final rewrites linear_entropy_rate_eq and linear_normalized_entropy_bias_eq give the stated exponent and effective dimension while retaining finite joint divergence.

04The parameter-entropy consequence §

Mathematical statement and proof

Project both the particle law and its Gibbs reference to their parameter coordinates. Data processing gives KL⁡(μkθ∥ΠNθ)≤Hk\operatorname{KL}(\mu_k^\theta\Vert\Pi_N^\theta)\le H_kKL(μkθ​∥ΠNθ​)≤Hk​. The joint theorem supplies Hk<∞H_k<\inftyHk​<∞, so the projected divergence is finite as well. Substitution of the full numerical estimate yields

KL⁡(μkθ∥ΠNθ)≤e−βγk/NH0+8γNDL2β.\operatorname{KL}(\mu_k^\theta\Vert\Pi_N^\theta)\le e^{-\beta\gamma k/N}H_0+\frac{8\gamma ND L^2}{\beta}.KL(μkθ​∥ΠNθ​)≤e−βγk/NH0​+β8γNDL2​.

The initial quantity is the full joint entropy H0H_0H0​. The parameter divergence is unnormalized, so its error retains factor NNN. If a joint bound is supplied per particle, it must first be multiplied by NNN. Data processing changes only the left-hand divergence and does not replace the initial joint entropy by a smaller projected quantity.

Earlier results used here: ULA-LSI, Section 3.1.3; IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.1; IPLA-SC, Section 3.1.6; IPLA-SC, Section 3.3.2.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

parameter_kl_bound_of_finite_kl installs probability instances for the numerical and target laws, then obtains hfinite and hb from the full joint entropy theorem. parameter_kl_ne_top_of_gibbs_joint supplies finite marginal divergence and parameter_kl_bound_of_gibbs_joint_bound transfers the full bound through the parameter projection. It is the unnormalized joint estimate that is passed to data processing.
