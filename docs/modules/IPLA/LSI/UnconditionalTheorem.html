Unconditional IPLA entropy from the actual lifted LSI

The actual IPLA state laws are ULA laws for the scaled potential VNV_NVN​, with step γ/N\gamma/Nγ/N, smoothness NLNLNL, and dimension D=dθ+NdxD=d_\theta+Nd_xD=dθ​+Ndx​. Applying the shared unconditional ULA theorem proves finite joint KL and its convergence estimate from smoothness, the actual lifted-target LSI, the positive step restriction, and finite initial KL. The module also derives normalized, linear-LSI, and parameter-projection consequences.

01Finite joint entropy and the full IPLA bound §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let N>0N>0N>0, D=dθ+NdxD=d_\theta+Nd_xD=dθ​+Ndx​, and VN(z)=∑iU(θ,Nzi)V_N(z)=\sum_iU(\theta,\sqrt N z_i)VN​(z)=∑i​U(θ,N​zi​). Assume differentiable UUU with LLL-Lipschitz gradient, a genuine LSI with constant β>0\beta>0β>0 for the actual lifted Gibbs target ΠN\Pi_NΠN​, a positive step γ≤β/(4NL2)\gamma\le\beta/(4NL^2)γ≤β/(4NL2), and finite initial joint entropy H0H_0H0​. Then every actual particle law has finite KL and

Hk≤e−βγk/NH0+8γNDL2β.H_k\le e^{-\beta\gamma k/N}H_0+\frac{8\gamma ND L^2}{\beta}.Hk​≤e−βγk/NH0​+β8γNDL2​.

The lifted potential is differentiable and has NLNLNL-Lipschitz gradient. The particle law is exactly its ULA law with step h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N. The step equivalence gives h≤β/(4(NL)2)h\le\beta/(4(NL)^2)h≤β/(4(NL)2), so the shared unconditional ULA theorem applies. Substituting h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N, dimension DDD, and smoothness NLNLNL transforms its rate into e−βγk/Ne^{-\beta\gamma k/N}e−βγk/N and its bias into 8γNDL2/β8\gamma ND L^2/\beta8γNDL2/β. The proof derives positive LLL from the positive admissible step before dividing.

The shared theorem constructs all Gaussian interpolation and entropy calculus. Neither compatibility nor regular interpolation data is a premise here. Finiteness is retained as the first component of the conclusion, before real-valued KL is used.

02Normalize the full joint entropy §

Mathematical statement and proof

Start with the finite full-state entropy theorem. Divide its real inequality by the positive number NNN, preserving the inequality direction:

HkN≤e−βγk/NH0N+8γDL2β.\frac{H_k}{N}\le e^{-\beta\gamma k/N}\frac{H_0}{N}+\frac{8\gamma D L^2}{\beta}.NHk​​≤e−βγk/NNH0​​+β8γDL2​.

The helper normalize_entropy_bound divides both the initial contribution and the bias, canceling one particle factor in the latter. The same finite-KL witness is retained for the unnormalized divergence. This quantity is the entropy of the full state divided by particle number; parameter projection is handled separately.

03An actual lifted LSI of strength N alpha §

Mathematical statement and proof

Suppose the actual lifted target satisfies LSI with constant β=Nα\beta=N\alphaβ=Nα, where α>0\alpha>0α>0. The positive particle count gives β>0\beta>0β>0, and the step equivalence simplifies γ≤β/(4NL2)\gamma\le\beta/(4NL^2)γ≤β/(4NL2) to γ≤α/(4L2)\gamma\le\alpha/(4L^2)γ≤α/(4L2). Apply the unconditional normalized theorem at this lifted constant.

The rate simplifies to e−αγke^{-\alpha\gamma k}e−αγk. Since D/N=dx+dθ/ND/N=d_x+d_\theta/ND/N=dx​+dθ​/N, its normalized bias becomes (8γL2/α)(dx+dθ/N)(8\gamma L^2/\alpha)(d_x+d_\theta/N)(8γL2/α)(dx​+dθ​/N). Thus

HkN≤e−αγkH0N+8γL2α(dx+dθN).\frac{H_k}{N}\le e^{-\alpha\gamma k}\frac{H_0}{N}+\frac{8\gamma L^2}{\alpha}\left(d_x+\frac{d_\theta}{N}\right).NHk​​≤e−αγkNH0​​+α8γL2​(dx​+Ndθ​​).

The LSI premise is for ΠN\Pi_NΠN​ itself. The proof performs these exact substitutions and retains finite output KL; it does not infer the lifted LSI by tensorizing a parameter–latent pair model.

04Project the actual joint law to the parameter §

Mathematical statement and proof

Apply the parameter projection to the actual particle law and to the same lifted Gibbs target. KL data processing gives KL⁡(μkθ∥ΠNθ)≤Hk\operatorname{KL}(\mu_k^\theta\Vert\Pi_N^\theta)\le H_kKL(μkθ​∥ΠNθ​)≤Hk​. The unconditional joint theorem first supplies Hk<∞H_k<\inftyHk​<∞, which also proves finiteness of the projected KL before real conversion.

Transitivity with the full joint estimate yields

KL⁡(μkθ∥ΠNθ)≤e−βγk/NH0+8γNDL2β.\operatorname{KL}(\mu_k^\theta\Vert\Pi_N^\theta)\le e^{-\beta\gamma k/N}H_0+\frac{8\gamma ND L^2}{\beta}.KL(μkθ​∥ΠNθ​)≤e−βγk/NH0​+β8γNDL2​.

The initial entropy on the right is the full joint H0H_0H0​. The bound is unnormalized, so the factor NNN in the bias is retained. A per-particle joint estimate must be multiplied by NNN before it bounds this unnormalized parameter divergence.
