Published source and unpublished extension

Source: Theorem 1, equation (34), and Appendix B of arXiv:2409.07101v2 (27 May 2025), using the ULA estimate of Dalalyan and Karagulyan. The independent parameter- and latent-dimension extension and its proof presented here are unpublished; equal dimensions recover the paper’s displayed bound.   • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv

Mathematical statement and proof

For a linear contraction TTT, any center bbb, and a coupling ccc of μ,π\mu,\piμ,π, write Tx−b=T(x−y)+(Ty−b)Tx-b=T(x-y)+(Ty-b)Tx−b=T(x−y)+(Ty−b). Minkowski and ∥T(x−y)∥≤∥x−y∥\|T(x-y)\|\le\|x-y\|∥T(x−y)∥≤∥x−y∥ imply

∥Tx−b∥L2(μ)≤cost⁡(c)+∥Ty−b∥L2(π).\|Tx-b\|_{L^2(\mu)}\le\operatorname{cost}(c)+\|Ty-b\|_{L^2(\pi)}.∥Tx−b∥L2(μ)​≤cost(c)+∥Ty−b∥L2(π)​.

Taking the infimum over couplings proves the same inequality with W2(μ,π)W_2(\mu,\pi)W2​(μ,π). Finite transport plus a finite equilibrium radius proves finiteness of the projected moment. Only then does the shared real conversion identify its value with ∫∥Tx−b∥2dμ\sqrt{\int\|Tx-b\|^2d\mu}∫∥Tx−b∥2dμ​. A second-moment bound at equilibrium supplies the concentration radius. No optimal coupling or separate Wasserstein triangle inequality is needed.
