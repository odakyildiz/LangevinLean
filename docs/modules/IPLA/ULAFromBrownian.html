Transport convergence from a Brownian reference

References and publication status for this development

Published source and unpublished extension

Source: Theorem 1, equation (34), and Appendix B of arXiv:2409.07101v2 (27 May 2025), using the ULA estimate of Dalalyan and Karagulyan. The independent parameter- and latent-dimension extension and its proof presented here are unpublished; equal dimensions recover the paper’s displayed bound. The assumption audit explains the joint-potential smoothness condition.

  • Alex Glyn-Davies, Connor Duffin, Ieva Kazlauskaite, Mark Girolami, and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2025). Statistical Finite Elements via Interacting Particle Langevin Dynamics. SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification 13(3), 1200–1227. Audited version: arXiv:2409.07101v2
  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik G. Karagulyan (2019). User-friendly guarantees for the Langevin Monte Carlo with inaccurate gradient. Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(12), 5278–5311. arXiv

Assume the Brownian reference satisfies the Langevin integral equation, stationarity, and the drift and noise moment estimates. Subtraction from the Euler update gives an affine recurrence for their mean-square discrepancy. Summation gives the finite-time coupling estimate. Equality of numerical marginals across initial couplings then permits the initial transport infimum.

This module exports the shared theorem that inserts a constructed Brownian reference into the analytic Dalalyan estimate. It adds no native declarations and no separate particle proof.

Mathematical statement and proof

Under the common-noise integral equation, stationary Gibbs law, and moment bounds, the discrepancy satisfies ek+1≤(1−mh)ek+(33/20)Lhhde_{k+1}\le(1-mh)e_k+(33/20)Lh\sqrt{hd}ek+1​≤(1−mh)ek​+(33/20)Lhhd​. Summation gives

W2(μk,π)≤(1−mh)ke0+3320Lmhd.W_2(\mu_k,\pi)\le(1-mh)^ke_0+\frac{33}{20}\frac Lm\sqrt{hd}.W2​(μk​,π)≤(1−mh)ke0​+2033​mL​hd​.

The common Gaussian increment cancels on subtraction. The remaining terms are the contractive gradient-step difference and the integral of the reference force minus its left-endpoint value. The local estimate bounds the latter uniformly on each interval. This intermediate result retains its reference-process assumptions. The Gaussian construction proves them and permits the initial coupling infimum. For IPLA the substitutions are V=VNV=V_NV=VN​, h=γ/Nh=\gamma/Nh=γ/N, mN=Nmm_N=NmmN​=Nm, and LN=NLL_N=NLLN​=NL. No invariant distribution of the discrete Euler chain is assumed.
