Statement and proof

01Brownian increments satisfy the complete algorithm noise specification §

Mathematical statement and proof

On the independent product of an initial coupling and a Brownian source, define ξk,a=(Ba((k+1)h)−Ba(kh))/h\xi_{k,a}=(B_a((k+1)h)-B_a(kh))/\sqrt hξk,a​=(Ba​((k+1)h)−Ba​(kh))/h​. Each Brownian coordinate has centered Gaussian increments of variance hhh, so each normalized coordinate has law N(0,1)\mathcal N(0,1)N(0,1). Independent Brownian coordinates and disjoint increments give mutual independence across coordinates and times. The product construction makes the complete noise path independent of the initial coupling. The shared product-noise theorems prove these three assertions, which are assembled into GaussianInnovations.

The local instance algorithmProductProbability follows because a coupling of probability measures has total mass one and the independent source law has mass one; their product therefore has total mass one. This justifies the probability-space hypotheses of the innovation and L² law theorems.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • productGaussianInnovations: The concrete product realization satisfies every distributional field of the actual IPLA algorithm. Standard Gaussian laws and all independence claims are conclusions from the independent Brownian coordinate source.

02Transfer the product realization to any implementation §

Mathematical statement and proof

The Brownian product's initial random variable has the prescribed law P∘Z0−1P\circ Z_0^{-1}P∘Z0−1​, and its innovations satisfy the Gaussian structure just proved. Implementation independence therefore identifies its IPLA law with any supplied Gaussian implementation having the same initial law. The shared product-chain law identity then gives

ρk=productOutputLaw⁡(VN,γ/N,μ0,k).\rho_k=\operatorname{productOutputLaw}(V_N,\gamma/N,\mu_0,k).ρk​=productOutputLaw(VN​,γ/N,μ0​,k).

Finite coupling cost and the target second moment are used here to construct the product initial state in L2L^2L2. Neither stationarity nor a reference diffusion is assumed for this law identity.

Canonical Brownian Euler increments are proved standard Gaussian in every coordinate, mutually independent across times and coordinates, and independent of the coupled initialization. These are exactly the three fields of GaussianInnovations. Their initial law agrees with the supplied initialization law on the product extension, so canonical-law uniqueness identifies the resulting algorithm output laws. The final Markov-step identity then shows that the actual supplied chain iterates the same ULA kernel at step γ/N\gamma/Nγ/N.

03The actual chain iterates the ULA Markov transition §

Mathematical statement and proof

The innovation hypotheses imply independent vector-valued Gaussian increments by grouping the scalar coordinate family. Applying the shared Hilbert-space law theorem to VNV_NVN​, step γ/N\gamma/Nγ/N, and smoothness NLNLNL gives

ρk=ulaLawStep⁡(VN,γ/N)[k](μ0).\rho_k=\operatorname{ulaLawStep}(V_N,\gamma/N)^{[k]}(\mu_0).ρk​=ulaLawStep(VN​,γ/N)[k](μ0​).

The continuous L2L^2L2 representatives are measurable; the initial state is independent of the full noise path by hypothesis. This exact transition identity transfers both convergence and finiteness statements from ULA to the actual IPLA laws.
