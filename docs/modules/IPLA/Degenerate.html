How the Lean proof follows this argument

zero_dimensions_bound takes μ π ν : Measure (State N 0 0) with [IsProbabilityMeasure μ] and [IsProbabilityMeasure π], and the real numbers m γ, L : ℝ≥0, θStar and k. Its right side is written literally with (0 : ℝ) in place of the dimensions, matching the specialization of the main theorem. The proof is simp [parameterRMS_zero_dimension, ULA.wasserstein2Real_eq_zero_of_subsingleton]: the first lemma is the previous step, and the second is the library theorem that the real-valued Wasserstein distance between two probability measures on a space with at most one point is 000, proved with the product coupling as above. The simplifier evaluates the remaining arithmetic (2⋅0=02\cdot0=02⋅0=0, 0/x=00/x=00/x=0, 0=0\sqrt0=00​=0, multiplication by 000).

In theorem1 this lemma is applied after subst has replaced dθ and dx by 000, with μ the initial law, π the target and ν the law at iteration k.
