01The parameter error vanishes when dθ = 0 §

Mathematical statement and proof

If dθ=0d_\theta=0dθ​=0, every parameter vector equals every other one. In particular pθz=θ∗p_\theta z=\theta_*pθ​z=θ∗​, so ∥pθz−θ∗∥2=0\|p_\theta z-\theta_*\|^2=0∥pθ​z−θ∗​∥2=0 pointwise. Its integral under any measure, and the square root of that integral, are zero. No probability, potential or noise assumption is required.

Correspondence with the displayed declarations.

  • parameterRMS_zero_dimension: There is no parameter error when the parameter dimension is zero. This holds for every latent dimension and every measure.

02Every term of the full zero-dimensional bound vanishes §

Mathematical statement and proof

If also dx=0d_x=0dx​=0, the whole state is a singleton. Any two probability measures on it are equal and their W2W_2W2​ distance is zero. The equilibrium radius 2dθ/(Nm)\sqrt{2d_\theta/(Nm)}2dθ​/(Nm)​ and discretization factor dx+dθ/N\sqrt{d_x+d_\theta/N}dx​+dθ​/N​ are also zero. Substituting these facts reduces the full claimed inequality to 0≤00\le00≤0, irrespective of the values of m,γ,Lm,\gamma,Lm,γ,L. This handles the case in which the nonempty-coordinate analytic theorem cannot be instantiated.

When dθ=0d_\theta=0dθ​=0, the parameter space is a singleton, hence every parameter error vector and its square vanish. If also dx=0d_x=0dx​=0, the whole state space is a singleton. Any probability law there is the same Dirac law, so its Wasserstein distance to the Gibbs probability is zero. The dimension factors in the remaining terms are also zero, giving the public bound in this case directly, without invoking a theorem requiring a nonempty coordinate index.
