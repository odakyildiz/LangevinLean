Mathematical statement and proof

A reference family supplies, for every finite-cost coupling ccc of μ0\mu_0μ0​ and π\piπ, an L2L^2L2 realization of the initial variables and common Euler noise. Its fields require that the initial L2L^2L2 difference be at most cost⁡(c)\operatorname{cost}(c)cost(c), and that every realization produce the same output law μk\mu_kμk​. The shared coupling theorem gives W2(μk,π)≤qkcost⁡(c)+bW_2(\mu_k,\pi)\le q^k\operatorname{cost}(c)+bW2​(μk​,π)≤qkcost(c)+b for each such ccc. Taking the cost infimum yields qkW2(μ0,π)+bq^kW_2(\mu_0,\pi)+bqkW2​(μ0​,π)+b. A finite-cost coupling also proves output finiteness before real conversion. These fields are explicit construction obligations in the reusable interface; the public Gaussian theorem discharges them.

The export commands expose the already proved declarations under the IPLA namespace. Their definitions and proofs are unchanged, so the mathematical argument is the one in the imported source modules; the dependency diagram below opens those full proofs. This file itself makes no additional analytic assumption and contains no replacement proof.
