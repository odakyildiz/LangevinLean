Main average-smoothness sampling theorems

References and publication status for this development

Supplied manuscript; checked main sampling proofs

The exact supplied manuscript is the correspondence authority. Its main average-smoothness LMC, decreasing-step, Laplacian-smooth LMC and fixed-point control-variate SGLD statements are proved for actual Gaussian sampling laws with the source constants. Extended transport endpoints cover arbitrary probability initializations. The Laplacian proof uses exactly C3C^3C3 regularity and the gradient-of-Laplacian condition. The complete source inventory distinguishes this main-theorem review scope from broader auxiliary statements not reproduced in full standalone generality; literal full-paper proof coverage is not claimed.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Avetik Karagulyan (undated manuscript). Improved Guarantees for Langevin Monte Carlo with Average Smoothness. Supplied Arxiv_version_May_26.tex; publication status not established.

This import bundle exports the Gaussian LMC, decreasing-step LMC, Laplacian LMC and control-variate SGLD bounds. Their coordinate-average smoothness constants, analytic estimates and distributional recurrences are proved. The source inventory distinguishes the main sampling results from broader auxiliary coverage.
