The hypotheses on which the main results rest

References and publication status for this development

Published source; checked diffusion and sampler proofs

The comparison uses the supplied KIPLMC main.tex and its sections, including the OBABO rate that depends on the particle number. KIPLMC1 is the kinetic exponential integrator applied to the scaled particle potential, with correlated Gaussian increments represented by a Cholesky factor. KIPLMC2 uses the OOO-BBB-AAA-BBB-OOO splitting. The results prove diffusion convergence in physical time, Gibbs invariance, root mean square parameter estimates for both Gaussian recursions, time-rescaling identities, concentration about the marginal maximizer, and iteration counts. The logistic example is checked analytically. The OBABO argument proves a stronger estimate and then deduces the stated coarser rate with a uniform constant. The continuous solutions are continuous in L2L^2L2. Numerical experiments are reported separately from these estimates.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv
  • Pierre Monmarché (2021). High-dimensional MCMC with a standard splitting scheme for the underdamped Langevin diffusion. Electronic Journal of Statistics 15(2), 4117–4166. Full paper

The principal statements of this report are proved from explicitly stated hypotheses only. For the potential: UUU is differentiable on Rdθ×Rdx\mathbb R^{d_\theta}\times\mathbb R^{d_x}Rdθ​×Rdx​, mmm-strongly convex with m>0m>0m>0, and ∇U\nabla U∇U is LLL-Lipschitz, which forces m≤Lm\le Lm≤L whenever dθ+dx>0d_\theta+d_x>0dθ​+dx​>0 and is assumed explicitly where it is used. For the particle system: N≥1N\ge1N≥1. For the dynamics and the algorithms: a positive friction γ\gammaγ with γ2≥m+L\gamma^2\ge m+Lγ2≥m+L for the diffusion; in addition a positive step size hhh with 4γLh≤m4\gamma Lh\le m4γLh≤m for KIPLMC1; and γ2≥4L\gamma^2\ge4Lγ2≥4L together with a positive step size satisfying 33γ3h≤m33\gamma^3h\le m33γ3h≤m for KIPLMC2. For the initialization: an initial position law with finite second moment, and an initial velocity distributed as N(0,N−1I)\mathcal N(0,N^{-1}I)N(0,N−1I) in scaled coordinates, independent of the initial position.

All further ingredients are constructed or proved rather than assumed: the Brownian motion driving the diffusion, the solution of the particle equation, the stationarity of the Gibbs phase law, the correlated Gaussian innovations of KIPLMC1 and the independent thermostat noises of KIPLMC2, the Markov laws of both chains, the contraction of the OBABO step, and the finiteness of every Wasserstein distance that is converted to a real number. In particular no discrete invariant law of the OBABO chain and no external bias estimate is presupposed. The Brownian motion is constructed as a centered Gaussian process with the Brownian covariance whose values are continuous in mean square; the arguments use only its finite-dimensional laws and this mean-square continuity, so no modification with continuous sample paths is required.
