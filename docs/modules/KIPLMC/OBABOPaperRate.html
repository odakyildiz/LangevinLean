The supplied KIPLMC2 rate with an explicit dimension-independent constant

References and publication status for this development

Published source; checked diffusion and sampler proofs

The audit uses the supplied KIPLMC main.tex and sections, including the stated particle-dependent OBABO rate. KIPLMC1 reuses the shared kinetic exponential integrator with its exact Cholesky-generated correlated Gaussian law; KIPLMC2 uses the checked O-B-A-B-O scheme. Physical-time Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both actual Gaussian parameter RMS theorems, exact clock and algorithm conjugacies, sharp MMLE concentration, explicit accuracy budgets, and the logistic analytic example are proved. The internal OBABO proof gives a stronger direct estimate and recovers the supplied coarse rate with an explicit uniform constant. Continuous solutions use the canonical continuous-L² model; empirical experiments are recorded separately.

  • Paul Valsecchi Oliva and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2026). Kinetic interacting particle Langevin Monte Carlo. Statistics and Computing 36, article 221. arXiv
  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv
  • Pierre Monmarché (2021). High-dimensional MCMC with a standard splitting scheme for the underdamped Langevin diffusion. Electronic Journal of Statistics 15(2), 4117–4166. Full paper

The preceding actual sampler theorem gives a stronger intensive bias. This module derives the precise coarser form printed in the supplied manuscript, including its additional transient term. The implicit constant is replaced by an explicit expression that depends only on m,L,γm,L,\gammam,L,γ, and the final result concerns the actual parameter-error integral.

01Turn the square-root geometric factor into the printed exponential §

Mathematical statement and proof

Let r=1−mh/(3γ)r=\sqrt{1-mh/(3\gamma)}r=1−mh/(3γ)​. The scalar bound 1−x≤e−x1-x\le e^{-x}1−x≤e−x, followed by taking square roots, gives r≤e−mh/(6γ)r\le e^{-mh/(6\gamma)}r≤e−mh/(6γ). Raising to the actual integer iteration index proves rn≤e−mhn/(6γ)r^n\le e^{-mhn/(6\gamma)}rn≤e−mhn/(6γ). The proof also handles a nonpositive square-root argument directly, so the scalar envelope itself does not need an extra admissibility hypothesis.

02Make the hidden constant explicit §

Mathematical statement and proof

The allowed step satisfies h≤m/(33γ3)h\le m/(33\gamma^3)h≤m/(33γ3). Consequently the physical bias coefficient is bounded by

B=62γm(4L+Lm66γ3).B=\frac{6\sqrt2\gamma}{m}\left(4\sqrt L+\frac{Lm}{66\gamma^3}\right).B=m62​γ​(4L​+66γ3Lm​).

Choose C=2+B+1/mC=\sqrt2+B+1/\sqrt mC=2​+B+1/m​. All three summands are nonnegative, so CCC dominates the transient coefficient, bias coefficient and equilibrium concentration coefficient separately. 04Apply the envelope to the actual particle parameter law §

The final theorem starts from the unconditional actual KIPLMC2 RMS estimate and supplies every scalar hypothesis using the original curvature, friction and step restrictions. With D=W2(ν,πN)D=W_2(\nu,\pi_N)D=W2​(ν,πN​), it proves

E∥θn−θ⋆∥2≤C[(ND+hNdz)e−mhn/(6γ)+hNdz+dθ/N].\sqrt{\mathbb E\lVert\theta_n-\theta_\star\rVert^2}\le C\left[(\sqrt ND+hN\sqrt{d_z})e^{-mhn/(6\gamma)}+h\sqrt{Nd_z}+\sqrt{d_\theta/N}\right].E∥θn​−θ⋆​∥2​≤C[(N​D+hNdz​​)e−mhn/(6γ)+hNdz​​+dθ​/N​].
