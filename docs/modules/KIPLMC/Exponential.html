KIPLMC1 reuses the shared exponential integrator

References and publication status for this development

Published source; checked diffusion and sampler proofs

The audit uses the supplied KIPLMC main.tex and sections, including the stated particle-dependent OBABO rate. KIPLMC1 reuses the shared kinetic exponential integrator with its exact Cholesky-generated correlated Gaussian law; KIPLMC2 uses the checked O-B-A-B-O scheme. Physical-time Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both actual Gaussian parameter RMS theorems, exact clock and algorithm conjugacies, sharp MMLE concentration, explicit accuracy budgets, and the logistic analytic example are proved. The internal OBABO proof gives a stronger direct estimate and recovers the supplied coarse rate with an explicit uniform constant. Continuous solutions use the canonical continuous-L² model; empirical experiments are recorded separately.

  • Paul Valsecchi Oliva and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2026). Kinetic interacting particle Langevin Monte Carlo. Statistics and Computing 36, article 221. arXiv
  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv
  • Pierre Monmarché (2021). High-dimensional MCMC with a standard splitting scheme for the underdamped Langevin diffusion. Electronic Journal of Statistics 15(2), 4117–4166. Full paper

There is one exponential-step implementation: KineticLangevin.exponentialStep. KIPLMC1 supplies the proved averaged particle force and the common scaled-coordinate thermal amplitude 2γ/N\sqrt{2\gamma/N}2γ/N​. The coordinate identities recover the paper's Algorithm 1 directly, including its separate velocity and position innovations. Noise covariance and independent-Gaussian law conditions must still be established for any stochastic convergence theorem.

01Supply the particle temperature to the shared map §

Mathematical statement and proof

kineticAmplitude is aN=2γ/Na_N=\sqrt{2\gamma/N}aN​=2γ/N​. kiplmc1Step calls the shared exponential map with force FFF and additive phase innovation aN noisea_N\,\text{noise}aN​noise. Consequently every block uses exactly the same friction response functions. In physical time and scaled coordinates this is an inverse temperature NNN kinetic system. The definition accepts arbitrary innovations; when used as the algorithm their velocity–position pair must have the common Gram covariance from the kinetic library.

02Recover both parameter updates §

Mathematical statement and proof

Parameter coordinates are unchanged by spatial rescaling. Substitute Fθ=N−1∑i∇θUiF_\theta=N^{-1}\sum_i\nabla_\theta U_iFθ​=N−1∑i​∇θ​Ui​ in the two shared expressions:

Vθ+=ψ0Vθ−ψ1N∑i∇θUi+aNξθ,θ+=θ+ψ1Vθ−ψ2N∑i∇θUi+aNξθ′.V^{\theta+}=\psi_0V^\theta-\frac{\psi_1}N\sum_i\nabla_\theta U_i+a_N\xi_\theta,\quad \theta^+=\theta+\psi_1V^\theta-\frac{\psi_2}N\sum_i\nabla_\theta U_i+a_N\xi'_\theta.Vθ+=ψ0​Vθ−Nψ1​​i∑​∇θ​Ui​+aN​ξθ​,θ+=θ+ψ1​Vθ−Nψ2​​i∑​∇θ​Ui​+aN​ξθ′​.

The proofs unfold the shared map, evaluate scalar multiplication in Euclidean coordinates, rewrite the proved force identity, and normalize division as multiplication by an inverse. The distinct projections of noise retain the correlated but generally unequal velocity and position increments.

03Undo the latent temperature and force scaling §

Mathematical statement and proof

For N>0N>0N>0, N2γ/N=2γ\sqrt N\sqrt{2\gamma/N}=\sqrt{2\gamma}N​2γ/N​=2γ​. Lean combines square roots using nonnegativity of NNN, then cancels the positive particle number inside the root. Multiply each latent coordinate of the shared step by N\sqrt NN​. The force identity gives NFi=∇xUi\sqrt NF_i=\nabla_xU_iN​Fi​=∇x​Ui​; the amplitude identity restores 2γ\sqrt{2\gamma}2γ​ noise. Thus the physical latent velocity and position updates have the unaveraged pair force and the paper's latent thermal scale. The two coordinate proofs distribute multiplication, rewrite these exact identities and finish scalar rearrangement with ring.

04Construct the numerical recursion from a supplied innovation stream §

Mathematical statement and proof

kiplmc1Chain starts from the given phase and uses innovation number nnn in the successor update. Each step is the shared exponential map specialized to the actual particle force. Recursive construction proves there is an explicit sequence for every prescribed stream; it is not an assumed sequence satisfying a desired estimate. To obtain the paper's actual random chain, one must additionally equip this stream with independent centered Gaussian pairs of the right covariance and prove its independence from initialization, measurability and finite moments.
