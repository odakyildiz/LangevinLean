Exact physical-temperature Gaussian velocity radius

References and publication status for this development

Published source; checked diffusion and sampler proofs

The audit uses the supplied KIPLMC main.tex and sections, including the stated particle-dependent OBABO rate. KIPLMC1 reuses the shared kinetic exponential integrator with its exact Cholesky-generated correlated Gaussian law; KIPLMC2 uses the checked O-B-A-B-O scheme. Physical-time Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both actual Gaussian parameter RMS theorems, exact clock and algorithm conjugacies, sharp MMLE concentration, explicit accuracy budgets, and the logistic analytic example are proved. The internal OBABO proof gives a stronger direct estimate and recovers the supplied coarse rate with an explicit uniform constant. Continuous solutions use the canonical continuous-L² model; empirical experiments are recorded separately.

In scaled particle coordinates every velocity coordinate has variance 1/N1/N1/N. The thermal target is explicitly the image of a standard Gaussian under multiplication by N−1/2N^{-1/2}N−1/2. This module proves the reverse normalization as an actual law equality and computes the exact L² radius (dθ+Ndx)/N\sqrt{(d_\theta+Nd_x)/N}(dθ​+Ndx​)/N​. No independence of coordinates is needed beyond what is already encoded by that Gaussian vector law; zero-dimensional spaces are included.

Statement and proof

01Undo the thermal normalization as a pushforward equality §

Mathematical statement and proof

For N>0N>0N>0, the scalar N\sqrt NN​ is nonzero. Composing the target's defining map v↦N−1/2vv\mapsto N^{-1/2}vv↦N−1/2v with v↦Nvv\mapsto\sqrt Nvv↦N​v gives the identity on the configuration space. Lean rewrites the two successive pushforwards with Measure.map_map, proves the two scalar multiplications cancel using smul_smul and mul_inv_cancel₀, then applies Measure.map_id. Both maps are continuous and hence measurable. This is an exact distributional normalization, rather than a covariance-only assertion.

02Read off the L² moment from the actual Gaussian marginal §

Mathematical statement and proof

Suppose the L² random velocity VVV has the thermal marginal. The preceding map equality and HasLaw.comp show that NV\sqrt NVN​V has the standard Gaussian law. The almost-everywhere scalar-multiplication rule for Lp representatives transfers this statement to the actual Lp class. The shared Gaussian-moment theorem therefore gives ∥NV∥L2=dθ+Ndx\|\sqrt NV\|_{L^2}=\sqrt{d_\theta+Nd_x}∥N​V∥L2​=dθ​+Ndx​​. Since N>0\sqrt N>0N​>0, the norm scaling rule and division yield

∥V∥L2=dθ+NdxN.\|V\|_{L^2}=\sqrt{\frac{d_\theta+Nd_x}{N}}.∥V∥L2​=Ndθ​+Ndx​​​.

Lean simplifies the cardinality of the sum/product coordinate index, rewrites the square root of a quotient, and divides by the strictly positive N\sqrt NN​. The moment is exactly the constant needed by the shared kinetic local-error argument at temperature 1/N1/N1/N.
