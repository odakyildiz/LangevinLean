The exact Gaussian velocity norm at particle temperature

References and publication status for this development

Published source; checked diffusion and sampler proofs

The comparison uses the supplied KIPLMC main.tex and its sections, including the OBABO rate that depends on the particle number. KIPLMC1 is the kinetic exponential integrator applied to the scaled particle potential, with correlated Gaussian increments represented by a Cholesky factor. KIPLMC2 uses the OOO-BBB-AAA-BBB-OOO splitting. The results prove diffusion convergence in physical time, Gibbs invariance, root mean square parameter estimates for both Gaussian recursions, time-rescaling identities, concentration about the marginal maximizer, and iteration counts. The logistic example is checked analytically. The OBABO argument proves a stronger estimate and then deduces the stated coarser rate with a uniform constant. The continuous solutions are continuous in L2L^2L2. Numerical experiments are reported separately from these estimates.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv
  • Pierre Monmarché (2021). High-dimensional MCMC with a standard splitting scheme for the underdamped Langevin diffusion. Electronic Journal of Statistics 15(2), 4117–4166. Full paper

In the scaled coordinates each velocity coordinate has the thermal variance 1/N1/N1/N: the velocity law is κN=N(0,N−1I)\kappa_N=\mathcal N(0,N^{-1}I)κN​=N(0,N−1I) on ZN\mathcal Z_NZN​, defined in the target section as the image of the standard Gaussian law under v↦N−1/2vv\mapsto N^{-1/2}vv↦N−1/2v. This section proves the reverse identity, that N V\sqrt N\,VN​V is standard Gaussian when VVV has law κN\kappa_NκN​, and computes the root mean square norm of a thermal velocity,

(E∥V∥2)1/2=dθ+NdxN=dθN+dx.\bigl(\mathbb E\|V\|^2\bigr)^{1/2}=\sqrt{\frac{d_\theta+Nd_x}N}=\sqrt{\frac{d_\theta}N+d_x}.(E∥V∥2)1/2=Ndθ​+Ndx​​​=Ndθ​​+dx​​.

This number is the velocity radius that enters the local error of the exponential integrator; it is the source of the factor dθ/N+dx\sqrt{d_\theta/N+d_x}dθ​/N+dx​​ in the discretization term of the KIPLMC1 bound, and of the corresponding factor for KIPLMC2.

Statement and proof

01Undoing the thermal scaling of the velocity law §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let N>0N>0N>0. The image of κN\kappa_NκN​ under v↦N vv\mapsto\sqrt N\,vv↦N​v is the standard Gaussian measure γZN\gamma_{\mathcal Z_N}γZN​​.

Proof. By definition κN=(sN)#γZN\kappa_N=(s_N)_\#\gamma_{\mathcal Z_N}κN​=(sN​)#​γZN​​ with sN(v)=(N)−1vs_N(v)=(\sqrt N)^{-1}vsN​(v)=(N​)−1v. Let rN(v)=N vr_N(v)=\sqrt N\,vrN​(v)=N​v. Both maps are linear, hence continuous and measurable, so the pushforward of a composition is the composition of pushforwards:

(rN)#κN=(rN)#(sN)#γZN=(rN∘sN)#γZN.(r_N)_\#\kappa_N=(r_N)_\#(s_N)_\#\gamma_{\mathcal Z_N}=(r_N\circ s_N)_\#\gamma_{\mathcal Z_N}.(rN​)#​κN​=(rN​)#​(sN​)#​γZN​​=(rN​∘sN​)#​γZN​​.

Since N>0N>0N>0, N≠0\sqrt N\ne0N​=0 and rN(sN(v))=(N⋅(N)−1)v=vr_N(s_N(v))=(\sqrt N\cdot(\sqrt N)^{-1})v=vrN​(sN​(v))=(N​⋅(N​)−1)v=v, so rN∘sNr_N\circ s_NrN​∘sN​ is the identity, and the pushforward of a measure by the identity is the measure itself. □\square□

This is an equality of measures, so every statement about the law of a standard Gaussian vector, in particular every moment identity, transfers to N V\sqrt N\,VN​V when V∼κNV\sim\kappa_NV∼κN​.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

velocityTarget_unscale assumes hN : 0 < N and states (velocityTarget N dθ dx).map (fun v ↦ Real.sqrt N • v) = stdGaussian (State N dθ dx). hs is N≠0\sqrt N\ne0N​=0, from Real.sqrt_ne_zero' and N>0N>0N>0. After unfolding velocityTarget, Measure.map_map merges the two pushforwards, both maps being measurable by fun_prop. hid proves that the composed map is the identity id, using smul_smul, mul_inv_cancel₀ hs and one_smul; after change displays the composed map, rw [hid, Measure.map_id] finishes.

02The root mean square norm of a thermal velocity §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. Let (Ω,F,P)(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)(Ω,F,P) be a measure space, N>0N>0N>0, and let VVV be a square-integrable random vector with values in ZN\mathcal Z_NZN​ whose law is κN\kappa_NκN​. Then

∥V∥L2=(∫Ω∥V∥2 dP)1/2=dθ+NdxN.\|V\|_{L^2}=\Bigl(\int_\Omega\|V\|^2\,d\mathbb P\Bigr)^{1/2}=\sqrt{\frac{d_\theta+Nd_x}N}.∥V∥L2​=(∫Ω​∥V∥2dP)1/2=Ndθ​+Ndx​​​.

Proof. By the previous step, N V=rN(V)\sqrt N\,V=r_N(V)N​V=rN​(V) has law (rN)#κN=γZN(r_N)_\#\kappa_N=\gamma_{\mathcal Z_N}(rN​)#​κN​=γZN​​, the standard Gaussian law on the space ZN\mathcal Z_NZN​ of dimension D=dθ+NdxD=d_\theta+Nd_xD=dθ​+Ndx​ (the number of elements of INI_NIN​). For a standard Gaussian vector GGG on RD\mathbb R^DRD the coordinates are standard normal, so

E∥G∥2=∑a∈INE Ga2=D,that is∥G∥L2=D\mathbb E\|G\|^2=\sum_{a\in I_N}\mathbb E\,G_a^2=D,\qquad\text{that is}\qquad\|G\|_{L^2}=\sqrt DE∥G∥2=a∈IN​∑​EGa2​=D,that is∥G∥L2​=D​

(Kinetic, Section 3.40.1). Applied to G=N VG=\sqrt N\,VG=N​V and combined with homogeneity of the L2L^2L2 norm and N>0\sqrt N>0N​>0, this gives N ∥V∥L2=D\sqrt N\,\|V\|_{L^2}=\sqrt DN​∥V∥L2​=D​. Dividing by N\sqrt NN​ and using D/N=D/N\sqrt D/\sqrt N=\sqrt{D/N}D​/N​=D/N​ for D≥0D\ge0D≥0, N>0N>0N>0 proves the claim. □\square□

The law of VVV is a probability measure, so the hypothesis forces P(Ω)=1\mathbb P(\Omega)=1P(Ω)=1. Each of the DDD coordinates has variance 1/N1/N1/N, and the squared radius D/N=dθ/N+dxD/N=d_\theta/N+d_xD/N=dθ​/N+dx​ is the sum of these variances. Its square root is the velocity radius at the thermal equilibrium of the particle system; it does not grow with NNN when dθd_\thetadθ​ and dxd_xdx​ are fixed, and the case of zero dimensions is included.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

thermal_velocity_Lp_norm takes a measure P on Ω, hN : 0 < N, an element V : Lp (State N dθ dx) 2 P of the space L2(P;ZN)L^2(\mathbb P;\mathcal Z_N)L2(P;ZN​) of square-integrable random vectors modulo almost sure equality, whose norm ‖V‖ is ∥V∥L2\|V\|_{L^2}∥V∥L2​, and hlaw : HasLaw V (velocityTarget N dθ dx) P. Mathlib's HasLaw X μ P states that XXX is almost everywhere measurable and P.map X = μ.

  1. hmap is the statement that the map rNr_NrN​ has law γZN\gamma_{\mathcal Z_N}γZN​​ under κN\kappa_NκN​, built from velocityTarget_unscale hN and measurability by fun_prop.
  2. hG composes it with hlaw (hmap.comp hlaw) and transfers the result to the element Real.sqrt N • V of L2L^2L2, whose representative agrees almost everywhere with N\sqrt NN​ times that of V (Lp.coeFn_smul).
  3. hm is the kinetic report's KineticLangevin.gaussian_velocity_Lp_norm, ∥N V∥L2=∣IN∣\|\sqrt N\,V\|_{L^2}=\sqrt{|I_N|}∥N​V∥L2​=∣IN​∣​; norm_smul and abs_of_pos hs turn its left side into N∥V∥L2\sqrt N\|V\|_{L^2}N​∥V∥L2​.
  4. hcard computes the cardinality of Fin dθ ⊕ (Fin N × Fin dx) as dθ+Ndxd_\theta+Nd_xdθ​+Ndx​, and the last lines rewrite D/N=D/N\sqrt{D/N}=\sqrt D/\sqrt ND/N​=D​/N​ (Real.sqrt_div) and divide by N\sqrt NN​ (eq_div_iff, with nlinarith closing the rearranged identity).
