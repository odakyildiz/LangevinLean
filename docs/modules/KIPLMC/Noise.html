Correlated Gaussian velocity and position innovations in particle coordinates

References and publication status for this development

Published source; checked diffusion and sampler proofs

The comparison uses the supplied KIPLMC main.tex and its sections, including the OBABO rate that depends on the particle number. KIPLMC1 is the kinetic exponential integrator applied to the scaled particle potential, with correlated Gaussian increments represented by a Cholesky factor. KIPLMC2 uses the OOO-BBB-AAA-BBB-OOO splitting. The results prove diffusion convergence in physical time, Gibbs invariance, root mean square parameter estimates for both Gaussian recursions, time-rescaling identities, concentration about the marginal maximizer, and iteration counts. The logistic example is checked analytically. The OBABO argument proves a stronger estimate and then deduces the stated coarser rate with a uniform constant. The continuous solutions are continuous in L2L^2L2. Numerical experiments are reported separately from these estimates.

  • Paul Valsecchi Oliva and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2026). Kinetic interacting particle Langevin Monte Carlo. Statistics and Computing 36, article 221. arXiv
  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv
  • Pierre Monmarché (2021). High-dimensional MCMC with a standard splitting scheme for the underdamped Langevin diffusion. Electronic Journal of Statistics 15(2), 4117–4166. Full paper

One KIPLMC1 step adds to the velocity and to the position two Wiener integrals of the same Brownian increment, ∫0hψ0(h−s) dWs\int_0^h\psi_0(h-s)\,dW_s∫0h​ψ0​(h−s)dWs​ and ∫0hψ1(h−s) dWs\int_0^h\psi_1(h-s)\,dW_s∫0h​ψ1​(h−s)dWs​, coordinate by coordinate. In each coordinate this pair is a centered Gaussian vector in R2\mathbb R^2R2 with the Gram covariance

C(γ,h)kl=∫0hψk(t)ψl(t) dt(k,l∈{0,1}),C(\gamma,h)_{kl}=\int_0^h\psi_k(t)\psi_l(t)\,dt\qquad(k,l\in\{0,1\}),C(γ,h)kl​=∫0h​ψk​(t)ψl​(t)dt(k,l∈{0,1}),

and different coordinates are independent. This section constructs the corresponding probability measure η1(γ,h)\eta_1(\gamma,h)η1​(γ,h) on ZN×ZN\mathcal Z_N\times\mathcal Z_NZN​×ZN​: take independent copies of the two-dimensional Gaussian law Gγ,h=N(0,C(γ,h))\mathcal G_{\gamma,h}=\mathcal N(0,C(\gamma,h))Gγ,h​=N(0,C(γ,h)), one for each coordinate of the scaled particle space, and collect the first entries into a velocity vector and the second entries into a position vector. We prove that the construction gives a probability measure, that the blocks of distinct coordinates are independent, and that the velocity–position pair of each coordinate has law exactly Gγ,h\mathcal G_{\gamma,h}Gγ,h​. The factor 2γ/N\sqrt{2\gamma/N}2γ/N​ is applied by the update, not by this measure. The construction works for arbitrary dimensions, including dθ=0d_\theta=0dθ​=0 or dx=0d_x=0dx​=0.

01Independent Gaussian blocks indexed by the coordinates §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definitions. The coordinate set of ZN\mathcal Z_NZN​ is IN={1,…,dθ}⊔({1,…,N}×{1,…,dx})I_N=\{1,\dots,d_\theta\}\sqcup(\{1,\dots,N\}\times\{1,\dots,d_x\})IN​={1,…,dθ​}⊔({1,…,N}×{1,…,dx​}). Let Gγ,h\mathcal G_{\gamma,h}Gγ,h​ be the centered Gaussian measure on R2\mathbb R^2R2 with covariance matrix C(γ,h)C(\gamma,h)C(γ,h) above, constructed in the kinetic report (Kinetic, Section 3.26.3). For h≥0h\ge0h≥0 the matrix C(γ,h)C(\gamma,h)C(γ,h) is symmetric and positive semidefinite, since for u∈R2u\in\mathbb R^2u∈R2

uTC(γ,h)u=∫0h(u0ψ0(t)+u1ψ1(t))2 dt≥0,u^{\mathsf T}C(\gamma,h)u=\int_0^h\bigl(u_0\psi_0(t)+u_1\psi_1(t)\bigr)^2\,dt\ge0,uTC(γ,h)u=∫0h​(u0​ψ0​(t)+u1​ψ1​(t))2dt≥0,

so Gγ,h\mathcal G_{\gamma,h}Gγ,h​ is the law of a centered Gaussian vector whose covariances are the entries of C(γ,h)C(\gamma,h)C(γ,h). The block measure is the finite product

βγ,h=⨂a∈INGγ,hon (R2)IN,\beta_{\gamma,h}=\bigotimes_{a\in I_N}\mathcal G_{\gamma,h}\qquad\text{on }(\mathbb R^2)^{I_N},βγ,h​=a∈IN​⨂​Gγ,h​on (R2)IN​,

the joint law of independent random vectors ωa∈R2\omega_a\in\mathbb R^2ωa​∈R2, a∈INa\in I_Na∈IN​, each with law Gγ,h\mathcal G_{\gamma,h}Gγ,h​.

Claim. βγ,h\beta_{\gamma,h}βγ,h​ is a probability measure.

Proof. A product of finitely many probability measures is a probability measure: the product measure of the whole space is the product of the total masses, ∏a∈IN1=1\prod_{a\in I_N}1=1∏a∈IN​​1=1. □\square□

The entries of C(γ,h)C(\gamma,h)C(γ,h) are the covariances of the two Wiener integrals displayed in the summary, by the Itô isometry and the substitution t=h−st=h-st=h−s; in particular the off-diagonal entry is positive for h>0h>0h>0, so velocity and position innovations of the same coordinate are correlated.

02Assembling the velocity and position innovations §

Mathematical statement and proof

Definitions. The assembly map A:(R2)IN→ZN×ZNA:(\mathbb R^2)^{I_N}\to\mathcal Z_N\times\mathcal Z_NA:(R2)IN​→ZN​×ZN​ collects first and second entries:

A(ω)=((ωa,0)a∈IN,  (ωa,1)a∈IN).A(\omega)=\bigl((\omega_{a,0})_{a\in I_N},\;(\omega_{a,1})_{a\in I_N}\bigr).A(ω)=((ωa,0​)a∈IN​​,(ωa,1​)a∈IN​​).

The KIPLMC1 innovation law is η1(γ,h)=A#βγ,h\eta_1(\gamma,h)=A_\#\beta_{\gamma,h}η1​(γ,h)=A#​βγ,h​; a sample (ξ,ξ′)(\xi,\xi')(ξ,ξ′) has velocity part ξa=ωa,0\xi_a=\omega_{a,0}ξa​=ωa,0​ and position part ξa′=ωa,1\xi'_a=\omega_{a,1}ξa′​=ωa,1​.

Claim. AAA is measurable, and η1(γ,h)\eta_1(\gamma,h)η1​(γ,h) is a probability measure.

Proof. Every coordinate of A(ω)A(\omega)A(ω) is a coordinate of ω\omegaω, so AAA is linear and continuous, hence Borel measurable. Therefore η1(γ,h)(ZN×ZN)=βγ,h(A−1(ZN×ZN))=βγ,h((R2)IN)=1\eta_1(\gamma,h)(\mathcal Z_N\times\mathcal Z_N)=\beta_{\gamma,h}(A^{-1}(\mathcal Z_N\times\mathcal Z_N))=\beta_{\gamma,h}((\mathbb R^2)^{I_N})=1η1​(γ,h)(ZN​×ZN​)=βγ,h​(A−1(ZN​×ZN​))=βγ,h​((R2)IN​)=1 by the previous step. □\square□

The assembly only rearranges coordinates, so all joint moments of η1\eta_1η1​ are those of the blocks: ξ\xiξ and ξ′\xi'ξ′ are centered Gaussian vectors in ZN\mathcal Z_NZN​ with covariances C00IC_{00}IC00​I and C11IC_{11}IC11​I and cross-covariance C01IC_{01}IC01​I.

03Independence across coordinates and the law of each pair §

Mathematical statement and proof

Claim. (a) Under βγ,h\beta_{\gamma,h}βγ,h​ the random vectors ω↦ωa\omega\mapsto\omega_aω↦ωa​, a∈INa\in I_Na∈IN​, are mutually independent. (b) For every coordinate a∈INa\in I_Na∈IN​, the image of η1(γ,h)\eta_1(\gamma,h)η1​(γ,h) under (ξ,ξ′)↦(ξa,ξa′)∈R2(\xi,\xi')\mapsto(\xi_a,\xi'_a)\in\mathbb R^2(ξ,ξ′)↦(ξa​,ξa′​)∈R2 is Gγ,h\mathcal G_{\gamma,h}Gγ,h​.

Proof. (a) For a product of probability measures ⨂aμa\bigotimes_a\mu_a⨂a​μa​ and measurable sets Sa⊆R2S_a\subseteq\mathbb R^2Sa​⊆R2, the event {ωa∈Sa for all a∈J}\{\omega_a\in S_a\text{ for all }a\in J\}{ωa​∈Sa​ for all a∈J} for a finite set JJJ of indices is the box ∏a∈JSa×∏a∉JR2\prod_{a\in J}S_a\times\prod_{a\notin J}\mathbb R^2∏a∈J​Sa​×∏a∈/J​R2, whose measure is ∏a∈Jμa(Sa)⋅1\prod_{a\in J}\mu_a(S_a)\cdot1∏a∈J​μa​(Sa​)⋅1. This is mutual independence of the coordinate maps.

(b) Let πa(ξ,ξ′)=(ξa,ξa′)\pi_a(\xi,\xi')=(\xi_a,\xi'_a)πa​(ξ,ξ′)=(ξa​,ξa′​). The image of η1=A#βγ,h\eta_1=A_\#\beta_{\gamma,h}η1​=A#​βγ,h​ under πa\pi_aπa​ is (πa∘A)#βγ,h(\pi_a\circ A)_\#\beta_{\gamma,h}(πa​∘A)#​βγ,h​, since both maps are measurable. By the definition of AAA, πa(A(ω))=(ωa,0,ωa,1)=ωa\pi_a(A(\omega))=(\omega_{a,0},\omega_{a,1})=\omega_aπa​(A(ω))=(ωa,0​,ωa,1​)=ωa​, checked on the two entries. Finally, the image of a product of probability measures under the evaluation ω↦ωa\omega\mapsto\omega_aω↦ωa​ is the factor Gγ,h\mathcal G_{\gamma,h}Gγ,h​: for measurable S⊆R2S\subseteq\mathbb R^2S⊆R2 the preimage is S×∏b≠aR2S\times\prod_{b\ne a}\mathbb R^2S×∏b=a​R2, of measure Gγ,h(S)\mathcal G_{\gamma,h}(S)Gγ,h​(S). □\square□

Thus η1(γ,h)\eta_1(\gamma,h)η1​(γ,h) is the law of independent coordinate pairs, each with covariance C(γ,h)C(\gamma,h)C(γ,h), which is the noise of Algorithm 1 in scaled coordinates: the parameter coordinates give (ε0,ε0′)(\varepsilon^0,\varepsilon^{0\prime})(ε0,ε0′) and the latent coordinates of particle iii give (εi,εi′)(\varepsilon^i,\varepsilon^{i\prime})(εi,εi′). Multiplication by 2γ/N\sqrt{2\gamma/N}2γ/N​ in the update, and by N\sqrt NN​ when the latent coordinates are unscaled, produces the amplitudes 2γ/N\sqrt{2\gamma/N}2γ/N​ and 2γ\sqrt{2\gamma}2γ​ of the paper.

How the Lean proof follows this argument

exponentialNoiseBlocks_independent states iIndepFun (fun i ω ↦ ω i) (exponentialNoiseBlocks N dθ dx γ h), mutual independence of the evaluation maps, and is Mathlib's iIndepFun_pi applied to the identity maps of the factors (whose measurability is measurable_id.aemeasurable).

  1. Measure.map_map combines the two pushforwards into one, after measurability is supplied by fun_prop and assembleExponentialNoise_measurable; the change line displays the composed map.
  2. he proves that the composed map equals fun ω ↦ ω i: funext and ext j reduce this to the entries, and fin_cases j checks the two values of j by simp [assembleExponentialNoise].
  3. (measurePreserving_eval (fun _ ↦ KineticLangevin.klmcGaussian γ h) i).map_eq is Mathlib's statement that evaluation at i pushes the product of probability measures to the ith factor.
