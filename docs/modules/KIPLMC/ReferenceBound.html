The shared exponential proof at particle temperature

References and publication status for this development

Published source; checked diffusion and sampler proofs

The audit uses the supplied KIPLMC main.tex and sections, including the stated particle-dependent OBABO rate. KIPLMC1 reuses the shared kinetic exponential integrator with its exact Cholesky-generated correlated Gaussian law; KIPLMC2 uses the checked O-B-A-B-O scheme. Physical-time Brownian integral-solution mixing, full Gibbs stationarity, both actual Gaussian parameter RMS theorems, exact clock and algorithm conjugacies, sharp MMLE concentration, explicit accuracy budgets, and the logistic analytic example are proved. The internal OBABO proof gives a stronger direct estimate and recovers the supplied coarse rate with an explicit uniform constant. Continuous solutions use the canonical continuous-L² model; empirical experiments are recorded separately.

  • Paul Valsecchi Oliva and Ö. Deniz Akyildiz (2026). Kinetic interacting particle Langevin Monte Carlo. Statistics and Computing 36, article 221. arXiv
  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv
  • Pierre Monmarché (2021). High-dimensional MCMC with a standard splitting scheme for the underdamped Langevin diffusion. Electronic Journal of Statistics 15(2), 4117–4166. Full paper

The first particle algorithm uses exactly the shared exponential step. Its analytical comparison should therefore reuse the same proof. This module applies the shared L² kinetic theorem to the averaged lifted potential, uses the actual thermal Gaussian radius, and projects the resulting transport estimate to the MMLE parameter. The result has all three constants in the supplied KIPLMC1 theorem, with the initial supplied coupling cost. Its concrete integral-segment and reference-marginal hypotheses are explicit: construction of a canonical stationary reference and identification of the independent Gaussian chain are still needed for the unconditional sampler theorem.

01Lift the actual physical particle force once §

Mathematical statement and proof

The averaged lifted potential UˉN=VN/N\bar U_N=V_N/NUˉN​=VN​/N has gradient FNF_NFN​, the physical-time force. The earlier geometry proves its original smoothness constant LLL and strong convexity constant mmm, independently of particle count. lpForce applies the existing pointwise ULA.lpDrift construction to this gradient. It is therefore the actual particle force on almost-everywhere representatives, with a well-defined square-integrable output. No new exponential update or substitute gradient algorithm is introduced.

Mathematical statement and proof

Let SnS_nSn​ be numerical phase states and RnR_nRn​ reference phase states in L². On each interval, the auxiliary exact path starts at SnS_nSn​, the reference exact path starts at RnR_nRn​ and ends at Rn+1R_{n+1}Rn+1​, and their integral equations have the same additive input. The numerical endpoint is the actual shared exponential step with velocity noise BhB_hBh​ and position noise ∫0hBsds\int_0^hB_sds∫0h​Bs​ds. Its mild velocity and kinematic identities are explicit inputs. The reference velocity marginal is the thermal Gaussian and its position marginal is the normalized lifted Gibbs target.

Every local-error and recurrence step is inherited, not assumed as a transport inequality.

The particular L² pair (SnX,RnX)(S_n^X,R_n^X)(SnX​,RnX​) supplies a finite transport coupling, so real-valued conversion cannot conceal infinity. The parameter projection has operator norm at most one. The earlier sharp Gibbs concentration theorem contributes dθ/(Nm)\sqrt{d_\theta/(Nm)}dθ​/(Nm)​. Lean invokes the proved transport-to-projected-RMS triangle bound, proves the projected moment is finite, rewrites it as the square root of the actual parameter second moment, and adds the three terms. The resulting theorem remains explicitly a reference comparison; it does not assert that the unresolved reference construction has already been discharged.
