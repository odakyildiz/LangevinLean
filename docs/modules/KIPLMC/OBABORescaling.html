Unit-temperature rescaling of KIPLMC2 trajectories and laws

References and publication status for this development

Published source; checked diffusion and sampler proofs

The comparison uses the supplied KIPLMC main.tex and its sections, including the OBABO rate that depends on the particle number. KIPLMC1 is the kinetic exponential integrator applied to the scaled particle potential, with correlated Gaussian increments represented by a Cholesky factor. KIPLMC2 uses the O-B-A-B-O splitting. The results prove diffusion convergence in physical time, Gibbs invariance, root mean square parameter estimates for both Gaussian recursions, time-rescaling identities, concentration about the marginal maximizer, and iteration counts. The logistic example is checked analytically. The OBABO argument proves a stronger estimate and then deduces the stated coarser rate with a uniform constant. The continuous solutions are continuous in L². Numerical experiments are reported separately from these estimates.

  • Arnak S. Dalalyan and Lionel Riou-Durand (2020). On sampling from a log-concave density using kinetic Langevin diffusions. Bernoulli 26(3), 1956–1988. arXiv
  • Pierre Monmarché (2021). High-dimensional MCMC with a standard splitting scheme for the underdamped Langevin diffusion. Electronic Journal of Statistics 15(2), 4117–4166. Full paper

Put c=Nc=\sqrt Nc=N​. Multiply the scaled physical velocity by ccc, keep position unchanged, replace the clock step by h/ch/ch/c, and replace friction by cγc\gammacγ. The averaged force then becomes the gradient of the sum potential. Every subflow, the whole defined trajectory, its independent Gaussian Markov law and its Gibbs target satisfy this exact change of variables. This is the identity of probability laws needed to reuse the shared OBABO sampling theorem.

The complete five-stage particle update and its weighted metric are transported through the clock change. Exact target and whole-chain identities enable reuse of the unit-temperature OBABO sampling theorem.

01Thermostat, drift and kick under the clock change §

Mathematical statement and proof

The thermostat sends vvv to δv+σξ\delta v+\sigma\xiδv+σξ; multiplying velocity by ccc changes its noise amplitude to cσc\sigmacσ. The drift satisfies (h/c)(cv)=hv(h/c)(cv)=hv(h/c)(cv)=hv, so position is unchanged. For the kick, (h/c)g=ch(c−2g)(h/c)g=c h(c^{-2}g)(h/c)g=ch(c−2g). One proves each identity by unfolding the map, distributing scalar multiplication and cancelling the nonzero clock factor. Combining these equalities in their original O–B–A–B–O order proves the full five-subflow conjugacy, including both distinct fresh innovations.

Under the velocity-and-clock change, the damping product is unchanged, while the drift and kick coefficients compensate for the velocity scale. The thermal amplitude is transformed at the same time. Verifying all five stages identifies the complete physical and unit-temperature steps.

Earlier results used here: Kinetic, Section 3.20.1.

02Exact invariance of the contracting metric §

Mathematical statement and proof

The transformed potential has smoothness c2Lc^2Lc2L and friction cγc\gammacγ. In its phase energy, the velocity term is ∥cv∥2/(c2L)=∥v∥2/L\lVert cv\rVert^2/(c^2L)=\lVert v\rVert^2/L∥cv∥2/(c2L)=∥v∥2/L, and the mixed term is 2⟨x,cv⟩/(cγ)=2⟨x,v⟩/γ2\langle x,cv\rangle/(c\gamma)=2\langle x,v\rangle/\gamma2⟨x,cv⟩/(cγ)=2⟨x,v⟩/γ. The position term is unchanged. Thus the entire weighted energy is invariant. The proof expands its definition, applies the norm and inner-product scalar rules, and cancels the positive ccc.

The weighted metric is designed from friction and phase coordinates. Scaling friction and velocity together preserves its value after the stated clock normalization. Thus transporting the contraction theorem introduces no hidden particle-dependent norm-equivalence factor.

Earlier results used here: Kinetic, Section 3.69.1.

03The defined particle step and entire trajectory §

Mathematical statement and proof

Take c=Nc=\sqrt Nc=N​. The force N−1∇VNN^{-1}\nabla V_NN−1∇VN​ is precisely c−2∇VNc^{-2}\nabla V_Nc−2∇VN​, damping depends only on γh\gamma hγh, and N(1−δ2)/N=1−δ2\sqrt N\sqrt{(1-\delta^2)/N}=\sqrt{1-\delta^2}N​(1−δ2)/N​=1−δ2​. These established identities specialize the generic conjugacy to Algorithm 2. Induction through its successor recurrence proves the entire transformed trajectory is the standard-temperature OBABO trajectory for VNV_NVN​, driven by exactly the same paired Gaussian vectors.

Apply the step conjugacy inductively with the transformed noises. The entire physical trajectory is mapped to the shared unit-temperature trajectory. Since position is unchanged by the velocity map, their position sequences agree under this coupling.

04Transport the Gibbs target and Markov laws §

Mathematical statement and proof

The unit-temperature phase target is N(0,I)⊗πVN\mathcal N(0,I)\otimes\pi_{V_N}N(0,I)⊗πVN​​. Multiplying its velocity by N−1/2N^{-1/2}N−1/2 gives exactly the defined KIPLMC thermal target; position remains πVN\pi_{V_N}πVN​​. The product pushforward identity proves this equality after strong convexity supplies probability normalization. For numerical laws, the proved step conjugacy and joint measurability feed the general Markov-law conjugacy theorem. It establishes the equality for the defined independent-Gaussian law at every iteration, so transferring a sampling estimate requires no assumed correspondence between two algorithms.

The thermal Gaussian velocity becomes standard Gaussian, and the position Gibbs target is unchanged. The exact transition conjugacy then transports both the invariant comparison target and all numerical Markov iterates. This is the equality of laws used by the particle OBABO error theorem.
