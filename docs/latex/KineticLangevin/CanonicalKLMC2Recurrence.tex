\section{\texorpdfstring{The exact four-noise input}{The exact four-noise input}}


The constructed innovation is the Brownian source lift of each of the four deterministic convolution kernels. Unfolding this definition and the analytic lifted-noise definition proves exact equality, since converting the nonnegative numerical step to a nonnegative-real duration does not change it.



The four-response input has the packed correlated Gaussian law and the correct source representatives. Substitution in the numerical update identifies the recurrence’s innovation variables with those of the constructed sampler, including the Hessian-multiplied responses.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Verification of the hypotheses using reference and numerical paths}{Verification of the hypotheses using reference and numerical paths}}


The reference segment is continuous, starts at the current reference state and ends at its next state. Its Gaussian-times-Gibbs phase law, free-OU mild equation and position primitive are proved internally. Past/\allowbreak{}future independence identifies the free-OU velocity law as standard Gaussian throughout the same fresh interval. Applying the generic KLMC2 one-step theorem therefore gives its exact source coefficient, Gaussian cutoff tail and cubic stationary local defect. Equality of the four noises and the numerical successor formula identify its left side with the next numerical/\allowbreak{}reference error. Symmetry of the transformed norm reverses the current reference-minus-numerical discrepancy to the chosen numerical-minus-reference convention.



The constructed reference and auxiliary paths supply continuity, exact integral equations, stationarity, and past/\allowbreak{}future independence. The repaired one-step theorem therefore applies at every iteration. Its conclusion is the constructed error recurrence, rather than an abstract conditional recurrence.
