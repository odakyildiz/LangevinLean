Draft mathematical exposition; these notes may contain mistakes.


The Gaussian cutoff in the repaired second-order proof applies to a free Ornstein–Uhlenbeck path on each reference interval. Its marginal law follows from proved stationarity and proved independence of future Brownian increments from the past solution.



Proved stationarity and past/\allowbreak{}future independence give the Gaussian marginal of the free-OU response starting from the reference. This fact holds at grid points and arbitrary continuous restart times.


\section{\texorpdfstring{Use the reference-grid joint law}{Use the reference-grid joint law}}


At the grid time \(a=nh\), let \(V_a\) be the velocity of the stationary reference chain and let \(N_{\psi_0}(a,r)\) be its fresh Brownian response. The past/\allowbreak{}future theorem gives the joint law \[\bigl(V_a,\sqrt{2\gamma}N_{\psi_0}(a,r)\bigr)\sim N(0,I)\otimes N\bigl(0,(1-e^{-2\gamma r})I\bigr).\] In particular, the first random variable is independent of this future response even though it depends on the entire past path. The proof specializes this exact joint law to the nonnegative real duration, then applies the proved Gaussian addition identity to \(R_0(r)=e^{-\gamma r}V_a+\sqrt{2\gamma}N_{\psi_0}(a,r)\). Its covariance is \(e^{-2\gamma r}I+(1-e^{-2\gamma r})I=I\), so \(R_0(r)\sim N(0,I)\). All variables are the \(L^{2}\) representatives used by the numerical coupling.



The reference velocity depends on the past, but the following Brownian interval is independent of that past by the constructed independence theorem. Their covariance contributions under free damping add to the identity. This supplies the Gaussian marginal required inside the random-Hessian cutoff estimate.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Apply the same result at arbitrary continuous reference times}{Apply the same result at arbitrary continuous reference times}}


The global reference solution has the same proven past/\allowbreak{}future joint law at every \(a\in[0,T]\). The identical Gaussian calculation therefore proves the free-OU response beginning at an arbitrary continuous reference time is standard Gaussian at every later duration. This supports local cutoff estimates independently of a chosen numerical grid. Neither statement assumes the desired marginal or an assumed adaptedness property; both invoke the already proved constructed independence and stationarity results.



The proof uses only stationarity at the restart time and independence of the following interval. It therefore does not depend on the restart being a grid point. This flexibility supports local estimates at any reference time in the finite horizon.
