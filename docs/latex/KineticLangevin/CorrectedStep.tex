The local Taylor estimate and the four-noise numerical map refer to exactly the same \(L^{2}\) random variables. This section proves the identification, including both stochastic Hessian corrections, by time differentiation and integration.



The analytic corrected path is expanded into its complete deterministic and stochastic response terms. Matching them exactly with the defined update is necessary before transferring the local Taylor estimate to KLMC2.


\section{\texorpdfstring{Differentiate the damped convolution}{Differentiate the damped convolution}}


For a continuous Banach-valued path \(F\), write \(D_\gamma F(t)=\int_0^te^{-\gamma(t-s)}F(s)\,ds\). Factoring the exponential yields \(D_\gamma F(t)=e^{-\gamma t}\int_0^te^{\gamma s}F(s)\,ds\). The argument applies the fundamental theorem of calculus to the continuous integrand and the product rule, then cancels \(e^{-\gamma t}e^{\gamma t}=1\). The result is the exact equation \((D_\gamma F)\prime=F-\gamma D_\gamma F\), for every real time.



The factorized expression separates the time dependence of the exponential from the moving upper integration limit. The product rule then gives one forcing term and one damping term. This identity is the differential identity underlying the corrected free-OU equations.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Integrate the entire stochastic correction}{Integrate the entire stochastic correction}}


The constructed Wiener kernel on an interval of zero duration is zero in \(L^{2}\); this follows from almost-everywhere equality of its deterministic kernel, followed by the isonormal map and spatial assembly. Thus the full correction \(C_k(t)=\varphi_{k+1}(t)V_0+L N_{\varphi_{k+1}}(a,t)\) vanishes initially and is continuous. The identity \(C_1+\gamma C_2=R_2\), together with \(C_2=D_\gamma R_2\), gives \(C_2\prime=C_1\) at every positive time. The fundamental theorem then proves \(C_2(t)=\int_0^tC_1(s)\,ds\). Also \(C_1=D_\gamma(\int_0^{\cdot}R_0)\), using the primitive \(R_1=\int R_0\). Both statements include fresh noise terms.



The correction’s zero initial value and its derivative identity determine its primitive uniquely. Both statements retain the stochastic response part. Thus integrating the complete correction yields the correct position term, including the Hessian-multiplied position noise.

\section{\texorpdfstring{The analytic velocity and position with full kernel formulas}{The analytic velocity and position with full kernel formulas}}


The Taylor analysis defines \(\widehat V_t=R_0(t)-\psi_1(t)F_0-A D_\gamma(\int R_0)(t)\). Substituting the proved identity gives \(\widehat V_t=R_0(t)-\psi_1(t)F_0-AC_1(t)\). Integrating this path gives \(\widehat X_t=x_0+R_1(t)-\psi_2(t)F_0-AC_2(t)\). First, the argument proves equality of the velocity integrands on the time interval, establishes all continuous summands are integrable, splits their Bochner integrals, and commutes the bounded Hessian operator with the integral. It then uses the primitive identities and additive algebra.



The frozen Hessian is constant in local time and defines a bounded operator on square-integrable variables. It can therefore be commuted with integration. This gives the position formula from the corrected velocity path, rather than guessing a matching endpoint expression.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Match the defined four-noise KLMC2 update exactly}{Match the defined four-noise KLMC2 update exactly}}


Let \(R\) be the unscaled source pullback and sample the four correlated responses
\[(N_{\psi_0},N_{\psi_1},N_{\varphi_2},N_{\varphi_3})\]
 on the same Brownian interval. The analytic free-OU path uses thermally scaled map \(L=\sqrt{2\gamma}\,R\). Expanding the proved velocity and position formulas gives precisely the defined \(\mathcal K_h\): its deterministic Hessian terms are \(-\varphi_2H_0V_0\) and \(-\varphi_3H_0V_0\), and its fresh terms are \(\sqrt{2\gamma}(N_{\psi_0}-H_0N_{\varphi_2})\) and \(\sqrt{2\gamma}(N_{\psi_1}-H_0N_{\varphi_3})\). The proof reduces the four finite indices, applies linearity of the Hessian and source maps, and proves the two component identities by vector-space algebra. No local error or noise-independence conclusion is assumed.



Expanding the four shared responses makes the two deterministic Hessian terms and the two stochastic Hessian terms explicit. This exact matching is required before applying the local Taylor estimate to the sampler. The identified map is the full defined KLMC2 update.
