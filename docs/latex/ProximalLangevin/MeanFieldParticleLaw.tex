\section{A measure-preserving copy has exactly the reference law}


The copy representative equals \(X(\omega_i)\) almost everywhere. Product-coordinate evaluation pushes \(Q^{\otimes N}\) to \(Q\), so the pushforward composition formula proves \(\mathcal L_{Q^{\otimes N}}(C_iX)=\mathcal L_Q(X)\). The almost everywhere change of representative does not affect the law.

\section{Use the actual tagged joint pair as a coupling}


On the product space couple the actual pair \((\Theta^N,X_i^N)\) with the genuine iid copy of \((\theta,X)\). Both are \(L^2\), so the coupling has finite cost. The joint-pair norm bound gives

\[W_2(\mathcal L(\Theta^N,X_i^N),\mathcal L(\theta,X))\le\|\Theta^N-\theta\|_2+\|X_i^N-X_i\|_2.\]

Finiteness is established before converting the extended transport value to a real number.

\section{Retain the true Brownian input in the final law theorem}


The joint pair \((\Theta_t^N,X_t^{i,N})\) has finite second moment, and each reference copy has exactly the actual law of \(Y_t=(\theta_t,X_t)\). Their constructed joint distribution is an admissible coupling. The pair L\ensuremath{{}^{2}} norm is at most the sum of its coordinate norms, giving


\[
W_2\!\left(\mathcal L(\Theta_t^N,X_t^{i,N}),\mathcal L(\theta_t,X_t)\right)
 \le\frac{C_T}{\sqrt N},\qquad 0\le t\le T.
\]


The final endpoint retains the particle integral equation, the exact iid initial law and the actual Brownian input alongside this bound. The Wasserstein distance is proved finite before converting it to a real number. This proves convergence of actual tagged one-time laws; pathwise strong-SDE, expected path-supremum and gradient-flow PDE statements remain outside the formalized result.
