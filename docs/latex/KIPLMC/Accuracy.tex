\section{\texorpdfstring{Round the exponential and statistical requirements}{Round the exponential and statistical requirements}}


The exponential error bound uses \(n\ge\lceil\max\{0,\log(24D/\varepsilon)/\alpha\}\rceil\), with a separate zero-\(D\) case. Monotonicity of the exponential and \(e^{-\log x}=x^{-1}\) give \(De^{-\alpha n}\le\varepsilon/24\). For a square-root statistical term \(C\sqrt{d/N}\), choose \(N=\max\{1,\lceil9C^2d/\varepsilon^2\rceil\}\). The ceiling inequality, multiplication through \(N>0\), and the square-root identity prove the statistical term is at most \(\varepsilon/3\). Zero parameter dimension is included.



The particle ceiling ensures a positive integer count and the desired concentration-error bound, including zero parameter dimension. The iteration ceiling similarly converts a real decay threshold into a valid integer iteration count. The zero-initial-error case avoids taking an unnecessary positive logarithm.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Choose the step and inspect the dimension algebra}{Choose the step and inspect the dimension algebra}}
