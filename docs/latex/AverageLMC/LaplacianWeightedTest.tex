The observation is the actual probability integral E[w F(X)]. Its continuity is proved from boundedness and a second-moment tail estimate, allowing weak Euler limits to be taken on the L\ensuremath{{}^{2}} state space.



The actual weighted observation is


\[\mathcal O_{w,F}(X)=\int w(a)F(X(a))\,P(da).\]


If \(|w|\le A\), \(|F|\le C\), and \(|F(x)-F(y)|\le\varepsilon\) whenever \(\|x-y\|\le\delta\), then


\[\left|\mathcal O_{w,F}(X)-\mathcal O_{w,F}(Y)\right|
\le A\left(\varepsilon+\frac{2C}{\delta^2}\|X-Y\|_{L^2(P)}^2\right).\]
\section{Define and integrate the weighted test}


Define lpWeightedTest using the actual representative of X and the measure P. Measurability of w and continuity of F give measurability of the product; the bounds |w|\ensuremath{\le}A almost everywhere and |F|\ensuremath{\le}C dominate it by the integrable constant AC.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{lpWeightedTest}, \texttt{lpWeightedTest\_integrable}.

\section{Make the continuity estimate explicit}


If F oscillates by at most ε at distance at most δ, then everywhere |F(x)−F(y)|\ensuremath{\le}ε+(2C/δ\ensuremath{{}^{2}})‖x−y‖\ensuremath{{}^{2}}. Integrate against the bounded weight and identify the squared L\ensuremath{{}^{2}} norm with the squared-distance integral. The resulting bound is A[ε+(2C/δ\ensuremath{{}^{2}})‖X−Y‖\ensuremath{{}^{2}}].



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{lpWeightedTest\_difference\_le}.

\section{Transfer uniform continuity from state tests}


Choose a small test oscillation first, then a sufficiently small L\ensuremath{{}^{2}} radius to control the quadratic tail term. This proves uniform continuity of X↦E[wF(X)] for every bounded uniformly continuous F.
