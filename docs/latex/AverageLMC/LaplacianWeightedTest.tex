\title{\texorpdfstring{Bounded weighted observations are uniformly continuous on L\ensuremath{{}^{2}}}{Bounded weighted observations are uniformly continuous on L²}}

Draft mathematical exposition; these notes may contain mistakes.


The observation is the actual probability integral E[w F(X)]. Its continuity is proved from boundedness and a second-moment tail estimate, allowing weak Euler limits to be taken on the L\ensuremath{{}^{2}} state space.



The actual weighted observation is


\[\mathcal O_{w,F}(X)=\int w(a)F(X(a))\,P(da).\]


If \(|w|\le A\), \(|F|\le C\), and \(|F(x)-F(y)|\le\varepsilon\) whenever \(\|x-y\|\le\delta\), then


\[\left|\mathcal O_{w,F}(X)-\mathcal O_{w,F}(Y)\right|
\le A\left(\varepsilon+\frac{2C}{\delta^2}\|X-Y\|_{L^2(P)}^2\right).\]

Earlier results used here: \cite[Section~3.13]{LangevinLean2026ULASC}.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Define and integrate the weighted test}{Define and integrate the weighted test}}


Define the weighted observation \(wF(X)\) using the actual representative of \(X\) and the measure \(P\). Measurability of w and continuity of F give measurability of the product; the bounds |w|\ensuremath{\le}A almost everywhere and |F|\ensuremath{\le}C dominate it by the integrable constant AC.


\section{\texorpdfstring{Make the continuity estimate explicit}{Make the continuity estimate explicit}}


If F oscillates by at most \ensuremath{\varepsilon} at distance at most \ensuremath{\delta}, then everywhere |F(x)\ensuremath{-}F(y)|\ensuremath{\le}\ensuremath{\varepsilon}+(2C/\allowbreak{}\ensuremath{\delta}\ensuremath{{}^{2}})\ensuremath{\Vert}x\ensuremath{-}y\ensuremath{\Vert}\ensuremath{{}^{2}}. Integrate against the bounded weight and identify the squared L\ensuremath{{}^{2}} norm with the squared-distance integral. The resulting bound is A[\ensuremath{\varepsilon}+(2C/\allowbreak{}\ensuremath{\delta}\ensuremath{{}^{2}})\ensuremath{\Vert}X\ensuremath{-}Y\ensuremath{\Vert}\ensuremath{{}^{2}}].


\section{\texorpdfstring{Transfer uniform continuity from state tests}{Transfer uniform continuity from state tests}}


Choose a small test oscillation first, then a sufficiently small L\ensuremath{{}^{2}} radius to control the quadratic tail term. This proves uniform continuity of \(X\mapsto\mathbb E[wF(X)]\) for every bounded uniformly continuous F.
