Main sampling statements use extended nonnegative transport, allowing an infinite initial second moment. Their finite-distance real versions are convenient for the analytic proofs.

\[W_2^2(\mu',\pi')\le aW_2^2(\mu,\pi)+b,\qquad a>0,\ b\ge0,\]

The inequality is interpreted in the extended nonnegative reals, including infinite initial distance.


\section{\texorpdfstring{Extend a positive affine squared bound to infinity}{Extend a positive affine squared bound to infinity}}


Split on finiteness of the initial Wasserstein distance. In the finite case, derive output finiteness and embed the nonnegative real estimate into the extended nonnegative reals, preserving squared costs and nonnegative arithmetic. If the initial distance is infinite, the strictly positive contraction coefficient makes the right side infinite. Thus the main endpoint needs no independent moment assumption.
