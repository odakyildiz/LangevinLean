\section{The strongly convex Wasserstein route}


The imported modules prove Gibbs normalization and score moments, construct independent Brownian coordinates and \(L^2\) Langevin solutions, establish Gibbs stationarity by invariant Metropolis approximation, and assemble a stationary coupling reference for every finite initial coupling. Infimizing the resulting cost gives

\[W_2(P_h^k\mu,\pi)\le(1-mh)^kW_2(\mu,\pi)+\frac{33}{20}\frac Lm\sqrt{hd}\]

under differentiability, \(m>0\) strong convexity, \(L\)-Lipschitz gradient, \(0<h\le2/(m+L)\), and finite initial \(W_2\). The alternative stable branch and the theorem for an implemented iid-Gaussian \(L^2\) recursion are imported as well.
