The Metropolis correction preserves the initial marginal whenever the accepted proposal flow is invariant under swapping its two states. A positive proposal-pair density makes this symmetry explicit.


\section{acceptedFlow}


Weight the current/proposal joint measure \(\nu(dx,dy)\) by acceptance probability \(a(x,y)\). Thus \(A(dx,dy)=a(x,y)\nu(dx,dy)\), with \texttt{ofReal} embedding its real nonnegative weight. The bounds \(0\le a\le1\) are supplied in the conservation theorems below.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{acceptedFlow}.

\section{rejectedFlow}


The rejected pair measure is \(R(dx,dy)=(1-a(x,y))\nu(dx,dy)\). It retains the proposed state as an auxiliary coordinate; the eventual output maps it to the original state \(x\), preserving all rejection mass.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{rejectedFlow}.

\section{metropolisOutput}


Define the output as \(\operatorname{snd}_*A+\operatorname{fst}_*R\). Accepted moves output \(y\), while rejected moves output \(x\). This is the complete transition measure and requires no renormalization of accepted moves.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{metropolisOutput}.

\section{acceptedFlow add rejectedFlow}


Since \(0\le a\le1\), the two nonnegative weights add to one after embedding in extended scalars. Additivity of with-density measures then gives \(A+R=\nu\). The proof checks both nonnegativity guards before combining \texttt{ofReal} values.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{acceptedFlow\_add\_rejectedFlow}.

\section{marginals eq of swap invariant}


The identity \(\operatorname{fst}\circ\operatorname{swap}=\operatorname{snd}\) and pushforward composition give \(\operatorname{snd}_*\nu=\operatorname{fst}_*(\operatorname{swap}_*\nu)\). If the pair measure is swap invariant, this is \(\operatorname{fst}_*\nu\), proving equality of its marginals.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{marginals\_eq\_of\_swap\_invariant}.

\section{metropolisOutput eq initial}


Detailed balance makes the two marginals of the accepted flow equal. Replace its second marginal by its first, then combine the accepted and rejected first marginals using \(A+R=\nu\). The output is therefore \(\operatorname{fst}_*\nu\), exactly the original state law.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{metropolisOutput\_eq\_initial}.

\section{map weighted comp}


For a measurable \(f\) and weight \(w\), prove \(f_*((w\circ f)\mu)=w(f_*\mu)\). On each measurable set, the pushforward definition and nonnegative change-of-variables formula turn both sides into \(\int\mathbf1_A(f(x))w(f(x))d\mu(x)\). This is the weight-transport identity used by the actual MALA proposal map.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{map\_weighted\_comp}.

\section{map weighted comp equiv}


Specialize the preceding pushforward/weight identity to a measurable equivalence. Its bundled measurability supplies the required hypothesis. The application below uses the coordinate swap equivalence to prove symmetry of an accepted density.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{map\_weighted\_comp\_equiv}.

\section{metropolisAcceptance}


For positive joint density \(w(x,y)\), define \(a(x,y)=\min(1,w(y,x)/w(x,y))\). The ratio is reverse flow divided by forward flow; positivity ensures both its meaning and nonnegative acceptance.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{metropolisAcceptance}.

\section{weight mul metropolisAcceptance}


Distribute the positive factor \(w(x,y)\) through the minimum. Cancellation of the nonzero denominator gives \(w(x,y)a(x,y)=\min(w(x,y),w(y,x))\). This algebra is the reason the accepted joint density is symmetric.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{weight\_mul\_metropolisAcceptance}.

\section{acceptedFlow density}


Two successive changes of density multiply their weights. Combine the original positive density \(w\) and acceptance \(a\), then apply the weight/minimum identity. The accepted flow becomes the base measure weighted by \(\min(w,w\circ\operatorname{swap})\), a visibly symmetric density.



\noindent Lean declarations: \texttt{acceptedFlow\_density}.

\section{metropolisOutput density eq initial}


The base measure is swap invariant, and the accepted minimum density is unchanged by swapping its arguments. The weight-transport theorem therefore proves accepted-flow detailed balance. The general accepted/rejected conservation theorem then identifies the output with the initial marginal.
