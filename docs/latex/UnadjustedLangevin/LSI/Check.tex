The final Gaussian ULA entropy theorem assumes a differentiable potential with a globally Lipschitz gradient, LSI for its Gibbs probability measure, the positive step restriction, and finite initial relative entropy. It proves the needed gradient moment, Gaussian density regularity, finite Fisher information, entropy absolute continuity, and the estimate near elapsed time zero. The conditional interpolation results retain their additional density and weak-equation assumptions.


\section{\texorpdfstring{Verification of the hypotheses and proof dependencies}{Verification of the hypotheses and proof dependencies}}


The mathematical implication is

\[H'\le-\tfrac34I+E\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H_{k+1}\le e^{-\alpha h}H_k+6h^2dL^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H_k\le e^{-\alpha hk}H_0+8hdL^2/\alpha.\]

The first implication also uses LSI and the specified gradient and discrepancy bounds. The second uses the step restriction and geometric summation. The final theorem proves the required analytic estimates for the Gaussian interpolation. Conditional variants require those estimates as explicit hypotheses.
