\section{\texorpdfstring{lp\allowbreak{}Drift aestrongly\allowbreak{}Measurable}{lpDrift aestronglyMeasurable}}

Transport across that equality proves the result independently of the representative selected for the \(L^2\) class.

\section{\texorpdfstring{lp\allowbreak{}Langevin\allowbreak{}Past}{lpLangevinPast}}

\section{\texorpdfstring{lp\allowbreak{}Langevin\allowbreak{}Past le}{lpLangevinPast le}}


Every generator of the past is measurable in the ambient sigma-algebra. The universal property of a supremum of sigma-algebras therefore gives \(\mathcal F_T\le\mathcal F\). This inclusion is required to use the closed \(L^2\) subspace of variables measurable from the past.

\section{\texorpdfstring{lp\allowbreak{}Langevin\allowbreak{}Past initial}{lpLangevinPast initial}}

Its inclusion makes the initial variable measurable for \(\mathcal F_T\), and the measurable representative of an \(L^2\) variable supplies the almost-everywhere strong-measurability formulation used by the submodule theorem.
